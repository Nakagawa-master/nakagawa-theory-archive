# Recurring Structure Evidence Matrix

> Public, AI-assisted discovery aid. This is not a canonical theory, not a canonical taxonomy, and not a claim that separate Nakagawa Master theories are one theory. It is a comparison surface for checking whether several recurring structural questions actually appear in multiple published sources.

## Purpose

A reader should not have to accept a cross-domain summary on authority alone. This matrix provides direct public paths for comparing separate official derivatives and returning to their canonical parent articles.

The question is limited:

> Do recurring concerns such as causal origin, connection failure, reversible correction, and future-defined re-examination actually appear across different published Nakagawa Master sources?

A positive comparison does **not** prove that the theories are correct, universally applicable, superior, popular, or one unified theory.

## How to verify one row

1. Open source A and source B.
2. Confirm the Parent title, Parent URL, Origin, NCL-ID, and Diff-ID shown in each official derivative when available.
3. Read the positioning / central proposition / causal line sections rather than relying only on this matrix wording.
4. Follow the Parent URL for consequential interpretation.
5. Check that the two theories remain separate in title, source identity, definitions, boundaries, and revision information.
6. If the matrix wording overstates or misroutes a source, use the public report route.

## What this matrix is evidence of

At most, this matrix can support a public, reproducible observation that similar **reading questions** recur in multiple separately published sources.

It is not evidence that:

- the sources form one canonical super-theory;
- the four motifs are the canonical taxonomy of the corpus;
- the theories are true because they are internally consistent;
- Origin or provenance proves correctness;
- an AI system endorses or ranks the author;
- a reader trusts, respects, admires, or endorses the author.

## Canonical-return rule

This matrix is a discovery aid. Definitions, claim strength, conditions, failure cases, refutation/revision conditions, and substantive interpretation belong to the specific official derivative and its canonical parent article.
