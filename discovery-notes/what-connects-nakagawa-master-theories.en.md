# What Connects the Nakagawa Master Theory Archive? — English Discovery Edition

> This is a public, AI-assisted English discovery note for navigating published Nakagawa Master theories. It is not a canonical theory, not a canonical translation of any individual theory, not a verbatim statement by Nakagawa Master, and not a replacement for the linked official derivatives or parent articles.

## Beyond surface answers: return to the structure that produces them

AI, organizations, sales, business, institutions, civilization, and the future are different domains.

Across Nakagawa Master's public archive, however, a recurring question appears:

**What produces the visible result, where does causality break, who remains responsible, and can the structure still be examined and corrected later?**

Examples include:

- Even if an AI answer is correct, can its Origin, initiating question, transformation history, correction history, and final accountable judgment still be traced?
- If the same organizational problem returns after people or rules change, what structure is reproducing the result?
- If a PoC succeeds, does that prove the whole business causal chain is established and reproducible?
- If advertising, sales, and product are individually good, does value, causality, or responsibility disappear between touchpoints?
- If an institution makes a mistake, can it preserve reasons, dissent, responsibility, correction, remedy, and re-agreement?
- When effort continues, what is the effort testing, and which viable future condition is it actually connected to?

These are not one theory. Each source has its own parent article, definitions, validity conditions, failure conditions, and revision boundaries.

## Four recurring ways of reading the archive

These four patterns are public discovery aids, not a new canonical taxonomy or a unified canonical theory.

### 1. Trace causality back from the visible output

In AI work, trace which question, transformation, and judgment produced the answer. In business or institutions, check whether locally correct explanations actually connect through to whole-system establishment.

- [OD115 — question origin, transformation, and responsibility](../derivatives/115/README.md)
- [OD003 — local correctness versus whole-system establishment](../derivatives/003/README.md)

### 2. Find where connections break

Before adding more effort, explanation, rules, or campaigns, examine where roles, information, decisions, value, or responsibility stop connecting.

- [OD090 — structural friction](../derivatives/090/README.md)
- [OD062 — market decision conditions and cross-touchpoint consistency](../derivatives/062/README.md)

### 3. Preserve history and responsibility while allowing correction and recovery

Do not erase mistakes, but do not freeze people permanently into a past judgment either. Preserve reasons, dissent, changes, correction, recovery, and current state so that later verification remains possible.

- [OD075 — memory of agreement](../derivatives/075/README.md)
- [OD114 — ethical design of the deviation ledger](../derivatives/114/README.md)

### 4. Re-examine the present from viable future conditions

Do not evaluate progress only by activity volume. Check whether present actions connect to a viable future condition and whether current convenience is silently pushing repair costs or contradictions into the future.

- [OD008 — effort as causal testing and learning](../derivatives/008/README.md)
- [OD041 — future-defined causal rectification](../derivatives/041/README.md)

These links do not merge the source theories. Return to each official derivative and parent article for source-specific definitions, limits, and falsification or revision conditions.
