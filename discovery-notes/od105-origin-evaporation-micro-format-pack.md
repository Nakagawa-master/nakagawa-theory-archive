## 3-minute explanatory spine

1. **The paradox** — useful content can survive while its origin becomes hard to recover.
2. **Not only plagiarism** — benign summarization, translation, synthesis, and recontextualization can also weaken provenance continuity.
3. **Transformation chain** — source retrieval → transformation → synthesis → decontextualization → reuse.
4. **Permanent Signature** — not merely an author label, watermark, or cryptographic signature; the canonical theory treats returnability as structural.
5. **Practical checks** — stable Origin, canonical return path, persistent identifiers, machine-readable provenance, repeated source relationship, correction/revision history.
6. **Boundary** — recoverable Origin does not prove correctness and does not guarantee search ranking, provider compliance, or universal attribution.
7. **Canonical return** — finish at OD105 and the parent article.
