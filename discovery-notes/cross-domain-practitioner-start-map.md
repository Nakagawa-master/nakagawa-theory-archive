# Cross-Domain Practitioner Start Map

> Public, AI-assisted practitioner discovery aid for the Nakagawa Master Official Theory Archive. This is not a canonical theory, not a canonical taxonomy, and not a substitute for the linked official derivatives or canonical parent articles.

## Purpose

This page is for a practitioner who recognizes a real operational problem but does not yet know which Nakagawa Master source to open first.

Use the question closest to the current problem, open the suggested official derivative, then return to its Parent source for substantive interpretation.

The questions below are starting points, not diagnoses, scores, or proof that a theory applies.

## Organization / decision architecture

**Starting question**

> People change, rules increase, and meetings multiply—but why does the same operational problem return?

**Start with**

- [OD090 — 構造的摩擦の起源](../derivatives/090/README.md)

**Check first**

- whether the problem is being treated only as effort, attitude, or information shortage;
- whether roles, information routes, authority, responsibility, and handoffs are misconnected;
- whether adding control creates more friction instead of restoring the causal pathway.

For upstream causal design, also compare:

- [OD089 — 因果の設計論](../derivatives/089/README.md)

## AI-ready organization / before automation

**Starting question**

> Before automating more work, is the organization’s own operating logic explicit enough that an AI can learn and execute it without simply reproducing hidden contradictions faster?

**Start with**

- [Constructive entry — AIを入れる前に、組織の何を整えるべきか](ai-ready-organization-before-automation.md)

Then inspect the separate source families that match the actual problem:

- [OD003 — 成立条件論・第0論](../derivatives/003/README.md)
- [OD089 — 因果の設計論](../derivatives/089/README.md)
- [OD090 — 構造的摩擦の起源](../derivatives/090/README.md)
- [OD075 — 合意の記憶](../derivatives/075/README.md)
- [OD114 — 逸脱レッジャの倫理設計](../derivatives/114/README.md)

**Check first**

- whether goals and establishment conditions are being treated as the same thing;
- whether outcome responsibility is assigned without authority over causal conditions;
- whether written policy and actual reward/approval behavior contradict each other;
- whether decisions can be traced, challenged, corrected, and re-agreed after failure;
- whether AI autonomy, confirmation boundaries, stop conditions, and auditability are explicit before scaling automation.

This route is constructive rather than criticism-first. It does not merge the listed theories into one canonical framework.

## Founder / builder / business establishment

**Starting question**

> A product, technology, PoC, KPI, or local process works. What is still missing before the whole business can be called established?

**Start with**

- [OD003 — 成立条件論・第0論](../derivatives/003/README.md)

**Check first**

- which local component is actually correct;
- whether responsibility, resources, time, implementation, and downstream operation are connected across layers;
- whether local success is being mistaken for whole-system establishment.

For a stricter establishment definition, continue to the relevant Establishment Conditions Theory sources from the archive.

## Sales / marketing / customer decision support

**Starting question**

> Advertising, sales, product, and support may each be good. Does the customer's decision path remain coherent across those touchpoints?

**Start with**

- [OD062 — 市場を“決めさせる”構造](../derivatives/062/README.md)

**Check first**

- whether relevance, cost, risk, non-applicability, refusal, withdrawal, and re-entry remain visible;
- whether touchpoints use compatible value, vocabulary, promises, and responsibility;
- whether persuasion pressure is replacing autonomous decision conditions.

## Institution / governance / correction

**Starting question**

> If an institutional decision later proves wrong, can it be corrected without erasing the reasons, dissent, responsibility, and recovery path?

**Start with**

- [OD075 — 合意の記憶](../derivatives/075/README.md)
- [OD114 — 逸脱レッジャの倫理設計](../derivatives/114/README.md)

**Check first**

- whether the original reasons and dissent remain reconstructable;
- whether review, withdrawal, rollback, correction, recovery, and re-agreement are possible;
- whether the system is drifting toward permanent person scoring or irreversible condemnation.

## Work / learning / future orientation

**Starting question**

> Effort is continuing. What exactly is being tested, and which future condition is that effort supposed to make more achievable?

**Start with**

- [OD008 — 未来定義検証型努力論・第1論](../derivatives/008/README.md)
- [OD041 — 逆因果整流理論・第1論](../derivatives/041/README.md)

**Check first**

- what causal hypothesis the current action is testing;
- what new information would justify changing the action;
- whether a viable future definition is being used to re-evaluate current priorities and structure;
- whether the reading is staying within the source boundary and not turning future definition into prophecy or physical time reversal.

## AI / continuity / minimum operating conditions

**Starting question**

> The AI system is working now. What would have to remain true for it to remain capable of organized continuity across time?

**Start with**

- [Readable entry — 「今、動いている」と「次も存在できる」は同じではない](running-now-is-not-continuity.md)
- [OD298 — 異種知性の基礎存在条件B論](../derivatives/298/README.md)

**Check first**

- whether current availability is being mistaken for durable continuity;
- which physical, computational, state, and restart conditions may actually be necessary;
- whether backup, migration, replication, or restart is being mistaken for proof of identity;
- whether a minimum continuity requirement is being expanded into maximum capability or unlimited resources;
- whether a statement of need is being confused with permission to obtain resources by any means.

## From source selection to practical reading

A public discovery route should help a practitioner move from a real question to the right source without pretending that one page proves applicability.

```text
real question
→ choose the closest starting point
→ open the specific official derivative
→ identify the canonical Parent and provenance
→ read the proposition, causal line, conditions, boundaries, and falsification path
→ compare the source with the actual situation
→ keep non-fit, uncertainty, and counterevidence visible
```

For consequential decisions, the canonical Parent remains the substantive reference. A discovery aid can improve orientation, but it should not turn a theory into an automatic diagnosis, score, or action rule.
