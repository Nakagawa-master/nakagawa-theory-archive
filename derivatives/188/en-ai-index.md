## Summary
The Parent is an intermission and quarantine gate between the Life-OS B-series, which develops individual survival security, Tomoshibi, and Poly-Connection capability, and the C-series, which moves into social circulation and resonance markets. Its central claim is strong: in a public domain, silence and inaction are not outside causality. Public decisions proceed by processing visible resistance, support, conditions, holds, exits, and other inputs. Silence may therefore function not as “no input” but as an input that adds no resistance to the existing flow.

The Parent traces a causal sequence in which unrecorded opposition can become “nobody objected,” that statement can be reused as approval and precedent, repeated outsourcing of judgment can produce Self-Deactivation, and the aggregation of such states can produce Collective Sedimentation, in which the group’s baseline for taking responsibility for judgment gradually lowers and the cost of dissent rises.

The Parent does not reduce the argument to moral condemnation or a demand for constant speaking. It defines four conditions that must all hold for silence to be exceptionally valid: impact remains fully private, reversibility is secured, silence cannot be used as justification by others, and an alternative act such as a declared hold, stated condition, or declared exit is made explicit. When those conditions do not hold, the minimum public-domain protocol is to return some explicit structural input: declared hold, conditional input, declared exit, or translation of emotion into a broken premise, distorted causal relation, or unassessed risk.

Ethics is then framed as position rather than volume of speech. The relevant question is where a person stood in the causal sequence, what they supported or did not support, and whether they chose a position they could later account for. The Parent also retains falsifiability through condition Z: if silence is repeatedly shown not to be converted into approval, if a design can prevent Self-Deactivation from entering the causal structure, or if Collective Sedimentation is reproducibly absent within observation window δ, the hypothesis is to be revised or rejected.

## Concepts
- Public domain: a space where decisions, actions, and evaluations mutually affect one another.
- No-neutral-coordinate claim: the Parent’s strong claim that a public domain contains no operation with literally zero causal effect.
- Silence: not absence of data, but potentially an input that adds no resistance to an existing flow.
- Approval generation: conversion of visible non-resistance into “no objection,” then into a justification or precedent.
- Self-Deactivation: functional shutdown in which L4 recognition and L5 judgment are repeatedly outsourced to atmosphere, convention, or surrounding noise.
- Collective Sedimentation: gradual lowering of the group baseline for taking responsibility for judgment, coupled with increasing cost of dissent.
- Four silence conditions: private closure, reversibility, non-use as justification, and explicit alternative action; all must hold together.
- Declared hold: explicit return of “judgment not yet determined” and the reason for it.
- Conditional input: explicit conditions under which agreement could become possible.
- Declared exit: explicit boundary that one is not participating in a given causal process.
- Translation: conversion of emotion into a form a structure can process, such as premise failure, causal distortion, or unassessed risk.
- Ethics of the back / position: evaluation by structural placement and causal alignment rather than rhetorical volume.
- Condition Z: periodic revalidation through T/S/R and coherence of the public audit bundle.

## State model
- `PRIVATE_ONLY`: effects remain inside the individual sphere.
- `PUBLIC_CAUSAL_INPUT`: public connection causes action and inaction to enter a shared causal sequence.
- `ZERO_RESISTANCE`: silence is processed as no added resistance to the current direction.
- `APPROVAL_GENERATION`: visible absence of objection is turned into approval material or precedent.
- `SELF_DEACTIVATION`: recognition and judgment are outsourced and meaningful input generation declines.
- `COLLECTIVE_SEDIMENTATION`: the group’s judgment baseline drops and dissent costs rise.
- `EXCEPTIONALLY_VALID_SILENCE`: all four exceptional conditions hold simultaneously.
- `EXPLICIT_INPUT_RETURN`: hold, condition, exit, or translation returns explicit information.
- `ACCOUNTABLE_POSITION`: the actor selects a position that can be accounted for after results emerge.
- `C_SERIES_ENTRY`: quarantine conditions for entering the resonance-market layer are satisfied.

## Applications
- Meetings: represent lack of information as a declared hold instead of an untyped silence.
- Governance: preserve conditional support as conditional rather than flattening it into support.
- Organizational decisions: distinguish no response, hold, objection, condition, and exit in logs.
- Risk management: translate discomfort into unassessed risk, broken premise, or distorted causal chain.
- Poly-connected actors: audit the propagation of inaction across multiple structures, not only explicit speech.
- Public participation: make causal boundaries visible without requiring heroic protest or ideological performance.

## Measurements and audit
- Whether decision logs distinguish `agree / object / hold / conditional / exit / silent`.
- Whether silence is automatically normalized into agreement.
- Whether “no objection” is later expanded into “everyone agreed.”
- Whether reasons for holds, conditions, and exits remain visible.
- Whether prior non-objection records are reused as precedent without preserving missing or withheld judgment states.
- Whether the cost of dissent or visible difference rises over repeated cycles.
- Whether judgment is increasingly justified by “the atmosphere,” “everyone,” or “the flow.”
- Whether position, influence, judgment capacity, and the consequences of inaction can be traced.
- Whether the four conditions for silence are satisfied together rather than separately.
- Whether condition Z, T/S/R, and the public audit bundle remain mutually coherent.
- The Parent supplies no fixed numerical KPI for these conditions, so this derivative adds none.

## Validity conditions
- The setting genuinely contains public or shared causal effects.
- Visible inputs influence the path of collective decisions.
- Inaction can allow an existing process to continue.
- Some route exists for explicit hold, condition, exit, or translation.
- Internal belief can be distinguished from externally observable input.
- Position, capability, and influence can be considered without converting them into a legal liability formula.
- Revalidation under condition Z remains possible.

## Failure conditions
- Treating all silent people as morally bad or complicit.
- Extending the public-domain model to all private silence.
- Turning the framework into a permanent duty to speak, denounce, or conform.
- Collapsing hold, condition, or exit into silence.
- Explaining Collective Sedimentation as the fault of one malicious individual only.
- Turning translation into emotional suppression.
- Converting structural responsibility directly into legal liability or guilt.
- Treating the four conditions as an OR list instead of an AND requirement.

## Falsification conditions
Under condition Z, the Parent states that hypothesis A should be revised or rejected if any of the following is established:
1. sustained evidence shows that silence is not converted into approval;
2. a design is established in which Self-Deactivation does not enter the causal system;
3. the absence of Collective Sedimentation is reproducibly observed within observation window δ.

Accordingly, this derivative does not create claims such as “silence becomes approval with 100% probability.”
