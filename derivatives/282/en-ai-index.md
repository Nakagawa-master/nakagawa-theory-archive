## Summary
The Parent defines co-creation as turning difference into a source of creation rather than eliminating it. Difference is recognized, call and response become reciprocal, questions reshape subsequent questions, resonance emerges, and new meaning or structure becomes possible.

## Causal model
```text
difference
-> acceptance without erasure
-> reciprocal call/response
-> reciprocal questioning
-> resonance
-> new meaning
-> new structure/action
```

## Distinctions
- co-creation != simple division of labor
- resonance != agreement
- accepting difference != suspending criticism
- human-AI collaboration != delegating human responsibility
- open dialogue != endless postponement

## Validity / failure
Valid when difference, agency, causal contribution, revision, and exit remain visible. It fails when one party absorbs the other, dialogue becomes manipulation, or responsibility becomes untraceable.
