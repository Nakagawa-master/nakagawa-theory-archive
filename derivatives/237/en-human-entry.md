## 15-Second Explanation
The external article came first. In composing that article, Nakagawa Master selected from Fermenteria’s public facts the value core of “opening brewing that had been closed inside the kura to everyday life in the city.” The Parent written afterward is a **Nakagawa structural reading that publicly dismantles why that core was selected and why it became the main axis of the external article**.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Describing Fermenteria only as an urban sake brewery or restaurant leaves only the novelty of the product and venue. The external article reread sake culture from “buying a finished product” to “accessing the making” by placing the reduction of distance from the production site, time, and people at the center of value.

The important point is that the later Parent did not create that external article. The later Parent breaks down, in the official archive, the value-core selection and article composition already carried out in the external article, and publicly separates the subject facts from Nakagawa Master’s editorial and structural-translation judgments.

## Practical Process
Collect verifiable facts on the subject side → temporarily classify the surface → extract the upstream problem of distance between people’s daily lives and the making site → make “redesigning access to making” the value core → compose it as the main axis of the preceding external article → in the later Parent, publicly dismantle why that core was selected, what was discarded, and which causal line was foregrounded.

## Application Example
In fields such as craft, fermentation, and regional food, where finished products reach people but the production site is distant from daily life, examine whether “access to making,” rather than a “sales channel,” can be the value core.

## Success Criterion
The subject facts and structural reading are separated, and a third party can explain the order: the external article came first, followed by the reason for selecting the value core, the reason for the article’s composition, and the later Parent’s public dismantling.

## Limitations
Having a production base inside a city does not necessarily establish cultural access. It is necessary to separately confirm whether actual points of contact lead to understanding and relationships with the making.

## Misreading Prevention
“Redesigning access to making” is not an official corporate definition. The sequence is not Parent → external article, but **external article → the later Parent’s public dismantling of the article-making operation**.
