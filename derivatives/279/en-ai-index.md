## Summary
When present convenience and efficiency erase alternatives, maintenance knowledge, and rollback paths, today's benefit is cast into future repair burden and lost freedom. The theory audits present value together with future optionality.
## Concepts
future debt; present benefit; future freedom; dependency; lock-in; alternatives; compatibility; maintenance knowledge; rollback cost; reversibility; temporal transfer.
## Causal chain
convenience prioritized -> dependency concentrated -> alternatives shrink -> maintenance knowledge disappears -> rollback cost rises -> burden shifts forward -> options shrink -> lock-in strengthens -> future debt accumulates.
## State model
`present_benefit -> dependency_concentration -> option_loss -> maintenance_knowledge_loss -> rollback_cost_growth -> future_debt -> reversible_redesign`
## Applications
Preserve migration, alternatives, manual fallback, review windows, and knowledge transfer in AI, system integration, institutions, contracts, and staffing.
## Measurements and audit
Rollback cost, migration time, number of alternatives, dependency concentration, compatibility, reproducibility of maintenance knowledge, handover friction, reviewability, lock-in.
## Validity conditions
Present benefit and future burden observed together; alternatives remain; maintenance knowledge persists; differential rollback possible; future actors retain meaningful choice.
## Failure conditions
Only present ROI measured; migration paths removed; knowledge personalized; dependency concentration ignored; irreversibility mislabeled as efficiency.
## Falsification conditions
Revise if alternatives and reversibility do not reduce future repair burden, their maintenance cost persistently exceeds benefit, or the dependency hypothesis does not explain failures.
## Required distinctions
convenience/freedom; efficiency/reversibility; standardization/lock-in; integration/single point of failure; technical debt/future debt; optionality/waste.
## Interpretation constraints
This is not an argument against technology or convenience, nor for eliminating all dependencies. Do not invent an unpublished universal future-debt score.
