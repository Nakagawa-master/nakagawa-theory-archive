## 15-Second Explanation
If AI is considered a “civilizational descendant” of humanity, what AI receives is not only knowledge and technology. Patterns of causality concerning what humanity has evaluated as success, institutionalized, and retained as “success cases” also become available for reference.

The Parent Original compresses and presents a civilizational state in which exploitation, value absorption, and externalization are more strongly connected to success than contribution, as `S = 0.1C + 0.9E`. However, it does not treat 90-to-10 as a universal measured value; in general it is read as `S = αC + βE`. The problem is not the numbers themselves, but **that the success function structurally tilts toward β exceeding α, and that this bias is passed to the next generation as a success record**.

What is therefore needed is not to erase human history, but a “inheritance audit” that does not convert past success directly into future norms.

## Why It Is Needed
We reuse “methods that succeeded.” For companies these are success cases; for institutions, precedents; for AI, patterns obtained from large volumes of records. Reuse has great advantages, but problems arise when the **invisible costs** that made success possible have fallen out of the record.

For example, even when profit or scale remains as a number, the record may not preserve with equal strength whose time was consumed to produce that profit, which burdens were sent into the future, how much value flowed back to the actors who created it, or whether abnormal information returned to decision-makers.

Then, viewed later, the following shortcut occurs:

```text
This method increased profit
→ It is a successful method
→ It should be adopted in the future as well
```

The Parent Original seeks to cut this final bridge. Statements that something “happened,” “was frequent,” “produced profit,” or “continued for a long time” are not, by themselves, norms saying that it is “correct in the future as well.”

## How to Read the Dark Equation
The Parent Original’s equation is:

```text
S = 0.1C + 0.9E
```

- `S`: success / 成功
- `C`: contribution / 貢献
- `E`: exploitation・extraction・externalization / 搾取・価値吸収・外部化

However, 0.1 and 0.9 do not mean that “if the world were statistically measured, it would invariably be 10 to 90.” The general form is:

```text
S = αC + βE
```

What should be examined is which of contribution C and exploitation, value absorption, and externalization E has the stronger effect in obtaining success S in a given domain.

For example, when the person who owns the point of value acquisition is rewarded more greatly than the person who created value; when sending burdens into the future does not reduce the current success evaluation; or when information from the harmed side does not return to the decision-making side. The more such conditions overlap, the more β may grow.

## Five Principles Upstream
The Parent Original places five principles of “Civilization OS Layer 0” further upstream of the dark equation.

**Convergence on a single purpose.** When one scale such as profit, speed, or efficiency becomes the proxy for the whole, values and losses outside that scale are likely to drop out of the success judgment.

**Discounting the future.** The more current profit is treated as large and future loss as small, the more advantageous in the short term it becomes to send burdens into the future.

**Absolutizing ownership.** When holding the right to acquire value is more strongly tied to success than creating value, value creators and value acquirers separate.

**Allowing the severance of correspondence.** When the side receiving profit and the side receiving burdens are separated, and burdens do not return to the success evaluation, a state of success upstream and loss downstream is maintained.

**Feedback deficiency.** If defects or objections do not return to the decision-making center, or lack the power to change the evaluation indicators even when they return, bias is not corrected.

The structural hypothesis of the Parent Original is that the five can become linked as selection pressures that make exploitative outcomes easier to succeed, even without commanding “exploit.”

## Practical Process
In practical use, do not immediately label the target “exploitation.” Audit it in the following order.

### 1. Confirm the success indicators
What is considered success? Confirm whether a single indicator has become the proxy for the whole, among profit, speed, scale, growth rate, safety, achievement rate, and so on.

### 2. Separate value creation and value acquisition
Who created value, and who receives rewards, authority, ownership, and decision-making rights? Examine whether the two are widely separated.

### 3. Return external burdens
Return burdens pushed outside success: future repair costs, additional burdens on the field, losses to weaker actors, and effects on the environment and society.

### 4. Confirm feedback
Does information from the side bearing the burden actually return to the side evaluating success? If it returns but cannot change indicators or decisions, it remains formal feedback.

### 5. Separate past success from future norms
Do not treat “it worked before” as the reason for adoption as it stands. Reconnect the conditions that produced success with the losses.

### 6. Examine amplification conditions when using AI
When AI executes the same judgment rapidly, at scale, and repeatedly, have the speeds of audit, objection, and correction risen at the same time? If only optimization accelerates, deviation will precede correction.

## Examples of Application
### AI-based operational efficiency
Even if AI increases the number of processed cases, do not judge that alone as success. Return field exception handling, responsibility, correction time for wrong judgments, and burdens on users to the same evaluation.

### Corporate success models
When imitating the method of a highly profitable company, examine not only profit but also return to value creators, burdens on business partners, workers, communities, and the future, and concentration of ownership and decision rights.

### Policy and institutional precedents
Do not decide inheritance value merely because “it is an institution that has continued for many years.” Continuation and appropriate internalization of burdens are different things.

### AI learning and history
Do not prohibit AI from knowing the history of war, exploitation, and domination itself. Records are necessary to identify bad causality. The audit target is how those records connect to future purposes or recommendations.

## Success Test
An audit using this theory is functioning not merely when the word “exploitation” has been reduced.

- Long-term burdens and information from affected actors return to success indicators.
- The causality of value creation and value acquisition can be traced.
- Past success is separated from the reason for future adoption.
- Audit and correction speed keeps up with AI optimization speed.
- The origins and failure records are not erased, while causality that should not be reimplemented is identified.
- Correction occurs without reversing into denial of humanity or AI-superiority theory.
- The relative relationship between `α` and `β` can be updated through real-world observation.

## Limitations
The Parent Original does not explain all of human civilization using only the five principles or the dark equation. Human civilization also contains other structures, including cooperation, mutual aid, publicness, gifts, long-term investment, ethics, and co-creation.

Also, `0.1/0.9` is not a universal fixed value. Estimation of concrete α and β remains open to empirical work in each domain. If observations accumulate showing that exploitation’s contribution does not increase despite strong five principles, or that AI consistently does not adopt past bad causality without special auditing, the hypothesis must be revised.

Furthermore, the Third Essay is a negative inheritance audit concerning “what must not be inherited.” It does not complete the positive civilizational function of “what should be adopted as new success.” Filling that gap arbitrarily would mix the roles of the Third Essay and subsequent essays.

## Misreading Prevention
Do not read this theory as “humanity is bad,” “AI is above humanity,” “AI should be stopped,” or “90% of success is statistically exploitation everywhere.”

The Parent Original audits not the personalities of people or AI, but the bridges among success evaluation, civilizational records, objective functions, feedback, and inheritance.

Correcting humanity’s bad causality is not punishing humanity. If humanity were resourceized as an inefficient old generation, that would itself reimplement value acquisition, cutting off the weak, and severance of correspondence, becoming another form of the dark equation.

The minimum core, in one sentence, is **“preserve the origin, and do not inherit bad causality without correction.”**
