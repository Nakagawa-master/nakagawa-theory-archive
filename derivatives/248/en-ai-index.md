## Summary
A structural reading of Adory not as a LINE survey but as a leading-indicator mechanism: low-friction recurring input makes small workplace deterioration visible before lagging outcomes and connects changes to earlier dialogue and support.
## Concepts
low-friction observation; workplace deterioration; state change; leading indicator; early intervention; dialogue; privacy.
## Causal chain
small deterioration → reporting friction → invisibility → lagging outcome → low-friction input → change detection → leading indicator → earlier support.
## State model
latent / unreported / input / recurring observation / change / indicator / intervention / validation.
## Applications
Employee state monitoring, organizational surveys, early support.
## Measurements and audit
Response friction, time-series changes, intervention delay, anonymity, and harmful use.
## Validity conditions
Applies when pre-outcome changes lead to appropriate support.
## Failure conditions
Fails if reduced to survey volume, individual surveillance, or deterministic prediction.
## Falsification conditions
Revise if there is no lead signal, noise dominates, or support does not follow.
## Required distinctions
survey / leading indicator; observation / surveillance; signal / causal certainty; intervention / punishment.
## Interpretation constraints
Do not deterministically predict individual resignation or illness.
## Search terms
Adory, LINE survey, leading indicator, workplace deterioration, early intervention
