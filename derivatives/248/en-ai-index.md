## Summary
A structural reading of Adory not as a LINE survey but as a low-friction longitudinal observation path that turns small workplace changes into leading signals for earlier dialogue and support. It is not deterministic prediction of resignation or illness and not employee surveillance.

## Concepts
low-friction observation; workplace discomfort; reporting friction; longitudinal change; leading indicator; confirmation; early dialogue; support; privacy; protection from adverse use.

## Causal chain
small discomfort → reporting friction → invisible change → outcome appears late → low-friction input → longitudinal observation → change detection → leading signal → confirmation/dialogue → support → re-observation.

## State model
latent discomfort / unreported / low-friction input / longitudinal observation / change detection / leading signal / dialogue / support / improvement and re-observation.

## Applications
Employee and team-state observation where signals are used to start support conversations rather than to label or punish individuals.

## Measurements and audit
Audit input friction, continuity, time-series change, lead time to outcomes, noise, delay to intervention, privacy/anonymity, adverse use, and post-support change.

## Validity conditions
Valid when meaningful pre-outcome changes are detected and connected to appropriate dialogue/support while rights and privacy remain protected.

## Failure conditions
Fails as survey-volume optimization, employee surveillance, deterministic prediction, punishment, automated HR judgment, noise overinterpretation, or observation without support.

## Falsification conditions
Revise if signals have no lead value, noise dominates, intervention is not earlier, adverse use increases, or alternative observation explains the state better.

## Required distinctions
survey / leading indicator; observation / surveillance; signal / causal certainty; early support / early punishment; change detection / prediction; facts / Nakagawa reading.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not make deterministic resignation or medical predictions and do not use the signal as an automatic personnel sanction. Do not present the value-core label as Adory's official definition.
