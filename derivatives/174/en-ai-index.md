## Summary
Nakagawa-style Learning OS defines learning not as knowledge accumulation, memorization, or reflection alone, but as an internal OS update that observes the structures producing phenomena, integrates those causal observations into recognition, judgment, action, and procedure, rectifies distortion, and thereby raises emergence precision. The Structural Wu-Wei sequence is 0 definition → 1 no resistance → 2–4 rectification → 5 active waiting → 6 emergence. Learning OS functions as the internal mechanism that improves the precision of this whole sequence. Knowledge is treated as a sensor that expands the number and resolution of observable structures. It gains practical force only when connected through knowledge → observation → rectification → higher emergence precision. A Future Line is not prophecy; it is a high-precision simulation produced by increasingly well-aligned structure, and Future Line precision is treated as a by-product of emergence precision.

## Concepts
- **Learning OS:** an internal update mechanism integrating causal observation, structural rectification, and higher emergence precision.
- **Knowledge as sensor:** knowledge expands what structures can be observed and at what resolution.
- **Causal observation:** observing the structure and causal line that generated a result rather than copying the result itself.
- **Internal OS integration:** incorporating observation into recognition, judgment, action, and procedures.
- **No Resistance:** finding an angle that does not unnecessarily oppose the causal flow or increase friction.
- **Rectification:** continuously removing distortion, blockage, and friction until causality can flow naturally.
- **Active Waiting:** observing causal density after structural alignment while waiting for maturation rather than abandoning the process.
- **Emergence:** a result arising when causal density crosses the relevant threshold.
- **Emergence precision:** the precision with which structural observation and rectification connect to real-world results.
- **Future Line:** a high-precision structural simulation, not supernatural prediction or a guaranteed future.

## Causal chain
```text

```

- In project delays, inspect approval flow, information waiting, dependencies, responsibility placement, and procedure rather than attributing the outcome only to effort.
- In AI use, analyze how input structure, context, evaluation criteria, and output differences relate instead of merely collecting successful prompts.

## Measurements and audit
- Distinguish increased knowledge volume from increased observable-structure count and resolution.
- Test whether the generating causal structure can be explained rather than merely naming success or failure.
- Confirm whether observations changed recognition, judgment, action, or procedure.
- Check whether recurrent friction, blockage, and rework decrease.
- Preserve the sequence 0 definition → 1 no resistance → 2–4 rectification → 5 waiting → 6 emergence.
- Treat rectification as a continuous process until causal flow is restored, not as a retry count.
- Continue observing causal density during waiting.
- Separate improved emergence precision from a favorable outcome caused mainly by chance.
- Treat Future Lines as simulations, not prophecy.
- Treat θ, δ, and M as object-specific revisable audit variables rather than universal fixed values.

## Validity conditions
- Do not reduce learning to knowledge accumulation.
- Use knowledge as a structural observation sensor.
- Distinguish outcomes from the structures that generate them.
- Separate chance, luck, emotion, and cognitive bias from the causal structure being inferred.
- Integrate observations into the internal OS.

## Failure conditions
- Measuring learning only by books read, courses taken, or information stored.
- Copying a successful method without identifying its generating structure.
- Reducing failure to emotion, willpower, or personal attributes alone.
- Increasing knowledge without changing recognition, judgment, action, or procedure.
- Increasing trial count without structural observation.
- Ending rectification while distortion remains.
- Turning active waiting into abandonment.
- Treating a Future Line as prophecy or a fixed future.
- Claiming higher emergence precision only because of a short-term favorable result.

## Falsification conditions
The structural hypothesis is revisable. If the observed causal structure is integrated into the internal OS and distortion is rectified, yet the same friction, asymmetry, or recurrence continues across observation window δ and emergence-precision indicators do not move in the expected direction relative to object-specific threshold θ, hypothesis A or the observed structural model should be revised. A favorable short-term result should not be fixed as successful learning when its structural explanation is not reproducible and chance dominates. θ, δ, and phenomenon M are revisable audit variables from the source, not universal guarantees.
