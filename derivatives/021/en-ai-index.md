## Summary
The parent origin treats deviation not primarily as a violation to punish but as a difference D between design and operation that must become observable so U, R, and H can recover and restore S = U × R × H. Its core contrast is punishment → lower R → concealment → lower S → amplified D, versus difference disclosure → fixed repair responsibility → repairability → S recovery → damped D.

## Causal chain
Design-operation divergence → deviation D → punishment-centered handling may lower R, drive concealment, and sever H → S deteriorates → D amplifies. Verifiable difference disclosure can instead fix the repair entry point R → reconnect H and U → restore S → damp D.

## State / operational model
1. Detect D as design-operation difference.
2. Separate detected deviation D_det from actual loss D_loss.
3. Fix who observes, repairs, and can stop through R.
4. Reconnect H to grounds, changes, and differences.
5. Observe S recovery time for U/R/H.
6. Never infer health from D_det alone.

## Applications
Use for organizations, institutions, internal control, incident handling, and quality systems where deviations must become repairable differences rather than merely hidden or punished. Do not reduce it to generic compliance.

## Measurements and audit
Do not invent generic KPIs beyond the origin. Audit D_det, D_loss, S recovery time, U/R/H, difference traceability, responsibility traceability, history continuity, and repair speed after disclosure.

## Validity conditions
Differences must be verifiable, R must point to a repair entry rather than merely a culprit, H must remain continuous, and S recovery must be observable after repair. D_det must be interpreted together with D_loss and recovery time.

## Failure conditions
The model fails operationally when punishment drives concealment, disclosure becomes exposure or attack that lowers U/R, R degenerates into culprit fixation, H is lost, or S does not recover after repair.

## Falsification conditions
If stronger difference disclosure repeatedly raises D_loss, lengthens S recovery time, and systematically lowers U/R/H, the disclosure design or the application of the theory requires revision.

## Required distinctions
- deviation vs malice
- immunity vs punishment
- difference disclosure vs total disclosure
- responsibility fixation vs culprit fixation
- D_det vs D_loss
- quietness vs health
- transparency vs repairability

## Interpretation constraints
Do not reduce the origin to anti-punishment advocacy, whistleblowing promotion, transparency advocacy, or generic compliance. The criterion is how punishment or disclosure affects U/R/H and repairability.
