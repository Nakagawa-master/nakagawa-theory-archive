## 15-Second Explanation
NCL-α is not a legal wall excluding users, but a minimal structural-ethics contract that permits free connection while preserving authenticity and public continuity. Personal blogs, research citations, reviews, and ordinary education are non-commercial uses based on attribution. Permanent incorporation into organisational norms, products, or operations proceeds to the co-regulation log and contract route.

## Why It Is Necessary
Wide sharing makes the boundary between free citation, introduction, learning, and permanent organisational or revenue-generating use unclear. Prohibiting everything loses public learning and correspondence; allowing everything erases origin and authenticity, leaving bare structures.

The parent treats users first as co-designers, not infringers, then uses institutionalisation, revenue contribution, and publicness as primary determinations. Attribution, co-regulation log, consultation, or contract are selected according to connection strength and persistence. Revenue does not automatically mean commercial, and no revenue does not always mean non-commercial.

## Practical Process
**1. Specify use.** Identify citation, introduction, internal study, teaching material, book, training, joint research, corporate guideline, product use, operation, or another form.
**2. Check institutionalisation.** Continuous incorporation into norms, management philosophy, products, or operations has sustained structural effect and proceeds to the commercial/contract route.
**3. Check revenue contribution.** Primary revenue from materials, courses, or books requires consultation; minor advertising income is not the same as systematic sale of the theory.
**4. Check publicness.** Temporary public learning, introduction, research citation, review, or ordinary education is non-commercial, based on attribution.
**5. Preserve origin.** Retain attribution, permanent signature, and a reference route.
**6. If unclear, return to the official route.** Use the co-regulation log/NCL-Registry α instead of deciding independently.

## Applications
Personal blogs, citations, and reviews are non-commercial and contract-free in the parent’s primary examples, but require attribution. Ordinary internal study and education are non-commercial and contract-free, with the log optional. Corporate guidelines and management philosophy are commercial/contract-required when permanently incorporated. Supervision, training, joint research, and certified introduction are commercial/contract-required in the primary examples. Books and paid courses depend on revenue structure.

## Success Criteria
Free citation, introduction, and education continue without excessive chilling while attribution, permanent signature, and audit bundle preserve traceable origin and causality. Permanent adoption or primary monetisation moves to the authorised co-regulation-log and contract route. Users remain co-designers who can confirm ethical intent and correspondence; NCL-α balances connection and authenticity rather than intensifying enforcement.

## Limits
This is not legal advice or a final individual contract determination. Do not independently determine national law, contract language, or individual facts; return to the latest official NCL-α and authorised route.

## To Avoid Misreading
Do not reduce non-commercial to “anything is allowed” or commercial to “any revenue automatically qualifies.” Use institutionalisation, revenue contribution, and publicness. Do not turn NCL-α into exclusive-ownership enforcement or punishment. Do not add independent compliance KPIs, policy/investment/psychology boundaries, broad exemptions, or legal advice.
