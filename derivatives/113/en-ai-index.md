## Summary

NCL-α is not a wall of legal exclusion. It is a minimal structural-ethics contract designed to preserve structural authenticity and public continuity while allowing free connection. The parent does not treat every user as an infringer by default; it starts from the user as a co-designer and selects attribution, co-resonance logging, consultation, or contract according to the structural strength of the use. Personal blogs, research quotation, commentary, and ordinary educational use are source examples of noncommercial use centered on attribution. Durable incorporation into organizational rules, products, or operations, major revenue contribution, supervision, training, joint research, or certified implementation moves toward consultation and contract.

The primary judgment axes are institutionalization, revenue contribution, and public character rather than the mere presence or absence of revenue. Permanent Signature and the Audit Bundle preserve origin, causality, and consistency, while resonance-based binding expresses an ethical duty of connection prior to legal enforcement. Ambiguous cases return to the co-resonance log and NCL-Registry α instead of being decided through independently created legal advice or generic compliance rules.

## Applications

- allow personal blogs, research quotation, and commentary to circulate with attribution without imposing a contract on every reference
- treat ordinary internal study and educational use as noncommercial when durable institutionalization or major monetization is absent
- identify incorporation into corporate project guidelines or management philosophy as institutionalization and route it toward contract
- route supervision, training, joint research, and certified implementation through the commercial contract path
- distinguish incidental revenue around a publication or course from systematic monetization of the protected structure and consult when needed
