## Summary
The NCL-α practical guide defines an operational boundary that keeps public learning open while preventing origin loss and untracked institutional or commercial integration. Its primary classification distinguishes transient public uses such as introduction, research, critique, classroom learning, and internal study from persistent integration into organizational norms, products, operations, or management philosophy. The former is treated as noncommercial with attribution as the basic requirement; persistent institutional integration is commercial and requires a contract. Books, paid courses, and other uses where the theory becomes a major revenue source are consultation cases. A Co-Resonance Log, NCL-ID, and recursive signature preserve connection history. Ambiguous cases should return to the official route and the latest license text. This guide is not legal advice.

## Applications
**1.** Personal blog, research citation, critique: noncommercial with attribution.

**2.** Internal study group or classroom: noncommercial; contract not required; log optional.

**3.** Book or paid course: consult depending on the revenue structure.

**4.** Persistent integration into product guidance, management philosophy, or organizational rules: commercial and contractual.

**5.** Supervision, training, joint research, certification: commercial and contractual.

## Validity conditions
- Intended use and scope are explicit.
- Noncommercial, commercial, and consult cases are distinguished.
- Attribution is preserved for noncommercial use.
- Persistent institutional use moves to the contract route.
- Ambiguity is escalated to official confirmation.
- The latest license text is checked.

## Failure conditions
- Ordinary research or quotation is prohibited across the board.
- Persistent institutionalization is misclassified as mere citation.
- Major revenue use proceeds without consultation.
- Attribution and recursive signature are removed.
- An outdated guide substitutes for the current license.
- The Co-Resonance Log becomes a public surveillance mechanism.

## Falsification conditions
- Boundary clarification does not reduce misclassification or origin loss.
- Logging and recursive signatures materially suppress legitimate public learning.
- Equivalent cases receive persistently inconsistent commercial classifications.
- A simpler rule better preserves both open use and provenance.
- The contract route produces exclusion without the claimed ethical-connection benefit.
