## Summary
The parent origin is not a theory of future prediction. It audits the future definition itself. A future definition becomes an auditable structural operation only when it specifies which present causal line it acts on, can be executed, produces observable intermediate and final states, can be corrected when deviations appear, and allows a third party to trace definition, action, result, and correction.

A statement may sound coherent and still be false rectification when its action on present causality cannot be inspected, its results cannot be observed, or its deviations cannot be corrected. The origin therefore separates auditable rectification from false rectification by structural traceability rather than rhetorical force.

A publicly operable structural boundary does not mean publishing all internal information. It means exposing the minimum structure required for third-party inspection of the relationship among future definition, causal action, operation, result, deviation, and correction.

## Causal chain
state a future definition → specify which present causal line it changes → translate it into executable operation → observe intermediate states, outcomes, and deviations → compare the definition with reality → correct causal lines, methods, or conditions → retain a form in which third parties can trace definition, action, result, and correction → exclude non-auditable rectification claims as false rectification → establish the publicly operable structural boundary.

## State model
- **Future-definition state**: a future state is defined with a bounded meaning.
- **Explicit-action state**: the present causal point, judgment, sequence, or condition to be changed is specified.
- **Execution state**: the definition becomes concrete action, allocation, order, or condition.
- **Observation state**: intermediate states, outcomes, deviations, and breaks can be reviewed.
- **Correction state**: causal lines, methods, or conditions can be changed and re-entered.
- **Third-party audit state**: definition through result and correction can be traced by an external reader.
- **False-rectification state**: language claims rectification but action, observation, correction, or auditability is absent.
- **Public-boundary state**: the structure required for inspection is exposed without expanding disclosure to unrelated internal material.

These states are not derivative-authored scoring systems. Do not invent audit scores, rectification rates, false-rectification rates, success probabilities, disclosure scores, or fixed thresholds absent from the parent origin.

## Applications
For strategy, projects, AI-assisted planning, and public explanations, evaluate not whether a future vision or detailed plan exists but whether present causal action, execution, observation, correction, and third-party inspectability are connected.

Do not replace the origin with future prediction, manifestation, self-help, generic KPI management, or a doctrine requiring full disclosure of confidential or personal information.

## Measurements and audit
Observe whether the future definition is bounded, the present causal action point is explicit, operation is executable, intermediate and final states are observable, deviations are detectable, correction is possible, third parties can trace definition-action-result-correction, and disclosure is limited to the structure required for inspection.

Preserve reversible evaluation. More planning detail does not improve auditability when causal action remains unclear. More KPIs can conceal false rectification when they are weakly connected to the future definition. Longer explanations or larger disclosure volumes can reduce inspectability when the action-result-correction path becomes harder to find.

## Validity conditions
Define the future state with a bounded meaning and specify which present causal relation it acts on. Convert that definition into executable operation, make intermediate states and outcomes observable, detect deviations, and keep causal lines, methods, and conditions correctable.

Third parties must be able to trace definition, action, result, and correction. Non-auditable statements must not be protected as rectification, and the public boundary must remain limited to the structure needed for inspection.

## Failure conditions
The reading fails if reduced to future prediction, wish fulfillment, manifestation, self-help, success doctrine, strategy slogans, or generic KPI management.

It also fails if detailed planning alone is treated as auditability, KPI count or reporting volume is used as a proxy for rectification quality, the causal action point is missing, outcomes cannot be observed, correction paths do not exist, authority substitutes for third-party inspection, or full internal disclosure is confused with public operability.

## Falsification conditions
If specifying present causal action and establishing execution, observation, correction, and third-party auditability does not repeatedly improve reproducibility, corrigibility, or explainability of operation, the scope of auditability as a condition of rectification should be reconsidered.

Conversely, if equal or better reproducibility, correction speed, and third-party explainability remain stable even when action points and observation / correction paths are largely unspecified, the necessary public structural boundary may be narrower than this model assumes. A single success or failure does not prove or falsify the structure.
