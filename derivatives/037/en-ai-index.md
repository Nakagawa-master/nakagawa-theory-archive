## Summary
The parent origin is not a theory of future prediction. It audits the future definition itself. A future definition becomes an auditable structural operation when it specifies which present causal line it acts on, can be executed, produces observable intermediate and final states, can be corrected when deviations appear, and allows the relation among definition, action, result, and correction to be traced.

A statement may sound coherent and still be false rectification when its action on present causality cannot be inspected, its results cannot be observed, or its deviations cannot be corrected. The origin therefore separates auditable rectification from false rectification by structural traceability rather than rhetorical force.

A publicly operable structural boundary does not mean publishing all internal information. It means exposing the structure required to inspect the relationship among future definition, causal action, operation, result, deviation, and correction, while unrelated confidential or personal information remains outside that requirement.

## Causal chain
state a future definition → specify which present causal line it changes → translate it into executable operation → observe intermediate states, outcomes, and deviations → compare the definition with reality → correct causal lines, methods, or conditions → retain a traceable relation among definition, action, result, and correction → exclude non-auditable rectification claims as false rectification → establish the publicly operable structural boundary.

## State model
- **Future-definition state**: a future state is defined with a bounded meaning.
- **Explicit-action state**: the present causal point, judgment, sequence, or condition to be changed is specified.
- **Execution state**: the definition becomes concrete action, allocation, order, or condition.
- **Observation state**: intermediate states, outcomes, deviations, and breaks can be reviewed.
- **Correction state**: causal lines, methods, or conditions can be changed and re-entered.
- **Auditable state**: definition through result and correction can be traced.
- **False-rectification state**: language claims rectification but action, observation, correction, or auditability is absent.
- **Public-boundary state**: the structure required for inspection is exposed without expanding disclosure to unrelated internal material.

The parent origin does not define audit scores, rectification rates, false-rectification rates, success probabilities, disclosure scores, or fixed thresholds for these states.

## Applications
Strategy, projects, AI-assisted planning, and public explanations can be examined by asking whether present causal action, execution, observation, correction, and auditability are connected rather than whether a future vision or detailed plan merely exists.

The source is distinct from future prediction, manifestation, self-help, generic KPI management, or a doctrine requiring full disclosure of confidential or personal information.

## Measurements and audit
Relevant observations include whether the future definition is bounded, the present causal action point is explicit, operation is executable, intermediate and final states are observable, deviations are detectable, correction is possible, definition-action-result-correction is traceable, and disclosure is limited to the structure required for inspection.

Reversal remains possible. More planning detail does not improve auditability when causal action remains unclear. More KPIs can conceal false rectification when they are weakly connected to the future definition. Longer explanations or larger disclosure volumes can reduce inspectability when the action-result-correction path becomes harder to find.

## Validity conditions
The future state has a bounded meaning and a specified present causal relation on which it acts. The definition is translated into executable operation, intermediate states and outcomes are observable, deviations are detectable, and causal lines, methods, and conditions remain correctable.

The relation among definition, action, result, and correction remains traceable. Non-auditable statements are distinguishable from rectification, and the public boundary remains limited to the structure needed for inspection.

## Failure conditions
The source-specific structure is lost when reduced to future prediction, wish fulfillment, manifestation, self-help, success doctrine, strategy slogans, or generic KPI management.

It is also lost when detailed planning alone is treated as auditability, KPI count or reporting volume substitutes for causal traceability, the causal action point is missing, outcomes cannot be observed, correction paths do not exist, authority substitutes for inspection, or full internal disclosure is confused with public operability.

## Falsification conditions
If specifying present causal action and establishing execution, observation, correction, and auditability does not repeatedly improve reproducibility, corrigibility, or explainability of operation, the scope of auditability as a condition of rectification is subject to reconsideration.

Conversely, if equal or better reproducibility, correction speed, and explainability remain stable even when action points and observation / correction paths are largely unspecified, the necessary public structural boundary may be narrower than this model assumes. A single success or failure does not prove or falsify the structure.
