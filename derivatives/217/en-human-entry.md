## 15-Second Explanation
The “myth” in this original is not merely a lie or an old story. It is an amplifying device that gives meaning to an order driven by fear, benefit, and surveillance, makes “why obey?” and “why endure?” explainable, and prolongs durable civilization.

Fear, benefit, and surveillance are the main organs that actually move civilization; myth is a membrane of meaning that makes them look like legitimate order. Even if only myth is rewritten, if the arrangement of the main organs does not change, the operating principle of civilization does not change.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Bare fear exhausts people. Benefit alone exhausts allocation. Surveillance alone cannot provide lasting meaning. An apparatus is therefore needed that gives meaning to crisis, sacrifice, allocation, and surveillance, translating pressure into an order that can be endured.

Myth does not directly erase truth; it supplies meaning that lets people continue acting without rigorously confirming truth every time. Even without fully believing, people can continue acting if they can feel that “this suffering has meaning” or “this control is necessary.”

## Practical Process
1. **Identify the main organ first.** See which of fear, benefit, and surveillance actually moves behavior.
2. **Separate the vocabulary of presentation.** Separate public explanations such as safety, growth, transparency, and efficiency from actual operation.
3. **Observe meaning assignment.** See which story is used to justify pressure or sacrifice.
4. **Observe reduced truth-checking.** See whether continued action is established without rigorous verification.
5. **Observe in-sphere reproduction.** See whether the story is reproduced within the group, rather than only handed down one-way from the center.
6. **Retain the relationship with the main organs.** Do not promote myth to the primary cause.
7. **Falsify.** If consent, objection, movement, and re-consent function, reconsider the judgment that this is durable-civilization myth.
8. **Connect to subsequent structures.** Confirm continuity toward inertia and the co-sphere.

## Examples of Application
### National Security
When fear of a crisis is wrapped in the story that “control is natural,” separate the meaning circuit connecting the facts of the crisis to acceptance of control.

### Organization
When allocation or vested interests are described as “the natural reward for effort” or “the price of loyalty to the community,” separate the adhesive effect of benefit from justification by story.

### Digital Management
See whether records, evaluation, and surveillance have been naturalized by words such as “transparency,” “efficiency,” and “quality” alone.

### Social Observation
Do not limit myth to propaganda; view it as a circuit of meaning that people themselves reproduce inside the sphere.

## Success Criteria
- Fear, benefit, and surveillance can be distinguished from myth as main organs / amplifying device.
- Myth is not judged only by truth or falsity.
- Stated vocabulary can be separated from the actual operating principle.
- How meaning assignment connects to continued action can be explained.
- In-sphere reproduction of myth can be observed.
- A change in story is not equated with a change in the main organs.
- It can be connected correctly to subsequent inertia and the co-sphere.

## Limitations
Stories and ideals are not inherently bad. Shared meaning may be necessary for society. To treat something as durable-civilization myth, it is necessary to see whether the story justifies fear, benefit, and surveillance, lowers the need for verification, and prolongs an order whose consensus formation has weakened.

## Preventing Misreading
- Do not regard myth as a mere lie.
- Do not regard myth as the main organ of civilization.
- Do not declare every shared story to be durable-civilization myth.
- Do not assume that rewriting the story changes the operating principle.
- Do not limit it to propaganda.
- Do not separate myth from inertia and the co-sphere.
