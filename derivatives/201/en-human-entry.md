## 15-Second Explanation
Real Estate Market OS Vol.4 is a responsibility design that places “who assumes responsibility and how far” on the same playing field, rather than sending AI-calculated figures directly into society. AI handles collection, formatting, primary calculations, and comparison, while experts guarantee responsibility domains such as law, taxation, registration, buildings, and finance. Outputs are divided into General / Estimate / Conditional, and uncertainties and exceptions are escalated to people with context. By building trust through responsibility boundaries and audits rather than goodwill, it reduces rework and conflict and raises market speed.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
AI can produce figures quickly, but AI itself cannot assume the consequences of people acting on those figures. Moreover, real estate involves taxes, registration, finance, daily life, inheritance, family relationships, and more; when an estimate that skipped its premises walks around as a “definitive answer,” misuse and disputes over responsibility arise before a mere calculation error does.

The original treats the main reason society rejects it not as insufficient AI performance, but as a gap in responsibility. Even if calculation is fast, users, experts, finance, and government find it difficult to participate if it is unclear who to return to when an accident occurs, what remains an estimate, and what must be handed to an expert. Vol.4 therefore maximizes AI’s areas of strength while clearly connecting responsibility to humans and institutional actors.

## Practical Process
1. **Fix AI’s area of responsibility.** Limit it to information collection, formatting, primary calculations, pattern detection, and visualization of condition differences.
2. **Fix AI’s prohibited area.** Do not let it move into final decision orders, individualized legal judgments, acts resembling exclusive professional work, or unconditional guarantees.
3. **Display output strength.** Distinguish General / Estimate / Conditional.
4. **Attach uncertainty to Estimates.** Display the margin of error, sensitivity, and premises.
5. **Attach recalculation conditions to Conditionals.** Show which premise would change the result if it broke down.
6. **Divide responsibility domains.** Connect taxation, registration, boundaries, buildings, brokerage, management, finance, and so on to responsible actors.
7. **Place experts as Guarantors.** They assume the authenticity, explainability, and updating responsibility of their assigned domain, rather than serving as advertising signboards.
8. **Divide errors into four layers.** Separate Input / Model / Interpretation / Operational Error.
9. **Escalate what is unconfirmed.** Pass to a person what has not been confirmed and which responsibility domain it belongs to, while retaining context.
10. **Set finance and government logic alongside it.** Make the rationality of the Market OS and the decisions of finance and institutions comparable on the same premises.
11. **Audit.** Continuously check assignments, updates, logic changes, accident rates, explanation-failure rates, deviation rates, and so on.
12. **Revise.** Adjust boundaries, allocation, and pathways according to Condition Z / T-S-R / θ / δ / Phenomenon M.

## Examples of Application
**AI cash-flow simulation.** When presenting a 10-year holding result, do not simply output “profit ¥○.” Display the premises, margin of error, sensitivity to interest rates, vacancy, and repairs, unconfirmed items, and required expert checks at the same time as an Estimate.

**Inheritance and taxation.** AI can organize the existence of systems, comparison materials, and required documents, but does not say, “For this case, this tax treatment is confirmed.” Individual judgments are handed to a tax accountant or other expert.

**Boundaries and buildings.** Do not fill unresolved boundaries, rebuilding conditions, building deterioration, and similar matters with a low-precision AI coefficient; connect them as matters requiring investigation to a land and house investigator, architect, or other expert.

**Financing.** Something may be rational in terms of future cash flow but fail a financial institution’s credit and repayment-resilience standards. Do not confuse “can buy” with “can lend”; place them on the same playing field.

**When an accident occurs.** When a figure is wrong, repair the system by distinguishing whether the source data was wrong, the model missed, the user misread an estimate as a certainty, or escalation was omitted.

## Success Criteria
Speed of AI output alone is not success. Users must understand the strength and limits of the output, see who assumes which domain, see unconfirmed matters handed to people, and be able to separate the causal layer and responsible actor when an accident occurs.

As a result, hesitation in decision-making, rework, inability to explain, backlash, and disputes over responsibility decrease, and experts, finance, and government become able to participate. The original understands trust not as a personal virtue but as a structure that removes uncertainty, and says that this connects to market speed and lower costs.

## Limitations
- Do not extend the structure that AI cannot assume responsibility into a rejection of AI use itself.
- Expert connection is not a design that simply “throws everything to an expert.”
- A Guarantor is not an unconditional guarantor of every output, but the party assuming the explicitly assigned domain.
- Displaying General / Estimate / Conditional alone does not complete responsibility design. Assignments, updates, escalation, and audits are necessary.
- “Can buy” and “can lend” are not meant to be made identical values.
- Private AI does not replace the coercive force of government and legal systems or state guarantees.
- Fixed θ values and δ periods are not presented as universal values.

## Preventing Misreading
Do not read “distributed responsibility” as evasion of responsibility. The original’s purpose is to eliminate gaps in responsibility among AI, experts, users, finance, and institutional actors, and to make the assignments finely divided and traceable.

Also, do not equate sponsor experts with advertisers. What the original values is not exposure but authenticity, explainability, updating responsibility, and participation in audits within the assigned domain. A structure that bends figures for sponsor convenience is, rather, subject to multi-party audits and prevention of pressure to tamper.
