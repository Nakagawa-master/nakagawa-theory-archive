## Summary
A successful institution tends to reduce references as repetition and proficiency increase. This lowers consensus cost C and accelerates operation, but it also compresses reasons, assumptions, exceptions, and differences. Third-party reproducibility U then declines and can pull responsibility traceability R and history openness H downward. The institution can remain internally fast and stable while becoming externally irreproducible. Judgment may shift from reasons to inference, trust, and authority. The recovery operation is explanation updating: re-expanding difference-bearing reasons rather than merely adding procedures.

## Concepts
- Meaning Compression: omission of reasons, assumptions, exceptions, and differences while surface procedures remain.
- n: operation count.
- C: consensus cost.
- U: third-party reproducibility / understandability.
- R: responsibility traceability.
- H: history and difference traceability.
- S = U × R × H: consensus stability.
- Reference / Decision / Difference / Responsibility Bundles: the history structure that constitutes an institution.
- Explanation Update: re-expansion of purpose, exceptions, assumptions, reasons, and references as tracked differences.

## Causal chain
Repeated operation → proficiency and reference reduction → C decreases → reasons are omitted → meaning compression → U decreases → R/H deteriorate → internal stability coexists with external irreproducibility → understanding shifts to inference, trust, and authority → S decreases and institutional reproducible lifetime shortens. Explanation updating is the counter-operation.

## Applications
Apply to approvals, reviews, hiring, audits, complaint handling, handovers, and AI automation. Do not evaluate only throughput. Test whether outsiders can reconstruct reasons, exceptions, differences, and responsibility nodes, especially in long-running institutions that appear stable.

## Measurements and audit
- Ask the same question: “Why does this rule exist?”
- Classify answers: A=conditional reason, B=custom, C=common sense/conformity, D=atmosphere/authority/refusal.
- Record agreement among reason answers as a practical proxy for U.
- Test whether a third party can reproduce common, occasional, and rare exceptions.
- Count explanation updates within observation window δ.
- Observe C and U/R/H together over time.
- Monitor S<θ, disappearance of difference-bearing reasons, and unidentified responsibility nodes.

## Validity conditions
Treat the institution as decision history rather than only a current document. Preserve reference, decision, difference, and responsibility paths. Measure efficiency separately from reproducibility and distinguish explanation updates from mere procedure growth.

## Failure conditions
The design fails when longevity is treated as proof of health, proficiency licenses reason omission, latest-version integration destroys difference history, more manuals are mistaken for restoration of meaning, or authority criticism substitutes for repairing reference paths.

## Falsification conditions
If n rises and C falls while U/R/H remain durable for long periods without explanation updates, the claimed structural necessity of meaning compression or its measurement definitions require revision. If S remains below θ, H loses difference-bearing reasons, R loses identifiable decision nodes, or explanation updating stops for δ, additional design constraints and difference publication are required. θ and δ are not universal fixed numbers.

## Required distinctions
Institutional persistence vs reproducibility; efficiency vs health; proficiency vs reference reduction; procedure addition vs explanation update; internal stability vs external reproducibility; trust vs reason-reference; latest version vs difference history; C decrease vs U decrease.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not read this as a moral rejection of efficiency, proficiency, or authority. It is not a demand to maximize explanatory text. The target is a reproducible reference path through reasons, assumptions, exceptions, differences, and responsibility, plus continued updates when that path changes.
