## Summary

Nakagawa-Style Marketing redefines marketing from a bundle of acquisition or harvesting tactics into a waterway-design operating system. The parent original assumes that markets already contain flows of dissatisfaction, expectation, latent discomfort, and desire. The design task is not to manufacture or forcibly bend demand, but to identify those flows and arrange low-resistance routes by which fitting people can encounter the brand or offering. Spontaneity is central: the recipient should be able to experience discovery as self-initiated rather than imposed. Quiet resonance triggers can surface an unarticulated gap and initiate exploration without converting attention capture into coercion.

A second requirement is vertical meaning alignment from L7, the worldview and value-function layer, through to L1, the concrete function, offering, price, and purchase layer. The entrance is not optimized for zero friction. Appropriate friction allows a light first step, increasing depth, and natural exit for non-fit cases. Structural filtering is based on fit conditions of the offered value, not on judgments of personality or attributes. Cognitive-load control means aligning attention, decision criteria, and self-relevance rather than merely deleting information. Marketing therefore prepares market-scale fit before the Sales OS handles individual dialogue.

Evaluation is not closed by traffic or short-term conversion. The parent audit names spontaneous inflow rate, long-term engagement, and market-trust indicators as case-bound measures, together with condition Z, Transparency/Safety/Reciprocity, case-specific theta, observation window delta, and phenomenon M. No universal values are supplied. Measurement requires a defined actor, object, period, method, permitted scope, and non-guarantee boundary. If harvesting, misleading design, unnecessary information load, non-fit inflow, or trust deterioration appears within the observation window, a short-term numerical gain can be reversed in evaluation and connection hypothesis A is revised to A-prime. ## Concepts

- Waterway design: arranging low-resistance routes for pre-existing desire flows
- Spontaneity: preserving the recipient's sense of self-discovery and self-selection
- Unarticulated gap: discomfort or expectation present before explicit demand language
- Quiet resonance trigger: a small point of recognition that starts exploration
- L7 value function: worldview, purpose, and the definition of what is considered good
- L1 purchase layer: concrete offering, function, price, and action
- Vertical meaning alignment: keeping L7-to-L1 meaning continuous
- Appropriate friction: neither zero friction nor needless obstruction
- Structural filtering: selection by fit conditions rather than personal worth
- Cognitive-load control: aligning attention and decision axes rather than simply reducing volume
- Market breathing: adjusting structure to market rhythm rather than controlling the ecosystem
- Natural convergence: fit-based arrival without external pressure
- T/S/R: Transparency, Safety, and Reciprocity
- theta/delta/M: case-bound revision threshold, observation window, and observed counter-phenomenon
- A to A-prime: revision of the market-connection hypothesis

## Applications

**1.** Design content entrances around latent discomfort before introducing product explanation. The entry should help the reader recognize an existing problem in their own context and then move through meaning, fit conditions, and concrete value without a coercive jump.

**2.** Connect brand purpose and value function to concrete features, price, and action in one meaning chain. A worldview that never reaches L1 remains abstract, while feature-only messaging loses the upstream reason the offering matters.

**3.** Use fit conditions in B2B entry pages so sales receives consultations with clearer expectation alignment. Conditions under which the value works well, does not fit, or should be replaced by another option remain visible before the handoff.

**4.** Evaluate ads, search, social contact points, and landing pages by the desire flow and route they serve rather than traffic volume alone. A contact point belongs to the waterway only when it preserves discovery agency and connects to the same meaning structure.

**5.** Reverse a positive short-term conversion judgment when misleading acquisition, non-fit demand, unnecessary information load, sales exhaustion, or trust deterioration increases. The downstream number does not override deterioration of the parent objective.

## Measurements and audit

- Distinguish pre-existing desire from campaign-created reaction, including the context in which the discomfort or expectation existed before contact.
- Observe preservation of self-directed discovery rather than treating apparent inbound behavior alone as proof of agency.
- Audit L7-to-L1 meaning continuity across worldview, value, offering, function, price, and next action.
- Assess appropriate rather than minimal friction; both needless obstruction and zero-friction loss of fit checking can fail.
- Assess offering-to-expectation fit and whether non-fit conditions are visible before the sales or service burden is created.
- Assess cognitive load through clarity of attention, decision axes, and self-relevance rather than through information volume alone.
- Treat spontaneous inflow rate as a case-bound parent indicator only when measurement actor, object, period, and definition are fixed.
- Treat long-term engagement as a case-bound longitudinal indicator separated from short-term contact volume.
- Treat market-trust indicators as method- and population-bound observations, not as a universal score.
- Apply reversal audit when traffic or conversion rises while harvesting, misleading design, unnecessary information load, non-fit demand, or trust loss also rises.

## Validity conditions

- Start from pre-existing desire rather than forced demand creation.
- Preserve self-directed discovery and meaningful choice.
- Maintain vertical L7-to-L1 meaning instead of worldview-only or feature-only messaging.
- Optimize friction rather than zeroing it.
- Filter by structural fit conditions rather than attributes or personal worth.
- Control cognitive load by aligning judgment axes, not merely deleting information.
- Connect Marketing OS and Sales OS as one pre/post flow.
- Keep metrics, theta, delta, and M case-bound and explicitly defined.

## Failure conditions

- Reduce marketing to traffic, media spend, campaign volume, or a tactic bundle.
- Manufacture demand through fear, coercion, concealed pressure, or misrecognition.
- Equate spontaneity with accidental inbound traffic or viral exposure.
- Disconnect worldview and value function from the concrete offering.
- Open the entrance indiscriminately and treat every retained visitor as a success.
- Turn structural filtering into attribute-based exclusion or judgments of personal worth.
- Treat information deletion alone as cognitive-load control.
- Declare success from short-term traffic, conversion, or sales while trust, fit, or long-term relation deteriorates.

## Falsification conditions

- Case-bound indicators repeatedly cross theta without reproducing the expected positive relation between the waterway design and the parent objective.
- Harvesting, misleading design, unnecessary information load, or another defined phenomenon M appears structurally within observation window delta.
- Transparency, Safety, or Reciprocity fails and apparent spontaneity becomes practical manipulation, concealment, or one-sided extraction.
- Stronger L7-L1 alignment repeatedly worsens comprehension, fit, or continuation rather than improving the intended connection.
- Fit filtering repeatedly excludes needed users without improving actual fit, requiring revision of entrance and filtering assumptions.
- Across multiple contexts, coercive acquisition consistently dominates on the parent objective including trust, agency, fit, and long-term relation; in that case the application range must be limited or revised.

## Required distinctions

- pre-existing desire ≠ fabricated demand
- waterway design ≠ tactic bundle
- self-discovery ≠ accidental inbound
- vertical meaning ≠ worldview-only or feature-only messaging
- appropriate friction ≠ zero friction or needless complexity
- structural filtering ≠ attribute discrimination
- cognitive-load control ≠ information deletion
- natural convergence ≠ passivity
- traffic growth ≠ proof of structural validity
- Marketing OS ≠ an acquisition department isolated from Sales OS

## Interpretation constraints

- Market design refers to contact points, information placement, meaning connection, and entrance conditions; it does not authorize control over persons.
- Spontaneity cannot be converted into concealed psychological manipulation that merely makes imposed choices feel self-directed.
- Appropriate friction cannot be reinterpreted as artificial scarcity, inconvenience, or pressure for its own sake.
- Fit filtering cannot become a judgment of personality, status, or human worth.
- Case-bound audit variables cannot receive universal numerical values not supplied by the parent original.
- Short-term performance cannot be used to infer guaranteed business outcomes or a single-cause proof of this structure.
