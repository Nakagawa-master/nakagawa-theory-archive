## 15-Second Explanation
Nakagawa-style marketing is not a customer-acquisition technique that chases and gathers people. It is the design of terrain so that a current of desire already present in the market naturally joins the company through the path of least resistance. It arranges the recipient’s agency of “finding it for oneself,” the connection of meaning from the L7 worldview to concrete L1 value, appropriate entrance friction, structural selection, and cognitive-load control as one waterway.

## Why It Is Needed
The market contains unnamed unease and expectations, dissatisfaction in daily life, and desires to change something. The Parent Original does not see marketing as injecting desire from outside to move people. It first asks which flow is trying to go where.

When a sentence, concrete example, or slice of problem structure that makes the recipient feel “Yes, that is what I wanted to say” is placed against that flow, the recipient does not feel sold to. They discover the problem themselves and begin exploring because they want to know what comes next. This “I found it myself,” “I chose it myself,” and “I arrived here myself” is the first core of Nakagawa-style marketing. It starts a flow from small resonance rather than shaking the market with a loud voice.

Next comes the vertical line of meaning. A relationship connected only at L1 through product features or price is easily broken when a cheaper or more convenient option appears. Yet speaking only about ideals and worldview never descends to a concrete exchange of value. The Parent Original therefore connects L7—why the brand exists and what kind of world it considers good—to L1—what problem is solved, with what value, through which function, service, and price—as one consistent meaning.

The entrance need not be as broad as possible. The first step can be light, but as people proceed, understanding must deepen until they can judge that “this concerns me.” Conversely, it is better if people who are not a fit can choose another option early. Friction here is not staged inconvenience; it is a stage for understanding and fit confirmation. The goal is not zero friction but appropriate friction.

Structural selection is not an evaluation of people’s attributes or personalities. By first stating the range of value the company can provide, the conditions under which it works best, the expectations it cannot meet, and the conditions under which another option is suitable, it creates a state in which only fitting people naturally remain. This aligns expectations and delivery scope during marketing and improves the fit of consultations reaching the sales OS. Marketing and sales are not separate departments, but one continuous flow from dialogue with the market to dialogue with the individual.

The Parent Original also does not equate cognitive load with the amount of information. The focus is not merely shortening text, but aligning the axes of judgment: what to notice, what to decide, and where to determine whether it concerns oneself. Hiding necessary information to make things appear simple is not cognitive-load control.

Evaluation does not end with traffic volume or short-term conversion. The integrated audit places voluntary inflow rate, long-term engagement, and market trust indicators among the candidates, but gives no universal fixed values. They have meaning only when subject, measuring party, period, and definition are fixed. Confirm alignment with condition Z and Transparency / Safety / Reciprocity. If subject-specific θ, observation window δ, or phenomena M such as harvesting, induced misunderstanding, or unnecessary information load appear, reverse the success evaluation even when short-term numbers are good, and revise connection hypothesis A to A'.

## Practical Process
**1. Read the desire flow.** Extract recurring undefined dissatisfaction, expectation, and reasons for exploration from search terms, inquiries, conversations, reasons for leaving, and unease existing customers had before adoption. Separate reactions created by a campaign from context that existed before it.

**2. Place a small resonance point.** Put at the entrance a sentence, concrete example, or structural slice that lets the person recognize “I felt that unease” or “That is what I wanted to say,” rather than using a strong stimulus to seize attention.

**3. Design the order of self-directed discovery.** Do not jump immediately to the product. Create an order from self-recognition of the problem to structural understanding, further exploration, and comparison of options, leaving room for the person to recognize that they arrived on their own.

**4. Connect L7 to L1.** Link reason for existence, value function, the target’s problem, delivered value, function, price, conditions, and next action through one line of meaning; do not split them into ideals only or functions only.

**5. Make entrance friction appropriate.** Reduce complexity unnecessary for understanding while keeping explanations and conditions needed for fit judgment. Make the first step light and the structure deeper as people proceed.

**6. State fit conditions.** Clarify the people, situations, and expectations for which the delivered value works well, as well as conditions that do not fit. Use structural fit, not personality or attributes, as the criterion.

**7. Align judgment axes.** Do not merely remove information. Organize what to notice, what to compare, and under which conditions to proceed, thereby lowering cognitive load.

**8. Connect to the sales OS.** Ensure that the unease, expectations, value criteria, and fit conditions organized during marketing are carried into individual dialogue.

**9. Keep reversal evaluation.** Even if inflow, conversion, or contact volume increases, check at the same time for misunderstanding, mismatch, harvesting, unnecessary information load, declining trust, and sales exhaustion.

**10. Revise the connection hypothesis.** Preserve condition Z and T/S/R. If observation of subject-specific θ, δ, and M shows that the waterway is not connected to the Parent Original’s purpose, revise A to A'.

## Examples of Application
**Content design.** Rather than listing product explanations first, place at the entrance an unease the target person has not yet been able to verbalize. Do not exaggerate the problem at the start of an article or video; show a concrete structure that lets the person compare it with their own experience, then connect it to delivered value.

**Brand site.** Show in one continuous line from L7 to L1 why the brand exists, what it values about a problem structure, and how that descends to concrete value, rather than presenting only an abstract “who we are” or only a feature and price list.

**B2B lead generation.** Before increasing meetings, articulate at the entrance the undefined operational unease of target companies, the conditions under which adoption works, and the conditions that do not fit. Since expectations are organized before sales receives the lead, do not make volume growth and declining fit the same success metric.

**Advertising and SEO.** Do not make clicks or ranking the sole purpose. Also examine which desire flow receives which contact point, whether contact leads to self-directed exploration, and whether short-term acquisition is increasing misunderstanding or excessive stimulation.

**Service sign-up.** Do not delete every input field or explanation; retain friction corresponding to necessary understanding and fit confirmation. If drop-off falls but mismatched sign-ups or cancellations rise, reverse the evaluation.

## Success Criteria
The first condition is that the recipient proceeds to the next action from their own problem recognition and exploration rather than external pressure, with self-directed discovery preserved. It is not enough for them simply to say “I chose it myself”; check that decision materials and options were retained and that misunderstanding or coercion was not the main cause.

The L7 worldview and L1 concrete value must be consistent, and entrance friction, fit conditions, and judgment axes must connect with neither excess nor deficiency. Strong identification with ideals is incomplete if it never reaches concrete value; strong understanding of functions is another incomplete state if its connection to the worldview is broken.

Confirm that consultations reaching sales and support have better fit, without increasing misunderstanding, harvesting, unnecessary load, or loss of trust in exchange for short-term inflow. If inflow volume improves while the original purpose worsens, do not call it success based on quantity alone.

Observe subject-specific voluntary inflow, long-term relationships, and market trust. Reproducible improvement within the range satisfying T/S/R and reversal conditions is an audit condition. Since the Parent Original sets no fixed success rate or threshold, specify subject, measuring party, period, and definition for every indicator.

## Limitations
The Parent Original does not define one identical waterway, friction, selection condition, or indicator value for every market. Desire flow, fit conditions, θ, δ, M, and audit indicators must be defined for each market, subject, period, and measurement method.

Structural design is not a result guarantee. Market change, competitors, price, delivery quality, capacity, regulation, and the economy also affect outcomes. Do not infer sales or long-term relationships as a sole causal result of waterway design.

## Preventing Misreading
“Gathering naturally” does not mean doing nothing. It involves active design of desire-flow observation, resonance points, meaning lines, entrances, selection, and cognitive load.

“Spontaneity” is not hidden psychological manipulation. Making people feel spontaneous by hiding information, exaggerating fear, or narrowing options is induced misunderstanding and a target for falsification.

“Selection” means stating fit conditions between delivered value and expectations, not evaluating human attributes.

“Appropriate friction” means retaining stages necessary for understanding and fit confirmation, not staging inconvenience to manufacture scarcity.

Do not treat inflow volume or short-term conversion alone as success; retain reversal evaluation for deterioration in agency, fit, trust, or long-term relationships.
