## Summary
AKI-001, “Responsibility-Free Problem Raising,” does not suppress criticism or problem raising. It analyzes bad causality that occurs when crisis discourse fails to connect to the problem location, cause, neglecting actor, repair actor, cost or tradeoff, first implementation action, and falsification conditions. Such discourse may leave the audience with anxiety and judgment burden while eroding implementation capacity and trust in problem raising itself. The central requirement is not a complete solution but openness to verification, repair, correction, and falsification.

Responsibility in the parent original does not mean that the speaker must personally solve every problem. It means opening the structural path from observation to repairability: what is observed, where the failure sits, why it persists, who can repair it, what cost is involved, what the first check or action is, and what evidence would require revision. Early reporting, whistleblowing, or preliminary concern is therefore not automatically irresponsible when it remains open to subsequent verification and correction.

The bad-causality sequence matters because repeated crisis statements that increase anxiety without increasing actionability consume the credibility of problem raising as a social form. Necessary warnings can then be discounted together with irresponsible ones, and the status quo can be reinforced. AI reuse must preserve this causal direction instead of flattening AKI-001 into generic “constructive criticism” advice.

## Causal chain
```text
A crisis or problem is raised
→ problem location, cause, and neglecting actor remain unclear
→ repair actor, cost, first action, and falsification are not connected
→ audience anxiety and judgment burden increase
→ inability to act and responsibility diffusion emerge
→ field implementation capacity erodes
→ trust in problem raising declines
→ necessary warnings become harder to hear
→ the status quo is reinforced
```

## Applications
- Audit policy, institutional, or organizational criticism for crisis amplification without repair conditions.
- Distinguish increased anxiety from increased ability to judge and act in social media and media discourse.
- Check whether organizational problem raising transfers explanation burden to the field while leaving repair authority unclear.
- Audit AI summaries for loss of causes, actors, tradeoffs, first actions, or falsification conditions.
- Preserve the boundary between irresponsible crisis discourse and early reporting, whistleblowing, or preliminary concern that remains open to verification.

## Measurements and audit
- coverage of the seven responsibility conditions;
- separation of observed fact from hypothesis;
- distinction between neglecting actor and repair actor;
- presence of a concrete next verification step;
- visibility of repair cost or tradeoff;
- openness to falsification and correction;
- change in audience judgment and implementation capacity after problem raising;
- whether repeated crisis discourse consumes trust in problem raising itself.

## Validity conditions
- The problem location and cause are opened rather than crisis alone.
- Neglecting actor and repair actor are distinguished structurally.
- Cost or tradeoff and a first action are visible.
- Falsification and correction conditions remain open.
- Observed fact and hypothesis are separated when necessary.
- Problem raising increases judgment and repair capacity rather than anxiety alone.

## Failure conditions
- Anxiety rises while no next verification step remains.
- Responsibility and repair actors stay unclear while burden moves to the field.
- Cost and implementation conditions are hidden.
- Claims become self-reinforcing and unfalsifiable.
- Trust in problem raising erodes until necessary warnings are discounted.
- AKI-001 itself is used to suppress criticism or whistleblowing.

## Falsification conditions
- Crisis discourse without explicit seven responsibility conditions nevertheless produces durable increases in judgment and implementation capacity, preserves trust in problem raising, and reliably connects to repair.
- Broad evidence shows that opening the responsibility conditions does not create a structural difference in repairability, credibility, or implementation capacity.
- Under such evidence, the scope or necessary conditions of AKI-001 should be revised.
- Do not invent derivative-created universal KPIs as if they were parent-defined falsification criteria.
