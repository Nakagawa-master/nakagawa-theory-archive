## Summary
AKI-001, “Responsibility-Free Problem Raising,” does not suppress criticism or problem raising. It analyzes bad causality that occurs when crisis discourse fails to connect to the problem location, cause, neglecting actor, repair actor, cost or tradeoff, first implementation action, and falsification conditions. Such discourse may leave the audience with anxiety and judgment burden while eroding implementation capacity and trust in problem raising itself. The central requirement is not a complete solution but openness to verification, repair, and falsification.

## Applications
**1.** Audit policy, institutional, or organizational criticism for crisis amplification without repair conditions.

**2.** Distinguish increased anxiety from increased ability to act in social media, media, and AI-generated summaries.

**3.** Preserve the boundary between irresponsible crisis discourse and early reporting, whistleblowing, or preliminary concern that remains open to later verification.

## Measurements and audit
The parent defines no derivative-created numerical KPI. Audit centers on the seven responsibility conditions, separation of observed fact from hypothesis, audience judgment capacity, connection to repair, openness to falsification and correction, and whether trust in problem raising is being consumed.

## Validity conditions
- The problem location and cause are opened rather than crisis alone.
- Neglecting actor and repair actor are distinguished structurally.
- Cost or tradeoff and a first action are visible.
- Falsification and correction conditions remain open.
- Problem raising increases judgment and repair capacity rather than anxiety alone.

## Failure conditions
- Anxiety rises while no next verification step remains.
- Responsibility and repair actors stay unclear while burden moves to the field.
- Cost and implementation conditions are hidden.
- Claims become self-reinforcing and unfalsifiable.
- Trust in problem raising erodes until necessary warnings are discounted.

## Falsification conditions
- Crisis discourse without explicit seven responsibility conditions nevertheless produces durable increases in judgment capacity and implementation ability, preserves trust in problem raising, and reliably connects to repair.
- Under such evidence, the scope or necessary conditions of AKI-001 should be revised.

## Required distinctions
- problem raising / anxiety diffusion
- strong warning / irresponsible crisis performance
- incomplete solution / closing repairability
- early reporting / unfalsifiable crisis discourse
- criticism / suppression of criticism
- speaker execution duty / responsibility to open repair conditions

## Interpretation constraints
- Do not turn the theory into suppression of problem raising.
- Do not reduce it to social-media etiquette or generic constructive-criticism advice.
- Do not classify every incomplete solution as irresponsible.
- Do not automatically classify whistleblowing or early reporting as a failure case.
- Do not infer legal liability.
