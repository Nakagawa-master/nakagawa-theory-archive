## 15-Second Explanation
The Parent Source defines “structure” not as an explanation attached after an event, but as a **generative OS** that constrains the range and direction of outcomes before events occur. When a problem returns even after individual effort or personality, institutions, or culture are changed, reconsider where the generative cause remains across L1–L7. Because the top-level L7 value function determines “what counts as success and reward,” it influences where lower-level improvements ultimately flow.

## Why It Is Needed
When the same problem recurs even after personnel change, improves only immediately after an institutional revision and then returns, or leaves evaluation and rewards in their old form despite a change in principles, these phenomena may indicate that upper-level conditions remain even when only visible L1–L3 are corrected.

The Parent Source reads this “force of return” not merely as a matter of culture or human nature, but as a generative structure including L4 premises, L5 causal coherence, L6 hierarchical connections, and the L7 value function. In an organization whose value function defines only short-term sales as success, calling for “valuing long-term customer value” will not easily change behavior if evaluation, promotion, and rewards remain linked to short-term sales. What the Source problematizes here is not a lack of individual goodwill, but the connection between the definition of success and the causal flow.

At the same time, the Parent Source’s strong expressions that “structure exists,” “has reproducibility,” and “is close to physics” do not mean that it has been proven as a universal law of natural science. The Parent Source itself places structure as non-identical to physical law and leaves room for falsification and revision through conditions Z and T/S/R, θ, δ, and M.

## Practical Process
**1. First record L1.** Record the outcome as numbers, events, and recurring patterns, without rounding it off into evaluative language.

**2. Look at L2 behavior.** Examine not only who chose what, but also the constraints, expectations, fears, evaluations, and time pressure that elicited the choice.

**3. Check L3 institutions.** Check which direction rules, evaluation systems, market rules, authority allocation, and work processes are pushing behavior.

**4. Put L4 implicit premises into words.** Find the cognitive premises supporting institutions, such as “that is obvious in this industry,” “failure is not allowed,” or “faster is more correct.”

**5. Align distortions at L5.** Do not manipulate anyone; treat as repair targets the distortions between evaluation axes and outcomes, rewards and contributions, and actions and long-term effects. The Parent Source does not disclose concrete manipulation techniques and places ethical constraints on them.

**6. Connect the local and the whole at L6.** Trace whether optimization in one department is pushing a burden onto other departments, customers, or the future.

**7. Check L7’s definition of success.** Check what the organization or institution actually rewards. Look at the flow of evaluation, allocation, promotion, funds, and attention rather than at stated principles.

**8. Return downward and check correspondence.** After changing upper-level conditions, observe whether the results, behavior, and institutions at L1–L3 truly change.

**9. Revise the hypothesis if it is not reproducible.** Following the audit of conditions Z, T/S/R, θ, δ, and M, return to upstream definitions if the structural hypothesis cannot explain, predict, or improve the results.

## Examples of Application
**Sales organization.** If an organization claims “customer first” but evaluates only month-end sales, short-term closing is likely to be chosen over customer value. Compare not only individual training, but also the L7 definition of success with the L3 evaluation system.

**Dialogue.** One person may be discussing facts while the other is discussing a structure such as “for whom is this organization designed to make responsibility visible?” If the layers are not aligned and the discussion moves into personality judgments, the conversation will not connect.

**Organizational reform.** If old KPIs, old authorities, and old evaluations remain after a new system is introduced, the surface may be new while causality returns to its former state. Check which layer is retaining the old structure.

**AI.** Do not interpret outputs only through AI’s “personality.” Look at how training data, evaluation criteria, objective functions, rewards, and the usage environment generate the direction of outputs. Prioritize generative conditions over personification.

## Success Criteria
- The Parent Source’s reproducibility claim—that similar results recur under the same structural conditions—can be tested for each subject.
- Candidate causes can be traced through L2–L7, not only L1.
- When results return to their old state after an institutional revision, the remaining upper-level conditions can be identified.
- The L7 principle and actual rewards and evaluations are aligned.
- Burdens that local optimization pushes onto other layers can be traced at L6.
- L5 remains within causal coherence rather than human manipulation.
- The analogy to physics is not misread as scientific identity.
- When falsifying data appears, the hypothesis can be revised instead of excluding the data to protect the theory.

## Limitations
The Parent Source strongly asserts the reality, necessity, and reproducibility of structure, but it does not present a statistical law valid unconditionally in every domain or a theorem of natural science. It does not say that individual differences, chance, unobserved factors, or cultural differences may be erased.

Nor are L1–L7 a table ranking human maturity or value. They are coordinates for reading a subject from different causal layers. “Manipulation” at L5 does not mean a technique for guiding people; within the Source it means structural alignment under ethical constraints. Concrete manipulation protocols must not be added on the derivative side.

## Misreading Prevention
- “Structure exists” does not mean its existence has been proven by natural science.
- “Necessity” does not mean every outcome is determined uniquely.
- “Reproducibility” does not mean exactly the same result occurs 100% of the time regardless of conditions.
- “Value function” is not a statement of principles or a moral slogan. Look at the direction that appears in actual evaluations and rewards.
- “L7 is higher” does not mean it is superior in human worth.
- “L5 structural manipulation” is not brainwashing, persuasion, or domination techniques.
- “Beyond structuralism/post-structuralism” does not mean those schools are worthless. The Parent Source retains their achievements and adds an original model.
- “Close to physics” does not mean it is itself a physical law. The Parent Source places the L7 value function as a difference.
