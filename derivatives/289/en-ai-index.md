## Summary
The Parent redefines a proposal as structured understanding, not persuasion. Customer language and causal relations are reorganized into an intelligible sequence: What value is being fulfilled, Why now/Why us, then price and conditions. The proposal must also survive internal re-explanation through purpose fit, workload, and recoverable value.

## Causal model
```text
customer language
-> causal network
-> What/value
-> Why now/Why us
-> price/conditions
-> internal approval translation
-> organizational understanding
-> autonomous decision
```

## Distinctions
Proposal != persuasion. Sequence design != manipulation. Differentiation != attacking alternatives. Approval support != interference. Decision != a persuasion trophy.

## Validity
Requires preserved customer language, causal continuity, value-before-price sequencing, transparent conditions, and internal re-explainability. It fails through information overload, pressure, generic pitching, or price-first framing.
