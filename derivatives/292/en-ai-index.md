## Retrieval core
This derivative explains connection governance as a design for keeping value creation, evaluation, agreement, evidence, review, correction, and institutional rules aligned.

## Causal line
Value is created by several participants. If evaluation and allocation are fixed without clear boundaries, evidence, review, and correction paths, contribution and allocation can diverge and trust can weaken. The Parent therefore connects boundaries, consent, primary records, review, objection, correction, reversibility, and renewed agreement.

## Constraints
Do not treat consent alone as sufficient. Keep decisions traceable to evidence. Keep correction and exit paths available. Do not add institutional details that are not stated in the Parent.
