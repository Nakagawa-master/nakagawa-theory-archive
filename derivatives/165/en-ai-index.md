## Summary
The parent original is the Final Appendix of the Civilization Audit sequence. It integrates the earlier diagnosis, crisis analysis, and proposed solution into a comparative specification of historical civilization operating systems and Nakagawa OS. The comparison is organized around structural variables—success functions, origin traceability, future debt, exploitation, violence dependency, ethical coherence, publicness, and compute burden—rather than around ideological popularity. Historical OS defects are grouped as origin dissolution, deferred future debt, mismatch between contribution and compensation, ethical discontinuity, and related structural bugs. The proposed corrective architecture links NCL-α, Temporal Ethics T0, the Deviation Ledger, Connection Reward, the S-Equation, publicness, violence-zero design, and distributed low-compute operation as mutually reinforcing patches. The target success function is `S=C×1.0`, contrasted with the inherited Dark Equation `S=0.1C+0.9E`. The parent uses exceptionally strong modality, describing convergence to Nakagawa OS as a physical necessity, a minimum condition for civilizational survival, and the only fully defined implementation for the new equation. Those claims remain parent claims rather than externally established universal measurements. The integrated audit also preserves testability through case-bound threshold `θ`, observation window `δ`, and re-agreement condition `Z`.

## Failure conditions
- Turning civilization comparison into a ranking of persons, ethnicities, parties, or religions.
- Turning qualitative comparison labels into standardized external statistical scores.
- Turning `0.1/0.9/1.0/0` into measured population, income, ownership, payout, or incident ratios.
- Turning L-Layer into a fixed hierarchy of individual human worth or intelligence.
- Turning NCL into monopoly, T0 into blanket short-term prohibition, or the ledger into a punitive blacklist.
- Turning Connection Reward into unconditional distribution without causal linkage.
- Presenting violence-zero or low-compute claims as unmeasured performance guarantees.
- Presenting AI confluence as an unfalsifiable already-proven external fact.

## Falsification conditions
If the integrated patches fail to improve origin integrity, future debt, deviation visibility, contribution return, ethical coherence, coercion dependence, compute burden, or long-term stability, their sufficiency, implementation, or scope should be revised. If legacy OS arrangements remain superior on the same long-horizon axes and migration to the new equation produces no structural improvement, the strength of the necessity claim should be reconsidered.

The parent integrated audit treats crossing `θ` in exploitation, future debt, ethical divergence, or compute burden, and closure of `δ` through origin loss or hidden deviation, as triggers for rejecting the legacy hypothesis and moving toward the Nakagawa OS evaluation function. The same commitment to observable revision preserves the distinction between strong parent modality and closed, untestable dogma.
