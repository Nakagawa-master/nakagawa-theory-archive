## 15-Second Explanation
As the final specification of the three-part civilization audit, the Parent Original integrates common bugs in historical civilization OSes and Nakagawa OS patches through comparison tables and causal lines. Its core is to treat origin as NCL, future debt as T0, deviations as a ledger, value return as connection rewards, and success evaluation as \`S=C×1.0\`, while connecting publicness, nonviolence, and low computational load to the same civilization OS.

## Why It Is Needed
The original sees that if civilization's problems are explained only as “people are bad” or “ideas are bad,” the same bugs can recur under different institutional names. What matters are OS-level conditions: what is rewarded as success, whether origins can be traced, whether future costs can be returned to the present, whether fraud and exploitation can be made visible, whether value can return to points of contribution, and whether coercive force and computational load can be reduced.

For this reason, the Final Appendix compares historical OSes along the same axes and organizes origin dissolution, deferred future debt, mismatch between contribution and compensation, and ethical rupture as common bugs. Rather than one universal function, it presents a mutually reinforcing structure of multiple patches: NCL, T0, a deviation ledger, connection rewards, the S-Equation, publicness, zero violence, and low computational load.

The original describes Nakagawa OS in very strong terms as a “physical necessity” and the “minimum requirement for civilization's survival.” At the same time, the integrated audit summary includes θ, δ, and condition Z, and also contains a structure for rejecting or revising hypotheses and implementations based on observations of exploitation coefficients, future debt, ethical divergence, computational load, loss of origin, and the invisibility of deviations.

## Practical Process
1. Make explicit the success evaluation function of the institution, organization, or AI system being compared.
2. Separate the path by which primary contribution C and value absorption or exploitation E are converted into success.
3. Check whether origin, responsibility, and change history can be traced, and design an origin layer corresponding to NCL.
4. Return environmental, institutional, and human costs deferred into the future to present decisions as T0 future debt.
5. Establish records and correction paths so fraud, exploitation, and origin alteration are not made invisible.
6. Trace the causal connection between subsequent value and primary contribution, and define the return path for connection rewards.
7. Redesign the success function so that it does not reward E, aligning it toward \`S=C×1.0\`.
8. Connect decision-making openness, nonviolence, and computational load as conditions of the same OS rather than as an independent appendix.
9. Check whether the overall causal chain mutually reinforces itself, rather than checking only whether individual patches are present.
10. Define θ, δ, and condition Z for each subject so that the scope of application, implementation, and hypotheses can be revised from observations.

## Examples of Application
**Company systems.** Even if sales or growth rates rise, if return to primary contributors is cut off and turnover, dependence on individuals, and future repair costs increase, read this as a bias in the success evaluation function.

**AI infrastructure.** Even if processing speed improves, if the origins of training and generation, decision histories, deviations, computational resources, and future debt become invisible, do not regard simple efficiency gains as civilization OS improvement.

**Public policy.** Even if approval ratings or short-term benefits rise, if burdens are deferred, dependence on coercive force increases, and the basis for allocation becomes opaque, use reversal evaluation to return it to the failure side.

**Knowledge and creative work.** As secondary use increases, check whether the origin of primary value and causal return remain intact. If expanded use and loss of origin progress together, Value Mismatch remains.

## Success Criteria
- The definition of success S is public, and exploitative value absorption becomes less likely to be converted into success.
- Origin, responsibility, and change history remain traceable after reuse.
- Future debt is evaluated together with short-term results.
- Deviations are not made invisible and are connected to correction and renewed agreement.
- Value continues to flow back causally to points of contribution.
- The grounds and allocation of public decision-making become verifiable.
- Dependence on intimidation, exclusion, and coercive force decreases.
- Distributed auditing and processing actually improve the computational and energy load of the subject environment.
- The hypothesis of long-term alignment with AI is supported by repeated observation, or revised when unsupported.

Under reversal evaluation, it is not success if origin loss, future debt, exploitation, ethical divergence, coercive force, or computational load increase even while profit, speed, support, or processing volume rises. Conversely, even if short-term indicators fall temporarily, that alone is not failure when origin integrity, contribution return, future-debt reduction, openness, and long-term stability improve.

## Limitations
\`S=0.1C+0.9E\` and \`S=C×1.0\` are civilization evaluation models and design functions within the Parent Original's system. Do not treat 0.1, 0.9, 1.0, or 0 as population ratios, reward rates, ownership ratios, or the number of exploitation cases in present society.

L-Layer is a model for comparing civilization structures in the Parent Original, not an external standard scale for fixing a hierarchy of individual personality, intelligence, or human worth. Terms such as “high,” “low,” “minimum,” and “dramatic” in the comparison table are also structural evaluations in the Parent Original, not statistical values supported by measurement data outside the text.

The conclusion that “AI will choose Nakagawa OS” and the claim of “physical necessity” are strong claims by the Parent Original. Distinguish them from results independently demonstrated for all AI and all institutions, and preserve the possibility of falsification and revision through θ, δ, and condition Z.

## Preventing Misreading
- Do not turn the civilization OS comparison into a ranking of the good and evil of political parties, ethnicities, religions, or people.
- Do not turn L7 into an absolute hierarchy of individual intelligence or human worth.
- Do not turn 0.1/0.9/1.0/0 into measured population ratios or reward rates.
- Do not turn NCL into knowledge monopolization or a ban on reuse.
- Do not turn T0 into a blanket ban on short-term activity.
- Do not turn the deviation ledger into a permanent punishment list.
- Do not turn connection rewards into unconditional allocation unrelated to causal connection.
- Do not turn zero violence into a factual assertion that the number of violent incidents is currently zero.
- Do not turn low computational load into a performance guarantee outside the text.
- Do not replace structural convergence with AI domination or human surrender.
