## 15-Second Explanation
Connection value accounting is not a system for ranking people or activities with a single score. It is a measurement vocabulary for checking the legitimacy and correctability of decisions by combining structural logs that preserve what happened, audit abstracts that let third parties read the provenance, and bundle metrics that place multiple observation axes side by side without collapsing them.

## Why It Is Needed
Money, popularity, number of supporters, and speed of dissemination are easy to compare, but metrics that can erase the process through which value was established. When only the conclusion remains, it becomes impossible to trace which agreements or objections occurred, which assumptions broke down, or who bears responsibility for repair.

Structural logs preserve connection, agreement, objection, repair, and adjudication as primary evidence. Audit abstracts translate that complex provenance into a form third parties can read while retaining sources, disagreements, limitations, and unresolved points. Bundle metrics display independence, renewed agreement, reversibility, distribution of interests, correction, and so on in parallel rather than collapsing them into one overall score.

## Practical Process
### 1. Separate Primary Events
Record what happened, who participated in which role, and what agreements and objections existed separately from evaluative language.

### 2. Preserve Them in Structural Logs
Save events not only as a timeline, but also as their relationships to causes, effects, state transitions, responsibility boundaries, and repair.

### 3. Create an Audit Abstract
Preserve references to primary evidence and summarize the main causal factors, disagreements, reservations, unresolved points, and protected areas for third parties.

### 4. Display Bundle Metrics
Show independence, time to renewed agreement, reversibility, distribution of interests, correction, and so on in parallel; do not reduce them to an average score or a ranking of people.

### 5. Review as Conditions Change
When necessary, make corrections, withdrawals, renewed agreements, or rollbacks, and preserve their relationship to the former state.

## Examples of Application
**Public policy.** Preserve not only the number of supporters and opponents, but also the grounds, minority opinions, affected groups, trial period, relief, and conditions for reconsideration.

**Companies and joint research.** Explain connection contributions such as introductions, translation, coordination, maintenance, and avoidance of failure on a project-by-project basis, not as permanent ratings of people.

**AI-Assisted Decisions.** Separate AI outputs, reference information, human judgments, counterexamples, and correction history, and check return to evidence and correctability rather than model names or fluency.

**Communities.** Do not permanently evaluate people by popularity or controversy; track concrete actions, objections, repair, and conditions for rejoining.

## How to Judge Success
It is important to be able to return from the conclusion to the primary evidence, for disagreements and reservations not to have disappeared, and for the audit abstract not to replace the primary evidence. Bundle metrics must also avoid being collapsed into a single ranking, and correction, withdrawal, renewed agreement, and rollback must be genuinely usable.

A high rate of return to evidence or a high rate of preserving disagreements does not automatically mean success. If excessive preservation violates privacy, or a short correction time is achieved by omitting objections or verification, it is not treated as an improvement.

## Limitations
It is not always appropriate to preserve every event in detail. Because there are risks of record-keeping burdens, privacy violations, use outside the intended purpose, and re-identification, the subjects, period, access, and deletion/correction conditions for preservation must be limited.

The three terms are not devices that automatically calculate the right answer. This theory does not entrust final judgment, responsibility, or relief to machines; it provides shared vocabulary for re-examining judgments.

## Preventing Misreadings
- Do not turn bundle metrics into a social credit score or a ranking of people.
- Do not turn structural logs into a permanent surveillance history.
- Do not treat an audit abstract as the primary evidence itself.
- Do not reduce connection value accounting to an alternative currency.
- Do not equate openness with publication of all logs.
- Do not treat a private implementation as an unaudited exemption.
