## Summary
Connected Value Accounting separates three basic terms: Structure Log as primary evidence, Audit Abstract as a third-party interpretation window, and Bundle Indicators as a multi-axis display. A Structure Log preserves connection, consent, objection, repair, adjudication, and state transitions. An Audit Abstract translates that evidence while retaining sources, dissent, limits, unresolved points, and conflicts of interest. Bundle Indicators display dimensions such as independence, re-agreement, reversibility, interest distribution, and correction without collapsing them into a universal score.

The purpose is to prevent money, popularity, vote count, authority, or speed from replacing legitimacy. Non-coercion, re-agreement, reversibility, objection, correction, and remedy are standing conditions rather than optional performance metrics.

## Causal chain
Single quantities dominate value judgments → popularity, authority, or speed substitutes for legitimacy → conclusions survive while consent, objections, causality, and repair disappear → Structure Logs preserve primary evidence → Audit Abstracts translate it for third-party review → Bundle Indicators display multiple dimensions without ranking people → decisions remain corrigible through correction, withdrawal, re-agreement, and rollback.

## Applications
- Public policy: preserve reasons, minority views, trial periods, remedy, and review conditions.
- Organizational collaboration: explain connection contributions at the case level without permanent ranking of people.
- AI-assisted judgment: separate model output, sources, human judgment, counterexamples, and correction history.
- Community governance: treat objection, repair, and re-entry as structural states rather than character judgments.

## Measurements and audit
The parent uses observational expressions such as traceability from conclusions to primary evidence, preservation of objections and unresolved points, time required for re-agreement/correction/withdrawal/rollback, incidents of personality scoring or secondary use, boundary violations between public abstracts and protected records, and the relation between recordkeeping burden and verified benefit.

Source: parent original. Measurement actor: actors operating and reviewing Structure Logs, Audit Abstracts, and Bundle Indicators. Measurement object: evidence traceability, objection preservation, corrigibility, information boundaries, and drift into gaming or surveillance. Source modality: operational observations attached to the parent lexicon. Permitted use scope: detecting single-metric domination, loss of evidence, loss of correction paths, and surveillance drift. Non-guarantee scope: these variables do not determine personality, creditworthiness, morality, social worth, employability, or a universal legitimacy score.

Higher traceability or objection-preservation rates are not automatically better when they depend on overcollection or re-identification. Faster correction is not improvement when objection and verification are removed. Fewer corrections may indicate blocked correction paths, while more corrections may indicate effective error detection and repair.

## Validity conditions
- Log, abstract, and indicators remain role-separated.
- Evidence and interpretation lineage remain traceable.
- Dissent, reservations, and unresolved points remain visible.
- Multiple dimensions are not collapsed into a universal score.
- Non-coercion, re-agreement, reversibility, objection, correction, and remedy are usable.
- Public and protected information have an explicit boundary.

## Failure conditions
The framework fails when Structure Logs become permanent surveillance histories, Audit Abstracts replace primary evidence, Bundle Indicators become social-credit or personality rankings, popularity/authority/speed substitutes for legitimacy, withdrawal is punished, or non-public implementation becomes an exemption from audit.

## Falsification conditions
Revise the scope if evidence traceability and corrigibility do not improve, bundle displays increase gaming, or recordkeeping burden, surveillance, retaliation, or discrimination increases.

## Required distinctions
- Structure Log / surveillance history
- Audit Abstract / final truth
- Bundle Indicators / total score
- legitimacy / popularity
- re-agreement / indecision
- reversibility / avoidance of responsibility
- transparency / disclosure of all data

## Interpretation constraints
Do not reduce Connected Value Accounting to currency, personality scoring, or social credit. Do not treat the vocabulary as publication of hidden algorithms or internal thresholds. Do not assume a log alone establishes legitimacy. Do not confuse a readable abstract with complete primary evidence. Do not average bundle indicators back into a universal ranking.
