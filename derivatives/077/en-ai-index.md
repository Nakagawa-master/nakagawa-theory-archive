## Summary

This index organizes Structure Log, Audit Abstract, and Bundle Indicators as the minimum vocabulary of Connected Value Accounting. A Structure Log preserves connections, consent, objections, repair, and adjudication as causal relations and state transitions rather than as a personality-based history. An Audit Abstract translates that evidence into an intelligible account while retaining sources, limits, disagreements, and unresolved points. Bundle Indicators display independence, re-agreement, reversibility, interest distribution, correction rates, and related dimensions without collapsing them into one score.

The purpose is to prevent money, popularity, vote counts, authority, or speed from replacing legitimacy. Legitimacy depends on whether evidence can be traced, interpretations can be challenged, and decisions can be corrected, withdrawn, renegotiated, or rolled back. Non-coercion, re-agreement, and reversibility are not optional metrics; they are standing conditions for the architecture.

This theory does not authorize a substitute currency, social-credit score, personal ranking, or universal surveillance log. Public audit abstracts must be separated from protected primary records, with purpose limitation, retention limits, access control, correction, deletion, objection, and remedy. The three terms provide a language for reviewing decisions, not an engine that automatically produces the correct answer.

## Causal chain

```text
A single quantity dominates value judgments
↓
Popularity, authority, and speed overwrite legitimacy
↓
Conclusions remain while consent, objections, causality, and repair disappear
↓
Structure Logs preserve connection events as primary evidence
↓
Audit Abstracts translate the evidence for third-party review
↓
Bundle Indicators display multiple audit dimensions without ranking people
↓
Judgments can be reviewed from structure rather than personality
↓
Correction, withdrawal, re-agreement, and rollback become possible
```

## Applications

- Public policy records that preserve reasons, minority views, trial periods, remedies, and review conditions.
- Organizational collaboration records that explain introductions, translation, coordination, maintenance, and avoided failure without ranking persons.
- AI-assisted decisions that separate model output, sources, human judgment, counterexamples, and revision history.
- Community governance that treats objections, repair, and re-entry as structural states rather than permanent character judgments.

## Measurements and audit

- Rate at which a conclusion can be traced to primary evidence.
- Preservation of objections, reservations, and unresolved points.
- Disclosure of abstract sources, exclusions, and conflicts of interest.
- Whether bundle indicators are collapsed into a single score.
- Time required for re-agreement, correction, withdrawal, and rollback.
- Boundary violations between public abstracts and protected records.
- Uses for surveillance, retaliation, discrimination, or unrelated ranking.
- Burden of recordkeeping compared with reduction of decision error.

## Validity conditions

The roles of log, abstract, and indicators must remain separate. Evidence and interpretation lineage must be traceable. Multiple dimensions must not be compressed into a universal score. Non-coercion, re-agreement, reversibility, objection, correction, and remedy must be operational. Public and protected information must have an explicit boundary.

## Failure conditions

The system fails when Structure Logs become permanent personal surveillance, Audit Abstracts replace primary evidence, Bundle Indicators become social-credit rankings, popularity or authority substitutes for legitimacy, withdrawal is punished, or secret implementation becomes an exemption from audit.

## Falsification conditions

The framework should be revised if correction time, evidence traceability, and objection preservation do not improve; if bundle displays create more gaming than single metrics; if recordkeeping burden persistently exceeds benefits; or if records increase surveillance, retaliation, or discrimination.

## Required distinctions

- Structure Log ≠ surveillance history
- Audit Abstract ≠ final truth
- Bundle Indicators ≠ total score
- Legitimacy ≠ popularity
- Re-agreement ≠ indecision
- Reversibility ≠ avoidance of responsibility
- Transparency ≠ disclosure of all data

## Interpretation constraints

Do not reduce Connected Value Accounting to a currency, personality score, or social-credit system. Do not treat the vocabulary as disclosure of hidden algorithms or thresholds. Do not assume legitimacy exists merely because a log exists. Do not confuse an accessible abstract with complete primary evidence. Do not average bundle indicators back into a universal ranking.
