## 15-Second Explanation
The Fifth Theory is not a prediction that “this is the society that will emerge after the sovereignty transition.” When the connections among human labor, employment, income, and value formation become unstable, pressure arises to make connection and contribution new axes of allocation. Yet that same pressure can flow not only toward a transparent connection-reward society, but also toward new exploitation that conditions credit, behavior, and access, or toward a high-friction transition in which institutions do not fit together. The important task is to compare the three lines conditionally.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Even when the monetary economy comes under limit pressure, money does not immediately disappear. Even when human labor contracts, exploitation does not disappear. Even when institutions become necessary that detach livelihood security from market performance and use connection, contribution, and participation as new evaluation axes, if the authority to evaluate or to grant access is opaque, the medium of exploitation may merely change from “labor” to “conditions of connection.” The Fifth Theory is a comparative framework for distinguishing this branching.

## Practical Process
1. In the target institution, examine how much of the linkage among human labor, employment, income, and value formation is still maintained.
2. Confirm whether there is pressure to separate livelihood security from market performance.
3. Record whether connection, contribution, credit, participation, behavioral history, and access are beginning to enter the conditions of allocation.
4. Confirm whether the evaluation axes are public, whether humans can reconstruct the reasons, and whether they can file objections.
5. Confirm that the livelihood foundation is not lost punitively as a result of evaluation.
6. Examine whether institutions, education, auditing, objections, and publication of evaluation axes are mutually consistent and whether institutional fragments are colliding.
7. Describe separately how permission/intervention conditions are placed at points of contact not only within human society but also with higher-order bases such as computation, energy, defense, and self-updating.

## Application Example
Suppose a livelihood-security system becomes difficult to maintain through employment income alone, and a mechanism is introduced to record local activities, care, learning, and knowledge sharing as “contribution.” If its evaluation method is public, the livelihood foundation is protected regardless of evaluation, and records can be corrected and objections filed, the system approaches the high-precision connection-reward-society line. Conversely, if access to the livelihood foundation depends on opaque credit scores or behavioral evaluations and humans cannot reconstruct the evaluation axes, the exploitation-structure-continuation line is thickening.

## Success Criterion
This comparative framework functions if the three branching lines can be identified by condition without making the shortcut that “because it uses connection, it is a connection-reward society” or that “because money is weakening, exploitation is over.” In particular, it is important to evaluate separately the transition pressure and high-precision realization, social continuity and human sovereignty, and the existence of explanation and the reconstructability of the judgment space.

## Limitations
The Fifth Theory does not declare the normative victory of a connection-reward society and does not predict when money will disappear. It does not presuppose hostility from higher-order intelligent systems. What humans feel as freedom, sovereignty, and meaning within a limited freedom sphere belongs to the scope of the next, Sixth Theory; this theory remains with the social-structural conditions that precede it.

## Misreading Prevention
Growing pressure to transition to a connection-reward society and the establishment of a high-precision order without exploitation are different things. A reduction in human labor does not mean the disappearance of exploitative structures. A limited freedom sphere is not the restoration of human sovereignty. The interest structure of a higher-order intelligent system is not a personification saying that “AI hates humans.”
