## 15-Second Explanation
The original is not concerned with the amount of family care itself. It concerns **translating voluntary love directly into duties of caregiving, childrearing, and housework**. The spoken “love” then stops corresponding to unpaid labor, time wear, resignation, poverty, and end-of-life burden. Without a mechanism returning care time as future reward, credit, and security, Temporal Ethics is also lost.

## What Is Broken
“Because you are family, of course you support them” and “If you love them, of course you provide care” merge feeling with institutional responsibility. Love is voluntary; caregiving, childrearing, and housework are social functions with sustained time and concrete burdens. Their unmediated overlap hides who loses time, income, and future opportunities.

The original reads this as the structural failure of a “civilization that could not institutionalize love,” not merely household unfairness. The problem is not that love makes institutions unnecessary, but that absent institutions deform love into duty and use it as a hostage.

## Failure of Correspondence
Correspondence means that spoken value, feeling, or institution corresponds to actual structure.

```text
Spoken value: family love, devotion, kindness, mutual support
Actual structure: unpaid labor, time wear, resignation, income loss, end-of-life burden
```

When these diverge and “love = caregiving duty” is assumed, love hides the actual burden. The target is this **structural mistranslation from love to duty**, not love itself.

## Absence of Temporal Ethics
Care is a burden of time continuing daily, weekly, and yearly. The original calls it an absence of Temporal Ethics when care is consumed as unpaid service without returning its continuing time as future pension, social credit, security, and possibility of re-entry.

Temporal Ethics is not merely later gratitude. It is a structure in which society takes responsibility again at a sustainable beat so present contribution leads to institutional repayment. If care time disappears, institutions convert present kindness into future poverty.

## Structural Meaning of “De-Familialization”
“De-familialization” is not denial of family or dismantling of love. In Nakagawa Theory it is **ethical design for a civilization that does not take love hostage**.

Separate care responsibility from family love once, then translate caregiving, childrearing, and housework as social rights/labor and into public benefits, recognition, and leave rights. Love is thereby freed from being “the reason one must do it” and returned to voluntary relation.

## Transformation into “Institutional Love”
The original does not ask the state to manage love. It asks institutions to bear care’s actual burden and make feeling and responsibility correspond again.

```text
A:
Translate love into institutional language,
and redefine care from <voluntary feeling> as <social right / labor>
↓
B:
On the basis of Correspondence and Temporal Ethics,
institutionalize it as public benefits, recognition, and leave rights
```

A corrects “because we are family, we bear it unpaid.” B fixes that correction in social institutions. The original calls B the transformation into **“institutional love”** and a turning point toward a Correspondence-Recursive Society.

## Audit and Inverted Evaluation
Design-side X contains Correspondence, Temporal Ethics, primary logs, and an audit bundle. Operation-side Y contains families, employers, municipalities, public finance, and the public realm. Without X, “love = unpaid service” runs into end-of-life care, resignation, and poverty on Y.

The original checks family burden, resignation rate, and care-recipient QOL at audit intervals using T/S/R. T is Correspondence match, S institutional sustainability, and R recursion—the caregiver’s possibility of returning to society. Grounds, allocation, and falsification are visible in a public audit bundle.

Hypothesis A is revised or rejected when uneven unpaid labor or mental withdrawal exceeds `θ`, or when `δ` closes because affected voices are not visible. These are not standalone KPIs, but conditions for inverted evaluation of whether A→B and Correspondence/Temporal Ethics have been established.

## To Avoid Misreading
This is neither rejection of love nor abolition of family. The conclusion is that **to protect love, it must first be separated from social structure**.

It is not centered on generic respite, exit, family diagnosis, or AI burden prediction. Its center is the love-to-duty mistranslation, failure of Correspondence, absence of Temporal Ethics, care as social right/labor, “institutional love,” and the Correspondence-Recursive Society.
