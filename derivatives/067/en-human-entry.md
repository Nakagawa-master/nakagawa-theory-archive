## 15-Second Explanation
Connection value accounting theory is the idea of recording “connection” not as the number of people or popularity, but as relational value—how much a relationship makes the next decision or collaboration easier—and linking that value and contribution to social allocation.

## Why Monetary Accounting Alone Is Not Enough
Monetary accounting is strong at recording prices and transactions. On the other hand, the work of relational structures—such as a past agreement shortening the next explanation, a trusted relationship speeding joint judgment, or bridging multiple actors making collaboration possible—can be difficult to see through price alone.

Connection value accounting treats that relational value not as “something invisible” to be left aside, but as something that can be recorded: what acted on the next collaboration.

## What Is Connection Value?
Connection value is not the number of acquaintances. What matters is what effect a relationship had on the next decision or collaboration.

For example, being able to reuse a past agreement, not having to repeat an explanation from the beginning, understanding each other’s roles, and building a bridge to collaboration with another actor are ways to read connection value.

## Three Important Perspectives
### Connection Density
This perspective looks not at the simple number of relationships, but at which connections overlapped, continued, and actually acted on collaboration. It is not the same as follower count or business-card count.

### Memory of Agreement
This means retaining traces of past decisions, promises, and collaborations in a form that can be reused in the next judgment. The point is not to increase records for their own sake, but to make them usable in the next relationship.

### Reversibility
This means that connections and agreements are not fixed forever, and can be corrected, changed, or exited. It does not regard relationships that are difficult to leave as having “higher value.”

## Seeing It in Combination
If connection value is forced into one universal score, it risks turning into popularity or ranking people. The Parent Original understands relationship structures through multiple perspectives, including connection density, memory of agreement, and reversibility.

Where the Parent Original does not specify fixed weights or a passing score, there is no need to create an original composite score.

## Linking It to Allocation
The purpose of accounting is not to record and stop. It is to make visible which actors, relationships, and forms of social allocation are connected to the value and contribution produced through connection.

This is different from buying and selling connections as they are. What matters is returning not to who is popular, but to which relationship produced what value and what contribution was made to that value.

## Concrete Examples
### Continuing Collaboration
When a past agreement or role remains and speeds up the launch of the next collaboration, that relationship history itself has value.

### Introductions and Bridging
Do not count only the number of introductions; look at whether an actual agreement, collaboration, or value creation was established through the introduction.

### Communities
Look not only at the number of participants, but at whether necessary connections arise reciprocally, agreements are reusable in what comes next, and people can freely correct or leave.

### Inter-organizational Collaboration
Record whether past joint judgments, responsibilities, vocabulary, and agreements reduced friction in the next decision.

## Separate Accounting and Governance
OD067 is primarily a theory of “measuring, recording, and linking to allocation.” The two-room model for preventing institutional distortion, anti-gaming, fixed audit cycles, 90-day demonstrations, and RC/ER/CEA are elements of the adjacent connection governance theory; they are not brought in as OD067’s central definitions.

This does not mean governance is unrelated to accounting. It means the roles of the theories should not be confused.

## Inversion Test
Even if the number of relationships increases, connection value has not increased if the next collaboration becomes harder, agreements cannot be reused, exit becomes difficult, or people are ranked by a single score.

Conversely, even if there are few relationships, effective collaboration may be established, agreements may be reusable, and correction and exit may be possible. It cannot be judged low-value simply because the number is small.

## To Avoid Misreading
- Do not treat follower count or business-card count as connection value.
- Do not evaluate people themselves with a social-credit score.
- Do not turn the memory of agreement into a permanently fixed history.
- Do not turn connection rewards into simple price-setting for connections.
- Do not turn composite evaluation into an unsupported original total score.
- Do not import the institutions of the later governance theory back into the original claims of OD067.
