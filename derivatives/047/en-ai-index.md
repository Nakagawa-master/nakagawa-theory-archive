## Structural summary
The source defines withdrawal not as cleanup after failure but as a core reversible operating procedure. Missing decision paths, poor resource efficiency, and absent client motivation are the main withdrawal triggers. Respectful closure language, logs of reasons and evidence, cooling windows, reallocation plans, and revisit conditions preserve relationship capital while releasing time and effort for higher-probability opportunities.

## Causal chain
```text
opportunity stalls
→ decision path, resource efficiency and motivation observed
→ continue / monitor / withdraw classified
→ relationship-preserving closure
→ reasons, evidence and revisit conditions logged
→ resources reallocated
→ reconnect when conditions change
```

## Required distinctions
- withdrawal is not defeat
- letting go is not relationship destruction
- resource reallocation is not contempt for the client
- continued observation is not indefinite neglect

## Interpretation constraints
Do not justify withdrawal solely by short-term numbers. Preserve predefined criteria, evidence, dignity, cooling periods, and revisit conditions. Return to the parent source for audit indicators, examples, and origin signature.
