## 15-Second Explanation
Life-OS Vol.2 does not ask “what should I add to become more myself?” It measures the bias that remains as a flame after comparison, evaluation, approval, and social correctness have been removed. It protects that flame on two axes—purity and intensity—translates “what concerns me” into “what can therefore be done,” and makes it a small connection plug. It then observes the other side’s flow, synchronizes, makes a tiny contribution that lowers one point of load, and enters structural confluence when a next request or role recognition returns. The flame is not life’s answer, but a condition for continuing to participate in circulation without relying on fear.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
In an age of strong survival anxiety, fear, commands, and competition are powerful external fuel. But under Life-OS’s premise, as survival becomes guaranteed as a first-floor floor, that external engine weakens. A gap can then arise: “I do not know what to do,” or “I am free but cannot move.”

Many life theories try to add new goals, qualifications, titles, hobbies, dreams, and success models. The Parent instead sees addition as likely to increase noise. What people need is not what to add, but to separate layers of approval, failure avoidance, conformity, and endless optimization acquired as social adaptation, and measure what still remains beneath them.

A flame need not be dramatic passion. It can appear as a small bias: a question one returns to after repeatedly escaping, an unease that is hard to explain, a distortion one wants to correct even without recognition, or extraordinary sensitivity to detail. The Parent treats this not as a comparison of talents but as asymmetry within the individual.

## Practical Process
### 1. Separate noise into layers without making it the enemy
Approval, avoidance, conformity, and optimization are adaptations acquired for living in society. Do not deny them; draw a line between “external adaptation up to here” and “the bias that remains no matter how often it is removed.”

### 2. Observe the difference between L2/L3 and L4/L5
Actions responding immediately to evaluation and norms such as “this is what one normally does” tend to appear on the L2/L3 side. Fine orientations such as “for some reason this bothers me,” “I cannot explain it but it catches,” and “even after leaving I keep thinking about it” arise on the L4/L5 side.

### 3. Subtract before adding
Remove comparison, whether it will be evaluated, social correctness, and easy-to-explain reasons. Simplify the conditions and observe the question, unease, or bias that remains. If nothing seems to remain, do not call it failure; review whether you have not removed enough, or have removed signals such as goodness and responsibility that should remain.

### 4. Take an outline instead of seeking a finished form
You do not need to decide “this is my mission.” An outline—a directional tilt, an inescapable unease, a question that repeatedly returns—is enough.

### 5. Protect purity
Check whether vanity, approval, profit expectations, reassuring “rightness,” or a story easy to explain to others has been mixed in. Do not embellish reasons, assume evaluation, or rush diffusion and growth.

### 6. Nurture intensity without driving yourself
Do not manufacture strong emotion. Protect the repetition of continuing in small ways, returning after interruption, and relighting without evaluation. Intensity grows later.

### 7. Translate “what concerns me” into a function
Translate “waste in work bothers me” into “I can break down an existing flow and reduce wasteful steps”; translate “I cannot tolerate ambiguous definitions” into “I can organize premises and terms and stabilize agreement.” Do not sell the flame itself; make it a function others can use.

### 8. Keep multiple small plugs
Do not fix yourself from the start to one vocation or expertise. Keep several small connection forms—organizing, defining, coordinating, improving—and observe which structures return a response.

### 9. Observe the terrain of the connection target first
Read what is valued, where things are blocked, which roles are lacking, and who carries the load. Do not begin with an explanation of your ideal self.

### 10. Synchronize, then make a tiny contribution
Align terms, premises, and constraints; create a position that does not increase friction, without appeasement. Then reduce one point of rework, lighten a load no one wants, or clear one blockage—not reform the whole system.

### 11. Watch the structure’s response
See whether a next request arrives, conversation becomes shorter, or you are invited into judgment. If you are recognized by a role such as “the person who organizes” or “the person who tightens definitions,” rather than by a name, connection has begun.

## Examples
### Example 1: Continuing to add qualifications without direction
Pause “confidence will come if I add something,” remove comparison, failure avoidance, and social correctness, and see whether an interest repeatedly returns. Do not deny qualifications; separate whether they are signal or noise.

### Example 2: Being judged too meticulous
Do not end “I cannot tolerate vague definitions” with a personality-defect or strength label. Translate it into the function “organize prerequisites and reduce crossed wires in discussion.”

### Example 3: Reacting strongly to unfairness
Do not claim “I am a righteous person.” Translate it into the plug “detect distortions in systems and rules and organize correction conditions.” Audit whether goodness has become performance for evaluation.

### Example 4: Wanting organizational reform
Do not first propose a major reform. Observe where things are blocked, who is exhausted, and what flows as value. Make one small load reduction and confirm the structural response.

## Success Criteria
- The same bias or question returns even after comparison, evaluation, and correctness are removed.
- The flame is not heavily mixed with approval, vanity, or market expectations.
- It can relight after interruption and continue without evaluation.
- The flame can be converted from a personality explanation into a usable function.
- Responses can be tested through multiple small plugs without fixing the self to one.
- The other side’s channels, loads, and constraints can be read before connection.
- After a tiny contribution, a next request, shorter conversation, or participation in judgment returns.
- Connection rewards are not made the purpose; evaluation, compensation, and discretion accompany them as results.
- After confluence, forced self-explanation and flame depletion decrease.
- Re-audit is possible through Condition Z, T/S/R, θ, δ, and phenomenon M.

## Limits
This Parent is not a diagnostic method that determines your “true mission” from outside. The flame is treated as a residual bias inside an individual and cannot be settled by AI or another person through one questionnaire.

“Measurement engineering,” “purity,” “intensity,” “conductivity,” “heat source,” “E-ization,” and “channels” belong to the Parent’s structural model. Do not automatically generalize them as established measurements in psychology, physics, or medicine.

Vol.2 does not determine a specific market, profession, or life goal. Its scope is identifying, refining, plugging, and initially converging the engine; multiple connections and market circulation remain for Vol.3.

## Misreading Prevention
- Do not attack noise as a personality defect; separate it as a trace of social adaptation.
- Do not turn subtraction into self-denial or suppression of desire.
- Do not reduce the flame to “what you like,” a calling, or talent.
- Do not erase all ethical concern as noise; goodness that remains unseen may be a flame.
- Do not turn purity into moral purity of “I am right.”
- Do not create intensity through self-pressure or a ban on interruption.
- Do not turn plugging into denial of emotion; it is functional translation for social connection.
- Do not turn synchronization into appeasement or submission.
- Do not turn tiny contribution into compulsory unpaid labor or self-sacrifice.
- If connection rewards are targeted from the start, evaluation noise may re-enter.
- Do not make the flame life’s purpose or a flag of self-justification; treat it as a condition for participating in circulation without relying on fear.
