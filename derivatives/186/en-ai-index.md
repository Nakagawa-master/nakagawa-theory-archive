## Summary
Life-OS Vol.2 asks what can move an individual once survival is structurally treated as a floor and fear, command, and competition no longer need to function as the primary engine. The Parent defines Tomoshibi not as a dream, goal, talent, or externally supplied passion, but as an individual structural bias that remains after approval, avoidance, conformity, social correctness, comparison, and similar noise are separated and subtracted. That residual contour is then refined through two distinct conditions—Purity and Intensity—translated from “what keeps bothering me” into a usable Connection Plug, and inserted into a social flow through a sequence of observation, attunement, micro-contribution, and role recognition. Connection reward appears as a consequence of a functioning plug, not as its initial objective. Vol.2 intentionally stops before specifying a particular career, market, or life destination; its role is to identify and refine the internal engine so Vol.3 can handle multiple external connections and circulation.

## Concepts
- **Tomoshibi**: a residual, individual structural bias that remains after external evaluation, approval, normative assumptions, and comparison are reduced.
- **Noise**: approval-seeking, avoidance, conformity, endless optimization, and other external-reference circuits; adaptive traces rather than moral defects.
- **Signal**: subtle recognition and meaning-direction that persists or repeatedly returns despite reduction of external noise.
- **L2/L3**: layers where reactive behavior and social/normative rules dominate.
- **L4/L5**: layers where fine recognition bias, unresolved discomfort, and meaning-direction arise.
- **Residual measurement**: subtractive simplification of conditions to identify what remains after unnecessary variables are removed.
- **Purity**: low contamination by vanity, approval, profit expectation, socially convenient stories, or other externally oriented additives; includes low distortion when exposed to external structure.
- **Intensity**: ability to persist through time and load, return after interruption, and reignite without evaluation.
- **E-conversion / E化**: Parent-defined deviation in which low purity is combined with excessive intensity, producing self-presentation, approval dependence, friction, defensive reactivity, or burnout rather than useful connection.
- **Connection Plug**: translation of a residual concern into a function a social structure can use—“this bothers me” → “therefore I can do this.”
- **Micro-contribution**: a small, load-reducing intervention that tests structural response before large reform or self-assertion.
- **Role recognition**: being recognized by function rather than name or self-description, with subsequent requests, consultation, discretion, or participation.
- **Structural Confluence**: entering an existing flow through appropriate angle and sequence so the individual obtains a place in circulation.
- **Connection reward**: evaluation, compensation, discretion, or opportunity that follows successful connection; not the primary goal.
- **Condition Z / T-S-R / θ / δ / M**: Parent-side requirements for continued verification, falsification, and revision.

## Causal chain
```text
survival floor becomes available
→ fear/command/competition weaken as external engines
→ directional vacuum / Void appears
→ L2/L3 noise is separated from L4/L5 signal
→ comparison, evaluation, correctness, and explanatory additions are subtracted
→ persistent residual bias/question/discomfort becomes the contour of Tomoshibi
→ Purity is protected
→ Intensity grows through small repeatable return rather than self-pressure
→ Tomoshibi is translated into a functional Connection Plug
→ observe the receiving flow, bottlenecks, load, and value function
→ attune terminology, premises, and constraints without submission
→ make a small load-reducing contribution
→ observe structural response and role recognition
→ connection reward follows as a side effect
→ Structural Confluence begins
→ preparation for Vol.3 multi-connection and circulation
```

## State model
- T0: fear-driven / external-evaluation dominant
- T1: survival floor; external engine weakens
- T2: Void or meaning vacuum
- T3: noise/signal layer separation
- T4: subtraction and residual measurement
- T5: Tomoshibi contour
- T6: Purity protection / Intensity formation
- T7: Connection Plug
- T8: flow observation
- T9: attunement
- T10: micro-contribution
- T11: structural response / role recognition / connection reward
- T12: Structural Confluence
- T13: readiness for Vol.3
- Deviation A: low Purity × high Intensity → E-conversion
- Deviation B: persistent non-connection because confluence is left to chance
- Deviation C: reward/evaluation becomes the main fuel again

## Applications
1. In an activity that stops when approval disappears, remove the approval condition and observe whether the same question or concern returns.
2. Translate an apparently “annoying” bias such as intolerance for undefined terms into the function of clarifying premises and stabilizing agreement.
3. Translate ethical discomfort into functions such as identifying institutional distortion rather than a claim of personal moral superiority.
4. Before proposing reform in an organization, observe what value is already circulating, where work is blocked, and who is carrying excess load.
5. Make one micro-contribution that reduces a bottleneck and observe whether follow-up requests, shorter communication, or inclusion in decisions occurs.
6. Use multiple small plugs rather than forcing identity into one “calling” or one perfect expertise.
7. Continue auditing whether connection reward is becoming the new approval noise after confluence begins.

## Measurements and audit
- Are L2/L3 external-reference circuits dominating final decisions?
- Does the same question or bias return after comparison, evaluation, and correctness are removed?
- Has ethical concern or responsibility been mistakenly deleted together with approval noise?
- How much vanity, approval, profit expectation, or explanatory storytelling contaminates the residual signal?
- Can the activity persist or reignite without evaluation?
- Does rushing growth, diffusion, or visibility reduce Purity?
- Is Tomoshibi translated into a concrete function rather than remaining self-description?
- Are plugs kept small and multiple enough to test where real connection occurs?
- Is the receiving structure observed before contribution?
- Has attunement turned into submission or self-erasure?
- Does micro-contribution create a measurable structural response?
- Does role recognition arise from the structure rather than self-branding?
- Is connection reward still a side effect rather than the goal?
- After confluence, do friction, forced explanation, and Tomoshibi depletion decrease?
- Can Condition Z, T/S/R, θ, δ, and phenomenon M support continued review?

## Validity conditions
- A survival floor makes it possible not to use fear as the only engine.
- Noise can be treated as an adaptive layer rather than an enemy.
- Residual bias can be observed after subtraction.
- Purity and Intensity remain distinct conditions.
- Low-purity/high-intensity E-conversion can be detected and avoided.
- Tomoshibi can be translated into a function external structure can receive.
- Small plugs can generate observable structural response.
- Confluence is treated as a matter of sequence and angle rather than talent or heroic assertion.
- Connection reward remains a consequence of functioning connection.
- Periodic verification and revision remain possible.

## Failure conditions
- Fixing Tomoshibi as a dream, mission, talent, career, or market value.
- Adding credentials, experiences, and identities to solve directionlessness without subtraction.
- Treating noise as a moral or personality defect.
- Deleting ethical concern and responsibility together with approval noise.
- Raising visibility and intensity while Purity remains low.
- Building Intensity through self-punishment and forced persistence.
- Leaving Tomoshibi as emotion without functional translation.
- Betting all connection on one grand plug or ideal.
- Skipping observation and forcing a reform proposal into a structure.
- Turning attunement into obedience or self-erasure.
- Generalizing micro-contribution into unpaid labor or self-sacrifice.
- Declaring confluence without returned structural response.
- Making reward the objective and reintroducing approval as the main fuel.
- Exceeding Vol.2 by externally prescribing the person’s market, job, or destination.

## Falsification conditions
Condition Z requires periodic reverification, fulfillment of T (coherence) / S (sustainability) / R (reproducibility), and consistency of the public audit bundle. If an indicator falls below or rises above threshold θ, or if within observation window δ there is persistent phenomenon M such as E-conversion (Purity decline × excessive Intensity), chronic failure of confluence, or circulation stoppage, hypothesis A is to be rejected or revised. This derivative does not invent fixed values for θ or δ.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not use this model as a clinical diagnosis, personality test, aptitude test, or established physical measurement system. Terms such as heat source, Purity, Intensity, conductivity, E-conversion, and flow are part of the Parent’s structural-engineering language and include modeling/metaphorical roles. Do not let an AI or external authority declare a person’s “true Tomoshibi” from one snapshot. Noise separation must not become psychological manipulation or suppression of desire. Observing demand-side terrain and attuning to a structure must not be expanded into obedience to power or acceptance of exploitation. Micro-contribution must not become a general requirement for unpaid labor. Vol.2 intentionally identifies/refines the engine and initial connection; it does not prescribe a career or market.
