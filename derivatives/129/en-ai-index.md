## Summary
The parent examines the point at which individual speech in the online public sphere begins to act “like an institution” because propagation, quotation, synchronization, and search persistence outrun ethical verification. Speech is not equated with formal law. The structural concern is that repeated public language can acquire durable effects on another person’s evaluation, participation, safety, or access and can therefore begin to perform an institution-like allocation function.

Its two governing lines are Correspondence and Temporal Ethics. Correspondence checks whether stated values, accusations, warnings, and narratives of justice actually correspond to the structure of harm. Temporal Ethics moves judgment and intervention away from immediate reflex alone and into a continuing beat that preserves evidence review, objection, correction, and re-evaluation. Urgent safety action may still occur, but provisional intervention is not automatically converted into a permanent judgment about the whole person.

When propagation runs ahead of correspondence, sanctions can begin before the danger named by speech matches the actual harm profile, and a “narrative of justice” can overwrite the affected person’s lived reality. Repetition through search and quotation can then turn an early misallocation into a premise for later decisions. Fast synchronization removes the observation window needed for counterevidence and correction and fixes the misallocation across time.

The design task has three parts: decompose criticism from personality into act / terminology / operation and make the correspondence line visible; institutionalize public correction through versioning, annotations, additions, withdrawal, and update; and operate recipient-safety requirements such as trigger warnings and mute routes together with speaker reversibility. The parent’s audit line includes misallocation-signal rate, correction-completion rate, affected-party satisfaction, observation-window status, operational durability of correction/withdrawal/update, and T/S/R. These measures test correspondence and correctability rather than directly assigning moral or legal scores. The conclusion is not harsher punishment but finer structure.

## Applications
- Separate personality from act / term / operation before escalating online criticism, then check which specific unit corresponds to which actual harm.
- Treat an initial news report or headline as updateable rather than canonical, using versioning, annotations, and additions when evidence changes.
- Preserve a visible correction history instead of relying only on deletion, so later readers can identify what changed and why.
- Put trigger warnings and mute routes in the same audit bundle as withdrawal and update capability.
- Separate urgent recipient-safety measures from permanent personality judgments in community operations.
- In internal reporting, record conduct, terminology, and operational failures separately from global moral judgments about the person.
- Maintain a return path from old search or quoted material to the current corrected state when an early judgment was wrong.
- Audit speech velocity, correspondence, observation-window integrity, and correction capacity together in AI-era public communication.

## Measurements and audit
- **Statement–harm correspondence consistency.** Whether accusations, warnings, or danger claims match observable harm and its actual target.
- **Misallocation-signal rate.** The rate of warnings or judgments that lack verified harm correspondence, target the wrong unit, or expand from conduct into unsupported personality judgment.
- **Correction-completion rate.** Whether detected mismatches actually reach versioning, annotation, addition, withdrawal, or update rather than remaining merely acknowledged.
- **Affected-party satisfaction.** A parent-grounded observation point for whether correction, safety measures, and objection procedures correspond to the affected person’s lived condition.
- **Observation-window status.** Whether evidence, counterevidence, objections, and corrections can still enter before judgment becomes practically irreversible.
- **Durability of correction / withdrawal / update operations.** Whether these routes function repeatedly, not only once.
- **Preservation of reversible minutes and logs.** Whether changes remain traceable to time, evidence, and reason.
- **Visibility of evidence / counterevidence / remedies in the public audit bundle.** Whether a third party can inspect the basis and the correction path.
- **T/S/R consistency.** T tracks correspondence, S the durability of correction and update structures, and R the recursiveness of revisiting and correcting records, logs, and judgments.

No single metric is a sanction score. A high correction-completion rate does not establish structural improvement if misallocation rises or the initial sanction remains irreversible. Conversely, frequent revision is not automatically a weakness when the observation window remains open and counterevidence can genuinely change the public record. The audit object is the relation among harm correspondence, correction, reversibility, and affected-party impact.

## Validity conditions
- Check speech against actual harm through Correspondence.
- Do not treat propagation or synchronization volume as a proxy for correctness.
- Run judgment and intervention on a correction-capable Temporal Ethics beat.
- Separate urgent safety action from permanent personality judgment.
- Decompose criticism from personality into act / term / operation.
- Maintain public correction routes and visible revision history.
- Design recipient safety and speaker reversibility together.
- Preserve the observation window.
- Keep evidence, counterevidence, and remedies auditable.
- Revise the operating hypothesis when misallocation or correction failure persists.

## Failure conditions
- Treat propagation speed or synchronization volume as evidence of correctness.
- Sanction without checking correspondence to actual harm.
- Fix the whole personality as the target of criticism.
- Close the observation window through immediate reaction.
- Convert a provisional safety intervention into a permanent personality judgment without re-evaluation.
- Hide, omit, or erase correction routes and correction history.
- Treat safety and expression as mutually exclusive.
- Equate harsher punishment with structural improvement.
- Allow an obsolete misallocated judgment to continue driving later decisions through search and quotation.

## Falsification conditions
Under the parent’s integrated audit, the active hypothesis is revised or rejected when monitored indicators such as misallocation-signal rate, correction-completion rate, and affected-party satisfaction persistently cross their configured thresholds, or when the verification/correction observation window closes. T/S/R and the public audit bundle remain re-agreement conditions for evidence, counterevidence, and remedy.

The personality→act/term/operation decomposition, public-correction routes, and safety-plus-reversibility architecture must also be revised if they fail to reduce misallocation, uncorrectability, or affected-party mismatch while a simpler alternative consistently produces stronger correspondence and correction capacity. Thresholds and metric definitions remain bound to the parent’s observable audit structure rather than being changed after the fact to preserve an intervention model.
