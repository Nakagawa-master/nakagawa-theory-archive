## Summary
Future-Definition Verification Effort Theory Vol. 2 does not reject effort, ambitious targets, KPIs, or exploratory action. It analyzes a bad causal structure that appears when a target value is mistaken for an established future definition and continued execution is demanded before establishment conditions, the causal path, verification conditions, correction conditions, and responsibility placement have been sufficiently examined upstream.

A target value is an intended future endpoint. A future definition is broader: it specifies how that endpoint could become established from the present structure, including resource, time, actor, environmental, institutional, and authority conditions; a causal hypothesis from present action to future outcome; what execution is supposed to verify; when the hypothesis or method should be revised; and how design, resource, execution, and evaluation responsibility are allocated. Setting an endpoint alone does not establish a future definition.

Omitted establishment testing does not disappear. It can be transferred downward. The field may have to interpret an ambiguous target, compensate for missing resources, discover the causal path, negotiate missing authority, improvise around design gaps, run experiments, and explain failure while still being evaluated for execution. The field then carries both execution cost and establishment-testing cost.

When outcomes fail and the organization does not re-test design, assumptions, resources, conditions, or evaluation criteria, but instead compresses the explanation into "insufficient effort," upstream uncertainty becomes less visible. Exhaustion, distrust, and silence can accumulate; observations and objections stop returning to design; failure no longer updates hypotheses; learning can stop; and new challenges can become rationally avoidable because experimentation produces liability without a reliable correction path. In that state, an effort demand can reverse from a mechanism for making a future real into a device that transfers unverified design responsibility downward.

The theory does not erase execution responsibility. It requires design and execution responsibility to remain distinguishable and jointly testable. It also does not demand complete certainty before action. Under uncertainty, action can be used as a test, provided the test target, observation criteria, correction conditions, and responsibility for the exploratory design are explicit.

The parent does not define a universal insufficient-effort rate, exhaustion rate, success probability, establishment-testing score, or withdrawal threshold. Reversal evaluation remains structural: if explicit establishment conditions, causal hypotheses, correction rules, and responsibility placement do not alter the exhaustion/responsibility-transfer/learning-shutdown structure, the application hypothesis weakens; if those elements are absent yet trust, learning, objection, and challenge persist broadly and durably, the theory's scope is also open to reconsideration.

## Applications
**Revenue and KPI targets.** A reading that would treat a number as a completed future definition differs from the parent-defined scope. Audit customer structure, supply capacity, resources, channels, pricing, authority, causal assumptions, and correction conditions. If every miss produces only a higher activity target, ask what the activity is actually testing.

**Education and development.** A desired achievement level can be ambitious without being illegitimate. The audit asks whether prior knowledge, learning time, materials, support, assessment, and revision conditions were designed, or whether the student's effort is expected to absorb every missing condition.

**Production and projects.** Deadlines and quality requirements can be valid, but if staffing, authority, dependencies, review time, and change conditions are missing, the field may be forced to absorb design gaps through improvisation. Valuable improvisation should not be confused with permanent transfer of establishment-testing responsibility.

**New ventures.** High uncertainty does not require knowing all conditions in advance. It does require specifying what is being hypothesized, what each experiment is meant to distinguish, what observation changes the next step, and who owns the design of the exploration. Uncertainty is not a license for unlimited effort demands.

**AI adoption.** Counts of users, generations, or deployed teams are target values, not necessarily future definitions. Audit which work mechanism is meant to change, what resources and workflow conditions are necessary, and what is re-tested when expected changes do not occur. A reading that would assume AI makes causality fully visible differs from the parent-defined scope.

**Research and experimentation.** Experiment count is not sufficient. Each experiment should discriminate among hypotheses or modify the next search space. If failure only leads to "run more experiments" without changing assumptions, the learning circuit can collapse into effort repetition.

**Personal goals.** The same structure can occur without a manager. A person can set a high goal while ignoring sleep, time, environment, resources, or causal assumptions and then reduce every failure to willpower. The theory applies to the structure of self-design, not only to organizational hierarchy.

## Measurements and audit
The parent defines no universal numeric management metric. A reading that would invent an "effort insufficiency rate," "exhaustion percentage," "future-definition completeness score," success probability, or withdrawal threshold differs from the parent-defined scope. The measurement object is the location and direction of structural conditions and costs.

Audit whether target value and future definition are explicitly distinguished; whether resource, time, actor, environment, institutional, and authority conditions are visible; whether the path from action to outcome is stated as a revisable causal hypothesis; whether each execution step has a verification object; whether success, failure, and provisional outcomes can be distinguished; and whether methods, resources, assumptions, goals, or evaluation criteria have correction or exit conditions.

Audit responsibility separately. Is design responsibility distinguishable from resource-provision responsibility, execution responsibility, and evaluation responsibility? Does the field silently compensate for ambiguous targets, resource gaps, missing authority, or causal uncertainty? Where do the time, attention, experimentation, coordination, and explanatory costs of establishment testing actually sit?

When results fail, inspect whether design, hypotheses, resources, conditions, and evaluation axes are also re-opened or whether the field alone is evaluated. When exhaustion, distrust, or silence appears, inspect whether those observations return to design or are treated only as attitude problems. When failure occurs, inspect whether it changes the next hypothesis. When challenge-taking declines, inspect whether experiments have become one-way responsibility risks.

Reversal evaluation is source-bounded. If explicit conditions, causal hypotheses, correction paths, and responsibility allocation are introduced yet the same exhaustion, responsibility transfer, and learning shutdown persist, the theory's connection hypothesis weakens. Conversely, if such structures remain absent while trust, learning, dissent, and experimentation persist broadly and durably, the presumed causal link must be limited. No derivative-created numerical cutoff is added to these tests.

## Validity conditions
- Target value and future definition are distinguished.
- Establishment conditions are examined and shared upstream.
- The causal line is stated as a revisable hypothesis.
- Execution has an explicit verification object.
- Success, failure, and provisional results have interpretable criteria.
- Correction and exit conditions exist for method, resources, assumptions, goals, or evaluation axes.
- Design and execution responsibility remain distinct.
- Resource-provision and evaluation responsibility are not compressed into effort volume.
- When outcomes fail, design, assumptions, conditions, resources, and evaluation criteria can be re-tested.
- Field observations, objections, and failure information return to subsequent design.
- Effort demands are positioned as testable execution rather than unlimited continuation.
- Uncertainty remains allowed, but the way uncertainty is tested is designed.

## Failure conditions
- A target value alone is treated as an established future definition.
- Establishment conditions are not examined and resource or institutional gaps are absorbed by field effort.
- Activity volume is specified while the verification object of the activity is undefined.
- A causal path is missing and each miss only increases the same effort demand.
- There are no correction or exit conditions.
- Design is exempted from verification while executors alone carry outcome responsibility.
- The field simultaneously carries execution, condition compensation, causal exploration, experimentation, and failure explanation.
- Exhaustion, distrust, and silence are processed only as attitude problems.
- Failure information does not update subsequent hypotheses.
- Challenge avoidance becomes rational while the design remains unchanged.

## Falsification conditions
The parent origin's integrated audit summary states: If causal lines, validity conditions, and verification conditions are explicit, frontline actors have verification authority and revision conditions, and failed outcomes trigger examination of hypotheses, resources, time, and actor conditions rather than effort volume, hypothesis A is rejected or revised.
