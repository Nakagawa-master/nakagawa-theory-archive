## Summary
The Parent redefines sales from “selling” to structural translation: discover needs and causal gaps that the other party may not yet have articulated, connect present conditions to a desired future, and position an offering as a value path. The desired decision emerges from understanding rather than pressure.

## Causal model
```text
surface request
-> question
-> latent need
-> causal clarification
-> present/future mapping
-> value connection
-> customer understanding
-> self-directed decision
-> value exchange
```

## Distinctions
sales != persuasion; questioning != interrogation; structural translation != inventing problems; emergent decision != manipulation; fulfilling value != free service.

## Validity
Requires hypothesis checking, use of the customer's own language, non-coercive choice, and post-contract verification. Fails when product pitch, pressure, or sales volume replaces causal understanding.

Use the Parent for the canonical redefinition of sales, questioning, resonance, and structural translation.
