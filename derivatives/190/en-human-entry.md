## 15-Second Explanation
C-series Vol.2 is an **implementation theory for moving from hunting-style sales—“chasing, persuading, and comparing in order to sell”—to attraction-style connection, in which sufficient C is accumulated, L7 is emitted as an undistorted Signal, and only compatible actors naturally approach.** It sets the minimum condition for gravity as `F = Mass × Purity` and treats Signaling, Filtering, Quarantine, Event Horizon, Price, continuous new C supply, insiders’ C, immunity against E-formation, and the Gravity Master as one continuous design.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Vol.1 made value a vector with an L7 direction rather than a scalar of price alone, and described aligned actors forming a market through Resonance. Vol.2 addresses how to bring that theory into real connections.

Traditional sales and marketing depend on the probability that broad gathering leaves some people and that more contacts create more closes. The Parent calls this “hunting,” recognizing that it worked when information was closed and choices were few. Under transparency and too many choices, people tire of being chased or forced to choose, and persuasion and comparison friction grow.

The connection principle therefore changes: create the conditions in which gravity arises on your side instead of chasing the other party. The minimum model is `F = Mass × Purity`. Mass is the accumulated C that can be provided—expertise, reproducible technique, experience, theory, and judgment. Purity is the low mixture of flattery, appeasement, trend-following, and excessive profit-seeking that distorts L7.

Gravity alone is invisible from outside, so Signaling makes explicit what one believes, provides, connects with, and refuses to connect with. A strong Signal does not gather everyone; it attracts compatible actors and lets incompatible actors leave early. Filtering then reduces explanation, doubt, and friction after entry.

The boundary where an outsider becomes an insider is designed as an Event Horizon, with Price as its final boundary. Price is not merely cost or market payment but a declaration of resolve, sovereignty, and boundary: “I take this position toward this L7 and C.”

## Practical Process
**1. Diagnose hunting dependence.** Check dependence on contact volume, follow-up, long explanations, comparison, and discounts; inspect the connection principle rather than only increasing rhetoric or advertising.

**2. Inventory Mass.** Confirm accumulated expertise, reproducible technique, practical experience, failures and verification, thought systems, and hard-to-substitute judgment that can act on other structures. Do not substitute followers or exposure.

**3. Inspect Purity.** Check whether saying something to sell, diluting it to avoid dislike, or following a trend distorts L7. Purity is directional consistency between Signal and C, not moral goodness.

**4. Make Signal explicit.** Clarify belief, provision, fit, non-fit, and protected boundaries; avoid “optimal for everyone.”

**5. Filter and quarantine before connection.** Observe values, behavior, rule acceptance, responsibility to the space, and willingness to contribute C. This checks compatibility with a Gravity Zone, not human superiority.

**6. Design Event Horizon.** Clarify changes in information, responsibility, decisions, and contribution between outside and inside. Crossing must not depend only on payment, but also on an act, rule, or responsibility fixing one’s position.

**7. Place Price as a boundary.** Treat it as a declaration of where L7 and C are fixed, and observe how discounts affect Purity, expectations, resolve, and internal structure.

**8. Keep producing new C.** Supply new views, questions, hypotheses, and practices; sustain Resonance through renewal, not headcount.

**9. Make insiders produce C.** Through knowledge sharing, feedback, practice reports, and bridging, make both sides contributors rather than creating a one-way receiving relationship.

**10. Observe cross-connections and immunity.** Check whether insiders connect without the center and circulation continues if it weakens. Treat E as structural mismatch that exits, not as a reason for anger or preaching.

## Examples of Application
**1. Prospect-chasing sales.** Before adding explanations and discounts for an unresponsive person, check Mass, Purity, Signal, and audience boundaries; more hunting alone leaves the upstream cause.

**2. Strong Signal.** Replace “we can do anything” with the problem solved, its L7 direction, and conditions for connection. Departures are not automatically failure.

**3. Community boundary.** Payment alone does not make an insider. Clarify rules, roles, responsibility, returned C, and the relationship change after joining.

**4. Price and fear.** Do not collapse Price merely because refusal is possible; examine effects on C, L7, boundaries, and Purity. This does not mean “higher is always better.”

**5. Maintenance by renewal.** A stable membership or sales figure can conceal decay when no new questions or practices appear and insiders only receive.

**6. E as immunity.** When others only consume or devalue C, or keep receiving without giving, release the connection structurally instead of condemning people.

## Success Criteria
Compatible actors approach voluntarily after seeing the Signal and incompatible actors leave early without mass contact, pursuit, or long explanations; explanation cost and doubt fall.

Mass grows as accumulated C rather than exposure, and Purity protects L7 direction.

After Filtering, values, rules, responsibility, and C output align without becoming human ranking or status formation.

Event-Horizon roles are clear, and Price, responsibility, information access, and decision participation form a coherent boundary rather than mere sales maximization.

New C enters the Gravity Zone, insiders cross-connect, and circulation continues without the center; E signs are handled as immunity rather than anger or condemnation. Gravity Master is a standing gravity source, not a central authority.

## Limits
`F = Mass × Purity` is the Parent’s minimum model, not a natural-science measurement or sales forecast; this volume fixes no common units or measured coefficients.

The Parent strongly discusses high Price, discount danger, and quantifying resolve, but the derivative does not generalize this unconditionally to every product, market, public service, or essential domain, nor turn it into “higher is always correct.”

Filtering and Quarantine check alignment with a particular Gravity Zone; they do not rank human value, income, ability, or character. Event Horizon’s irreversible choices do not justify coercive confinement or inability to exit.

This implementation theory is not a universal demand that all organizations use identical Signal, Filter, Price, or rites. Keep it within the Parent’s gravity-zone and audit/falsification conditions.

## Preventing Misreading
Do not replace Mass with followers, titles, advertising, or funding; it is expertise, technique, experience, thought, and judgment accumulated as C.

Do not make Purity goodness or ideological purity; it concerns distortion of L7 and C by flattery, appeasement, and trend-following.

Do not turn Signaling into hype, controversy, or universal appeal; it clarifies direction, boundaries, targets, and non-targets.

Do not turn Filtering, Quarantine, or discharge into discrimination, status, enemy designation, or character condemnation; it is only a connection judgment for this Gravity Zone.

Do not make Price mean “higher is better” or “those unable to pay are unqualified.” Do not turn insiders’ C into unpaid labor or exploitation.

Do not turn Gravity Master into a charismatic leader, priest, authority, or ruler; it is the posture of fixing L7 and standing without distorting, diluting, or fleeing.
