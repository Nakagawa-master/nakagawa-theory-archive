## Summary
C-series Vol.2 turns the general Resonance Market theory of Vol.1 into an implementation protocol. It diagnoses the conventional Hunting / Sales OS as a probabilistic model: contact many people, pursue them, explain, compare, persuade, discount, and convert a small subset. The parent argues that this model loses effectiveness as information becomes transparent and alternatives multiply, because people become resistant to being chased and forced to choose under persuasive pressure.

The implementation alternative is attraction through engineered market gravity. The parent states a minimal source model, `F = Mass × Purity`. Mass is the accumulated volume of contribution C compressed into long-term expertise, reproducible techniques, practical experience, theory, and difficult-to-substitute judgment. Purity is the degree to which the actor's L7 direction is not distorted by flattery, conformity, trend-following, or excessive money-seeking motives. The model requires both; mass without purity becomes weak or repellent, while purity without mass lacks force.

Gravity must be externally legible to create connection, so Signaling makes L7, contribution C, target, non-target, and protected boundaries explicit. Strong signals are not designed for universal approval: they attract aligned actors and cause misaligned actors to leave early. Filtering and quarantine then protect the internal field before entry. The parent next designs an Event Horizon where the relationship changes qualitatively from outsider to insider, with rites of passage that confirm self-position, rules, responsibility, and commitment. Price is presented not merely as consideration but as a numerical boundary that fixes L7, resolve, sovereignty, and inside/outside position.

A gravity zone is not a reservoir. It must receive new C continuously as a living river. Insiders also contribute C through knowledge, feedback, practice, and bridges. When insiders connect horizontally without depending on the center, the field becomes a network and circulation can continue even if the center weakens. E-signals such as receiving only, devaluing contribution, or consuming others' C are treated as circulation failures and handled through structural immunity rather than anger or moral condemnation. The conclusion moves from selling to the Gravity Master: a subject who fixes L7 at one point and stands without distorting, thinning, or escaping it, allowing existence and influence rather than pursuit to generate attraction.

## Concepts
- **Hunting / Sales OS**: probabilistic connection through mass outreach, pursuit, persuasion, comparison, and conversion.
- **Attraction / Signaling**: connection generated by making one's own direction clear so aligned actors approach voluntarily.
- **F = Mass × Purity**: the parent's minimal market-gravity model; not an empirically calibrated physics equation.
- **Mass**: accumulated contribution C in expertise, technology, experience, theory, and judgment.
- **Purity**: low distortion of L7 by flattery, conformity, trend-following, or excessive money-first motives; not moral purity.
- **Signal**: external declaration of L7, contribution, target, non-target, and boundary.
- **Filtering**: early separation of aligned and misaligned connection possibilities through clear signals.
- **Quarantine**: pre-entry inspection of L7, behavior, rules, responsibility, and willingness to contribute C.
- **Event Horizon**: the boundary where outsider/insider self-position, responsibility, and relationship change qualitatively.
- **Rite of Passage**: a condition that makes the participant confirm position and commitment, not a ranking of human worth.
- **Price as Boundary**: price used as a numerical declaration of L7, resolve, sovereignty, and the inside/outside line.
- **Living Gravity Zone**: a flowing field continuously renewed by new C rather than a closed customer reservoir.
- **Member Contribution**: insiders are also contributors of C rather than passive recipients only.
- **Horizontal Connection**: direct insider-to-insider links that reduce dependence on the center.
- **E**: a circulation-failure state visible in one-way receiving, contribution devaluation, or consumption of others' C.
- **Structural Immunity**: removing E-like misalignment structurally rather than through anger, preaching, or condemnation.
- **Gravity Master**: a subject who fixes L7 clearly and keeps standing without distortion, dilution, or retreat.
- **Condition Z / T-S-R / θ / δ / M**: audit, maintenance, falsification, and revision conditions.

- Turning Filtering into discrimination, status ranking, or a human-worth score.
- Treating non-target actors as enemies or inferior people.
- Turning Event Horizon into coercive lock-in or denial of exit.
- Universalizing Price into “higher is always better.”

## Interpretation constraints
`F = Mass × Purity`, Gravity, Mass, Purity, and Event Horizon are explicit source concepts and must be preserved at source strength, but they are not promoted into empirically calibrated natural-science laws, guaranteed sales equations, or fixed probabilities of connection.

The parent makes strong prescriptions about Price, high price, discounting, resolve, and sovereignty. Those claims remain visible, but this derivative does not add new propositions such as “higher price is universally better,” “lower-income actors are unfit,” or “discounting is wrong in every market.” The scope remains the parent's L7, contribution, boundary, and gravity-zone implementation model.

Filtering, quarantine, and E-removal concern alignment with a specific gravity field and its circulation; they are not generalized into systems for ranking human dignity, ability, or worth. Likewise, Event Horizon is not converted into justification for coercive real-world lock-in. Gravity Master remains a posture of undistorted L7 rather than a status of authority.
