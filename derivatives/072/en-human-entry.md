## 15-Second Explanation
Connection adjudication design theory is an adjudication structure that, when disputes or deviations occur, stops harm, preserves evidence, makes proportionate judgments, and connects them to correction, withdrawal, renewed consent, compensation, and phased restoration without relying on outrage, authority, popularity, majorities, or permanent expulsion.

What matters is not making adjudication a mechanism for deciding who is “a bad person.” It separates provisional protection from final judgment, does not treat a person’s reputation or follower count as evidence, confirms consent, boundaries, primary evidence, evidence provenance, and conflicts of interest, and preserves a state in which a judgment can be reconsidered when new evidence appears.

## Why It Is Necessary
Even when an institution is carefully designed, forced connection, use outside consent, indicator gaming, log tampering, group pressure, and privacy violations can occur in actual operation. Without a formal processing route, victims are driven to public accusations, operators become self-protective, and those around them tend to judge by popularity or volume of voice.

Conversely, if only strong punitive authority is created, provisional measures can effectively become permanent expulsion, making misidentification or new evidence impossible to correct. To avoid these extremes, the Parent Original establishes legitimacy, proportionality, repair priority, and independence as four principles, and separates intake, provisional measures, investigation, adjudication, remedies, and restoration.

Repair priority does not mean asking the victim to reconcile or reconnect. If safety, deletion, distance, compensation, or authority restrictions are needed, they come first. Restoration is not automatic either; it proceeds in stages while confirming recurrence prevention, renewed consent, learning records, and limited observation.

## Practical Process
1. Accept a petition and fix the case ID, time, parties, consent status, boundary claims, and scope of impact.
2. Do not decide guilt or innocence at intake; prioritize evidence preservation and preventing harm from spreading.
3. Choose provisional measures that can be reviewed later, such as suspending connection, reducing authority, or limiting the publication scope.
4. Show the reasons, deadline, conditions for lifting, and objection route for provisional measures.
5. Organize primary evidence, secondary evidence, evidence provenance, omissions, and the possibility of tampering separately.
6. Check adjudicator conflicts of interest and, when necessary, recuse or delegate externally.
7. Separate public summaries from protected detailed records and maintain the boundary between openness and privacy.
8. Choose proportionate remedies from correction, withdrawal, renewed consent, compensation, education, and necessary authority restrictions.
9. Confirm recurrence prevention and renewed consent, then consider observation return, restricted return, or full return in stages.
10. If new evidence or a serious procedural violation appears, rehear the case and preserve the history of changes to the judgment.

## Examples of Application
**Use of information outside consent.** Temporarily suspend use and confirm the consent wording, purpose of use, recipients, storage status, and possibility of deletion. Combine correction, deletion, explanation, and necessary compensation, and limit authority until recurrence prevention is confirmed.

**Indicator gaming.** When connection counts or praise surge in a short period, investigate homogeneous connections, in-group evaluation, and brigading. Look not at popularity itself but at consent renewal, dissent handling, the possibility of exit, and primary logs.

**Tampering with structural logs.** Preserve original logs, signatures, timestamps, diffs, and access histories, and consider remedies proportionate to the scope and recurrence possibility of the impact.

**Group pressure.** Do not treat a large number of homogeneous petitions as proof that the majority is right; confirm connection relationships and temporal concentration. Combine the target’s safety, external observation, cooling-off, and reduced counterclaim costs.

**Restoration after a past violation.** Do not restore automatically based on an apology alone; confirm recurrence prevention, renewed consent, learning records, and limited observation. Do not force contact or reconciliation on the victim.

## Success Criteria
The Parent Original presents intake within 24 hours, provisional measures within 48 hours, and a primary adjudication within 14 days as operational guidelines for preventing secondary harm from delay. These are not universal legal deadlines or guarantees of outcomes; they are criteria for the operating body to observe processing delays in the adjudication institution.

Success is not determined by speed alone. Even if intake or adjudication is fast, the situation can worsen if evidence review, objections, independence, or proportionality is weak. A decrease in petitions is not success if petitions have become harder to file. An increase in restoration is not success if victim safety or recurrence prevention has weakened.

What should be observed is the relationship among whether harm can be stopped from spreading, whether primary evidence and provenance can be preserved, whether provisional measures can be reviewed, whether conflicts of interest can be avoided, whether correction, withdrawal, and renewed consent actually function, whether similar harm recurs, whether mistaken judgments can be corrected by rehearing, and whether public summaries increase re-identification or retaliation.

## Limits
Rapid provisional measures are necessary, but speed is not the same as correctness. Provisional measures to stop harm from spreading must be separated from a final adjudication that establishes responsibility.

Repair priority is not an exemption from responsibility. It does not prioritize relationship repair at the expense of victim safety, compensation, deletion, distance, or necessary authority restrictions.

Openness should not be increased unconditionally. Because an adjudication summary can re-identify a person and produce outrage or retaliation, boundaries such as anonymization, aggregation, delayed publication, and access restrictions are necessary.

AI may assist with organizing evidence, comparison, and anomaly detection, but final responsibility, explanation, objections, stopping, and rehearing remain with accountable people and institutions.

## Misreading Prevention
Do not turn adjudication into personality evaluation or a popularity vote. Majorities, titles, and follower counts do not prove the facts of a case, consent, boundaries, or harm.

Do not equate provisional measures with a finding of guilt. Do not equate restoration with forgetting harm. Do not equate repair priority with forcing reconciliation on the victim.

The 24-hour, 48-hour, and 14-day time standards are operational guidelines in the Parent Original’s institutional design. The numbers alone do not guarantee the correctness, safety, or remedial effect of an adjudication.
