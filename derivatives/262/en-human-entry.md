## 15-second explanation
Friction in the AI era does not arise simply because people dislike one another. It becomes dangerous when different values, rules, and methods of exchange meet at high speed, their differences are amplified by algorithms or organizational structures, and dialogue or governance lacks buffering. The solution is not to erase value differences, but to **suppress amplification, thicken buffering, and operate contact safely through thresholds, silence, and reversibility**.

## Why it becomes necessary
AI rapidly moves the boundaries of work, institutions, ethics, knowledge, evaluation, and values. The faster change becomes, the more often different ways of thinking come into contact.

If value differences are treated only as causes of conflict, the result tends to be suppressing dissent, forcing everyone to the same standard, or imposing agreement. Yet differences can also generate discovery and role differentiation. The original turns friction from destruction into learning by examining **what amplifies the difference and what buffering is missing**, rather than treating difference itself as the problem.

## Basic structure
The original treats friction through the following conceptual approximation.

```text
Friction F ≈ (Value difference ΔV × Amplification G) / Buffering B
```

The important point is not numerical calculation.

- The goal is not to make ΔV zero.
- When G is large, even a small difference can ignite.
- When B is thick, even a large difference can be handled without breaking.

Therefore, the main levers for designers are **amplification control and buffering design**, not eliminating difference.

## Three safety principles
### Threshold｜Threshold
A boundary that prevents immediate deep connection. Participation is staged, such as audience → participant → co-designer, and devices are placed to translate between expert and everyday language and between external institutions and internal context.

### Silence｜Silence
During escalation or overheating, do not pile on immediate reactions; secure cooling time. Edit issues asynchronously and separate public logs from internal editorial logs. Silence is a safety device that slows reaction, not concealment, but it requires a period, responsibility, and explanation afterward.

### Reversibility｜Reversibility
Design institutions and operations so they can be restored from the start. Time-limited agreements, re-review, withdrawal, data migration, and contract termination make failure a learning process rather than a catastrophe.

## Double syntax
Place laws, rules, public standards, accountability, and audits in **Public**.

Place trust, agreement histories, relationship-specific circumstances, tacit knowledge, emotional protection, and context still being edited in **Private**.

Mixing everything leaks sensitive material; separating everything makes the inside irresponsible. The two must therefore mutually refer to each other.

## Practical process
1. Separate which values and rules are colliding.
2. Identify amplification factor G.
3. Identify the dialogue, translation, role boundaries, and appeals missing as buffering B.
4. Stage participation.
5. Place translation devices at boundaries.
6. Decide the cooling window in advance.
7. Decide the purpose, period, and responsibility of silence.
8. Design rollback, withdrawal, and exit methods.
9. Try it for a limited period.
10. Re-review and confirm whether friction became learning.

## Examples of application
**AI adoption.** Do not treat advocates and cautious parties as a win-or-lose contest. Put law and security in Public, field concerns and hypotheses in Private, and provide a limited PoC with reversibility.

**Social-media flame wars.** Stop chains of rebuttal, provide a cooling window, and separate public explanation from internal review. Fulfill accountability after the period.

**Public institutions.** Provide staged participation, appeals, translation support, third-party review, and a right to exit so that the weaker side is not irreversibly drawn in.

## Success criteria
- Hostility decreases while value differences remain.
- Amplification factors can be identified as structure.
- Translation, dialogue, and appeals function.
- The cooling window is protected during escalation.
- An explanation is given after cooling.
- Rollback and exit can actually be used.
- Time-limited agreements are re-reviewed.
- Transparency does not turn into exposure or surveillance.

## Limits
This is not a design for eliminating friction completely. Also, F≈(ΔV×G)/B is an approximation for understanding social structure, not a universal formula of natural science. Specific thresholds, periods, and values differ with the conditions of the domain.

## Preventing misreading
- Do not regard value difference as evil.
- Do not turn taking the safer side into excluding opponents.
- Do not equate silence with concealment, but do not leave its transformation into concealment unchecked.
- Do not use reversibility to evade responsibility.
- Do not turn Public/Private into secrecy.
- Do not assert the approximation as a law of physics.
- Do not create fixed coefficients or universal thresholds absent from the Parent.
