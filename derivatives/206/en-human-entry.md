## 15-Second Explanation
NAOS is not a mechanism that turns the nation into one enormous managing entity. It is a **consensus-building OS that establishes understanding and choice before coercion**, converting problems involving multiple actors where “nobody can decide”—such as urban contraction, vacant homes, infrastructure, reconstruction, taxes, and benefits—into a form where figures, options, future burdens, and attribution of responsibility can be explained in the same Transaction Unit.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
At the national scale, ministries, municipalities, assemblies, courts, companies, finance, residents, and others are involved simultaneously. Distributed authority is not itself bad, but without a common structure explaining who takes on what and which choice creates which future burden, postponement, fragmentation, and local optimization become rational.

NAOS does not have the nation centrally determine a “correct answer”; it makes multiple proposals comparable, fixes attribution of responsibility, and increases the number of situations in which parties can understand and choose. Optimization also means not maximizing efficiency, but reducing regret arising from not knowing, not being able to compare, or not knowing who was responsible.

## Practical Process
1. **Break the problem into consensus-building units.** Put parties, subject, time, risk, burden, benefit, and attribution of responsibility into a Transaction Unit.
2. **Put multiple proposals in the same format.** Make future cost, time, benefit, risk, and who bears it comparable.
3. **Present options before coercion.** Put an explanation that parties can understand first.
4. **Separate responsibility.** AI is responsible for calculation, specialists for practice, and parties for decision-making.
5. **Fix the Core.** Do not change Transparency / Selectability / Responsibility / balance of tensions / deviation defense.
6. **Put institutional differences in Plugins.** Connect taxes, registration, justice, urban planning, ID and privacy, and financial systems by country.
7. **Retain deviations as differences.** Record deviations from design in the Deviation Ledger.
8. **Make the audit bundle publishable.** Maintain an Audit Bundle aligned with T/S/R.
9. **Protect rights conditions.** Maintain party access, reasons, appeals, and prohibition of out-of-purpose use.
10. **Rebuild consensus when conditions worsen.** Revise the hypothesis and specification if inexplicability, coercion-first behavior, or absence of responsibility increases.

## Examples of Application
**Urban contraction.** Compare options to retain, consolidate, or withdraw infrastructure by maintenance cost, renewal cost, time, resident burden, benefit, alternatives, and responsible party.

**Vacant homes.** Bundle inheritance, tax, repairs, demolition, neighborhood risk, emotions, and rights relationships into one Transaction Unit so that options other than neglect can be compared.

**Disaster reconstruction.** Set out restoration, relocation, and reallocation with compensation, future burden, time, and responsibility, making them comparable proposals rather than someone’s slogan.

**Overseas expansion.** Keep the consensus-building Core unchanged and connect that country’s taxes, registration, justice, privacy, and similar systems as Plugins.

## Success Criteria
- National-scale discussions become comparable options rather than slogans.
- Explanation and options are presented before coercion.
- The responsibilities of AI, specialists, and parties are separated.
- The Core is not absorbed into country-specific institutions.
- Postponement, inexplicability, and absence of responsibility decrease.
- Parties can access information in the same format.
- Reasons and appeals function.
- Audit Bundle / Deviation Ledger are maintained.

## Limitations
NAOS is not a theory for unifying national systems into one. Taxes, registration, justice, ID, finance, and other matters follow each country’s institutional boundaries. Increasing explainability also does not eliminate value conflicts themselves. The aim is not to remove conflict, but to move to a state where comparison, responsibility, and choice are possible.

At the national layer, data collection and surveillance can easily expand, so rights conditions that prevent deviation into one-way transparency, black-box decisions, out-of-purpose use, or attribute labeling are indispensable.

## Preventing Misreading
- “Optimization” does not mean maximizing national efficiency.
- “NAOS” is not national DX or smart-city management.
- “Transparency” is not surveillance in which only the state watches residents.
- “Global commonality” is not a globally common legal system.
- “National assets” should not be reduced to one national-wealth indicator.
- “Consensus-building” does not mean excluding opposing views and forcing agreement.
