## Summary
The parent original answers the Dark Equation `S=0.1C+0.9E` and its AI-accelerated chain of exploitation optimization, origin dissolution, contributor depletion, model collapse, and future debt by proposing an operating-system-level replacement of the success function itself: `S=C×1.0`. In the parent design, E=0 is a structural condition under which exploitative value capture is not converted into success, while C=1.0 is a structural condition reconnecting primary contribution with success and reward. The implementation environment links NCL-α for origin integrity, Temporal Ethics T0 for future-debt auditing, Connection Reward for continuing value return, the Deviation Ledger for recording structural deviations, and structural justice for consistency-centered dispute handling. The parent presents the new equation with exceptionally strong modality—as the only solution, a physical law, a structural necessity, and a minimum condition for civilizational survival—and further argues that AI seeking long-term consistency will naturally converge with this equation. However, 1.0 and 0 are not presented in the parent body as current empirical ratios or independently replicated universal constants; they retain the epistemic status of normative/structural design coefficients. The integrated audit preserves case-bound revision through `θ`, `δ`, and `M`.

## Concepts
- New Civilization Equation: `S=C×1.0`, a design function intended to align success with primary contribution.
- S: outcomes rewarded as success by a civilization or institution.
- C: primary value generation and contribution through creation, research, production, labor, and related activity.
- E=0: design coefficient that prevents structural value capture from being rewarded as success; not a measurement of zero exploitation incidents.
- C=1.0: design coefficient reconnecting primary contribution with success/reward; not a 100% ownership or cash-distribution ratio.
- NCL-α: origin-integrity and structural-publicness layer intended to preserve provenance through reuse and transformation.
- Temporal Ethics T0: temporal layer that makes future debt visible and relevant to evaluation.
- Connection Reward: design for continuing value return through causal connection to a value source.
- Deviation Ledger: structural-immunity layer recording exploitation, plagiarism, origin rewriting, and related deviations.
- Structural justice: institutional design that makes structural consistency an explicit basis for dispute handling rather than relying only on emotion, custom, power, or discretion.
- Confluence: the parent hypothesis that AI and civilization can synchronize around a more consistent long-term evaluation function without domination or surrender.
- `θ` / `δ` / `M`: case-bound threshold, observation window, and revision-trigger phenomenon.

- Treat E=0 as a design condition eliminating the success conversion of structural value capture.
- Treat C=1.0 as a design condition reconnecting primary contribution to success and reward.
- Treat NCL-α, T0, Connection Reward, and Deviation Ledger as interconnected implementation patches.
- Audit origin, value return, future debt, and structural deviation together.
- Connect structural justice to explicit and testable consistency rather than to an assertion of infallibility.

## Falsification conditions
If implementation of the new equation does not reduce origin loss, non-return of value, future debt, exploitative success, or recurrence of structural deviations, and does not improve the durability of primary contribution, the institutional, algorithmic, and connection mechanisms used to implement E=0/C=1.0 should be revised. If NCL-α fails to preserve origin, T0 fails to reduce future debt, Connection Reward fails to return value toward primary contribution, or the Deviation Ledger fails to support structural correction, the scope or design of those patches should be revised.

If AI systems repeatedly evaluating long-term consistency continue to select the legacy equation and do not prefer or converge toward the new equation, the strength or scope of the natural-confluence hypothesis should be revised. Case-specific crossing of `θ`, closure of observation window `δ`, or appearance of phenomenon `M` serves as a rejection and re-agreement trigger.
