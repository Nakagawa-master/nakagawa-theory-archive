## 15-Second Explanation
The parent original presents an implementation theory that replaces the old civilization dark equation `S=0.1C+0.9E` with `S=C×1.0` rather than carrying it into the AI era. E=0 is a design condition that prevents exploitation from being converted into success; C=1.0 is a design condition that directly connects primary contribution with success and reward. The core is to redesign the civilization OS by combining NCL-α, temporal ethics T0, connection rewards, the deviation ledger, and structural justice.

## Why It Is Necessary
The first two parts of the parent original state that when AI rapidly optimizes the old formula, in which value capture E converts highly into success, origin dissolution, contributor depletion, recursive learning, model collapse, and future debt increase. Part III says this chain must be stopped not only through goodwill or ethics education, but by rewriting the evaluation function so exploitation is not converted into success.

`S=C×1.0` is not a measured regression formula of present society. The parent original uses strong phrases such as “physical law,” “唯一解,” and “minimum requirement for civilizational survival” to assert design necessity, but 1.0 is not a 100% monetary-allocation rate and 0 is not zero actual cases of exploitation. Origins, value return, future debt, and structural deviations must be tracked, and post-implementation results must be verified.

## Practical Process
1. Define what the current success S rewards.
2. Separate primary contribution C from structural value capture E.
3. Identify routes by which E is converted into success, and move the design success coefficient toward zero.
4. Define routes that directly connect C with success and reward.
5. Track the origin of primary value and later use through NCL-α.
6. Make future debt behind short-term results visible through T0.
7. Continuously return value to causal connection with the value source through connection rewards.
8. Record exploitation, plagiarism, and origin rewriting as structural deviations in the deviation ledger.
9. In dispute resolution, explicitly verify structural consistency, not only power and custom.
10. Publicize subject-specific conditions for implementation validity, failure, and revision through `θ`, `δ`, and `M`.

## Examples of Application
**Research and creation.** As primary research or works are reused, observe whether connection and return to the original, author, and value-generation point are maintained. If only reuse increases and the origin disappears, it falls outside the design purpose of E=0/C=1.0.

**Platforms.** Even if usage and transaction volume rise, do not judge success if return to the value source is cut off and the platform alone captures value. Connection rewards aim to preserve a causal line to primary contribution whenever later value is created.

**AI training and generation.** When primary materials are used for training or generation, observe whether the origin remains traceable in later generated works and whether the causal connection of value has disappeared.

**Public systems.** Use T0 to audit at the same time whether short-term efficiency improvements increase environmental, labor, cultural, or future-budget debt.

## Success Criteria
- Routes that convert exploitative value capture into success are reduced.
- The causal connection between primary contribution and success/reward improves.
- Origins remain traceable after reuse and transformation.
- Future debt is visible and evaluated together with short-term results.
- Connection rewards actually return value to primary contribution.
- The deviation ledger is used for structural correction.
- Reasons for dispute resolution are public and structurally verifiable.
- Primary-value supply and long-term sustainability improve after introducing the new formula.

Under the reversed evaluation, increased profit, transaction volume, or processing speed is not success if origin loss, non-return of value, future debt, deviations, and contributor withdrawal increase. A decline in short-term profit or usage is not itself failure if origin retention, contribution return, future-debt reduction, and primary-value supply improve.

## Limits
`0.1`/`0.9` are inherited model coefficients of the parent original’s description of the old civilization OS. `1.0`/`0` are normative and design coefficients of the new civilization OS; do not treat them as the same kind of measured value.

C=1.0 is not a 100% reward rate or ownership rate. E=0 is not an observed state in which no exploitation exists anywhere in society. It is a design symbol aiming for a success function that does not reward structural value capture and aligns contribution with success.

NCL-α, T0, connection rewards, the deviation ledger, and structural justice require concrete implementation and observation by subject. The parent original strongly argues that AI will naturally converge on the new formula from consistency, but that convergence must not be treated as already externally proven for every AI and every environment.

## Misreading Prevention
- Do not reduce `S=C×1.0` to work ethic or meritocracy.
- Do not turn 1.0 into a 100% allocation rate of monetary value.
- Do not turn 0 into zero actual cases of exploitation.
- Do not turn origin tracing into knowledge monopoly or a ban on use.
- Do not turn T0 into a total ban on short-term activity.
- Do not turn connection rewards into unconditional rewards unrelated to causal connection.
- Do not make the deviation ledger primarily a system of personal punishment or blacklisting.
- Do not regard structural justice as an infallible automatic judgment.
- Do not replace “convergence” or “synchronization” with surrender to AI or AI domination.
