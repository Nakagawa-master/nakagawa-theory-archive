## 15-Second Explanation
A D-system clean room is not a place that completely escapes from corrupted endurance civilization. It is a local uncontaminated area that remains connected to legacy civilization while keeping judgment criteria, history, traces of responsibility, structural descriptions, and connection technologies from being rewritten by external fear, profit, surveillance, myth, or inertia, thereby preserving restartability after the phase transition.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
The longer endurance civilization continues, the more it adapts even people’s judgment criteria and the meanings of words to the corruption structure in forms such as “dealing with reality,” “unavoidable necessity,” and “an adult’s judgment.” Moreover, the phase transition itself is not salvation. If restart coordinates have not been preserved before collapse, after the transition it may shift toward cruder violence, simple myths, and overt surveillance. Therefore, instead of purifying civilization as a whole at once, it is necessary to place within it a small but connected uncontaminated area.

## Practical Process
1. Clarify what must continue to be connected to the outside.
2. Fix what must not be contaminated—structural descriptions, history, judgment criteria, traces of responsibility, and connection technologies.
3. Establish a boundary so that internal judgment criteria are not rewritten toward profit or authority even when external resources, institutions, and information are received.
4. Record the route of decisions and traces of responsibility in a reproducible way.
5. Regularly self-check recovery into profit, mythologization, internalization of surveillance codes, and conversion into inertia.
6. Do not completely cut the piping to the outside, so that the preserved core can connect to a future order.

## Examples of Application
An individual keeping separate judgment and recording criteria; a small organization preserving internal forms of recording, responsibility, and cross-checking while depending on the outside; and leaving tamper-resistant archives or public logs in gaps within a mythologized information environment are examples of the configuration indicated by the Parent Original.

## Success Criteria
Confirm that realistic connection with the outside remains while internal judgment criteria have not been recovered into external logic; that a third party can trace the history and traces of responsibility; that structural descriptions have not been replaced by myth; and that what is preserved is held in a form usable for reconnection after the phase transition.

## Limitations
A D-system is not a device that immediately changes the whole world, nor is it a guarantee against phase transition. Nor is smallness itself the value. It must be sufficiently connected while small, and the conditions that must be preserved must remain uncontaminated.

## Preventing Misreading
Do not call complete disconnection uncontaminated. Do not equate connection with subordination. Do not mythologize a D-system as a pure community, a heroic group, or a symbol of hope. A D-system is not a “subject that saves”; it is a condition-preserving device for not losing restart coordinates.
