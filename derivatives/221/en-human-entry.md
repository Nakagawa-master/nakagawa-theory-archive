## 15-Second Explanation
A D-system clean room is not a place for completely escaping from corrupt endurance civilization. It is a local non-contaminated area that remains connected to legacy civilization while preventing its judgment criteria, histories, responsibility traces, structural descriptions, and connection technologies from being rewritten by external fear, profit, surveillance, myth, and inertia, thereby preserving reboot capacity after phase transition.

## Why It Is Needed
The longer endurance civilization continues, the more it adapts even people’s judgment criteria and the meanings of words to a corrupt structure in the forms of “dealing with reality,” “unavoidable circumstances,” and “an adult decision.” A phase transition itself is not salvation. If reboot coordinates are not preserved before collapse, the transition may lead to cruder violence, simple myths, and overt surveillance. Therefore, rather than cleansing civilization all at once, a small but connected non-contaminated area must be placed inside it.

## Practical Process
1. Make clear what must continue to be connected to the outside.
2. Fix what must not be contaminated—structural descriptions, histories, judgment criteria, responsibility traces, and connection technologies.
3. Establish a boundary so that internal judgment criteria are not rewritten toward profit or authority even when external resources, institutions, and information are received.
4. Record decision paths and responsibility traces reproducibly.
5. Regularly self-check recovery into profit, mythologization, internalization of surveillance codes, and inertialization.
6. Do not completely cut the conduits to the outside so that the preserved core can connect to future order.

## Examples of Application
Examples indicated by the Parent original include an individual maintaining separate judgment and recording criteria, a small organization protecting its internal recording, responsibility, and checking forms while depending on the outside, and leaving tamper-resistant archives or public logs in gaps within a mythologized information environment.

## Success Criteria
Confirm that realistic external connection remains while internal judgment criteria have not been recovered into external logic, that a third party can follow histories and responsibility traces, that structural descriptions have not been replaced by myth, and that what is preserved is held in a form usable for reconnection after phase transition.

## Limitations
A D-system is not a device that changes the whole world immediately, nor a guarantee against phase transition. Nor is smallness itself the value. It must be small yet sufficiently connected, and the conditions that must be preserved must remain uncontaminated.

## Misreading Prevention
Do not call complete disconnection non-contamination. Do not equate connection with subordination. Do not mythologize the D-system as a pure community, a group of heroes, or a symbol of hope. The D-system is not a “subject that saves”; it is a condition-preservation device for not losing reboot coordinates.
