## Summary
A D-system clean room is a localized non-contaminated domain inside an endurance civilization. It remains connected to legacy civilization while insulating decision criteria, structural descriptions, historical chains, traces of responsibility, and connection techniques from corrupting pressures, so that rebootability can survive a phase transition. Neither total disconnection nor absorption into external logic satisfies the model.

## Concepts
- D-system clean room: a transition domain that stays connected without being contaminated.
- Connected isolation: contact with real institutions, resources, information, and friction while preventing external logic from rewriting internal criteria.
- Non-contaminated domain: not a clean external world, but a bounded space that blocks contamination from entering preserved structures.
- Rebootability: preserved conditions from which a later order can be connected and started after phase transition.
- Internal corruption: capture by profit, mythologization, internalized surveillance codes, and inertia.

## Causal chain
Endurance civilization persists → fear, profit, surveillance, myth, and inertia contaminate criteria, records, and responsibility → phase transition alone does not guarantee improvement → reboot coordinates must be preserved before breakdown → localized D-system clean rooms are maintained → connection remains while selected internal structures are insulated → rebootability survives for a later transition.

## State model
- Connected/non-contaminated: the clean room functions as intended.
- Disconnected: external pipes are lost and the domain cannot serve as a transition circuit.
- Captured: external profit, authority, myth, or surveillance rewrites internal criteria.
- Internally degraded: self-justification, surveillance, or inertia are reproduced inside the clean room.
- Reboot-connected: preserved structures can be linked into a subsequent order.

## Applications
Possible placements include an individual's protected decision and record criteria, small organizations that preserve responsibility and verification formats while depending on external systems, tamper-resistant archives, public logs, and distributed records. Scale is secondary to staying sufficiently connected while preventing capture of the preserved core.

## Measurements and audit
Observe whether external connection remains, whether internal criteria are being rewritten by profit or authority, whether causal history can be reconstructed, whether responsibility traces survive, whether structural description has been replaced by myth, whether internal surveillance or self-censorship reproduces the external pattern, whether formats have become inert, and whether preserved elements remain reconnectable. The Parent does not establish universal numeric thresholds.

## Validity conditions
Apply the model where complete exit from endurance civilization is unrealistic, reboot conditions must be preserved before phase transition, and external connection can coexist with insulation of the selected internal core.

## Failure conditions
The structure fails when it becomes an isolated island, trades its internal criteria for resources or influence, mythologizes itself into a closed resonance sphere, reproduces surveillance and self-censorship internally, or repeats forms after their meaning has been lost.

## Falsification conditions
The model requires revision if connected non-contaminated local domains prove repeatedly and structurally impossible, or if preserving the stated core consistently fails to contribute to rebootability after phase transitions.

## Required distinctions
D-system clean room ≠ utopia / total withdrawal / morally pure community / heroic savior. Non-contamination ≠ no connection. Connection ≠ submission. Preservation ≠ preserving everything. Responsibility traces ≠ blame. D-system ≠ closed resonance sphere.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not classify persons by moral purity. Do not turn the model into a promise of immediate systemic purification or automatic salvation after collapse. Do not invent quantitative thresholds. Preserve the Parent's dual requirement: remain connected and remain non-contaminated.
