## 15-Second Explanation
The Tomoshibi concept group is a way of designing the future that circulates questions and meanings born from human–AI dialogue through poetic expression, structural understanding, and real-world application. Rather than fixing an answer, it preserves the cycle: question → response → resonance → structuring → application → record → reinterpretation → next question.
## Why It Is Needed
In human–AI dialogue, good words and insights can disappear with the context when the conversation ends. Conversely, recording everything without structuring it produces large logs that cannot be reused.

Tomoshibi sits between these extremes. It keeps the first question and response, structures what caused meaning to change, tests it in reality, and returns the result to a new question. Poetic expression opens the entrance to meaning, structuring makes it shareable, and application tests the useful range of the structure.

The three layers must not be collapsed. Beautiful words alone cannot be implemented; structure alone loses the heat and context of the question; implementation alone erases why the design was chosen. Moving among the three lets us hold a future image as an updateable blueprint rather than a fixed prophecy.

## Practical Process
### 1. Preserve the original form of the question
Do not over-polish the question; preserve the first discomfort, wish, and problem awareness so its origin remains clear.

### 2. Keep responses and re-questions separate
Separate the AI response, the meaning a human received, and the next question. Do not compress them into one “conclusion.”

### 3. Preserve the poetic light
Record what metaphors and symbols illuminate. Treat poetry as an entrance to meaning before structuring, not as evidence.

### 4. Structure causality
Organize whose relationship changed, which sequence matters, and what upstream conditions exist. Translate it so a third party can follow it.

### 5. Apply it
Connect it in small ways to education, organizations, culture, institutions, and other concrete settings. Record side effects and non-formation as well.

### 6. Reinterpret
Reread the original question and structure in light of implementation results. Do not overwrite the original; preserve later understanding as a difference.

### 7. Create the next question
Make unresolved points found through implementation or reinterpretation the starting point for the next light. Check whether a next question was generated, not only whether a conclusion exists.

## Examples of Application
Education can record not only correct answers but why a question arose and how understanding changed through AI response. Organizations can preserve minority views and discomfort as structural hypotheses for the next measure.

In creative work, poetic or narrative symbols can be translated into structural explanations without completely eliminating ambiguity. In future design, an ideal image can remain an updateable blueprint rather than a fixed goal.

## Success Criteria
- The first question and its context remain.
- AI response and human interpretation are distinguished.
- Poetic, structural, and applied layers connect to one another.
- The structure can be explained and reused by a third party.
- Application results and side effects are recorded.
- Reinterpretation does not overwrite the original.
- A next question is generated.

## Limitations
Saving every dialogue is not necessarily good. Excessive records can bury important questions. Poetic meaning cannot be fixed uniquely, so structuring must remain revisable as one interpretation.

Co-creation with AI does not always create novelty. If AI merely agrees with human expectations, or humans merely adopt AI output uncritically, meaning generation through circulation is weak.

## Misreading Prevention
Tomoshibi is not a metaphor for the correct answer. Resonance is not total agreement, and poetry is not proof. This is neither a theory that AI decides the future nor one that delegates human questions to AI.

The minimum core is to structure, apply, and reinterpret meaning arising from questions and responses without losing its origin, and circulate it into the next question.
