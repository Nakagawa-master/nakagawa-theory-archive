## 15-Second Explanation
Money is good at handling “quantities that work for everyone,” while connection is good at handling “qualities that exist only in that relationship.” Unifying them into only one breaks the function of one side. This argument therefore retains the external monetary order and internal connection order as a dual structure and operates them through mutual reference.

## Why It Is Needed
Money is a strong common language that can cross prices, contracts, markets, and institutions. However, the value of trust, intimacy, and long-term relationships cannot be directly converted into quantity.

Connection, on the other hand, can create unique trust and depth between people, but its value cannot be transferred externally as it is and can become closed. Society as a whole cannot be supported by money alone or connection alone.

What is needed is not collapsing them into one, but connecting two layers with different causalities while preserving both.

## Central Structure
```text
Money = quantity, universality, exchangeability, external order
Connection = quality, trust, relational specificity, internal order
↓
Unifying into only one loses the necessary function of the other
↓
Retain external and internal layers as a dual structure
↓
Make them mutually referential, not merely parallel
↓
Connection conditions the formation of transactions and the flow of money
↓
Money connects connection value to external institutions and exchange
↓
Maintain an appropriate tension without complete fusion
↓
Observe divergence through primary logs, double ledgers, and audits
↓
Rollback and renewed agreement when necessary
```

## Practical Process
1. **Separate the two causalities.** Do not force what can be measured in money and relationship-specific qualities into the same field.
2. **Define the external layer.** Make explicit the foundations applicable to everyone, such as law, contracts, prices, and universal rights.
3. **Define the internal layer.** Make explicit values established within a specific relationship, such as trust, continuity, and community norms.
4. **Set mutual-reference points.** Trace how connection conditions affected transactions and allocation, and conversely how external institutions supported relationships.
5. **Observe with double ledgers.** Record the money side and connection side separately instead of collapsing them into one comprehensive score.
6. **Do not erase tension too much.** Preserve the difference between them and prevent complete domination by either.
7. **Adjust on a small scale.** Promote autonomous reintegration through the minimum necessary intervention, not coercion.
8. **Keep a way back.** Make it possible to review allocation and conversion conditions if distortion or gaming appears.

## Application Examples
**Rewards.** Do not treat only sales as the outcome; observe continuing trust and relationship value that is referred to again on a separate axis. However, do not fix connection into one money-converted score.

**Transactions.** Even when amounts are the same, if contract conditions differ because of continuing responsibility or trust conditions, make those conditions explicit and traceable.

**Public institutions.** While protecting universal rights, law, and contracts, treat trust specific to a region or community as an internal layer. Do not use internal relationships alone to exclude external rights.

## Success Criteria
- The roles of money and connection are not confused.
- External and internal layers can mutually refer.
- The effect of connection on transactions and allocation can be traced.
- Primary logs and history remain.
- Divergence in the double ledgers can be observed.
- One indicator does not completely overwrite the other.
- Gaming and arbitrary preferential treatment can be detected.
- Rollback and renewed agreement are possible when needed.

## Limitations
Connection value is not an objective unit that can be fully converted into money. Also, using trust or intimacy as transaction conditions must not become justification for favoritism toward insiders or exclusion of outsiders.

It is necessary to design the range in which internal relationship value is referenced while maintaining the external layer’s law, rights, contracts, and universal rules.

## Preventing Misreading
- This is not an argument for abolishing money.
- It is not a theory that converts all connection into money.
- The dual structure is not merely two parallel tracks; mutual reference is necessary.
- Do not use the depth of connection as grounds for arbitrary preferential treatment.
- Do not turn the Parent Original’s example audit indicators into fixed KPIs.
- Preserving tension is not leaving problems unattended.
