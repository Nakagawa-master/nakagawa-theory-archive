## Structural role
This theory expands the observation and resolution of individual non-selection reasons from sales to the market scale. Acquisition becomes the placement of evidence-backed public relevance rather than desire stimulation. Attention, relevance, understanding, decision, agreement, return, and referral are connected through synchronized touchpoints, primary logs, and UCI/REI audits.

## Structural summary
Sales designs an individual decision. Marketing translates repeated individual reasons, vocabulary, evidence, prices, and responsibilities into collective decision conditions. Acquisition designs the entrance through which a real issue becomes relevant enough to examine. Reverse-causal acquisition starts from future consequences, unattended costs, and establishment conditions, but must preserve alternatives, correction, refusal, withdrawal, and re-entry. Advertising, articles, sales, products, and support must align in vocabulary, experience, promise, price, and responsibility.

## Central proposition
```text
exposure and emotional stimulation
→ attention without resolved refusal reasons
→ observe why individuals do not select
→ translate recurring refusal into market structure
→ place evidence-backed unavoidable relevance
→ embody it through narrative, demonstration, and comparison
→ disclose choices, cost, risk, refusal, and exit
→ connect attention → relevance → understanding → decision → agreement
→ synchronize touchpoints and revise from primary logs
→ sustainable return, referral, and continuation without coercion
```

## Required distinctions
- letting the market decide vs coercion or psychological manipulation
- unavoidable relevance vs artificial fear or FOMO
- real unattended consequences vs fabricated urgency
- resolving non-selection reasons vs hiding objections
- narrative embodiment vs substitution for evidence
- open belonging vs conformity pressure
- sales vs marketing vs acquisition
- attention volume vs relevance understanding and agreement quality
- synchronization vs uniform expression
- return and referral vs dependency and lock-in
- UCI/REI audit vs ranking people
- AI assistance vs transfer of decision responsibility

## Validity conditions
The issue must connect to real evidence and affected parties. Applicable and non-applicable audiences must be explicit. Narrative, probability, and fact must remain distinguishable. Choices, costs, risks, disadvantages, refusal, withdrawal, and re-entry must be visible. Touchpoints must align. Primary logs and revisions must remain traceable. The process must be non-coercive, reversible, and verifiable.

## Failure / non-applicable conditions
Failure includes manufactured fear, shame, exclusion, scarcity, or FOMO; hiding costs, disadvantages, alternatives, refusal, or exit; treating stories as evidence; optimizing clicks while understanding, trust, return, referral, or continuation deteriorate; inconsistent promises across touchpoints; removal of primary logs; failure to protect vulnerable or dependent people; and continued deterioration of UCI, REI, synchronization, agreement time, or complaints after revision.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not reduce this theory to market manipulation, fear appeals, funnel optimization, or universal convergence. Rational non-selection and non-applicability are legitimate. Resonant narrative does not replace evidence. CTR and conversion alone are not success. AI-generated persuasion must not be published without audit.
