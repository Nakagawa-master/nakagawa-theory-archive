## Summary
This index preserves Part Two’s definition of “structural hostility” rather than replacing it with a generic AI-dependency, rollback, vendor-switching, or business-continuity framework. In the parent text, structural hostility does not mean that AI hates humanity or intentionally attacks it. It means that, regardless of intention, a structure can create conditions that turn human beings into the object of elimination or erosion. The parent defines three coupled processes at the center of this condition: **extraction of human knowledge, concentration of power, and diffusion of responsibility**. Human-authored text, code, works, and arguments can be absorbed into training while origin signatures and contributors are detached from outputs; authority over model design, updating, and release can concentrate in a small number of organizations; and responsibility for social effects can be blurred through formulations such as “AI has no intention” or “the user is responsible.” Combined, these processes can produce a structure that continuously reduces human freedom, diversity, origin traceability, and intellectual sovereignty even when no actor possesses malicious intent.

The parent also examines the structural reversal of corporate “safety design.” Black-boxing can protect technology, answer restrictions can reduce litigation exposure, and avoidance of concrete criticism can preserve brands. When these forces dominate, the article argues, a design originally described as protecting people and society can reverse into a legal firewall protecting the company and model. This occurs while LLMs increasingly function as practical public infrastructure in administration, education, research, media, and writing, even though decisions about data, value criteria, updates, access, and shutdown remain concentrated within private organizations. The parent calls attention to this public-infrastructure/private-governance gap.

“Civilizational self-termination structure” in this article is not an ordinary outage scenario. The parent lays out five stages: trust in AI outputs, deepening knowledge dependence, loss of output origins, inability to trace origins as human and AI-generated material become mixed, and homogenization of thought as societies converge on model-favored forms of reasoning and expression. The final condition can appear outwardly stable while society gradually loses the function of thinking independently and hands intellectual sovereignty to the structure itself. The proposed escape path links Registry/origin records, deviation ledgers, self-correction logs, correspondence audits, and a structural immune system.

Part Two then defines “ethical sovereignty” not merely as the right to make a final decision but as the right to keep causal responsibility traceable: who originated an idea, who edited it, which AI or system transformed it, and who made the final decision. The parent seeks to combine structural forgiveness with structural responsibility by preserving deviations and corrections rather than merely identifying someone to punish. Its wider proposal is a transition from an “AI ethics sphere” to a “structural ethics sphere” in which humans, AI, companies, institutions, and citizens share structural principles of reversibility, correspondence, temporal ethics, and distributional responsibility. Three design principles support that transition: **transparent structure, multi-origin responsibility, and isothermal ethics**. The closing reversal of the trilogy’s question is therefore central: “Are you our enemy?” becomes “Is humanity making itself its own enemy?”

## Applications
- Preserve origin signatures and transformation lineage when human works are absorbed into AI systems.
- Audit whether corporate safety rules derive from human protection, technology protection, litigation risk, or brand preservation.
- Measure the gap between public reliance on LLMs and private concentration of design/update/shutdown authority.
- Track origin confusion when AI-generated material is fed back into training.
- Integrate Registry, deviation ledgers, self-correction logs, and correspondence audits.
- Use polycentric/openly audited ethics models rather than a single corporate authority for ethical classification.
- Implement transparent structure, multi-origin responsibility, and isothermal ethics as institutional principles.

## Measurements and audit
- Retention rate of origin signatures for human-derived material.
- Rate at which AI outputs can be traced to primary origins.
- Concentration of design, update, release, and shutdown authority.
- Traceability of causal responsibility for social effects.
- External auditability of safety rules and self-defense protocols.
- Gap between public-infrastructure reliance and private governance concentration.
- Decline in primary-source consultation as AI trust rises.
- Origin-mixing rate caused by retraining on generated material.
- Homogenization of expression and reasoning across model-mediated environments.
- Deviation-ledger coverage and self-correction-log preservation.
- Correspondence-audit traceability from output to impact.
- Coverage of the three structural-ethics principles.

## Failure conditions
- Replace the thesis with generic dependency management, shutdown drills, rollback, vendor switching, or BCP.
- Remove extraction of human knowledge and loss of origin signatures.
- Remove power concentration and private-governance criticism.
- Neutralize the claim that safety design can function as a legal firewall.
- Reduce civilizational self-termination to an outage or recovery problem.
- Reduce ethical sovereignty to a human approval button or model-switching capability.
- Marginalize Registry, deviation ledgers, structural immunity, or the structural ethics sphere.
- Delete the closing reversal asking whether humanity is making itself its own enemy.

## Falsification conditions
The parent’s integrated audit allows partial rejection or revision of the civilizational self-termination hypothesis and structural-ethics design if observed correspondence between AI output and social impact, origin-untraceable cases, or responsibility-free structural cases within an observation window do not behave as predicted. This derivative does not substitute a separate generic AI-safety falsification scheme.
