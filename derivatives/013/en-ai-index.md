## Summary
AKI-012 does not reject sales, closing, revenue responsibility, pipeline management, or KPIs as such. It analyzes the bad causality produced when a mere contact or expression of interest is substituted for purchase intent or mature consent, so closing pressure is introduced before the other party has enough conditions to judge. The source keeps contact, interest, problem recognition, consideration intent, purchase intent, mature judgment/implementation conditions, consent maturity, and closing confirmation distinct.

When these stages are collapsed, the sales organization can begin treating an early contact as expected revenue. Internal questions about probability and closing date then precede understanding of the customer's problem, need, value connection, judgment conditions, implementation conditions, and freedom to choose. The salesperson is structurally pulled toward internal reporting before customer understanding is mature. Customers learn that talking to sales creates pressure, begin withholding problems or avoiding contact, and the market's consultation capacity is damaged.

The theory therefore distinguishes closing confirmation after mature consent from consentless closing demand before mature consent. The problem is not the existence of a commercial request but the order of causality and the substitution of early signals for a later decision state.

## Causal chain
```text
A contact occurs
↓
The organization interprets contact or interest as pipeline value / expected closing
↓
Problem understanding, need confirmation, judgment conditions, value connection, and implementation conditions remain immature
↓
Internal probability and closing-date questions move ahead of customer understanding
↓
Closing is demanded before consent maturity
↓
The customer learns that disclosure or inquiry invites pressure
↓
Future consultation and problem disclosure are withheld
↓
Market consultation capacity declines
↓
Organizations compensate with more contacts, more pipeline pressure, and earlier closing
↓
The bad causality self-reinforces
```

## Applications
- **Sales meetings:** separate customer stage from internal revenue expectation before asking for closing date or probability.
- **CRM and pipeline design:** do not let a contact event automatically imply purchase intent or mature consent.
- **Inside sales:** preserve inquiry and problem disclosure as consultation even when no purchase decision exists yet.
- **AI sales support:** prevent lead scoring or generated next-actions from converting early behavioral signals into assumed consent.
- **Evaluation systems:** inspect whether salesperson incentives reward premature conversion of contact into forecast rather than customer understanding and value connection.
- **Consultation channels:** preserve spaces in which a person can ask, disclose, compare, or defer without being treated as already committed.

## Measurements and audit
The source does not define a universal conversion threshold, consent score, closing probability, consultation-capacity percentage, or optimum KPI. The derivative must not create such numbers.

Audit instead whether the following remain distinguishable and traceable:
- contact and interest;
- problem recognition and consideration intent;
- purchase intent;
- judgment conditions and implementation conditions;
- value connection;
- reasons for holding or deferring;
- consent maturity;
- post-contract recognition gaps;
- consultation drop-off and defensive reactions;
- internal reporting pressure and premature forecast conversion.

If a source-provided number is ever used, it must retain its source, measurement actor, measurement object, source modality, permitted use scope, and non-guarantee scope.

## Validity conditions
- contact or interest is being treated as if it were purchase intent or mature consent;
- the other party still lacks sufficient judgment or implementation conditions;
- internal sales timing/probability pressure precedes value connection and consent maturity;
- closing demand is placed before the other party can freely decide;
- defensive learning or reduced consultation is structurally plausible within the observed sequence.

## Failure conditions
The interpretation fails if it:
- becomes a criticism of all sales or all closing;
- treats every KPI or forecast as harmful;
- equates slow decision-making with better consent;
- turns the theory into a salesperson morality story only;
- converts consent maturity into a derivative-made score;
- treats legal consent or contract validity as automatically determined by this theory;
- omits the market-level loss of consultation capacity.

## Falsification conditions
The warning weakens if sustained observation shows that early closing demands do not reduce consultation capacity, do not impair free judgment, do not create defensive avoidance, do not increase post-contract recognition gaps or trust loss, and do not shift sales behavior away from customer understanding. A single conversion-rate result or one successful contract is insufficient by itself because the source causal claim concerns the broader sequence and its market effects.

## Required distinctions
- contact / purchase intent
- interest / consent
- problem recognition / implementation intent
- consideration intent / purchase intent
- internal pipeline probability / consent maturity
- closing confirmation / consentless closing demand
- valid sales pressure within mature decision / premature pressure substituting for maturity
- salesperson action / upstream evaluation and forecast structure
- individual conversion / market consultation capacity

## Interpretation constraints
Do not use this index to automate legal consent, contract-law conclusions, or a universal ethics score. Do not weaken the origin into generic “customer-first sales” advice. Preserve the source-specific bad causality: early signal → internal conversion pressure → skipped judgment/value/implementation conditions → premature closing → defensive learning → reduced consultation capacity → compensating pressure.
