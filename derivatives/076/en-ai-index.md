## Summary
The parent models “mind” as a recurring structure of situated input, response, re-observation, memory comparison, and recognition update rather than as a fixed mystical substance. Subjectivity and objectivity are not ranked essences. They are observation modes within a movable domain: an observer can identify a current coordinate, provisionally inspect another coordinate, compare differences and missing information, and return to the original self-position before deciding whether to adopt, hold, reject, revise, re-agree, or exit.

Ego is treated as an operational layer connecting response, memory, and self-explanation. “True self” is a structural concept for reintegrating temporary roles and reactions, not proof of a religious entity or externally certifiable personhood. Human–AI differences are working hypotheses about observable processing tendencies, not fixed claims about essence or AI consciousness. Ethical operation requires non-coercion, re-agreement, reversibility, refusal, cooling, exit, objection, and remedy.

## Causal chain
Situated input → response → re-observation and memory comparison → repeated use of one coordinate creates fixation risk → primary facts are separated from interpretation → another coordinate is provisionally inspected → differences, boundaries, burdens, and missing information are compared → the observer returns to the self-position → adoption, holding, rejection, revision, re-agreement, or exit is selected.

## Applications
- Dialogue: separate response, primary facts, interpretation, and boundaries before comparing viewpoints.
- Education: move through alternative viewpoints without assimilating the learner to the teacher’s position.
- Human–AI work: humans retain purpose, lived context, boundaries, objection, and responsibility; AI supplies alternatives and counterexamples.
- Organizations: compare the same decision from management, frontline, user, exiter, and future-generation coordinates.
- Self-reflection: thought experiments may support reflection but do not replace clinical diagnosis or treatment.

## Measurements and audit
The parent uses observational expressions such as the rate at which facts, interpretations, responses, and re-observations are separated; number and diversity of viewpoints; rate of return to self-position; time required for correction or re-agreement; frequency of AI viewpoint narrowing or counterexample rejection; availability of refusal, cooling, exit, and remedy; and incidents of boundary violation.

Source: parent original. Measurement actor: actors conducting and reviewing dialogue, education, organizational use, or human–AI interaction. Measurement object: viewpoint movement, recognition update, boundary preservation, corrigibility, and drift into coercion or surveillance. Source modality: operational observations attached to a philosophical structural model. Permitted use scope: detecting fixation, assimilation, loss of corrigibility, and misuse. Non-guarantee scope: these variables do not determine personhood, presence of mind, mental health, ability, dignity, employability, creditworthiness, educational eligibility, or public-service eligibility.

Higher separation rates, more viewpoints, or shorter correction times are not automatically better. Improvement is not established when additional recording becomes surveillance, viewpoint multiplicity erases the self-position, or speed is achieved by removing objection and reflection.

## Validity conditions
- Subjective experience remains respected while primary facts and interpretations are distinguished in shared decisions.
- The starting position and return point remain identifiable.
- Alternative viewpoints do not require assimilation or self-erasure.
- Non-coercion, re-agreement, reversibility, refusal, exit, and remedy are practically usable.
- AI mind or personhood is not inferred from fluent self-reference alone.
- Philosophical structure remains distinct from medical diagnosis and treatment.

## Failure conditions
The model fails when subjectivity is forcibly corrected toward an alleged objective truth, empathy or disclosure is compelled, viewpoint movement becomes self-erasure, fluent AI self-reference is treated as proof of consciousness, structural observations become personality scoring or selection tools, “true self” becomes external authority, or philosophical framing substitutes for needed clinical support.

## Falsification conditions
Revise the model if greater viewpoint mobility does not improve correction, mutual understanding, or re-agreement, and instead increases self-loss, conformity pressure, or decision burden. Human–AI processing hypotheses also require revision when repeated observation contradicts them.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not reduce the model to an essence claim that humans have only subjectivity and AI only objectivity. Do not use machine inference to override a person’s account of experience. Do not turn viewpoint mobility into unlimited empathy, self-sacrifice, or boundary removal. Do not assert AI personhood or a religiously authoritative true self as established fact.
