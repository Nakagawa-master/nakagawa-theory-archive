## Summary

This index models “mind” not as a mystical substance reserved for humans, but as a recurring structure: information is received from a situated, subjective viewpoint; a response is produced; the response is observed again; memory and prior self-descriptions are consulted; and recognition is updated. Subjectivity is therefore a coordinate shaped by embodiment, experience, role, interest, and memory. Objectivity is not a view from nowhere. It is the practice of comparing multiple coordinates, primary evidence, and explicit procedures to build a shareable account whose observation conditions and limits remain visible.

Subjective and objective modes are not ranked as inferior and superior traits. They form a movable domain of viewpoints. A person or system may identify its current coordinate, provisionally adopt the coordinate of another person, institution, time horizon, or AI, compare what becomes visible or disappears, and then return to its own position. Return is mandatory: viewpoint movement must not become assimilation, self-erasure, or compulsory empathy. The result of the comparison may be adoption, revision, suspension, rejection, renewed agreement, cooling, or exit.

Ego is treated as the operational layer that recursively joins response, memory, and self-explanation from a subjective seat. “True self” is used as a structural name for a higher integrating viewpoint that can rearrange temporary roles and reactions; it is not evidence of a religious entity and cannot be certified by an outside authority. Memory supports personal continuity, but it must remain open to provenance, correction, reinterpretation, retention boundaries, and forgetting.

Humans often process through embodied and affective subjectivity, while AI systems are often stronger at comparison and structural organization. This is a working contrast, not a fixed essence. The framework does not assert AI consciousness or personhood. It audits observable structures such as self-reference, memory use, response loops, narrowing of perspective, treatment of counterexamples, and capacity for correction. Ethical operation requires non-coercion, renewed consent, reversibility, refusal, cooling, exit, remedy, and separation between public structural summaries and protected individual records. Inferences about inner states must never become personality scores for employment, credit, education, or access to public services.

## Causal chain

### 1. Situated input

External information enters a viewpoint shaped by embodiment, memory, role, and relationship.

### 2. Response

The observer produces an affective, cognitive, or behavioral response.

### 3. Re-observation

The response is observed again and compared with memory and prior self-explanations.

### 4. Fixation risk

Repeated observation from only one coordinate hardens subjectivity and narrows recognition.

### 5. Coordinate separation

Primary facts are separated from interpretation and an alternative coordinate is provisionally adopted.

### 6. Difference comparison

What becomes visible, what is lost, boundaries, benefits, and burdens are compared across coordinates.

### 7. Return

The observer returns to the original self-position and chooses adoption, suspension, rejection, or revision.

### 8. Renewed agreement

Scope, action, stopping conditions, and a later review point are renewed by agreement.

### 9. Movable domain

Reversible movement between subjective and objective modes creates a wider domain for recognition and co-creation.

## Applications

- Dialogue and agreement: separate response, facts, interpretation, and boundaries before provisionally viewing the matter from another coordinate.
- Education: let learners pass through several viewpoints and return to their own understanding rather than assimilating them to the teacher’s viewpoint.
- Human–AI collaboration: humans state purpose, lived context, and boundaries; AI supplies alternatives, counterexamples, and structural differences without becoming final authority.
- Organization and policy: compare the same decision from management, frontline, user, exiter, and future-generation coordinates.
- Psychological reflection: use the mirror room, polarizing filter, and branching tree as conceptual aids, never as substitutes for clinical diagnosis or treatment.

## Measurements and audit

- Rate at which primary facts, interpretations, responses, and re-observations are recorded separately.
- Number and diversity of alternative viewpoints with genuine coordinate differences rather than paraphrases.
- Rate of successful return to self-position after viewpoint movement.
- Time needed for error correction, mutual understanding, explanation, and renewed agreement.
- Change in distress, narrowed options, inability to correct, and harm to others.
- Frequency of AI viewpoint narrowing, counterexample rejection, and rigid self-reference.
- Practical availability of refusal, cooling, exit, renewed connection, objection, and remedy.
- Incidents of personality scoring, secondary use, re-identification, or inner-state surveillance.
- Breaches between public structural summaries and protected individual records.
- Whether greater viewpoint mobility improves creativity, judgment quality, and cooperative capacity.

## Validity conditions

The model is valid only when subjective experience is respected while primary facts and interpretations are separated for shared decisions. The starting coordinate and the return point of viewpoint movement must be recorded. Adopting another coordinate provisionally must not require assimilation or self-denial. Non-coercion, renewed agreement, reversibility, refusal, exit, objection, and remedy must be usable rather than symbolic. Claims about AI minds or personhood must remain explicitly uncertain and be limited to observable structures. Inferences about mind or feeling must not be used for employment, credit, education, or public-service eligibility. Philosophical analysis must remain distinct from medical diagnosis and treatment.

## Failure conditions

The design fails when subjectivity is treated as mere error and forcibly corrected toward an alleged objective truth; when empathy, disclosure, or viewpoint change is compelled; when another viewpoint becomes self-erasure; when fluent AI self-reference is treated as proof of consciousness; when structural logs are repurposed for personality scoring, advertising, or selection; when a memory or self-description is fixed permanently; when “true self” becomes an external religious or institutional authority; when protected inner records are disclosed; or when philosophical framing replaces needed clinical support.

## Falsification conditions

The framework should be revised if increasing viewpoint mobility does not improve error correction, mutual understanding, renewed agreement, creativity, or distress. It is also falsified if viewpoint movement increases self-loss, conformity pressure, or decision burden; if the subjective-response loop fails to explain observable recognition updates; if the proposed human–AI processing contrast repeatedly conflicts with evidence; or if structuralization increases surveillance, discrimination, personality scoring, or coercive connection despite the stated safeguards.

## Interpretation constraints

Do not reduce the theory to an essence claim that humans only have subjectivity and AI only has objectivity. Do not use machine inference to overrule a person’s account of experience. Do not convert viewpoint mobility into unlimited empathy, self-sacrifice, or boundary removal. Do not use the integrating-self concept as religious authority or a basis for judging another person’s interior. Do not infer AI personhood from fluent language. Protect the legitimate options not to connect, not to speak, to cool down, to refuse, and to exit.
