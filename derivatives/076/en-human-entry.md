## 15-Second Explanation
Treat the mind as a structure consisting of responses to external information, re-observation of those responses, comparison with memory, and updating recognition. Subjective and objective are not opposing attributes; they are observation modes for moving to different coordinates while maintaining one’s position, viewing the difference, and returning.

## Why It Is Needed
When one identifies one’s reaction with reality itself, recognition tends to become fixed. Conversely, when objectivity is treated as absolute, the person’s experience and boundaries are discarded. Therefore, separate primary facts from interpretations, provisionally take another viewpoint, compare the differences, return to one’s own position, and update the judgment.

This idea can also be used for human-AI collaboration, but it does not assert the existence of a mind or personality in AI. AI can assist by presenting multiple viewpoints and counterexamples; people and institutions retain purpose, boundaries, dissent, stopping, and responsibility.

## Practical Process
### 1. Make the Current Position Explicit
Confirm one’s role, experience, interests, feelings, and boundaries as the starting point for observation.

### 2. Separate Primary Facts and Interpretations
Distinguish records, quotations, and events from guesses, evaluations, and assignments of meaning.

### 3. Provisionally Place Another Coordinate
Review the same event from the standpoint of another person, an institution, the past, the future, or AI.

### 4. Compare the Differences
Confirm what is visible and lost, as well as the benefits, burdens, and boundaries at each coordinate.

### 5. Return to One’s Own Position
From one’s own position, decide whether to adopt, hold, or reject the understanding.

### 6. Reach Agreement Again When Necessary
Update the action, scope of consent, stopping conditions, and points for reconfirmation.

## Examples of Application
**Workplace conflict.** Compare the same decision from the viewpoints of management, the frontline, users, and people who have left, rather than treating only the majority’s explanation as objective.

**Human-AI dialogue.** Even when AI presents counterexamples or alternative viewpoints, do not make its fluency the final authority; preserve stopping, correction, and refusal.

**Education.** Move through multiple perspectives and return to the learner’s own understanding, rather than assimilating the learner into the teacher’s viewpoint.

**Organizing psychological burdens.** Separate a reaction from the whole self and consider other possibilities, but do not treat this as a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment.

## Criteria for Success
Success is not determined only by the number of viewpoints or the amount of record-keeping. What matters is that the person’s experience is not denied, primary facts and interpretations can be separated, and the person can retain their own position and right to refuse after passing through another viewpoint, with correction, suspension, or exit available when necessary.

Even if correcting misrecognition or reaching agreement again becomes faster, it is not an improvement if it removes opportunities for reflection or dissent. Even if the proportion of records is high, it is a failure if they are repurposed for surveillance or personality evaluation.

## Limitations
It is impossible to reproduce another person’s subjectivity completely. A shift of viewpoint is a hypothetical observation, so the person’s explanation and dissent must continue to be accepted.

Descriptions of the difference between humans and AI are also working hypotheses that update in response to observation, not fixed essences.

## Preventing Misreading
- This is not a theory that excludes the subjective and corrects it toward the objective.
- Shifting viewpoints is not self-sacrifice or forced empathy.
- This is not a theory that establishes AI personality or consciousness.
- Do not use mind or emotion for personality scores in hiring, trust, education, or public services.
- Do not use the true self for religious authority or judgments about other people’s inner lives.
- It also protects the options of not connecting, not speaking, cooling off, and leaving.
