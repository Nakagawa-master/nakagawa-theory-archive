## Summary
This derivative reads an AI bull market as a reflexive structure in which narratives about future value attract capital, rising prices are reused as evidence that the narrative was correct, and additional financing amplifies the loop. The comparison with 1929 is an audit lens, not a deterministic prediction. Real AI productivity may rise while valuations still overshoot; therefore price, earnings, adoption, productivity, leverage and liquidity must be measured separately. The model remains falsifiable and applies the same scrutiny to bullish and bearish narratives.

## Causal chain
Narrative expansion → capital inflow → valuation rise → price validates story → follow-on financing → fundamentals gap → liquidity stress → narrative reversal → re-audit.

## Measurements and audit
Valuation-to-earnings gap, revenue growth, leverage, liquidity concentration, capital inflows, narrative diffusion, sensitivity to expectation revisions.

## Validity conditions
Separate technology value from market price; record similarities and differences with history; keep counter-scenarios open; update with current evidence.

## Failure conditions
Treating 1929 as destiny, equating price with productivity, rejecting counterevidence, or turning the framework into investment advice.

## Falsification conditions
If elevated valuations remain consistently supported by realized productivity and earnings and narrative intensity does not explain flows or prices, weaken the overheating hypothesis.
