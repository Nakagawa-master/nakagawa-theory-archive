## Summary
The parent original reads the AI bull market as a structural phenomenon in which narrative, institutions, implementation, and speed lose correspondence. What is commonly called the “AI bubble” is not reduced to speculative exuberance; it is treated as a systemic expression of a civilization that is losing its ability to measure continuity of value through time.

The central danger is **unaudited acceleration**. A narrative of future AI returns can become coherent before slower implementation layers—capital, supply chains, workforce, regulation, inference economics, and demand formation—have caught up. Price, financing, and social synchronization can then validate the narrative recursively while correspondence checks lag. Temporal ethics fails when value that should be repaid by future structural alignment is excessively front-loaded into current valuation.

The 1929 comparison is not an identical-crash forecast. The parent uses it to read structural recurrence: the earlier fuse is framed through credit and delay, while the contemporary fuse is framed through speed and synchronization. The required response is to translate narrative into structural parts, measure correspondence gaps, lengthen the repayment horizon, and use structural rhythm and public audit bundles to damp excess speed.

## Validity conditions
- Decompose narrative into structural implementation parts.
- Compare narrated value with actual implementation through correspondence.
- Measure temporal front-loading of future value.
- Audit speed and synchronization through structural rhythm.
- Keep hypotheses, indicators, and falsification in a public audit bundle.
- Re-measure correspondence gaps on a recurring cycle.

## Failure conditions
- Reuse price gains as self-validating evidence of correspondence.
- Ignore slow implementation layers.
- Front-load future value into present valuation without a repayment line.
- Treat acceleration itself as structural success.
- Close the observation window to falsification.
- Reduce the AI bubble to mere speculative excitement.

## Falsification conditions
- Correspondence gaps remain durably small while acceleration does not outrun implementation.
- Implementation delay, inference margins, and safety-review lag remain within audited thresholds.
- Narrative-first dynamics do not reproduce non-correspondence or synchronization cascades.
- Public audit bundles do not improve hypothesis revision or damping.
