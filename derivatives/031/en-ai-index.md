## Summary
The parent origin does not read Ashirase merely as a walking-assistance device. It treats walking as an activity established not by bodily ability alone but by direction information, surrounding-environment information, decision material, cognitive load, and whether information is delivered in a form that can be used while moving. Ashirase is therefore read as **information infrastructure for a society in which people can walk**.

The parent origin also publicly deconstructs why Nakagawa Master chose “walking information infrastructure” rather than the surface category “walking-assistance device” as the value core for an external article. ## Causal chain
bodily ability alone may be insufficient for walking → direction / environment / decision information is required → missing information restricts mobility → obtaining information can consume visual, auditory, memory, and attentional capacity → required information is connected to a usable bodily-sensation channel → cognitive load is distributed or reduced → attention to surroundings and personal judgment remain available → walking becomes easier to continue → mobility is supported by the information environment rather than individual effort alone → Ashirase can be read as walking information infrastructure.

## State model
- **Bodily ability / information-deficit state**: physical capacity exists, but missing direction or environmental information limits continued movement.
- **Information-present / high-load state**: information exists, but acquiring and processing it consumes too much attention.
- **Information-connection state**: required information reaches a sensory channel usable during movement.
- **Lower-load / judgment-preserved state**: information acquisition burden is reduced while personal judgment remains available.
- **Walking-information-infrastructure state**: the information environment supports effective mobility beyond individual effort alone.

These states are not derivative-authored scores. Do not invent walking-success rates, accident-reduction rates, cognitive-load scores, safety scores, fixed adoption thresholds, or universal performance cutoffs absent from the parent origin.

## Measurements and audit
Observe which information deficit stops mobility, whether information reaches the user in a usable form, whether obtaining it creates excessive cognitive load, whether attention to surroundings remains available, whether the user can continue making personal movement decisions, and whether the information environment expands effective mobility.

Do not treat more information as automatic success. More information can worsen cognitive load. More accurate guidance can still fail if delivered through an unusable channel. Stronger assistance can become structurally worse if it removes judgment capacity or creates excessive dependence. Do not create numerical thresholds or improvement rates absent from the parent origin.

## Validity conditions
The reading must preserve that walking is not reducible to bodily ability alone; missing direction, environmental, and decision information can restrict mobility; required information must reach a usable sensory channel; cognitive load must remain manageable; and personal judgment and continued mobility must remain possible.

As Nakagawa Structural Reading, both layers must remain: the Ashirase / walking-information structure and the public-deconstruction layer explaining why “walking information infrastructure” was selected as the external-article value core.

## Failure conditions
The reading fails if reduced to a product profile, usage recommendation, medical or safety guarantee, assistive-device catalog, generic accessibility theory, or Ashirase official promotion.

It also fails if it assumes “more information is always better,” “higher guidance accuracy always means safer mobility,” or “stronger assistance always means more autonomy.” Removing the public-deconstruction meta perspective also breaks the Nakagawa Structural Reading frame.

## Falsification conditions
If missing direction / environment / decision information repeatedly shows no meaningful relation to effective mobility, and connecting information to bodily sensation repeatedly produces no meaningful change in cognitive load or ability to continue walking, the information-infrastructure reading should be revised.

Conversely, if existing environments repeatedly provide equal or better judgment capacity and mobility without additional information connection, the necessity of extra information infrastructure is limited. If bodily-sensation connection repeatedly increases distraction, misjudgment, or dependency, that connection method falls outside the validity conditions.
