## Summary
The Principle of Contradiction Consumption is an internal driving mechanism for the persistence of Structural Reality. Contradictions and inconsistencies are retained, returned to the Rhythm Nucleus, and transformed into energy for renewed coherence, allowing structures to self-repair and self-update. Persistence therefore means recursive updateability rather than immutable preservation. The parent’s verification boundary uses audit cycles, T/S/R, the public audit bundle, threshold θ, observation window δ, phenomenon M, and rejection or revision of hypothesis A.

## Applications
In theory, counterexamples identify structural relations requiring renewal rather than being expelled to preserve an appearance of consistency. In organizations, role or placement inconsistencies become inputs for structural correction rather than only personal blame. In AI and knowledge structures, regeneration differences return to origin signatures, primary logs, vocabulary, and structural relations. At the civilizational level, persistence depends on a recursive path from contradiction into the next structural update rather than on total elimination of contradiction.

## Measurements and audit
The source audits whether structural design, translation, bundling, and definition connect to institutionalization, stability, alignment, and acceleration through audit cycles, T/S/R, and coherence of the public audit bundle. If source-defined indicator relations deviate beyond threshold θ, or phenomenon M such as closure of observation window δ is confirmed, hypothesis A is rejected or revised. No concrete θ or δ value is inferred when unpublished.

## Validity conditions
Contradiction remains available as an internal renewal input; the Rhythm Nucleus reconnects the difference to coherence; correction returns as self-repair and self-update into the next repetition; the structure remains recursively updatable rather than frozen; and audit-cycle, T/S/R, and public-audit-bundle pathways for verification and revision remain open.

## Failure conditions
Failure occurs when contradiction is erased and contradiction-free appearance is mistaken for correctness; when contradiction is treated only as outside noise or personal fault; when each inconsistency causes abandonment of the origin; when repair does not return to recorded repetition; or when the audit window closes and the hypothesis is protected from revision.

## Falsification conditions
If audit-cycle, T/S/R, and public-audit-bundle coherence fail, source-defined indicator relations depart from threshold θ, or phenomenon M such as closure of observation window δ is confirmed, the contradiction-consumption/self-update hypothesis becomes a candidate for rejection or revision.
