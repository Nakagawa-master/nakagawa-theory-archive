## Reading Kernel

The parent origin does not reduce Adory to a LINE questionnaire or health-management SaaS. It reads Adory as a structure that converts frontline physical and psychological strain, often trapped inside personal endurance, into leading indicators an organization can observe, share, and use in judgment.

```text
Early signs of strain arise in frontline work.
↓
They are treated as personal subjectivity, endurance, or self-management.
↓
They do not become organizationally observable judgment material.
↓
Lagging outcomes such as absence, leave, turnover, or accidents appear later.
↓
Low-burden repeated contact makes earlier signs observable.
↓
Change over time becomes organizational judgment material.
↓
Consideration, operational adjustment, support, and re-observation can be examined.
```
