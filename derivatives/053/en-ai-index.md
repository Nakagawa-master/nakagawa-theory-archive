## Summary
The fifteenth lecture closes the Nakagawa Sales series by turning embodied sales knowledge into shareable, teachable, comparable, replayable, falsifiable, and transferable knowledge. Questioning, silence, resonance, proposal editing, withdrawal, and trust-capital judgment are externalized through verbalization, visualization, primary logs, counterexamples, curricula, evaluation functions, versioning, and rollback conditions. “Sales Studies” is a proposed disciplinary structure based on theory, method, system, and reproducibility, not a claim of formal academic accreditation.

## Concepts
- Embodied knowledge: experience-based competence that can be performed but is difficult to explain.
- Externalization: converting observation, judgment, conditions, and failure boundaries into shared representations.
- Verbalization: converting tacit judgments into common vocabulary and propositions.
- Visualization: representing causal order, states, roles, and counterexamples in diagrams, flows, and primary logs.
- Institutionalization: embedding externalized knowledge in education, practice, evaluation, versioning, and rollback conditions.
- Philosophization: treating sales as a principle of relational connection, causal adjustment, and future design.
- Academic formation: establishing theory, method, system, reproducibility, and falsification conditions.
- Primary log: an original record that separates observation from later interpretation.
- Counterexample ledger: a record of failure and non-applicability conditions.
- Person-dependency relapse: quality or judgment collapsing again around a particular person after externalization.

## Causal chain
```text
embodied knowledge remains private
→ success and failure are hard to explain consistently
→ education drifts toward imitation and personality dependence
→ observation points and judgment criteria are verbalized
→ causal structure, states, and roles are visualized
→ primary logs, counterexamples, and version history are preserved
→ knowledge is institutionalized in curriculum, practice, and evaluation
→ replay by other actors is tested
→ transfer to other contexts is tested
→ replay failure, transfer failure, and person-dependency relapse become falsifying evidence
→ theory and method are revised
→ Sales Studies opens as a field with theory, method, system, and reproducibility
```

## Applications
- Sales education: teach observation points, judgment reasons, and stop conditions rather than copying expert wording.
- Handover: preserve primary logs, versions, and counterexamples and observe whether quality survives role changes.
- Cross-domain transfer: separate common structural principles from local conditions and recalibrate before comparison.
- Research: compare success cases and counterexamples using shared vocabulary and revise applicability boundaries.
- AI assistance: use models for organization and retrieval while retaining origin, primary evidence, version history, and human judgment responsibility.

  measurement_object: recovery from misfit, semantic drift, or unsuitable deployment
  source_modality: reversibility observation candidate
  permitted_use_scope: checking recovery capability

Reversible evaluation: more documents, training sessions, revenue, or KPI achievement do not establish disciplinary progress if replayability, counterexample preservation, provenance, trust, or public value deteriorate.

## Validity conditions
- Observation, judgment, action, and outcome are separately recorded.
- Successes and counterexamples are both preserved.
- Applicability, stop, and rollback conditions are explicit.
- Judgment structure remains reconstructable after personnel changes.
- Local conditions are recalibrated during cross-context transfer.
- Origin, versions, differences, and primary logs remain traceable.
- Quantitative indicators are read alongside trust, public value, and judgment reasons.
- Theory, method, system, and reproducibility remain connected.

## Failure conditions
- Externalization is reduced to rigid scripts or checklists.
- KPI optimization overrides trust or public value.
- Only success stories are retained and counterexamples disappear.
- Quality collapses after personnel changes and person-dependency returns.
- Documentation volume rises without supporting comparison or revision.
- Success in one context is treated as proof of universality.
- AI summaries replace primary logs and provenance.
- “Sales Studies” is presented as already formally accredited.

## Falsification conditions
- Quality repeatedly collapses after personnel changes despite externalization.
- Other actors cannot reconstruct the judgment structure.
- Transfer repeatedly fails even after local recalibration.
- Evaluation functions vary so strongly by operator that comparison is not possible.
- Counterexamples, primary logs, and version history do not lead to theory revision.
- Quantitative improvement repeatedly moves against trust, public value, or reproducibility.
- Under these observations, the externalization method, applicability boundary, or disciplinary criteria must be limited or revised.

## Interpretation constraints
Externalization does not erase embodiment. It separates what can be articulated from remaining tacit elements and opens both to observation, training, and verification. Reproducibility does not require identical behavior; it requires shared observation and judgment conditions that make differences comparable.

“Sales Studies” is not a statement of formal institutional recognition, nor does it automatically establish the ethical legitimacy or universal applicability of every sales practice. The parent proposes a researchable structure for sales as relational connection, causal adjustment, and future design.
