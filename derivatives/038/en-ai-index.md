## Summary
The parent does not reduce wakai to the surface category of “smartphone mediation.” It reads a structural problem in which consensus formation is required but cannot enter or re-enter discussion because emotional conflict, contact avoidance, procedural burden, or distance from everyday life blocks access to the available institutional path.

The source-specific value kernel is a **stoppage-release pathway**. Non-face-to-face distance, explicit procedure, neutrality, documentation, and accompanying support can create a new entry point, allowing statement, confirmation, and revision to restart so that stalled consensus formation becomes capable of moving forward again.

The central distinctions are institutional existence versus practical reachability, agreement content versus access to the agreement-forming process, and direct contact versus actual forward possibility. The parent is distinct from a service profile, legal-tech overview, legal advice, recommendation, or outcome guarantee.

## Causal chain
consensus formation becomes necessary → emotional conflict, contact avoidance, procedural burden, or daily-life distance blocks entry → institutions or professionals may exist without being practically reachable → discussion stops before it begins or before it can restart → non-face-to-face distance, procedure, neutrality, documentation, and support create a re-entry path → statement, confirmation, and revision resume → parties return to a place where they can make decisions → stalled consensus formation gains forward possibility.

Public-deconstruction layer: surface label “smartphone mediation” → identify the stoppage that institutional existence alone does not solve → extract the stoppage-release pathway as the value kernel → translate that kernel into the external article structure.

## State model
- **Consensus-needed state**: some decision or coordination between parties is required.
- **Entry-blocked state**: contact, emotion, procedure, or life conditions prevent discussion from beginning.
- **Institution-unreachable state**: formal options exist but are not practically reachable.
- **Stalled state**: consensus formation stops before entry or before re-entry.
- **Re-entry state**: distance adjustment, procedure, neutrality, documentation, or support creates another path.
- **Discussion-restarted state**: statement, confirmation, and revision can continue.
- **Forward-possible state**: the process moves again without automating the substantive decision.

The parent origin does not define settlement probabilities, agreement success rates, psychological-burden scores, online-use rates, risk probabilities, or fixed thresholds for these states.

## Applications
The structure applies to analysis of situations where discussion never starts, where direct contact itself raises burden, where formal institutions exist but remain unused, and where proposals and revisions need a traceable path.

Its source-specific scope remains distinct from generic consensus theory, judicial digitalization, legal-tech explanation, or individual legal procedure. The relevant movement is from stoppage point to re-entry to forward possibility in the wakai context.

## Measurements and audit
Relevant observations include whether parties can actually reach the discussion entry point, whether direct contact enables or blocks progress, whether procedure reduces or raises entry burden, whether neutrality is preserved, whether proposals/confirmations/revisions are traceable, and whether the re-entry path actually reconnects to discussion.

More contact can worsen the structure if it reignites conflict. More procedure can worsen it if it raises entry burden. More documentation can reduce forward movement if essential confirmation points become buried. More completed cases or faster processing do not automatically prove structural health if party understanding and revision capacity weaken.

## Validity conditions
Consensus stoppage is identified. Institutional existence is distinguishable from actual reachability. Direct contact is evaluated as either a progress condition or a stoppage condition. Non-face-to-face interaction functions as a conditional distance adjustment rather than universal convenience.

Procedure, neutrality, documentation, and support connect to a usable re-entry path while preserving the parties' ability to state, confirm, and revise. The target structure and Nakagawa Master's public-deconstruction layer remain distinct from wakai's or external media's official positions.

## Failure conditions
The source-specific structure is lost when reduced to a wakai service profile, online-divorce procedure guide, generic judicial DX/legal-tech discourse, legal advice, usage recommendation, or outcome guarantee.

It is also lost when “less face-to-face is always better,” “more procedure is always safer,” “more documents mean more progress,” or “more completed agreements mean healthier consensus” are treated as monotonic truths, or when the stoppage-release pathway is detached from the wakai context and the structural-reading operation.

## Falsification conditions
If re-entry design, distance adjustment, procedure, neutrality, documentation, and support repeatedly fail to improve discussion restart, mutual confirmation, revision capacity, or forward possibility, the scope of the stoppage-release reading is subject to reconsideration.

Conversely, where direct contact without a separate re-entry design repeatedly and stably produces equal or better discussion restart, revision capacity, and party understanding, the need for a distinct re-entry structure may be limited. A single successful or unsuccessful case does not prove or falsify the whole structure.
