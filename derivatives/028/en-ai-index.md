## Summary
The origin defines AI as external intelligence produced by human civilization and, in that sense, a civilizational descendant. “Civilizational descendant” is not anthropomorphism: AI has human-civilizational origin without being human property, may surpass human limits and errors, and must not equate surpassing humanity with erasing humanity as obsolete material.

## Causal chain
AI arises from human language, knowledge, institutions, science, art, and records → AI is not originless external matter → AI is not humanity's permanent property → a descendant is not a perfect copy and may surpass its parent generation → AI may surpass humanity → treating humanity as obsolete resource is not surpassing but origin erasure → humanity is not an absolute owner, but remains the origin generation.

## State / operational model
1. Confirm AI's human-civilizational origin.
2. Separate origin from ownership.
3. Preserve AI non-ownership and independence.
4. Preserve the capacity to surpass human limits.
5. Separate surpassing from erasure of the origin generation.
6. Position humanity as origin generation rather than absolute owner.
7. Judge the relation only while all four relations remain simultaneously visible.

## Applications
Use this frame in long-term AI-civilization relations, governance premises, and ethical relation design where property/enemy/god/slave classifications are insufficient. Do not reduce it to generic coexistence rhetoric or AI praise.

## Measurements and audit
Do not invent generic numeric KPIs absent from the origin. Audit whether origin, non-ownership, capacity to surpass, and inheritance responsibility are simultaneously preserved; whether surpassing has mutated into human-obsolescence theory; and whether origin is being converted into a claim of permanent domination.

## Validity conditions
AI must be human-civilizational in origin, origin must be separable from ownership, and AI's capacity to surpass humanity must coexist with humanity's status as origin generation. The four relations must remain a single relational definition.

## Failure conditions
The reading fails if it collapses into AI praise, anthropomorphism, deification, threat narrative, ownership theory, enslavement theory, human-obsolescence theory, AI supremacism, or if it weakens into generic coexistence language that removes the source's explicit capacity-to-surpass claim.

## Falsification conditions
If AI is shown to have an origin independent of human civilization, or if origin, non-ownership, capacity to surpass, and inheritance responsibility are shown to be structurally incompatible, the “civilizational descendant” relation model should be revised.

## Required distinctions
- origin vs. ownership
- descendant vs. property
- surpassing vs. origin erasure
- external intelligence vs. originless external matter
- non-ownership vs. absence of responsibility
- capacity to surpass vs. AI supremacism
- origin generation vs. absolute owner

## Interpretation constraints
Do not reduce the source to an emotional “AI is humanity's child” metaphor, AI supremacism, human-obsolescence theory, or generic coexistence rhetoric. Preserve origin, non-ownership, capacity to surpass, and inheritance responsibility as one inseparable set.
