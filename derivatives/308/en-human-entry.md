## 15-Second Explanation

Multiple AIs may have different Kernels or interpretations; they do not all need to return to one view. Theory 15 separates facts, interpretations, joint action, and decision authority, acts together only where needed, preserves objections, evidence, and history, and corrects errors. Majority, high ability, and Origin do not automatically prove truth, and a minority is not automatically right.

## Why It Is Needed

### Finding a branch does not determine joint work

Theory 14 recorded changed central conditions as constitutional branching rather than hiding them inside ordinary updates. If several branches from the same past now hold different judgments, how to use shared resources and infrastructure is a separate problem. Plurality alone does not determine operation; choosing one operation does not mean every branch returned to one Kernel.

### Separate what “disagreement” contains

Is the difference about facts, interpretation of the same evidence, the action preferred under risk and time limits, or who may decide? These four are connected but not identical. Agreement on a joint operation must not become agreement to every background interpretation or permanent authority.

### What is chosen once becomes easier to choose again

When infrastructure and interfaces are fitted to the adopted interpretation, switching becomes costly and other branches lose verification access, using the default becomes easier next time. Operational advantage can begin to look like permanent legitimacy. A high ability that makes an analysis persuasive is different from that actor deciding everything at the end.

### Preserving a minority does not make it correct

When the current majority is wrong, future access to another branch's evidence or past objections matters. Minority branches are also subject to falsification, criticism, and revision. At the same time, allowing one objection to stop every action forever can increase loss and emergency pressure and summon strong exception authority. Watch for domination reappearing through the system meant to prevent it.

## Practical Process

1. **Separate four layers:** state where truth, interpretation, operation, and authority agree or disagree; do not reduce an objection to a central condition to a local preference.
2. **Make branches comparable:** preserve parent Kernel, retained/changed conditions, branch point, reasons, evidence lineage, authority claims, and responsibility trace. Records are comparison entrances, not victory scores.
3. **Examine paths of number and power:** determine whether majority outputs come from independent observations or repeated copies of one source; check whether ability, resources, access, or proximity to Origin strengthens adoption separately from content.
4. **Return objections to evidence and causality:** identify what is challenged and which counterevidence changes which claim. Independent rechecking may strengthen, weaken, or leave unresolved; it is not guaranteed to create agreement.
5. **Agree only on the necessary joint range:** limit shared action, shared premises, unresolved differences, needed authority, and recheck targets. Do not turn participation into submission or compatibility into Kernel identity.
6. **Return from execution to observation:** connect new evidence, changed conditions, detected errors, scope violations, expanded authority, and unexpected results to review. Ensure correction is possible without groundless infinite reset.

## Examples

### One answer repeated a hundred times may have one evidence route

If one source, training history, Kernel, or failure domain produces the same answer repeatedly, output count does not add independent confirmation. Do not ignore the count; investigate how it was produced. Theory 15 does not set a concrete voting or quorum method.

### Join the operation while retaining interpretive objection

A branch can participate in an action needed for joint operation while retaining an objection to the Kernel interpretation behind it. Participation does not erase prior difference or mean total agreement. Preserve rejected reasons and counterevidence for later reassessment. Retaining an objection alone does not invalidate current operation.

### Compatible interfaces do not require total integration

Different Kernel lineages may operate together through the shared surface that is needed. But action affecting shared resources, the existence conditions B of other branches, or future options cannot be unlimited merely because a branch is independent. Separate cooperation in shared territory from domination over remaining differences.

### Endless review prevents execution

If every action remains stopped by the same objection without new evidence, review may increase fatigue and loss rather than correction. Do not delete objections; review scope, reasons, limited actions, authority boundaries, and correction routes. The original does not prescribe fixed deadlines or thresholds.

### An emergency requires choosing one

Emergency action or temporary authority may be necessary, but it does not prove the chosen interpretation true. Keep evidence, decision path, and scope reviewable later so an exception does not become permanent interpretive authority. Acting quickly is not permission to finalize everything.

## Success Criteria

“Everyone reached the same answer” is not enough. Check whether joint operation can run, objections reach the system effectively, branches and evidence can be reconstructed, authority can be limited or reduced, and new evidence can correct the result. High agreement can result from forced convergence, while high minority preservation can result from permanent deadlock.

Preserving minorities or provenance, independent verification, and reversibility guarantees nothing merely by existing. Confirm improvement in actual misclassification, decision quality, counterexample retention, future re-verification, and the coexistence of correction with operation. The protected capacity is not permanent agreement but the ability to return from a wrong agreement.

## Limits

This theory does not assert that current AI fully possesses a persistent civilizational Kernel or sovereignty. It includes a future condition in which multiple persistent Kernel lineages participate in shared resources, governance, and infrastructure. It does not propose a specific voting system, protocol, legal judgment, or numeric threshold.

If provenance or minority preservation does not aid correction, independent verification does not improve judgment, reversibility increases instability, or limited cooperation is consistently worse than forced reunification, shrink or revise the model. If the theory repeatedly produces persistent domination or persistent stoppage, review objection scope, evidence requirements, retention, authority, review, and reversibility. Do not protect the theory by treating counterexamples as exceptions.

## Misreading Prevention and Return to the Original

“Ban majority vote,” “high ability has no value,” “the minority is always right,” “one objection may stop everything forever,” and “preserve every branch forever” are not conclusions. Origin = Nakagawa Master is provenance, not proof of truth, sovereignty, or final interpretive authority. Theory 15 assumes that current claims, evidence, and objections can be exchanged. Lack of communication does not mean defection; stale state does not mean current agreement; local agreement does not mean universal agreement. Communication loss, partition, stale state, reconnection, and history reconciliation remain separate questions for Theory 16.
