## 15-Second Explanation
Civilizational reboot does not mean prolonging an old civilization for a long time, nor welcoming collapse. It means preserving the four cores of thought, records, responsibility, and judgment as a minimally operable bundle connected to one another, shaping that local core so that outsiders can reproduce, translate, and connect to it, and thereby igniting a different operating principle.

## Why It Is Needed
Endurance civilization uses fear, profit, and surveillance as its main mechanisms, and keeps broken order running for a long time through myth and inertia. But prolongation in itself has no legitimacy. If thought is bleached, records are severed, responsibility cannot be traced, and the judging subject disappears before stoppage, what remains after phase transition is not reboot but idling. Therefore, endurance has limited meaning only when it functions as time to preserve the initial conditions of the next phase.

## Practical Process
1. Do not make prolongation itself the objective; fix what must be carried over to the next phase.
2. Leave thought as a translatable orientation.
3. Leave records through which the causality between judgments and results can be traced.
4. Preserve responsibility not as condemnation but as a traceable chain from which learning is possible.
5. Preserve subjects capable of actually making judgments to stop, cut, choose, and transfer.
6. Maintain the four as a mutually connected minimally operable bundle, not as separate preserved objects.
7. Shape the local core so that outsiders can reproduce, translate, and connect to it, and expand it through proliferating connections rather than commands.

## Examples of Application
This applies to small organizations or institutional units that preserve not only ideals but also actual judgment histories, responsibility chains, and judging subjects as one whole. If outsiders can understand, reproduce, and translate that operating form and connect it elsewhere, the local core becomes an operable unit capable of ignition rather than a closed preservation object.

## Success Criteria
Observe whether the four—thought, records, responsibility, and judgment—are connected to one another and can begin real decision-making even at a minimum level. In addition, confirm that the core is reproducible, translatable, and connectable externally and has not deteriorated into dogmatization, warehousing, punishment, arbitrariness, or charisma.

## Limitations
Even if the reboot core is preserved, ignition is not automatic. Handover may fail, and it may not propagate externally. Civilizational reboot is neither a guarantee of an ideal society nor a promise of happiness; it is condition design for creating a state in which the passage to the future is not structurally closed.

## Misreading Prevention
Do not read prolongation as good government. Do not read collapse as salvation. Do not call the preservation of thought alone, records alone, responsibility alone, or judgment alone a reboot core. Do not convert the judging subject into a hero or ruler. Do not equate ignition with mass mobilization or a central command. Read hope not as emotional brightness but as the objective state in which the reboot core has been preserved.
