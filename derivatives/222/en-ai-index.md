## Summary
Civilizational reboot is not prolonged endurance and not mere destruction of the old order. It requires preserving ideology, records, responsibility, and judgment as one minimally operable interconnected bundle, then enabling that local kernel to be reproduced, translated, and connected outward so a different operating principle can ignite.

## Concepts
- Endurance: an operating phase that delays shutdown without restoring healthy regeneration.
- Preservation window: the only limited value endurance may have when it protects reboot conditions.
- Reboot kernel: ideology, records, responsibility, and judgment preserved as one minimally operable bundle.
- Ignition: outward propagation of a local kernel through reproducibility, translatability, and connectability rather than centralized command.
- New primary engine: an operating principle centered on explainable acceptability, traceable responsibility, connectability, public auditability, and reselectability.

## Causal chain
Loss of consensus formation → substitute operation by fear, profit, and surveillance → prolongation by myth and inertia → erosion, responsibility evaporation, and semantic hollowing → approach to phase transition → risk of losing next-phase initial conditions → preserve ideology, records, responsibility, and judgment → maintain a minimally operable bundle → ignition through reproduction, translation, and connection → reboot into an order with different primary engines.

## State model
- Endurance phase: substitute mechanisms delay shutdown.
- Preservation phase: remaining time is used to protect the reboot kernel.
- Kernel-loss phase: links among the four elements break and endurance becomes future debt.
- Local ignition phase: the preserved kernel operates in reality and becomes reproducible and translatable externally.
- Reboot phase: a different operating principle spreads through connected replication.

## Applications
Use small organizations, institutional units, record systems, or transition teams to preserve doctrine, decision history, responsibility chains, and accountable decision units as one functioning bundle. The operating form should be understandable, reproducible, translatable, and connectable by outsiders.

## Measurements and audit
Observe whether ideology remains translatable, records remain referential, decision-to-outcome chains remain responsibility-traceable, decision units can actually stop/cut/select/move, the four elements remain interconnected, and the local kernel remains reproducible, translatable, and connectable. Check for doctrinalization, warehouse-like record accumulation, punitive responsibility, arbitrary judgment, charisma dependence, or closure. The Parent sets no universal numeric thresholds.

## Validity conditions
Apply where civilization is in an endurance rather than regenerative phase and next-phase initial conditions must be preserved before shutdown. The four elements must remain interconnected as a minimally operable bundle and must be reproducible, translatable, and connectable outward.

## Failure conditions
The model fails when endurance itself becomes the goal, ideology becomes doctrine, records become inert relics, responsibility becomes punishment, judgment becomes arbitrariness, decision units become charismatic rulers, or the local kernel closes itself off and cannot be reproduced or translated externally.

## Falsification conditions
Revise the model if sustained civilizational reboot repeatedly occurs without preserving the four elements as an operable bundle, or if preserving them and making the local kernel externally connectable consistently fails to contribute to reboot.

## Required distinctions
Endurance ≠ regeneration. Prolongation ≠ legitimacy. Preservation ≠ total preservation. Reboot ≠ revolution. Reboot kernel ≠ knowledge stockpile. Responsibility tracing ≠ blame. Decision unit ≠ charismatic ruler. Ignition ≠ mobilization. Hope ≠ emotion.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not infer automatic salvation after collapse, unconditional approval of prolongation, ideology-only preservation, or one-leader reboot. Do not invent quantitative thresholds. Preserve the interconnection among ideology, records, responsibility, and judgment, plus the external reproducibility, translatability, and connectability of the local kernel.
