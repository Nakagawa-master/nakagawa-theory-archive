## 1. Summary
The Connection Transition Strategy treats institutional transition as staged replacement rather than forced destruction of the legacy order. Legacy KPIs and connection KPIs remain semantically separate during a dual-operation period. An Adapter Layer explains one-way causal relations between connection-side changes and friction in existing systems, but does not convert connection value into money or a universal score. Dual accounting, compliance states, multiple observation nodes, objection and exit procedures, protected/public information boundaries, and decision windows at 30, 90, 180, and 365 days keep transition corrigible and reversible.

## 2. Concepts
- dual operation: coexistence of legacy and connection KPIs without semantic merger.
- Adapter Layer: explanation of causal relation, not monetary conversion.
- dual accounting: separate records for monetary and connection-side outcomes.
- CDI: connection-density observation, not simple maximization of connection count.
- MAI: observation of renegotiation time and friction.
- RS: reversibility through exit, cooling, and reconnection.
- CRI: capacity to handle contradiction and dissent.
- KQI: connection quality.
- Self-Declared / Assessed / Certified: procedural verification states, not rank or prestige.
- Federation: multiple observation nodes across municipalities, universities, companies, and citizens.
- Counter-Friction: translating resistance into information about loss, burden, and constrained mobility.
- rollback: returning an invalid transition component to a safer procedure.

## 3. Causal chain
```text
legacy KPIs cannot fully observe connection value
→ connection KPIs are introduced
→ both KPI families conflict in the same operational field
→ forced conversion increases fatigue, resistance, and symbolic compliance
→ legacy and connection KPIs remain semantically separate
→ the Adapter Layer explains relation without monetary conversion
→ dual accounting, procedural states, independent observation, objection, and exit are installed
→ each 30/90/180/365-day window tests continuation, scale-down, stop, or rollback
→ only verified structures are retained and expanded
```

## 5. Applications
In companies, revenue can remain while renegotiation time, reusable explanation, continued cooperation, and renewed negotiation after withdrawal are observed separately. Municipal participation can include dissent, exit, re-entry, correction, and reuse of agreement summaries rather than attendance alone. University collaboration can observe purpose updates, data consent, attribution renegotiation, and explanation burden. Communities can avoid turning posts, praise, followers, or loyalty into institutional value.

## 6. Measurements and audit
Relevant observations include conflicts between legacy and connection KPIs, meeting and explanation burden, renegotiation time, real usability of exit/cooling/reconnection, relation between connection density and diversity, update/downgrade/withdrawal of compliance states, objections and corrections, concentration of authority, and actual usability of rollback.

A shorter renegotiation time does not establish improvement if understanding, objection, correction, or exit opportunities are reduced. Higher CDI does not establish improvement if homogeneity or dependency on a central figure rises. More Certified states do not establish success if barriers to entry or authority concentration increase. The parent does not supply fixed success thresholds, probabilities, or exchange rates beyond the named structural elements.

## 7. Validity conditions
Legacy and connection KPIs retain separate meanings. Connection value is not directly monetized. Adapter mappings retain evidence and limits. Objection, correction, exit, cooling, and reconnection are usable in practice. Compliance states expire and support update, downgrade, withdrawal, and reassessment. Multiple institutions provide independent observation. Public/protected boundaries are explainable and protected areas remain auditable. Each stage permits scale-down, stop, and rollback.

## 8. Failure conditions
The structure is lost when transition becomes compulsory one-time conversion, when the two KPI families are merged into one score or currency, when compliance states become status or licensing barriers, when leavers and objectors are disadvantaged, when one actor controls definition/certification/allocation/appeal, when protected areas become unreviewable secret rule, when popularity or praise influences institutional allocation, or when reaching a calendar day is treated as success.

## 9. Falsification conditions
Revise the design if dual operation does not reduce fatigue and explanation burden, if objection/exit/correction remain unusable, if compliance states increase capture or exclusion, if federation does not reduce authority concentration or purpose drift, or if rollback is only nominal. Apparent KPI improvement is not evidence of success when dissent is suppressed, homogeneity rises, exit becomes harder, or personality dependence increases.

## 10. Required distinctions
- transition / destruction of the old system
- dual operation / unlimited KPI accumulation
- Adapter Layer / monetary conversion
- dual accounting / universal score
- compliance state / ranking of people or organizations
- federation / disappearance of responsibility
- counter-friction / suppression of opposition
- reversibility / absence of direction
- 30/90/180/365 days / universal deadlines
- KPI increase / structural improvement

## 11. Interpretation constraints
Do not recast the parent as immediate currency abolition, compulsory connection scoring, certification business, personality worship, or exclusion of critics. Do not turn CDI, MAI, RS, CRI, or KQI into human-value scores or fixed rankings. Do not invent parent-absent success probabilities, cutoffs, or exchange rates.
