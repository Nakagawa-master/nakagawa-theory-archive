## Summary
The Parent extends the objective viewpoint into a future implementation model called an attuned civilization: social friction is reduced by aligning conditions rather than by victory or domination. The objective viewpoint functions as a posture engine for attuning vocabulary, roles, and time. AI is positioned not as judge or ruler but as a medium that externalizes intent, synchronizes terminology, and preserves condition differences. A minimal reproducible framework—declared consensus unit, explicit sufficient conditions, and a three-line record—can spread through posture sharing, framework transfer, and public habit, forming a social OS that is not tied to one product or institution.

## Causal chain
Structural friction erodes outcomes → objective viewpoint places all actors, purposes, constraints, and consensus units on one map → vocabulary, roles, and time are attuned → blame language shifts toward structural mismatch → AI translates subjective heat into structural information → intent, terms, and condition differences become visible → consensus unit is declared → sufficient conditions are explicit → three-line records preserve replayability → information symmetry and explainability rise → responsibility is drawn by function and failure becomes a hypothesis difference → withdrawal cost falls → posture is shared → framework transfers across settings → habit becomes public → a social OS emerges as reproducible practice.

## Applications
Public policy can specify whether agreement concerns purpose, method, or resources before deciding. Organizations can draw responsibilities by function and record failures as hypothesis differences. Education can let learners design goal, current state, and next step while teachers act as design companions rather than judges. Human–AI collaboration can synchronize intent, assumptions, constraints, alternatives, terminology, and condition differences while withholding final authority from AI.

## Measurements and audit
Audit vocabulary alignment, clarity of functional responsibility boundaries, recurrence of the same disagreement, withdrawal cost, phase stability S, time to consensus, withdrawal success, governance delay, observation window delta, conformity pressure, harmful lock-in, auditability of primary logs / terminology dictionaries / role boundaries, and coherence with Threshold–Silence–Reversibility (T/S/R).

## Validity conditions
Place self and others on one map; preserve the three attunements; restrict AI to mediation; declare consensus units and sufficient conditions; keep three-line records and condition differences; describe responsibility by function; preserve resource, time, and authority constraints; maintain withdrawal paths and T/S/R; publish only posture, framework, and observable variables.

## Failure conditions
Return to victory, domination, or ranking people; promote AI to judge or governor; accelerate agreement while terminology remains misaligned; bind responsibility to personalities; agree without sufficient conditions; retain only formal documentation; omit resource/time/authority constraints; generate denunciation, conformity pressure, harmful lock-in, universal-key rhetoric, or inferred publication of hidden procedures.

## Falsification conditions
Revise or reject the objective-viewpoint → attunement-habit → social-OS hypothesis when phase stability, vocabulary alignment, time to consensus, withdrawal success, or governance delay move beyond audit bounds; recurrence and withdrawal cost fail to improve; or observation windows reveal conformity pressure, harmful lock-in, or loss of reversibility.
