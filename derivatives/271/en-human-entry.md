## 15-Second Explanation
Rather than resolving social friction by asking “who is at fault,” this approach **attunes vocabulary, roles, and time, and makes the habit of sharing agreement conditions itself into a social OS**. AI is placed not as an arbiter, but as a mediator translating human intentions and assumptions into structural information.

## What Advances from OD270
OD270 was the operation of placing oneself and others on the same drawing. The Parent advances from there toward implementation: when sharing the drawing is repeated, attunement of vocabulary, roles, and time becomes a social habit and rises as a “social OS” that does not depend on a particular person or product. Objective viewpoint thus develops from a one-time meeting technique into a public habit that permits renewed agreement.

## Three Attunements
- **Vocabulary**: align the meaning and granularity of key terms.
- **Roles**: draw responsibility boundaries by function names, not personal names.
- **Time**: stop competing on speed and move toward “right speed,” where sufficient conditions are in place.

The three are not independent. Even if vocabulary is aligned, responsibility drifts when roles are vague; even if roles are clear, premature closure occurs if time differences cannot be absorbed. Attunement treats all three as a bundle.

## Practical Framework
1. Place everyone, including oneself, and the purpose and constraints on the same drawing.
2. Declare what is being agreed this time.
3. List the sufficient conditions needed for agreement.
4. When using AI, synchronize intentions, assumptions, alternatives, and terms.
5. Leave conclusion, grounds, and next move in three lines.
6. Record failure as a difference in conditions, not an individual’s sin.
7. Preserve reversibility and a route to renewed agreement.

## What Is a Social OS?
It does not refer to software from a particular company or a central institution. In the Parent, the framework of objective viewpoint and attunement is compared to an OS because it is a **public design habit** transplanted across multiple settings and repeated without depending on one person. Accordingly, fixing one owner or requiring dependence on a single platform is the opposite direction.

## AI’s Position
AI does not decide the right answer in place of humans. It supports articulating purposes and constraints, synchronizing key terms, recording differences in conditions, and comparing alternatives; it mediates the movement from heated subjectivity to structural information without erasing it. Acceptance and responsibility remain with humans, and AI output also remains retractable by difference.

## Success Criteria
Vocabulary alignment rises; responsibility boundaries become clear; recurrence of the same issue declines; time to agreement stabilizes; withdrawal cost falls; and the framework can be replayed after a person in charge changes. Look at reproducibility, renewed agreement, and reversibility rather than formal adoption rate.

## Structural Necessity Expansion
The Parent’s structure requires reading the spread of a social OS not as central rollout but as **shared stance → transplanted framework → public habituation**. Since an objective viewpoint is a principle independent of people and products, implementation must avoid lock-in to a single actor and allow the minimum set to be reconstructed elsewhere. AI is also positioned not as the OS’s ruler, but as a mediating layer synchronizing vocabulary and conditions across different settings.

## Falsification and Revision Conditions
Revise if AI becomes an arbiter, only the form remains, the system returns to ranking people, accusations replace structural adjustment, resources, time, or authority are ignored, conformity pressure or harmful lock-in arises, or the framework cannot be replayed after staff changes.

## Misreading Prevention and Public Boundary
“Necessity” is not fixed to mean that the future is predetermined. “Social OS” is neither a product name nor a central governing apparatus. What is public is the stance, framework, and observation variables; do not infer or supplement confidential procedures or internal thresholds. Attunement is not erasing dissent, but preparing conditions for dissent to be handled on the same drawing.
