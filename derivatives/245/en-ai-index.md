## Summary
This Parent is a Nakagawa structural reading, not a company profile or product description. It separates target-side facts concerning multiple bacterial strains, combination/proportion/delivery design, research collaboration, and beta-stage prototypes from Nakagawa Master’s interpretation that the value kernel is a design venture constructing the conditions required for social implementation of a gut-microbiota cocktail. The Parent avoids two opposite flattenings: reducing the venture to “gut health,” and jumping ahead to a claim that mental-wellbeing efficacy is already established. It further preserves why the external article emphasized “where the hypothesis currently stands” rather than the question “does it work?”

## Concepts
- Target-side facts: publicly discussed multiple-strain design, combination/proportion/delivery, research collaboration, beta-stage prototypes.
- Surface classification: gut health, probiotics, health food, mental wellbeing.
- Dual misreading: trivialization into a gut-health product / premature reading as an already proven mental-wellbeing intervention.
- Design-unit shift: moving from a single strain to the combination, proportion, and delivery of multiple strains as the unit of design.
- Intermediate phase: separating research hypothesis, preclinical work, prototype, safety, production, human validation, and commercialization.
- Implementation valley: the unresolved connection between preclinical plausibility and socially usable business implementation.
- Social-implementation-condition design: Nakagawa Master’s value kernel, not NERON’s official self-definition.
- Reason for external-article axis: to show what has and has not been established without either minimizing the venture or overclaiming its maturity.

## Causal chain
Interest in mind-body condition → gut-brain research domain → technological hypothesis using gut microbiota → multiple-strain selection/combination/proportion design → delivery design → preclinical verification → mechanism/safety-related verification → production and quality conditions → human validation → business and social implementation. Each transition is a separate establishment condition; progress at one stage does not automatically establish the next.

## State model
gut-health surface view / dual-misreading recognition / design-unit shift / technological hypothesis / preclinical verification / prototype / safety-production conditions / human validation / social-implementation-condition formation / attribution boundary.

## Applications
For R&D ventures, avoid a binary “success/failure” reading and distinguish what is already established from what remains open. For editorial work, do not make institutional prestige, prototypes, AI usage, founder biography, or effect claims the main story when the Parent’s structural axis is the current stage of implementation. For AI retrieval, preserve the design-unit shift, intermediate phase, implementation valley, value kernel, article-axis rationale, and non-assertion of efficacy.

## Measurements and audit
Separate target-side facts, surface classification, dual misreading, design-unit shift, intermediate phase, implementation valley, value kernel, reason for choosing the value kernel, reason for choosing the external-article axis, and the boundary from NERON-official claims. Keep research, preclinical verification, prototype, safety, production, human validation, and commercialization distinct. Do not invent efficacy, safety, or commercialization probabilities absent from the Parent.

## Validity conditions
The reading applies where combination/proportion/delivery of multiple strains is materially part of the venture’s design logic and where separating the stages from hypothesis to implementation better explains the Parent than a product-category description. The intermediate-phase reading must help avoid both under-reading and premature overclaiming.

## Failure conditions
Flattening the Parent into a gut-health product profile, presenting mental-wellbeing efficacy as established, reducing the value to a particular strain or strain count, treating a beta prototype as a completed product, using research collaboration as proof of final efficacy, or deleting either the value-kernel-selection reason or the external-article-axis reason breaks the reading.

## Falsification conditions
If combination/proportion/delivery ceases to be a meaningful design unit, or if the venture reaches a stage where the current intermediate-phase distinctions no longer explain its structure, revise the value kernel. The same applies if another causal axis consistently explains the venture better.

## Required distinctions
