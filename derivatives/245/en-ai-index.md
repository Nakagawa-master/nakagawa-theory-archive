The key conceptual split is between **research possibility** and **social implementation viability**. A technical or research possibility does not automatically specify the conditions under which it can be supplied, operated, explained, verified, and connected to real-world use.

## Validity conditions
The reading is valid when movement from research possibility to sustained social use genuinely depends on multiple connected conditions, and when “assembling implementation conditions” explains the target’s upstream value better than the surface category of gut health. The condition set must be causally related to viability, not merely a checklist attached after the fact.

The meta-layer is also required: Nakagawa Master first used the value core in the external article and the later Parent then made that editorial/structural translation explicit.

## Failure conditions

## Falsification conditions
