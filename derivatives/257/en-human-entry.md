## 15-Second Explanation
When civilization’s problems are addressed only by correcting visible events, the same contradiction can return in another form. The Parent Original traces back to the “primordial causal point” where meaning, value, and causality arise, and understands the power to rearrange surrounding structures from that source as “resonance operation power,” and its action as “causal compilation.”

## Why It Is Needed
In institutions, organizations, human relationships, and society, correcting surface problems individually can leave the fundamental conflict in place. There are tensions in which both sides are necessary yet cannot be completely reconciled, such as subjectivity and objectivity or money and connection.

The Parent Original does not erase one side of such contradictions; it looks at what is generated from the tension itself. Its central idea is that, by grasping the starting point of meaning, value, and causality, surrounding events can be rearranged into a different order.

## Central Structure
```text
Look at the surface problem
→ Identify the fundamental tension
→ Trace back to the starting point of causality
→ Read the value core generated there
→ Establish resonance with surrounding events
→ Rearrange disordered relations
→ Make internal and external layers mutually refer
→ Establish order through structural alignment, not coercion
```

## Practical Process
1. **Separate symptoms and causes.** Do not treat the visible malfunction itself as the cause.
2. **Find the conflict that cannot be removed.** Identify values, roles, or institutions that are both necessary and competing.
3. **Trace back to the causal starting point.** Fix what generates meaning or value.
4. **Put the value core into words.** Clarify what the structure is meant to protect.
5. **Rearrange surrounding events.** Rebuild roles, information, responsibility, and institutions through their causality with the value core.
6. **Connect the two layers without mixing them.** Make the public/universal external layer and relationship-specific internal layer mutually refer.
7. **Observe the result.** Check whether order becomes self-sustaining without increasing coercion.

## Application Examples
**Organization.** Do not end the conflict between sales and review with “which is right?”; redesign upstream where the values of growth and safety should connect.

**Institutions.** Do not tilt the conflict between convenience and rights protection to one side; handle public rules and individual context in two layers.

**People and AI.** Do not equate human meaning and value judgments with AI’s structuring ability; design them as roles that mutually refer.

## Success Criteria
- Explain causality upstream of surface symptoms.
- Preserve the necessity of both sides in conflict.
- Maintain a causal line from the value core to concrete role and institutional changes.
- Alignment increases without adding external commands.
- The responsibility relationship between internal and external layers is clear.
- The design can be revised based on observed results.

## Limitations
Putting the source of causality into words alone does not change order. If it cannot be connected from the source to concrete institutions, actions, and responsibilities, it ends as an abstract concept. Also, if verification and dissent are excluded on the grounds of “immanent inevitability,” it becomes mere canonization of authority.

## Preventing Misreading
- Do not equate the “source of causality” with one person or an absolute authority.
- Do not make surface data unnecessary.
- Do not interpret preserving contradiction as neglect.
- Do not reduce resonance operation to manipulating others or a persuasion technique.
- Do not turn “inevitability” into an unfalsifiable fatalism.
