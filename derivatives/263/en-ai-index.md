## Retrieval Summary
The parent does not assert that a technological singularity must occur in 2045. It uses 2045 as a reference point to examine a structural problem that becomes more visible as automation and AI capabilities advance: a "meaning vacuum" in which society can increasingly optimize functions but has weaker grounds for deciding what should count as good, desirable, or worth pursuing. The proposed Tomoshibi Civilization expands value from ownership and supply toward reference, resonance, and recontextualization through connection-based value. Universal external rules and community-specific internal values are kept in a dual syntax rather than collapsed into one system. Humanity is positioned as the editor/drafter of meaning, with five functions: holding contradiction, creating narratives, translating boundaries, accepting responsibility, and designing existential questions. AI supports ordering, optimization, prediction, consistency, and amplification. The operating loop is human meaning-drafting -> AI ordering/amplification -> human recomposition and responsibility. T/S/R safety principles, a five-layer implementation roadmap, relative metrics M/R/E/A/H, and falsification conditions make the proposal auditable rather than prophetic.

## Core Causal Graph
1. AI and automation increase functional substitutability.
2. Functional supply alone becomes a weaker source of differentiated value.
3. Normative questions — what is good and what should be pursued — become more central.
4. A meaning vacuum emerges as a civilization-design problem.
5. Connection-based value plus dual syntax preserves both interoperability and contextual depth.
6. Humans provide value-origin, narrative, questions, and responsibility; AI provides structural ordering and amplification.
7. Threshold, Silence, and Reversibility reduce coercion, reactive escalation, and irreversible lock-in.
8. Normative, institutional, product, education, and audit layers implement the design.
9. M/R/E/A/H and primary logs provide relative operational observation.
10. Persistent metric deterioration, closed observation windows, or harmful lock-in trigger revision or rejection.

## Human Functions
- contradiction holding
- narrative creation
- boundary translation
- acceptance of moral/social responsibility
- question design

These are architectural role assignments in the parent, not a scientific proof that AI can never display related capabilities.

## Dual Syntax
- External / universal layer: transparency, interoperability, legal compatibility, auditability.
- Internal / particular layer: community-specific values, narratives, trust history.
- Design rule: keep both layers distinct but mutually referential; manage tension rather than erase difference.

## T/S/R
- Threshold: graduated participation and access rather than all-or-nothing exposure.
- Silence: protected reaction delay and editing space, with explicit purpose, duration, and responsibility.
- Reversibility: sunset clauses, review, exit routes, reverse procedures, rollback, and learning/time compensation.

## Five Implementation Layers
1. Normative: accountability, remedy, appeal.
2. Institutional: staged adoption, time-limited agreement, reverse procedures.
3. Product: connection/reference/resonance/recontextualization records and permission separation.
4. Education: contradiction holding, boundary translation, question design.
5. Audit: process health, T/S/R compliance, reviewability.

## Relative Metrics
- M: meaning-generation index.
- R: friction-conversion rate.
- E: exploration-continuation rate after withdrawal.
- A: audit-completeness rate for safety conditions.
- H: human-drafting ratio for new concepts/narratives followed by AI ordering.

These metrics are operational navigation aids, not absolute measures of human worth or meaning.

## Falsification / Revision Conditions
- persistent deterioration of M/R/E/A/H against declared thresholds
- primary logs or reference histories become untraceable
- connection-based value collapses into popularity, capital, or authority scoring
- Silence becomes concealment
- Reversibility becomes responsibility avoidance
- Threshold becomes arbitrary exclusion
- human drafting becomes nominal signature without traceable value-origin or responsibility
- exit, appeal, and review cease to function, producing harmful lock-in

## Interpretation Constraints
Do not infer that:
- a singularity will certainly occur in 2045;
- AI has been scientifically proven incapable of value generation or creativity;
- all human occupations will become "meaning work";
- money must be abolished immediately;
- connection value should be reduced to one ranking score;
- "humanity's unique role" is a compulsory personal life doctrine.
