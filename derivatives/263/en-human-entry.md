## 15-second explanation
In a future where AI and automation are advanced, what will truly be scarce is not processing capacity but the **starting point of meaning**: what to regard as good and what to make the goal. The original divides roles between humans as editors of meaning and AI as the agent of structuring, ordering, and amplification, then turns the cycle of **human drafting of meaning → AI ordering → human re-editing** into civilization design through connection value, double syntax, T/S/R, five-layer implementation, and audit indicators.

## Why it becomes necessary
2045 is not a date of certain prediction. The original addresses the structure in which, as AI and automation increase what can be done, capability and supply alone cannot decide what should be done.

Optimization works strongly once a goal is supplied. But where the goal itself has not yet been settled, a normative starting point is needed: what to call good, what to protect, and which contradictions to retain. The original treats this lack as a **meaning vacuum**.

## Basic structure of Tomoshibi Civilization
Tomoshibi Civilization is a transition from measuring value only through ownership and supply toward recognizing reference, resonance, and recontextualization as value as well.

This does not mean abolishing money immediately. Put a universal layer of transparency, interoperability, legal conformity, and auditability outside, and community-specific value norms, stories, and trust histories inside. Do not mix or sever the two; operate them as **double syntax** that translates at the boundary.

## Human “editing of meaning”
The original places humanity’s civilizational role in drafting and updating meaning. This is not an order to limit one’s profession to a single occupation.

### Holding contradictions
Do not immediately close opposing meanings into a win or loss; hold their tension.

### Creating stories
Give names to undefined values and turn them into shareable stories.

### Translating boundaries
Convert different contexts—specialist and everyday, local and universal—into forms that can read one another.

### Accepting responsibility
Do not externalize the consequences of uncertain decisions as mere calculation results; accept them.

### Designing questions
Rebuild not only optimizable problems but also the existential and value question of what to regard as good.

## Division of roles with AI
AI is not the opposing side. It handles structuring, optimization, prediction, consistency, comparison, and amplification.

```text
Humans draft meaning and questions
↓
AI structures, orders, and amplifies
↓
Humans re-edit, decide acceptance, and take responsibility
↓
New questions emerge
```

The original’s direction is to design this cycle itself as the unit of value generation, rather than a competition of abilities.

## Three safety principles
### Threshold｜Threshold
Stage participation, connection, and authority to avoid the choice between full opening and full exclusion. Place translation, remedy, and appeals at boundaries.

### Silence｜Silence
During flame wars or the drafting of new values, stop immediate reactions and secure time for editing and re-observation. Silence is not concealment, but without purpose, period, responsible actor, and explanation afterward it turns into concealment.

### Reversibility｜Reversibility
Give new institutions and functions deadlines, re-review, exit, rollback, and reverse procedures. Include learning and compensation for time when withdrawing, so exploration can continue while responsibility remains.

## Five-layer implementation
1. **Normative layer**: Put accountability, remedies, and appeals into writing.
2. **Institutional layer**: Institutionalize staged introduction, time-limited agreements, and reverse procedures.
3. **Product layer**: Record deep reference, resonance time, and recontextualization, and separate viewing rights from editing rights.
4. **Education layer**: Learn to hold contradictions, translate boundaries, and design questions.
5. **Audit layer**: Check the health of processes such as silence, reversible conditions, and remedies, not only outcomes.

Repeat **minimum implementation → indicator audit → staged expansion**, rather than introducing everything at once.

## How to think about measurement
The original does not reduce meaning itself to a single score. It places relative indicators as an operational compass.

- **M**: Meaning-generation index for deep reference and resonance.
- **R**: Friction-conversion rate at which conflict moves into emergent updating.
- **E**: Exploration-continuation rate at which another attempt is possible after withdrawal.
- **A**: Audit-sufficiency rate for explicit safety and audit conditions.
- **H**: Human-drafting ratio for human drafting → AI ordering of new concepts and stories.

These are aids for observing institutional movement, not a ranking of human value.

## Success criteria
- Do not leap from rising AI capability to the disappearance of human value.
- Human drafting of meaning and AI ordering are substantively divided.
- Universal and particular layers can mutually refer through double syntax.
- Connection value does not become another name for popularity, capital, or authority.
- The purpose, period, and responsibility of silence can be traced.
- Rollback, exit, appeals, and re-review are actually usable.
- M/R/E/A/H are used for trend auditing, not absolute evaluation.
- Expansion proceeds step by step from minimum implementation.

## Limits
The original is not a certain prediction of events in 2045. Nor is it a scientific proof that “only humans can create forever.” Humanity’s “one role” is a role proposition for civilization design, not a demand that individuals adopt one way of life.

## Preventing misreading
- Do not assert that a singularity must occur in 2045.
- Do not turn AI’s permanent exclusion from value and creativity into a claim about ability.
- Do not turn this into an employment forecast that human work will become one kind.
- Do not turn it into an immediate abolition-of-money argument.
- Do not reduce connection value to a popularity score.
- Do not rank personality or human value with M/R/E/A/H.
- Do not turn Silence into concealment, Reversibility into evasion of responsibility, or Threshold into exclusion.
