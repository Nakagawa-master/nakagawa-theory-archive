## Summary
An ethical deviation ledger is a recovery and structural-immunity record, not a blacklist. It separates facts from inference, records impact and dissent, tracks repair and structural changes, monitors recurrence, and governs retention and access.

## Central thesis
Learning from deviation requires preserving correction rights and recovery while preventing permanent personal stigma and purpose creep.

## Failure conditions
Blacklisting, personality scoring, no correction rights, excessive disclosure, purpose creep, permanent stigma.

## Falsification conditions
If the ledger reduces reporting or increases discrimination, secondary harm, concealment, or recurrence, redesign or reduce it.

## Interpretation constraints
Record ≠ condemnation. Immunity ≠ exclusion. Transparency ≠ unlimited disclosure. Recovery ≠ immunity from responsibility.
