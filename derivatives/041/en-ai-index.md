## Summary
The parent defines Reverse-Causal Rectification Theory as a process that places a viable future definition as a terminal constraint and uses it to realign present meaning, action, resources, relationships, time allocation, and structure.

The “reverse” direction is not physical time reversal. The future definition is placed first as a criterion for evaluating the present. Current elements are then tested for whether they connect to that future line; choices that defer contradiction or repair cost into the future are examined as future debt and become candidates to retain, remove, or reorganize.

This index is a public aid for third-party AI readers. It preserves the distinctions between future definition and wish, terminal constraint and goal slogan, reversal of the evaluation criterion and physical retrocausality, rectification and false rectification, and current cost reduction versus future-debt reduction.

## Causal chain
place a viable future definition → use it as the terminal constraint for evaluating the present → re-evaluate present meaning, action, resources, relationships, and time → separate elements that connect to the future line from those that conflict → identify choices that create future debt → retain, remove, or reorganize → realign present structure toward the future definition → observe execution results and correct remaining misalignment.

The reversal concerns what evaluates the present, not the direction of physical time. It is not a causal claim that future events rewrite past facts.

## State model
- **Future-definition state**: a terminal-side definition for present evaluation is explicit.
- **Terminal-constraint state**: current choices can be tested for connection to the future line.
- **Present-conflict state**: meaning, action, resources, relationships, or time remain inconsistent with the future line.
- **Future-debt state**: present convenience defers distortion or repair burden into the future.
- **Rectified state**: present structure is realigned toward the future definition.
- **False-rectification state**: the surface looks organized while future debt or structural contradiction increases.
- **Observe-and-recorrect state**: execution results are checked and misalignment is corrected again.

## Applications
The structure can support review of strategy, work design, resource allocation, relationships, and time allocation by asking whether the present structure actually changes toward the future line rather than merely stating a future image.

Reduction, concentration, and efficiency are not automatically rectification. If they increase deferred repair burden, destroy needed redundancy, or reduce correction capacity, apparent improvement may become false rectification.

## Measurements and audit
Observe whether the future definition actually functions as a criterion for present evaluation, where current meaning/action/resources/relationships/time conflict with the future line, whether future-debt-producing choices remain, and whether execution results can be observed and corrected.

Preserve reversible evaluation. More reduction can worsen the structure if future debt rises. More concentration is not rectification if correction capacity disappears. More changes do not help if alignment with the future line worsens. More detailed plans do not mean realignment if the present structure remains unchanged.

## Validity conditions
Place a viable future definition and make it function as a terminal constraint for present evaluation. Re-evaluate present meaning, action, resources, relationships, and time; identify choices that fail to connect to the future line and configurations that create future debt.

Distinguish surface organization from structural rectification, observe results after execution, and re-correct when necessary. Do not claim physical time reversal or rewriting of the past.

## Failure conditions
The reading fails if transformed into future prediction, prophecy, wish fulfillment, manifestation, or physical quantum time reversal.

It also fails if reduced to simple goal setting or plan backcasting, if reduction/concentration/efficiency are treated as automatic rectification, if future debt is hidden behind an orderly surface, or if detailed thresholds, universal protocols, or guaranteed success are invented beyond the parent origin.

## Falsification conditions
If using a future definition as terminal constraint and realigning present structure repeatedly produces no distinguishable improvement in contradiction reduction, future-debt suppression, or post-execution correction capacity compared with ordinary goal setting or plan changes, the distinctive scope of the theory should be reconsidered.

Conversely, where equal or better contradiction reduction, future-debt suppression, and correction capacity repeatedly occur without using a future definition as terminal constraint, the scope in which the terminal constraint is necessary may be revised. A single success or failure does not prove or falsify the whole theory.
