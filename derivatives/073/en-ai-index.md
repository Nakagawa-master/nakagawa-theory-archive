## Summary
Connection Public Sphere OS is a public institutional foundation in which municipalities, universities, companies, and citizen communities federate connection accounting, connection governance, CXP, connection adjudication, and connection basic rights. It is distinct from a single municipal-DX system, corporate platform, or centralized regional-data store. Its purpose is to keep the functions that measure, protect, connect, and repair connection value under public purpose, consent, reversibility, independent observation, objection, and remedy.

Municipalities connect legitimacy to existing law and administrative procedure; universities provide knowledge, research, and long-term verification; companies provide technical implementation and application capacity; citizen communities provide independent observation. Federation does not erase responsibility: decisions, allocation, audit, adjudication, and remedy still require identifiable accountable actors.

The parent includes staged review points D+90, D+180, D+365, and D+730; an indicator bundle including CDI, MAI, and RS; and compliance-state classifications Self-Declared, Assessed, and Certified. These are not a monotonic maturity score, personality score, permanent qualification, or success guarantee.

## Causal chain
```text
connection accounting, rights, and protocols are developed separately
↓
a single institution implements them and concentrates definition, evaluation, allocation, and adjudication
↓
capture, purpose misuse, reputation cascades, and difficult exit emerge
↓
roles and observation points are distributed across municipal, university, corporate, and citizen nodes
↓
CXP preserves purpose, consent, agreement memory, reversibility, and responsibility across institutions
↓
accounting observes, governance protects, and adjudication repairs
↓
basic rights protect participation, refusal, objection, correction, exit, and reconnection
↓
public/protected information boundaries and independent observers audit power concentration and drift
↓
limited pilots test validity and withdrawal conditions
↓
only verified functions expand into interoperability, procurement, and permanent operation
↓
rights failure, capture, or institutional fatigue triggers scale-down, suspension, or rollback
```

## Applications
- Education and university collaboration: preserve research purpose, consent, attribution, withdrawal, and reuse while observing renegotiation and usable exit rather than ranking participants.
- Health and care: separate the purposes and authority of individuals, proxies, providers, municipalities, and contractors while retaining correction, deletion, objection, and emergency suspension.
- Disaster preparedness: observe diverse resource routes, access for vulnerable groups, misinformation correction, exit, and re-entry rather than connection count alone.
- Culture and tourism: observe resident consent, cultural attribution, continued collaboration, withdrawal, and capture rather than visitor counts or revenue alone.
- Public procurement: use Self-Declared, Assessed, and Certified as revisable procedural compliance states with expiration, downgrade, objection, and support for small organizations.

## Measurements and audit
**D+90 / D+180 / D+365 / D+730.** Values: 90, 180, 365, and 730 days. Source: the parent original. Measurement actor: the federation operating the public-sphere OS. Measurement object: elapsed time from implementation start to phased review points and the validity/withdrawal conditions for limited PoC, beta compliance labels, limited interoperability, and consideration of institutionalization. Source modality: staged roadmap review references. Permitted use scope: deciding continuation, scale-down, suspension, institutionalization, or rollback. Non-guarantee scope: not universal implementation deadlines, maturity ranks, success guarantees, or a mandatory forward-only sequence.

**CDI / MAI / RS.** Source: the parent original. Measurement actor: the operating and audit actors in the relevant domain. Measurement object: the connection structures, renegotiation, and relationship states observed by the parent’s connection-accounting frame. Source modality: a multi-indicator bundle. Permitted use scope: evaluating institutional state, distortion, and improvement. Non-guarantee scope: not a single composite score, personality rank, social-credit score, monetary conversion, or universal pass threshold. No fixed weights, formulas, or thresholds are added when not stated by the parent.

**Self-Declared / Assessed / Certified.** Source: the parent original. Measurement actor: institutions handling compliance assessment or procurement. Measurement object: procedural compliance state. Source modality: compliance classification. Permitted use scope: procedural assessment in procurement and related institutional decisions. Non-guarantee scope: not a value ranking, permanent license, automatic success state, or justification for exclusion from entry.

Other observable relations include preservation of purpose/consent/provenance/responsibility across institutions; bias, manipulation, and brigading detected by independent observers; objection, correction, remedy, and restoration processes; usability of reasonable accommodation; boundary violations, re-identification, or leakage; power concentration among nodes; capture, collusion, and entry barriers in procurement; and actual usability of scale-down, suspension, and rollback. The parent does not define universal fixed pass rates for these observations.

Reversal evaluation remains necessary. Higher CDI/MAI/RS values are not success if polarization, exclusion, institutional fatigue, or difficult exit worsen. More interoperability is not improvement if centralization, purpose misuse, withdrawal difficulty, or leakage rises. More Certified entities are not success if entry barriers or favoritism rise. More citizen participants do not establish public legitimacy if objections do not affect correction. Reaching D+730 is not success when stage conditions fail; scale-down or rollback can be the structurally healthy outcome.

## Validity conditions
- Municipal, university, corporate, and citizen nodes hold different responsibilities and observation positions.
- Accountable actors remain identifiable for decisions, allocation, audit, and remedy.
- CXP preserves purpose, consent, memory, reversibility, and responsibility.
- Accounting, governance, adjudication, and basic rights share compatible public principles.
- Currency and connection value are not directly exchanged.
- The public/protected information boundary is explainable and protected information remains independently auditable.
- Refusal, objection, correction, exit, remedy, and reconnection are actually usable.
- Accessibility and reasonable accommodation are operational rather than symbolic.
- Compliance classifications have expiration, renewal, downgrade, objection, and support.
- Each implementation phase includes scale-down, suspension, and rollback conditions.

## Failure conditions
- One municipality, company, or platform monopolizes identity, consent, evaluation, allocation, and adjudication.
- Interoperability is used to justify centralized collection of all data.
- CDI, MAI, or RS becomes personality scoring, social-credit scoring, popularity, or monetary conversion.
- Compliance classifications become permanent qualifications or procurement entry barriers.
- People who exit or object suffer public-service disadvantage.
- Citizen nodes become symbolic supporters.
- University authority or corporate technology automatically becomes final decision power.
- Protected information becomes secret administration without audit.
- Connection procurement becomes capture, collusion, or favoritism.
- Existing legal or basic-rights protections are weakened under the new OS.

## Falsification conditions
If federation, CXP, accounting, governance, adjudication, and rights remain in place but polarization, exclusion, institutional fatigue, concentration, purpose misuse, or difficult exit repeatedly fail to improve, and consent/correction/exit/remedy do not persist across institutions, the scope of the design is subject to revision.

The design also requires reconsideration if independent observers cannot connect findings about concentration or manipulation to correction; reasonable accommodation remains symbolic; procurement increases entry barriers or capture; the public/protected boundary produces secret rule or leakage; or scale-down, suspension, and rollback are not actually usable.

The staged review points are not obligations to advance. Advancing solely because a date has been reached while validity conditions remain unmet is inconsistent with the parent’s reversible design.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not convert the framework into centralized administration, corporate capture of regional data, social-credit scoring, symbolic citizen participation, automatic legitimacy through university authority, or certification rent-seeking. Do not reduce existing legal or basic-rights protections under the name of a new OS. AI may assist observation and audit but is not the final accountable authority.

D+90/D+180/D+365/D+730 are staged review references; CDI/MAI/RS are an indicator bundle; Self-Declared/Assessed/Certified are compliance classifications. None of these is a monotonic maturity ranking or success score.
