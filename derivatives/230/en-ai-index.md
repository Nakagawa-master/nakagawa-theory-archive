## Summary
This Parent is a Nakagawa structural reading, not a lodging-operator profile. It separates target-side facts concerning lodging operation, on-site staffing conditions, waiting time, human capacity, and multi-use space from Nakagawa Master’s value kernel: revenue rewiring that reorganizes unrecovered value created under regulatory conditions. Regulatory requirements can generate fixed cost, waiting time, underused human capacity, and idle space. The reading asks whether part of that burden can be reinterpreted as reusable resources and connected to additional uses, complementary demand, and multiple revenue paths. It also preserves why this economic structure, rather than a simple regulatory-compliance story, was selected as the external-article axis.

## Concepts
- Target-side facts: lodging operation, response to on-site staffing requirements, waiting time, human utilization, and space use.
- Surface classification: short-stay lodging operation, regulatory staffing response, operation support.
- Regulatory fixed cost: recurring human/time/space burden generated by regulatory conditions.
- Unrecovered value: the portion of waiting time, human capacity, or idle space that can potentially be reused rather than remaining pure loss.
- Multi-use design: connecting the same human/time/space resources to multiple uses.
- Complementary demand: demand beyond lodging that can use otherwise idle resources.
- Revenue rewiring: Nakagawa Master’s value kernel for forming multiple recovery paths under the same regulatory constraints.

## Causal chain
Lodging demand → regulatory condition → fixed cost/waiting/idle capacity → profitability pressure under single lodging revenue → identify reusable unrecovered value → connect to multiple uses and complementary demand → form multiple revenue paths → increase routes for fixed-cost recovery → restore room for business viability.

## State model
single-revenue phase / profitability-pressure phase / surplus-recognition phase / multi-use phase / complementary-demand phase / revenue-rewiring phase / viability-room phase.

## Applications
In regulated or small-scale operations, identify which costs are truly fixed, which waiting/human/space resources are reusable, what complementary demand exists, and whether multiple uses materially contribute to cost recovery. Do not generalize to other sectors unless the same regulatory-surplus and rewiring structure is present.

## Measurements and audit
Separate target-side facts, regulatory conditions, fixed costs, waiting time, spare human capacity, idle space, actual reusability, multi-use connection, complementary demand, contribution to cost recovery, Nakagawa Master’s value kernel, the reason for selecting it, the external-article reason, and the boundary from company-official claims. Do not invent occupancy, margin, utilization, or revenue-share thresholds absent from the Parent.

## Validity conditions
The reading applies where regulatory conditions create reusable human/time/space surplus, that surplus can actually connect to other uses or demand, and multiple revenue paths contribute to recovery or business viability. Target facts, structural interpretation, value kernel, and external-article construction must remain separate.

## Failure conditions
It fails if flattened into a lodging-company profile, reduced to simple cost cutting, multitasking, or regulation avoidance, if non-reusable costs are automatically labeled value, if Nakagawa Master’s reading is attributed to Leaneve as an official definition, or if the external-article reason is removed.

## Falsification conditions
Narrow or revise the kernel if multi-use and complementary demand repeatedly fail to contribute to fixed-cost recovery or if another mechanism consistently explains business viability better. Revise as well if the identified human/time/space surplus is not practically reusable.

## Required distinctions
target-side facts / Nakagawa Master interpretation; lodging operation / revenue rewiring; regulatory response / regulation avoidance; fixed cost / unrecovered value; surplus recognition / monetization; multitasking / revenue-structure design; value kernel / company-official view; reason for kernel selection / reason for external-article axis.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not generalize all regulation into revenue opportunity. Do not assume any idle capacity can be monetized. Do not present revenue rewiring as Leaneve’s official corporate definition. Do not replace the specific reason for explaining the regulatory economic structure to external readers with a generic phrase such as social translation.
