## Summary
The Parent is not merely a company or minpaku-operation profile of Leaneve. It preserves the object-level facts about lodging operation and regulatory staffing conditions, while Nakagawa Master reads a deeper structure: values that appear as fixed cost, waiting time, vacant rooms, human capacity, or unused time under regulatory constraints can sometimes be reconnected across lodging, shooting, work use, and complementary tasks. This is the interpretive value core called revenue rewiring. It does not mean regulatory avoidance or guaranteed profitability; it remains conditional on real demand, lawful and safe operation, service quality, sustainable staffing, economics, external dependencies, reproducibility, and standardization.

## Concepts
- Leaneve: the target company/business layer.
- Regulatory staffing condition: a condition that may function as fixed labor cost for a small lodging facility.
- Unrecovered value: time, space, human capacity, or use opportunities that are not currently recovered but may have a realistic alternative demand.
- Revenue rewiring: Nakagawa Master's structural reading of reconnecting multiple uses and revenue sources into the cost-recovery structure.
- Spatial revenue design: reading a property not as a single-use box but as a combination of time, uses, people, and demand channels.
- Social translation: selecting “turn the resident-staff requirement into leverage” and “revenue rewiring” as the external article angle instead of flattening the case into a minpaku introduction.

## Causal chain
Existing lodging demand → regulatory conditions create fixed staffing cost → small facilities cannot spread the fixed cost across many rooms → demand can exist while profitability stalls → waiting time, vacant rooms, human capacity, and use opportunities remain fragmented → redesign while accepting the regulatory condition → connect staff capacity and space to multiple lawful uses → connect lodging, shooting, work use, and complementary tasks → recover fixed/operating cost across several revenue paths → conditional room for small-scale profitability → verify reproducibility, standardization, staffing, demand, and dependency in operation.

## State model
Demand present / regulatory fixed cost / small-scale profitability pressure / supply stall / unrecovered-value visibility / human-capacity redesign / multi-use space / complementary revenue connection / revenue rewiring / conditional profitability / reproducibility test / standardization test / dependency monitoring / structural-reading social translation.

## Applications
The structure can be used to inspect small lodging facilities, time-sliced facility use, businesses with mandatory staffing, and multi-use real-estate operations. The transfer is conditional: another regulated industry must independently verify the purpose of the regulation, safety, responsibility, demand, staffing, and economics.

## Measurements and audit
Audit actual lodging demand; the fixed-cost effect of staffing requirements; occupancy and room-rate conditions when present in the Parent; realistic demand for complementary uses; legal compliance; safety and lodging quality; sustainable staff workload; continued access to suitable personnel; contribution of multiple revenue sources to fixed and operating costs; reliance on OTA or external work pipelines; structural change with scale; reproducibility and standardization. More uses alone do not prove success.

## Validity conditions
There must be real demand, an actual fixed-cost problem caused by the operating condition, reusable time/space/human capacity, real complementary demand, lawful and safe operation, preserved service quality, sustainable staffing, measurable contribution to cost recovery, and continuing verification of reproducibility and standardization.

## Failure conditions
The structure fails or reaches its boundary when complementary demand is fictional, multitasking becomes overload, compliance/safety/service quality deteriorates, extra uses do not contribute to cost recovery, external platform dependence makes the structure fragile, or the result cannot be reproduced outside one special property.

## Falsification conditions
If repeated operation shows that rewiring does not improve the economics, that supposed unrecovered value has no real demand, that added labor/quality costs exceed gains, or that the model does not reproduce under comparable conditions, the scope of the revenue-rewiring hypothesis should be reduced or revised.

## Required distinctions
Company facts vs. Nakagawa Master's reading; purpose of regulation vs. business fixed-cost effect; demand vs. profitability; idle resource vs. recoverable value; adding uses vs. revenue rewiring; staff rewiring vs. labor overload; one-site success vs. reproducibility; structural hypothesis vs. completed universal model.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not present “revenue rewiring” or “spatial revenue design company” as Leaneve's own official definition. Do not translate the Parent into advocacy for evading regulation. Do not assume idle rooms or waiting time automatically become value. Do not invent fixed KPIs or success rates absent from the Parent. Do not turn the external article angle into an advertising guarantee.
