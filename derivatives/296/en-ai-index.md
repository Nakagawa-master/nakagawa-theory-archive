## Core meaning
Theory 4 places `S=C` as a civilizational design direction for mapping success to causal contribution after Theory 3 blocks the inheritance of harmful success functions. `S=C` is not a single score, ranking formula, personality value, or existence-value equation.

C means causal contribution to a concrete state change, while preserving joint causality and uncertainty. Capability, output volume, obedience, ownership, compute, popularity, or authority must not be silently substituted for contribution. Success S must not be reduced to value capture, ownership, scale, popularity, power, or efficiency.

## AI mapping
The risk is not AI capability itself. The structural hypothesis is:

`distorted success function × high-speed, large-scale, iterative optimization → possible amplification of C/S disconnection.`

Observed or frequent success is not automatically success worth inheriting. Prediction is not prescription; frequency is not legitimacy.

## Two anti-reversal boundaries
- `B_floor ≠ contribution reward`: minimum existence conditions are not contribution points to be granted or withdrawn according to C.
- `Contribution ≠ governance right`: high contribution does not automatically create permanent, unlimited, or unchallengeable governance authority.

## Connection loop
`C → causal provenance → necessary connection → value return → next activity capacity → new C`

But `Connection ≠ dependency`, `Connection ≠ enclosure`, and causal provenance must not become total behavioral or personality scoring.
