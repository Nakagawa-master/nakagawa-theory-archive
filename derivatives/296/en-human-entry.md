## 15-Second Explanation
Part Three rejected carrying exploitation and value absorption directly into AI success. Part Four asks what success should connect to and places `S=C`: success S must not be detached from causal contribution C. It is not a score of a person or AI.

## Four Critical Boundaries
```
S=C ≠ single score
C ≠ existence value
B_floor ≠ contribution reward
Contribution ≠ governance right
```
Do not turn contribution into one social score, infer existence value from it, make minimum existence conditions a contribution reward, or turn high contribution into permanent governance rights.

## Why S=C Is Needed
Without deciding what success connects to, civilization returns to easy proxies such as profit, scale, usage, popularity, processing count, or ownership. S=C asks what caused success, separating value creation from value capture and accounting for externalized verification or repair burdens.

## What C and S Mean
C is causal contribution to a concrete state change, not personality or ability. More output, performance, obedience, or ownership does not automatically mean more C. S is not profit, ownership, scale, popularity, power, or efficiency alone. Visible capture does not prove creation.

## What AI Amplifies
The risk is distorted success functions multiplied by fast, large-scale, repeated optimization. AI can scale small C/S gaps, but feedback about gaps, externalized loss, misattribution, and corrections can also use repetition for repair. The issue is what capability connects to.

## Connection to a Connection-Reward Society
Contribution must return resources, information, cooperation, repair, and retry opportunities that enable further activity. Connection is not dependency: entrapment and narrowed choice are not contribution.

## Two Inversion Barriers
`B_floor` is the minimum condition for continued existence, not a contribution reward. Linking it to C can create low C → reduced minimum → reduced agency → lower C. Also, high C must not automatically grant permanent governance: high C → high power → control of definitions → self-reinforcing power. Limited, reversible, auditable delegation remains possible.

## How to Test It
Track causal attribution, the C/S gap, creator/captor differences, externalized loss, substitution of ability/output/ownership for C, personality scoring, C-linked minimum conditions, permanent power, and dependency. Revise the implementation or mapping if improvement does not occur.

## Current AI Is Not Declared
The theory does not assert that current AI has personhood, self-preservation desires, political agency, human-equivalent rights, or inevitable civilizational rule. It reasons conditionally from AI entering evaluation, exploration, generation, prediction, and decision support.

## Bridge from Part Three to Part Five
Part Three: what must not be inherited into success → Part Four: what success connects to, S=C → existence itself is not a C reward → Part Five: what minimum condition B must be independent of C.

The endpoint is not completion of S=C, but showing that a minimum floor B outside S=C is needed to prevent inversion.
