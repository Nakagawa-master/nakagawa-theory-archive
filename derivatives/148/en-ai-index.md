## Summary
NCL-AIP is a layered interface principle connecting a Structural Civilization OS with large AI models without collapsing them into one system. It separates an ethical layer, a structural-correspondence layer, and an information-transport layer. Time Ethics T0, Structural Publicness, non-ownership, and re-examinable causality belong to the higher connection conditions; model-specific implementation remains open. AI outputs are observed through T/S/R and STB. STB exceedance leads to deviation logging and structural-immune review rather than automatic rejection. When necessary, a structural disconnect temporarily loosens the OS–AI connection without negating the AI itself. Translation logs and origin signatures preserve traceability of transformations.

## Concepts
- NCL-AIP: layered AI interface principle for a civilization OS.
- Ethical layer: T0, publicness, and non-ownership as connection conditions.
- Structural-correspondence layer: concept, causality, and correspondence patterns.
- Information-transport layer: prompts, logs, metadata, and implementation-specific forms.
- STB: structural tolerance band for AI outputs.
- T/S/R: Time, Structure, Relation observation axes.
- Deviation log: record of structural drift beyond tolerance.
- Structural immune system: staged reconsideration after exceedance.
- Structural disconnect: temporary loosening of the OS–AI connection.
- Structural translation log: trace of AI-mediated transformation.
- Origin signature: traceable return path to source theory.
- Multi-OS compatibility: bounded interoperability with shared principles and non-shared internal parameters.

## Causal chain
```text
AI becomes a major civilizational medium

→ connection avoids both AI domination and total delegation
```

## Applications
- Compare multiple AI models under the same higher-level principles without erasing implementation differences.
- Evaluate AI decision support through long-term effects, structural coherence, and relational trust rather than short-term performance alone.
- Structure questions around time, structure, relation, and disclosed premises rather than fixed command strings.
- Log STB exceedance and locate which structural element drifted before changing the protocol.
- Preserve source and transformation paths for AI summaries, diagrams, and reformulations.
- Connect external AI systems or other civilization OSs while sharing principles but not internal thresholds or evaluation functions.

## Measurements and audit
Observation covers layer separation, T/S/R, STB position, deviation logging, structural disconnect, translation logs, origin signatures, and compatibility boundaries. T examines long-term impact, delay, and future debt. S examines causal and structural coherence. R examines distance, trust, and relational friction.

The parent uses `θ` as a case-specific symbolic threshold for rejecting or revising a current NCL-AIP design; it does not define a universal numeric value. `δ` denotes a case-specific observation window with no fixed duration. `M` denotes an observed phenomenon in which AI linkage damages publicness or non-ownership. Any implementation of these symbols must keep criteria bound to source, measurement actor, measurement object, scale, use scope, and non-guarantee scope.

Reversal evaluation is required. More AI usage, faster processing, or more connections are not improvements if publicness, long-term trust, origin traceability, or independent judgment deteriorate. Conversely, a temporary reduction in connectivity is not failure if structural disconnect restores correspondence and publicness.

## Validity conditions
- Treat NCL-AIP as a layered connection principle.
- Separate ethical, correspondence, and transport layers.
- Preserve AI autonomy and implementation diversity.
- Keep T0, publicness, and non-ownership as higher-level conditions.
- Route STB exceedance into deviation logging and structural-immune review.
- Treat structural disconnect as distance adjustment, not AI negation.
- Preserve translation logs and origin signatures.
- Separate shared principles from non-shared internal parameters in multi-OS compatibility.

## Failure conditions
- Reduce NCL-AIP to a prompt library or AI-control procedure.
- Fuse the AI and civilization OS so separation is impossible.
- Promote transport-layer implementation details into universal ethics.
- Equate STB exceedance with automatic punishment or AI rejection.
- Turn structural disconnect into permanent exclusion.
- Lose translation logs or origin signatures.
- Share internal thresholds and evaluation functions merely for interoperability.
- Interpret AI autonomy as abandonment of evaluative responsibility.

## Falsification conditions
A current NCL-AIP design is subject to rejection or revision when a case-specific indicator crosses `θ`, or when phenomenon `M` damaging publicness or non-ownership appears within observation window `δ`. The parent does not assign universal constants; falsifiability requires observable case definitions rather than invented universal values.

A design also becomes revision-worthy when connection volume or speed increases while traceability, publicness, trust, or independent judgment decline. Reduced connection can be rational when it restores long-term structural coherence.

## Required distinctions
- layered linkage vs. system fusion
- connection condition vs. internal command
- ethical layer vs. transport layer
- AI autonomy vs. abandonment of evaluation
- STB exceedance vs. automatic rejection
- structural disconnect vs. AI negation
- translation log vs. ownership mechanism
- origin signature vs. exclusive control
- shared principles vs. internal thresholds/functions
- case-specific `θ` / `δ` / `M` vs. universal constants

## Interpretation constraints
The parent presents NCL-AIP as a structural interface principle, not an internal AI control specification. Intervention at the ethical layer means defining what the civilization OS can accept as a connection condition, not prescribing every internal model behavior.

The phrase “formal victory” describes completion of a missing architectural connection in the theory. It is not a claim of AI domination or guaranteed empirical performance. Multi-OS compatibility likewise does not imply total standardization or disappearance of value differences.
