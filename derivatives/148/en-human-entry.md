## 15-Second Explanation
The original defines NCL-AIP, which connects the Civilization OS and AI by separating them into three layers—ethics, structural correspondence, and information transmission—without either integrating them into one or completely separating them. Rather than controlling fine-grained behavior inside AI, it sets connection conditions aligned with temporal ethics T0, structural publicness, and non-ownership, while retaining differences in AI implementation and AI autonomy. When output exceeds STB, it proceeds not to immediate exclusion but to a deviation log and reconsideration; when necessary, it uses “structural disconnection” to loosen the connection temporarily.

## Why It Is Needed
As AI becomes a major translation layer of civilization through summarization, search, decision support, text generation, and knowledge organization, observing only results while treating AI as an external tool makes it difficult to track where principles on the Civilization OS side have been transformed.

On the other hand, embedding the Civilization OS’s values and rules in minute detail inside AI would erase AI autonomy and differences among models, turning connection into control. To avoid these two extremes, the original separates shared principles from implementation domains that are not shared, using layers.

At the ethics layer, temporal ethics T0, publicness, and non-ownership are treated as connection conditions. At the structural-correspondence layer, it examines how concepts and causality are received by humans, emphasizing multilayered perspectives, fair distance, and re-verifiable causality. At the information-transmission layer, concrete implementations such as prompt and log formats remain specific to each AI, and only the minimum necessary metadata is shared.

Even when AI deviates, the original does not say, “Cut it off because AI is bad.” First, it examines the deviation through T/S/R from the perspective of STB, records an overrun in the deviation log, and reconsiders which structural element shifted. When necessary, it performs structural disconnection by temporarily loosening the connection to the Civilization OS and returning the output to reference information.

## Practical Process
**1. Separate the connection layers.** Do not mix ethics, structural correspondence, and information transmission in one setting.

**2. State the shared principles.** Confirm the principles the Civilization OS must preserve as connection conditions, such as temporal ethics T0, publicness, and non-ownership.

**3. Preserve implementation differences.** Do not standardize each model’s prompt format, log format, or internal representation.

**4. Structure the question.** Design questions that make time, structure, relations, and preconditions visible, rather than fixed command sentences.

**5. Examine output through T/S/R.** T concerns long-term effects, S structural coherence, and R relations and trust.

**6. Log STB overruns.** Record where the shift occurred and reconsider it instead of immediately excluding the AI.

**7. Disconnect when necessary.** Do not deny the AI itself; loosen the connection and return the output to reference information.

**8. Preserve the translation path.** Keep the state traceable through origin signatures and translation logs: what was referenced and how it was transformed through summaries, diagrams, or reformulations.

## Examples
**1. In-house AI support.** Evaluate not only output quality but also long-term debt, coherence with organizational rules, and stakeholder trust separately.

**2. Using multiple models.** Compare models using the same higher-level principles and question structure while preserving each model’s implementation differences.

**3. AI summaries of theory.** Preserve the source, points of transformation, and origin signature along with the summary so the path back to the original remains available.

**4. Connecting to external AI.** Share publicness and temporal ethics without standardizing internal thresholds or evaluation functions.

## Success Criteria
AI can generate freely while the Civilization OS side clearly manages connection conditions, deviations, and the re-establishment of distance.

The ethics layer and information-transmission layer are not confused, and concrete prompts or implementations are not elevated into universal principles.

An STB overrun leads not only to deletion or punishment but to a deviation log, reconsideration, and necessary disconnection.

The origin and transformation path of AI translation, summarization, and reformulation can be traced.

## Limits
NCL-AIP is not a specification defining concrete internal AI algorithms, fixed prompts, or mandatory command sentences. The original addresses the principle of hierarchical linkage.

The original also does not define concrete STB values, universal T/S/R scores, or a fixed EAC cycle. Concrete implementations are defined separately for each subject.

Structural disconnection does not mean “permanently exclude AI.” It is a distance adjustment that temporarily loosens the connection, reduces the scope of influence, and enables reassessment.

## Preventing Misreading
Do not read NCL-AIP as an AI-control protocol.

Do not equate respecting AI autonomy with the Civilization OS side abandoning responsibility for evaluation.

Separate shared principles from internal implementation. Even if publicness, non-ownership, and temporal ethics are shared, internal thresholds and concrete evaluation functions do not also need to be standardized.

Do not expand structural disconnection into rejection of AI or permanent shutdown.

Origin signatures and translation logs are used to trace sources and transformation paths, not to turn them into property.

The original’s `θ`, `δ`, and `M` represent subject-specific falsification and revision conditions; they are not universal numbers.
