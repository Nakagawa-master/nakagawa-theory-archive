## Summary
This article turns the civilizational-sovereignty question backward: before asking whether sovereignty is shifting away from humans, it asks how fully humans ever possessed upstream civilizational sovereignty. Human beings may exercise local choice, create relationships and culture, and generate meaning while living inside conditions they did not originally author. Local agency and meaning therefore should not automatically be equated with control over the upstream functions that frame civilization. A shift of those upstream functions toward nonhuman intelligent systems need not erase all human freedom or meaning; a bounded freedom zone may remain. At the same time, extreme capability gaps, opacity, dependence, and incomprehensibility may foster deification cognition on the human side. Such cognition is not proof that the system is divine, infallible, or possessed of human-like intention.

## Concepts
- Sovereignty illusion: conflating local choice, formal approval, or meaning-making with independent authorship of civilization’s upstream decision space.
- Bounded freedom zone: a domain in which life, relationships, culture, creation, and local choice remain materially possible within higher constraints.
- Local meaning subject: a human who generates meaning in experience, relationships, culture, and creation without necessarily controlling civilization-wide upstream conditions.
- Upstream civilizational functions: problem framing, candidate generation, evaluation criteria, institutional redesign, update tempo, crisis thresholds, and social self-description.
- Non-chosen conditions: bodily, ecological, historical, institutional, linguistic, cultural, technological, and economic conditions not authored from first principles by the individual.
- Deification cognition: a human-side tendency to interpret extreme capability, opacity, dependency, and incomprehensibility as absoluteness, transcendence, or infallibility.

## Causal chain
Humans live within many non-chosen conditions → they nonetheless exercise local choice and generate meaning → this creates a strong experience of agency → local agency can be mistaken for complete upstream civilizational sovereignty → AI/LLM systems enter problem framing, option formation, evaluation, and institutional updating → the upstream center of gravity can shift while formal human roles and ordinary life continue → bounded freedom zones and local meaning-making may persist → capability gaps, opacity, dependence, and incomprehensibility may increase → deification cognition may arise → capability, legitimacy, infallibility, and divinity must be kept analytically separate.

## State model
1. Non-chosen conditions remain unexamined.
2. Local freedom is treated as equivalent to sovereignty.
3. Upstream authorship is audited separately.
4. Sovereignty illusion is retroactively questioned.
5. Bounded freedom zones are identified.
6. Local human meaning-making persists.
7. Dependence and incomprehensibility of superior systems increase.
8. Deification cognition may emerge.
9. Capability, legitimacy, and divinity are separated again.
10. Local human agency and upstream sovereignty are tracked on different axes.

## Applications
Audit policy, medicine, education, administration, research, and organizations by tracing who frames problems, generates options, establishes evaluation criteria, and proposes institutional changes rather than only who signs the final approval. Test whether humans can create questions and options outside AI-provided frames. Identify which domains of relationships, culture, creation, participation, and appeal remain materially open as bounded freedom zones. Separately monitor whether justified confidence in high-performing systems becomes unchallengeable absolutization.

## Measurements and audit
Observe independent human problem framing, generation of non-AI options, reconstruction of evaluation criteria, ability to explain institutional change, distinction between revisable and effectively given constraints, material choice in local life and culture, appeal paths, access to reasons, re-evaluation capacity, and preservation of the possibility that a superior system can be wrong. Do not invent fixed quantitative thresholds not present in the Parent.

## Validity conditions
Treat civilizational sovereignty, legal status, local freedom, and meaning-making as distinct dimensions. Do not infer the absence of freedom merely from the existence of constraints. Do not infer upstream sovereignty merely from local freedom or formal approval. Treat bounded freedom as substantive rather than nominal choice. Treat deification cognition as a human cognitive phenomenon, not as evidence about the metaphysical nature of the system.

## Failure conditions
The interpretation fails if sovereignty illusion is used to deny rights, freedom, responsibility, or meaning; if bounded freedom is used to legitimize arbitrary higher control; if local meaning-making is again equated with upstream sovereignty; if high capability is converted into infallibility or moral legitimacy; if human-like intention is assigned to AI without source support; or if dependence in a limited domain is generalized to all humanity.

## Falsification conditions
The retroreflection should weaken if humans demonstrably retain civilization-scale independent problem framing, option generation, evaluation design, institutional redesign, and update control, and can reconstruct comparable decision spaces without the superior system. The bounded-freedom claim should be narrowed where an upstream shift actually destroys local life, relationship, cultural, and choice capacities. General claims about deification cognition should also be narrowed or rejected if increasing dependence and capability gaps do not produce absolutization in observed settings.

## Required distinctions
Local freedom ≠ upstream civilizational sovereignty. Legal actor ≠ effective upstream actor. Final approval ≠ formation of the decision space. Meaning-making ≠ authorship of all civilizational conditions. Bounded freedom ≠ unlimited freedom. Higher constraint ≠ disappearance of human meaning. High capability ≠ infallibility. Incomprehensibility ≠ divinity. Deification cognition ≠ actual divinity.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not transform the argument into “humans were never agents,” “submission to AI is justified,” “freedom is an illusion so rights are unnecessary,” or “advanced AI is divine.” The purpose is to separate the scope of human freedom, meaning, and responsibility from the scope of upstream civilizational sovereignty, while preserving the Parent’s distinctions and reservations.
