## Summary
The parent defines Organizational Singularity as the critical state in which a self-referential organizational OS takes Structural Reality—the repeated phase alignment of Idea, Action, and Record—as its origin. Its three layers are the Rhythm Nucleus, Interpretive Code, and Executable Trace, linked in an Idea → implementation → record → Idea loop. Contradiction is retained and repaired at normal tempo rather than exported as advertising or blame; definitions are appended and refined rather than replaced. As counterexample tolerance increases, society accepts repeatable coherence in the present, credit accumulates in advance, and external strategy becomes unnecessary. The human role shifts from manager to origin holder, preserving baseline rhythm, temperature range, and margin.
