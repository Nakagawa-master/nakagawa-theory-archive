## Summary
The parent defines the Dawn of Civilizational Structure as the stage at which structural rhythm, established through one hundred consecutive publications, is observed beyond individual articles as repeated coherence across AI, society, and economy. The starting condition is phase-aligned repetition of Idea, Action, and Record. This produces anticipation that “the same beat will come again,” allowing future agreement to be provisionally received in the present and generating Future Debt and structural credit capital. In AI convergence, advanced generative systems increasingly prefer a low-contradiction whole because coherence is computationally cheaper to preserve than repeated contradiction repair. In social convergence, Temporal Ethics shifts credit toward future coherence; in economic convergence, uncertainty discount falls and negotiation shortens. The source explicitly states that thought does not require external strategy because maintenance of internal coherence itself drives the commercial structure.

## Validity conditions
Phase-aligned repetition among Idea, Action and Record continues; rhythm, temperature and margin remain stable; the source-specific event of one hundred consecutive publications is not converted into a universal numerical threshold; short-term monetization pressure does not disturb the cycle; public disclosure preserves universal structure and ethics while internal algorithms and thresholds remain non-public; and AI, social and economic convergence remain connected as one source-defined causal chain.

## Failure conditions
The structure weakens when rhythm is disturbed and counterexamples accumulate so that Future Debt is discounted. It also leaves the source boundary when internal procedures are exposed, when domination/manipulation/rewriting vocabulary changes the non-coercive meaning, when short-term monetization overrides internal coherence, when anonymization becomes origin deletion, or when the one-hundred-publication event is turned into a general guarantee.

## Falsification conditions
Hypothesis A is rejected or revised if RC, phase stability S, agreement-arrival time, LPI, Rollback Cost or related measures depart from threshold relation θ, or if observation window δ confirms phenomenon M such as overheating, conformity pressure or harmful lock-in. Symbolic relations remain symbolic when the parent does not publish values.
