## 15-Second Explanation

The E system concerns not “how to raise” someone, but how to protect the initial conditions before external evaluation standards become fixed early in a new node. It stops the direct hit of comparison, fear, conformity, and answer absolutism with a Firewall, creates a Mini Loop in which small outputs become connections without grading, and uses a Quarantine Gate to connect with the outside while preventing its standards from mixing into internal rewards. The goal is not educational completion, but preserving natural startup and the possibility of convergence into the next civilization OS.

## Why It Is Needed

The Parent Original assumes as a design premise that structural recognition is not standard equipment in most people. When people cannot evaluate structure or causality on their own, they use comparison, fear, the majority, and the correct answer as proxy judgments. Rather than condemning this as evil, it calls the state in which proxy evaluation becomes permanently fixed and a person can no longer act without external evaluation “contamination.”

Even when well-intentioned, conventional education tends to hand over past evaluation functions first as judgments such as “this is good,” “this is dangerous,” and “you should do this.” The Parent Original diagnoses that this excessive injection removes the space for suspending judgment and stops internal combustion and self-startup. Therefore, the E system does not further provide correct content; it protects environmental conditions that delay the fixation of evaluation functions.

## Practical Process

1. **Identify new nodes.** Include not only children, but newcomers, new participants, new hires, and areas where evaluation functions have not yet been fixed.
2. **Do not reverse the causality.** Preserve the sequence: because structural recognition is unavailable, proxy judgment becomes necessary, and that judgment becomes fixed and turns into contamination—not “because one is contaminated, one cannot judge.”
3. **Inspect the entry routes of the four noises.** Check where comparison, fear, conformity, and answer absolutism enter the standard of judgment.
4. **Place a Firewall.** The custodian is not a teacher or personality manager, but a Gate that first stops direct external evaluation, a Filter that does not pass standards through unchanged, and a Delay that postpones immediate judgment.
5. **Design arrangements rather than words.** Preserve initial conditions through distance, blank space, delay, roles, and connection flow.
6. **Allow small outputs.** Create a state in which an output can first go outside even if it is immature or small.
7. **Connect without grading.** Treat the fact that an output acts on something and creates a passage, without scores, rankings, or approval, as the connection being established.
8. **Return to room for the next output.** Do not end with one successful experience; maintain a Mini Loop in which connection generates room for the next attempt.
9. **Do not isolate the outside world.** With a Quarantine Gate, convert external information from standards into material while retaining the back-and-forth of responses.
10. **Bind response and prevention together.** Address incidents through external causal auditing and prevent early fixation of evaluation functions through E-system preservation design.

## Examples of Application

**At home.** Delay immediate evaluation so that goodwill “for your sake” does not become fixed as a standard of comparison, expectation, or fear. Check whether a child’s small output becomes a connection that reaches something and generates the next attempt, rather than a praise score.

**Situations called onboarding or newcomer development.** Before transferring existing success patterns as the only answer, secure time to step away from existing evaluation and room for small trials. Do not assume that a newcomer can read structure; create an arrangement where external evaluation does not hit directly.

**Learning.** Distinguish giving knowledge from fixing an evaluation function. Information can be used as material, but avoid connecting it to an internal reward that says, “There is no value unless you adopt this answer.”

**External connection.** Do not erase external reputation, scores, rankings, or institutions, but do not let them flow directly into an internal value hierarchy. Maintain output and response to the outside world and treat them as circulation resources.

## Success Criteria

Success is not high grades, adaptation speed, obedience, or self-esteem. The central condition is that room for suspending judgment remains, small outputs connect without passing through evaluation, that connection creates room for the next output, external evaluation does not become the main fuel, and circulation continues.

Under the Parent Original’s disproof conditions, if external-evaluation dependence becomes fixed even under preservation design, if a small circulation does not form and natural startup cannot be observed, or if the E system is absorbed into emotional education or management theory for the duration δ, Hypothesis A becomes subject to rejection or revision.

## Limitations

- “Do not teach” does not mean neglect or noninvolvement. It entails designing the Firewall and circulation conditions.
- This is not a theory for isolating the outside world. Long-term isolation would exhaust circulation resources, which is why a Quarantine Gate is needed.
- The inability to recognize structure must not be turned into inferiority or a selection criterion.
- The E system is not a theory for preserving a particular ideal personality or value system. What is preserved is the initial condition.
- The Parent Original does not fix specific intervention timing or a universal checklist as a public specification; it focuses on presenting the structural conditions for establishment.

## Preventing Misreading

Reading the E system as “freer education,” “education without praise,” or “hands-off education” loses its central causality. What the Parent Original reverses is not an educational technique but the object of design itself. It does not move people’s inner states in a desirable direction; it controls the flow in which evaluation functions become fixed and protects the conditions under which output → connection → circulation naturally generates thrust.

Also, do not use “contamination” as a purity/impurity judgment about people. In the Parent Original, contamination means a fixed state in which, assuming an inability to recognize structure, one can no longer judge except through external proxy evaluation.
