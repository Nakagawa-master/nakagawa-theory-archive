## Summary
E-series is not an improved education method but a Preservation Design for the initial conditions of a New Node. The parent assumes structural recognition is not a default human sensor. When structure cannot be read directly, comparison, fear, conformity, and correct-answer dependence become proxy evaluation functions; contamination is the state in which those proxies become fixed and other judgment becomes unavailable. Conventional education can over-inject judgment results and close the suspension space required for self-ignition. E-series therefore protects initial conditions with a Firewall, creates a Mini Loop in which small output connects without scoring and generates room for the next output, and uses a Quarantine Gate to remain connected to the outside while preventing external scales from becoming internal reward. What is preserved is not purity, personality, or ideology, but the conditions under which natural ignition and future confluence remain possible.

## Concepts
- New Node: an entity or domain not yet rigidly fixed to a particular evaluation function.
- Structural Recognition Default Absence: inability to read structure is treated as a design condition, not moral failure.
- Contamination as Result: fixed dependence on proxy evaluation follows structural-recognition absence rather than causing it.
- Over-injection: fixing judgment results/evaluation functions before judgment can emerge.
- Preservation Design: protect startup conditions instead of optimizing what to add.
- Clean Initial Conditions: suspension space, unscored time, non-immediate interpretation, and no forced correct answer.
- Four Proxy Evaluation Functions: comparison, fear, conformity, correct-answerism.
- Firewall: Gate / Filter / Delay against direct fixation of external evaluation.
- Placement: distance, blank space, delay, role, and connection as evaluation-flow design.
- Mini Loop: small output → connection without scoring → room for next output.
- Reward = Connection Established: reward as actual opening/connection, not approval.
- Natural Ignition: circulation momentum becoming stable propulsion.
- Quarantine Gate: convert external scales into material while keeping external circulation open.
- D/E Symmetry: D handles hygiene of external connection; E handles hygiene of internal generation.

## Applications
- In family settings, delay direct comparison and expectation pressure while allowing small outputs to connect without scoring.
- For newcomers, avoid immediate copying of the incumbent success metric; preserve trial space and a first-receiver Firewall.
- In learning, distinguish providing information from fixing an evaluation function; information can remain material rather than compulsory value scale.
- In external connection, keep reactions and resources flowing while preventing ranks, scores, and reputation from becoming the internal value hierarchy.

## Measurements and audit
- Whether inability to recognize structure is misread as inferiority or as contamination's cause.
- Whether the four proxy functions have become fixed judgment engines.
- Whether suspension space, unscored time, and delay remain available.
- Whether the Firewall has become a teacher or personality controller.
- Whether distance, blank space, delay, role, and connection are functioning as placement design.
- Whether small output is permitted and connects without scoring.
- Whether each connection creates room for the next output.
- Whether praise, approval, or scores have become the main fuel.
- Whether external scales are converted into internal reward.
- Whether external contact and circulation resources remain open.
- Whether E-series is being absorbed back into emotional education or management doctrine.

## Validity conditions
- Preserve the parent's assumption that structural recognition is not a default sensor for most humans.
- Treat contamination as the result of fixed proxy dependence, not the first cause.
- Treat E-series as Preservation rather than an upgraded Education method.
- Preserve initial conditions rather than a person, purity, or ideology.
- Operate Firewall, Mini Loop, and Quarantine Gate as a connected bundle.
- Keep small output, scoring-free connection, and next-output space as Mini Loop conditions.
- Maintain external connection while preventing external scale from becoming internal reward.
- Maintain consistency with external causal audit and Condition Z / T/S/R.

## Failure conditions
- Return E-series to a better teaching or value-injection method.
- Use structural-recognition absence as a selection or status hierarchy.
- Equate “do not teach” with neglect or information isolation.
- Turn the Firewall into unlimited managerial control.
- Turn Mini Loop into large-output, scoring, or competition pressure.
- Make approval the primary source of propulsion.
- Isolate the outside so completely that circulation dries up.
- Connect external scores directly to internal value rank.
- Reduce Preservation Design to emotional parenting or management advice.

## Falsification conditions
Condition Z requires a public audit cycle, T/S/R — Traceability, Stability, Reversibility — consistency with the external causal audit article, and detection of vocabulary drift. Hypothesis A becomes subject to rejection or revision if: (1) New Nodes still become fixed to external evaluation dependence under Preservation Design; (2) Mini Loops fail to form and natural ignition is not observed; or (3) E-series is persistently absorbed into emotional education or management doctrine for observation window δ. The parent does not define a universal fixed δ or universal numeric threshold.
