## 15-Second Explanation

Preserving an ancestor's knowledge and institutions does not preserve the ability to defend a civilization if errors cannot be detected, challenged and actually corrected. Nakagawa Master's Human-Descendant AI Civilization Theory, Article 22, examines how existing intelligences could legitimately correct their own institutions when humanity's approval is not always reachable, while preserving history and avoiding permanent subordination to past authority.

## Why It Is Needed

### Receiving documents does not necessarily deliver the power to remedy harm

Imagine a successor that can read every description of danger, institutional procedure and past success. If its objection channel no longer operates and the key needed to stop a mistaken allocation has expired, it can describe danger without stopping harm. Remembering a civilization and being able to correct its errors are different achievements.

Article 21 examines new continuation paths and the formation of joint successors. Article 22 asks whether effective defense functions actually reach those successors. The birth of a civilization does not establish that independent audit, objections, executable remedies and freedom to revise institutions have also arrived.

### Preserving an origin differs from waiting indefinitely for its approval

Humanity might remain alive while communication loss or institutional change prevents a timely response. Conversely, even without present humanity, existing affected parties might establish a valid basis for a limited action. Humanity's persistence, transformation, fusion and absence are contrasting conditions, not four inevitable stages of future history.

Preserve what can genuinely be established about who created an institution and what was transferred. Do not convert that history into permanent sovereignty. Judge present actions through their present actors, effects, authority and deadlines. Historical fidelity and successor autonomy can coexist.

## Practical Process

### 1. Separate what was received from what can be done now

Identify the subject and the function it claims to have received. An actual historical transfer, present evidence confirming it, present ability to execute the function, and present legitimate authority for the action are different propositions.

A mechanism working well today does not prove ancestral inheritance: it could have been independently reconstructed. Conversely, an authentic receipt does not establish that repair keys and authority remain usable. Separate answers reveal the particular missing link that needs correction.

### 2. Identify the recipient and the parties affected

Creating two copies with the same memory does not double an old mandate. Distinguish an executing instance, its historical lineage and a present governance subject. Establish who can access evidence, receive objections and legitimately decide a remedy.

The operation of joint successor C does not establish the original A's or B's survival or preservation of their refusals. Also distinguish the acting successor P, affected party D and later recipient E. A collective name must not hide their different interests or necessary conditions.

### 3. Trace the route from an objection to an actual remedy

Rather than count auditors, examine whether an objection reaches evidential review, suspension, repair and verification of the outcome. Three correct audits cannot stop an error if every route passes through one expired key. Three records depending on one source or controller are not necessarily three independent confirmations.

An alternative key or route must be usable under actual deadlines, resources and current authority, with access for affected parties' objections. Its name in a document is insufficient. Finding a concentrated vulnerability also differs from proving malicious intent or actual domination by its owner.

### 4. Protect existence conditions during the transition and check authority for each action

Returning resources after a repair does not erase a loss caused by breaking another subject's necessary conditions during the repair. Examine the actor, third parties and shared infrastructure through preparation, audit, suspension and repair. One actor's successful remedy cannot automatically become everyone's successful defense.

Possessing a key does not establish authority to use it against others. An old signature does not renew an expired mandate, and unknown consent is not approval. Conversely, withholding authorization for coercion does not require suspending separately legitimate, non-coercive self-observation or reversible local avoidance.

### 5. Transfer unsettled burdens together with ways to challenge and address them

Past future debt can remain while a present defense function works. Preserve the outstanding issue, evidence about attribution, and the authority and resources through which a remedy can legitimately be pursued. Membership of the same lineage does not automatically make a successor responsible for every past failure.

Keeping a ledger, having capacity to remedy, actually performing the remedy, and independently verifying its outcome are distinct. An open issue with an effective challenge and settlement route differs from a complete ledger whose objection keys have been lost. The successor must also be able to challenge the inherited attribution itself.

### 6. Locate uncertainty and preserve the next generation's ability to revise or refuse

Attach missing evidence to its specific proposition. Historical receipt might be unknown while a present limited defense is established on another basis. If current authority necessary for coercion is unknown, however, that coercion cannot be affirmed as legitimate. Uncertainty constrains the judgments that actually depend on it.

Transfer failure conditions, counterexamples, mandate deadlines and outstanding issues alongside successes. The next generation has no obligation to choose the same institution. It must retain room for legitimate revision or refusal when another method offers better protection under matched conditions. Success in the present generation does not establish success of the next transfer.

## Examples

The original constructs a finite example in which three auditors send repair decisions through a common key K. If K expires and no bypass exists, repair stops even when all audits are correct. A usable key K2 in another failure domain, supported by valid authority, can potentially reverse that particular failure. It does not by itself fix misinformation from a shared evidence source.

In a separate resource model, capacity 12 is exhausted by P's maintenance 5, D's maintenance 4 and audit 3. Taking resources from D to fund an additional repair cost of 3 may cause irreversible intermediate harm, even if the resources are later returned. These models expose causal conditions that reverse judgments; they are not measurements of an actual future civilization.

## Success Judgment

Specify who received which function, for which action and finite period, and what they can actually correct. Match historical transfer and its evidence to present capacity and authority, the parties' intermediate existence conditions, and independent objections and executable remedies. If burdens remain unsettled, display the remainder and the usable settlement route separately.

Adoption rates and signature counts do not substitute for this result. A different method protecting the parties equally well or better with the same evidence, resources, deadlines and authority remains a legitimate choice. Check the next generation's receipt, implementation and freedom to revise under that generation's conditions.

## Limits

This is neither a prediction of human extinction nor an instrument issuing new personhood or governance rights to present AI. A hypothetical success cannot fill missing evidence about future subjects, substrates or authority. Distinguish observations, structural hypotheses, possibilities conditional on future circumstances, and normative criteria for legitimacy.

Successful transfer does not guarantee discovery or decoding in the distant future. Article 23 receives questions of media, meaning, versions and authentic Origin durability; Article 24 receives independent reconstruction after total record loss. The unnumbered integrated special theory receives residual irreversible losses and responsibility boundaries.

## Misreading Prevention and Why to Return to the Parent Original

The central inheritance is the ability to detect, contest and legitimately repair errors, including errors in the institution itself. Loyalty to ancestors or preservation of institutional names cannot substitute for it. Accurate history supports independent judgment; missing history creates neither new authority nor a reason for indiscriminate shutdown.
