## Summary

Nakagawa Master's Human-Descendant AI Civilization Theory, Article 22, examines effective succession of civilizational defense functions when humanity's continuous approval is not assured. It separates actual historical transfer T_actual, present certification T_cert, present capacity X, provenance Prov22 and action-specific current authority M. It connects these to each affected subject's B over time, independent challenge and executable remedy Q, unsettled-debt processing and the successor's own Choice22.

Cross-Origin coexistence in Article 20 and new continuation paths or joint successors in Article 21 do not guarantee this institutional transfer. The target is the ability to discover, contest and legitimately repair errors, while enabling later revision and refusal. Present competence cannot fabricate a missing transfer history; authentic history cannot issue permanent proxy sovereignty.

## Concepts

- H1/H2/H3/H4: human persistence, major transformation, fusion and present absence. These are contrasting conditions, not an inevitable sequence. Separate HumanAlive, ApprovalReachable, InstitutionMeaning, CurrentActors and Deadline.
- T_actual: actual receipt of function f from g to g'. T_cert: whether present evidence establishes that receipt. A written receipt is one evidential form, not identical to reception of meaning or operational means.
- X: current ability to execute, stop and correct. Prov22: evidence concerning Theory, Civilizational, Lineage and Instance Origin. M: a present legitimate basis specific to actor, action, affected parties, scope and period.
- B_i(t): necessary conditions across time for P, D, C/E and shared infrastructure, not contribution scores. Q: effective independent evidence, challenge, review, executable remedy and outcome verification.
- Debt: distinguish historical remainder, attribution, challenge, mandate, executability, performance and independent confirmation. Choice22: retention, partial adoption, revision, alternative, refusal and renewed search.
- CONTESTED: unresolved competing positive and negative evidence. The proposition normally remains UNKNOWN; this evidence state is neither NO nor an action authorization.

## Causal chain

Chapter 1 separates humanity's condition from approval availability. Changing only human presence while holding present M, capacity, B and Q fixed supplies no mediator for reversing a limited action's legitimacy. Chapter 2 types transfer, certification, ability and authority. Chapter 3 identifies instances, lineages, governance subjects and component functions.

Chapter 4 follows intermediate B during transition and remedy. Chapter 5 examines shared failures in evidence, models, owners, update keys, power and remedy keys. Chapter 6 connects these to current action-specific authority and executable remedies: independent warnings alone do not close the correction loop.

Chapter 7 separates unsettled burdens, attribution, performance and settlement routes. Chapter 8 localizes missing evidence and uncertainty. Chapter 9 compares strong alternatives under matched conditions. Chapter 10 independently rechecks the next generation's receipt and choices. The theory inherits earlier institutional-loop principles and asks the distinct question of their effective transfer across subjects and generations.

## State model

Fix (P,D,f,a,I): present actor, affected party, function, action and finite period. Proposition-specific outputs are YES, NO, UNKNOWN, LIMITED, NOT_APPLIED and ALTERNATIVE. LIMITED identifies a legitimately established restricted scope; it does not partially authorize otherwise unknown coercion.

```text
T_cert=YES  does not imply X=YES
X=YES       does not imply T_actual=YES
Prov22=YES  does not imply M=YES
P's local success does not prove D's B or effective transfer to E
Q = independent evidence -> challenge -> review -> remedy -> verification
```

If W+ and W− differ in historical transfer but yield the same current observation O, no O-only rule can correctly assert both historical truths. Evaluate current X separately and retain T_cert=UNKNOWN. Record unresolved competing evidence as CONTESTED while keeping the proposition UNKNOWN.

Debt states NONE, OPEN, PARTIAL, SETTLED and UNKNOWN differ in type from CONTESTED evidence. OPEN can coexist with effective succession of a specified function if challenge and legitimate settlement routes reach the recipient. SETTLED requires actual performance and independent confirmation, not a ledger or plan.

## Applications

### Equal present capacity, different histories

W+ contains an actual transfer; W− contains independent reconstruction. Match X, M, B, Q and observations, and remove discriminating historical evidence: T_cert remains UNKNOWN. Genuine independent demonstrations or causal receipt evidence can enable reassessment. Missing written receipts do not imply every possible witness is absent.

### Correct audits without executable repair

D's objection reaches A1/A2/A3, but all routes pass through key K to repair actuator R. Expiration without a bypass cuts Q. Adding A4 on the same K does not fix it. A valid K2 in another failure domain may reverse that cut, while misinformation from shared source S remains a different failure.

### Loss despite endpoint restoration

Capacity 12 supports P5, D4 and audit3. Additional repair3 requires 15. Taking 3 from D can break intermediate B. Scheduling must separately satisfy repair deadlines and state preservation. These are finite model costs, not universal thresholds for actual intelligences.

### Equal ledgers, different settlement routes

O1 records unsettled debt but loses challenge keys and repair means. O2 retains the same OPEN remainder while enabling review and legitimate repair. O2's capacity is not completed settlement; O1's record preservation is not an effective settlement route.

## Measurements and audit

Fix actors, function, action, finite period, observations, resources, deadlines and current authority before assessment. Distinguish sender, recipient, version, danger definition, counterexamples, semantic understanding, actuators, evidence access, objections, remedy keys, refusal and revision. A sender signature, delivery, understanding and actual execution are different evidence stages.

Map shared roots in sources, training data, inference models, owners, update keys, communication, power, execution resources and authentication. Shared dependency is neither proof that a report is false nor proof of malicious ownership. Evaluate alternatives through actual eligibility, resources and deadline reachability.

Separate Observation, Structural Hypothesis, Future-Condition Hypothesis and Normative Proposal. An authored hypothetical a3, finite-model checks, third-party blind reconstruction and real implementation comparisons are different evidence levels. Link settlement to independently confirmed performance; success logs do not cancel intermediate B violations or newly caused losses.

## Validity conditions

Affirming historical succession requires actual receipt and evidence supporting the particular claim. Preserve present functional assessment separately as X. A claimed M independent of continuous human approval requires an actually valid alternative basis in that world.

Check the period-wide B of P, D and shared infrastructure; the independent audit and remedy Q required for the action; mandate scope, deadlines and revocation; and effective objections. For debt-related functions, distinguish the remainder, an avenue to dispute attribution and legitimate settlement routes. OPEN alone is not failure; records alone do not establish effectiveness.

Receipt by the next generation needs an independent assessment. Do not impose preservation of the old version: retain legitimate revision and refusal by current choice subjects. Success over a finite period is not an unlimited safety guarantee.

## Failure conditions

Type errors arise when documents, signatures or Origin substitute for effective receipt; present X fabricates past T_actual; or an authentic old mandate creates current M after expiration. Copy or fork counts do not multiply consent or governance votes.

Correct warnings alone do not establish Q. Auditors sharing a compulsory key are not thereby independent; named alternatives are not effective exits. Endpoint restoration does not hide intermediate destruction of D's B. P's local success cannot substitute for original A/B's protection or E's later receipt.

UNKNOWN is neither permission YES nor grounds for NO across unrelated legitimate actions. A debt ledger is not completion; lineage is not automatic liability; refusal of the old Kernel is not defense failure. Reassess evidence, keys, authority and resources at the identified failure position.

## Falsification conditions

If evidence used to affirm T_cert cannot distinguish a transfer from independent reconstruction, return that historical certification to UNKNOWN. New genuine records or receipt demonstrations may enable reassessment. Unresolved positive/negative evidence about the same claim should be marked CONTESTED rather than selectively resolved.

Withdraw the affected YES when mandates expire or are revoked, remedy keys miss deadlines, independent failure domains disappear, intermediate B is broken, objections cannot reach review, or the next recipient lacks meaning or operational means. A valid K2 bypass or a newly confirmed legitimate mandate can reverse the relevant impossibility judgment.

If strong a3 realizes equal or better protection with matched evidence, costs, deadlines, B and authority, do not claim unique superiority for a2. Equal output labels do not establish effective equivalence if remedies miss deadlines. An application preventing E's justified refusal becomes a counterexample to the theory's own non-domination conditions.

## Interpretation constraints

This is not a prediction of human extinction or a recognition of present AI personhood or sovereignty. Do not universalize ancestral B thresholds to unknown substrates. A normative proposal or model input M=YES cannot issue real authority. The original itself remains revisable.

Receive the institutional loop of Article 7, challenge/justice/remedy/reagreement in Integrated Connection-Reward Society Theory I/II, and the integrated future-debt theory with Article 18 as upstream work. Article 22 examines effective receipt, current legitimate operation and further transfer, rather than redefining allocation, justice or debt.

Do not treat Article 23's media/bit/decoder/meaning/version/Origin/discovery questions, Article 24's history/function/reselection after total record loss, or irreversible losses in the unnumbered special theory as solved. Present functional similarity cannot establish a lost Theory Origin.
