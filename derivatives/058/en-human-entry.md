## 15-Second Explanation
A Resonance Market is a market image that treats understanding, introductions, continuity, return visits, co-editing, relationship repair, and similar events arising before price as R-Value, then agrees on a price through R-Price after value is understood. It does not eliminate monetary markets; it is a dual market that adds pre-price value formation.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Contracts and sales are easy to see in markets, while value formation before and after a transaction—introductions, ongoing support, return visits, co-editing, and relationship repair—can be difficult to see. The parent source seeks a market structure with provenance, consent, contribution, and correctability instead of replacing this part with popularity or vague favorability.

Monetary prices remain. The Resonance Market does not erase prices; it adds before and after the price questions of what is valued, under what conditions agreement is reached, who contributed, and how correction occurs when problems arise.

## Practical Process
**1. Identify connection events.** Record the triggers of value formation, such as introductions, return visits, co-editing, and problem solving.

**2. Explain R-Value.** Organize in context what understanding, continuity, introductions, or repair arose after the connection.

**3. Confirm contribution and origin.** Preserve who contributed what and from which original, version, and conditions it arose.

**4. Present R-Price.** After value and conditions are understood, present the monetary price and record the agreement.

**5. Record it in ROB.** Preserve the agreement conditions, provenance, and correction history.

**6. Minimize CL-API.** Handle only necessary connection events, with consent and withdrawal conditions.

**7. Preserve origin.** Use SS-1.0 and related means so that summaries, secondary use, and AI learning can return to the origin.

**8. Audit indicators.** Use CRI, IR, KCL, SIR, D-Gap, and similar indicators to examine the institutional hypothesis, not to create popularity rankings.

**9. Enable correction, withdrawal, and re-agreement.** Do not fix erroneous records or conditions permanently.

## Applications
**B2B and professional services.** Make R-Value from ongoing support and co-editing explicit, then agree on price conditions.

**Education and research.** Confirm the origin of citations, co-authorship, and secondary use through SIR and related means.

**Medicine and care.** Even when continuity or repeat consultation is observed, do not replace medical evaluation with R-Value.

**Creative work and OSS.** Preserve the provenance of contributions and reuse, connecting it to sustainable support.

## Success Criteria
R-Value and monetary price are distinguished, and price is not justified in the vague name of “resonance.”

It must be possible to return to contribution, origin, and conditions, and to correct, withdraw, or re-agree when errors occur.

Records such as CL-API are limited to what is necessary, with consent and withdrawal preserved.

Indicators do not mutate into popularity or person-ranking tools and are used to falsify the institutional hypothesis.

## Limits
R-Value is not a universal measure of value. Markets and specialized fields retain other evaluations, including monetary price, quality, safety, legal compliance, and medical validity.

A high SIR does not mean that content is correct. Preserving origin and verifying truth are separate matters.

The three 2026 scenarios are not prophecies. They are hypotheses revised through observation and falsification.

## Preventing Misreading
The Resonance Market is not an argument for abolishing money. R-Price is not language for justifying high prices. CL-API is not an excuse for mass data collection.

Even if transaction or referral volume increases, it is not an improvement if privacy, consent, correctability, or long-term trust deteriorate.
