## 1. Summary
The parent proposes a market architecture in which connection, resonance, continuity, referral, co-editing, reconnection, and trust capital are treated as value-forming events that precede price agreement. It does not abolish monetary markets. Instead, it introduces a second layer in which pre-price value formation is observed and recorded before monetary price is negotiated.

The design combines R-Value, R-Price, ROB, CL-API, SS-1.0, trust capital, and the observation concepts CRI, IR, KCL, SIR, and D-Gap. These are not a single human-ranking score. They are distinct structures or observation frames used to test whether connection-derived value is actually being formed, preserved, attributed, corrected, and made reversible.

The parent also presents three 2026 scenarios—gradual penetration, cross-border reflux, and shock acceleration. They are scenarios to be tested and revised, not guaranteed forecasts.

## 2. Concepts
- **R-Value:** value formed through connection and resonance events before final price agreement.
- **R-Price:** a protocol for placing price after value understanding; not a license to justify arbitrary high pricing.
- **ROB (Resonance Order Book):** a ledger of agreements, conditions, lineage, and corrections around R-Value.
- **CL-API (Connection Ledger API):** a minimal interface for recording connection events under consent, privacy, correction, and withdrawal constraints.
- **SS-1.0 (Signature Schema):** a provenance-preservation convention for summaries, reuse, and AI learning.
- **Trust capital:** contextual history of continuity, referral, correction, reconnection, and related relational events.
- **Dual market:** coexistence of monetary price markets and resonance-value formation, not immediate replacement of money.
- **CRI / IR / KCL / SIR / D-Gap:** distinct observation or structural concepts tied to the parent’s audit logic; not interchangeable universal scores.

## 5. Applications

- **Education and research:** preserve attribution, co-authorship, knowledge transfer, and later reuse as connected value events.
- **Care contexts:** distinguish continuity and natural reconnection from one-time outcomes without turning people into scores.

## 6. Measurements and audit
The parent uses several named observation concepts. Their epistemic status must remain separated.

- **CRI:** observes aspects of connection and resonance after interaction. It does not authorize a universal human ranking.
- **IR:** concerns what kind of return, referral, influence, or continued response follows a connection. Raw reaction count alone is insufficient.
- **KCL:** concerns the layer through which knowledge connections persist and circulate. It is not simply a knowledge-volume KPI.
- **SIR:** concerns preservation of origin/signature through summaries or secondary use. Provenance preservation does not prove substantive truth.
- **D-Gap:** concerns a mismatch between intended structural depth and actual depth reached. A smaller gap is not automatically success if consent, relevance, or burden worsens.
- **R-Value / R-Price:** value articulation and price placement must remain distinct; no fixed conversion ratio is created unless the parent explicitly supplies one.
- **2026 scenarios:** three named scenarios are a source-explicit scenario classification, not probabilities or guaranteed outcomes.

Reversible evaluation is central: more connections, referrals, records, model participation, or faster agreement do not count as improvement if trust, freedom to refuse, privacy, attribution, correction, or long-term value formation deteriorate.

## 7. Validity conditions
- Actors, purpose, consent, and roles are explicit.
- R-Value definitions and observation windows are interpretable.
- Contributor, origin, condition, and revision history remain traceable.
- Monetary price and resonance-derived value are not collapsed into one construct.
- Refusal, correction, withdrawal, redistribution, and re-agreement remain possible.
- CL-API style recording is limited to necessary data and bounded by privacy.
- Indicator use does not become a popularity or human-worth ranking.
- Scenario claims remain conditional and revisable.
- T/S/R, RB, UCI/REI, phase logs, or related audit vocabulary are not stripped of their source modality.

## 8. Failure conditions
- Connection count or referral count is treated as resonance value by itself.
- R-Price is used to rationalize arbitrary price inflation.
- Trust capital becomes a credit score or human ranking.
- CL-API becomes surveillance or non-consensual data collection.
- Signature preservation becomes authority worship or criticism suppression.
- Indicator gaming outperforms substantive value formation.
- Correction, withdrawal, or re-agreement does not function.
- Scenario language is restated as a certainty that the market will appear in 2026.

## 9. Falsification conditions
The institutional hypothesis requires revision when the observation structure repeatedly diverges from the intended effects—for example, when connection density increases while trust declines, when lineage is preserved but factual quality worsens, when faster agreement reduces informed choice, when privacy costs rise, when old versions cannot be withdrawn, or when the named audit variables remain outside their intended bounds.

No new numeric threshold is inferred when the parent does not provide one. Symbolic or named audit relations remain symbolic or descriptive unless the source explicitly binds values.
