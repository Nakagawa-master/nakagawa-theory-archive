## Structural summary

The Resonance Market moves price from the origin of value formation to a later confirmation layer. Connection, understanding, referral, continuation, revisit, and collaborative editing are first observed as R-Value. R-Price, ROB, CL-API, and SS-1.0 then connect that value to agreement, provenance, revision, and monetary settlement. The model adds a trust-capital market layer before the monetary layer rather than immediately abolishing money.

## Central proposition

```text
market value
is not exhausted by transaction price

market value arises through
connection
→ resonance
→ continuation / referral / revisit
→ provenance-aware agreement
→ monetary settlement where appropriate
```

## Causal chain

```text
price-centered market
→ pre-transaction contribution disappears
→ reward diverges from connection value
→ connection events are observed

→ R-Price and monetary price are ordered
→ ROB / CL-API preserve agreement and provenance
→ CRI / IR / KCL / SIR / D-Gap audit the result
→ a dual resonance-and-monetary market becomes possible
```

## Validity conditions

- Actors, purpose, consent, roles, and origin are explicit.
- R-Value definitions, observation windows, and allocation rules are transparent.
- Contributors, conditions, and revisions remain traceable.
- Refusal, correction, withdrawal, and re-agreement are possible.
- Monetary price, cost, risk, and alternatives remain visible.
- Data collection is minimal and purpose-limited.
- Multiple metrics and qualitative records are combined.
- T/S/R, RB, UCI/REI, and phase logs can detect overheating, drift, and personalization.

## Failure / non-applicable conditions

- Contact, referral, or dwell counts become the sole value measure.
- R-Price becomes a vague premium-pricing justification.
- Trust capital becomes a permanent human score.
- CL-API becomes non-consensual surveillance or secondary-use infrastructure.
- A closed community monopolizes evaluation.
- Metric gaming exceeds substantive value creation.
- Correction, withdrawal, and re-agreement do not function.
- Resonance metrics replace legal prices, emergency rules, or scientific professional judgment.

## Interpretation constraints

The Resonance Market is not monetary abolition, an emotion market, popularity voting, a social-credit system, author worship, or guaranteed prophecy. The object of evaluation is the connection structure and value circulation, while agency, privacy, price transparency, objection, and reversibility remain protected.
