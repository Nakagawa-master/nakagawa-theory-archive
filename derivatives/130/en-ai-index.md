## Summary
Defense is modeled as an interaction among threat assessment, capability gaps, procurement, deterrence, adversary response, lifecycle costs, alliances, diplomacy and civilian resilience. More equipment does not automatically equal more security, while insufficient capability can also cause real harm. The framework tests marginal security effects, escalation risks and opportunity costs without presuming either militarization or disarmament is always correct.

## Concepts
threat, deterrence, procurement, escalation, lifecycle cost, alliance, civilian resilience, opportunity cost, proportionality, capability gap, security dilemma, re-audit

## Causal chain
Threat perception → procurement → deterrence expectation → adversary response → regional buildup → renewed threat → cost growth → alternative comparison → re-evaluation.

## State model

## Applications
Procurement, basing, alliances, cyber defense, civil protection, public budgeting.

## Measurements and audit
Readiness, lifecycle cost, threat-evidence updates, deterrence hypotheses, escalation signals, civil-resilience investment, alternative comparison.

## Validity conditions
Update threat evidence; include maintenance; model adversary response; compare diplomatic and civilian options; audit proportionality.

## Failure conditions
Fixed threat assumptions, equipment count as security, ignoring adversary response, hidden maintenance cost, dismissing civilian resilience, silencing criticism.

## Falsification conditions
If additional equipment consistently produces high marginal security benefits without reinforcing escalation or material opportunity cost, weaken the critical loop hypothesis.

## Required distinctions
defense/equipment volume; capability/intent; deterrence/reassurance; purchase/lifecycle cost; alliance/dependence; criticism/anti-defense; peace/unpreparedness.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not use this derivative to determine legality or operational necessity of specific procurements. Do not automatically reject military capability or threat claims.
