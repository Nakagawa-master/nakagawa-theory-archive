## 15-Second Explanation
A system that measures connection value and links it to evaluation or allocation can, if poorly designed, turn into popularity contests, favoritism, lock-in, surveillance, or concentration of power. Connection governance design theory is a theory for integrating public purpose, separation of powers, composite indicators, independent audits, and objection, exit, correction, reconnection, and rollback, creating a state in which the system itself can find and correct distortions.

## Why It Is Necessary
Once connection value becomes observable, someone sets the indicators, someone evaluates, and someone allocates. The more evaluation is connected to benefits or eligibility, the more participants adapt to the scoring method instead of the original collaboration, and the more operators are tempted to monopolize purpose, measurement, judgment, allocation, and objection handling.

Therefore, rather than adding audits later, the system must state its public purpose, prohibited uses, and affected actors from the start; separate measurement, judgment, allocation, and objection handling; and build in independent audits and reversible remedies.

## Main Structure
- **Public purpose**: Publish why connections are observed and what they must not be used for.
- **Separation of powers**: Do not concentrate measurement, judgment, allocation, and objection handling in one actor.
- **Audit separation**: Design What / Who / How / When separately.
- **Composite indicator bundle**: Do not collapse CDI, MAI, RS, CRI, and KQI into one personality score.
- **Anti-gaming**: Use moving targets, inverse incentives, disturbance tests, and qualitative evidence together.
- **Publication/protection separation**: Make principles and audit summaries verifiable; protect personal information, weights, thresholds, and defense procedures within the necessary scope.
- **Reversibility and remedies**: Make objections, corrections, exit, deletion, reconnection, mediation, and rollback actually usable.

## How to Read the 30-, 90-, and 180-Day Periods
The Parent Original contains a staged 30-, 90-, and 180-day introduction and verification roadmap. This is a time design within the Parent Original for trying, auditing, and correcting a system in stages; it is not a statutory deadline or a guarantee of success that applies automatically to every system.

Passing a period alone does not constitute success. At each stage, check the public purpose, concentration of power, the possibility of objection and exit, indicator gaming, exhaustion, lock-in, information protection, and whether system changes work in practice; correct or roll back when necessary.

## Concrete Examples
**Connection value accounting.** Even when a high evaluation value appears, do not call the system successful if dependence on central figures, exhaustion, lock-in, or difficulty of exit is increasing.

**Research and civic collaboration.** Observe not only the number of meetings or participants, but also renewed agreement, handling of dissent, continuing collaboration, minority views, and re-participation after exit.

**AI-assisted systems.** AI may assist with anomaly detection, comparison, and summarization, but final adjudication and responsibility must not be transferred to AI. Make inputs, grounds, human judgment, correction, stopping, and re-review traceable.

**Communities.** Do not directly equate volume of voice, number of posts, or number of introductions with trust; look at whether objections or exit are being converted into disadvantages.

## How to Read the Measurements
CDI, MAI, RS, CRI, and KQI should be treated as a composite indicator bundle for observing different aspects of a connection system. Do not integrate them into one total score to rank people or organizations.

In addition, the time required for renewed agreement, correction, mediation, and rollback; exit success rate; reconnection success rate; objection handling; and changes in decisions are observation targets for seeing whether reversibility and self-correction actually work. They are not fixed passing scores or targets for single-variable maximization.

## Inversion Test
Even if indicator values or participation rise, it is not success if exhaustion, silence, exclusion, lock-in, concentration of power, or the cost of exit worsens. Even if the number of objections or the exit rate falls, it is not improvement if the result comes from making the objection or exit routes difficult to use. Even if audit frequency increases, it is not soundness if it does not lead to institutional change.

## Conditions for Establishment
- Public purpose, prohibited uses, and affected actors are stated.
- Measurement, judgment, allocation, and objection handling are separated.
- Composite indicators and primary evidence are used together.
- Independent audits function substantively.
- Objection, correction, exit, deletion, reconnection, mediation, and rollback are executable.
- The boundary between public and protected information can be explained.
- Even when AI assistance is used, the human responsible party and remedy route remain.

## Limits and Misreading Prevention
Complete publication can expose personal information and defense procedures, while an overly broad confidential area can become secret domination. Transparency is not publication of all information; it is a state in which purpose, judgment, and change are verifiable, and protected areas also undergo independent audits.

Do not reinterpret this as a social-credit score, centralized evaluation ranking, permanent surveillance, or unaudited secret governance. Do not turn CDI/MAI/RS/CRI/KQI into personality scores, or turn 30/90/180 days into universal guaranteed deadlines.
