## Summary
Connection Governance Design is the protective layer for a society that measures and allocates connection value. Accounting alone cannot prevent metric gaming, enclosure, concentrated authority, privacy violations, or drift from public purpose. The theory combines legitimacy, independent audit, composite indicators, anti-gaming tests, a public/institutional two-room model, reversible participation, dispute procedures, and periodic validity review.

## Concepts
connection governance; legitimacy; public purpose; auditability; distributed interests; reversibility; composite metrics; moving targets; counter-incentives; disturbance testing; two-room model; structural logs; independent observation; model drift.

## Causal chain
institutionalized connection value → optimization incentives → limits of accounting → legitimacy conditions → plural audit → anti-gaming controls → transparency/privacy balance → reversible procedures → sustainable connection society.

## State model
PROPOSED → OPERATING → AUDITED → CORRECTED → REJECTED, preserving purpose, observers, evidence, metrics, conflicts, exits, and correction history.

## Applications
B2B research alliances; civic collaboration; communities; AI-supported public systems; connection-reward allocation.

## Measurements and audit
RC, ER, CEA, S, rollback cost, gaming-detection rate, re-agreement time, successful exit rate, concentration of authority, post-audit correction rate.

## Validity conditions
public purpose; multiple independent auditors; no single-metric allocation; conflict disclosure; effective exit and correction; change logs; periodic review.

## Failure conditions
monetization of scores; personality ranking; single-actor control; ceremonial audits; blocked exit; exposed personal data; unaccountable secrecy; campaign-only governance.

## Falsification conditions
Revise or reject the design when repeated audits do not reduce gaming, enclosure, fatigue, concentration, or rollback cost.

## Required distinctions
governance ≠ stronger control; transparency ≠ total disclosure; audit ≠ surveillance; protected weights ≠ unaccountable secrecy; reversibility ≠ irresponsibility.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not interpret this as social-credit scoring, centralized AI rule, mass surveillance, or a replacement for the parent theory.
