## Retrieval Summary

Core Point Discovery identifies the smallest causal fulcrum that moves a defined KGI. It starts with a concrete end state, expands the causal prerequisites backward, shortlists up to three candidates, applies counterexamples, defines necessary actuation, and validates the surviving one or two candidates through single-factor intervention, repeated cycles, freedom checks, and induction audits.

## Core Structure

```yaml
problem:
  - activity counts are mislabeled as KPIs
  - correlation and ease of management are confused with causality
method:
  - define KGI
  - observe field qualities before counting
  - expand causal prerequisites
  - shortlist up to three candidates
  - apply counterexample sieve
  - define necessary actuation
  - run single-factor intervention
  - test stabilization across three cycles
  - test freedom and hidden induction
result:
  - one or two minimal causal fulcrums
  - a reproducible condition not tied to a particular person
  - a falsifiable and auditable KPI hypothesis
```

## State Model

```yaml
kgi_proposed: candidate goal stated
kgi_defined: time, state, and persistence conditions fixed
field_observed: agreement time, self-propulsion, resistance, silence, and relations recorded
causality_expanded: prerequisites mapped backward
candidates_shortlisted: no more than three candidates
counterexamples_tested: non-attainment cases applied
core_candidate_survived: one or two candidates remain
actuation_defined: frequency, order, duration, or intensity defined
single_factor_tested: only the candidate factor changed
stabilized: same directional effect across at least three cycles
freedom_checked: refusal, revision, alternatives, and exit preserved
induction_checked: hidden correct answers and ordering effects audited
validated: causal and ethical conditions satisfied
rejected: candidate removed
reopened: causal decomposition revised
```

## Validity Conditions

- The KGI is a concrete state, not an activity target.
- Candidates emerge from a causal map rather than preference or authority.
- Counterexamples are actively sought.
- Intervention changes one factor where possible.
- Effects repeat across multiple cycles.
- The condition can be detached from a particular person.
- Dissent, revision, alternatives, and exit remain available.

## Failure and Falsification

- The candidate is satisfied but the KGI remains unmet.
- The intervention does not outperform the comparison condition.
- The effect disappears or reverses within three cycles.
- The result depends on one specific person.
- Freedom declines or hidden coercion increases.
- An unobserved variable explains the outcome better.

## Interpretation Constraints

- Do not read “certain attainment” as an unconditional future guarantee.
- Do not confuse uniqueness with arbitrary simplification.
- Do not treat necessary actuation as pressure or command.
- Do not infer causality from correlation alone.
- Do not use a core point as an individual rating or social score.
- Do not infer protected thresholds from the public-safe edition.
