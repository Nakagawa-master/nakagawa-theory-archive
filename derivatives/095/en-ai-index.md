## 1. Summary
The parent original makes a strong civilizational claim that the collapse of money-centered economy is unavoidable and that transition toward a Connection-Driven Reward System is necessary. It moves the origin of value away from labor time and single outputs toward contribution, trust, and resonance generated through connection to social and structural systems. The three pressures driving the transition are AI/automation, declining exchange costs, and information transparency.

Implementation relies on redesigning dialogue structure, structural defense against imitation and distortion, and multi-layer rewards involving connection density, contribution, ethics, trust, and resonance. Integrated audit links connection-value accounting, rights, arbitration, vocabulary, and primary logs; RC/ER/CEA/S, Consent Failure rate, revisit/referral rates, Rollback Cost, threshold θ, and observation window δ appear in falsification relations. The source gives no concrete θ or δ values.

## 5. Applications
Contribution includes structural problem-solving such as open-source work, social-problem proposals, and environmental-load reduction. Trust includes transparent decision processes and durable relationships. Resonance concerns a concept or structure becoming part of another actor's cognitive or behavioral foundation rather than mere imitation or sharing. Dialogue order, interval, and placement are designed as part of value generation.

## 6. Measurements and audit
The source names connection density, contribution degree, and ethics as reward dimensions and uses RC/ER/CEA/S, Consent Failure rate, revisit/referral rate, Rollback Cost, and local revisit/referral/conformity measures in audit. These must not be collapsed into a single person score. They remain connected to primary logs, relationship quality, allocation, and reconsensus.

## 7. Validity conditions
Contribution, trust, and resonance remain distinguishable; allocation can be traced back to primary logs; voice volume and short-term exposure do not determine value alone; multi-layer reward is not collapsed into one ranking; structural defense and arbitration preserve origin and allocation integrity.

## 8. Failure conditions
Popularity or imitation count is mislabeled as resonance; short-term monetization pressure dominates connection value; loudness overrides trust; reputation self-amplifies into allocation distortion; allocation cannot be reconstructed from primary logs; structural defense becomes a mechanism for suppressing disagreement.

## 9. Falsification conditions
Revise the hypothesis when RC/ER/CEA/S, Consent Failure rate, revisit/referral measures, Rollback Cost or related audit relations breach their source-defined conditions, or when observation window δ reveals early-win lock-in, reputation runaway, or allocation distortion. Threshold θ and δ have no concrete values in the parent and must not be invented.
