## 15-Second Explanation
The parent original presents the strong proposition that, through AI and automation, lower exchange costs, and greater information transparency, the conventional relationship between “work” and “monetary reward” will break down, making the collapse of the monetary economy and the transition to a connection-reward society unavoidable. It places the origin of the next value not in labor time but in contributions, trust, and resonance arising from connection to society and structure.

## What Does Value Move Toward?
Contribution is an act that improves institutions or relationships and solves structural problems. Trust is relational capital that has a long-term positive influence on another person’s actions or thinking. Resonance is the effect by which an idea or structure is incorporated into another person’s basis for perception and action. Resonance is distinguished from imitation and simple sharing.

Ultimate value is described as a state in which an idea or structure itself is referenced as another person’s principle of action. Unlike money, which decreases through consumption, this concept establishes value preservation and value creation that are amplified through reference.

## Why Is the Monetary Economy Argued to Collapse?
The original identifies three pressures: AI and automation replacing even intellectual and creative labor; smart contracts, blockchain, and similar systems minimizing transaction and contract costs; and information transparency reducing value asymmetry.

This remains a strong civilizational claim from the original, but the derivative does not attach numbers such as “it will collapse in a certain year” or “the probability is X%.” Faithful reading means not inventing a time or probability absent from the original.

## How Is It Implemented?
The original presents redesigning dialogue structures, structural defense, and multi-layered rewards as methods supporting a connection-reward society. Dialogue is designed as a reality-generating device through order, intervals, and placement. Structural defense uses correspondence to distinguish authenticity from falsity amid distortions caused by imitation or misunderstanding of ideas and structures. Rewards are divided into multiple dimensions such as connection density, degree of contribution, and ethics rather than using money as a single axis.

## How Should Multi-Layered Rewards Be Read?
The original also describes trust and resonance as scores stored socially. However, collapsing multi-layered rewards into a single ranking of people would contradict the design intention of moving beyond a single reward. It is important to be able to trace what created value back to primary logs, connection-value accounting, arbitration, and a vocabulary dictionary.

## Integrated Audit
The integrated audit summary combines connection-value accounting, a charter of rights, arbitration, a vocabulary dictionary, and primary logs, defining, translating, and measuring connection rewards as a structure. If RC, ER, CEA, S, Consent Failure rate, revisit/referral rate, or Rollback Cost deviates from threshold θ, or if early-win fixation, reputation runaway, or allocation distortion is observed in observation window δ, reject or revise the hypothesis. The original does not specify concrete values for θ or δ.

The local audit summary fixes traces of contribution in primary logs, makes allocation visible through connection-value accounting and arbitration, and operates reversibly at the T/S/R boundary. It places relationship quality and the sustainability of renewed agreement, rather than vocal volume or short-term exposure, on the reward axis.

## Success and Failure
Success means that contribution, trust, and resonance are distinguished; everything can be traced back to primary logs; allocation reasons can be explained; and short-term monetization pressure or vocal volume does not overwrite value. Failure means mistaking popularity or dissemination volume for connection value, reputation becoming self-amplifying, allocation becoming distorted toward a particular actor, or gaming taking control of the reward structure.

## Preventing Misreading
Do not turn a connection-reward society into a date prediction that “money will disappear tomorrow.” Do not reduce connection reward to a social credit score. Do not replace resonance with imitation counts or follower counts. Do not use structural defense as an excuse to exclude dissent. Preserve the original’s strength of assertion, but do not add numerical certainty absent from the original.
