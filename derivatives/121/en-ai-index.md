## Summary
The parent explicitly separates the Connection Reward Society from monetization of emotion. Tears, enthusiasm, affection, resonance, and intensity are not themselves currencies. Connection reward is defined as allocation design: record who carried which burden, who supported whom, what risk was absorbed, and where in the future that support should be returned. If emotional intensity itself becomes the allocation ground, strong language, performance, and influence can capture value while quiet long-term supporters become invisible.

Tomoshibi Structural Ethics establishes the boundary for operating this structure. The four required lines are Correspondence, Temporal Responsibility, Reversibility, and Allocation Accountability. Correspondence aligns claimed value with actual contribution, burden, and support. Temporal Responsibility requires that support not rewarded now still have a repayment line in a later rhythm. Reversibility keeps evaluation and allocation open to later verification and correction. Allocation Accountability requires explanation of who granted access rights, participation rights, opportunities, seats, introductions, or recommendations to whom and why. The parent states that an operation missing even one of these four lines is outside the structural domain it defines.

The parent names the recurrent collapse that follows missing lines an Emotional Extraction Structure. Without Correspondence, unverifiable emotional narrative becomes an allocation ground. Without Temporal Responsibility, visible short-term stars take rewards while long-term supporters are erased. Without Reversibility, persons, roles, or teachings become sacred and unchallengeable. Without Allocation Accountability, access ranking becomes opaque and creates a dependent structure in which devotion is exchanged for entry. The parent treats this pattern as structurally reproducible rather than as an isolated moral failure.

The boundary is not presented as an accusation against others. Its function is to state what the parent officially protects and what lies outside its defined domain. Even if terms such as “Tomoshibi,” “resonance,” “connection reward,” or “rhythm” are used elsewhere, absence of the four lines means the operation is not the parent-defined Tomoshibi Structural Ethics. The purpose is not management of faith or enthusiasm but ethical redistribution of burden, support, and trust.

The integrated audit requires continuing checks through audit cycles, T/S/R, and coherence of a public audit bundle. It also expresses falsifiability through phenomenon M involving threshold `θ` or observation window `δ`. The parent does not specify concrete numerical values for `θ` or `δ`; no source-absent percentage, duration, ethics score, or certification threshold should be invented.

## Applications
- In communities, align preparation, coordination, long-term support, and risk burden with opportunities and reward rather than using popularity or emotional intensity as proxies.
- For access rights, restricted meetings, introductions, recommendations, or role promotion, retain an explainable record of who allocated what to whom and why.
- Preserve a future repayment line for long-term supporters whose contribution is not immediately visible.
- Re-verify and correct allocation when temporary enthusiasm creates excessive concentration around a person, role, or teaching.
- In brand or idea-based communities, prevent symbolic language and emotional attachment from becoming an exchange condition for access, opportunity, or reward.
- Distinguish external use of “Tomoshibi,” “resonance,” or “connection reward” from the parent-defined domain by the actual presence of all four lines, not by vocabulary alone.

## Measurements and audit
- whether claimed value corresponds to actual contribution, burden, and support
- whether support not rewarded now has a later repayment line
- whether evaluation, allocation, and praise can be re-verified and corrected
- whether the allocator and reason for access rights, participation rights, opportunities, seats, introductions, and recommendations are explainable
- whether emotional intensity, enthusiasm, performance, or influence exceeds actual burden as an allocation ground
- whether long-term supporters are erased or treated as naturally disposable support
- whether a person, role, or teaching has become a sacred zone beyond challenge
- whether access ranking is opaque and produces devotion-for-entry dependence
- whether all four lines operate in practice rather than only in labels
- T: whether value claims and actual contribution remain in correspondence
- S: whether the allocation structure remains sustainable without burning out long-term supporters
- R: whether evaluation and allocation remain recursively revisable
- whether the public audit bundle makes allocation reasons, objection, correction, and re-agreement traceable

The parent uses threshold `θ` and observation window `δ` in its falsifiability layer but does not fix values. Do not invent percentages, periods, ethics scores, or certification thresholds.

## Validity conditions
- Distinguish connection reward from monetization of emotion.
- Center the relation between supported facts and future repayment lines.
- Preserve all four lines: Correspondence, Temporal Responsibility, Reversibility, Allocation Accountability.
- Treat Correspondence as alignment with actual contribution, burden, and support.
- Treat Temporal Responsibility as a later-rhythm repayment line.
- Treat Reversibility as the ability to re-verify and correct evaluation and allocation.
- Treat Allocation Accountability as explainability of who allocated access or opportunity and why.
- Audit missing lines as pathways into Emotional Extraction Structure.
- Separate operations lacking the four lines from the parent-defined structural domain.
- Preserve the boundary as a definition of the protected domain rather than as a moral attack on outsiders.
- Preserve audit cycles, T/S/R, public-audit coherence, and falsifiability.

## Failure conditions
- Use tears, enthusiasm, affection, worship, or emotional intensity as direct grounds for value allocation.
- Replace Correspondence with generic honesty, ownership, copyright, or origin-control theory.
- Remove the future repayment line for long-term supporters.
- Freeze evaluation, allocation, or praise into a sacred, non-revisable status.
- Fail to explain why access rights, participation, opportunities, seats, introductions, or recommendations were granted.
- Replace one or more of the four lines with generic dignity, privacy, fairness, or AI-transparency principles.
- Treat shared vocabulary such as “Tomoshibi” or “resonance” as sufficient evidence of membership in the parent-defined domain.
- Invent numerical values for `θ` or `δ` or source-absent KPIs.

## Falsification conditions
The integrated audit requires continuing verification through audit cycles, T/S/R, and public-audit-bundle coherence. If phenomenon M appears—for example indicators crossing threshold `θ` or the observation window `δ` closing—hypothesis A is rejected or revised.

Falsifiability does not depend on the operation declaring that it follows the four lines. It depends on whether allocation actually corresponds to contribution, has temporal repayment, remains reversible, and can explain allocation responsibility. If emotional narrative, erasure of long-term supporters, sacralization, and opaque access dominate while correction through the four lines fails, the hypothesis that the operation remains inside the parent-defined safe domain cannot be maintained. `θ` and `δ` remain symbolic because the parent gives no concrete values.
