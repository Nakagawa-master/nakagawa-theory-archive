## 15-Second Explanation
The connection-reward society in the original is not a society that uses tears, excitement, or favor as currency. It is an allocation design that records “who carries which burden, how someone is supported, and where and when that support is fairly returned in the future.” Its four lines—Correspondence, Temporal Ethics, Reversibility, and Allocation Accountability—prevent operation from falling into emotional extraction. An operation missing even one of the four lines lies outside the structural sphere of Tomoshibi Structural Ethics as defined by the original.

## Why Four Lines Are Needed
Connections and resonance between people are tied to the allocation of support, opportunities, introductions, access, and roles. But if “how moved, how excited, or how strongly liked” becomes the reason for allocation itself, value tends to gather around people with strong words, staging, or influence rather than actual burden and long-term support.

The original sees this not merely as unfairness but as an entry into an Emotional Extraction Structure. A community may look warm and noble from outside, while inside it can produce a subordinate structure in which “if you want to be chosen, you must offer yourself,” “you must not doubt,” and “those who supported you are treated as a given.”

Tomoshibi Structural Ethics treats this danger as structural lines for judging whether allocation is being operated fairly, rather than as a general good-and-evil argument. Its purpose is not to attack anyone but to state clearly, “This far is the safe zone defined by the original.”

## Four Standards
**1. Correspondence.** The value being spoken about corresponds to actual contribution, burden, and support. A person does not take more reward than reality warrants merely because they have a loud voice, speak skillfully, or possess influence; the basis for allocation is tied to actual burden.

**2. Temporal Responsibility.** It assumes that support and burden not valued now will be returned in a later beat. It prevents rewards from concentrating only on immediately visible roles while people who supported the frontline over time or protected the environment while wearing down burn out. The original places “distribution without a beat” outside its ethical line.

**3. Reversibility.** Evaluation and allocation can be examined later and redone if necessary. Even if a person, role, or teaching becomes concentrated with value during temporary excitement, it remains possible to re-examine whether this was truly appropriate. This is an institutional safety device against fixing a particular person as a sanctuary beyond doubt.

**4. Allocation Accountability.** It is possible to explain who gave access, participation rights, opportunities, seats, introductions, or recommendations to whom and why. If closed favoritism or back routes are hidden as natural evaluation, “devotion in exchange for entry” will be demanded and connection will turn into subordination.

## What Happens When a Line Is Missing
Without Correspondence, unverifiable emotional narratives become the reason for value allocation. Without Temporal Ethics, only momentary stars are rewarded and long-term supporters become invisible. Without Reversibility, people or teachings become sanctified and objection or correction becomes impossible. Without Allocation Accountability, access rankings become closed, and subordination in exchange for devotion emerges.

When all four overlap, connection and resonance become a system that encloses emotion as a resource rather than a system that returns the fact of support. Even if the same words—“tomoshibi,” “resonance,” or “connection reward”—are used, the original separates operation without the four lines from its own theoretical operation.

## Examples of Application
**1. Community operation.** Check whether preparation, coordination, long-term support, and risk-bearing correspond to actual opportunities, roles, and rewards, rather than evaluating only speaking volume or popularity.

**2. Access allocation.** Make it explainable who allocated limited meetings, introductions, recommendations, role promotions, and participation qualifications, and why.

**3. Repayment to long-term supporters.** Do not consume inconspicuous support as “a given”; retain an institutional line for returning it in a later beat.

**4. Correcting overvaluation.** Re-examine praise and allocation concentrated during an excited period later, and make it possible to adjust the weighting when necessary.

**5. Brand and thought operation.** Even when strong words or symbols induce devotion, do not make that emotion itself an exchange condition for access or opportunity.

## Success Criteria
If connection rewards are being operated fairly, spoken value and actual contribution correspond; long-term support has a repayment line in a later beat; evaluation and allocation can be corrected; and the reasons for allocating access and opportunities can be explained. In addition, T = Correspondence, S = structural sustainability, R = recursion, and a public audit bundle allow outside confirmation that the four lines are actually working.

The integrated audit summary at the end of the original also places phenomenon M concerning threshold `θ` and observation window `δ` as a condition for falsification and revision. Concrete values are not fixed. Therefore, this page preserves evaluability without adding arbitrary percentages, periods, or original scores.

## Limitations
These four lines are not presented as a general AI-ethics checklist or universal ethical items to be mechanically applied to every community. They are the operating boundary of the “connection-reward society” and “Tomoshibi Structural Ethics” defined by the original.

Nor is the purpose of showing the boundary to morally condemn others. The original separates the matter by saying that other operations may exist, but they are not the structural sphere it defines. This is a reference line that prevents its own theory name and vocabulary from being used for emotional extraction, not a condemnation of other theories.

## Misreading Prevention
Do not turn connection rewards into “the person who cried wins,” the amount of “thank you,” or the strength of excitement.

Do not weaken Correspondence into mere honesty, Temporal Ethics into an ambiguous promise of future reward, Reversibility into humility, or Allocation Accountability into general transparency. Each has a concrete function in allocation structure.

Do not replace the four lines with other general ethical items. The original’s four lines are Correspondence, Temporal Ethics, Reversibility, and Allocation Accountability.

Do not add AI certification criteria, legal conditions, privacy conditions, original KPIs, or concrete values for `θ` or `δ` absent from the original.
