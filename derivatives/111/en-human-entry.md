## 15-Second Explanation
Layer Three opens without breaking by protecting the principle bundle as an unalterable reference point while making only the derivative bundle editable. Selective editing fields, diff logs, signature chains, exploration quotas/dividends, L0–L2 audits, non-coercive resilience, and the principle/non-interference boundary make this operational.

## Why It Is Necessary
A strong structure can look closed or make recipients passive. The parent separates the layer to protect from the layer to edit: the principle bundle holds the mothership signature, terminology, integrated audit summary, and renewed-agreement window; the derivative bundle holds extensions, cases, and translations. Record what, why, and how changes were made, preserve return paths to the principles, protect exploratory room, and connect audits into one bundle.

## Practical Process
**1. Fix the principle bundle.** Treat its signature, definitions, audit summary, and renewed-agreement window as reference points.
**2. Open the derivative bundle.** Separate editable extensions, applications, and translations.
**3. Record edits.** Keep selective editing fields and diff logs.
**4. Maintain the signature chain.** Keep every derivative traceable to the principles.
**5. Protect exploration.** Reserve non-result-oriented time and treat prototypes, failures, and questions as future value.
**6. Link L0–L2.** Connect general, practical, and specialist audits instead of creating a closed hierarchy.
**7. Set boundaries.** Use mirror bundles, signature chains, a Fail-open summary, and a renewed-agreement cycle.

## Applications
Translations preserve principle terms and signatures while exposing derivative edits. Applied cases separate original connections from derivative judgments. Audits connect reader misunderstanding, practitioner consistency, and specialist boundaries. Ethics, art, interiority, and love remain observation domains rather than objects of causal trampling.

## Success Criteria
The principles remain reference points and derivatives preserve editor self-determination. Openness increases peripheral agency without erasing origin or freezing editing; exploratory room survives result pressure; L0–L2 connect; and resilience enables avoidance rather than destruction.

## Limits
A vague principle/derivative boundary turns editing into alteration of the original. Freezing derivatives destroys agency. The non-interference domain does not legitimise unverifiable claims; it protects observation, record, correspondence, and renewed agreement.

## To Avoid Misreading
This is not a general AI-responsibility KPI model. Do not replace its non-coercive resilience with rollback/remedy governance, dismiss exploration as waste, or treat this derivative as a substitute for the parent. Return to the parent for the strict links among principle/derivative bundles, exploratory room, audits, dual publicness, and the correspondence-world model.
