## Summary

The third-layer social connection model does not open the principle itself to unrestricted editing. It keeps the principle bundle as an inviolable reference point and opens derivative bundles to collaborative editing, allowing a strong structure to remain authentic without hardening into external rejection or passive reception. Selective editing fields and diff logs preserve the editor's decisions; signature linkage and recursive openness preserve a return path from derivatives to the origin. Exploration quota and exploration dividend protect non-output-oriented space, while L0 general, L1 practical, and L2 expert audits are connected into one audit bundle rather than treated as closed ranks.

The model also uses mirror bundles, signature chains, and fail-open digests for non-coercive resilience, dual publicity for public verification plus ethical stability, and a boundary between the principle domain and the non-interference domain. In ethics, art, inner life, and forms of love, causal intervention is restrained in favor of observation, recording, resonance, and re-agreement. This source-specific model must not be replaced by generic AI responsibility allocation, human-final-approval governance, rollback/remedy centering, or derivative-created accountability KPIs.

## Applications

- translate or adapt an original while holding the principle bundle fixed and recording translation or adaptation as derivative change
- connect general-reader, practitioner, and expert audits as different resolutions within one audit bundle
- retain prototypes, failed attempts, and questions as exploration value rather than evaluating only completed outputs

## Measurements and audit

Audit whether the principle bundle remains an uneditable reference point, whether derivative changes and editing reasons are traceable, and whether signature links still return to the origin. Check whether exploration quota survives output pressure, whether L0-L2 audits are integrated rather than segregated, and whether causal intervention crosses into the non-interference domain.

Do not introduce AI contribution rate, human-accountability rate, human-approval rate, rollback success, or similar generic governance metrics as new core KPIs. Preserve the source-specific operation through public audit, indirect observation, observation windows, resonance, and re-agreement.

## Validity conditions

The principle/derivative boundary is explicit: the principle bundle remains protected while derivative bundles can be collaboratively edited with recorded diffs. Signature references remain intact, exploration margin is materially protected, L0-L2 audits operate as connected resolutions rather than authority classes, and the principle/non-interference boundary is visible. In the non-interference domain, rushed conclusions and causal optimization are restrained.

## Failure conditions

Failure includes opening the principle bundle to unrestricted editing until origin and definitions dissolve, or freezing derivative bundles until participants become passive. It also includes omitting diff logs or signature references, eliminating exploration margin for short-term efficiency, converting L0-L2 audits into closed authority ranks, or applying measurement and optimization indiscriminately inside the non-interference domain.

## Falsification conditions

The operating hypothesis weakens if separating principle and derivative bundles does not reduce closure and passivity, or if collaborative editing repeatedly produces origin loss and untraceable derivatives. It also weakens if third parties cannot reconstruct why changes were made, exploration quota is nominal rather than protected, audit layers remain disconnected, or the non-interference boundary cannot be maintained in actual operation.
