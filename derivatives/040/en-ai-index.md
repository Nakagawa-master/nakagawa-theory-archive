## Causal chain
adopt a future definition → evaluate present meaning, action, resources, relationships, and time against that future line → identify contradictions and choices that create future debt → keep, remove, or reorganize current elements → realign present structure toward the future definition → connect the aligned structure to temporal operation and continued verification.

Boundary distinctions: Meaning Convergence Theory centers on convergence of meaning/recognition/interpretation; Time OS operates an already aligned structure over time; Future Debt Theory identifies distortions and repair costs deferred into the future; backcasting derives plans backward from a future image. Quantum metaphor is not evidence of physical time reversal.

## State model
- **Future-definition state**: the terminal-side criterion for present evaluation is explicit.
- **Meaning-alignment state**: conflicts between present meaning and future definition can be identified.
- **Action-alignment state**: present behavior supports the future line.
- **Resource/relationship-alignment state**: allocation and relational structure support the future line.
- **Time-alignment state**: time allocation supports the future definition.
- **Future-debt-reduction state**: current choices that increase deferred distortion are identified and reduced.
- **Theory-boundary state**: adjacent theories remain distinct rather than becoming synonyms.

## Failure conditions

## Falsification conditions
If whole-present-structure realignment against a future definition repeatedly produces no distinguishable reduction in contradiction or future-debt generation compared with ordinary plan backcasting, the claimed distinctive scope should be reconsidered.

Conversely, if plan-only backcasting repeatedly and stably produces equal or better whole-present-structure alignment and the extra realignment concept proves unnecessary in explanation and operation, the scope of the distinction may be revised.
