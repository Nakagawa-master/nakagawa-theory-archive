## 15-Second Explanation
The parent original does not lump the theory of causal rectification from the future into “a story of working backward from the future.” It clarifies the difference in role between Meaning Convergence Theory, Time OS, Future Debt Theory, backcasting, and quantum theory metaphors.

The distinctive difference of causal rectification from the future is to use an adopted definition of the future as the standard and realign present meaning, actions, resources, relationships, time allocation, and other elements toward the future line. It is not a theory of future prediction, wish fulfillment, or the physical rewriting of the past by the future.

## Main Differences
**Meaning Convergence Theory.** It addresses the convergence of meaning, recognition, and interpretation. Causal rectification from the future reflects that meaning into present actions, resources, relationships, and time, and organizes the structure.

**Time OS.** It operates an already-organized structure on the axis of time. Causal rectification from the future organizes the present structure against the definition of the future before that operation.

**Future Debt Theory.** It captures distortions and repair costs deferred into the future. Causal rectification from the future removes present choices that generate that debt.

**Backcasting.** It works backward from a future vision to make a plan. Causal rectification from the future realigns not only the plan but the present structure, including meaning, actions, resources, relationships, and time.

**Quantum theory metaphor.** It is not evidence that the future physically rewrites the past. The definition of the future is a standard for evaluating and realigning the present.

## What to Check in Practice
Check whether the definition of the future is clear; where present meaning, actions, resources, relationships, and time contradict the future line; whether the work has ended at making a plan; and whether present choices that generate future debt remain.

A detailed plan does not advance rectification if the present structure does not change. Even if meaning converges, it is not causal rectification from the future if actions or resources do not change. More KPIs or change items are not improvement if consistency with the future line worsens.

## Success Criteria
Success is not judged by the detail of a plan, the number of KPIs, the number of changes, or the attractiveness of the future vision. Look at whether contradictions in the present structure decrease against the definition of the future and whether choices that generate future debt are being removed.

The parent original does not define a rectification rate, future-consistency rate, success probability, quantum-effect value, or fixed threshold.

## Limitations and Misreading Prevention
Do not convert this into future prediction, wish-fulfillment theory, manifestation theory, or reality alteration in quantum physics. Do not treat it as a synonym for backcasting, Meaning Convergence Theory, Time OS, or Future Debt Theory; preserve the differences in role, object, and stage.

The differences between causal rectification from the future and neighboring concepts, the realignment of the present structure against the definition of the future, and the boundary of the quantum theory metaphor can be confirmed through the Parent URL, Parent NCL-ID, and Parent Diff-ID.
