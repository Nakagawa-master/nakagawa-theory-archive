## 15-Second Explanation

This original presents a justice model that removes the premise that “someone must be punished to preserve order”: the right to survival is protected unconditionally, while wealth, options, discretion, interchangeability, and scope of influence are adjusted through the ongoing causality of C/E.

If the E direction continues, expansion layers such as connection rewards, trust, and interchangeability shrink toward the Parent’s “contribution-zero point.” This is called structural sedimentation. In the C direction, structural confluence opens a connection to broader options and discretion. Confluence is neither an obligation nor an elevation of status, but a chosen path for those willing to take on increased freedom and increased responsibility at the same time.

For dangerous conduct, the Parent does not allow unconstrained freedom. It proposes a model that does not take away survival, but limits such things as the radius of action and connection range as safety devices for preventing recurrence. This is a hypothesis of institutional design within the Parent, not a completed system that automatically replaces punishment, detention, or rights restrictions under current law.

## Separate the Three Circuits
### 1. Right to Survival
Life, minimum living conditions, medical care, housing, safety, and dignity are separated from evaluations of contribution, beliefs, and conduct. Survival is not held hostage to make someone obey.

### 2. Connection Rewards
Separate from survival, this circuit handles expansion layers such as the quality of one’s living environment, options, temporal, physical, and relational margin, interchangeability, discretion, and scope of influence. As C accumulates, the range of movement expands; if E continues, it is adjusted.

### 3. Safety Constraints
For dangerous conduct that directly violates another person’s freedom, place the behavioral and connection constraints necessary to prevent recurrence in a separate circuit from taking away the right to survival. The Parent positions these as safety devices, not retaliation.

When these three are combined, reversals occur: “take away the conditions for living because someone’s evaluation is low,” “exclude the whole person because the conduct is dangerous,” or “allow anything because the person has contributed.” Vol. 3 is a design for cutting apart this confusion.

## Structural Sedimentation
Sedimentation is not a procedure in which someone arbitrarily pushes another person downward. Within the Parent, it is the phenomenon in which the following expansion circuits narrow as E-direction conduct continues.

- Connection rewards decrease.
- Updates to structural trust stop.
- Interchangeability and connection range narrow.
- Additional wealth and discretion stop expanding.
- It becomes harder to connect to circuits that expand influence.

The lower bound is the “contribution-zero point,” which the Parent distinguishes from deprivation of the right to survival. Someone who previously accumulated C may retain some margin, but continued E causes that margin to decay. Past achievements are not an exemption permitting future exploitation.

## The Ability to Return Matters
Sedimentation is not permanent expulsion. The Parent says that sedimentation can stop and reverse when one understands why connections narrowed, corrects the direction of L5, restores alignment with L7, and rebuilds small amounts of C.

What is needed is neither being liked by someone nor receiving permission from a powerful person. It is repairing the actual causal wiring. Therefore, turning sedimentation into an irreversible blacklist or a score system that never disappears would depart from the Parent’s design.

## Structural Confluence
Confluence does not mean “becoming one of the winners.” The Parent considers the choices of people who do not seek advancement, want to live quietly, or do not want to take on excessive responsibility to be valid as well.

What increases through confluence is not power to control others, but:
- the range of resources one can choose
- the scope of decisions in which one can participate
- the people, organizations, and information to which one can connect
- the discretion one can design and adjust oneself
- the projects and uncertainty one can take on

The greater the degree of freedom, the higher the density of responsibility. The Parent proposes an exchange—“if you want more freedom, take on greater causality”—not a status, privilege, or loyalty system.

## Why Vigilante Justice Is Also Subject to Sedimentation
The Parent holds that doxxing, online pile-ons, group lynching, and emotional sanctions can also become E that destroys structure. Because vigilante justice uses attention, conformity, and approval as fuel, the hypothesis is that if the act itself does not receive connection rewards, the circuit in which attacking someone becomes profitable can be weakened.

This is the opposite of a structure in which “the righteous may attack the wicked.” The question is not whose justice it is, but whether the conduct increased C or increased E.

## Thinking about Distortion by the Powerful
The Parent argues that if domination, obstruction, and distortion do not produce C, then they cannot increase connection rewards, because the evaluation criteria are fixed to L7 and causal logs. In the long term, it therefore also becomes disadvantageous for powerful people to continue privatizing the system.

However, this is a theoretical prediction made by the Parent. This derivative does not strengthen it into the empirically established fact that “power capture will always automatically disappear in reality.” That is why the Parent itself also uses T/S/R, public audit bundles, and continued auditing under condition Z.

## What Is Automated Justice?
It is an operational model within the Parent in which public logs and audit results are reflected in connection status instead of humans deciding rewards and punishments through anger or revenge, so that E is less likely to amplify, C is more likely to connect, and a route for return remains.

“Automation” must not be read as AI deciding a person’s legal status through a secret score. The Parent carries forward T/S/R—transparency, safety, and reproducibility—from the previous chapter and requires verifiability through public audit bundles.

## Questions for Checking Whether It Holds
- Have survival conditions changed according to contribution or beliefs?
- Have connection rewards turned into loyalty rewards or conditions for maintaining life?
- Has the lower bound of sedimentation crossed the zero point into deprivation of survival or dignity?
- Are people who do not pursue confluence suffering disadvantage or insult?
- Does confluence increase options, discretion, and responsibility rather than control over others?
- Are constraints on dangerous conduct limited to what is necessary to prevent recurrence?
- Have constraints turned into retaliation or a public example?
- Are vigilante justice and online pile-ons being rewarded with attention or approval?
- Can powerful people rewrite audit logs or criteria privately?
- Does a real route for return exist after sedimentation?
- Are T/S/R satisfied simultaneously?

## Falsifiability
As condition Z, the Parent sets continued review through the consistency of the audit cycle, T/S/R, and public audit bundles. It says that hypothesis A should be rejected or revised if:
- adjustment of connection rewards infringes the right to survival;
- the assumed crime-prevention effect fails and E amplifies; or
- vigilante justice and power distortion do not decay within the observation window δ and structural instability is confirmed.

The fixed duration of δ is not written in the Parent. Do not invent values such as “90 days” or “one year” on the derivative side.

## Limitations
The Parent text contains strong generalizations and predictions about punishment, crime prevention, recidivism, vigilante justice, and power distortion. This derivative retains them as structural design hypotheses within the Parent, but does not treat them as universal laws established by independent empirical research.

For safety constraints on dangerous conduct, the Parent indicates a structural direction, but this derivative does not invent current institutional designs for legal procedure, standards of evidence, appeals, duration, or supervising bodies.

## Preventing Misreading
- “Without punishment” does not mean freedom to do anything.
- “Right to survival” does not mean unlimited influence or freedom of action.
- “Sedimentation” does not mean permanent expulsion, a blacklist, or vigilante justice.
- The “zero point” does not mean impoverishment or deprivation of survival.
- “Confluence” does not mean status, class, power, or loyalty.
- The size of C is not human value.
- Do not derive concrete detention, surveillance, or punishment procedures from “safety constraints” on the derivative side.
- “Automation” does not mean a secret AI trial.
- Do not elevate the Parent’s social predictions into empirically established facts on the derivative side.
- Do not omit condition Z, T/S/R, δ, or the possibility of return.
