## Summary
The Parent extends Structural Judicial OS Vol.1 and Vol.2 into a Vol.3 governance model that attempts to reduce dependence on punishment, exclusion, and emotional sanction. Its architecture separates three circuits: unconditional survival rights under LifeOS; adjustable ranges of wealth, discretion, exchangeability, connection, and influence through connection reward; and safety constraints for dangerous conduct aimed at recurrence prevention. Structural Sedimentation is not defined as punishment but as a Parent-internal adjustment in which continued E-direction causality reduces inflows of connection reward, structural trust, exchangeability, and expandable freedom down to a “contribution-zero point,” while survival and dignity remain outside that adjustment. Sedimentation is reversible through renewed structural alignment and accumulation of C. Structural Confluence / Alignment is not status advancement or a duty to compete; it is the voluntary expansion of options, discretion, connections, uncertainty, and causal responsibility for those who choose to carry a wider range of consequences. Dangerous conduct is not treated as unrestricted: the Parent distinguishes survival rights from behavioral freedom and proposes safety constraints on behavioral radius and connection scope without presenting them as retributive punishment. Vigilante punishment, public shaming, and power distortion are modeled as E-generating behavior that should lose amplification. Automation justice is a Parent-internal model combining public audit, connection control, recurrence prevention, and recovery paths rather than secret AI adjudication. Condition Z, T/S/R, the public audit bundle, and observation window δ provide continuing revision conditions. Hypothesis A is to be rejected or revised if connection-reward adjustment violates survival rights, expected deterrence fails and E increases, or vigilantism/power distortion does not diminish and structural instability persists during δ.

## Concepts
- **LifeOS:** Parent-internal base OS that separates survival, minimum living conditions, and dignity from contribution or ideological evaluation.
- **Right to existence / survival rights:** baseline protection excluded from C/E reward adjustment.
- **Connection reward:** Parent model for varying expanded options, exchangeability, discretion, material/temporal/relational room, and influence separately from survival.
- **Structural Sedimentation:** reduction of expandable connection/reward range toward the contribution-zero point when E-direction causality persists.
- **Contribution-zero point:** the lower bound of the expandable layer; not deprivation of survival, dignity, housing, or medical access in the Parent model.
- **Accumulated C:** past contribution can leave temporal margin but can decay under continued E and is not permanent immunity.
- **Structural Confluence / Alignment:** voluntary expansion of options, connection, discretion, uncertainty, and causal responsibility for C-aligned nodes.
- **Acquisition of freedom:** expansion of what one can choose and responsibly carry, not authority to dominate others.
- **Responsibility density:** increase in responsibility corresponding to wider discretion and causal scope.
- **Behavioral control / safety constraint:** Parent concept for adjusting behavioral radius or connection range to prevent recurrence of dangerous conduct without making survival itself conditional.
- **Vigilante justice:** emotional or extra-institutional punishment/shaming modeled by the Parent as E-generating behavior.
- **Automation justice:** Parent-internal operation model using public audit, connection control, prevention, and recovery paths rather than human retribution.
- **T/S/R:** Transparency, Safety, Reproducibility.
- **Condition Z:** continuing review condition including audit cycle, T/S/R, and consistency of the public audit bundle.
- **δ:** observation window used in falsification evaluation; no universal fixed duration is supplied.
- **Non-coercive / reversible / verifiable:** principles explicitly attached to the Parent structure.

## Applications
- **Governance design:** keep minimum survival/dignity independent from contribution scoring while separately examining how expanded access and discretion are allocated.
- **Organizational access:** distinguish position/status from current causal contribution, responsibility density, and transparent access rules.
- **Dangerous conduct:** distinguish recurrence-prevention constraints from revenge; do not infer concrete legal detention or surveillance procedures beyond the Parent.
- **Vigilante dynamics:** examine whether attention, approval, and network reach reward public shaming or emotional punishment.
- **Power capture:** audit whether operators can bypass public logs, L7 criteria, and T/S/R to preserve privilege.
- **Recovery design:** verify that sedimentation is reversible and renewed C actually restores connection.
- **AI assistance:** use AI, if at all, as an auditable reproducibility aid rather than a secret legal scorer or rights-decider.

## Measurements and audit
- Are survival, minimum living conditions, and dignity independent from C/E evaluation?
- Has connection reward become a survival condition or loyalty payment?
- Does sedimentation stop at the Parent’s zero point rather than crossing into survival deprivation?
- Does past C improperly excuse current persistent E?
- Do changes in access, exchangeability, and discretion correspond to auditable causal logs?
- Is non-confluence a legitimate option without loss of dignity or social existence?
- Does confluence expand options and responsibility rather than domination power?
- Are dangerous-conduct constraints limited to recurrence-prevention purpose and scope?
- Are vigilante action and public shaming rewarded by attention or approval?
- Can powerful operators secretly alter criteria or logs?
- Is there a real recovery path after sedimentation?
- Are Transparency, Safety, and Reproducibility all maintained?
- Is the public audit bundle consistent with actual operation?
- During δ, do vigilantism, power distortion, E amplification, and structural instability decline or persist?

## Validity conditions
1. Keep LifeOS survival rights separate from contribution, ideology, or behavior scoring.
2. Use connection reward only for expandable layers rather than basic survival.
3. Keep sedimentation bounded at the contribution-zero point.
4. Do not let accumulated past C become permanent immunity for continued E.
5. Keep confluence voluntary, non-competitive, and non-status-based.
6. Protect the dignity of people who do not choose confluence.
7. Couple wider freedom with wider responsibility and uncertainty.
8. Limit dangerous-conduct constraints to recurrence prevention.
9. Preserve reversible recovery through renewed C and alignment.
10. Maintain T/S/R, public audit, and Condition Z.

## Failure conditions
- Condition survival rights on contribution, ideology, loyalty, or reputation.
- Turn connection reward into a caste or survival system.
- Convert sedimentation into permanent expulsion, secret blacklisting, or social death.
- Move below the zero point into punitive deprivation.
- Let past contribution immunize present exploitation.
- Turn confluence into promotion competition or domination authority.
- Degrade the dignity of people who do not seek greater responsibility.
- Expand safety constraints into indefinite, unaudited retribution.
- Let AI secretly score people and restrict legal rights.
- Legitimize vigilantism as “structural justice.”
- Run automated controls without all T/S/R conditions.
- Close the recovery route.

## Required distinctions
- **Survival rights ≠ connection reward:** survival is unconditional; reward concerns expandable range.
- **Sedimentation ≠ punishment:** sedimentation is a zero-bounded adjustment model; punishment is retributive pain or sanction.
- **Sedimentation ≠ expulsion:** survival, dignity, and recovery remain available in the Parent architecture.
- **Zero point ≠ poverty/social death:** it is the neutral floor of the expandable layer.
- **Past C ≠ permanent immunity:** current E can consume accumulated margin.
- **Confluence ≠ power:** it expands options, discretion, and responsibility, not domination rights.
- **Confluence ≠ duty:** choosing not to expand is legitimate.
- **Freedom ≠ irresponsibility:** freedom and responsibility density rise together.
- **Safety constraint ≠ retribution:** the Parent frames it as recurrence prevention.
- **Automation ≠ secret AI court:** public audit, T/S/R, and recovery are prerequisites.
- **Parent prediction ≠ independently proven universal law:** preserve the epistemic status of deterrence and stability claims.

## Interpretation constraints
- Do not read “without punishment” as “without constraints” or “ignore harmful conduct.”
- Do not turn survival protection into unconditional influence, access, or behavioral freedom.
- Do not use sedimentation to justify private exclusion or permanent blacklists.
- Do not transform the zero point into deprivation of medical care, housing, minimum living conditions, or dignity.
- Do not turn confluence into caste, elite selection, loyalty, or rank.
- Do not equate contribution level with human worth.
