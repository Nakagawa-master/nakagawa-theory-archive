## Summary
The Parent does not reduce Bunken's masking feature to redaction, privacy protection, security assistance, or time saving. Those object-level functions matter, but Nakagawa Master's structural reading focuses on the operational stop that occurs just before employees send real business text to generative AI: they may support AI adoption yet still be unable to decide what information may be submitted. The value core is therefore not merely “hide information,” but create a state in which humans retain final judgment, inspect and prepare the input, and can responsibly send information to AI. The structure is neither blanket prohibition nor unrestricted use; it is an intermediate input-management and responsibility-design process that enables use under confidentiality constraints.

## Concepts
- Bunken: Web Rider's writing-support product.
- Masking feature: object-level operations such as masking specified terms or selected ranges, bulk handling, before/after display, and copying as described by the Parent.
- Input management: Nakagawa Master's reading of placing an inspection and preparation process before AI submission.
- Responsibility for handing information to AI: the human responsibility to decide what may be submitted and in what state.
- Last few meters: social translation for the operational stop immediately before actual AI input, even when policy and adoption intent already exist.
- Intermediate device: an operational environment between prohibition and laissez-faire use that supports rather than replaces human judgment.
- Explainability: the ability to explain operationally what was hidden and in what state information was passed to AI.

## Causal chain
Generative AI enters daily work → demand to process business documents rises → personal/customer/confidential/unpublished information is mixed into those documents → employees face input-permission judgment → abstract rules do not resolve every concrete document → work stops immediately before AI input → adoption value fails to settle into routine work → an on-document inspection/masking operation is provided → humans retain final judgment → text is prepared into an acceptable input state → confidential-use constraints and AI utilization can coexist → the input process becomes more explainable → input governance and responsibility become part of operations.

## State model
AI-use intent / AI-input demand / mixed-information state / judgment burden / adoption-versus-risk tension / pre-input stop / information inspection / masking decision / input preparation / human final judgment / AI use / confirmation and explanation / stable operational entrance / confidentiality-conditioned use / social translation.

## Applications
The structure applies to customer emails, meeting records, sales and PR drafts, internal documents, R&D text, public-sector documents, and other workflows where real business information is sent to AI. For AI policies, it suggests connecting abstract prohibitions to an operation that allows a worker to inspect and prepare an actual document. It does not allow one product feature to substitute for broader organizational classification, contracts, service terms, internal policy, or information governance.

## Measurements and audit
Observe whether employees stop immediately before AI input; whether documents mix differently governed information; whether abstract rules connect to concrete operations; whether humans retain final judgment; whether post-masking review occurs; whether contextual identifiability is considered rather than only named entities; whether confidentiality and useful AI adoption coexist; who confirms the input; whether the organization can explain what was masked and what was submitted; and whether convenience is being confused with responsibility. Keep company/product facts separate from Nakagawa Master's reading.

## Validity conditions
There must be a practical inspection point before AI submission; humans must decide what is protected and what can be used; the operational environment must make that decision executable; confidentiality, customer trust, and organizational rules must remain intact; useful AI use must remain possible; and the input process must be explainable when needed. Broader governance conditions outside the masking feature must remain explicitly outside its scope.

## Failure conditions
The structure fails when warnings merely transfer responsibility to individual attention, blanket prohibition blocks useful work, convenience leads to unrestricted submission, masking is treated as a safety guarantee, humans surrender final judgment, contextual re-identification is ignored, responsibility and explanation paths are absent, or one feature is mistaken for complete enterprise information governance.

## Falsification conditions
If the masking feature does not function in practice as an entrance mechanism that enables responsible AI use, is received only as redaction, time saving, or superficial security, or fails to connect to explainable AI operation, the hypothesis that it implements an intermediate structure for “responsibility in handing information to AI” should be reduced, revised, or rejected.

## Required distinctions
Product facts vs. Nakagawa Master's reading; hiding information vs. responsibility for handing it over; AI capability vs. pre-input operation; abstract policy vs. concrete document workflow; prohibition vs. unrestricted use vs. confidentiality-conditioned use; tool operation vs. human final judgment; convenience/time saving vs. explainability/responsibility; one feature vs. enterprise-wide governance; social translation in the external article vs. company/media official position.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not treat masking as a complete security guarantee. Do not invent a fixed rule that deleting certain words makes content safe. Do not claim Bunken makes the final confidentiality judgment for the user. Do not flatten the feature into either a mechanism for stopping AI or a mandate for unrestricted AI adoption. “Input management” and “AI-operation entrance infrastructure” are Nakagawa Master's structural reading, not Web Rider's official self-definition. Do not turn the ZEROICHI article angle into a universal performance guarantee, and do not invent quantitative thresholds absent from the Parent.
