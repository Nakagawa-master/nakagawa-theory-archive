## Summary
This Parent is a Nakagawa structural reading, not a feature profile of Bunken masking. It separates target-side facts such as deletion/replacement of specified terms, bulk masking, and masking of selected ranges from Nakagawa Master’s value kernel: designing responsibility for what information is handed to AI. In organizational AI use, uncertainty over what may be submitted can stop adoption immediately before input. The reading therefore centers not on automatic concealment but on keeping humans as final decision makers who inspect information, mask or replace necessary parts, verify the result, and submit an explainable input. It also preserves why this entrance-stage responsibility, rather than mere convenience, was selected as the external-article axis.

## Concepts
- Target-side facts: deletion/replacement of specified terms, bulk masking, selected-range masking, and related functions.
- Surface classification: redaction, personal-information handling, security assistance, pre-copy convenience.
- Input-decision stoppage: AI use is desired but mixed sensitive information makes submission decisions unclear.
- Human inspection: a person decides what must be protected and what may be submitted.
- Input shaping: masking, replacement, or removal before AI submission.
- Explainable input: a submission state in which what was changed and retained can be checked.
- Responsibility for handing information to AI: Nakagawa Master’s value kernel, not a company-official definition.

## Causal chain
AI-use demand → sensitive information mixed into candidate input → submission decision becomes unclear → human inspection → masking/replacement/removal → post-processing verification → explainable input state → AI submission → use can proceed while judgment responsibility remains located.

## State model
use-demand phase / information-mixing phase / decision-stoppage phase / human-inspection phase / masking phase / final-verification phase / explainable-input phase / use-forward phase.

## Applications
In organizational AI use and document workflows, avoid reducing input governance to total prohibition or unchecked submission. Audit who decides what to protect, what transformations are applied, whether the transformed text is rechecked, and whether the reason for submission remains explainable. Do not expand feature facts into independent legal or technical safety guarantees.

## Measurements and audit
Separate target-side facts, surface classification, the input stoppage point, whether humans remain final decision makers, the inspection process, masking/replacement contents, post-processing verification, explainability of the input decision, Nakagawa Master’s value kernel, why that kernel was selected, why it became the external-article axis, and the boundary from company-official claims. Do not invent safety or incident-rate thresholds absent from the Parent.

## Validity conditions
The reading applies where humans retain final submission judgment and the sequence inspection → masking/replacement → verification → explainable input connects to actual use, creating a responsible path between total prohibition and unchecked submission.

## Failure conditions
It fails if flattened into a redaction or automation feature profile, if masking is equated with guaranteed safety, if human final judgment disappears, if Nakagawa Master’s value kernel is attributed to the company as an official definition, or if the kernel-selection and external-article reasons are omitted.

## Falsification conditions
Narrow or revise the kernel if pre-input inspection, shaping, and verification repeatedly fail to contribute to safer, explainable AI use and another mechanism better resolves input-decision stoppage. Revise as well if preserving a human final decision maker does not function operationally.

## Required distinctions
target-side facts / Nakagawa Master interpretation; masking feature / responsibility for handing information to AI; hiding information / safety guarantee; automated processing / human final judgment; total prohibition / responsible input design; unchecked access / productive use; value kernel / company-official view; reason for kernel selection / reason for external-article axis.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not claim that masking makes any information safe to submit. Do not claim the feature guarantees legal compliance or complete security. Do not silently replace human judgment with automation. Do not replace the concrete external-article reason—showing a responsible path between prohibition and unrestricted submission—with a generic phrase such as social translation.
