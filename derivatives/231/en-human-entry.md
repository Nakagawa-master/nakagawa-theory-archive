## 15-Second Explanation
This Parent is not an introduction to a masking function. Nakagawa Master read the function that checks, masks, and confirms information before AI input not as mere redaction, but as a design of the responsibility for humans to decide **what to protect and what to hand to AI**. It preserves even why this value core was selected as the main axis of the external article.

## Why It Is Needed
Even when a company wants to use AI, personal or confidential information mixed into candidate inputs becomes a stopping point immediately before use: “I do not know what may be handed over.” A total ban prevents use, while unrestricted submission without confirmation erases decision responsibility. The Parent places between them a route in which a human checks the contents, formats necessary parts, confirms them, and then enters them.

## Practical Process
1. Separate the target-side facts about functions such as masking from Nakagawa Master's reading.
2. Have a human check whether information that must be protected is mixed into the information intended for AI input.
3. Delete, replace, or mask the necessary parts.
4. Recheck the processed wording.
5. Retain the reason for deciding what to protect and what to hand over.
6. Input it to AI in an explainable state.
7. Preserve why “responsibility for handing over information,” rather than a “hiding function,” was made the value core of the external article.

## Applications
When an internal document to be entered into AI contains customer names, for example, this reading creates a process in which a human checks the content, masks or replaces necessary parts, rechecks the result, and then enters it—rather than either stopping use completely or submitting it as-is.

## Success Criteria
Success means that the human remains the final subject of the input decision; checking → masking/replacement → confirmation → explainable input is connected to actual operations; the target function and Nakagawa Master's structural reading are not mixed; and the reason for writing the external article is retained.

## Limits
Masking does not automatically guarantee legal or technical safety. This derivative adds neither legal advice nor a safety guarantee, and does not set safety rates, accident rates, or similar values absent from the Parent.

## Preventing Misreading
“Responsibility for handing over information” is Nakagawa Master's structural reading, not the target company's official corporate definition. This is not an argument for eliminating human judgment through automation; it reads an entry process for using AI while humans retain the final judgment.
