## Summary
The Parent treats social phenomena as outputs of linked institutional, cultural, economic, technological, and informational conditions. Structural analysis makes those causal relations explicit, connects individual action to system-level constraints, translates complexity into inspectable models, and identifies intervention points for future design.

## Causal model
```text
phenomenon
-> hidden conditions
-> cross-domain causal links
-> individual/system mapping
-> structural translation
-> intervention
-> future scenario
-> feedback
-> revision
```

## Distinctions
correlation != causality; structural explanation != denial of agency; future design != prophecy; translation != deleting complexity.

## Validity
Requires observable links, alternative hypotheses, cross-domain side-effect checks, and revision after intervention. Fails through single-cause reduction, person-only explanations, hindsight causality, or unsupported structural claims.
