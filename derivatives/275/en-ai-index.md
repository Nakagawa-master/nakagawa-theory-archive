## Summary
The Parent treats civilizational collapse not as a single dramatic instant but as a progressive loss of the capacity to reverse local errors, reconstruct causal explanations, and form agreement again. The Irreversible Point (IP) is therefore not a disclosed fixed number. It is a trend-critical condition in which Reversal Cost (RC) persistently outruns replenishable resources while Causal Edit Adaptability (CEA) declines, causing what should be local corrections to expand into system-wide rollback.

When causal maps, dictionaries, and responsibility boundaries deteriorate, CEA falls because actors can no longer see which change affects which dependency. Rollback scope then expands, RC consumes time, trust, personnel, and political or organizational slack, and re-agreement becomes harder. Before that loop becomes self-reinforcing, the Parent introduces a Structural Airbag: temporarily pause expansion, re-declare the unit of agreement, shrink rollback to difference-sized slots, restore causal maps and dictionaries, then reopen change only after CEA and reversibility recover. The safeguard is reversible and non-punitive; it is not an authorization for permanent emergency control.

## Causal chain
causal map / dictionary / responsibility boundary degrades -> effect range becomes hard to trace -> CEA declines -> local repair becomes difficult -> rollback scope expands -> RC rises -> time/trust/personnel/political slack is consumed -> RC growth exceeds replenishment -> explanation, reversal and re-agreement become harder -> IP risk rises -> Structural Airbag activates -> decision expansion pauses -> agreement unit is re-declared -> rollback unit is reduced to a difference slot -> causal map and dictionary are restored -> CEA recovers -> RC pressure falls -> re-agreement reopens -> recoverability is preserved.

## Applications
For policy change, split broad transitions into trials, time-bounded implementation, and re-review while preserving authority and explanation lines before and after each difference. For mergers, separate organizational, data, contract, specification, and accountability changes so each can be reversed without undoing everything. For AI updates, retain independent differences for model, prompt, evaluation criteria, data, permissions, and audit logs so failure does not leave only a full-system shutdown option.

The structural point is not “make every change small.” The causal requirement is to keep changes in units whose dependencies, responsibility, and rollback consequences remain observable. Small changes without a maintained map or dictionary can still produce low CEA and high RC.

## Measurements and audit
Observe RC trend, CEA trend, replenishment capacity, boundary clarity, map/dictionary maintainability, phase stability S, time-to-reagreement, Rollback Cost, LPI, harmful-lock-in detection, and coercive dynamics or drift inside the observation window. Do not collapse the bundle into a single KPI and do not infer unpublished thresholds or equations.

Audit whether local rollback is actually usable, whether rights/explanation/accountability were removed merely to lower RC, whether a temporary pause has become an indefinite freeze, and whether higher CEA leads to real restoration of re-agreement and reversal capacity.

## Validity conditions
RC and CEA are observed as a bundle; available-resource replenishment is tracked over time; rollback units can be made smaller; causal maps, dictionaries, specification differences, and responsibility boundaries remain revisable; silence functions as an observation/editing window; a pause has a stated purpose and reopening condition; audit is used for recovery rather than punishment; re-agreement, objection, reversal, and re-review paths remain open; even beneficial lock-in retains a rollback path.

## Failure conditions
Treat IP as a secret universal number; use crisis language to remove reversal, objection, explanation, or rights; turn the Structural Airbag into permanent emergency authority; lower RC by deleting accountability or audit; interpret CEA as unconstrained freedom to change anything; divide changes mechanically while causal maps remain stale; allow a temporary pause to become a freeze without reopening conditions; label harmful lock-in as stability; invent unpublished thresholds, formulas, or intervention algorithms.

## Falsification conditions
Revise the model if higher CEA does not restore local rollback, lower RC does not improve re-agreement, restored maps still fail to make impact ranges traceable, phase stability worsens, rollback cost rises, harmful lock-in increases, or coercion/drift repeatedly appears during observation. If re-agreement collapses without a corresponding RC rise, RC alone is insufficient and the detection model must be revised rather than protected from counterevidence.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not derive hidden danger numbers or automatic trigger rules from the phrase “final threshold.” The Parent treats IP as a structural and temporal criticality. “Forced silence” in the public-safe context means pausing decision expansion until the unit of agreement is aligned; it does not authorize suppressing participation. Improving CEA by deleting explanation, rights, or responsibility is self-contradictory. Beneficial lock-in means staged stabilization with a return path, not irreversibility as a goal.
