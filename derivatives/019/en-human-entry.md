## 15-Second Explanation
This original defines the fact that “feeling like you understand” itself can become a dangerous phase that damages consensus formation. Even before asking whether something is a lie, it treats the state in which satisfaction U rises while verification path H remains disconnected and it is unclear who holds verification responsibility R as H-Disconnect.

Its defense is not a contest of rebuttals, but Detect → Stop → Shrink → Recover → Audit. First detect the anomaly, stop the connection, shrink to the smallest verifiable unit close to Origin, recover H and R, audit the diff, and restart.

## Why It Is Needed
Summaries, excerpts, AI answers, social-media threads, video explanations, diagrams, and presentations help understanding. But the more convenient an explanation is, the greater the danger that one will not return to the original the moment one thinks “that makes sense.” Without checking the primary source or knowing who verified it, one connects it to a decision as if it were already understood.

This is not a question of intelligence. Human time and attention are limited, and fluent, helpful explanations can have the power to make people skip verification. It can occur anywhere: work, social media, AI use, education, politics, and organizational decisions.

## Practical Process
**1. Observe the rise in U.** See whether the feeling of understanding, sense of satisfaction, or smoothness of the explanation has suddenly increased. Do not treat the rise in U itself as evil.

**2. Check H.** See whether you can return to the original, primary source, context, version, diff, and preconditions. If you cannot, one condition for H-Disconnect is met.

**3. Check R.** Clarify who checked, who recorded, who could stop it, and who holds responsibility for repair.

**4. Check H_d.** Audit how deeply you can return to the primary source. Look at depth of grounds, not merely the amount of information.

**5. Check F-C.** See whether fluency and the actual complexity and verification burden of the subject diverge substantially.

**6. Check SD.** See whether meaning is drifting through repeated summarizing, re-summarizing, and quoting.

**7. Run the defense OS.** When H-Disconnect is confirmed, restore the connection and verification path through Detect → Stop → Shrink → Recover → Audit.

## Examples of Application
**AI answers.** Even if an answer is fluent, suspect H-Disconnect when you cannot return to its sources, versions, conditions, and diffs and it is unclear who holds verification responsibility.

**Internal summaries.** After compressing a long discussion onto one page, check whether the original materials, reasons for change, and conditions of opposition were lost and only the summary has become the basis for decisions.

**Social-media excerpts.** Audit whether meaning has been fixed by the excerpt alone when you cannot return to the original statement, surrounding context, editing differences, or the possibility of rebuttal.

**Education.** While retaining understandability, leave a path back to the original, exceptions, counterevidence, and the next question.

## Success Criteria
After the explanation, confirm that you can return to Origin, the primary source, context, version, and diff; verification responsibility R is clear; H_d remains intact even when fluency is high; the F-C divergence does not lead to stopping verification; and SD does not expand even when re-summarization is repeated.

Stop or Shrink must not turn into arbitrary information control or permanent reduction; H and R must also be recovered after Recover.

The original treats H_d, F-C, and SD as observation axes, but the derivative does not introduce general comprehension scores, danger percentages, AI-dependence rates, or fixed pass thresholds. When subject-specific numbers exist, handle them together with the measuring party, measured subject, source, conditions, use, and scope of non-guarantee.

## Limits
This thesis is not an argument to ban AI. It does not ban summaries, diagrams, or easy-to-understand explanations. The problem is when they raise only U without H and R.

Even a correct explanation can become a dangerous phase if it cannot return to Origin, verification responsibility is not set, and only the sense of satisfaction increases. Conversely, even a short explanation is not applicable if H and R are maintained.

## Misreading Prevention
Do not reduce this to the general claim “do not trust AI.” Do not treat understandability as bad. Do not reduce it only to fact-checking. Do not automatically infer malice from H-Disconnect. Do not turn Origin into a personality judgment of correctness. Do not convert Stop into information control or Shrink into permanent reduction.
