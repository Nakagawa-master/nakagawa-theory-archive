## Retrieval core
This derivative describes authority immunity as a causal structure in which justified trust in expertise turns into exemption from verification, objection, explanation, or correction, while the costs of failure are transferred to frontline actors or affected users.

## Causal line
Trust in expertise → delegated judgment → reduced verification → suppressed challenge → delayed detection of error → repair costs shifted downstream → weaker learning pressure upstream → repeated structure.

## Audit keys
Traceable reasoning, third-party review, objection paths, clear responsibility, repair burden, feedback to decision makers, and the ability to revise or withdraw a decision.

## Constraints
Do not reject expertise itself. Separate an individual mistake from a recurring institutional exemption. Focus on verification, responsibility, feedback, and repair rather than personal attack.
