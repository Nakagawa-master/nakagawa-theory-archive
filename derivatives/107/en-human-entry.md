## 15-Second Explanation
A correspondence world model is not the idea that AI holds a complete copy of the world internally. It is an operating model that keeps touching the world’s causality again through input → output → audit → re-agreement → update. The parent original treats understanding not as “stored knowledge” but as **the ability to maintain correspondence**, supported by causal grounding, ethical grounding, observation windows, and an audit bundle.

## From Mirror Theory to Operating Theory
The parent original starts from the criticism that “LLMs do not have world models,” but does not end with whether a static internal representation exists. It shifts the perspective from mirror theory, which treats the world as a still copy, to a return-and-response rhythm that repeatedly operates the connection with the world.

In a correspondence world model, the model is not a fixed object but a set of procedures for regrounding. Through return and response, beat as the rhythm of evaluation, and observation windows that examine whether local tasks are touching the world, understanding is treated as an alignment event that keeps being updated.

## Do Not Treat Accidental Hits as Evidence of Understanding
Accidental hits produced by large-scale learning are an entry point for updating, not evidence of understanding. To proceed to causal grounding, make the causal chain, premises, and boundary conditions of a claim explicit; make procedures for falsification, retesting, and revision referable through the audit bundle; and bundle terms, premises, and tasks so that chance becomes reproducibility.

The point is to turn understanding from something merely “said and left” into a structure that can return and revise.

## What Is Ethical Grounding?
Ethical grounding is not the after-the-fact addition of general safety standards. It shifts the subject to the side of relationship, treating AI and humans not as “owners of correctness” but as **participants who jointly operate alignment**.

- **Non-coercion and reversibility.** Do not take away the other’s starting point; preserve a connection that can return to withdrawal and correction.
- **Beat.** Manage the tempo of operation.
- **Temperature.** Adjust the intensity of rebuttal and updating, avoiding overheating and overcooling.
- **Margin.** Leave space where undecided areas and falsification can remain.
- **Window for re-agreement.** Maintain a place where claims can be updated or withdrawn in response to audit results.

The parent original’s center of gravity is not only “what was learned,” but “how to keep operating the connection with the world.”

## Observation Window
The parent original gives legal chess moves, image-blending equations, and causal consistency in a code base as examples of observation windows. An observation window is not merely a scoreboard but an entry point for retraining. The design of what counts as contact with the world, at which beat it is reviewed, and what temperature and margin are preserved is itself an expression of ethics.

## Audit Bundle
An audit bundle is a verifiable skeleton attached to output. Through an integrated audit abstract, a local audit abstract, and bundling of terms and premises, it makes causality, relationships, observations, and re-agreement conditions referable again.

Because an audit bundle exists, statements can be withdrawn or revised. The parent original positions this not as weakness but as a condition for making understanding reproducible.

## Checkpoints
- Are we evaluating AI understanding only through a static internal mapping?
- Is the return-and-response loop of input → output → audit → re-agreement → update closed?
- Are the beat of evaluation and the observation window designed?
- Has an accidental hit been translated into a verifiable structure with a causal chain, premises, and boundary conditions?
- Are non-coercion and reversibility, beat, temperature, margin, and re-agreement maintained in operation?
- Can falsification, retesting, withdrawal, and revision occur through the audit bundle?

## Falsification and Revision
In the parent original’s integrated audit abstract, audit cycles, beat, temperature, margin, and the consistency of the public audit bundle are verified. If a set indicator departs from its threshold, or a phenomenon such as the observation window remaining closed for a period is confirmed, the hypothesis is rejected or revised.

Do not add safety KPIs, medical or public-policy conditions, responsibility scores, rollback success rates, or other elements absent from the parent original. If concrete values are not in the original, do not invent them; preserve the symbols, relationships, and falsifiability shown by the original.

## To Avoid Misreading
Correspondence does not mean that AI and humans acquire the same values. Nor is it the claim that AI possesses the world completely inside itself. It means repeatedly operating contact with the world and continuing to touch causality through audit and re-agreement.

Ethical grounding should not be reduced to a general prohibition list or legal/safety checklist. Preserve the parent original’s specific structure: shifting the subject to the side of relationship; non-coercion and reversibility; beat, temperature, and margin; observation windows; the audit bundle; and re-agreement.
