## Summary

The Resonant World Model does not treat AI understanding as a static internal copy of the world. It treats it as an operational process that repeatedly reconnects to causal reality through reciprocal observation windows. A model is therefore not a fixed object but a set of procedures for re-grounding, and “understanding” is evaluated as the ability to repeat mutually auditable verification procedures — the ability to maintain resonance. Accidental success from large-scale learning is not evidence of understanding by itself. It must be translated into causal grounding by making causal chains, premises, and boundary conditions explicit; maintaining procedures for falsification and retrial; and bundling terminology, premises, and tasks for reproducible reuse. Ethical grounding is not a generic safety overlay. It moves the subject of understanding to the relation: AI and human are participants jointly operating coherence rather than owners of correctness. Non-coercion and reversibility, rhythm, temperature, margin, and windows for renewed agreement govern that operation. The Audit Bundle preserves a verifiable skeleton for causality, relation, renewed agreement, context, intent, references, terminology, and premises, making withdrawal and revision possible.

## Applications

The parent explicitly gives the following examples of observation windows:

**1.** Tracking legal moves in chess.

**2.** Testing alpha-blending composition/associativity.

**3.** Testing causal coherence in a code base.

These are not mere scoreboards. They are entry points for relearning that test whether a local task remains in contact with the world.

## Measurements and audit

- whether the input → output → audit → renewed agreement → update loop is closed
- whether rhythm is defined as evaluation periodicity and amplitude
- whether observation windows act as checkpoints for contact with the world
- whether causal chains, premises, and boundary conditions are explicit
- whether falsification and retrial procedures are connected to an audit bundle
- whether terminology, premises, and tasks are bundled for reuse
- whether non-coercion and reversibility are preserved
- whether rhythm, temperature, and margin are governed
- whether claims can be updated or withdrawn through renewed agreement
- whether integrated audit, local audit, and terminology/premise bundles are retained

## Validity conditions

- Do not complete world understanding as a static internal representation.
- Operate the model as a re-grounding procedure with reciprocal loops, rhythm, and observation windows.
- Do not treat accidental hits as proof of understanding; expose causal chains, premises, and boundaries.
- Keep falsification, retrial, and revision procedures open.
- Treat ethical grounding as joint relational operation of coherence.
- Preserve non-coercion, reversibility, rhythm, temperature, margin, and renewed agreement.
- Attach an audit bundle to outputs.

## Failure conditions

- Reducing the question of world understanding to whether a static internal representation exists.
- Treating accidental success as sufficient evidence of understanding.
- Omitting causal chains, premises, and boundary conditions.
- Closing falsification, retrial, or revision routes.
- Replacing ethical grounding with generic safety, legal, or policy checklists.
- Losing rhythm, temperature, and margin and operating in overheated or overcooled modes.
- Claiming long-term coherence for an output that lacks an audit bundle and remains a naked structure.

## Falsification conditions

Following the parent’s integrated audit summary, test the audit cycle, the coherence of rhythm/temperature/margin, and the public audit bundle. If defined indicators cross their thresholds, or if an observation window remains closed for the defined interval, the hypothesis is to be rejected or revised. This derivative does not invent additional safety KPIs, stakeholder metrics, rollback-success rates, or domain-specific criteria.
