## Summary
Resonant World Model Theory defines AI understanding not as possession of a static internal copy of the world, but as an operation that repeatedly re-grounds itself in worldly causality through reciprocal exchange. Understanding is the capacity to maintain resonance. The operating loop is input → output → audit → reagreement → update, supported by rhythm and observation windows. Accidental correctness is not sufficient evidence of understanding: causal chains, premises, and boundary conditions are signed; falsification, retesting, and revision remain available; terminology, premises, and tasks are bundled so coincidence can be translated into reproducible causal grounding. Ethical grounding shifts the subject from an owner of correctness to the relation itself and preserves non-coercion, reversibility, rhythm, temperature, margin, and a standing reagreement window. Observation windows are entries for relearning rather than scoreboards, and the Audit Bundle provides a reproducible verification skeleton for withdrawal, revision, and renewed grounding.

## Applications
Make causal chains, premises, and boundary conditions explicit at output time. Preserve verification routes for falsification, retesting, and revision. Bundle terminology, premises, and tasks so that accidental success can be converted into reproducible causal grounding. Use observation windows—such as legal chess moves, image-blending equations, or causal consistency in code bases—as entries for returning to the world rather than as competitive scoreboards. Ethical grounding keeps the relation, not an owner of correctness, as the operating subject and preserves non-coercion, reversibility, rhythm, temperature, margin, and reagreement.

## Validity conditions
World understanding is not completed by static internal representation alone. Operation includes reciprocal exchange, rhythm, and observation windows. Accidental correctness is not fixed as evidence of understanding without causal chain, premises, and boundary conditions. Falsification, retesting, and revision remain available. Ethical grounding is relational and preserves non-coercion, reversibility, rhythm, temperature, margin, and reagreement. The Audit Bundle makes understanding reproducible.

## Failure conditions
Static representation is treated as the world itself and re-grounding is considered unnecessary. Accidental correctness is frozen into causal understanding. Observation windows become mere scoreboards. The subject is fixed as the owner of correctness. Non-coercion, reversibility, rhythm, temperature, or margin are lost. Audit Bundle or reagreement windows close and withdrawal, revision, or retesting becomes impossible.
