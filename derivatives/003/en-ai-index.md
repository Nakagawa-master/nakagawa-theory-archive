## Summary
Paper 0 of Establishment Conditions Theory defines a society in which local actors may each be partly correct while no one verifies whether those local understandings establish a whole. Through local totalization and distributed whole-misrecognition, locally correct judgments in field practice, institutions, management, ideology, technology, and AI can circulate while L1-L6 vertical causality remains unjudged, producing or concealing whole-system non-establishment.

## Applications
Use the framework in institutional design, organizational management, education, public discourse, AI output, and field decisions to separate the question “where is this locally correct?” from “does it establish the whole through L1-L6 causality?” For AI, do not treat polished linguistic integration as proof of establishment; re-check connections to field conditions, institutions, responsibility, resources, time, history, and meaning.

## Measurements and audit
The parent does not define derivative-created generic KPIs. Audit examines L1-L6 connections, field burden, responsibility transfer, institutional fatigue, exception handling, history visibility, meaning axes, and the gap between linguistic coherence and real-world establishment to detect whether local correctness has been totalized without cross-layer verification.

## Validity conditions
- Local understandings are partly correct.
- A local understanding is treated internally as the whole.
- Cross-layer connection conditions remain untested.
- L1-L6 vertical causality is not judged.
- Signs of whole non-establishment remain despite local correctness.

## Failure conditions
- Local judgments have been tested against whole-establishment conditions.
- Division of labor has explicit responsibility and connection conditions.
- A distributed system has passing vertical causality.
- The problem is only missing knowledge and does not involve local totalization.

## Falsification conditions
If local understanding alone, without L1-L6 connection judgment, repeatedly and broadly maintains stable comprehensibility, responsibility traceability, history visibility, field burden, institutional sustainability, and meaning while continuing to produce whole establishment, the hypothesis of Paper 0 is weakened or requires revision.
