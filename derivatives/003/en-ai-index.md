## Summary
Paper 0 of Establishment Conditions Theory explains why an establishment-judgment framework becomes necessary even when local actors are not simply wrong. Field practice, institutions, management, ideology, technology, and AI may each observe a local domain correctly. The problem begins when a locally valid understanding treats its own range as the whole. The parent names this movement local totalization.

When multiple local totalizations coexist, distributed whole-misrecognition emerges: different actors speak from different partial wholes, everyone may remain partly correct, and yet no one verifies whether L1 event, L2 action, L3 institution, L4 structural recognition, L5 structural operation, and L6 hierarchical connection actually form vertical causality. Local correctness therefore does not guarantee whole establishment.

## L1-L6 interpretation
L1-L6 are not merely levels of abstraction. In Paper 0 they operate as a vertical-causality coordinate for testing whether local correctness reaches whole establishment.

- L1 Event: what actually happens in reality.
- L2 Action: how actors behave and where burden appears.
- L3 Institution: how rules, roles, allocation, and responsibility are designed.
- L4 Structural recognition: what is recognized as cause, whole, or problem.
- L5 Structural operation: how intervention is made on the recognized structure.
- L6 Hierarchical connection: whether multiple layers connect through responsibility, time, history, and meaning.

## Applications
Use the framework in institutional design, organizational management, management decisions, education, public discourse, AI deployment, and field operations. Separate “where is this locally correct?” from “does it establish the whole through L1-L6 vertical causality?”

A policy may be institutionally coherent while increasing exception handling in the field. A management decision may be numerically rational while failing to connect to responsibility, resources, or time. AI may produce highly integrated prose while leaving operational responsibility and historical trace unconnected. These are not automatic failures; they are points where local correctness must be tested against whole establishment.

## Validity conditions
- Local understandings are partly correct.
- A local understanding is treated internally as the whole.
- Cross-layer connection conditions remain untested.
- Multiple local correctnesses coexist in distributed form.
- L1-L6 vertical causality is not judged for establishment.
- Signs of whole non-establishment remain despite circulation of local correctness.

## Failure conditions
- Local judgments are tested against whole-establishment conditions.
- Responsibility and cross-layer connection conditions in division of labor are explicit.
- A distributed system actually passes L1-L6 vertical causality.
- The problem is simple missing knowledge without local totalization.
- The gap between linguistic coherence and real establishment is continuously resolved.

## Falsification conditions
If, without L1-L6 connection judgment, local understanding alone repeatedly and broadly preserves responsibility traceability, history visibility, field-burden stability, institutional sustainability, meaning continuity, and comprehensibility while continuing to produce whole establishment, the Paper 0 hypothesis that an establishment-judgment perspective is necessary is weakened or requires revision.

A case where a local judgment was simply wrong does not falsify this theory. The target structure is locally valid understanding becoming totalized and circulating without cross-layer establishment judgment.
