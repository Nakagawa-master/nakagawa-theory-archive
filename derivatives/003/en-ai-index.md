## Summary

Paper 0 of Establishment Conditions Theory defines a social failure mode in which local actors can each be partly correct while no one verifies whether those local understandings establish a coherent whole. Its core mechanism is not simple ignorance. A field operator, institution, manager, theorist, engineer, or AI system may correctly understand a bounded domain and then implicitly treat that bounded understanding as the whole. When many such local totalizations coexist, a distributed whole-misrecognition forms: local correctness circulates while the vertical causal connections required for actual establishment remain unjudged.

The parent original uses L1-L6 as a vertical causal inspection coordinate rather than as a generic abstraction taxonomy. The key question is whether observable events, behavior, institutions, structural cognition, structural operation, and cross-hierarchy linkage actually connect without shifting burden, responsibility, exceptions, history loss, or meaning failure elsewhere. A linguistically coherent explanation, especially one produced by AI, can therefore look globally integrated while remaining locally correct and structurally unestablished. The derivative must preserve that distinction between linguistic integration and real establishment.

This paper is the entry point explaining why establishment judgment itself is necessary. It does not demand an omniscient actor who sees everything, nor does it reduce the problem to ordinary silo criticism. It requires explicit inspection of the connection conditions that make separate local truths establish a functioning whole.

## Applications

- Institutional design: separate whether a rule is locally rational from whether it establishes across field burden, responsibility, resources, exceptions, and downstream effects.
- Organizational management: inspect whether each department is locally correct while cross-department causal connections remain unowned or unverified.
- Education and public discourse: distinguish coherent explanations from explanations that actually preserve implementation, responsibility, history, and meaning.
- AI output review: do not treat polished synthesis as proof that L1-L6 connections exist in reality; re-check the underlying conditions.
- Field operations: identify where burden, exception processing, or responsibility is displaced to another layer even though the originating decision appears correct.

## Validity conditions

- Local actors or systems are at least partly correct within bounded domains.
- A bounded local understanding is treated as if it were sufficient for the whole.
- Cross-layer connection conditions remain untested or unowned.
- L1-L6 vertical causality is not explicitly judged.
- Signs of whole non-establishment, burden shift, responsibility diffusion, exception accumulation, history loss, or meaning failure remain despite local correctness.
- The analysis preserves the difference between local validity and whole establishment.

## Failure conditions

- Local decisions are already tested against explicit whole-establishment conditions.
- Division of labor includes clear ownership of cross-layer connections and failure handling.
- A distributed system repeatedly demonstrates stable L1-L6 causal connection without hidden burden transfer.
- The problem is merely missing information and does not involve local totalization.
- Linguistic coherence is not being mistaken for real-world establishment.

## Falsification conditions

- If systems that never perform explicit L1-L6 connection judgment nonetheless maintain long-term, broad stability in field burden, responsibility traceability, exception handling, history visibility, meaning, and whole establishment, the Paper 0 hypothesis should be weakened or narrowed.
- If local understandings consistently combine into reliable whole establishment without producing distributed whole-misrecognition, the claimed necessity of establishment judgment requires revision.
- If AI-generated linguistic integration reliably predicts real establishment without independent structural verification, the AI-amplification warning would need to be narrowed.
