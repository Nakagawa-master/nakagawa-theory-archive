## 15-Second Explanation
Treatise 0 of Establishment Conditions Theory does not discuss “a society where no one sees anything.” It addresses the structure in which the judgment of whether something is established as a whole drops out because the field, institutions, management, thought, technology, and AI may each understand their own locality partially correctly while treating that locality as the whole. The original calls this cognitive movement “localization-as-wholeness,” and understands the state in which it coexists in multiple places in society as “distributed whole-misrecognition.”

The important point is that being locally correct and being established as a whole are not the same. Even if there are many partial truths—correct institutionally, rational numerically, technically possible, coherent as writing, and correct as a field intuition—establishment in reality is not guaranteed unless it has been checked whether the vertical causality from L1 to L6 actually passes through.

## Why It Is Needed
A completely wrong explanation is relatively easy to collapse when refuted. An explanation that is correct from one standpoint, however, persists strongly. People in the field see field burdens. Institutional staff see rules and procedures. Management sees numbers and strategy. Engineers see implementability. AI can organize large amounts of information and produce writing that looks like a linguistically coherent whole. Each can be partially correct.

The problem is that this local correctness is treated internally as the whole. When the feeling that “what I see is certainly correct” shifts into the feeling that “therefore I understand the whole problem,” the conditions connecting it to other layers become less likely to be examined. This is localization-as-wholeness.

When localization-as-wholeness occurs simultaneously from multiple standpoints, everyone is seeing something correctly, yet no one is seeing the same whole. Even if field correctness, institutional correctness, management correctness, technical correctness, and AI’s coherent explanation coexist, the whole remains unestablished if responsibility, resources, time, exception handling, history, and axes of meaning connecting the layers have not been confirmed.

As a result, additional burdens may arise in the field, exception handling may increase in institutions, responsibility may be pushed between departments or layers, histories may become formalities, and the original meaning may thin out. Yet each locality is correct within its own range, making it hard to find “what is wrong.” Treatise 0 requires converting this state not into criticism of people, but into a judgment of establishment: does the vertical causality from L1 to L6 really pass through?

In the AI era, this problem becomes even harder to see. AI can quickly create explanations crossing multiple domains, combining local truths into a smooth explanation that resembles a whole. But high coverage or consistency in writing is different from actual connection to field action, institutions, responsibility, resources, time, history, and axes of meaning. Treatise 0 shows the need to examine linguistic coherence and establishment in reality separately.

## Practical Process
**1. Separate “correct where?”** First divide the target judgment into localities such as the field, action, institution, management, technology, principles, and AI output. Do not reduce it to the binary “correct/wrong”; state the range of observation in which it is correct.

**2. Check the L1–L6 connections.** Check how far causality passes, and where it breaks, among L1 events, L2 actions, L3 institutions, L4 structural cognition, L5 structural operation, and L6 hierarchical connection. Do not merely fill in layer names; follow the actual causal connections.

**3. Check unprocessed burdens.** Check whether additional field work, exception handling, implicit coordination, shifting of responsibility, or excess time is arising to cover failed connections. Even if a local judgment is correct, whole-system establishment remains unconfirmed if invisible burdens required to make that correctness work are pushed into another layer.

**4. Check for gaps in responsibility.** While each actor can say “it is correct within my range,” check whether no actor remains responsible for failed connections between layers. In distributed whole-misrecognition, the problem is not the actor who is wrong but the absence of responsibility for connection.

**5. Confirm history and meaning.** Check whether it is possible to later refer back to why a judgment was made, which conditions changed, and where exceptions occurred. When history disappears, local judgments cannot be reconnected to whole-system results.

**6. Separate AI’s sense of integration from establishment.** Do not reject the fact that AI writing is polished. Separately inspect which field action its output enters, which institution it connects to, who bears responsibility, what resources and time it requires, and which history it leaves behind.

**7. Do not demand an omniscient person.** The solution is not to show one person everything. What is needed is a structure in which divided localities are connected as vertical causality and people can jointly judge where establishment holds and where it breaks.

## Examples of Application
**Organizations.** Even when management figures or strategy are rational, if field burdens, emergency responses, and coordination roles keep increasing during execution, separate the correctness of the management locality from whole-system establishment. Do not assume the figures are wrong; inspect the connection from the figures to the field.

**Administration and institutions.** Even when procedures are correct, if exception handling increases, users or field staff implicitly fill the gaps, and it is unclear who will repair a failed connection, do not treat institutional coherence alone as establishment.

**Education and public discourse.** Even when an explanation is ideologically or logically correct, if recipients cannot return to its premises, history, origin, and meaning and cannot reproduce the understanding, separate a good explanation from established understanding.

**Projects.** Even when each specialist team has completed its own assignment, if specifications, responsibility, time, or exception conditions are blank at the handoff point, do not treat the sum of individual completion as whole-system establishment.

**AI use.** Even when an AI proposal or summary is high quality, if it has not been confirmed who makes the final decision, which institution it enters, which field action it changes, what burden or responsibility it creates, and which history it leaves, establishment in reality remains undetermined.

**Division of labor.** Division of labor itself is not the problem. If inputs, outputs, responsibility, handoff conditions, and correction routes for exceptions are clear, and local judgments connect to the whole through L1–L6, division of labor can be established.

## Success Criteria
Without denying the expertise or correctness of each locality, one can reconfirm at which layer the judgment is established, what it passes to the next layer, who is responsible, and with what resources and time it connects to reality. This state approaches a judgment of establishment.

It is also important that field burdens and exception handling are not hidden as “someone will take care of it,” and that the location of a failed connection and responsibility for correction can be identified. Aim for a state in which institutional coherence, numerical rationality, and linguistic coherence each retain value without being equated with establishment in reality.

In addition, judgment history, change history, exception history, and axes of meaning must remain so that one can later re-examine “why it was established / why it was not established.” The center of the success judgment is being able to reread whether causality actually passed through L1–L6, rather than merely filling them in as a classification table.

## Limits
This treatise does not reject division of labor, specialization, or local knowledge themselves. Even when no one directly observes the whole, the problem of localization-as-wholeness is weakened in a distributed system where responsibility and connection conditions are explicit and each locality is linked through vertical causality.

If the problem is only a simple lack of information and there is no cognitive movement that treats local understanding as the whole, the scope of this treatise is limited. It is necessary to distinguish a problem solvable by adding unknown information from a problem in which partial correctness is made into the whole.

The Parent Source does not present, in this public reading, a generalizable establishment rate, wholeness score, success probability, or numerical threshold. Therefore, this derivative also does not invent numbers; it observes whether connections exist, movement of burdens and responsibility, preservation of history and meaning, and the difference between linguistic coherence and establishment in reality.

Treatise 0 is an entry point to establishment-conditions theory; it does not complete every detailed definition of establishment itself here. Definitions handed to later treatises must not be preempted by this public reading and turned into settled facts.

## Preventing Misreading
Do not turn this into criticism of people with “narrow perspectives.” Localization-as-wholeness is a cognitive structure that occurs precisely because the locality is actually correct.

Do not replace one locality with a new whole by saying “only the field is correct” or “only management is wrong.” That repeats the same localization-as-wholeness elsewhere.

Do not demand omniscience by saying “just appoint someone who knows everything.” What is needed is to make the connections among divided localities judgeable.

Do not reduce it to a general silo critique or whole-system optimization theory. Treatise 0’s distinctive point is to separate local correctness from whole-system establishment and require a judgment of establishment through the vertical causality of L1–L6.

Do not treat L1–L6 as a mere classification of levels of abstraction. Follow the actual connections, responsibility, burdens, time, and history existing at each layer.

Do not treat AI’s textual coherence, summary accuracy, or coverage as proof of establishment in reality. Conversely, do not automatically declare AI the cause of localization-as-wholeness. Judge how the output connected to real conditions.
