## Summary
The Parent defines an audit summary not as a compressed abstract but as a **reading window** that exposes causality, relationships, counterevidence, and conditions for renewed agreement while keeping a path back to primary evidence. It addresses environments where volume, popularity, authority, speed, or a single metric can overwrite substantive legitimacy. Quantitative consensus can still move decisions forward; legitimacy consensus continues to test whether the decision remains structurally supportable. The audit-summary window keeps those layers parallel so speed does not require irreversibility.

## Concepts
- audit summary: a minimal public reading surface for causality, relations, falsification/counterevidence, and renewed-agreement conditions.
- person separation: retain origin and accountability while preventing popularity or status from substituting for evidence.
- quantitative consensus: the layer that moves decisions through votes, counts, and operational speed.
- legitimacy consensus: the layer that continuously tests structural validity against primary evidence.
- structural log: a traceable record that allows later reconstruction of evidence, decision, and change.
- memory of agreement: preservation of the conditions under which an agreement was made and can later be reconsidered.
- bundle metrics: reading multiple structural axes rather than collapsing legitimacy into one KPI.
- reversibility: the ability to revise, withdraw, or re-agree when conditions or evidence change.

## Causal chain
loud signals, status, speed, or single metrics dominate attention → content, causality, and primary evidence become harder to read → legitimacy becomes person- or volume-dependent → decisions harden through quantitative consensus alone → an audit-summary window separates the claim from popularity → causality, relations, counterevidence, and re-agreement conditions become visible → primary evidence and structural logs remain reachable → quantitative consensus and legitimacy consensus run in parallel → changed conditions or failures can be observed → revision, withdrawal, or renewed agreement becomes possible → structural legitimacy becomes readable.

## Applications
1. Media: append a compact surface for central causality, affected relationships, major counterevidence, review conditions, and primary-source links.
2. Education: require causal structure and falsification conditions before reducing debate to support/opposition.
3. Local government or civic coalitions: preserve decision speed while publishing a continuing legitimacy-review window.
4. Organizations: attach evidence, affected relationships, counterevidence, and reconsideration conditions to major decisions.
5. AI retrieval: retain provenance, falsification, and re-agreement constraints rather than returning only a conclusion.

## Measurements and audit
- Are causality, relationships, counterevidence, and re-agreement conditions separately readable?
- Can the reader return to primary evidence and structural logs?
- Are popularity, authority, and reach prevented from substituting for evidence?
- Are quantitative consensus and legitimacy consensus kept distinct?
- Does the review window remain open after a decision is made?
- Can counterevidence actually trigger revision?
- Are multiple structural axes read without collapsing them into one score?
- Are public axes distinguished from sensitive weighting/composition/threshold rules where disclosure would invite gaming?
- Is the audit summary itself becoming a new prestige or ranking device?

## Validity conditions
The design is valid when claims remain traceable to primary evidence; the four dimensions can be separated; operational decisions and legitimacy review can proceed in parallel; counterevidence and changed conditions can actually trigger revision; and a real re-agreement window remains available. Public interpretability must also be separated from internal weighting or threshold details where necessary to avoid gaming.

## Failure conditions
It fails when the surface becomes a PR abstract, a signature is treated as proof of audit, a single metric substitutes for structural review, counterevidence is decorative, sources are not traceable, review conditions cannot produce actual revision, all decisions are frozen pending audit, or the audit-summary format itself becomes another popularity contest.

## Falsification conditions
The design should be revised if audit summaries do not reduce person dependence or misreading, do not improve source traceability, fail to make counterevidence operational, make re-agreement more rigid, or become a new authority/gaming layer. Numerical examples in the Parent must not be converted into universal fixed thresholds unless the relevant domain separately establishes them.

## Required distinctions
- abstract / audit summary
- person separation / erasing origin
- popularity or volume / substantive validity
- quantitative consensus / legitimacy consensus
- decision operation / legitimacy review
- opposing opinion / falsification condition
- rechecking / renewed agreement
- single KPI / bundle metrics
- public dimensions / nonpublic weights and thresholds
- emotional attention flow / structural-legitimacy flow

## Interpretation constraints
Do not treat the audit summary as a final judge, universal score, or generic summarization template. Person separation does not remove accountability or provenance. Do not abolish quantitative consensus or indefinitely delay action in the name of review. Do not convert the Parent's examples such as RC/ER/CEA/S or Rollback Cost into universal fixed KPIs detached from their original audit context.
