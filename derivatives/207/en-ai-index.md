## Summary
Real Estate Market OS Vol.10 is not a new feature layer but an origin-fixation layer. Its single proposition is that market optimization means optimizing the capability for consensus formation, not merely optimizing price. Social technologies can drift from efficiency to optimization, monetization, and domination while retaining the same name. NCL is therefore framed not as a restrictive terms-of-use regime but as a structural-identity protocol: implementation, technology, and institutional connectors may change, but transparency must not be reduced, selectability must not be narrowed, and responsibility must not be blurred. Origin is retained as a responsibility coordinate rather than a property or power coordinate; supervision is maintenance rather than rule; and deviation is recorded as structural difference in a Deviation Ledger rather than converted into personal attack.

## Concepts
- Single Proposition: optimize consensus capability, not price alone.
- Origin Fixation: preserve causal direction rather than implementation form.
- Social-Technology Drift: efficiency → optimization → monetization → domination.
- Direction Constraint: structural identity depends on direction, not feature count.
- NCL: structural-identity protocol.
- Changeable Layer: UI, implementation, APIs, AI models, IDs, cryptography, institutional connectors, audit cycle.
- Immutable Principles: do not reduce transparency, narrow selectability, or blur responsibility.
- Origin as Responsibility Coordinate: retain causal origin without turning it into ownership power.
- Supervision as Maintenance: preserve direction and difference rather than create permission authority.
- Deviation Ledger: public structural difference record.
- T/S/R: Transparency / consensus with understanding and selectability / Responsibility.
- Future Responsibility: enable future generations to change implementations while retaining principles.

## Applications
- Audit UI or AI-model changes for loss of visible grounds, options, or accountable actors.
- Permit monetization while checking whether transparency has been converted into paid lock-in.
- Permit institutional adoption while rejecting conversion of NCL into obedience or permission control.
- Allow forks while requiring explicit structural differences when inheritance principles are broken.
- Trigger additional audit after major implementation changes, institutional change, major backlash, administrative intervention, or litigation risk.

- Share of transactions or explanations that affected parties cannot understand.

- Rate of unidentified responsible actors or absent supervision paths.
- Deviation Ledger update status.
- Public availability of the Audit Bundle.

## Validity conditions
- Preserve origin as causal direction rather than a fixed feature list.
- Keep freedom to change implementation, technology, and institutions.
- Do not reduce transparency.
- Do not narrow selectability.
- Do not blur responsibility.
- Treat Origin as a responsibility coordinate.
- Maintain the Deviation Ledger and Audit Bundle.
- Keep supervision as maintenance rather than permission authority.

## Failure conditions
- Convert NCL into usage prohibition, ownership control, or permission authority.
- Treat Origin as sovereign ownership or exclusive interpretation power.
- Convert transparency into lock-in.
- Convert selectability into inducement or coercion.
- Convert responsibility into immunity or anonymity.
- Convert tension equilibrium into winner-take-all logic.
- Convert deviation defense into attack or exclusion.
- Freeze implementation or institutional evolution in the name of origin fixation.
- Close the Deviation Ledger or objection path.

## Falsification conditions
Condition Z includes periodic audit and additional audit after implementation changes, institutional changes, major backlash, administrative intervention, or litigation risk. If black-box ratios, unpublished assumptions, unexplainable-transaction rates, complaint rates, unidentified-responsibility rates, or absent-supervision rates cross threshold θ, or if phenomena M such as an unmaintained Deviation Ledger, a closed Audit Bundle, or blocked inquiry channels persist across observation window δ, the design, supervision model, or inheritance method must be revised. If origin fixation itself becomes a control mechanism that reduces transparency, selectability, or responsibility, that is a self-contradiction and a revision trigger.

θ is a falsification-threshold symbol and δ an observation-window symbol. The parent does not specify one universal fixed number or duration for every implementation.
