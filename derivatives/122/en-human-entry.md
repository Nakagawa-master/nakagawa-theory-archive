## 15-Second Explanation
The original is not warning against having emotions. It warns that the intensity of resonance or being moved becomes the basis for rights, allocation, and rank while the lines of Correspondence, Temporal Ethics, Reversibility, and Allocation Accountability disappear. Then people who can speak, cry, gather excitement, or control the entrance become central instead of those who actually supported others, and emotion itself turns into a currency that binds people. The original defines this as an Emotional Extraction Structure.

## Why It Is Dangerous
Even a “kind community” or “economy of connection” can stop verifying actual contribution and burden when being moved itself becomes evidence of legitimacy. If emotional intensity such as “I was saved” or “I believe this so strongly” decides allocation, people who supported the structure for a long time and backstage burdens disappear.

The original also points out the danger that the explanation “you were moved by yourself” or “you chose by yourself” can be reclaimed as a restraint that justifies opaque favoritism or subordination. When the objective shifts from repairing the structure to reproducing emotion, human warmth itself becomes fuel.

This structure can arise naturally anywhere resonance, mutual support, fandom, or a sense of salvation connects to value: AI services, religious groups, fan communities, companies, or small circles of companions. The original does not accuse a particular person; it names the “other side of the barrier” that can arise anywhere.

## What Happens When the Four Lines Are Missing
**No Correspondence.** Spoken value and actual contribution, burden, and support do not match. Emotional expression becomes the basis of justice, and the power to speak or stage things takes over value judgment. The original calls this Emotional Justice Theory.

**No Temporal Ethics.** The line for returning currently unrewarded support in a future beat disappears. Only those who briefly attract attention are rewarded, while long-term supporters are consumed as an assumed foundation. The original calls this cycle the Burnout Market.

**No Reversibility.** Evaluation and allocation cannot be redone. Sanctification occurs through claims such as “that person is special” and “doubting is betrayal.” Objections are suppressed and self-censorship spreads. The original explains this as a condition for cult-of-personality formation.

**No Allocation Accountability.** It becomes impossible to explain who gave a seat, opportunity, or access right to whom and why. The gap between “chosen people” and “those who cannot enter” becomes fixed behind closed doors, and the entrance itself becomes power. The original calls this Access Monopoly.

## Typical Progression
The original organizes several recurring forms of Emotional Extraction Structure: the Faith-Transfer Model, which treats emotional intensity as proof of truth; the Resonance-Currency Model, which makes “thank you” and “I was saved” pseudo-currency; the Access-Monopoly Model, in which a few people control entrances and exits; and the AI-Faith Model, in which responsibility and reversibility dissolve into “it is right because AI said so.”

If **three or more of the four lines are missing, the original’s missing-line matrix classifies it as a Complete Emotional Extraction Structure (EES)**. In this condition, correspondence, repayment, correction, and allocation do not function, and social and ethical redistribution stops.

## Examples of Application
**1. Fan communities.** Check whether emotions such as “I like this,” “I was saved,” and “I support them” are directly exchanged for seats, contact opportunities, internal access, or rank of legitimacy.

**2. Companies.** Check whether questioning a particular executive, superior, or idea is treated as betrayal and evaluation and allocation have become impossible to revise later.

**3. AI services.** Check whether AI output is placed outside ethical review merely because “AI said it,” erasing the responsible party and reversibility.

**4. Support communities.** Check whether long-term supporters and backstage maintainers are consumed as an assumed foundation while only people who stand out momentarily receive praise, opportunities, and access.

**5. Access design.** Confirm whether it is possible to explain who distributed participation rights, opportunities, seats, introductions, and recommendations to whom and why.

## Success Criteria
If recovery is underway, emotional intensity and allocation legitimacy are separated; spoken value corresponds to actual contribution; long-term support has a future repayment line; evaluation and allocation can be corrected later; and the reasons for granting access can be explained. When all four lines return, resonance returns from fuel that consumes human warmth to a relationship that circulates support and trust.

Audits use T = Correspondence, S = structural sustainability, R = recursion, and a public audit bundle to continuously check missing lines, allocation reasons, and correction routes. The threshold `θ` and observation window `δ` at the end of the original are retained as symbols of falsifiability, but their concrete values are not fixed.

## Limitations
Emotional Extraction Structure is not a label for diagnosing a particular person or group from outward appearance alone. It is not a judgment of “EES because they are enthusiastic” or “EES because it looks religious”; it examines structurally whether emotion has become allocation currency, how many of the four lines are missing, and whether the original condition of three or more missing lines applies.

Nor is it a theory that denies emotion itself. The original’s firebreak is not to erase emotion but to require Correspondence, Temporal Ethics, Reversibility, and Allocation Accountability in order to return emotion from extraction to circulation.

## Misreading Prevention
Do not use EES as an individual diagnosis, accusation against a group, or general “dangerous community” label.

Do not omit the original’s explicit classification of “three or more lines missing” or replace it with an arbitrary danger score.

Do not dilute the original-specific causal terms—Emotional Justice Theory, Burnout Market, cult-of-personality formation, Access Monopoly, Faith Transfer, Resonance Currency, AI Faith, and structural thermal death—into generalities.

Do not replace Tomoshibi Structural Ethics with general consent, exit, platform-safety, or AI-ethics concepts; preserve the four lines as the recovery lines themselves.
