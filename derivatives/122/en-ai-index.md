## Structural role
A reverse-side map of Tomoshibi Structural Ethics. It defines how a resonance economy turns into an Emotional Extraction Structure when one or more of four lines—correspondence, temporal responsibility, reversibility, and allocation accountability—are missing.

## Structural summary
The parent defines emotional extraction as a structure in which emotional intensity or declarations of resonance become direct grounds for value, rights, priority, or allocation while one or more of the four ethical lines are absent. Missing correspondence produces emotional-justice logic; missing temporal responsibility produces a burnout market; missing reversibility produces sanctification and guru-like authority; missing allocation accountability produces access monopoly. The parent classifies a state with three or more missing lines as a complete Emotional Extraction Structure (EES). Short-term intensity eventually consumes long-term supporters, hides fatigue and debt, removes correction rights, closes access, and moves toward “structural heat death.” The firewall is the simultaneous restoration of all four lines through Tomoshibi Structural Ethics.

## Central proposition
```text
When resonance itself becomes the basis of allocation
and correspondence, temporal responsibility, reversibility,
and allocation accountability are lost,
emotion becomes fuel for domination, dependence, and extraction
rather than circulation.
```

## Source-defined patterns
1. **Faith-transfer model:** emotional intensity is treated as proof of truth.
2. **Resonance-currency model:** gratitude, rescue narratives, or expressions of need function as pseudo-currency.
3. **Access-monopoly model:** a person or small group controls entry points without explaining allocation responsibility.
4. **AI-faith model:** AI output is treated as authority outside ethical review, dissolving reversibility and responsibility.

## Validity conditions
Within the parent-defined Tomoshibi operating domain, all four lines are required together. They are not presented merely as moral slogans but as a use condition, safety device, and recovery line. Both design-side and operation-side pressures are subject to audit.
