## Summary
The parent defines the Emotional Extraction Structure as the reverse-side map of Tomoshibi Structural Ethics. Tomoshibi Structural Ethics establishes four lines—Correspondence, Temporal Responsibility, Reversibility, and Allocation Accountability—for handling resonance and connection fairly. This parent asks what happens when those lines disappear. It does not target a named organization or person; the structure can arise in AI services, religious groups, fan communities, companies, or small circles wherever connection, resonance, mutual support, or fandom becomes a ground of value.

The ignition point is not emotion itself. It is the conversion of emotional intensity into legitimacy for rights, priority, allocation, and rank. When one or more of the four supporting lines is missing, expressing, offering, or presenting emotion as evidence becomes a currency of distribution. Actual contribution, burden, long-term support, correction rights, and allocation reasons disappear from view. Even voluntary feeling can be recovered as a constraint: “you chose this” or “you were moved by this” can be used to justify dependence or opaque preferential treatment.

The parent maps each missing line to a distinct distortion. Missing Correspondence produces Emotional Justice: storytelling, tears, and performance replace verification of actual contribution and burden. Missing Temporal Responsibility produces a Burnout Market: rewards concentrate on momentary stars while long-term supporters are consumed as an assumed foundation. Missing Reversibility produces conditions for guru formation or sacralization: persons and frameworks become unchallengeable and dissent becomes betrayal. Missing Allocation Accountability produces Access Monopoly: a small group controls entry while criteria remain closed and both insiders and outsiders are bound by selection-related debt.

The deficiency matrix contains a quantitative classification that must remain explicit: **when three or more of the four lines are missing, the parent classifies the condition as a Complete Emotional Extraction Structure (EES)**. The parent also gives four recurring models—Faith-Transfer, Resonance-Currency, Access-Monopoly, and AI-Faith. Although such structures can initially appear energetic, long-term supporters remain unrewarded, fatigue and debt disappear from accounting, correction becomes impossible, and access closes. Resonance changes from mutual support into obligation and indebtedness; the endpoint is named Structural Heat Death.

The recovery firewall is restoration of all four Tomoshibi Structural Ethics lines. Correspondence restores the link between claimed value and actual burden; Temporal Responsibility returns long-term support in a later rhythm; Reversibility keeps evaluation and allocation correctable; Allocation Accountability explains who allocated access, opportunity, or seats and why. The integrated audit treats formalization of the four lines as A and their actual functioning as allocation standards that prevent emotional extraction as B. Audit cycles, T/S/R, and a public audit bundle form condition Z; phenomenon M associated with threshold `θ` or observation window `δ` can require rejection or revision, but the parent does not specify concrete values.

## Applications
- In fan communities, test whether “liking,” “being saved,” or “supporting” is directly exchanged for seats, contact opportunities, insider access, or legitimacy rank.
- In companies, test whether questioning a leader or framework is treated as betrayal and whether evaluation or allocation can still be corrected later.
- In AI services, test whether “the AI said so” places output beyond ethical review and dissolves responsibility or reversibility.
- In support communities, test whether long-term supporters and backstage maintainers are consumed as an assumed foundation while visible actors receive praise and opportunity.
- In access design, make allocation of participation, opportunity, seats, introductions, and recommendations explainable.
- Compare the number and type of missing lines against the parent deficiency matrix, preserving the three-or-more threshold for Complete EES.
