## Summary
The Parent develops the second part of the Structural Judicial OS by turning the first part’s principle—repair structural misalignment rather than judging persons—into a method for distinguishing C (Contribution) from E (Exploitation). Good intentions, effort, reputation, politeness, and short-term results at L1 cannot reliably separate C from E. A well-intentioned intervention can still generate dependence, exhaustion, personalization of decisions, loss of autonomy, and reduced future options. C is defined as cumulative causality that increases structural sustainability, circulation, trust balance, reproducibility, and non-dependence, leaves reusable value after a person exits, and ultimately strengthens the Parent’s L7 value-function model `S = C × 1.0`. E is cumulative causality that consumes trust, circulation, and future room while increasing short-term gain, dependency, black-boxing, or positional extraction. The Multilayer Value Audit connects L1 through L7 as one causal line and checks alignment, distortion, and disconnection. The audit is valid only when T = Transparency, S = Safety, and R = Reproducibility hold together as an AND condition. AI is positioned as a measurement aid for reproducibility, not as a judge. Condition Z, the target-specific threshold θ, observation window δ, and Safety violations provide revision conditions. C/E, structural innocence, sedimentation, and alignment are Parent-internal structural concepts, not equivalents of legal guilt, innocence, punishment, or rights restriction.

## Concepts
- **C / Contribution:** cumulative causality that increases sustainability, circulation, trust, reproducibility, non-dependence, and L7 alignment.
- **E / Exploitation:** cumulative causality that consumes structural assets while producing short-term gain, dependence, black-boxing, exhaustion, or reduced future options.
- **Structural net assets:** the Parent’s cumulative direction of trust, reproducibility, sustainability, connection density, and future room.
- **Mimicry of E:** E that appears at L1 as goodwill, contribution, justice, politeness, or short-term success.
- **Multilayer Value Audit:** a vertical audit connecting L1 through L7 as one causal line.
- **L1:** observable phenomena, statements, actions, and results treated as logs rather than personality judgments.
- **L2:** repetition, behavioral tendency, burden distribution, and response to failure.
- **L3:** relation to roles and institutions: use, parasitism, or improvement.
- **L4:** structural cognition: distinguishing phenomena from causes and results from generating structures.
- **L5:** direction of structural alignment: self-protection vs construction, self-expansion vs whole-system alignment. The Parent does not disclose concrete manipulation techniques.
- **L6:** linkage from local action to organization, society, future, and other layers.
- **L7:** the Parent’s top-level value function, represented here as `S=C×1.0`.
- **T / Transparency:** audit layers, observed misalignment, and causal path are explainable and publishable.
- **S / Safety:** the audit, disclosure, and operation do not themselves destroy the structure through mob punishment, blocked recovery, or trust loss.
- **R / Reproducibility:** the same logs, structure, and observation window δ lead different auditors toward the same judgment.
- **Structural Signature:** long-term causal traces such as continued stability, growth, sharing, and reproducibility after the person exits.
- **Structural innocence:** a Parent-internal concept referring to a long-term trajectory of accumulated C; it is not legal acquittal.
- **Sedimentation:** a Parent-internal model in which E is not connected to amplifying circuits such as reward, authority, or central access.
- **Alignment / Confluence:** a Parent-internal model in which C-producing nodes gain connection, reward, and influence; not loyalty or recommendation-based approval.
- **Condition Z:** recurring audit conditions including audit cycle, T/S/R, and consistency of the public audit bundle.
- **θ:** target-specific threshold for C accumulation rate; no universal fixed value is given.
- **δ:** observation window / time width; no universal fixed duration is given.

## Applications
- **Organization:** evaluate whether well-intentioned intervention reduces others’ autonomy or creates “nothing works without me” dependence.
- **Process design:** treat reusable standards, documented criteria, handoffability, and repair paths as signs of cumulative C.
- **Sales / market activity:** distinguish short-term revenue from long-term trust consumption and reduced future options.
- **Decision meetings:** do not classify dissent as E merely because it disrupts harmony; ask whether it improves structural cognition and repairability.
- **AI-assisted audit:** provide the same public logs, structural definitions, and δ to humans and multiple models, then compare judgment differences. AI remains a measurement aid, not a final personality judge.
- **Recovery:** allow a node previously moving in an E direction to accumulate C and be reassessed rather than permanently labeled.

## Measurements and audit
- Did trust balance increase after the action?
- Did decision cost decrease?
- Does the system continue when the person is absent?
- Are knowledge and decision criteria shareable and reusable?
- Did others gain autonomy and future options?
- Are burden and responsibility less concentrated?
- Is there a repair path when failure occurs?
- Does local success externalize exhaustion or debt to another layer?
- Is L1 appearance aligned with the causal direction of L4–L7?
- T: are used layers, observed misalignment, and causal path publishable?
- S: does the judgment avoid mob punishment, blocked recovery, and trust destruction?
- R: do the same logs, structure, and δ produce the same result across auditors?
- Is C accumulation above or below the target-specific θ?
- Does E amplification persist inside observation window δ?
- Does Condition Z preserve audit cycle, T/S/R, and consistency of the public audit bundle?

## Validity conditions
1. Judge cumulative causality rather than moral character, goodwill, popularity, or loyalty.
2. Connect L1 through L7 vertically rather than relying on L1 alone.
3. Use time-directional logs rather than a single event.
4. Preserve the Parent’s definition of C as structural accumulation and non-dependence.
5. Preserve E as structural asset consumption and dependency generation rather than a synonym for malice.
6. Require T/S/R simultaneously.
7. Make logs, structure, and δ comparable.
8. Keep the causal path and reasons explainable.
9. Keep recovery, rewiring, and renewed accumulation of C possible.
10. Reassess periodically under Condition Z.

## Failure conditions
- Use goodwill, personality, reputation, or popularity as the final C/E criterion.
- Equate higher short-term numbers with C.
- Permanently label a person E from one error or one statement.
- Prioritize authority over reproducibility.
- Keep judgment reasons secret.
- Let an E judgment trigger mob punishment, public shaming, or blocked recovery.
- Promote AI to a final legal or personality judge.
- Turn sedimentation into attack or permanent exclusion.
- Turn alignment into loyalty or recommendation-based privilege.

## Falsification conditions
The Parent’s integrated audit summary states that hypothesis A must be rejected or revised when any of the following occurs:

```text
C accumulation rate < target-specific threshold θ
OR
continued E amplification is observed within window δ
OR
the audit process itself causes structural destruction and violates Safety
```

If T, S, or R is missing and the same logs, structure, and observation conditions do not reproduce the judgment, the Parent’s validity conditions are not satisfied. The Parent does not specify universal numerical values for θ or δ.

## Required distinctions
- **Goodwill ≠ C:** goodwill is motive; C is cumulative structural contribution.
- **Malice ≠ E:** malice is a moral/personality assessment; E is structural asset consumption.
- **Short-term success ≠ C:** success can coexist with long-term exhaustion, dependence, and trust loss.
- **Reputation ≠ Structural Signature:** reputation is L1; Structural Signature is a long-term causal trace.
- **Defense statement ≠ time-directional log:** self-claims do not replace accumulated causal evidence.
- **Structural innocence ≠ legal innocence:** the former is a Parent-internal C trajectory concept.
- **AI measurement aid ≠ judge:** AI supports reproducibility; it does not become the final legal or moral authority.
- **Sedimentation ≠ punishment:** sedimentation removes amplification access rather than inflicting pain.
- **Alignment ≠ loyalty:** alignment is causal connection to C, not obedience or endorsement.
- **θ ≠ fixed score / δ ≠ fixed number of days:** the Parent gives no universal fixed values.
