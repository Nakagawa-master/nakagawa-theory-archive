## 15-Second Explanation
This Parent Source is not concerned with whether someone has goodwill, is trying hard, or is liked. It offers a way to distinguish C (contribution) from E (exploitation) by asking whether **the whole structure’s net assets increased or decreased after the action**.

C is a causal direction that increases trust, reproducibility, sustainability, others’ autonomy, transferability, and future options, ultimately strengthening the Parent’s L7 value function \`S=C×1.0\`. E may create results or a favorable impression in the short term, but leaves exhaustion, dependence on individuals, black boxes, and reduced room for the future.

The judgment is not made from the appearance at L1 alone, but through a multilayer connected-value audit running vertically across L1–L7. The audit itself is invalid unless it simultaneously satisfies T/S/R: transparency, safety, and reproducibility.

## Why It Is Needed
People tend to evaluate what they can see. Careful statements, an apparently sincere expression, effort, popularity, and temporary numbers are easy to share and evaluate. But the Parent Source sees precisely this L1 as the layer easiest to disguise.

For example, a manager who keeps helping subordinates out of goodwill may end up concentrating every decision in their own hands. The person may mean well, but if subordinates’ autonomy declines, work cannot be handed over, and the organization stops when the manager is absent, the structure is thinning.

Conversely, if someone is disliked in the short term but puts ambiguous decision criteria into words, leaves a mechanism anyone can use, and enables improvement to continue after they are gone, the structure may be growing stronger.

If this difference is judged through good and evil, like and dislike, or loyalty, C and E are confused. The Parent Source therefore removes personality evaluation and looks at “what remains in the field” as causality in the direction of time.

## Practical Process
### 1. Log L1 facts
First record what was said, what was done, and what results appeared as facts. Do not mix in personality interpretations such as “a good person” or “has bad intentions.”

### 2. Look for repetition, not just one instance
Did the behavior happen once, or is it repeated? Who bears the burden? When it fails, is it corrected or hidden? Look at the pattern over time.

### 3. Examine the connection to institutions
Is the institution being used to improve the whole, or are gaps in the institution being used so that only one person benefits? Do not judge from the single point that the rules were followed.

### 4. Examine structural cognition and direction
Check whether cause and effect are being confused. Look at whether the direction is toward self-protection and self-expansion, or toward reusable construction and overall coherence. The Parent Source does not disclose concrete L5 structural manipulation techniques; only direction is audited.

### 5. Connect the local and the whole at L6
Check whether one person’s achievement is pushing distortion onto another department, customers, future generations, or lower-level staff. Something can be C locally but become E for the whole if it transfers a burden somewhere above or below.

### 6. Connect to L7
Within the Parent Source, the final question is whether the causal direction strengthens \`S=C×1.0\`. Look not at effort or popularity, but at whether the structure connecting C to success has been strengthened.

### 7. Audit the audit with T/S/R
- T / Transparency: Can the layers used, observed misalignments, and causal path be explained and disclosed?
- S / Safety: Will the judgment or publication produce mob punishment, group lynching, blocked recovery paths, or damage to trust?
- R / Reproducibility: With the same log, structure, and observation width δ, would a different actor approach the same judgment?

In the Parent Source these are AND conditions. If even one is missing, a conclusion may sound plausible but is invalid as an audit.

### 8. Do not fix the judgment permanently
If the log changes, change the judgment. Do not close off rebuilding C from the E direction, rewiring, or returning to confluence. Treat it as an update of structural state, not a permanent label.

## Examples of Application
### A well-intentioned manager
The manager thinks, “I want to help my subordinates.” But they make every decision on others’ behalf, gather information and authority to themselves, and work stops when they are absent. On the Parent’s axis, not goodwill itself but dependency generation, non-reproducibility, and reduced room for the future are signs pointing toward E.

### An unglamorous standardization lead
There are no flashy results, but the person documents decision criteria, turns them into a reusable form anyone can use, and leaves a correction path for failures. If others can operate it after the person leaves, accumulation, non-dependence, and reproducibility point toward C.

### A short-term sales initiative
The numbers look successful. But if it consumes customer trust, increases exhaustion on the front line, and reduces options for the next time, the direction of the L1 result and the structure’s net assets has reversed. The Parent Source looks at cumulative direction over time rather than short-term results.

### A dissent that disrupts the atmosphere
Do not classify someone who voices an unpopular opinion in a meeting as E merely because they disturbed the atmosphere. If the dissent makes an overlooked causal factor visible and increases the decision criteria or the possibility of correction, it may point toward C.

### AI-assisted judgment
Give AI public logs and the same structural conditions, then examine differences from judgments by other people or models. AI is not a judge deciding who is a good person; in the Parent Source it is a measurement aid for finding differences in reproducibility.

## Success Criteria
The Parent’s direction of success is not simply an increase in the amount of outcomes. Check whether the following structural assets are increasing:

- Trust increases.
- The cost of judgment decreases.
- The system works even when the person is absent.
- Know-how is shared rather than monopolized.
- Others’ autonomy increases.
- Concentration of burden and responsibility decreases.
- A correction path remains.
- Future options increase.
- Local optimization does not push burdens onto upper or lower layers.
- The causal direction across L1–L7 is coherent.
- T/S/R hold simultaneously.

The Parent Source continuously checks audit cycles, T/S/R, and the coherence of the publicly auditable bundle as condition Z. If the C accumulation rate falls below threshold θ, E amplification continues within observation window δ, or the audit itself destroys the structure through a Safety violation, hypothesis A is rejected or revised.

The Parent Source has no universal fixed values for θ and δ. Do not invent subject-specific values from outside.

## Limitations
This theory is not an infallible method for seeing the essence of someone’s personality. The reason the Parent Source itself moves judgment toward logs, structure, time span, and publicly explainable causality is that inner states cannot be read directly.

Also, “structural innocence,” “justice,” “sedimentation,” and “confluence” are structural-theory terms within the Parent Source. There is no basis for treating them as the same as acquittal, punishment, administrative sanction, or restriction of rights under current law.

The Parent’s strong expression “structure cannot lie” must not be expanded to mean that log omissions or observation errors are absolutely impossible. This is why continuing audits through T/S/R and condition Z are needed.

Furthermore, Part Two centers on the C/E judgment mechanism and leaves implementation of sedimentation and confluence as institutions to the next chapter. Do not add an original sanction system or authority-allocation rule from this derivative.

## Misreading Prevention
- “Not using good and evil” does not mean ethics or law is unnecessary.
- C/E are directional classifications of cumulative causality, not labels for people.
- An action can be E despite goodwill, and an action can be C despite being disliked.
- \`S=C×1.0\` is a value-function model within the Parent Source, not a scientifically measured law.
- Do not invent fixed numerical values for θ and δ.
- Do not make AI the final judge.
- Do not turn an E judgment into mob punishment, attack, or permanent expulsion.
- Keep the state updateable when the logs change.
- Do not extrapolate concrete L5 structural manipulation techniques.
