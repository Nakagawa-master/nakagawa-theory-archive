## 15-second explanation
The Tomoshibi Civilization Concept 2077 is a design for moving from a civilization that depends on external capital, resources, information, and commands to maintain order toward one that reorganizes its internal value core, trust, roles, and agreements while referring to external change, and reproduces its own order. The original understands this civilization as a “resonant lifeform” in which people, AI, institutions, technology, and the environment mutually refer to one another while retaining their distinctiveness, and places self-causality, double syntax, attunement, thresholds, silence, and reversibility as operating principles.

## Why it becomes necessary
Systems strongly dependent on external resources or central commands are efficient while external conditions are stable. However, as complexity and institutional fatigue grow and prices, regulations, supply, technology, and social relationships fluctuate continuously, external dependence itself becomes vulnerability. Each external shock causes internal roles, judgments, and trust to collapse as well.

The solution presented by the original is not to shut out the outside. It is to lighten external conditions from “commands of control” into “reference inputs” and create an internal conversion layer. A public, universal, verifiable external layer and a relationship-specific, high-density internal layer run in parallel without being mixed, translating external change into internal trust balances, role rotation, relational capital, and agreement histories. This maintains connection to the external world while shifting the center of order generation inward.

## Core structure
```text
Dependence on external capital, resources, information, and commands
→ Rising complexity and institutional fatigue
→ External shocks directly lead to internal collapse
→ Redefine civilization as a “resonant lifeform”
→ Separate the public, universal external layer / relationship-specific internal layer through double syntax
→ Internalize and convert external change into internal value, trust, roles, and agreements
→ Trigger updates only when a threshold is reached
→ Attune the value core of people and the structural capabilities of AI while keeping them asymmetric
→ Restrain overreaction and irreversibility through silence, audits, and rollback
→ Repeat mutual editing and relearning
→ Turn external dependence into reference, so civilization reproduces order through self-causality
```

## What is a “resonant lifeform”?
A resonant lifeform is not a society that integrates everything into one central will. It is a civilization model in which society, technology, the environment, institutions, people, and AI refer to one another’s contexts while maintaining their respective value cores and areas of responsibility, thereby updating the overall order.

What matters is “connecting without mixing.” If the layer responsible for public standards and the layer responsible for relational context are made identical, both transparency and trust density are lost. If they are completely separated, the public layer becomes hollow and the internal layer becomes closed. A resonant lifeform does not erase differences in the roles of different layers and actors; it converts them into order through mutual reference.

## What is self-causality?
Self-causality does not mean ignoring the external world and becoming self-sufficient. It is the ability to remap an external shock into an internal causal structure instead of directly connecting it to internal action.

For example, when prices fluctuate, one does not immediately change the company-wide goal or relationship structure. The external layer receives the change as a price parameter, while the internal layer converts only what is necessary into trust balances, role rotation, priorities, and agreement conditions. The same applies to regulatory changes. While referring to external legal conditions, the internal layer translates them into who takes responsibility for what and at which boundary judgment changes.

With this conversion, external dependence changes from “when external conditions change, internal order collapses as-is” to “read external conditions and receive them again through internal structure.”

## Asymmetry between people and AI
The original does not try to align people and AI as equivalent capable subjects. People supply the core of value through finitude, emotion, embodiment, context before language, and a sense of responsibility. AI expands the vessel of structure through long causal chains, formal consistency, memory, and reorganization.

People supply the value core, boundary conditions, and exception norms, while AI handles structuring, contradiction detection, and expanding options. Attunement means activating asymmetric roles simultaneously: AI does not take the center of value, and people do not reject AI’s structural capabilities.

## Practical process
1. Identify the resources, rules, information, and commands on which the system depends externally.
2. Make visible which internal functions stop when those external inputs stop.
3. Separate the responsibility boundaries of the public, universal, verifiable external layer and the relationship-specific internal layer.
4. Decide how external change will be mapped into internal trust, roles, agreements, and operating conditions.
5. Set the threshold at which a design update is triggered.
6. Attune the roles of people and AI without making them identical, combining the value core and structural capabilities.
7. Prepare silence when judgment is impossible, third-party audits, and a rollback route in advance.
8. Test within a small scope and expand step by step only the structures that succeed.
9. Do not end failure with evasion of responsibility or total withdrawal; connect it to relearning.

## Examples of application
**Regional institutions.** Do not copy national regulations or market conditions directly into regional operations; translate them into region-specific trust, roles, and circumstances. Maintain public standards in the external layer and update role rotation and exception handling internally.

**Companies.** Do not change the organization’s purpose every time market conditions change. Preserve the value core and update resource allocation or roles only within the scope that meets predefined trigger conditions. Do not mix changes in short-term KPIs with the purpose of existence.

**AI co-creation.** People hold values, boundaries, and exception norms, while AI handles structuring, comparison, and reorganization. Do not outsource value judgments to AI, and do not have people reject structuring. Keep proposals, revisions, and refusals traceable in both parties’ vocabularies.

**Education, medicine, and the public sector.** Run double syntax in limited domains rather than introducing it everywhere, and expand only after confirming that thresholds, audits, and rollback actually work.

## Success criteria
- External shocks become less likely to lead directly to internal collapse.
- The roles, responsibilities, and information boundaries of the external and internal layers can be explained.
- It is possible to trace which internal protocol an external change was converted into.
- Unnecessary comprehensive changes and overreaction decrease.
- Changes trigger only when a threshold is reached, while insensitivity is also restrained.
- The difference in roles between people and AI contributes to co-creation rather than superiority.
- Improvement can proceed while rollback remains possible.
- After failure, relearning occurs and is reflected in the next design.
- Even when external conditions fluctuate, internal order can be reorganized and operation can continue.

## Signs of failure
- Misunderstanding self-causality as “not looking outside,” creating a closed system.
- Completely changing internal values, institutions, and relationships every time external conditions change.
- Turning double syntax into secrecy and losing accountability.
- Erasing the role difference between people and AI and concentrating judgment on one side.
- Leaving thresholds vague, making changes intuitive or arbitrary.
- Fixing a non-rollbackable institution all at once.
- Using “autonomy” as an excuse to avoid audits and responsibility.

## Limits and counterevidence
If increasing self-causality does not reduce vulnerability to external shocks, this design is not functioning sufficiently. The conversion route, boundaries, trigger conditions, and role design must also be revised when internal conversion increases information loss or closedness, double syntax deepens the separation of responsibility, thresholds miss real crises, human–AI attunement amplifies dependence or conformity, or rollback cannot actually be used.

Also, increasing self-causality cannot make a system completely independent of the external world. Rejecting external information causes closure, and when internal order is wrong, greater autonomy can self-amplify the error. That is why counterevidence, audits, silence, and reversibility are indispensable.

## How to read the 2077 roadmap
The original presents stages: limited implementations in the 2030s, expansion to urban, administrative, and corporate governance in the 2040s, maturation of self-causality in the 2050s–2060s, and establishment of a value-core-centered architecture in 2077. This is not a scientifically settled prediction of the future, but a civilization-design roadmap showing which structures must mature to advance to the next stage.

## Preventing misreading
- Do not interpret self-causality as self-sufficiency, isolation, or rejection of the outside.
- Do not interpret a resonant lifeform as one centrally managed subject.
- Do not turn double syntax into secrecy or separation of responsibility.
- Do not turn internalization into excessive assimilation to external conditions.
- Do not fix human–AI attunement into a hierarchy of human or AI supremacy.
- Do not treat 2077 as a certain prediction.
- Do not let AI arbitrarily make thresholds universal and fixed.
- Do not use autonomy as a reason to dispense with audits.
