## 15-Second Explanation
Rather than choosing one of two opposing ideas and eliminating the other, this approach uses the “tension” between them as material for generating the next structure. It first aligns the higher-level purpose, creates room for reconsideration through SQS, places a conversion layer between different vocabularies, and decides conditions for returning after failure before trying on a small scale.

## Why It Is Needed
There are structures in which both sides are necessary yet conflict with each other, such as money and connection, subjectivity and objectivity, or creation and inheritance. If one argues only over “which is correct,” each choice loses another necessary condition, increasing conflict and exhaustion.

The Parent Original shows a method that neither leaves contradiction unattended nor dilutes it through compromise: observe the tension produced by different structures and regroup it toward a higher-level purpose.

## Central Structure
```text
Different structures collide
→ Exhaustion through either/or
→ Agree on the higher-level axis of meaning first
→ Place who is protecting what
→ Stop immediate reactions with SQS
→ Provisionally place a conversion layer between different languages
→ Decide rollback conditions before failure
→ Try on a small scale
→ Record with structural logs and an audit summary
→ Turn tension into the generative force of the next structure
```

## Practical Process
1. **Set the axis of meaning first.** Agree on one top priority before discussing means.
2. **Place the structures.** Make issues and interests visible through proximity/opposition, and check who is protecting what.
3. **Insert SQS.** Use questions and silence to move from reflexive response to reconsideration.
4. **Provisionally place a conversion layer.** Make different value languages mutually readable for the time being; do not make it a fixed truth.
5. **Decide reversible conditions.** Document in advance when, how far, and how to return.
6. **Implement on a small scale.** Test within a verifiable range without making it irreversible all at once.
7. **Keep logs.** Record subject, time, arrangement, intervention, and reversible conditions.
8. **Look at counterexamples.** Do not hide situations where it did not work; revise the scope of application.

## Application Examples
**Money × connection.** Do not treat either alone as value; provisionally use double-entry accounting and observe the value of connection through multiple axes such as density, continuity, and reversibility.

**Subjectivity × objectivity.** Do not discard subjectivity; use the objective viewpoint as a place of retreat so that the person can disclose subjectivity only to the necessary extent.

**Creation × inheritance.** Do not disclose all proprietary techniques; share the safe parts—principles, procedures, and verification—and raise only what can be reproduced into an inheritable form.

## Success Criteria
- Discussion can return to the higher-level purpose shared before the conflict.
- A person’s preference increases without coercion.
- Reconsideration, not only reaction, occurs after SQS.
- A conversion hypothesis between different languages can be tested.
- It is genuinely possible to return after failure.
- Causality, relations, and conditions for renewed agreement can be traced later.
- Conditions under which it did not work are recorded.

## Limitations
Keeping contradiction is not valuable in itself. Without bundling toward a higher-level purpose, verification, and reversibility, it becomes merely prolonged conflict. Also, using SQS or arrangement to hide another person’s options falls outside the Parent Original’s public-safety edition.

## Preventing Misreading
- This does not end with relativism of “both are correct.”
- It is not a theory of leaving conflict unattended.
- SQS is not a technique for forcing silence.
- A conversion formula is a provisional hypothesis, not a fixed KPI.
- Rollback capability is not abandonment of reform, but a condition for safe experimentation.
- Preserve the boundary between the public-safety edition and proprietary know-how.
