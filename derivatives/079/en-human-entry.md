## 15-Second Explanation
Social institutions do not shift merely because a problem has grown larger. A limited transition can be considered when a potential critical point that exceeds the self-repair capacity of existing institutions overlaps with a policy window in which alternative vocabulary, responsible actors, places for trials, and legal room are available. The 12 signs are observation items for reading that overlap; they are not a 12-point maturity score or a prediction of the future.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
AI-driven job replacement, changes in income pathways, social-security burdens, and faster decision-making form interconnected chains. Looking only at a single crisis can confuse a temporary fluctuation with structural change, while simply presenting alternatives risks forcing an institution into place without a social basis to receive it.

Therefore, record ordinary-time friction as a baseline, and check how much multiple signs appearing in technology, culture, and institutions overlap, how long they persist, and what counterexamples exist. At the same time, separately check whether alternative vocabulary, responsible actors, limited trials, legal room, objections, remedies, and withdrawal are present.

## Practical Process
### 1. Create a baseline for ordinary friction
Record institutional exceptions, avoidance behavior, adjustment costs, correction time, and routes to remedy from ordinary times onward.

### 2. Observe the 12 signs by domain
Divide them into technology and infrastructure, society and culture, and institutions and governance. Look not at the count, but at simultaneity, persistence, interaction, and counterexamples.

### 3. Evaluate potential critical points
Check whether friction across multiple domains can recover through adjustment of existing institutions. Do not decide based on one person’s forecast or a single numerical value.

### 4. Confirm the policy window
Check whether alternative vocabulary, responsible actors, trial locations, legal room, objections, remedies, withdrawal, and auditing are all in place.

### 5. Conduct reversible trials
Set the target, period, responsibility, stopping conditions, remedies, and rollback in advance, and expand only the range that has succeeded after renewed agreement.

## Examples of Application
**Municipalities.** Check not only participation rates, but also whether preservation of dissent, access for vulnerable people, withdrawal, renewed agreement, and remedies can be used.

**AI-driven job replacement.** Track not only the number of jobs, but also income, responsibility for decisions, retraining, social security, and the burden on public services as a causal chain.

**Education.** Handle the operating speed of majority rule in parallel with a legitimacy audit that records minority opinions, grounds, burdens, and conditions for reconsideration.

## Judging Success
A large number of the 12 signs appearing is not itself success. It is necessary to confirm that signs from different domains overlap persistently and that their relationship with institutional friction can be verified even after including counterexamples and regional differences.

A limited trial is healthy only when objections, cooling-off, renewed agreement, stopping, remedies, and rollback can actually be used. Even when a trial is stopped or withdrawn, if harm was contained and the hypothesis could be updated, the verification function has worked.

## Limitations
The signs do not determine the future. They are affected by the observation range, missing data, policy bias, and regional differences. A policy window can also fall into post hoc explanation, so its opening and closing conditions and failure cases must be recorded in advance.

## Preventing Misreading
- Do not turn the 12 signs into a 12-point maturity score.
- Do not treat the year as a fixed deadline.
- Do not conclude that the arrival of Connection Civilization is certain merely from AI adoption.
- Do not suspend rights or opposing views on the grounds of a critical point.
- Do not use a policy window to make emergency powers permanent.
- Do not repurpose this as a definite prediction of investment, politics, or organizational control.
