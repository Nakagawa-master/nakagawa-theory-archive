## 15-Second Explanation

If the problem of AI output is considered only as “whose is it?”, the starting point of the question that initiated the output disappears. The parent places at the foundation of AI ethics a record of whose question moved AI, what structure that question passed through and how it was transformed and expanded, preserved as a question signature, responsibility allocation, and audit bundle.

## Why This Question Is Necessary

When AI-generated work is viewed only through ownership or authorship, the “question” that existed before the output becomes difficult to see. Yet even with the same AI, the causal provenance of the generated work differs according to who asked what, from which context the input came, and where AI transformed or expanded it.

The parent is concerned not with ownership judgments themselves, but with the evaporation of origin and causality that precedes them. Once the starting point of the question disappears, it becomes impossible to see whose thinking initiated the structure, where AI acted, or where responsibility should be reconfirmed. The question signature is the device that prevents this.

## The Original Causal Line

Treating AI output only as a matter of ownership

## What Is a Question Signature?

A question signature is not a mechanism that automatically decides “whose property this output is.” It identifies whose question moved which structure, so that the output’s causal starting point is not lost.

Therefore, converting a question signature into an automatic authorship judgment or a claim to exclusive rights departs from the parent. What matters is making the starting point before the output available for reference again, so that causal memory remains through summarisation, reuse, and distribution.

## Causal Provenance and Responsibility Allocation

Treat the path from the question through AI transformation and expansion to the output as one causal line. Whether AI simply responds, rephrases the question, expands the structure, or adds new connections, its action is made visible as part of the causal route.

Responsibility allocation prevents this involvement from becoming ambiguous. It does not turn AI contribution directly into AI’s legal responsibility, nor does it always fix humans as sole responsible parties. The focus is to make visible who participated in which origin, transformation, and use, preventing responsibility from evaporating into a state of “nobody’s question.”

## Unsigned Structures and the Loss of the Correspondence Waveform

When the origin signature is lost, the work may circulate, but the route back to the question, causality, and responsibility behind it disappears. The parent understands this as an unsigned structure and connects it to the loss of the correspondence waveform.

The purpose of retaining a signature is not to monopolise ownership. It is to make it possible to trace whose question moved which structure later, and to allow reusers to connect without erasing origin and causality.

## Audit Bundles and Observation Windows

The question signature and responsibility allocation connect to an audit bundle rather than existing as isolated labels. Through audits and renewed agreement, the provenance of origin, causality, and responsibility is continuously reconfirmed.

If an AI response has a sustained external effect on a particular entity’s market or credit, that effect is also returned to the observation window for renewed audit. This does not establish general external-impact KPIs or credit scores; it is a revision boundary for checking whether an ethical design based on the origin signature withstands real-world effects.

## Conditions for Refutation and Revision

If question signatures or responsibility allocation are ignored in practice and ethical judgments belonging to “nobody’s question” become normal, the mechanism is not fulfilling its expected role. Also, if an AI response continues to one-sidedly erode one entity’s market or credit in the observation window and does not improve through signature, audit, and renewed agreement, the hypothesis of origin-signature-based ethical maintenance itself becomes subject to revision.

The parent provides no concrete AI-contribution rate, human-responsibility rate, or credit-impact threshold, so do not invent numbers and substitute them for refutation conditions.

## To Avoid Misreading

This is not an automatic system for determining ownership, authorship, or legal responsibility. Do not convert the question signature into evidence of exclusive rights, AI contribution into AI’s legal responsibility, or add out-of-scope KPIs such as human approval rate, AI contribution rate, or credit-allocation rate.

Do not turn “protecting the origin” into enclosure that rejects reuse or criticism. The purpose is to prevent origin evaporation and create a state in which connection and distribution remain possible while causal memory is preserved.
