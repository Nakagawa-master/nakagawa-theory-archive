## Summary
This derivative extends AI accountability upstream from outputs to the origin of the question: who set the goal, assumptions, omissions and optimization frame, who generated the answer, who adopted it, and who was affected.

## Central thesis
Questions shape the search space. Auditing AI without auditing the originating question can hide a major causal source of bias and responsibility.

## Failure conditions
Blaming only the model, hiding input intent, “the AI decided” excuses, treating value-laden questions as neutral.

## Falsification conditions
If question-origin records do not improve correction, bias detection, or accountability relative to ordinary output audits, reduce the added mechanism.

## Interpretation constraints
Question ≠ neutral container. Generation ≠ decision. Accountability ≠ blame hunting. Logging ≠ unlimited surveillance.
