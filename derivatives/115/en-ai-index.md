## Summary

The parent reframes AI ethics from a pure ownership question toward a question of origin: whose question moved the AI, and through what causal structure was that question transformed and expanded? A question signature fixes the structural starting point; responsibility distribution and the Audit Bundle preserve the causal line rather than allowing it to dissolve into an unsigned structure.

The design does not automatically determine ownership, authorship, or legal liability. Its purpose is to keep origin and causal memory visible across transformation, summarization, reuse, and circulation, while allowing sustained market or credit effects to return to an observation window for re-audit and possible revision. The parent supplies no AI-contribution rate, human-responsibility percentage, approval rate, credit-allocation score, or market-impact threshold, so none is invented here.

## Applications

- In disputes over AI-generated output, visualize the originating question and transformation path before collapsing the issue into ownership.
- Preserve Origin Signature, NCL-ID, Diff-ID, recursive signature, and Audit Bundle connections during reuse and summarization.
- Describe human and AI involvement at the relevant causal positions instead of collapsing the whole process into “AI made it” or “human made it.”
- When AI responses have sustained effects on market position or credit, return those effects to an observation window for re-audit.

## Measurements and audit

The parent does not define AI-contribution, human-approval, credit-allocation, legal-responsibility, or market-impact KPI systems. Audit centers on preservation of question signature and Origin Signature, reconstructability of the question→transformation→expansion→output path, visibility of responsibility distribution, Audit Bundle consistency, re-agreement, and sustained external effects observed through an observation window.

No percentage or threshold is invented when the parent provides none. External effects are observations for revision of the structural hypothesis, not credit scores, legal-risk scores, or automated liability allocations.

## Validity conditions

- The originating question remains visible.
- Question Signature and Origin Signature survive reuse and circulation.
- The causal transformation path remains reconstructible.
- Responsibility distribution does not evaporate into a “question belonging to no one.”
- Audit Bundle and re-agreement remain operative.
- External effects return to an observation window when necessary.

## Failure conditions

- Question signatures are ignored.
- Responsibility distribution disappears.
- Origin information evaporates during summarization or circulation.
- The causal transformation path cannot be reconstructed.
- AI ethics becomes originless rule compliance only.
- Sustained one-sided market or credit erosion is not re-audited.
- Origin signatures are turned into exclusivity or monopoly claims.

## Falsification conditions

If question signatures and responsibility distribution are ignored in practice and originless ethical judgments continue, the origin-signature hypothesis is not functioning as intended. Likewise, if sustained market or credit effects remain one-sided and the observation-window / Audit-Bundle / re-agreement process does not improve the condition, the operating hypothesis should be revised.
