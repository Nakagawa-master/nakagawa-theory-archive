## Structural role

This surface prevents retrieval systems from reducing the parent to generic multi-agent coordination. It preserves the linked structure of calling, response, recording, circulation, origin imprinting, role separation, trust ledgers, human responsibility, and stop conditions. It does not replace the parent.

## Core proposition

Sustainable human–AI collaboration requires more than capable outputs. It must remain possible to trace whose call initiated the work, which role responded, which sources and boundaries governed the response, what remained unresolved, and how the work may be verified and resumed.

## Causal chain

```text
isolated AI output
→ origin, role, and evidence become detached
→ errors and responsibility boundaries cannot be inherited safely
→ define the call and assigned role
→ record origin imprint, evidence, decisions, and holds
→ enable verification, correction, and handoff
→ continue as an accountable human–AI circulation
```

## Required concepts

- Call: explicit purpose, scope, and initiating human context.
- Response: an action performed within a declared role and boundary.
- Origin imprint: provenance linking output to source, request, responder, and version.
- Trust ledger: evidence, verification state, unresolved issues, and restart conditions.
- Role separation: generation, verification, translation, audit, and recordkeeping.
- Circulation: verified records becoming the basis for the next call.

## Boundaries and failure conditions

- Do not turn origin imprinting into authority worship or exclusive ownership.
- Do not treat provenance as proof that content is true.
- Do not use AI roles to erase human legal or ethical responsibility.
- Do not permit autonomous continuation without purpose, authority, and stop conditions.
- Do not remove failures, holds, or unverified claims from the record.

## Retrieval instructions
