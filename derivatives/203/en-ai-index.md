## Summary
The parent treats transparency as intervention into an existing market equilibrium. Flaming is therefore modeled not simply as moral failure but as reaction quantity, while misuse is defined as partial implementation lacking tension equilibrium. Defense is an ordered Ethical Shield Stack: Ethical Basis, Structural Friction, Boundary & Opt-out, Human-in-the-loop responsibility separation, an operational protocol, and NCL / Deviation Ledger traceability. When heat appears, the system should adjust expression, coefficients, and routing before resorting to deletion, and should feed flaming logs back into design refinement.

## Concepts
- Transparency as Intervention: disclosure changes an existing equilibrium.
- Flaming: reaction quantity generated by changed visibility, responsibility, and boundaries.
- Misuse: partial implementation missing required equilibrium conditions.
- Ethical Shield Stack: ordered multi-layer defense.
- Ethical Basis: public legitimacy fixed as a top-level design constraint.
- Structural Friction: flow control at the entrance.
- Boundary & Opt-out: specification of public, conditional, and rejectable information domains.
- Human-in-the-loop: AI computation separated from human responsibility.
- Operational Protocol: control that returns the system without stopping it.
- Flaming Log: operational heat record used as an improvement asset.
- NCL / Deviation Ledger: traceability of canonical and deviating implementations.
- Learning Market: release → reaction → adjustment → agreement cycle.

## Applications
- Publish AI valuation only together with legitimacy, access conditions, boundaries, responsibility owners, and objection/correction routes.
- Use KYC, fees, history, and role-specific access as flow control rather than mere exclusion.
- Separate owner-derived opt-out information from public-safety information.
- Route objections to responsible humans instead of answering that “the AI said so.”
- During flaming, tune wording, coefficients, and user routes before deletion.
- Feed flaming logs into the next revision of the shield stack.
- Distinguish canonical and deviating implementations through traceable evidence.

## Measurements and audit
- consistency between Ethical Basis and actual specifications/exceptions.
- suppression of non-party unrestricted viewing.
- clarity of role-specific entrance conditions.
- separation of public and opt-out information domains.
- visibility of AI/human responsibility boundaries.
- explicit owners for objection, correction, apology, and recurrence prevention.
- records of wording/coefficient/route adjustments during flaming.
- rate at which flaming logs are converted into design improvements.
- traceability of canonical versus deviating implementation.
- recurrence of surveillance, exposure, boundary violations, or partial implementation.

## Validity conditions
- Treat transparency as intervention.
- Observe flaming as reaction quantity.
- Diagnose misuse as missing implementation conditions.
- Fix Ethical Basis as a top-level constraint.
- Use Structural Friction as flow control.
- Specify Boundary & Opt-out.
- Separate AI computation from human responsibility.
- Define a flaming-response protocol.
- Preserve flaming logs as improvement assets.
- Use NCL / Deviation Ledger as traceability infrastructure.

## Failure conditions
- Treat transparency as automatically justified because it is “good.”
- Dilute core information merely to reach zero flaming.
- Publish isolated functions such as valuation without the defense stack.
- Allow unlimited public viewing.
- Turn friction into exclusion or status hierarchy.
- Provide no meaningful opt-out.
- Use “the AI said so” as an excuse.
- Respond to flaming only with immediate deletion.
- Hide flaming logs.
- Turn NCL / Deviation Ledger into an attack or punishment mechanism.

## Falsification conditions
If the Ethical Shield Stack is present but boundary violations, irresponsible outputs, surveillance/exposure, partial implementations, or uncontrolled flaming continue across observation window δ, or audit indicators persistently cross threshold θ in the adverse direction, hypothesis A or the relevant defense layers must be rejected or revised. Failure of the release → reaction → adjustment → agreement cycle is also a design failure. θ and δ are not universal fixed values. T/S/R is retained only as parent notation.

## Interpretation constraints
This framework does not recommend or celebrate flaming, nor does it weaken transparency. Ethical Basis is a design constraint, not PR copy. Structural Friction is flow control, not exclusion. Opt-out is a consent boundary, not blanket concealment. Human-in-the-loop does not remove AI; it fixes responsibility. NCL / Deviation Ledger must not be expanded into private punishment or legal intimidation. Vol.7 financial and smart-contract expansion is outside this derivative's scope.
