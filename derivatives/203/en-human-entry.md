## 15-Second Explanation
Transparency is not an act of making the market quiet; because it intervenes in existing reassurance, profit, and concealment, it inevitably creates friction. Vol.6 treats backlash not as evil to erase but as “amount of resistance,” and checks misuse not as “malice” but as a partial implementation lacking tension equilibrium. By layering Ethical Basis, entry friction, Boundary and Opt-out, human responsibility, operating protocols, and the NCL / Deviation Ledger into an Ethical Shield Stack, it returns a market that has caught fire to adjustment and learning without stopping it.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Making something transparent alone does not make a market safe. Partial implementations—publishing only appraisals, allowing anyone to view freely, making responsibility for AI output ambiguous, having no Opt-out, or immediately deleting something once backlash occurs—can make transparency itself look like attack or surveillance.

Because backlash can also be a side effect of “effective transparency,” thinning core information to aim for zero returns the market to its original opacity. What is needed is to identify the heat source and design flow, boundaries, responsibility, and operations.

## Practical Process
1. Treat transparency as an intervention in equilibrium, not as goodness.
2. Observe backlash as an amount of resistance and separate whose resistance it is and to what.
3. Check misuse as a missing necessary condition, skipped sequence, or partial implementation.
4. Fix Ethical Basis as the highest-level Design Constraint, not PR.
5. Place Structural Friction through KYC, payment, history, role-based entrances, and so on.
6. Fix publishable areas and rejectable/withdrawable areas through Boundary & Opt-out.
7. AI handles calculation, presentation, and primary responses; humans handle final judgments, signatures, corrections, and objections.
8. During backlash, adjust wording, coefficients, and pathways; deletion comes last.
9. Do not hide backlash logs as shame; return them to the next defense-stack update.
10. Make standard and deviating implementations traceable through the NCL / Deviation Ledger.
11. Run the cycle of publish → resistance → adjustment → agreement, and revise through Condition Z / T-S-R / θ / δ / Phenomenon M.

## Examples of Application
**Publishing appraisals.** Do not output only appraisal values; design the reason for existence such as disaster prevention and asset protection, viewing conditions, rejectable areas, responsible actors, and an objection pathway at the same time.

**Viewing control.** Do not make it viewable by anyone; change entrance conditions according to role and status as a party. However, treat this as flow control, not as creating a closed market.

**Owner’s refusal.** Give personal-origin information areas where Opt-out is possible, and do not treat them the same as public-safety information.

**Objection to AI.** Do not answer criticism of AI output with “AI said so”; the responsible human assumes reasons, correction, and recurrence prevention.

**Backlash response.** Record which wording, number, boundary, or pathway is the heat source, and adjust wording, coefficients, and pathways. Do not end with deletion alone.

## Success Criteria
- The reason for publication is reflected in the specification as an upper-level constraint.
- There is role-based entry friction rather than free viewing.
- Publishable areas and Opt-out areas are separated.
- The responsibility boundary between AI and humans is visible.
- The actors for objections, corrections, apologies, and recurrence prevention are clear.
- Backlash can be returned to adjustment rather than immediate deletion.
- Backlash logs remain as improvement data.
- Standard and deviating implementations can be distinguished by records.
- The cycle of publish → resistance → adjustment → agreement can be maintained without weakening transparency.

## Limitations
The original handles risk defense and dynamic operation of transparency. It does not guarantee zero backlash, nor does it mean that legal responsibility or individual dispute handling can be replaced by the NCL / Deviation Ledger. The connection to finance, large capital, and smart contracts handled in Vol.7 is not anticipated here.

## Preventing Misreading
- This is not a theory that welcomes or incites backlash.
- This defense does not weaken transparency; it makes transparency sustainable.
- Ethical Basis is not a PR copy.
- Structural Friction is flow control, not exclusion or a status system.
- Opt-out is not freedom to conceal, but a device for boundaries and consent.
- Human-in-the-loop does not mean stopping AI use; it fixes the arrow of responsibility to humans.
- Do not use the NCL / Deviation Ledger for attacks or private sanctions.
