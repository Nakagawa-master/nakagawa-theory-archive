## 15-Second Explanation
Even if AI is introduced, documents, FAQs, strategy documents, and knowledge increase, and specialized work in each department accelerates, if there is a blank in “linked responsibility” that connects business value, customer judgment, sales, marketing, public relations, product, management, information assets, and AI reference systems to the same conditions of viability, locally correct deliverables remain misaligned while being formalized as authorized knowledge. AKI-014 addresses the bad causality in which humans and AI refer back to that misalignment, the business OS reproduces the contamination itself, and eventually the company can no longer explain its own business.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
In modern companies, AI use, DX, expert use, knowledge management, and cross-department projects advance at the same time. These are originally effective, but the more deliverables increase, the easier it becomes to confuse “being well organized” with “the business being viable.”

Sales materials are correct as sales. Marketing measures are sound as marketing. The public-relations context is orderly as public relations. AI generates natural prose and classifications from the premises it is given. However, if there is no agent who takes responsibility for whether these lie on the same causal line as the conditions under which customers actually make decisions, product value, field implementation, and management conception, the quality of individual pieces does not guarantee overall consistency.

What is even more dangerous is that once a deliverable is created, it becomes official internal information. When misalignments are stored in the internal wiki, CRM, sales templates, FAQs, brand guides, AI search infrastructure, prompts, meeting minutes, and strategy materials, subsequent humans and AI reuse them as “confirmed premises.” The error is then not a one-off, but becomes self-reproducing business OS contamination.

## Practical Process
**1. Write the conditions of business viability first.** Before department-specific deliverables, define what generates customer judgment and which value connects to product, sales, marketing, public relations, operations, and management.

**2. Fix linked responsibility.** When the judgments of departments collide, make explicit who reconnects them to the conditions of viability for the business as a whole. Fix the location of repair responsibility, not merely the existence of a meeting structure.

**3. Trace the premises of deliverables.** Make it possible to work backward from AI-generated and expert deliverables to the customer understanding, primary information, past judgments, and product conditions they presupposed.

**4. Audit before formalizing knowledge.** Before putting something into the wiki, CRM, FAQ, search infrastructure, or AI reference information, confirm that it is connected to the conditions and meaning that make the business viable as a whole.

**5. Do not end a difference with local correction.** Once one document is fixed, trace the derivative destinations, including sales materials, AI prompts, FAQs, internal knowledge, and management materials that reference the same premises.

**6. Do not confuse AI speed with results.** Even if the number of generations, processing time, and document quality improve, speed is not a success indicator if customer judgment or the business’s ability to explain itself has not improved.

**7. Periodically conduct self-explanation tests.** Check whether a third party from another department can reproduce on the same causal line “why this product is sold to this customer in these words,” “who can change the premises,” and “what should be fixed to improve the whole.”

## Application Examples
**AI knowledge infrastructure.** Even if a large volume of departmental documents is loaded for search and summarization, if the premises among documents contradict one another, AI will smoothly present the contradictions again rather than integrate them. A linked audit before ingestion and reverse links from answers back to sources are necessary.

**Sales and marketing coordination.** If the customer image defined by marketing differs from the reasons for judgment observed by sales in the field, and AI integrates both as the same “customer understanding,” the more polished the proposal materials, the more firmly they fix the misalignment. Someone must be responsible for updating the authorized definition of customer judgment.

**Management strategy.** Even if AI integrates information from multiple departments and produces a strategy proposal, if the conditions of viability of the source information are not connected to one another, integration is not structural understanding. A person responsible for linkage who can reproduce the overall causality is necessary.

## Success Criteria
Success is not judged by “the number of AI uses increased,” “generation time became shorter,” or “the wording of documents became standardized.” These can also increase the speed at which misaligned premises are reproduced.

Success can be judged when the same conditions of viability can be traced from business value through customer judgment, each department, information assets, and AI reference systems; premise changes propagate to derivative destinations; a third party can reproduce the same causal line; and the organization itself can explain “why this business is viable.”

If AI generation speed increases while self-explanation ability falls, it is failure; if generation speed falls while the organization recovers self-recognition by cutting off an erroneous bundle of references, it is improvement. Preserve this reversal-based evaluation.

## Limitations
The parent original does not reject the use of AI or experts itself. Where linked responsibility is established and premises, grounds, differences, and derivative destinations can be traced, both AI and experts can strengthen the business OS.

Nor is every local optimization bad. Excessively centralizing areas that each department may properly process independently would harm decision speed and expertise. What is needed is to clarify only the connection points that affect the conditions of business viability.

Do not invent an AI maturity score, contamination rate, danger probability, or fixed threshold absent from the original in this public reading. Even when quantifying, do not cross the boundaries of meaning in expressions that exist in the original.

## Misreading Prevention
Do not turn it into the simplistic anti-AI argument that “AI destroys companies.” The bad causality in the original is a structure in which a blank in linked responsibility comes first, and AI formalization normalizes and recurs that misalignment.

Do not turn it into the argument that “experts are bad.” The point is that even if experts are correct in their own domains, locally correct answers can collide when overall linkage is blank.

Do not reduce it to the generalities “increase cross-department meetings” or “create a common format.” Linked responsibility is not a meeting or a format; it is responsibility for where and by whom the conditions of business viability are connected and repaired.

Do not equate “structure” with diagrams, organization, or frameworks. In the original, structure is a bundle of causality that causes events to occur, persist, and repeat.
