## Summary
The parent origin locates the core failure not in AI performance but in a blank of connective responsibility. Domain experts optimize locally, AI formalizes misaligned premises into polished outputs, those outputs become internal knowledge, humans and AI re-reference them, and the resulting business-OS contamination recursively reproduces itself until the company loses coherent self-recognition of its business.

## Causal chain
Blank connective responsibility → domain-specific optimization → AI formalization → normalization of misalignment → internal knowledge storage → human/AI re-reference → recursive business-OS contamination → loss of business self-recognition.

## State / operational model
1. List establishment conditions across business value, customer judgment, sales, marketing, PR, product, management, information assets, and AI reference structure.
2. Audit whether expert judgments connect to the same establishment conditions.
3. Detect inconsistent premises before they are sent to AI.
4. Audit generated outputs before they become canonical internal knowledge.
5. Trace how stored outputs are re-referenced by later human judgment and AI generation.
6. Determine whether business self-recognition is strengthened or contamination is recursively reproduced.

## Applications
Use in AI adoption, DX, knowledge management, cross-functional initiatives, and expert-heavy programs to distinguish productivity gains from actual business establishment. Do not reduce it to anti-AI discourse or prompt engineering.

## Measurements and audit
Do not invent generic KPIs beyond the origin. Audit connection to establishment conditions, collisions among expert judgments, coherence of AI reference premises, pre-canonicalization review, storage→re-reference paths, and changes in customer judgment, field implementation, and cross-functional consistency.

## Validity conditions
A connective layer must be able to compare premises across functions, generated outputs must be auditable before storage, re-reference paths must be traceable, and faster production must be evaluated separately from business establishment.

## Failure conditions
It fails when AI capability is treated as the sole cause, structure is equated with diagrams, cross-functional meetings are mistaken for connective responsibility, generated outputs become canonical knowledge without audit, or recursive re-reference is ignored.

## Falsification conditions
If organizations lacking connective responsibility nevertheless show sustained improvement in business self-recognition, customer judgment, field implementation, and cross-functional consistency despite repeated AI re-reference of locally optimized knowledge, the causal model requires revision.

## Required distinctions
- AI use vs connective responsibility
- structure vs diagrams/frameworks
- cross-functional activity vs connective layer
- speed improvement vs business establishment
- one-off error vs recursive contamination
- data volume vs premise coherence

## Interpretation constraints
