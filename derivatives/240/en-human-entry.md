## 15-Second Explanation
**The external article came first.** When writing that article, Nakagawa Master selected the value core of “turning orders that cannot be judged into issues” from the public facts about HAP “Kaidoc” and made it the article’s central axis. The later Parent is a Nakagawa structural reading that publicly disassembles the reason for that selection and the article’s composition.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Writing only “development consultation” or “requirements-definition support” cannot explain why ordering stops even when a consultation partner exists. In the preceding external article, Nakagawa Master read that what the client lacks is not only technical knowledge, but also units of decision, undecided matters, and comparison axes. Therefore, **issue formation** was made the value core, above “specification generation.”

The later Parent is not a newly planned external article. It looks back on a structural translation already carried out and publicly explains why this core was selected and why decision-making structure, rather than surface functions, was made the main axis.

## Practical Process
Separate purpose and means → turn the user, scope, budget, schedule, quality, maintenance, and responsibility into issues → separate decided / undecided / hypothesis / constraint → place proposals on common comparison axes → retain differences in premises and reasons for acceptance or rejection → proceed to agreement or additional investigation → revalidate premises after implementation.

## Application Example
AI introduction, new systems, core-system renewal, and proposal comparison. The form should let the ordering party retain reasons for judgment, rather than having an expert fill in the answers.

## Success Criterion
The party can explain what must be decided, does not hide undecided matters, can compare premise differences among multiple proposals, retains reasons for acceptance or rejection, and moves judgment forward.

## Limitations
If the main causes are absence of budget, authority, institutions, or legal constraints, issue formation alone cannot solve them. It does not guarantee an optimal specification or the lowest price.

## Misreading Prevention
Separate HAP’s official explanation from Nakagawa Master’s reading. The sequence is **preceding external article → later Parent’s public disassembly**, not Parent → external article.
