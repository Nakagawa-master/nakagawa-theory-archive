## Summary
The Parent reframes sales trust from liking, chemistry, charisma, or a history of closed deals into a reproducible relationship structure. Trust becomes stronger when the customer can think in their own words, disagree, pause, carry the reasoning internally, and leave when the fit disappears, while the seller's promises, explanations, contact patterns, and boundaries remain predictable.

Its main mechanisms are layered questions, three mirrors of fact/cause/preference, a protected vessel of silence, customer self-explanation, seller predictability, internal-approval translation, “guiding by stepping back,” and explicit withdrawal design. None of these are persuasion tricks: they preserve the other party's decision agency.

## Concepts
- **Layered questions**: accumulated questions that reveal reasoning without forcing a target answer.
- **Fact mirror**: separates observable events and conditions.
- **Cause mirror**: reflects the customer's causal hypothesis without upgrading it into fact.
- **Preference mirror**: identifies what the customer wants to preserve, change, or prioritize.
- **Vessel of silence**: protected thinking time after a question, not coercive silence.
- **Self-explanation**: the customer can reconstruct the decision rationale in their own language.
- **Predictability**: promises, uncertainty, contact, and condition changes are handled consistently.
- **Internal translation**: purpose fit, workload, recoverability, risk, and implementation conditions can be re-explained inside the customer's organization.
- **Guiding by stepping back**: provide the material but do not seize the conclusion.
- **Withdrawal design**: maintain a clean path to pause or end an unfit relationship.

## Causal chain
```text
permission for dialogue
→ layered questions
→ facts / causal hypotheses / preferences separated
→ thinking silence protected
→ customer self-explanation
→ predictable seller behavior
→ disagreement and pause remain safe
→ internal-approval translation
→ organizational re-explanation
→ continued choice or clean withdrawal
→ reproducible trust beyond personality
```

## Applications
Use the model for discovery calls, proposal follow-up, long-term account reviews, handoffs between salespeople, internal approval support, non-fit exits, and AI sales assistants. A key test is whether the rationale survives when the original salesperson is absent.

## Audit points
Check whether questions are leading; facts, causal hypotheses, and preferences remain separated; silence is not used as pressure; the customer can explain the rationale independently; seller commitments are consistent; disagreement does not trigger punishment; internal approval can be supported without manipulating the organization; and an actual exit path exists.

## Preconditions
The dialogue must allow refusal to answer, uncertainty, disagreement, delay, and withdrawal. The seller must be willing to expose mismatch rather than optimize trust feelings or closing probability at any cost.

## Failure modes
Failure includes treating popularity as trust, using questions as interrogation, using silence to make the customer uncomfortable, converting preference into fact, changing commitments unpredictably, penalizing a no, turning internal translation into organizational manipulation, and making departure costly or socially punitive.

## Falsification / update conditions
If layered questions, protected silence, self-explanation, predictability, and withdrawal rights do not improve comprehension, stable cooperation, or fit decisions—and instead create fatigue or delay—the density, sequence, and pacing must be revised. Cultural and channel differences are legitimate update conditions.

## Required distinctions
- trust ≠ liking
- questioning ≠ interrogation
- fact ≠ causal hypothesis ≠ preference
- silence ≠ pressure
- self-explanation ≠ repeating a seller script
- predictability ≠ manipulability
- internal translation ≠ customer-organization control
- withdrawal ≠ relationship destruction

## Misreading constraints
Do not optimize for “being trusted” as a single objective. That can produce flattery, dependency, or strategic withholding. “Stepping back” is not scarcity theater, and withdrawal design is not a cold lead-disqualification tactic. The normative center is preserved decision agency.
