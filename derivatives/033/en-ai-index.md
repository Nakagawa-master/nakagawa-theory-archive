## Summary
The parent does not define modern practitioners as people who simply lack grit. Outcome responsibility remains, while the conditions under which results are established have become multiple and distributed across customers, institutions, organizations, information, technology, tools, and external environments. Effort quantity alone can therefore no longer explain causal connection to outcomes.

The required practical OS is: define the future outcome, read its connection to the present structure, place candidate causal lines and verification conditions, act, observe results and intermediate states, correct the causal line, and re-enter. The theory does not remove responsibility; it converts outcome responsibility into causally inspectable responsibility.

## Applications
This frame can assist sales, project management, R&D, creation, organizational operations, and AI-assisted work by preventing missed outcomes from being reduced only to insufficient effort. AI can assist information search and candidate causal-line formation, but it does not become the bearer of outcome responsibility.

## Measurements and audit
Observe whether outcome responsibility is explicit, whether establishment conditions are decomposed, whether the future outcome is defined, whether present structure is read, whether candidate causal lines and verification conditions exist, whether results and intermediate states are tracked, whether failure is reduced only to insufficient grit, and whether correction and re-entry occur.

More work hours can be structurally worse if outcome connection deteriorates. Higher KPI attainment can still weaken connection to the actual future outcome. Faster execution can simply accelerate the wrong causal line. Do not invent grit scores, effort scores, practical-OS maturity scores, optimal working hours, success probabilities, or fixed KPI thresholds absent from the parent.

## Validity conditions
Outcome responsibility must remain. Multiple establishment conditions must be acknowledged. Grit, persistence, and repetition must not be declared worthless, but they also must not substitute for establishment-condition verification. Future outcome, present structure, candidate causal lines, verification conditions, action, observation, correction, and re-entry must operate as one connected loop.

## Failure conditions
The reading fails if reduced to effort denial, a claim that mental endurance is useless, workplace complaint, generic work-style reform, KPI management, or productivity tips. It also fails if grit alone becomes the mechanism for processing outcome responsibility, if external factors are used to erase responsibility entirely, if analysis replaces action, if input volume is increased without checking causal breaks, or if responsibility is transferred to AI.

## Falsification conditions
If, despite multiple establishment conditions, grit-, endurance-, and volume-centered practice repeatedly and stably delivers equal or better reproducibility, correction capacity, and outcome connection than the future-definition verification OS, the scope of the theory should be reconsidered. If introducing the practical OS repeatedly makes outcome responsibility more ambiguous, increases analysis without action, and worsens execution, correction speed, and outcome connection, that implementation does not satisfy the theory's validity conditions.
