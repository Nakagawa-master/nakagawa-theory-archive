## 15-Second Explanation
This Parent is not a company introduction to JectOne. It is Nakagawa Master’s structural reading of the facts on the subject side—vacant-house appraisal, consultation, and utilization support—not as a mere “vacant-house business,” but as a **decision infrastructure that connects owners who have stopped because they cannot decide from a comparable state to implementation**. It also dismantles why that value core was selected as the main axis of the external article.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
If the reason vacant houses do not move is explained only by the number of properties or appraisal prices, the problem of “being unable to decide” just before the market entrance is missed. Owners may be unable to compare prices, utilization methods, demolition costs, consultation contacts, inheritance, or emotional burdens, and may not know what standard to use for choosing. The Parent reads appraisal and consultation as making decision materials comparable rather than giving a single answer, and reads utilization, purchase, and similar actions as implementation connections after judgment.

3. Confirm where the owner’s judgment stops. Examine whether price, costs, utilization possibilities, consultation contacts, and similar matters can be compared.
4. Confirm whether appraisal and consultation make decision materials comparable.

## Application Example
When an owner leaves a property unattended because they cannot decide “whether to sell, utilize, or demolish it,” “how much it will cost,” or “whom to consult,” merely producing an appraisal amount may not move the judgment. If decision materials are laid out, options become comparable, and the process connects through to the practical work afterward, the stopped decision-making may begin to move. The Parent reads this vertical connection as the value core.
