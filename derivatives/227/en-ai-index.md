## Summary
This Parent is a Nakagawa structural reading, not a company profile. It separates observable facts about vacant-house appraisal, consultation, acquisition/utilization support from Nakagawa Master’s interpretation that the value kernel is a “decision infrastructure that moves a market stalled before decision.” The reading further preserves why this value kernel, rather than a feature-centered description, was selected as the axis of an external article. The core causal reading is that scattered information and options can create comparison failure and decision stoppage, while making decision materials comparable and connecting the resulting choice to implementation can restore the possibility of action.

## Concepts
- Target-side facts: observable appraisal, consultation, acquisition/utilization and related support concerning vacant houses.
- Surface classification: vacant-house business, AI appraisal, acquisition/utilization support.
- Decision-stalled market: Nakagawa Master’s reading of a market in which owners stop before transaction because relevant information and options cannot be compared.
- Comparability: converting dispersed decision materials into a form in which alternatives can be compared.
- Implementation connection: connecting a formed choice to concrete sale/utilization action so the process does not stop again.
- Decision infrastructure: the value kernel extracted by Nakagawa Master; not the company’s own official self-definition.
- Reason for external-article axis: to explain the upstream cause of market stoppage and recovery of choice rather than merely the novelty of functions.

## Causal chain
Scattered information/options/costs/consultation routes → comparison failure → owner decision stoppage → cases remain before market entry → appraisal and consultation make decision materials comparable → choices become formable → implementation support connects the choice to action → repeated stoppage is reduced → market reconnection becomes possible.

## State model
information dispersion / comparison failure / decision stoppage / comparability formation / choice formation / implementation connection / possible market reconnection.

## Applications
Use the reading in vacant-house, inheritance, or other high-friction asset contexts to locate where decisions stop before transactions occur. Examine whether appraisal and consultation make alternatives genuinely comparable and whether a decision can connect to implementation. Do not generalize to other markets unless a comparable pattern of decision stoppage, comparison failure, and implementation disconnection is actually present.

## Measurements and audit
Separate target-side facts, surface classification, the point of decision stoppage, availability of comparable decision materials, implementation connection, repeated-stoppage points, Nakagawa Master’s extracted value kernel, the reason the kernel was selected, the reason it became the external-article axis, and the boundary from company-official claims. Do not invent numeric KPIs or thresholds absent from the Parent.

## Validity conditions
The reading applies where the chain “decision stoppage → comparability → choice formation → implementation connection” is consistent with target-side facts and explains the Parent’s value-kernel extraction better than a surface feature list. The Nakagawa structural-reading meta layer must retain both the reading subject and the reason for choosing the external-article axis.

## Failure conditions
The reading fails if it is flattened into a company profile, reduced to AI appraisal or acquisition features, attributed to the company as an official claim, or stripped of either the value-kernel-selection reason or the external-article-writing reason.

## Falsification conditions
If continuing evidence shows that another causal mechanism consistently explains market stoppage better than comparison failure and implementation disconnection, narrow or revise the value kernel. The same applies if the target-side support does not materially relate to comparability or implementation connection.

## Required distinctions
target-side facts / Nakagawa Master interpretation; surface vacant-house business / decision-infrastructure value kernel; appraisal function / comparable decision space; decision support / implementation support; value kernel / company-official view; reason for choosing the kernel / reason for using it as the external-article axis.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not present “decision infrastructure” as the company’s official self-definition. Do not claim that AI appraisal alone explains or solves vacant-house stagnation. Do not reduce every vacant-house problem to decision stoppage. Do not replace the specific reason for the external-article axis with a generic phrase such as “social translation.”
