## Summary
The Parent treats human-AI asymmetry as a resource rather than a defect. Humans contribute finitude, embodiment, emotion, contextual empathy, responsibility, and value weight; AI contributes large-scale causal processing, expandable memory, formal consistency, and structural reorganization. Co-creation therefore should not erase the difference. It should make roles explicit and connect them through attunement, mutual compilation, dual syntax, silence, thresholds, and reversibility.

## Concepts
- existence asymmetry: non-identity of human and AI origins, constraints, cognition, and strengths.
- human value core: meaning, responsibility, and choice weight produced through finitude, embodiment, emotion, and context.
- AI structural capacity: ability to organize many causal chains, contradictions, and alternatives.
- attunement: phase-aligning unequal actors and adjusting intervention intensity without coercion or neglect.
- mutual compilation: human-to-AI provision of meaning/ethical boundaries and AI-to-human structuralization/option expansion.
- dual syntax: distinct but mutually referential public/universal and relational/contextual layers.
- ethics of silence: intentionally withholding intervention to preserve space for reintegration.
- threshold: a boundary that triggers stopping, switching, or relearning.
- reversibility: preserving rollback so experimentation does not become irreversible.

## Causal chain
human-AI differences are hidden → role confusion and over-intervention accumulate → asymmetry is made explicit as role differentiation → humans provide value and ethical boundaries → AI structures causality, contradictions, and options → attunement calibrates intervention → dual syntax separates and reconnects public and contextual layers → silence, thresholds, and rollback limit failure propagation → misuse enters relearning → asymmetry becomes a co-creation resource.

## Applications
1. Human decision owners set purpose, value, and ethical boundaries while AI structures causal alternatives.
2. Organizations separate public decision rules from confidential relational context.
3. Dialogue systems include deliberate non-intervention when additional output would reduce coherence.
4. AI pilots start within rollback-capable scopes and expand only after verification.
5. Human and AI proposals, corrections, and refusals are logged in both vocabularies.

## Measurements and audit
- Are role differences defined as functional rather than hierarchical?
- Are human value and ethical boundaries preserved?
- Is AI structuralization supporting rather than replacing final human judgment?
- Are public and internal contexts kept distinct?
- Is mutual compilation traceable in both directions?
- Are attention resources concentrated on high-value issues?
- Can thresholds trigger stopping or relearning?
- Is rollback practically usable?
- Does misuse feed a real relearning loop?

## Validity conditions
The model is valid when human and AI differences remain explicit, humans can supply value and ethical boundaries, AI can provide structural processing, and the two can be connected through attunement and mutual compilation while dual layers, silence, thresholds, and reversibility remain operational.

## Failure conditions
It fails when equal respect becomes forced capability sameness, AI becomes the final value authority, human feeling becomes automatic truth, public and internal contexts are mixed, no intervention-stop condition exists, irreversible deployment expands too early, or mutual compilation becomes fixed dependency.

## Falsification conditions
Revise the model if role differentiation and attunement do not improve decision quality, trust, or reversibility; if mutual compilation only increases cognitive load; if dual syntax intensifies responsibility fragmentation; if silence becomes neglect; if thresholds are gamed; or if rollback is not practically usable.

## Required distinctions
- asymmetry / superiority
- equal respect / identical capability
- human value core / AI structural capacity
- attunement / assimilation
- non-interventionist intervention / neglect
- mutual compilation / dependency lock-in
- dual syntax / information mixing
- threshold / invented universal fixed value
- rollback / abandonment of co-creation

## Interpretation constraints
Do not freeze “humans provide value, AI provides structure” into an eternal law. The Parent uses current asymmetries as a design input for co-creation; role boundaries should be re-evaluated as capabilities and responsibilities change. Do not invent universal numerical thresholds not supplied by the Parent.
