## Structural summary

Resonance Operating Power is the local capacity to align the structure of self and counterpart, observe words, attitudes, silence, hesitation, and discomfort, and regulate the causal flow of an interaction. UCI is the broader cross-domain structure of influence; Resonance Operating Power is the operational substrate that makes such a broad structure function at concrete points of contact.

```text
structural contact
→ observable response difference
→ detect order, silence, and discomfort
→ make a minimal adjustment
→ local resonance or clarified disagreement
→ record and repeat
→ connect local learning to UCI
```

## Central proposition

A cross-domain architecture such as UCI remains abstract unless it can operate through concrete relationships and contexts. Repeated, recorded, and reversible local resonance provides the bridge from a broad architecture to actual influence.

## Core concepts

- **Resonance:** structural contact that reveals alignment or difference; it is not limited to agreement.
- **Regulation:** adjustment of content, order, conditions, roles, timing, or language without removing the counterpart’s agency.
- **Order principle:** arrangement of information in a causal sequence that can be understood; not a universal fixed script.
- **Silence:** a space for judgment, comparison, emotional processing, and question formation; not automatically consent.
- **Discomfort detection:** use of mismatch signals to form testable hypotheses; not mind-reading.
- **UCI:** a broad integrated structure acting across domains; not an unrestricted right to dominate.

## Operational state model

```yaml
UNRESOLVED_CONTACT: purpose, assumptions, or roles remain mixed

MISMATCH_IDENTIFIED: a testable mismatch class is identified
ADJUSTING: one minimal variable is changed
RESONANT: understanding, disagreement, and agency remain visible
HOLD: information, authority, time, or safety is insufficient
FAILED_COERCIVE: pressure, forced agreement, surveillance, or manipulation has replaced resonance
```

## Required distinctions

- resonance ≠ compliance
- regulation ≠ coercive manipulation
- respecting silence ≠ using silence as pressure
- detecting discomfort ≠ asserting another person’s internal state
- order principle ≠ fixed sales script
- local success ≠ universal validity
- connection to UCI ≠ unlimited intervention
- influence ≠ removal of free agency

## Validity conditions

- Purpose, context, relationship, and power asymmetry are identified.
- The counterpart can question, disagree, pause, or withdraw.
- Changes and responses can be recorded.
- Facts, professional standards, law, and ethics remain independent checks.
- Local results are not generalized without repeated evidence.
- Return to UCI means structural learning and institutional improvement, not expansion of domination.

## Failure / non-applicable conditions

- Agreement exists without demonstrated understanding or free agency.
- Silence is used to corner or pressure a decision.
- Discomfort is treated as a defect in the counterpart.
- One sequence is mechanically imposed on all contexts.
- Conversion, approval, or praise is the only success metric.
- Long-term trust, correction, or withdrawal becomes harder.
- AI is made compliant at the cost of factual challenge or dissent.
- The method becomes surveillance, ranking, or psychological control.
- UCI is interpreted as omnipotence or sovereign authority.

## Interpretation constraints

“Operation” refers to regulation of causal sequence and contact conditions, not ownership of another person’s choices. “Resonance” may produce a clear disagreement or a justified hold. Silence must be interpreted through context. Discomfort is a signal requiring confirmation, not proof. Do not add AI-generated completions as confirmed statements by Nakagawa Master.
