## Structural summary

Resonance Operating Power is the local capacity to bring the structure of self and counterpart into correspondence, observe words, attitudes, silence, hesitation, and discomfort, and regulate the causal flow of an interaction. UCI is the broader cross-domain structure of influence; Resonance Operating Power is the operational substrate that makes such a broad structure function at concrete points of contact. Local resonance does not mean obtaining agreement. It includes clarified disagreement, informed hold, correction, and withdrawal when agency remains visible. Results must be recorded with context, counterevidence, limits, and side effects before any reusable pattern is returned to wider UCI design.

## Core concepts

- Resonance: structural contact that reveals alignment or difference; not limited to agreement.
- Regulation: adjustment of content, order, conditions, roles, timing, or language without removing agency.
- Order principle: arrangement of information in a causal sequence that can be understood; not a universal script.
- Silence: a space for judgment, comparison, emotional processing, and question formation; not consent.
- Discomfort detection: use of mismatch signals to form testable hypotheses; not mind-reading.
- Local resonance: an interaction in which understanding, explicit disagreement, or justified hold remains possible.
- UCI: a broad integrated structure acting across domains; not an unrestricted right to dominate.

## Validity conditions

- Purpose, context, relationship, and power asymmetry are identified.
- The counterpart can question, disagree, pause, or withdraw.
- The changed variable and observed response are recorded.
- Facts, professional standards, law, and ethics remain independent checks.
- Local results are not generalized without repeated evidence.
- Failures, counterexamples, side effects, and holds are retained.
- Return to UCI means structural learning and institutional improvement, not expansion of domination.

## Failure / non-applicable conditions

- Agreement exists without demonstrated understanding or free agency.
- Silence is used to corner or pressure a decision.
- Discomfort is treated as a defect in the counterpart.
- One sequence is mechanically imposed on all contexts.
- Conversion, approval, or praise is the only success metric.
- Long-term trust, correction, or withdrawal becomes harder.
- AI is made compliant at the cost of factual challenge or dissent.
- The method becomes surveillance, ranking, or psychological control.
- UCI is interpreted as omnipotence or sovereign authority.
- Parent text, identity, or publication state cannot be verified.
