## Summary
Resonance Operating Power is the local capacity to compare the structures of self and counterpart and tune causal flow through language, sequence, silence, and detected dissonance. Local resonance is recorded, repeated, and institutionalized, then returned to UCI as feedback so a large-scale integrative structure can act in concrete relationships and decisions. The theory is distinct from manipulation, forced persuasion, rigid scripts, or claims of unlimited UCI control.

## Concepts
- Resonance Operating Power: local structural tuning across a concrete relationship and context.
- Sequence principle: choosing an order in which meaning can form naturally.
- Silence: space for meaning formation, emotional regulation, and judgment update.
- Dissonance detection: reading delays, repeated questions, wording shifts, or unexpected responses as possible structural mismatch.
- Minimal adjustment: changing only the necessary content, sequence, condition, role, or timing.
- Resonance: structural correspondence, not mere agreement.
- UCI connection: returning local results into the large-scale structure.
- River-and-tributary relation: the parent metaphor for local and global interaction.
- Free choice: preserving refusal, hold, withdrawal, and falsification.

## Causal chain
```text
self and counterpart structures meet
→ response differences appear in words, attitude, silence, delay
→ mismatch in content, sequence, conditions, role, or timing is detected
→ minimal adjustment is applied
→ response is observed again while free choice remains
→ local resonance and causal alignment may form
→ success, failure, hold, and withdrawal are recorded
→ reusable structure is separated from context-specific exceptions
→ local evidence returns to UCI-level design
```

## Applications
- Sales: treat silence or repeated questions as possible stopping points in value comprehension, conditions, budget, authority, or sequence.
- Human–AI collaboration: adjust question, premise, role, source, or abstraction level instead of merely forcing agreement.
- Education: distinguish reflection, fear, question formation, and lack of understanding rather than interpreting silence immediately.
- Organization: use repeated questions, withdrawal, and discomfort as signals for institutional or role mismatch.
- Policy and markets: observe voiced reactions, silence, exit, hold, and return as possible structural signals without diagnosing hidden psychology.

## Validity conditions
- Self and counterpart purposes and premises remain distinct.
- Responses are not reduced to obedience, liking, or conversion.
- Sequence, silence, and dissonance can be observed with adequate time and contact.
- Dissonance is treated as a structural signal, not a psychological diagnosis.
- Adjustment does not become coercion that removes free choice.
- Changes and responses are recorded.
- Local success is not immediately universalized.
- UCI connection is cumulative structural alignment, not expansion of domination.

## Failure conditions
- Agreement or conversion alone is treated as resonance.
- Silence is used as pressure.
- Dissonance is assigned to counterpart ignorance or resistance without checking the self side.
- Sequence principles become rigid scripts.
- Short-term outcomes rise while trust, continuity, return, or correction capability fall.
- AI outputs are forced into compliance and lose factual challenge or falsification capability.
- Local resonance is converted into surveillance, domination, or psychological manipulation.
- UCI is interpreted as unlimited control or omnipotence.

## Falsification conditions
- Sequence adjustment repeatedly fails to improve understanding, free choice, or sustained alignment.
- Silence design repeatedly produces pressure, inhibition, or judgment paralysis.
- Dissonance-based adjustment repeatedly causes misdiagnosis, harm, or trust loss.
- Resonance success or agreement speed improves while sustained trust or correction capability worsens.
- Returning local results to UCI fails to improve large-scale structural alignment or action.
- Persistent failure requires limiting or revising sequence principles, dissonance interpretation, tuning methods, or UCI connection scope.

## Required distinctions
- resonance / compliance
- tuning / manipulative control
- respectful silence / pressure through silence
- dissonance detection / psychological diagnosis
- sequence principle / rigid script
- local success / universal validity
- UCI connection / unlimited intervention
- influence / removal of free choice
- agreement speed / agreement quality
- agreement rate / resonance success

## Interpretation constraints
Do not reduce Resonance Operating Power to psychological manipulation or persuasion. Silence is not a tool for cornering the counterpart. Dissonance is a signal to re-examine structural mismatch including the self side, not a right to diagnose another person’s inner state. Sequence principles are not rigid scripts. UCI connection means returning local evidence into a larger structure, not authorizing omnipotence, unlimited control, or universal intervention.
