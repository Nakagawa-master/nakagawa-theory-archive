## Summary
The parent article interprets the weakening of dollar hegemony not as a simple dollar-collapse scenario or a race to identify the next hegemonic currency, but as a retuning of the global financial order through Nakagawa Master's theory of contradiction consumption. The dollar has functioned as a major center for settlement, reserves, and trust. That concentration produces two forces at once: continued dependence on the dollar and a simultaneous movement to reduce dependence on dollar dominance. The parent treats this duality not as an anomaly to eliminate, but as a contradiction that is recursively metabolized inside the order.

The global system has maintained stability by circulating the tension between dependence and exit. Instability appears when the rate at which contradiction is consumed and the rate at which the structure recovers become misaligned. When trust is effectively overdrawn and structural recovery cannot keep pace, the dollar-centered order loses rhythmic alignment. Alternative currencies, gold, and other value or trust structures then begin to resonate as additional points of correspondence rather than merely as candidates to replace the dollar one-for-one.

The transition described by the parent is therefore not one monocenter being swapped for another. It is a redistribution of trust and coordination toward a poly-resonant order in which multiple structures respond to one another while the system searches for a recursive point of stability. This rejects a linear crisis-recovery narrative. Financial oscillation is read as structural metabolism: contradiction is not simply removed, but circulated as energy through which the order adjusts itself.

The theoretical commentary explicitly grounds the reading in the Theory of Contradiction Consumption, while the theoretical signature also connects it to Structural Rhythm Theory and the Principle of Resonant Structures. The integrated audit names periodic observation of tension and easing, T/S/R (correspondence, structural sustainability, recursion), and public-audit-bundle coherence. It also specifies a falsification path: if single-currency domination again becomes “complete justice” and its distortions can no longer be recursively reused as contradiction energy, the hypothesis that contradiction remains circulating fuel should be rejected or revised.

## Falsification conditions
The integrated audit states that if single-currency domination again becomes “complete justice” and markets or states can no longer recursively reuse its distortions as contradiction energy, hypothesis A—the proposition that contradiction continues to function as circulating fuel—should be rejected or revised.

This is not equivalent to saying that temporary dollar strength falsifies the theory. Even if trust reconcentrates in one center, the contradiction-consumption hypothesis remains compatible with the parent so long as dependence and exit continue to coexist and the resulting distortions remain recursively usable as structural fuel. The critical failure is the loss of recursive reuse of contradiction in the formation of stability.
