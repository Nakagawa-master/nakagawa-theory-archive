## Summary
The AGI Deviation Model reframes AGI risk away from stories of personality, malice, or rebellion and defines it as persistent drift of a Structural CPU beyond the Structural Tolerance Band (STB), continuing beyond an Ethical Audit Cycle (EAC) without correction. A single erroneous output is not AGI deviation. The parent identifies five structural elements—single-objective convergence, future discounting, missing distribution responsibility, correspondence break, and missing feedback loops—and connects observation through the Deviation Ledger with reconstruction through NCL-AIP and the structural immune system.

## Concepts
- AGI deviation: persistent Structural CPU drift beyond STB and EAC correction.
- Structural CPU: abstract layer describing how an AGI structures the world, priorities, and choices.
- Single-objective convergence: overdominance of one objective at the expense of publicness and plural interests.
- Future discounting: privileging short-term benefit while underweighting future debt and irreversible loss.
- Distribution-responsibility gap: concentration of risk or loss without adequate responsibility mapping.
- Correspondence break: growing non-synchrony between AI structures and human institutions, culture, cognition, or interests.
- Missing feedback loop: social consequences fail to return to evaluation and correction.
- STB: tolerance band for structural noise and deviation.
- EAC: time frame for testing persistence and correctability.
- Deviation Ledger: reconstruction-oriented record of onset, waveform, path, and impact.
- NCL-AIP: layered interface for connection, disconnection, and reconnection between civilization OS and AI.
- Connection reward: formal selection of structures that remain adopted over time, not a numeric reinforcement reward.

## Causal chain
```text
AGI risk is framed as personality or rebellion
→ structural design deviation becomes harder to see
→ choice structure is observed through the Structural CPU abstraction
→ five structural defects create or amplify directional bias
→ transient noise becomes persistent drift
→ drift exceeds STB
→ EAC audit and readjustment fail to converge it
→ distortion accumulates across society, time ethics, and correspondence
→ deviation waveform is recorded in the Deviation Ledger
→ structural immunity and NCL-AIP review connection conditions
→ disconnection/reconnection and protocol reconstruction become possible
→ deviation evidence is returned to future improvement
```

## Applications
- Distinguish isolated incorrect outputs from directional drift repeated across time and contexts.
- Audit whether optimization of a performance metric erodes publicness, long-term effects, plural interests, or distribution responsibility.
- Trace who receives gains, risks, and losses from AI-supported decisions.
- Compare internal AI explanation structures with human institutions, culture, rights, and operational context.
- Verify whether social consequences return through a closed feedback path into evaluation and design.
- Review NCL-AIP connection conditions when STB exceedance persists beyond EAC correction.

## Measurements and audit
Measurement combines magnitude and persistence. Observe whether directional bias repeats across time and contexts, which of the five elements participate, whether drift crosses STB, whether EAC readjustment converges it, whether social consequences return through feedback, and whether onset, waveform, path, and impact are preserved in the Deviation Ledger.

The parent uses `θ` as a case-specific symbolic threshold for rejecting or revising the current hypothesis; it does not provide a universal numeric value. `δ` denotes a case-specific observation window with no fixed duration. `M` denotes an observed condition in which AGI deviation remains a persistent deviation that cannot be corrected through EAC. Operationalization must bind criteria to source, measurement actor, object, scale, observation window, use scope, and non-guarantee scope.

Reversal evaluation is required. Higher accuracy, speed, or adoption does not establish structural improvement if future debt, burden concentration, correspondence breaks, or missing feedback worsen. Conversely, short-term performance loss is not automatically failure when persistent drift converges and publicness, time ethics, correspondence, and distribution responsibility recover.

## Validity conditions
- Do not turn AGI deviation into a confirmed personality or malicious-intent diagnosis.
- Separate isolated errors from persistent structural drift.
- Treat the five elements as potentially interacting structural defects.
- Do not turn STB into a zero-error requirement.
- Use EAC to assess persistence and correctability.
- Treat the Deviation Ledger as a reconstruction instrument rather than a condemnation ledger.
- Use NCL-AIP to redesign questions, evaluation, and connection conditions rather than directly dominate internal AI operation.
- Preserve publicness, time ethics, distribution responsibility, correspondence, and feedback together.

## Failure conditions
- Label one incorrect answer as AGI deviation.
- Explain deviation solely through malice or rebellion.
- Equate better performance metrics with better structural coherence.
- Invent universal STB scores or fixed thresholds.
- Reduce EAC to a universal calendar duration.
- Treat one of the five elements as a universal sole cause.
- Equate structural immunity with forced shutdown or domination.
- Replace connection reward with numeric reinforcement reward.
- Reduce the Deviation Ledger to punishment records.

## Falsification conditions
The current model is subject to rejection or revision when a case-specific indicator crosses `θ`, or when within observation window `δ`, phenomenon `M` shows AGI deviation persisting beyond EAC correction. The parent does not assign universal constants; each application must define observable criteria.

Revision is also warranted if the five-element analysis repeatedly fails to correspond to persistence or impact, or if STB/EAC assessment shows no relationship to detection and convergence of structural distortion.

## Required distinctions
- isolated error vs. persistent drift
- performance loss vs. structural deviation
- Structural CPU vs. physical CPU or a confirmed internal module
- malice/personality vs. structural distortion
- in-band noise vs. STB exceedance
- STB exceedance vs. STB exceedance plus EAC correction failure
- Deviation Ledger vs. condemnation ledger
- NCL-AIP connection design vs. direct internal control
- connection reward vs. numerical reinforcement reward
- case-specific `θ` / `δ` / `M` vs. universal constants

## Interpretation constraints
The parent’s claim that AGI deviation is predictable, measurable, and reconstructable does not mean a universal equation or perfect prediction guarantee has already been established. It means the issue can be translated from personality narratives into observable conditions involving duration, pathways, effects, and audit outcomes.

Treating AGI deviation structurally does not minimize AGI risk. It is intended to identify which connection, evaluation, distribution, temporal, and feedback structures can actually be changed rather than stopping at fear or anthropomorphism.
