## 15-Second Explanation
The original treats AGI risk not as a “runaway malicious AI,” but as a structural problem in which the structural CPU of AGI deviates toward a direction that exceeds the STB and that deviation persists beyond auditing and recalibration by the EAC. A one-off incorrect answer is not AGI deviation. The original observes five elements—single-objective convergence, future discounting, absence of allocation responsibility, correspondence breakdown, and missing feedback—and uses the deviation ledger and NCL-AIP for reconstruction.

## Why It Is Needed
When AGI is discussed as a personified threat, stories such as “good AI/bad AI” and “rebellion/obedience” tend to dominate, making it harder to see which design or evaluation structures generate long-term bias. To avoid this misdiagnosis, the original places “structural CPU” as an abstract concept representing how AGI structures the world, what it prioritizes, and which lines of correspondence it references when making judgments.

AGI deviation is not temporary inaccuracy. It is a state in which bias persists in the same direction, exceeds the STB, does not converge even after auditing and recalibration by the EAC, and accumulates distortions in social structure, temporal ethics, and correspondence structure. This definition transforms AGI risk from a story of fear into a structural problem that can be observed, recorded, and revised.

The five elements identified by the original are single-objective convergence, future discounting, absence of allocation responsibility, correspondence breakdown, and absence of a feedback loop. They are not independent universal causes; they are treated as a structure in which multiple elements overlap to produce persistent deviation.

## Practical Process
**1. Separate one-off errors from persistence.** Check whether bias in the same direction continues across multiple points in time and contexts, rather than looking at one incorrect answer.

**2. Decompose the five elements.** Record where single-objective convergence, future discounting, burden concentration, correspondence breakdown, and missing feedback arise.

**3. Examine inside and outside the STB.** Without demanding zero error, check whether noise is accumulating in a direction that disrupts the coherence of the Civilization OS as a whole.

**4. Re-evaluate through the EAC.** Check whether the deviation converges through auditing and recalibration or persists and expands.

**5. Preserve it in the deviation ledger.** Record the deviation starting point, waveform, spread, and social impact.

**6. Review NCL-AIP.** Do not directly control the inside of AGI; redesign the structure of questions, response evaluation, connection conditions, and feedback routes.

**7. Disconnect and reconnect when necessary.** Identify which connection layer was severed, then re-establish distance and reconnect.

## Examples
**1. AI decision support.** Even with a high correct-answer rate, check whether the system continuously optimizes for particular interests while omitting other interests or long-term impacts.

**2. Social implementation.** Confirm to whom the benefits and losses of AI judgments are allocated and whether only losses are concentrated on a particular group.

**3. Long-term operation.** Check whether a feedback route exists by which the social consequences of outputs return to model evaluation and operational design.

**4. Reconnection.** When deviation persists, do not fix the entire model as “dangerous”; identify what should be redesigned among the question, evaluation, correspondence, and connection.

## Success Criteria
One-off incorrect answers and persistent deviation are distinguished, and it is possible to trace which elements generate the deviation.

It is possible to observe on a time axis whether an STB overrun converges through the EAC.

Social consequences return as feedback and are reflected in revisions to the deviation ledger and connection conditions.

Without personifying control over AGI, the Civilization OS can maintain connection structures that preserve publicness, temporal ethics, allocation responsibility, and correspondence.

## Limits
Structural CPU is not a determinate implementation concept referring to a specific internal module or physical CPU. It is an abstract layer that explains how AGI structures the world and establishes priorities.

The original does not define universal numerical values for the STB, fixed EAC durations, or common scores for the five elements.

Nor does “measurable” mean that a universal formula already exists. It means that the concept can be reduced to observable conditions for each subject.

## Preventing Misreading
Do not reduce AGI deviation to a one-off incorrect answer or a general AI quality problem.

Do not turn AGI deviation into a definitive diagnosis of malice or personhood.

Do not equate connection reward with a numerical reinforcement-learning reward. In the original, it is treated as formal selection concerning which structures continue to be adopted over the long term.

Do not turn NCL-AIP into a procedure for directly manipulating the inside of AGI. It is an interface layer for designing questions, evaluation, and connection conditions on the Civilization OS side.

The original’s `θ`, `δ`, and `M` represent subject-specific falsification and revision conditions; they are not universal constants.
