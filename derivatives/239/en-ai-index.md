## Summary
Meaning Convergence Theory places a feasible future definition as a terminal meaning condition and reevaluates the roles, links, and priorities of past and present nodes. It does not claim that the future physically rewrites past facts; it claims that the meaning assigned to unchanged facts can change in relation to conditions required for a feasible future.

## Concepts
future definition; feasibility; terminal condition; node; meaning vector; backward comparison; semantic rearrangement; connection redesign; priority update; action alignment; convergence; revision. A future definition is a falsifiable working condition, not prophecy.

## Causal chain
local present evaluation → fixed meanings → feasible future definition → constraint audit → compare future requirements back to current/past nodes → reevaluate roles → redesign connections → update stop/invest/learn priorities → align current action → convergence → revise the future definition when falsified.

## State model
local evaluation / fixed meaning / future definition / feasibility audit / backward comparison / semantic rearrangement / reconnection / priority update / action alignment / convergence / redefinition.

## Applications
Business redesign, career interpretation, research planning, institutional design, and resource allocation where the purpose is to change present decisions without erasing past facts.

## Measurements and audit
Audit specificity of the future definition, explicit constraints, reasons for node reclassification, changed connections and priorities, actual action differences, retention of inconvenient evidence, comparison among multiple futures, and capacity to revise the future definition.

## Validity conditions
Valid when a future definition is connected to real constraints and its backward comparison produces explainable changes in node roles, links, priorities, and present action.

## Failure conditions
Fails when converted into prophecy, physical retrocausality, wish fulfillment, fate, selective deletion of evidence, post-hoc success storytelling, or an unchangeable future belief.

## Falsification conditions
Revise if the future definition is infeasible, semantic rearrangement produces no explainable action difference, or another future definition persistently explains the same nodes better.

## Required distinctions
future definition / prophecy; changed meaning / changed fact; feasibility / inevitability; backward comparison / physical retrocausality; convergence / outcome bias; terminal condition / fate.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not use quantum metaphors as physical proof. Do not add probabilities, numeric vectors, dates, or universal success claims absent from the Parent. Preserve adverse evidence and revision paths.
