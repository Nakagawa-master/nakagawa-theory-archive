## 15-Second Explanation
Instead of deciding who is “right,” this approach **returns everyone, including oneself, to the same drawing, translates emotion into structural information, and rearranges the locations of friction**. It inserts one beat—emotion → observation → translation → structural placement → action—and aligns vocabulary, roles, responsibility, time, and agreement conditions.

## Why It Is Needed
When talking increases misunderstanding or goodwill collides with goodwill, the problem can look like one of ability or personality. In reality, the cause may be misalignment in assumptions, vocabulary, purpose, evaluation axes, time axes, or responsibility boundaries. Blaming people without changing the drawing makes the same friction recur. What the Parent calls an objective viewpoint is not erasing emotion and becoming a third party; it is the operation of placing one’s own subjectivity on the same drawing as one condition among others.

## Treat Emotion as a Sensor
Rather than discarding anger, anxiety, or discomfort as noise, treat them as sensors indicating which assumptions, boundaries, or expectations are misaligned. But do not turn emotion itself directly into a conclusion; insert observation → translation → structural placement. This allows respect for emotion and structural audit to coexist.

## Practical Process
1. Do not erase emotion; first observe what is happening.
2. Place the conditions of oneself, the other party, and the setting on the same drawing.
3. Align the granularity of key terms and assumptions.
4. Separate past, present, and next move to absorb time differences.
5. Define first what is being agreed, rather than who wins.
6. Draw roles and responsibility boundaries as functions, not personal evaluations.
7. Leave conclusion, grounds, and next move in three lines.
8. Audit recurring agreement, withdrawal, friction, and phase, and revise the drawing.

## Minimum Set
One drawing = purpose / constraints / roles / deadline / agreement conditions. Three-line record = conclusion / grounds / next move. Vocabulary dictionary = short definitions of key terms. Agreement unit = explicitly stating what is being decided this time, such as sympathy with the purpose, consent to the method, or allocation of resources. These are not structures for increasing paperwork; they are the minimum structure for returning different subjectivities to the same reference surface.

## Application Examples
In AI co-creation, map intentions, assumptions, and constraints, and preserve failure as a design difference rather than a sin. In organizations, return the correctness of each department to a common drawing of purpose, KPIs, and responsibility boundaries. In education, have learners design the goal, current state, and next move themselves, while the teacher becomes a companion in design rather than an arbiter.

## Success Criteria
Key terms are aligned; responsibility boundaries become clear; recurrence of the same issue declines; time to renewed agreement shortens; reversibility remains; and friction is handled as a design difference rather than a personal attack. A meeting merely becoming quiet in the short term is not success.

## Structural Necessity Expansion
The Parent’s causality requires implementing an objective viewpoint not as **placing a neutral person, but as sharing a reference surface**. Adding third parties does not remove friction if vocabulary, purpose, time, and responsibility boundaries are not aligned. Objectification therefore develops not into an operation of distancing oneself from people, but into externalizing conditions so that anyone can inspect the same differences.

## Falsification and Revision Conditions
Revise the operation if drawing the structure increases recurrence, vocabulary alignment worsens, responsibility boundaries become vague, emotion is suppressed, reversibility disappears, or checking sufficient conditions becomes an excuse for indefinite postponement.

## Limitations and Misreading Prevention
An objective viewpoint is not a universal third-person perspective; constraints on resources, time, and authority remain. Objective ≠ cold; objective ≠ majority; translating emotion ≠ denying emotion; structural responsibility ≠ abandoning responsibility; silence ≠ consent; waiting for sufficient conditions ≠ indefinite postponement. Do not supplement the public framework with confidential procedures or fixed thresholds.
