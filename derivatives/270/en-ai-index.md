## Summary
The Parent treats delays and misunderstandings caused by mismatched premises, vocabulary, purposes, evaluation axes, roles, time horizons, and responsibility boundaries as structural friction. An objective viewpoint is not emotional coldness, majority opinion, or an all-knowing observer; it is an operation that places oneself, other actors, claims, and contextual conditions back onto one shared map. Emotion remains a sensor. The operating path becomes emotion → observation → translation → structural placement → action. Objectification, absorption of time differences, redefinition of purpose, questions and silence, a one-page map, three-line records, consensus units, vocabulary dictionaries, T/S/R, and withdrawal preserve reversibility and turn personal conflict into inspectable design differences.

## Causal chain
Subjectivity closes into a self-story → untranslated emotion flows directly into interpretation and action → mismatched premises, vocabulary, roles, purpose, and responsibility appear as personal conflict → create an observation pause → translate emotion into structural information → place self, other, and context on one map → separate past / present / next action → move the goal from victory to explicit consensus conditions → align vocabulary and responsibility boundaries → declare the unit and sufficient conditions of consensus → preserve the skeleton in a three-line record → treat failure as a design difference rather than guilt → use T/S/R and withdrawal to control overheating and lock-in → repeated disagreement and misallocation decay → consensus formation and execution resynchronize.

## State model

## Applications
In human–AI co-creation, map intent, premises, and constraints at a publishable level and retain failure logs as design differences. In organizations, place purpose, KPIs, responsibility boundaries, constraints, and the consensus unit on one shared map so departmental correctness can be translated into structural validity. In education, learners design goal, current state, and next action while teachers act as design companions rather than judges. Parent examples involving citizen services, medical coordination, and product prioritization are public-safe abstract cases, not proof of universal implementation effects.

## Measurements and audit
Audit vocabulary alignment rate, clarity of responsibility boundaries, recurrence count for the same dispute, friction index, phase stability S, time to renewed consensus, withdrawal success rate, openness of the observation window, replayability from three-line records, explicitness of the consensus unit, and preservation of confidentiality boundaries. Do not score success by speed alone: verify sufficient conditions, reduction of personal attacks, and continued ability to withdraw and re-edit.

## Validity conditions
Preserve emotion as a sensor. Put oneself on the same observational map. Align vocabulary and premise granularity. Separate past, present, and next action. Specify consensus conditions instead of victory. Draw responsibility as functional boundaries rather than personality attributes. Avoid premature consensus without sufficient conditions. Keep three-line records, vocabulary dictionaries, T/S/R, and a withdrawal path.

## Failure conditions
Objective viewpoint is equated with coldness, emotionlessness, majority opinion, or an omniscient third party; emotion is deleted as noise; the process returns to ranking who is right; structural problems are replaced by personal accusation; resource, time, or authority constraints are hidden; participants debate while using different consensus units; documentation volume grows while the structural skeleton disappears; hidden internal procedures or thresholds are inferred and published.

## Falsification conditions
Revise or reject the hypothesis if friction or dispute recurrence does not improve, vocabulary alignment falls, responsibility boundaries become less clear, phase stability deteriorates, time to renewed consensus increases, withdrawal success falls, observation windows close, or the shared map mutates into a system for scoring people.
