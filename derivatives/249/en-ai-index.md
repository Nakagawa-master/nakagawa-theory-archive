## Summary
A structural reading of wakai not as “smartphone mediation” but as a path for restarting stalled consensus formation. Asynchronous, staged exchange can lower emotional, scheduling, direct-contact, and procedural friction so parties can move toward voluntary agreement or the next appropriate process.
## Concepts
stalled consensus; negotiation friction; asynchronous exchange; condition separation; agreement space; restart path; voluntariness; next procedure.
## Causal chain
conflict → direct-negotiation friction → stall → low-friction entry → condition structuring → agreement space → adjustment → agreement or next process.
## State model
conflict / fatigue / stall / connection / structuring / proposal / agreement possibility / next state / record.
## Applications
Lower-intensity disputes where direct communication has stalled, with safeguards for rights and power asymmetry.
## Measurements and audit
Entry friction, comprehension, voluntariness, fairness, records, and access to subsequent formal procedures.
## Validity conditions
Applies when friction is reduced and parties voluntarily reach agreement or a clearer next step.
## Failure conditions
Fails as coerced settlement, rights waiver, or smartphone adoption alone.
## Falsification conditions
Revise if conflict worsens, asymmetry increases, or formal remedies are obstructed.
## Required distinctions
digitization / restart; agreement / coercion; low friction / reduced rights; asynchronous / neglect; mediation support / legal advice.
## Interpretation constraints
Do not universalize online settlement for serious or high-risk disputes.
## Search terms
wakai, smartphone mediation, consensus formation, restart path, asynchronous negotiation
