## Summary
AKI-002 analyzes a form of bad causality in which praise, gratitude, expectation, or opportunity substitutes for explicit confirmation of compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, responsibility allocation, continuing compensation, and the freedom to refuse. The parent original does not reject praise. Its concern is the structural reversal that occurs when language that appears to recognize value stops the process of determining where that value belongs and under what conditions it may be used.

The positive surface is essential to the mechanism. A creator, worker, caregiver, organizer, or other value-producing person may hear that their contribution is excellent, indispensable, helpful, or a valuable opportunity. At the same time, additional work, coordination, revision, emotional labor, responsibility, or continuing support may increase. If compensation, attribution, rights, usage conditions, continuing terms, and refusal margin do not increase or become clearer in parallel, the language of recognition can conceal one-way value transfer.

The theory therefore does not classify people according to presumed intent. A counterparty may be sincerely grateful and the bad causality may still arise as a structural result. Conversely, praise may be present without AKI-002 applying if conditions remain explicit, added burdens lead to updated compensation and rights, both sides retain negotiation space, and the person producing value remains free to refuse. The audit object is not the personality of the speaker but the movement of burden, value, rights, attribution, and freedom before and after the praise event.

Repeated operation matters because a single apparently positive exchange can become a durable expectation. “You always help,” “only you can do this,” or “this is good exposure” can turn into an informal rule under which additional value is expected without a corresponding update of conditions. Over time the creator’s price sense, negotiation power, professional value, and refusal freedom can erode while the user side accumulates output, credit, continuity, and use value. AKI-002 isolates that causal pathway rather than treating all praise as exploitation or all uncompensated activity as the same phenomenon.

## Applications
**Creative and intellectual production.** A work, article, design, concept, system, or body of knowledge may be praised while attribution, secondary use, continuing use, revision duties, and compensation remain unclear. The relevant question is not whether the work was praised but whether the praise was followed by an explicit allocation of the value that the user side continues to receive.

**Employment and organizational work.** “Only you can do this,” “we can always rely on you,” or similar recognition may be followed by work outside the assigned role, emergency response, coordination, mentoring, or invisible support. AKI-002 becomes relevant when added burden is normalized while role, compensation, authority, staffing, and refusal margin remain unchanged.

**Education, community activity, family work, and care.** Gratitude may coexist with an increasing concentration of scheduling, mediation, care, emotional labor, and maintenance on one person. The framework separates sincere appreciation from the structural question of whether responsibility and recovery time are being redistributed fairly and explicitly.

**Exposure, experience, and opportunity.** These can be genuine forms of value when the person concerned can choose them knowingly and the conditions are explicit. They become relevant to AKI-002 when they are used as reasons not to discuss compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, or the limits of continuing obligations.

**Continuing use.** A one-time request or one-time agreement can gradually be treated as permission for unlimited reuse, revisions, consultation, or support. AKI-002 requires distinguishing the positive event that opened the relationship from the later expansion of use and burden.

**Knowledge and AI-era reuse.** Ideas, explanations, taxonomies, designs, and structured knowledge may be praised as useful while origin, attribution, reuse scope, and continuing value are lost. The theory does not create a separate AI-specific doctrine; it applies the same value-attribution question to a setting in which reuse can become especially easy to detach from origin.

## Measurements and audit
The parent original does not define a general exploitation score, probability, percentage, ranking, or numerical threshold that may be carried into derivative use. No such number is created here. Reversal-evaluation capability is maintained by comparing the same structural variables before and after praise, gratitude, expectation, or opportunity is introduced.

- Change in workload, responsibility, revision load, coordination load, and continuing support.
- Whether compensation is made explicit or updated when burden increases.
- Whether rights, attribution, usage scope, secondary use, and continuing use are made explicit.
- Whether continuing use is paired with continuing compensation or clearly bounded responsibility.
- Whether the value-producing person can refuse or renegotiate without losing the relationship by default.
- Whether output, credit, continuity, savings, or use value accumulate mainly on the user side.
- Whether the creator’s price sense, negotiation power, professional value, or refusal freedom weakens across repeated interactions.
- Whether praise is added on top of condition-setting or used in place of condition-setting.

These observations are not derivative-created legal tests or generic labor KPIs. Their function is to keep the parent causal line reversible and comparable: if the hypothesized mechanism is present, the positive-language event and the displacement of conditions should be structurally connected to one-way value transfer and erosion; if those connections are absent, the AKI-002 application should weaken.

## Validity conditions
- Praise, gratitude, expectation, or opportunity functions as pressure to accept additional burden.
- Explicit condition-setting or refusal becomes harder after the positive signal.
- Compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, responsibility allocation, or continuing terms move into the background.
- Work, responsibility, coordination, revision, care, or continuing support increases.
- Value created by one side continues to move toward the user side without an equivalent update of conditions.
- Price sense, negotiation power, professional value, or freedom to refuse erodes on the creator side.
- The positive surface makes the transfer easier to interpret as a good relationship rather than as a condition problem.

## Failure conditions
- Praise coexists with explicit condition-setting rather than replacing it.
- Added burden is accompanied by updated compensation, role definition, rights, and responsibility allocation.
- Attribution, usage scope, secondary use, and continuing use remain explicit.
- Both sides retain practical negotiation space and freedom to refuse.
- Continuing use has a defined scope, continuing terms, and an end condition.
- Output use and value return do not persistently move in one direction.
- Repeated observation shows no erosion of price sense, negotiation power, professional value, or refusal freedom following praise.

Praise alone is therefore not evidence that AKI-002 applies. Rejecting all appreciation, gratitude, opportunity, or voluntary contribution would also fall outside the parent theory because it removes the required distinction between value recognition and the substitution of recognition for conditions.

## Falsification conditions
The parent origin's integrated audit summary states: If praise is added on top of explicit compensation and rights rather than substituting for them, or both sides have sufficient information and room to negotiate and no imbalance in value transfer is observed, the theory is rejected, limited, or revised for that case.
