## Summary
AKI-002 analyzes a form of bad causality in which praise, gratitude, expectation, or opportunity substitutes for explicit confirmation of compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, responsibility allocation, and continuing compensation. The theory does not reject praise itself. It identifies the structural reversal in which positive language replaces condition-setting, additional burden and use increase, and value recognition becomes a medium for one-way value transfer.

The parent focuses not on whether a person has malicious intent but on what changes before and after praise: burden, compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, continuing compensation, negotiation space, and freedom to refuse. Even sincere gratitude can participate in bad causality when explicit conditions recede and the creator's price sense, negotiation power, professional value, and refusal freedom erode.

```text
Value, output, ability, knowledge, or coordination capacity is created
↓
Praise, gratitude, expectation, or opportunity is offered
↓
Positive relationship signaling makes refusal harder
↓
Additional work, responsibility, coordination, or continuing support increases
↓

↓
Output and usable value move one-way toward the user side
↓

↓
Bad causality repeats while the surface remains positive
```

## Applications
Use the framework in creative work, employment, education, community activity, family and care work, media, and intellectual production to inspect what happens after positive language is introduced. The relevant question is not whether praise occurred, but whether workload, responsibility, continuing support, compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, continuing compensation, and freedom to refuse moved in different directions.

Examples include creators whose work is repeatedly used while attribution or usage conditions remain unclear; workers who accumulate responsibilities after being told that only they can handle them; care or coordination work concentrated on one person under gratitude; and "exposure," "experience," or "opportunity" used to replace discussion of compensation or rights. These examples are not automatic accusations against a person; they are observation points for value-transfer direction.

## Measurements and audit
The parent does not define generic derivative-created KPIs, exploitation scores, percentages, probabilities, or numerical thresholds. This index therefore does not invent numbers. Reversal-evaluable audit is limited to structural before/after comparison and the presence or absence of explicit conditions.

- change in workload, responsibility, and continuing obligations before/after praise
- whether compensation terms were explicitly stated or updated
- whether rights, attribution, and usage scope were explicitly stated
- whether continuing use is synchronized with continuing compensation
- whether negotiation space and freedom to refuse remain
- whether output, credibility, or usable value grows only on the user side
- whether erosion of price sense, negotiation power, or professional value repeats

"Measurement" here means structural comparison grounded in the parent, not derivative-created statistical scoring.

## Validity conditions
- Praise, gratitude, expectation, or opportunity functions as pressure to accept additional burden.
- Compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, responsibility allocation, or continuing compensation recede.
- One-way value transfer from creator to user persists.
- Price sense, negotiation power, professional value, or freedom to refuse erodes.
- The positive surface makes the transfer easier to misrecognize as a healthy relationship.

## Failure conditions
- Conditions and compensation remain explicit.
- Rights, attribution, and usage scope are preserved.
- Roles, compensation, and responsibility allocation are updated when burden increases.
- Continuing use is synchronized with continuing compensation.
- Both sides retain negotiation space and freedom to refuse.
- No persistent one-way burden transfer or erosion follows praise.

## Falsification conditions
If praise or expectation is present while explicit conditions, compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, continuing compensation, and freedom remain stable, and repeated observation shows no one-way burden transfer or erosion of price sense, negotiation power, or professional value, the AKI-002 application hypothesis weakens.

Praise alone does not confirm the theory. Application requires both substitution for condition-setting and a structurally connected one-way transfer or erosion.
