## Summary
AKI-002 analyzes a bad causal structure in which praise, gratitude, expectation, or opportunity substitutes for explicit confirmation of compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, and continuing compensation. The parent original does not reject praise itself. It asks what changes before and after positive language is introduced: workload, responsibility, compensation, ownership-like rights, attribution, usage conditions, continuing value return, negotiation space, and the practical freedom to refuse.

The central distinction is between praise added on top of clear conditions and praise used instead of clear conditions. In the latter case, value can move one-way from the creator toward the user while the creator's price sense, negotiation power, professional value, and refusal margin erode. This index preserves that structural causal line without turning it into a legal classification, a generic labor KPI, or an automatic victim/exploiter label.

## Causal chain
```text
Value, output, skill, knowledge, or emotional labor is created
→ praise, gratitude, expectation, or opportunity is offered
→ the relationship is framed positively and refusal becomes harder
→ extra work, responsibility, coordination, or continuing support increases
→ compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, and continuing compensation recede from explicit negotiation
→ output and use-value move toward the user side
→ creator price sense, negotiation power, professional value, and refusal margin erode
→ bad causality repeats beneath a positive surface
```

## Applications
- Audit creative work that is highly praised while attribution, usage scope, and continuing compensation remain vague.
- Check whether "only you can do this" or "we expect a lot from you" is followed by recurring extra work without condition revision.
- Inspect care, education, family, or community settings where gratitude coexists with concentration of coordination and emotional labor on one person.
- Examine whether "exposure," "experience," or "opportunity" is functioning as a substitute for compensation or rights confirmation.
- In knowledge production or media use, compare value recognition with actual value attribution and continuing return.

## Measurements and audit
- Change in workload, responsibility, coordination, and continuing support before and after praise.
- Whether compensation is explicitly stated and agreed.
- Whether rights, attribution, and usage scope remain traceable.
- Whether continuing use has a continuing-compensation condition.
- Whether practical refusal and renegotiation remain available.
- Whether value derived from use moves mainly in one direction.
- Whether price sense, negotiation power, or professional value erodes across repetitions.
- Whether positive language becomes the endpoint that stops condition-setting.

## Validity conditions
- Praise, expectation, or opportunity functions as pressure to accept additional burden.
- Compensation, rights, attribution, or usage conditions move into the background.
- Creator burden rises while user-side value capture rises.
- One-way value transfer persists or repeats rather than appearing only once.
- Price sense, negotiation power, or professional value erodes.
- Positive language substitutes for explicit condition-setting.

## Failure conditions
- Compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, responsibilities, and continuing compensation remain explicit.
- Both sides retain negotiation space and practical freedom to refuse.
- Additional burden does not accumulate one-way after praise.
- Value attribution and compensation remain connected to continuing use.
- No continuing erosion of price sense or negotiation power is observed.
- Praise is added after conditions are agreed rather than replacing them.

## Falsification conditions
- Repeated cases with explicit conditions, compensation, rights, attribution, usage scope, continuing compensation, and refusal freedom show no one-way burden transfer after praise.
- Under the same conditions, price sense, negotiation power, and professional value do not erode.
- The observed value transfer is fully explained by other variables and no causal contribution from praise-as-substitute can be identified.
