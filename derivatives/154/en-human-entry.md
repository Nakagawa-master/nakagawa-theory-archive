## 15-Second Explanation
The original does not treat AGI deviation and natural disasters as the same phenomenon. It extracts their common structure: future debt accumulates, exceeds STB, and emerges as structural deviation. It then uses four lines—correspondence, temporal ethics T0, reversibility, and allocation responsibility—and five stages—detection → correction → selection → settlement → reassignment—to connect crises to a permanent self-reinforcing loop in the Civilization OS.

## Why It Is Needed
Simply accumulating separate symptomatic treatments for each crisis can miss the future debt and structural deviation they share. But treating everything as the same crisis erases differences among phenomena. The original avoids both extremes, preserving the differences between informational and physical sides while bundling only their shared structures into permanent OS modules.

On the AGI side, problems include discounting the future, cutting the objective function off from the public interest, and lacking feedback. On the disaster side, problems include postponing infrastructure investment, stagnating institutional updates, and thinning information transmission. Their causes differ, but they are isomorphic in that long-term debt accumulates and is exposed all at once in a crisis.

## Practical Process
**1. Detection.** Treat an STB overrun as a structural signal rather than a simple abnormal value, and record which future debt, correspondence break, and allocation deviation have emerged.

**2. Correction.** Use EAC to check whether the deviation is temporary or persistent, and make the minimum necessary correction while preserving reversibility.

**3. Selection.** Through NCL-AIP, adoption protocols, and similar means, decide which output or reconstruction proposal to adopt into the Civilization OS. The amount adopted is not itself success.

**4. Settlement.** Based on T0, identify the causal structure and remainder of future debt, and decide on which time axis it will be settled.

**5. Reassignment.** Reallocate burdens, benefits, and decision rights along allocation-responsibility lines, updating the structure while preserving publicness and non-ownership.

## How to Read the Four Lines
**Correspondence.** For AGI, this concerns correspondence among outputs, society, institutions, and psychology; for disasters, it concerns reconnecting disconnected information and decision-making.

**Temporal ethics T0.** For AGI, it handles discounting the future; for disasters, future debt and time compression, asking whether present improvement increases future burdens.

**Reversibility.** For AGI, it stores deviation outputs in logs and preserves the ability to retry; for disasters, it treats break points as cracks that can be reconstituted.

**Allocation responsibility.** For AGI, it makes the impact and responsibility of deviation visible; for disasters, it structurally visualizes recovery resources, long-term investment, and burden allocation.

## Examples of Application
- Even if AI performance improves, it is not structural improvement if future debt or burden concentration increases.
- Even if disaster recovery is fast, it is not success if it reproduces the same vulnerabilities or information disconnections.
- In organizational misconduct, do not stop at individual responsibility; trace long-term structural deviation through reassignment.
- In compound crises, conduct a shared audit with the four lines and five stages while preserving differences among phenomena.

## Success Criteria
Do not judge by the number of crises handled, recovery speed, adoption rate, or processing speed alone. Check whether future debt has decreased, structural deviation has converged, correspondence/reversibility/publicness have improved, and the uneven distribution of burdens, benefits, and decision rights has decreased.

The original’s integrated audit retains subject-specific thresholds `θ`, observation windows `δ`, and phenomena `M` that should reject or revise applying a single structural solution. There are no universal fixed values of `θ` or `δ`.

In reversal evaluation, short-term efficiency is not success if future debt, irreversibility, or uneven burdens increase. Conversely, even if short-term efficiency declines, it is not failure based only on short-term indicators if deviation converges and publicness, reversibility, and long-term alignment improve.

## Limits
“Treating all crises through one structure” does not mean applying the same concrete measures to everything. AGI and disasters have different causes and phenomena; only their common structural elements are bundled at OS level.

The original also does not promise universal fixed numbers, a fixed process, or that crises will disappear. Values in individual implementations must be tied to the subject, source, measuring party, scale, observation period, scope of use, and scope not guaranteed.

## Preventing Misreading
- Do not reduce AGI deviation and natural disasters to the same physical cause.
- Do not read integration as a simple mixture of two theories.
- Do not turn the four lines and five stages into a universal score or fixed procedure.
- Do not turn using crisis as a self-reinforcing trigger into approval of crises or damage.
- Do not replace selection or adoption with direct control inside AI.
- Do not reverse reassignment into stronger ownership or control by a central actor.
- Do not interpret structural victory as crisis elimination or a safety guarantee.
- Do not add fixed values to `θ`, `δ`, or `M` that the original does not define.
