## Summary
AKI-011 does not attack clarity itself. It analyzes the bad causality in which an explanation lacking sufficient structural decomposition manufactures a sense of completed understanding, stops questioning and verification, fixes shallow interpretation into shared vocabulary, and degrades recognition, judgment, implementation capacity, and trust. Understanding preserves conditions, limits, exceptions, falsification, uncertainty, and the next question; performance of understanding creates completion without that structure.

## Causal chain
```text
A complex phenomenon creates uncertainty
↓
Demand grows for a clear explanation
↓
A metaphor, assertion, trend phrase, or summary is supplied without enough structural decomposition
↓
A sense of "already understood" forms
↓
Questions about conditions, exceptions, falsification, and uncertainty stop
↓
The shallow explanation becomes shared vocabulary
↓
Misrecognition enters judgment and implementation
↓
Recognition, judgment accuracy, implementation capacity, and trust degrade
```

## State / operational model
- **Entry explanation**: concise while preserving conditions, exceptions, falsification, and next questions.
- **Performance candidate**: strong completion feeling with hidden conditions or uncertainty.
- **Recognition fixation**: shared vocabulary forms and challengers inherit the burden of proof.
- **Recognition pollution**: the shallow premise repeatedly enters judgment, responsibility, and implementation.

## Applications
Use in AI summaries, social media, meetings, executive explanations, commentary, education, and internal vocabulary to distinguish explanations that open inquiry from explanations that end it prematurely. Do not reduce the origin to generic communication advice or media literacy.

## Measurements and audit
Do not invent derivative numeric KPIs. Audit whether the explanation preserves next questions, establishment conditions, exceptions, falsification conditions, applicability limits, and unresolved areas; whether metaphors expose their failure boundary; whether judgment and implementation precision actually improve; and whether shared vocabulary has stopped verification.

## Validity conditions
- Do not treat clarity itself as harmful.
- Separate understanding from performance of understanding.
- Inspect whether questions remain open.
- Preserve conditions, exceptions, falsification, and uncertainty.
- Inspect verifiability after vocabulary becomes shared.

## Failure conditions
The reading fails when reduced to criticism of lying, pretended expertise, communication skills, low comprehension, or praise of difficulty. It also fails if AI summaries, metaphors, or concise explanations are prohibited categorically.

## Falsification conditions
If concise explanations and shared vocabulary spread while recognition accuracy, judgment accuracy, implementation capacity, and correctability consistently improve, with no observable closure of questions or verification stop, the bad-causality hypothesis weakens.

## Required distinctions
- understanding / performance of understanding
- clarity / completion feeling
- explanation as entry / explanation as endpoint
- shared vocabulary / unverifiable shared premise
- summary / deletion of conditions
- concision / closure of inquiry
