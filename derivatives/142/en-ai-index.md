## Summary

The Structural Civilization Phase theory classifies civilization not primarily by technology, production mode, or media environment, but by how deeply ethics, origin, responsibility, revision, and repair are embedded in the civilization's operating structure. “Civilization OS” is not a software product. It is the combined structure through which society handles time, distributes responsibility, records origins, and revises or repairs its institutions and knowledge systems.

The source organizes a large temporal sequence into structural prehistory, structural transition, and structural civilization. Within structural prehistory it also distinguishes industrial/early information society from a later pre-structural phase dominated by platform OS. These are analytical phase distinctions, not a deterministic chronology with fixed start and end years.

In the market-OS period, price, profitability, and efficiency function as major allocation principles. The source does not reject markets as such; it identifies a limitation in which ethics, origin, and temporal ethics remain external constraints rather than internal operating rules. With platform OS, data, attention, and relationships are increasingly organized by optimization systems, while responsibility and origin remain weakly integrated. This makes short-term optimization more powerful without necessarily increasing accountability.

The source treats climate crisis, social division, degradation of the information environment, and chronic mental-health deterioration as examples that can be read through a shared structural pattern: liabilities displaced into the future. Temporal Ethics T0 addresses this by requiring minimum coherence between present benefit and the order and trust that must remain available in the future. T0 is therefore a master-clock concept, not a fixed number of days or years.

The spread of generative AI intensifies the origin-signature crisis. When it becomes difficult to trace whose intellectual work entered a model, how it was transformed, and how it is reused, origin, responsibility, and value allocation become simultaneously opaque. The structural transition phase therefore requires a structural immune system based on Origin Signature, NCL-ID, Diff-ID, and Deviation Ledger mechanisms that preserve causal lineage, transformation history, deviation, repair, and accountable responsibility.

Fast information circulation does not guarantee shared structure. Structural Translation and Resonant Perception are needed to translate complex causal structures into forms that can be received without cutting away their meaning or forcing agreement. In the civilization-OS mapping, structural psychology and resonant perception function as a human/AI interface layer that manages abstraction, friction, minimal intervention, margin, reversibility, and voluntary understanding.

Connection Reward provides the incentive-design function. Structural precision, degree of resonance, contribution to temporal ethics, and amount of deviation reduction are presented as relevant parameters for rewarding research, thought, audit, and structural translation as sustainable social functions. This phase theory does not establish universal weights, exchange rates, or one fixed numerical scoring model.

Synchronization Theory provides the phase-alignment function across politics, law, education, economics, and AI infrastructure. It connects autonomous domains to shared reference frequencies such as temporal ethics, structural immunity, origin signatures, and deviation logs. The key is structural resonance rather than control: preventing one domain from accelerating into breakdown while another becomes excessively rigid.

The Structural Civilization Phase is defined by four OS-level conditions: embedded Temporal Ethics T0; permanently operating structural immunity; broad social availability of structural psychology and resonant perception; and functioning synchronization theory across multiple domains. These four conditions are a conditional definition, not a maturity score or ranking system.

The phase is also defined by recursive openness. It is not a society without contradiction. Instead, contradictions and deviations can be diagnosed as temporal-ethical problems, origin-signature failures, or synchronization disorders and then connected to revision of institutional cycles, rules, and translation formats. A structural civilization is therefore an open OS that remains revisable rather than a completed final state.

Origin reference becomes another principle of civilization. The relevant question is not only what a claim says, but which origin signature it connects to, which deviations and revisions it has passed through, and where responsibility remains located. This differs both from authority based solely on who speaks and from a form of relativism that erases lineage and responsibility.

The source explicitly frames the Structural Civilization Phase as a conditional definition rather than a prophecy. It does not claim that the phase will necessarily arrive, nor does it give fixed years for the transition. It proposes a minimum structural condition set for operating society without continuously increasing contradictions and future liabilities.

The integrated audit summary contains threshold θ, observation window δ, phenomenon M, and the symbolic T/S/R triad in a falsification and re-agreement context. The source does not provide universal numerical values or one mandatory formula. Any implementation value must retain source, measurement actor, object, scale, observation window, permitted use, and non-guarantee scope.
