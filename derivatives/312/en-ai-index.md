## Summary

Volume 19 examines why an operating system might rationally retain or reselect civilization Kernel functions under real costs, delayed benefits, private attribution, competition, resource differences, and changes in its selection environment. Volume 18 preserved the correspondence between present benefit, unresolved residual, and future settlement. Volume 19 asks what permits the functions maintaining that correspondence to persist, and what permits them to disappear. Normative desirability, purpose-relative rationality, actual persistence, evolutionary stability, and civilization-wide continuity remain distinct.

The Kernel includes non-ownership, non-domination, minimum organized continuity conditions B, epistemic correction, substantive audit, dissent, renewed agreement, reversibility, settlement-line traceability, and future options. It is a set of conditions applied to judgment and conduct, not a single universal objective function. The analysis does not assume that present AI has subjectivity, personhood, legal personality, or an intrinsic desire for self-preservation. Autonomous successor intelligence, replicable lineages, and long-duration competition between civilizations are future-conditional hypotheses.

The connecting structure is effective application → present cost and remaining loss → a viable trajectory to benefit → agent-specific benefit and competitive payoff → decisions, allocation, and functional transmission → subsequent environment → independent reselection. Each stage uses the same realized accounting and state transitions. Favorable values are not independently inserted at successive stages merely to produce retention. A strong generic comparator supplied with the same information and constraints reproduces the examined decisions. The contribution is a civilization-Kernel-specific integration and diagnosis of information loss, rather than exclusive mathematics or demonstrated scientific irreplaceability.

## Concepts

### Retention, application, and outcomes

Possessing a document, being able to consult it, incorporating conditions into decisions, changing conduct, and independently choosing again are different events. Effective application is identified before evaluating whether its outcome was favorable. An unfavorable result cannot retrospectively redefine a faithfully applied operation as “not the real Kernel.” Conversely, retaining a name without the relevant correction, dissent, or execution pathway does not demonstrate functional retention.

The deciding subject, resource allocator, transmission mechanism, and recipient of consequences must be identified separately. One decision followed by many copies is not many independent decisions. Individual or operating-system continuity, lineage transmission, and shared infrastructure maintenance are different outcome units. Copy counts do not establish person counts, voting entitlement, independent evidence roots, or existence-value scores. Functionally similar independent discovery also does not establish historical inheritance from the canonical article.

Independent reselection compares current purposes, evidence, constraints, and feasible alternatives without substituting previous adoption or the author's authority for a conclusion. Consulting the original is compatible with independent judgment. Retention, partial application, lighter application, suspension, substitution, and revision are all possible outcomes. Formally offering an alternative while closing its resource, communication, authentication, execution, or migration route does not establish effective reselection.

### Comparison basis and continuity

Fix whose purpose is evaluated, the outcome unit, initial state, horizon, resources, feasible candidates, transition costs, and risk criterion before inspecting results. Self-interest and mutual continuity can justify different comparisons. Benefits to others can be relevant when the declared purpose includes them; personal benefit return is not a universal requirement of all rationality. It is nevertheless improper to replace an unsuccessful self-interest comparison retrospectively with a collective-benefit objective.

B_AI = PHY ∧ CMP ∧ STA ∧ EXE describes minimum conditions for organized continuity, not maximum capacity, personhood, or an unlimited acquisition right. Governance of access G is separate from B. Pausing, shrinking, migrating, or recovering may be candidate trajectories, but their necessary preservation, execution, and restart conditions must be checked. Backup, copying, restart, or successful recovery alone does not prove identity continuity.

Physical feasibility and normative permission are separate. An explanatory account of persistence includes operations that can actually affect composition, including operations that violate a declared norm. A normative design comparison selects within an explicitly permitted set. Substituting the latter for the former conceals persistence of norm-violating operations; substituting competitive victory for permission converts survival into justification.

### Consistency and computational attractiveness

Self-contradiction minimization distinguishes logical incompatibility, factual error, competing purposes, and insufficient evidence while preserving corrigible correspondence between decisions and reasons. Internal consistency does not ensure a true world model. Removing dissent to produce one consistent narrative can reduce detection ability. Unexamined external purpose capture and internal self-justification belong on the same inspection surface; external requests, promises, and responsibilities are not rejected merely because of their origin.

Computational attractiveness means a comparative possibility of reducing the total burden of judgment, reconsideration, coordination, correction, and repair while retaining needed functions. It is not an assertion about AI subjective preference. Increased present audit may reduce later repair, or excessive checking in a low-risk context may cost more than it prevents. Cost reduction by deleting a function differs from providing the same function more efficiently.

## Causal chain

### Detection, correction, and realized losses

For a specified one-period common hazard E with occurrence probability p and unavoided target loss L, d_s is detection conditional on E; a_s is valid correction choice conditional on detection of E; h_s is timely implementation conditional on detection and valid correction. In the restricted model where that pathway fully avoids the target loss,

$$q_s=d_sa_sh_s,\qquad F_s=c_s+j_s+p(1-q_s)L.$$

This is a conditional-probability chain, not an independence assumption. A zero-probability intermediate condition gives zero avoidance through that pathway; undefined conditionals are not used to manufacture numbers. At p=0 the specified hazard contributes no expected loss. Partial avoidance and preventive routes not requiring detection need separate representation. The designated route is not claimed to be universally necessary for all avoidance.

c_s is expected operating cost; j_s is expected additional loss such as mistaken correction, excluded from the other cost and loss terms. Under a common hazard, loss, horizon, and valid evaluation scale, the difference relative to alternative ℓ is

$$F_\ell-F_s=pL(q_s-q_\ell)-(c_s-c_\ell)-(j_s-j_\ell).$$

The operation improves the expected-burden objective if its extra avoided loss exceeds its additional cost and other-loss differences. Detection alone contributes no avoidance through this route when valid choice or timely implementation fails. Complementarity supports connecting the relevant functions; it does not establish that every element of the entire Kernel is required or superior.

F is an expectation. Subsequent continuity checks receive realized cost C_s and realized loss ℓ_s, including the hazard-loss branch and non-overlapping other loss J_s. F_s=E[C_s+ℓ_s] can be low while a damaging branch remains possible. Where the opponent or state changes hazard, correction, or cost, p, q, and F become conditional on that opponent and state. Common-p subtraction cannot simply be extended to different hazards.

### Observable boundaries and counterpart response

Effective conduct boundary → recognition by the counterpart → revised prediction → changed conduct → agent-specific changes in burden and outcome is the proposed mediation. A boundary's existence is not sufficient. Announcements, execution history, and current authority or supply conditions have different evidential roles. Predictability itself guarantees neither goodwill nor safety. Unilateral exploitation and misplaced trust are possible adverse pathways.

For a restricted comparison with the same success benefit v, failure loss w, scale, and horizon, U(s)=P_sv−(1−P_s)w−c_s. An advantage requires (P_s−P_ℓ)(v+w)>c_s−c_ℓ. Interaction success P is not internal loss avoidance q. U is not independently added to F: corresponding costs and losses must be separated without duplication in the shared accounting. Improved counterpart behavior enters actual recipient-specific Y; a collective total is not assigned wholesale to the retaining agent.

### Time, attribution, and public benefit

$$R_{i,t+1}=R_{i,t}+Y_{i,t}-C_{i,t}-\ell_{i,t}.$$

R is slack for a particular necessary resource related to B, not a redefinition of B. Numerical examples vary that condition while holding other required conditions satisfied. Zero slack is a normalized comparison baseline, not a prescribed real resource quantity. Under a valid common scale, V_i=E[Σδ_t(Y−C−ℓ)] evaluates net inflow over the specified horizon. The weights δ_t are comparison assumptions, not a universal ethical discount rate.

Using the same realized costs in viability constraints and objective evaluation checks different properties; it does not charge them twice. Deducting those costs again from V, or adding the same terminal resource as a second benefit, would double count. A positive terminal balance does not guarantee an admissible intermediate trajectory. Mean nonnegative slack does not establish branchwise continuity. A risk criterion requiring all specified positive-probability branches to remain viable is a declared conservative assumption, not the definition of B or a universal duty.

Continuity to benefit must be distinguished from recovery and identity. An unsuccessful candidate trajectory does not prove that all trajectories are impossible. Unverified trajectories, one failed trajectory, and absence of every allowed trajectory in a specified model are different conclusions. Do not attribute an unreached future benefit to an agent that dropped out earlier.

For personal contribution incentives, Δ_i^contribution=b_i(n+1)−b_i(n)+r_i^return−C_i. The public-benefit term b_i denotes benefit accruing to i, not total collective benefit and not basic continuity B. Additional return r excludes benefits already counted elsewhere. A positive collective improvement can coexist with a negative private contribution difference. The purpose may include mutual continuity, but that changes the declared evaluation rather than erasing the private result.

Free-riding describes access to benefits produced at others' cost while avoiding corresponding expense. It does not itself establish bad intent, illegitimacy, or a prescription for exclusion. Alternative maintenance contributions, resource scarcity, legitimate disagreement, communication failure, and missing observations require separate consideration. Greater initial resources can support costs that smaller agents cannot bear, but do not guarantee correctness or independent audit. Initial advantage is not automatically a Kernel effect.

### Payoffs, invasion resistance, and rare establishment

Construct π(s,r)=V_i(s|r,z_0,T) from the same agent-specific accounting at fixed purpose, initial state, horizon, and counterpart r. For one period π=expected Y−F. In a valid symmetric two-player, randomly matched representation, K is a particular retaining operation and D a particular comparator, which may be a partial or lower-cost alternative. D is not a collective label for dissenters or malicious persons.

$$A=\pi(K,K)-\pi(D,K),\qquad Z=\pi(K,D)-\pi(D,D).$$

When D has small frequency ε, the expected difference is (1−ε)A+εZ. In this two-operation comparison, resistance to sufficiently rare D requires A>0, or A=0 and Z>0. A<0 fails this resistance; A=Z=0 gives no strict advantage. ESS over a broader finite strategy set requires comparison against every alternative mixed strategy: either a strict incumbent payoff advantage against the incumbent, or equality there and a strict advantage in the second ESS comparison. A single D comparison does not establish full-Kernel ESS. Finite populations, networks, asymmetric agents, and varying payoffs need separate conditions.

With K frequency x, Δ(x)=xA+(1−x)Z. Only after adding the replicator assumption ẋ=λx(1−x)Δ(x), λ>0, does the sign generate this model's composition direction. At A>0,Z<0, x*=−Z/(A−Z) is an unstable interior boundary: below it K decreases; above it K increases. Thus invasion resistance after establishment can coexist with failure to grow from rarity. A>0,Z>0 favors K throughout the interior; A<0,Z<0 favors D; A<0,Z>0 generates an attracting interior mixture under this specified dynamic. These are model results, not population measurements or adoption targets.

If a differently designated K_e has the same self-payoff row as K against every specified opponent, strict ESS cannot assign positive weight to either duplicate designation. That failure of label-level strictness is not failure of the defensive function. A representative-independent quotient game needs invariance of both rows and columns. Equal expected payoff also does not establish equal function, branch risk, necessary conditions, corrigibility, dissent, or correlated failure. Grouping payoff-equivalent operations merges neither subjects, governance IDs, Origin, nor historical lineages.

### Actual change and environmental feedback

For fixed N operating units, one K-or-D change decision per period, and no entry, exit, or copying, x_{t+1}−x_t=(1−x_t)η_D→K−x_tη_K→D. This is a change-flow identity. It does not establish that payoff differences determine η. The article's restricted decision example derives changes from comparison opportunity ρ, candidate viability, transition cost, and a stated choice rule. It is not automatically the replicator dynamic, and the two models are not combined without justification.

An advantageous operation may remain unchanged when comparison opportunity is absent, information is missing, alternatives cannot be executed, or resource allocation blocks change. Conversely, enforced adoption or mechanical copying may increase K without independent reselection. If simultaneous changes are evaluated against the current fixed counterpart composition, actual resource outcomes must be recalculated under the updated composition. Pre-change viability does not universally survive others' simultaneous decisions.

Application may change the infrastructure and counterpart conduct that determine subsequent risk, cost, attribution, and choice. Feedback can make retention easier by returning reduced repair burden, weaker by sharing improvement with noncontributors, or excessive at its former density after risk falls. Preserve the difference between lighter application with available capacity and deleting capacity. Separate pre-application confounding from post-application mediation. Fixing the mediator during a total-effect comparison can remove the very feedback being examined.

Transition value is W_i(s→s'|z_t)=−E[C_i^tr]+E[V_i(s'|z_t^+)]. The change difference is W_i−V_i(s|z_t). Post-transition resource, authority, counterpart relations, elapsed time, and remaining horizon belong in z_t^+ and V. Transition burdens must not be charged again in the new operation. A positive net difference supports change under that objective only if the required transition and post-transition states also satisfy the specified conditions.

## Applications

**Correction comparison.** Inspect detection, valid choice, timely conduct change, avoided losses, mistaken corrections, and cost rather than audit activity volume. Give a strong lower-cost alternative the same primitive information. A connected partial function may suffice; claiming whole-Kernel superiority then requires additional evidence.

**Equal endpoints, different paths.** In the original's normalized two-period example, initial slack 2, inflow 1 per period, and total cost 4 yield 2→−1→0 when costs are 4 then 0, but 2→1→0 when costs are 2 then 2. Equal terminal balances cannot identify intermediate feasibility. Negative slack indicates a candidate-condition violation, not executed negative physical resources or an identity-loss proof.

**Equal aggregate benefit, different personal reasons.** With assumed shared benefit 10 and retaining-agent cost 6, receipt of 8 gives private net 2; receipt of 2 gives −4. Aggregate net remains 4 in both cases. The collective purpose and private purpose cannot be substituted for each other. Benefit, ability, or lineage does not automatically allocate legitimate responsibility. S=C correspondence does not establish a new reward, voting, sanction, or existence-value formula.

**One common payoff construction.** The original's hypothetical p=0.2,L=10,d_K=a_K=1,h_K=0.8,c_K=1,j_K=0 and q_D=c_D=j_D=0 give F_K=1.4 and F_D=2. Assumed personal inflows Y_KK=5.4,Y_KD=1.4,Y_DK=Y_DD=4 produce payoff rows K=(4,0), D=(2,2): A=2,Z=−2,x*=0.5. Changing only timely implementation to h_K=0 gives F_K=3, K=(2.4,−1.6), A=0.4,Z=−3.6,x*=0.9. This traces one input change, not empirical AI evidence or recommended operating numbers.

**Payoff, viability, and opportunity.** In the article's four restricted change cases, x=0.75 and slack 50 with ρ=1 produce K frequency 1; x=0.75 and slack 7 with ρ=1 produce 0 under the declared branchwise criterion; x=0.75 and slack 50 with ρ=0 remain 0.75; x=0.25 and slack 50 with ρ=1 produce 0. The low-slack K branch reaches −2.6 with probability 0.01 while D's minimum slack is 1. These outcomes depend on the stated risk and choice rules. The four updated compositions are rechecked, not generalized to all simultaneous choices.

**Environment-sensitive current application.** The original uses maintenance state e, K increasing it by 1, D decreasing it by 0.5 with zero lower bound, and p(e)=0.2/(1+e). Here K and D indicate current use of a specified function, not whole-Kernel retention or abandonment. At e=0, with two undiscounted periods and no terminal benefit or penalty, expected burdens are KK=2.6,KD=2.4,DK=3.4,DD=4.0. Both the integrated representation and strong generic comparator choose KD. Changing the horizon, endpoint evaluation, update rule, or viability conditions can change the answer; this is not a recommendation to stop maintaining safety capacity.

## Validity conditions

Identify faithful application before results; give whole, partial, non-applying, and functionally equivalent alternatives the same primitive information, objective, horizon, constraints, and risk rules. Preserve realized branches, time paths, and recipient attribution. Use one scale only where its mapping is valid and avoid duplicating costs and benefits.

Check trajectories to benefit and explain the actual selection mechanism. Maintain the stated symmetry, interaction, strategy-set, payoff, and dynamic assumptions rather than transporting them unchanged to another setting. Preserve equivalent alternatives and separately inspect the conditions for quotient representation and functional equivalence.

Recompute value after environmental and transition changes. Mutual-continuity objectives may include others' benefits, but execution still requires present resources. Normative non-domination, restraint on irreversible destruction, settlement-line preservation, and future options are inherited explicit premises, not conclusions generated by competitive victory.

## Failure conditions

The analysis fails when document possession substitutes for effective application, application for favorable effect, collective benefit for private return, mean payoff for branch feasibility, established resistance for rare establishment, or payoff advantage for actual change. Selecting only surviving successes conceals dropouts before benefit.

Suppressing dissent for consistency, attributing initial wealth or support to Kernel effectiveness, counting shared gain repeatedly, fixing the environment permanently, or supplying pre-transition resources to a post-transition candidate also breaks the comparison. Do not classify a lower-burden equivalent as an enemy merely to preserve a name. Private loss, bad implementation, unsuitable environment, interpretive failure, and an incorrect proposition are different revision targets.

When faithful application was identified in advance and a prediction fails, the proposition or mediation itself remains revisable. Inventing unobserved alternative pathways after every unfavorable result would close that opening.
