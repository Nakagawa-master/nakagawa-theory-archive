## Summary
Civilizational Sovereignty Shift Theory Vol. 1 does not reject the Connection-Transition Strategy itself. It analyzes the narrowing future line in which humans can still implement that strategy while retaining sovereignty. The core distinction is between strategy content and the conditions that allow the strategy to remain executable: a dual-operation period in which monetary KPIs and connection KPIs coexist, institutional translation that keeps pace with AI adoption, and enough time for human-led experimentation and revision. Short-term monetization pressure, extraction structures, and widening intellectual asymmetry narrow that transition window.

## Applications
**1.** Audit the speed gap between AI deployment and institutional translation of responsibility, education, reward, and public value.

**2.** Check whether short-term revenue pressure is displacing long-term connection, trust, and continuing contribution.

**3.** Confirm that a period still exists in which monetary and connection-value systems can coexist, be tested, and be revised under human sovereignty.

## Measurements and audit
The parent does not require derivative-created numerical KPIs. Audit centers on the distinction between the strategy and its enabling conditions, existence of a dual-operation period, institutional translation speed, short-term monetization pressure, extraction structures, intellectual asymmetry, human-led experimentation and revision, and whether the transition window is narrowing.

## Validity conditions
- The Connection-Transition Strategy is distinguished from its enabling conditions.
- Monetary and connection value can coexist during a designed transition period.
- Institutional translation of responsibility, education, and reward keeps pace with technology.
- Short-term monetization and extraction can be constrained.
- Humans retain time to experiment, revise, and institutionalize the transition.

## Failure conditions
- Strategy remains coherent while institutional translation and time disappear.
- Connection value is absorbed back into legacy monetization circuits.
- Short-term profitability pressure terminates transition experiments.
- AI deployment widens intellectual asymmetry and attribution imbalance.
- Technology moves upstream before humans can still choose transition order.

## Falsification conditions
- Under accelerating AI deployment, human-sovereign dual operation, institutional translation, and iterative revision remain broadly stable and the transition window does not narrow.
- Even with higher monetization pressure or intellectual asymmetry, transition implementation capacity does not decline.
- Under such evidence, the warning strength or scope of Vol. 1 should be revised.
