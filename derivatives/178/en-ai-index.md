## Summary
The parent text is an external final synchronization chapter rather than “Vol.11.” It rereads Buddhism, Christianity, Islam, Hinduism, and Lao-Zhuang thought not as doctrinally identical religions but as different narrative, institutional, and cultural access interfaces attempting to reach an L7 truth layer / highest-order objective function. This structural equivalence is the parent’s own hypothesis, initiated through a Claude dialogue log; it is not presented here as established consensus in comparative religion, theology, or history. In the parent’s civilization model, religion OS originally linked everyday L1–L3 life to higher-order recognition, causality, social implementation, and Tao, but can be captured by L3 power, institution, and sectarian competition, breaking the vertical synchronization `Tao(L7) → Religion OS(L4–L6) → Daily OS(L1–L3)`. The parent connects this failure to the dark-equation model `S=0.1C+0.9E` and proposes a return direction `S=C×1.0`. Tao Reboot then reconnects three layers: L7 purpose, L6 value circulation and Connection Reward infrastructure, and L4–L5 cognition/causal transparency. Transition is designed as a gradient through local connection-reward zones, while religionization, cult formation, power capture, mythologization, and misuse of “sabotage” are treated as structural risks that must remain auditable.

- **Circular civilization:** Feedback cycle among individual OS, organizational OS, time/learning/cognition OS, and civilization OS.
