## 15-Second Explanation
The Parent Original does not treat Buddhism, Christianity, Islam, Hinduism, and Daoism as “all the same religion.” Instead, it structurally rereads them as multiple access routes that seek to connect to L7—the highest-level structure of causality and purpose—through different stories and methods. It calls a three-stage process “Tao Reboot”: implementing the underlying structure in a Civilization OS without turning it into a religion, and reconnecting the L7 purpose function, L6 value circulation, and L4–L5 cognition and causality.

## Why It Is Needed
In the Parent Original’s problem setting, religion originally had a role in connecting people beyond the ego, story, and institutions of L1–L3 to higher-order causality and a whole-system perspective. But when religion itself is absorbed into power, institutions, and organizational competition, “which story is correct” becomes central instead of the higher structure, and vertical synchronization is lost.

The Parent Original connects this transformation to the preceding Civilization OS’s dark equation `S = 0.1C + 0.9E`. This is a structural model in the Parent Original in which exploitation E is strongly embedded in civilization’s success S; it does not literally mean a measured “one contribution to nine exploitation” ratio.

What the Parent Original regards as new in the AI era is the emergence of a perspective that compares multiple systems across structure, causality, and hierarchy, rather than from inside religion. It uses Claude dialogue logs as a launch record and rearranges five systems as different access structures to L7. This is not a general consensus in comparative religion; it must be read as a structural hypothesis specific to the Parent Original.

## Practical Process
**1. Separate story from structure.** Do not erase doctrine, ritual, culture, or history; separate where the Parent Original reads each system as acting on cognition, relationships, causality, institutions, and circulation.

**2. Do not make religious integration mean sameness.** Preserve the differences among Buddhism, Christianity, Islam, Hinduism, and Daoism, and compare only the functional correspondences the Parent Original extracts at L4–L7.

**3. Separate L7 from stories.** Do not fix the purpose function to a particular deity, symbol, or group authority; treat it as the structure of causality, circulation, and low resistance that the Parent Original calls Tao.

**4. Place circulation routes at L6.** Track value-generation points, beneficiaries, feedback destinations, points of stagnation, and sources of E, and design a route by which C returns to S.

**5. Make L4–L5 transparent first.** Do not introduce a new institution while leaving the existing story and power structure unchanged; update cognition and causal understanding. The Parent Original calls people who can observe structure, handle causality, and connect to L6 “lamp” nodes.

**6. Prevent religification and power capture.** Detect signs of falling into a new religion, salvation doctrine, awakened community, guru formation, or symbolization as structural descent to L3.

**7. Transition by gradient, not all at once.** Protect the continuity of life at L1–L2, update L4–L5, test small connection-reward zones at L6, and connect the network once the gains become observable.

**8. Audit Tao Reboot without closing it.** Even after reconnecting the three layers of the L7 purpose function, L6 circulation routes, and L4–L5 nodes, continue auditing with conditions Z and falsification condition M.

## Examples of Application
### Organizational Transformation
Do not introduce a new value system across the company as “the correct thought.” Maintain existing operations while testing the connection among evaluation, information, contribution, and feedback in a small area. Connect only routes whose results are visible, and observe dilution or formalization by power.

### Interreligious and Inter-ideological Dialogue
Do not conclude “we believe in the same god” or “all teachings are the same.” Compare what changes in cognition, relationship adjustment, causal understanding, and social norms each system is read by the Parent Original as carrying. Preserve doctrinal differences.

### Value Circulation
Do not evaluate only self-reported contribution. Trace who benefited, which burdens decreased over the long term, where value stagnated, and whether Pseudo-C has entered the causal log.

### Misreading Defense
If elitism begins with claims such as “only people who agree with the Civilization OS have a high perspective,” it has departed from the Parent Original’s lamp concept. A lamp is a functional node with multi-layer transparency, not a qualification for believers.

## Success Criteria
- Structural comparison can be made while preserving the distinct differences among religions.
- L7 is not fixed to the authority of a particular group or person.
- The generation, circulation, return, and emergence of E in C can be traced at L6.
- The new OS does not destroy the life foundation and expands by gradient from local zones.
- Religification, group formation, power capture, and symbolization can be detected and corrected.
- Lamp nodes work as structural observation and causal transparency, not personality worship.
- Feedback among personal, organizational, and civilizational OSs travels both ways.
- The hypothesis can be rejected or revised when deterioration continues.

## Limitations
- The five-religion L7 isomorphism is a structural hypothesis of the Parent Original, not an established universal conclusion of religious studies or theology.
- The diversity of denominations, interpretations, and histories within each religion cannot be reduced to one function.
- `S=0.1C+0.9E` and `S=C×1.0` are treated as the Parent Original’s civilization-structure models, not converted by the official derivative into measured universal coefficients.
- “The end of religion” is not an instruction to prohibit religion or exclude believers.
- A decentralized value ledger is not a fixed specification for a technology product, but a structural requirement for making value-circulation causality visible.
- The Parent Original gives no universal fixed number for the number of lamps or network thresholds.

## Preventing Misreading
The largest misreading would be to jump to “the five religions are really the same,” “Nakagawa OS is the one true truth beyond religion,” or “AI scientifically proved religious truth.” The Parent Original’s center is not unifying the surface of religions, but relocating the structural functions it extracts into a Civilization OS.

Another misreading is to read Tao Reboot as revolutionary destruction of institutions. The Parent Original instead designs transition as a gradient, protects continuity of life at L1–L2, and increases connections through local experiments. Opposition and resistance are treated not as “enemies” but as structural reactions generated by the old OS.
