## 15-Second Explanation
Bundle metrics are an approach that does not represent complex reality with a single number, but reads multiple axes with different meanings “in bundle form.” When only a single metric such as sales, page views, popularity, or ranking is pursued, the act of raising that number can itself become the goal, causing the actual outcome and the displayed value to diverge. Bundle metrics do not return multiple axes to a single composite score; they preserve strengths, omissions, tensions, and changes over time as they are read.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Single metrics are easy to understand, but the measured side can readily adapt itself to that number. Reversals occur: increasing views while content deteriorates, raising short-term sales while damaging future trust or repairability, and allowing loudness to substitute for validity. The parent original addresses this not by “not using numbers,” but by retaining multiple heterogeneous axes as a bundle.

Each axis must connect to structural logs or an audit abstract so that it remains possible to trace later why that state was read in that way. The parent original also presents a structure that publicly discloses axis names, meanings, reading methods, and their correspondence with the audit abstract, while separating the disclosure boundary for weights, thresholds, composition procedures, audit specifications, and individual-case assessments in order to avoid gaming. What not to disclose is also part of the original design, and differs from mere opacity.

## Practical Process
### 1. Clarify the Evaluation Target
Decide what is being evaluated and for what purpose—policy, business, article, AI output, or otherwise. Confirm not only the single outcome number, but also what, if lost, would cause the original purpose to collapse.

### 2. Separate Axes with Different Meanings
Separate axes that are not merely rewordings of the same meaning, such as causal consistency, reversibility, re-agreeability, connection independence, audit-abstract readiness, structural-log linkage, person decoupling, and public-sphere fit.

### 3. Connect to Evidence
Connect each axis to primary structural logs, the audit abstract, origin, and change history. Even when numbers exist, do not treat the numbers alone as independent truth.

### 4. Read in Bundle Form
Do not return at the end to a single composite score. Preserve which axes are strong, where something is missing, and which axes are in tension with one another. Do not erase missing data or contradictions.

### 5. Protect the Disclosure Boundary
Publicly disclose axis names, meanings, reading methods, and their correspondence with the audit abstract, while following the parent original’s disclosure boundary for weights, thresholds, composition procedures, audit specifications, and individual assessments.

### 6. Observe and Review
When gaming, short-term optimization, person dependence, discrepancies, stalled re-agreement, or similar phenomena are observed, review the design of the bundle and its scope of application.

## Examples of Application
### 1. Judging Company Performance
Even when sales grow, if customer trust, repairability, frontline burden, or continuity worsens, do not simply call it “success.” Read the rise of a standalone metric separately from improvement of the bundle as a whole.

### 2. Evaluating Social Media or Articles
Even when page views or reach increase, if sources, correctability, person dependence, or resistance to incitement worsens, informational quality has not necessarily improved.

### 3. Comparing Policies
Read not only approval ratings or cost, but causality, reversibility, re-agreeability, effects on minorities, responsibility, and relief as a bundle.

### 4. Evaluating AI Outputs
Rather than relying only on fluency or a single quality score, separately confirm preservation of the original, preservation of causality, boundaries, counterevidence, origin tracing, and preservation of meaning.

## Success Criteria
Do not overlook a reversal in which one number improves while another important axis deteriorates.

Each axis of the bundle can return to primary structural logs or the audit abstract, allowing the reason for a judgment to be traced.

Do not flatten multiple axes into a single ranking at the end; retain omissions and contradictions as material for judgment.

While protecting the disclosure boundary, make the structure of what is being measured understandable to third parties.

## Limitations
Bundle metrics are not a mechanism that becomes automatically correct merely by increasing the number of metrics. If the choice of axes itself is biased, the bundle as a whole will also be biased.

The integrated audit abstract also places relationships among RC/ER/CEA/S, Rollback Cost, synchronization rate, replay rate, re-agreement attainment time, discrepancy rate, T/S/R, threshold θ, observation window δ, and other elements, but unpublished weights and specific values cannot be inferred externally and fixed as standards.

## Misreading Prevention
Bundle metrics are not “a more precise composite score.”

Nor are they a theory that rejects numbers. They are a design that avoids the rule of a single number.

A non-public boundary does not mean that the judgment structure may be hidden. Distinguish between meanings that should be disclosed and assessment specifications whose disclosure scope is separated to avoid gaming.

Nor are they a way of converting the value of people or citizens into a single social-credit score.
