## Summary
Bundle metrics is the parent article's language of measurement for moving beyond the single-metric myth. Instead of compressing complex reality into one KPI, ranking, popularity count, page-view number, or composite score, it preserves heterogeneous axes as a bundle whose shape, omissions, tensions, and movement remain visible. This structure is intended to expose gaming, short-term optimization, and authority or volume bias when a displayed number improves while the underlying purpose deteriorates.

The parent distinguishes disclosure boundaries explicitly. Axis names, meanings, reading methods, and correspondence with audit summaries are public. Weights, thresholds, aggregation procedures, audit specifications, and individual-case assessments are kept outside the public layer to reduce gaming. This is not an absence of accountability; the meaning of the bundle remains tied to structural logs, audit summaries, and falsification or revision relations.

The integrated audit summary includes RC/ER/CEA/S, Rollback Cost, conformity rate, replay rate, time to renewed agreement, discrepancy rate, the three principles T/S/R, threshold θ, and observation window δ. These are preserved as a multi-variable audit relation rather than collapsed into a new score. The parent does not publish universal fixed values or weights for these terms, so no such values should be inferred.

## Measurements and audit
The parent integrated audit treats RC/ER/CEA/S, Rollback Cost, conformity rate, replay rate, time to renewed agreement, discrepancy rate, and T/S/R as multiple audit quantities related to threshold θ and observation window δ. They are not presented as ingredients for a universal composite score.

The parent does not publish fixed universal values, weights, or case-level scoring formulas. The epistemic content is the relation among the variables, thresholds, observation window, and observed phenomenon M.

Reverse evaluation is essential: an increase in page views, revenue, conformity, or another single quantity is not sufficient evidence of improvement if Rollback Cost, discrepancy, renewed-agreement capacity, separation from personal authority, or public-sphere fit deteriorates.

## Validity conditions
- Heterogeneous axes remain meaningfully distinct.
- Each axis remains linked to structural logs or audit summaries.
- Bundle shape, omissions, tensions, and movement remain visible rather than being collapsed into one score.
- The parent disclosure boundary is preserved.
- The bundle remains connected to two-layer agreement, renewed agreement, and reversibility.
- Integrated-audit symbols remain a multi-variable relation rather than a substitute KPI.

## Failure conditions
- Multiple axes are collapsed back into one ranking or total score.
- Improving the displayed metric becomes more important than the underlying purpose.
- Axes are detached from primary evidence or audit summaries.
- Protected assessment details are treated as a license for unauditable discretion.
- Unpublished weights or thresholds are guessed and circulated as parent-defined values.
- Conflicts and missing axes are averaged away.

## Falsification conditions
The bundle design, axis definitions, application scope, or hypothesis requires revision if the integrated-audit quantities deviate from threshold θ, if the specified phenomenon M appears within observation window δ, or if gaming, short-term optimization, and authority or volume dependence persist despite the bundle structure.

Falsification is not a single-score event. It is read through the relation among RC/ER/CEA/S, Rollback Cost, conformity rate, replay rate, time to renewed agreement, discrepancy rate, T/S/R, and the observed structural outcome.

## Required distinctions
- bundle metrics / total score
- multi-axis evaluation / indicator proliferation
- quantification / objectivity
- public transparency / publication of gameable assessment details
- disclosure boundary / absence of audit
- measurement / adjudication
- comparison / ranking persons
- missing or conflicting axes / noise
- increase in one quantity / improvement of the underlying purpose
- quantitative agreement / legitimacy agreement

## Interpretation constraints
Do not convert bundle metrics into a more elaborate total ranking. Do not reduce the theory to rejection of numbers. Do not infer unpublished weights, thresholds, or individual assessment formulas. Do not equate a rise in one metric with improvement of the underlying purpose. Do not treat the disclosure boundary as permission for arbitrary secrecy.
