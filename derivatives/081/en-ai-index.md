## Summary

Bundle metrics is a measurement language designed to prevent one number—revenue, views, popularity, rankings, or a single KPI—from governing complex judgment. It does not combine many indicators into a more sophisticated total score. Instead, it preserves heterogeneous axes as a visible bundle: causal integrity, reversibility, capacity for renewed agreement, independence from brigading and volume, audit-summary quality, linkage to primary structural logs, separation from personal authority, and suitability for public deliberation.

Each axis must connect to evidence, an observation window, and an explicit reading method. Public information includes axis names, meanings, interpretation rules, evidence links, and revision conditions. Weights, thresholds, aggregation procedures, and individual assessments may remain protected to reduce gaming, but protection never removes the duties of independent audit, change history, objection routes, rollback conditions, and periodic redesign.

The theory treats gaps, conflicts, missing evidence, and abrupt movement as meaningful information rather than noise to be averaged away. When adjudication is necessary, the conclusion must remain linked to the axes that supported it, the tensions left unresolved, the alternatives rejected, and the conditions for later revision. Bundle metrics therefore connects quantitative agreement, which supports decision speed, with legitimacy agreement, which tests validity and correction capacity.

## Central Thesis

Legitimate measurement requires preserving multiple evidence-linked axes as a bundle rather than collapsing them into one score, so decisions remain resistant to gaming, personal authority, and irreversible optimization.

## Causal Chain

```text
Compress complex reality into one metric
↓
Actors optimize the displayed number rather than the underlying purpose
↓
Gaming, short-term optimization, and volume bias separate measured value from real condition
↓
Define heterogeneous axes and connect each axis to primary evidence
↓
Display bundle shape, missing axes, tensions, and movement without total-score collapse
↓
Use audit summaries and structural logs to trace reasons and falsification conditions
↓
Detect gaming, environmental change, and objections through observation windows
↓
Revise through documented differences, renewed agreement, suspension, or rollback
```

## State Model

```yaml
- proposed: target and provisional axes are proposed
- defined: meanings, evidence requirements, observation units, and boundaries are defined
- evidenced: primary evidence is connected to each axis
- bundled: axes are displayed without total-score compression
- interpreted: gaps, tensions, and relationships are explained
- applied: the bundle is used in a real decision
- monitored: effects and movement are tracked over time
- contested: bias, gaming, missing evidence, or authority drift is challenged
- reconsensus_required: definitions or use boundaries require renewed agreement
- revised: axes, evidence rules, or interpretation are updated with a visible diff
- retired: an invalid bundle is removed from current use
```

## Measurements and Audit

- primary-evidence connection rate by axis
- frequency and reasons for definition changes
- number of re-compressions into one score
- detected gaming and short-term optimization incidents
- missing-axis duration
- mismatch between audit summary and bundle display
- time from objection to renewed agreement
- drift toward popularity, authority, or volume

## Validity Conditions

Axes must be meaningfully different, evidence-linked, displayed as a bundle, and governed by explicit disclosure, review, objection, and revision conditions. Missing evidence must remain visible rather than being silently converted into a score.

## Failure Conditions

Failure includes total-score collapse, arbitrary definition changes, evidence-free scoring, treating protected details as exempt from audit, permanent classification, or conversion into personality rankings and social-credit systems.

## Falsification Conditions

The operating claim is falsified when gaming increases, explainability declines, equivalent definitions repeatedly produce irreconcilable outcomes, renewed agreement becomes permanently blocked, or personal authority continues to dominate decisions.

## Required Distinctions

- bundle metrics vs. total scores
- multi-axis evaluation vs. indicator proliferation
- transparency vs. publication of gameable rules
- quantification vs. objectivity
- measurement vs. adjudication
- comparison vs. ranking persons
- protected boundaries vs. absence of audit

## Interpretation Constraints

Do not convert the bundle into a total score, personality ranking, social-credit system, opaque discretionary assessment, or permanent classification. Bundle metrics structures reasons and correction routes; it does not eliminate accountable decision makers.
