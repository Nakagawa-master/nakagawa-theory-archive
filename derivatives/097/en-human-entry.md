## 15-Second Explanation
Co-creation is not made merely by adding meetings or persuasion. When the beats of principles, actions, and records align, and experiences of repairing deviations on the same premises accumulate, a silent consensus arises: “the same consistency will come next.” When that consensus moves into the terrain of future decisions, relationships self-generate through correspondence rather than coercion. This is the parent original’s central idea.

## Why Is It Needed?
When a relationship is unstable, simply increasing explanation, contact frequency, meetings, persuasion, or compromise can conceal beat mismatches and contradictory premises. The parent original understands relationship strength not by how much agreement exists, but by whether principles, actions, and records repeat in the same phase; whether repair after deviation is observable; and whether premises can avoid being conveniently replaced even when a counterexample is presented.

Correspondence has three principles. Synchronization on the time axis means sharing a standard beat without violating each other’s sense of time. Ethical inviolability means not changing premises for convenience. The margin of existence means treating silence, distance, deferral, and taking matters away not as relational absence but as structures for autonomy. Standing on the same terrain is different from following the same conclusion; non-coercive resonance exists only when phase alignment and diversity of trajectories coexist.

The parent original also places “the freedom to enter a relationship” and “the freedom to leave” together. When beats do not align, it is ethical not to force the frequency upward, and increased duration or contact frequency is not itself success. Critical observation and counterexamples are not excluded, but converted into material for repair.

## Practical Flow
**1. Confirm the standard beat.** Share what the relationship is meant to protect and the standard beat of updates, responses, and reviews; do not change premises for convenience each time.

**2. Leave public traces.** Preserve not only the consistency of principles, actions, and records, but also deviation and repair histories in a form third parties can follow. Prioritize few omissions over appearance or volume.

**3. Institutionalize margin.** Do not treat silence, deferral, distance, or taking matters away as failure; do not demand immediate answers or agreement. Make both continued participation and departure genuinely selectable.

**4. Adjust the relationship through abstract forms.** Use long-wave fixed/short-wave variable, a temperature gradient, and horizontal linkage of traces so that short-term trials can change without losing the long-term standard beat.

**5. Test with counterexamples.** Do not treat phase stability, RC, CEA, LPI, withdrawal success rate, and similar measures as a single success score; check whether synchronization pressure, overheating, or harmful lock-in is also increasing. Do not add numerical values to thresholds θ or observation window δ when the original gives none.

## Examples of Application
**Meetings and collaboration.** Before increasing meeting count, keep the entry, ending, deferral, and handling of homework consistent, and see whether the next session can restart from the same premises.

**Co-production.** Horizontally link traces so the same premises can be referenced even when people in charge change. Prioritize whether the consistency of principles, actions, and records is inherited over individual compatibility.

**Long-term relationships.** Fix the long-wave standard beat and flexibly change short-wave frequency or form rather than increasing contact frequency. Do not immediately declare a relationship a failure when distance appears.

**Phased transition.** When moving to a new phase, make the shift observable through small changes in intervals, temperature, and endings, rather than strong declarations or increased frequency.

## Success Criteria
Success is not judged only by increased agreement or contact. It is important that third parties can follow the standard beat, deviation, and repair from public traces; that premises are not replaced when counterexamples appear; and that different trajectories can remain on the same terrain.

If phase stability rises while dissent or departure falls, this may be synchronization pressure rather than correspondence. Even a high withdrawal success rate does not ensure health if harmful lock-in or participation pressure exists. Fast responses weaken non-coercion if margin disappears. Reversal evaluation is necessary; do not directly equate improvement in a single indicator with success.

## Limits
Correspondence is not a psychological technique for naturally moving another person. Individual dialogue logs, emotional manipulation procedures, and person-specific methods are not brought into the public structure; what is handled is the structure that serves as the relationship’s container.

Silence is also not a justification for refusing explanation or hiding information. Premises, traces, and repairs that should be public are made observable, while margin remains to protect individual subjectivity and distance. General legal or safety systems not present in the original are not added as requirements unique to this theory.

## Preventing Misreading
- Correspondence is not conformity.
- Silent consensus is not blind faith.
- Phase synchronization is not uniform behavior.
- The margin of existence is not abandonment of explanation.
- Relationship continuity is not dependence.
- Both the freedom to enter and the freedom to leave are necessary.
- Correspondence is not judged by contact frequency, agreement rate, or duration alone.
