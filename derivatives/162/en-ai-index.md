## Summary
The parent original audits historical “success” as a civilization-level evaluation function and reports that multiple AI systems, including GPT, Gemini, and Claude, independently converged on `S = 0.1C + 0.9E`. Here S denotes states socially treated as success such as wealth, power, influence, and fame; C denotes substantive value creation through invention, production, research, creation, or labor; E denotes structural value capture through information asymmetry, intermediation extraction, structural imbalance, vested interests, dominating contracts, hidden coercion, and related mechanisms. The parent strongly describes this equation as a statistical observation, a specification of the legacy civilization OS, and a civilizational “physical law.” However, the parent body does not publish the external dataset, sampling design, regression specification, estimation procedure, confidence interval, or error range. Therefore 0.1 and 0.9 must retain their status as coefficients in the parent-reported civilizational audit model, not be converted into a headcount ratio or silently promoted into an independently replicated natural-science constant. The parent further argues that AI trained on historical success can reimplement an E-dominant evaluation function at high speed and scale. As a replacement, it presents `S=C×1.0` with an exploitation coefficient of zero as a normative/design evaluation function, supported by NCL-α, Temporal Ethics T0, Connection Reward, and the Deviation Ledger.

## Concepts
- S: socially recognized success states including wealth, power, influence, and fame.
- C: substantive value generation through invention, production, research, creation, labor, and similar contribution.
- E: structural value capture through information asymmetry, extraction, imbalance, vested interests, dominating contracts, hidden coercion, and similar mechanisms.
- Dark Equation: `S=0.1C+0.9E`, the parent’s legacy-civilization success function.
- 0.1 / 0.9: parent-reported model coefficients; not a headcount ratio.
- New Civilization Equation: `S=C×1.0` with exploitation coefficient 0, a design target rather than a current empirical ratio.
- AI reimplementation risk: risk that optimization over historical success reproduces E-dominant strategies.
- NCL-α: origin/difference tracking substrate.

- Connection Reward: mechanism intended to reconnect generated value with causal contributors.
- Deviation Ledger: audit mechanism for recording structural value capture, responsibility breaks, and future debt.
