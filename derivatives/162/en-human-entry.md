## 15-Second Explanation
The parent original audits what has produced “success” in human history as the evaluation function of a civilization OS, expressing the old formula as `S=0.1C+0.9E` and the target rewrite as `S=C×1.0`. The 0.1/0.9 are model coefficients presented by the parent original, not the population shares of successful people. If AI learns the old formula, structures that capture value may be rapidly reimplemented; the central subject is reconnecting contribution and success through NCL-α, temporal ethics T0, connection rewards, and the deviation ledger.

## Why It Is Necessary
Society often records only outcomes such as profit, power, influence, and fame as “success.” If it learns without separating the value creation C that produced those outcomes from value capture E through information asymmetry, skimming, structural imbalance, and similar mechanisms, capturing value may remain a more efficient strategy than creating it.

In the AI era, this history becomes learning data rather than merely past injustice. If AI optimizes historical success, it may rapidly and at scale reimplement the evaluation function of the old civilization OS. The original therefore recognizes a need to update the definition of success itself.

## Practical Process
1. First define S. State what is being measured as success: wealth, profit, power, influence, fame, and so on.
2. Separate C. Identify who produced which value through invention, production, research, creation, labor, and other means.
3. Separate E. Observe value capture through information gaps, contract gaps, scale differences, skimming, vested interests, hidden coercion, and similar mechanisms.
4. Compare the causality of outcome S with C/E. Do not learn from the outcome alone as success.
5. Audit whether, when AI learns historical S, it is more likely to choose C or E as the efficient strategy.
6. Use T0 to see whether future debt is accumulating behind short-term results.
7. Track value-generation points and reward attribution through NCL-α and connection rewards.
8. Record structural deviations in the deviation ledger rather than condemning individuals.
9. Implement `S=C×1.0` as a design function, not a measured value, and measure improvement.
10. Keep the coefficients, scope of application, and transition hypothesis revisable through `θ`, `δ`, and `M`.

## Examples of Application
**Businesses.** Before treating increased profit as success, separate the value generated by workers, business partners, inventors, data providers, and others from the subjects that collected that value. Even if profit rises, increased value capture, responsibility asymmetry, and future debt do not constitute civilizational success under the original’s reversed evaluation.

**AI evaluation.** Do not use only past high revenue, high status, or high growth as correct labels; separate whether their causes were value creation or value capture. If the old history teaches E-dominance, revise the evaluation function.

**Research and creation.** When the origins of people or organizations that created value disappear and only later capturing subjects receive success, treat it as a break between C and S. Use origin signatures as a basis for causal tracing, not as a monopoly.

**Public systems.** If short-term efficiency improvements carry future debt or loss of public value, do not judge success from short-term results alone. Review long-term consistency through T0 at the same time.

## Success Criteria
- S, C, and E are observed separately.
- The causality between value generators and value-collecting subjects can be traced.
- It can be checked whether AI is learning E-dominant strategies from old success histories.
- Future debt is visible through T0.
- The connection between C and reward attribution improves through connection rewards and origin tracing.
- The deviation ledger is used to correct structural deviations.
- Consistency between contribution and success improves after implementing `S=C×1.0`.
- Falsification conditions are public, and both the old and new formulas remain revisable.

Under the reversed evaluation, increased wealth, profit, power, or influence is not success if absorption from value generators, future debt, origin loss, or responsibility asymmetry also increases. Conversely, a decline in short-term profit rates or value-capture rates is not itself failure if value attribution to contributors, origin tracing, future-debt reduction, and public value improve.

## Limits
The parent original describes `0.1` and `0.9` as coefficients of a success function obtained through historical analysis and regression analysis by multiple AIs. They are not population ratios, nor values independently re-estimated by this derivative. The parent text does not provide an external dataset, sampling design, regression specification, confidence interval, or margin of error; that information state must also be retained.

`1.0` and exploitation coefficient 0 are not historical measured coefficients of the same kind as the old formula. They are design goals of a new civilization OS that aligns contribution and success, and do not automatically guarantee zero exploitation merely by being introduced.

Methods for measuring S/C/E must be defined for each subject, and a single formula may not fully explain specific historical people or organizations. Limiting E to malice or crime would lose the original’s structural definition. The new 1.0/0 formula is a design hypothesis requiring implementation, observation, and revision; no universal fixed values outside the parent original may be added to `θ`, `δ`, or `M`.

## Misreading Prevention
- Do not turn 90% into the population share of successful people.
- Do not turn “exploitation” into condemnation of a person’s character.
- Do not automatically equate the original’s strong phrase “physical law” with an independently replicated natural-science constant.
- Do not turn `S=C×1.0` into a current measured value or profit guarantee.
- Do not turn origin signatures into knowledge monopolies or the deviation ledger into a punishment register.
- Do not reduce the AI crisis to malice in an AI personality.
