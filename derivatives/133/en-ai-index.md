## Summary
Human-bear coexistence is modeled through habitat, food availability, human attractants, population dynamics, prevention, justified capture, post-capture resource use and local management capacity. Bear meat is not presented as a universal solution or a reason to increase capture. Where capture is independently justified, safe and lawful resource circulation may reduce waste and support local management, but commercial demand must not create capture pressure.

## Validity conditions

## Failure conditions

## Falsification conditions
If circulation does not reduce waste or improve local capacity and instead increases overcapture or ecological harm, weaken the framework.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not automatically promote hunting or consumption. Follow wildlife, food-safety and local law. Keep capture decisions independent from sales incentives.
