## 15-Second Explanation
The real estate market becomes murky not because participants have many titles, but because **roles become mixed and responsibility disappears**. Separate sellers, purchasers, investors, operators, and brokers by function, and even put them into separate modes when the same company holds multiple roles. Treat purchasers as protected parties, investors and operators as market drivers, and brokers as safety coordinators, and separate viewing rights from offer rights. Do not prohibit profit; convert transparency, performance, explanation quality, and records into trust and keep the market moving.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
No matter how much information is made visible, decision-making does not become sound if it is unclear from whose position the number is being presented. Mixing the broker’s face with the pursuit of acquisition profit, treating purchasers and investors at the same protection level, or unifying viewing and offer rights creates exclusive dealing, lowballing, and the disappearance of accountability for explanations.

Vol.5 separates participants by function, authority, duty, and evaluation axis rather than judging them as good or bad. It does not prohibit multiple occupations; it assumes the reality that the same actor may hold several roles, switches modes for each role, and leaves records.

## Practical Process
1. Identify transaction participants by function, not title.
2. State whether the mode is seller, purchaser, investor, operator, or broker.
3. When one actor has multiple roles, separate Permission / Obligation / Evaluation / Log by mode.
4. In broker mode, make brokerage, safety, performance, coordination, and records the primary duties.
5. In operator and investor modes, display profit-and-loss responsibility, business structure, funds, exit, and feasibility of performance.
6. In purchaser mode, secure access to numbers and risks, presentation of conditions, and expert consultation as the minimum line.
7. Separate viewing rights and offer rights.
8. Attach conditions, proof of funds, performance records, and so on to offers.
9. Place professional specifications in a separate lane from the purchaser UI, and make logs and explanation duties heavier as functions become stronger.
10. Move price negotiation from an information war to Consensus based on shared premises, responsibility, and business structure.
11. Continuously audit explanation quality, performance rate, dispute rate, deadline compliance, exclusive dealing and offer abuse, purchaser understanding, and so on.

## Examples of Application
**Brokerage plus buy-and-resell company.** Even for the same company, show a separate mode: safety coordinator when brokering, profit-and-loss actor when buying. Do not hide acquisition terms by using brokerage trust.

**Individual investor.** Do not treat someone as a protected party merely because they are an individual; if they have the function of making profit from properties, treat them on the operator side. Along with viewing and offer permissions, require records of funds, performance, and explanations.

**Purchaser for own use.** Create a state in which the purchaser can compare not only price but also taxes, repairs, disasters, regulations, rights, building condition, and so on, and can consult while including the decision not to buy.

**Professional specifications.** Add advanced functions such as construction costs, business plans, cash flow, and rental management to a separate lane; do not mix them into the purchaser UI.

## Success Criteria
- Participants are identified by function.
- Multiple roles of the same actor are put into separate modes.
- The responsibilities of brokerage, business, investment, and purchasing are not mixed.
- The purchaser’s rights to view, make offers, and consult are secured.
- Investors’ and operators’ permissions carry records and explanation duties.
- Viewing rights and offer rights are separated.
- The basis for a price can be explained as a business structure.
- Performance records and transparency are converted into trust.
- Disputes, exclusive dealing, abuse, and lack of understanding can be observed and returned to revision.

## Limitations
The original handles the wiring of participants, permissions, and incentives. Defenses against backlash, surveillance, exposure, partial abuse, and abuse of responsibility for AI explanations are sent to Vol.6’s Ethical Shield. Nor does this functional classification itself replace legal occupational categories or legal judgments in individual cases.

## Preventing Misreading
- This is not an argument for excluding investors. Investors are designed as major market drivers.
- This is not an argument against operator profits. It makes the basis for profit explainable.
- Purifying the broker role is not itself a ban on multiple occupations; it means separate modes and separated responsibility.
- Do not confuse purchaser protection with expert pathways. Expert pathways are open to all participants.
- Professional specifications are not privilege; they are a design that isolates advanced functions from the purchaser UI.
- The expanded terms for T/S/R cannot be fixed from the body of the original, so do not add them arbitrarily.
