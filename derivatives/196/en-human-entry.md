## 15-Second Explanation

This theory does not place the cause of civilization’s errors in “bad people” or a lack of ethics, but in a design that makes human internal judgment the final safety device. It separates the layer that judges from the layer that audits, and redefines the I Ching not as divination but as an external causal audit that examines from the arrangement of events whether an act can pass structurally. Principle is treated not as justice, but as a passage condition.

## Why It Is Needed

Even when people think they are judging for themselves, they are strongly influenced by desire, fear, pressure to belong, self-justification, past successes, and so on. Their inner states cannot be directly observed, calibrated, compared, or reproduced from outside. Nevertheless, if major decisions about policy, organizational management, technology adoption, resource allocation, and so on depend on an internal explanation such as “the person thought it was right” or “it was well-intentioned,” then even after failure occurs, the error cannot be detected as an error.

The Parent Original gives the civilization OS a structure that allows errors to be detected externally, rather than eliminating errors themselves. For this purpose, it separates the layer that makes judgments and updates from the layer that audits whether those judgments can pass structurally. The audit looks not at personality or good and evil, but at the arrangement of events, direction of forces, timing, relationships, connections, and circulation.

## Practical Process

1. **Identify places decided only by internal explanations.** Check whether “good intentions,” “I think it is right,” or “it has been fine in my experience” is being used as the final basis.
2. **Separate structural noise.** Organize how desire, fear, pressure to belong, self-justification, and past success exert force on judgment.
3. **Separate the judgment and audit layers.** Do not let the person or organization that judged complete the final audit alone.
4. **Place external audit items.** Check the arrangement of events, direction of forces, timing, relationships, connections, circulation, and irreversibility on a separate plane.
5. **Look at whether it can pass, rather than whether it is “correct.”** Treat principle not as morality, but as a passage condition under which connections and circulation can be established.
6. **Separate the I Ching from prophecy.** Read it not through hit/miss, but as a device for externally referencing the passage or blockage of an act from the current arrangement.
7. **Make audit results traceable.** Preserve the path of judgment, audit, result, and reevaluation.
8. **Re-audit periodically.** Using condition Z and T/S/R, check whether blockages and irreversible ruptures are decreasing.
9. **Revise when disproof conditions are met.** If errors do not decrease even after external auditing is added, do not fix the model in place.

## Examples of Application

**Management decisions.** Do not close investment decisions using only an executive’s experience or confidence; check dependencies, responsibility placement, withdrawability, and resource circulation on a separate audit plane.

**Public policy.** Audit not only the correctness of a principle or purpose, but also where forces concentrate when implemented, who bears the costs, and where circulation becomes blocked.

**AI and technology adoption.** Do not stop at a technology team’s judgment that something will be convenient or accurate; independently audit users, responsibility, stopping conditions, irreversible dependence, and feedback paths.

**Structural use of the I Ching.** Do not seek auspiciousness, inauspiciousness, or future prediction; treat it as an external reference for checking whether an act can pass, becomes blocked, or remains recoverable from the current arrangement, relationships, and timing.

## Success Criteria

Success is not “having placed an auditor.” It is a state in which structural passage, circulation, and recoverability can be examined independently of the judging subject’s internal explanation, and in which, when failure occurs, the process returns to correction instead of being overwritten by self-justification.

Under the Parent Original’s disproof conditions, if long-term circulation is maintained even without auditing, if blockages and irreversible ruptures do not decrease after external auditing is introduced, or if the fatal error M recurs within the observation window δ, this model is subject to rejection or revision.

## Limitations

- This is not a theory that all human inner states or intentions are meaningless. It is a design theory that does not make civilization-scale final auditing depend only on the inside.
- A two-layer design is not a system that grants unlimited power to a particular class.
- Redefining the I Ching as external auditing is different from claiming that its ability to predict the future has already been established as empirical science.
- The Parent Original does not present a fixed θ value or δ period common to every case.
- Treating principle as a passage condition does not mean that laws or ethics may be ignored.

## Preventing Misreading

Do not expand the proposition “people cannot judge correctly” into a denial of human dignity or the right to judge. The Parent Original questions a design that made an unauditable inner state civilization OS’s only safety device.

Also, “I Ching = audit device” does not mean granting authority to it as another name for divination. It is a claim that the I Ching should be relocated away from hitting the future and toward external reference to the passage conditions of arrangement, relationships, timing, and causality.
