## 1. Summary
The parent original redefines market credit through temporal ethics: not simply past performance, speed, or short-term efficiency, but the observed expectation that future coherence will continue. When principle, action, and record repeat in phase as structural rhythm, remain externally observable, and survive counterexamples, observers form provisional-completion recognition—an expectation that the same coherence will recur. This converts future coherence into present structural credit capital.

Credit design is described through cadence, tone, margin, and traceability, plus three observational patterns: fixed long-wave/flexible short-wave, tone gradient, and horizontal linkage of traces. The source explicitly says these are attitudes rather than KPIs and excludes specific financial products, investment advice, internal logic, concrete thresholds, and leverage operation.

## 5. Applications
Maintain a visible long-wave publication/review cadence while allowing short-wave events to vary; introduce new domains through a sequence of opening, normal state, and margin rather than abrupt heat; connect records across media, personnel, and projects under the same premises. The purpose is observability of future coherence, not price control.

## 6. Measurements and audit
The integrated audit uses deviation in connection reward, credit half-life, RC, CEA, phase stability S, withdrawal success rate, threshold θ, and observation window δ. The hypothesis is revised when those relations breach their audit conditions or δ reveals credit spinning or harmful lock-in. The parent gives no concrete values for θ or δ.

## 7. Validity conditions
Principle, action, and record repeat in phase; cadence is visible to third parties; premises and traces survive changes in personnel/media; repair after failure preserves the rhythm; counterexamples are recorded rather than hidden; future-coherence expectations can be reconstructed from durable traces.

## 8. Failure conditions
Short-term monetization pressure destroys the baseline cadence; large tone swings produce contradictory signals; excessive explanation removes margin; outcome display replaces durable traces; credit depends on personal charisma; structural credit capital is treated as a tradable financial product.

## 9. Falsification conditions
Revise when in-phase repetition fails, long-wave stability does not reproduce across personnel/media, credit does not recover after transparent repair, audit measures breach source-defined conditions, or the observation window shows persistent ungrounded credit or harmful lock-in.
