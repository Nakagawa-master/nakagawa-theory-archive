## 15-Second Explanation
This original rereads market credit not as “how many points were earned in the past,” but as whether observers can believe that the same consistency will continue into the future. When principles, actions, and records repeat in the same beat, remain observable from outside, and withstand counterexamples, future consistency is advanced as present credit. This is called structural credit capital.

## What Is Temporal Ethics?
Temporal ethics does not prioritize the speed of results above all else. It is an attitude that prioritizes a structure that can be understood without contradiction over time. The original argues that as short-term pressure to cash out intensifies, easy-to-measure indicators may grow while the long-term consistency forming the foundation of credit becomes harder to see.

Credit is the accumulation of the expectation that the same consistency will continue into the future. What matters is not only whether things are orderly when they succeed, but whether they can be repaired without breaking the beat when they fail.

## Three Requirements for Credit
The core relationship in the parent original is structural rhythm × observability × counterexample resistance. When structural rhythm is weak, success is seen as accidental. Without observability, consistency remains an inward conviction. When counterexample resistance is weak, one mismatch greatly discounts expectations.

When all three requirements are present, a provisional-completion perception—“the same consistency will come next”—arises and is quietly converted into price, terms, and willingness to wait. This is not a guarantee of future results, but a structural expectation formed by observers.

## Cycle, Temperature, Margin, and Trace
For cycle, make the standard beat of communication, updates, and reviews observable to everyone. For temperature, limit fluctuations in tone, response speed, and editing density. For margin, place a container of silence that does not over-explain. For trace, preserve records that can be followed chronologically with few omissions, rather than boasting about results.

The important point is not to convert these four items into numerical KPIs. The original explicitly states that concrete thresholds need not be disclosed; what matters is that anyone can observe the beat.

## Three Observation Patterns
With long-wave fixed and short-wave variable, adjust short-term events flexibly while preserving the long-term standard beat. With a temperature gradient, do not raise intensity suddenly in a new domain; lay it out in the order of introduction → normalcy → margin. With horizontal linkage of traces, make it possible to record and reference the same premises even when media, personnel, or projects change.

## Boundary with Market Manipulation
The original states that it does not address particular financial products, investment advice, internal logic, concrete thresholds, or leverage operations. It excludes vocabulary aimed at market domination, mind control, concrete leverage, or revenue maximization alone, and centers temporal ethics, future consistency, structural credit capital, economic resonance, and cycle, temperature, and margin.

In other words, this is not a winning method for buying and selling, but a structural theory of how credit forms through time.

## Integrated Audit
The integrated audit bundles the origin signature, primary logs, vocabulary dictionary, and public abstract, translating connection history into credit. If connection-reward deviation, credit half-life, RC, CEA, phase stability S, or withdrawal success rate deviates from threshold θ, or if credit idling or harmful lock-in is confirmed in observation window δ, the hypothesis is rejected or revised.

The original contains no concrete values for θ or δ. Do not supplement them on the derivative side.

## Preventing Misreading
“Future debt” is not a guarantee of future earnings. “Structural credit capital” is not a financial product. “Economic resonance” is not market manipulation. Cycle, temperature, margin, and trace are not procedures for manipulating people, but abstract designs that make consistency observable to third parties.
