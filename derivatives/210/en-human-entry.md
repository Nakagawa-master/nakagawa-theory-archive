## 15-Second Explanation
An institution does not die only after it breaks; it undergoes time decay at a rate at which a third party can no longer reproduce the same judgment. This paper sets that rate as T and the operational observable shadow as \`T̂ = −ΔU/Δt\`, and measures institutional lifespan as “the time until U enters the critical zone.”

## Why It Becomes Necessary
When an institution weakens, people tend to add training, meetings, documents, and rules. But if third-party reproducibility U does not recover, the update is merely cosmetic. Changes in the external environment and the compression of meaning within the institution make explanations outdated regardless of goodwill or effort.

Therefore, rather than asking who is at fault, it is necessary to observe when U began to fall, which routes were severed, and at what rate the system approached criticality.

## Practical Process
1. In the operational phase, fix one to three core judgment logs L.
2. Give the same document bundle to a first-time third party P.
3. Record the proportion U(t) that reaches the same judgment.
4. Classify failure points as premises / terminology / references / responsibility / grounds.
5. Calculate \`T̂ = −ΔU/Δt\` on the same time scale.
6. Implement Update not as adding documents, but as reconnecting premises, terminology, reference bundles, responsibility nodes, and history.
7. In the next observation window δ, confirm whether U recovered and T̂ eased.
8. Check R/H as well as U, and see whether the whole S=U×R×H recovered.

## Examples of Application
**Handover.** Instead of merely adding manuals, measure whether a newcomer can follow the premises, terminology, source references, decision branches, and responsibility entry point to reach the same judgment.

**Rule revision.** Do not use the number of revisions or page count as KPIs; judge success by whether U rose after the revision.

**AI operations.** Rather than continually adding FAQs and instructions until a reference maze is created, realign the entry point, grounds, exceptions, and change differences, and confirm whether a third party can reproduce an output judgment under the same conditions.

## Success Criteria
- U recovers after Update.
- The worsening trend in T̂ eases or reverses.
- R functions uniquely as an entry point for repair.
- H functions not as formal publication, but as a history that a third party can verify.
- The amount of explanation does not exceed K, and the entry point and reference route can be followed.
- The exploration phase and operational phase are not mixed together.

## Limitations
T̂ is not T itself; it is the observable shadow of declining U into which changes in the external environment and internal compression of meaning have been folded. No universally fixed values are assigned to θ, δ, or T̂; they depend on the institution being studied and the measurement design.

This paper also does not indiscriminately apply the reproducibility discipline of the operational phase to the exploration phase. Exploration may require uncertainty and large prediction errors.

## Preventing Misreading
This does not deny education, effort, meetings, or document creation. It distinguishes them from an update structure that lowers the rate of time decay.

The act of “having updated” is not itself an achievement. The decision point is whether third-party reproducibility U recovered after the update. If U does not rise and formal H, broken references, a merely displayed R, or K-exceeding explanations remain, reconnection is incomplete.
