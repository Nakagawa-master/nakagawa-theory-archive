## Summary
This parent models institutional decay as a time-dependent loss of third-party reproducibility rather than as a moral failure or a sudden event. S = U × R × H is a state quantity; T is a velocity quantity describing how explanations fall behind environmental change. In operation, `T_hat = -ΔU/Δt` is an observable shadow of that decay. Institutional lifetime is the time until U enters the critical region. Update is not document growth but reconnection of premises, terminology, references, responsibility nodes, and verifiable history so that U recovers.

## Concepts
- U: third-party reproducibility
- R: unique, usable responsibility / repair node
- H: verifiable openness of decision history
- S = U × R × H: consensus-stability state quantity
- T: time-decay velocity of explanation lag
- T_hat = -ΔU/Δt: operational observable shadow of T
- institutional lifetime: time until U enters a critical region
- meaning compression: internal decay source produced by successful repetition and abbreviation
- Update: reconnection that restores U
- pseudo update: an update after which U does not recover
- K: cognitive bandwidth
- formal H: published but non-verifiable history
- hanging R: nominal or punitive responsibility that cannot function as a repair entry point
- exploration / operation: distinct phases with different optimization criteria

## Causal chain
Repeated operation → external environment changes + internal meaning compression → explanations drift from current conditions → premises disappear, terminology drifts, references shortcut, responsibility nodes dissolve → U(t) declines → T_hat exposes the slope → R/H may also degrade → S falls → S < theta for delta triggers critical transition. Repair requires reconnection, not additive paperwork, followed by fresh measurement of U/R/H/S.

## Applications
- Testing third-party reproducibility after policy or rule revision
- Reconnecting premises, terms, references, responsibility and history during handoffs
- Monthly or quarterly U and T_hat observation in administration or compliance
- Detecting rule sprawl and reference mazes in AI operations
- Checking whether responsibility nodes actually enable repair

## Measurements and audit
Fix one to three core decision logs L. Give the same bundle to a first-time third party P at each observation point. Record U(t), classify failure points, calculate T_hat on a consistent timescale, perform Update where needed, then remeasure after delta. Confirm not only U but R, H and S. Do not optimize proxy counts such as number of updates, pages, meetings, or trainings.

## Validity conditions
- Fix the object and procedure used to measure U.
- Keep state S distinct from velocity T.
- Treat T_hat as an observable shadow, not T itself.
- Observe both external change and internal meaning compression.
- Implement Update as reconnection.
- Recheck R and H, not only U.
- Separate exploration from operation.

## Failure conditions
- Equating institutional age with institutional lifetime.
- Treating effort, education, meetings, or document volume as evidence that T fell.
- Treating the act of updating as success without measuring U.
- Leaving formal H, broken references, hanging R, or K overflow unresolved.
- Goodharting proxy update metrics.
- Applying operational reproducibility discipline indiscriminately to exploration.

## Falsification conditions
Revise the model if repeated observations show that declining U and T_hat do not track institutional decay, that lifetime extends even when Update fails to restore U, or that S remains stable without recovery of U/R/H. Also reconsider the pseudo-update assumption if added documents, meetings, or training repeatedly improve third-party reproducibility without causing K overflow or reference mazes. Theta, delta and T_hat are not universal fixed values.

## Required distinctions
- S / T
- T / T_hat
- years of existence / institutional lifetime
- Update / added work
- amount of explanation / U
- amount published / verifiable H
- nominal responsibility / repair-capable R
- education and effort / update structure
- quietness / observable stability

## Interpretation constraints
The parent does not reject education, effort, meetings, or documentation. It separates them from the structure that actually lowers time-decay risk. It also does not demand maximum U in every phase: uncertainty can be productive in exploration. The main scope is institutional lifetime in operation.
