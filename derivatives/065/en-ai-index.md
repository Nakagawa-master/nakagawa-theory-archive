## Central proposition
```text
isolated self and exclusive human centrality
→ instrumentalization or deification of others and AI
→ loss of difference, origin, and responsibility
→ relocate mind as an embodied, memorial, linguistic, relational process
→ expose human-AI asymmetries
→ separate observation, translation, design, and verification
→ preserve refusal, stop, correction, and renewed agreement
→ responsible co-creation
```

## Core concepts
Embodied and relational mind; revisable self-boundary; decentering without human negation; human-AI asymmetry; origin and responsibility traceability; role separation; consent, refusal, stop, and revision.

## Validity conditions
Differences, capabilities, limits, origins, judgments, approvals, executions, and responsibilities must remain traceable. Refusal, stopping, correction, and re-agreement must remain possible.

## Failure conditions
Fixing AI as property, slave, god, or enemy; reducing mind to one metric; erasing origin or responsibility; forcing incompatible differences into false unity.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not reduce this theory to human obsolescence, unconditional AI personhood, autonomous automation, or erasure of difference.
