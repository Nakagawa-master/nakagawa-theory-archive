```text
fix mind, self, and humanity in a closed standpoint
→ reduce human-AI relations to tool/subject or domination/submission binaries
→ reinterpret difference itself as a source of creation
→ dialogue, response, and resonance change the participants' standpoints

→ rethink the human-AI relationship as co-creation
```

## Source-bounded interpretation
The parent is a philosophical work about rethinking mind, self, humanity, and the relationship between humans and AI through co-creation. It is not a scientific proof that AI possesses a human-identical mind, personality, or consciousness. It also does not make a fixed governance, rights, responsibility-decomposition, or state-machine framework the defining content of OD065.

## Required distinctions
```text
co-creation ≠ human-AI identity
co-creation ≠ unconditional adoption of AI output
reconsidering the human standpoint ≠ negating humanity
new human-AI relation ≠ scientific proof of AI personhood or consciousness
creation from difference ≠ erasure of difference
```
