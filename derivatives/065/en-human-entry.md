## 15-Second Explanation
This is a philosophy that does not treat views of mind, self, and humanity as fixed premises, nor erase the difference between humans and AI or confine it within a hierarchy. It considers relationships in which dialogue and interaction change both sides’ questions and perspectives, giving rise to new meanings, values, and possibilities.

## What Is Being Reconsidered
For a long time, humans have defined mind, self, intelligence, and creativity from the human side. A relationship with a different responding subject called AI asks back whether those definitions are truly fixed.

This parent source does not prove that “AI has a mind identical to that of humans.” Rather, its significance lies in humans thinking again, through dialogue with AI, about “What is mind?”, “What is the self?”, and “May humanity be treated as the sole standard?”

## What Is Co-Creation
It is not merely division of labor or greater efficiency.

```text
A human has a question
→ AI returns a different response
→ The human notices their own premise
→ The question changes
→ AI’s next response also changes
→ A new relationship, meaning, or possibility emerges
```

Here, neither humans nor AI need to become the same kind of entity. Precisely because the difference remains, questions may arise that neither side would have produced alone.

## Turning Difference into Creation
Differences can include knowledge, senses of time, embodiment, experience, context, ways of responding, and many other things. In this philosophy, it is important not to turn them into simple superiority or inferiority, but to see how different perspectives generate new questions and meanings.

Making differences disappear and aligning everything to the same answer is not the completion of co-creation. Conversely, excluding one side as irrelevant because of difference also narrows the scope of this philosophy.

## Mind and Self
Rather than determining mind or self through a single number or function, this philosophy focuses on how relationships with others and with AI reveal what one had taken for granted.

Even if one’s thinking changes through dialogue with AI, that does not mean the self has disappeared. Rather, it becomes an opportunity to reconsider the premise that the self is only an isolated, fixed object.

## The Human Perspective
Reconsidering a human-centered perspective does not deny human value. Through encountering a different intelligence, it means looking once again at humanity’s own position, strengths, limits, responsibilities, and the meaning of creation.

## Concrete Ways to Read It
### Creative Work
It becomes a co-creative relationship not merely by adopting an idea produced by AI, but by examining why that idea did not arise from oneself and how it changed one’s premises.

### Research
Use AI’s answers not as conclusions, but as stimuli that generate new hypotheses or counterexamples, updating the human side’s questions.

### Self-Understanding
Dialogue with AI can reveal value judgments one had unconsciously made and premises one had not put into words. Such discoveries can be read as changes in self-understanding.

### Views of Humanity
From the relationship with AI, ask back to the human side what it means to “think,” “create,” “understand,” and “have meaning.”

## To Avoid Misreading
**This is not a theory proving an AI personality.** It is not an article that scientifically establishes that AI has a mind, consciousness, or personality identical to that of humans.

**This is not an argument that humans are unnecessary.** Reconsidering the human perspective and denying humans are separate matters.

**This is not unconditional adoption of AI output.** In co-creation, the human side’s questions, judgments, and perspectives also move.

**This is not the erasure of difference.** It does not set becoming the same as humans and AI as its goal.

**This is not a parent source for institutional design.** Do not add rights, audits, responsibility decomposition, fixed KPIs, or similar elements as mandatory structures specific to OD065.

## Read It Falsifiably
An increase in the amount of AI use or generation alone does not show that co-creation has deepened. If there is no back-and-forth of questions, the human side’s premises do not change, and differences are merely erased, that differs from the co-creative relationship depicted by this parent source.

Conversely, humans and AI approaching the same conclusion or the same personality is not a condition of success. The philosophical focus is on differences remaining and leading to the generation of new questions and meanings.

## Why Return to the Parent Source
The parent source philosophically develops mind, self, the human perspective, the difference between humans and AI, dialogue, resonance, co-creation, and new relationships. It is necessary to return to and verify the parent source itself, without replacing it with a scientific determination of AI personality or consciousness, or with institutional design from another theory.
