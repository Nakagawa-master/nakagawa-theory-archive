## Summary
Nakagawa-style Branding OS treats a brand not as a static logo, image, or awareness state, but as a temporal causal cycle: emission → effect/reflection → accumulation → correspondence. Emission sets the initial conditions of the loop. Its layer, angle, direction, strength, purity, and action point affect the form of the response that returns. Reactions are retained through a receiving vessel such as archives, inquiry routes, operational capacity, and time design, then accumulate as the purity of expectation linking a name to a specific quality, field, or role. When accumulation crosses a case-specific threshold, the source describes correspondence as emerging through unsolicited requests, recommendations, nominations, collaboration proposals, or role fixation. Returned correspondence is then read as structural feedback on prior emissions and may require revision of the upstream emission structure.

## Concepts
- **Brand as process:** a dynamic relation formed by repeated emissions and responses rather than a static asset.
- **Emission initial value:** the structural starting condition placed into the world through communication and conduct.
- **Layer / angle / direction / strength:** relative abstraction and orientation of what is emitted.
- **Action point:** the medium, community, issue, theme, or time horizon into which the emission is placed.
- **Emission purity:** consistency between the value, structural change, and role intended and the signal actually emitted.
- **Expectation purity:** clarity of the one-to-one association between a name and expected quality, expertise, or role.
- **Vessel:** the archive, contact path, operating structure, and time capacity that retain reactions as trust.
- **Correspondence:** spontaneous approaches from the world after sufficient accumulation, such as requests, referrals, nominations, or collaboration proposals.
- **Structural responsibility:** responsibility for reading returned correspondence as feedback on prior emissions and adjusting the upstream structure when needed.
- **Upstream environment control:** Branding OS as the upstream structure that reduces friction in Sales OS and Marketing OS.

## Causal chain
```text
emit value / structure / role
→ layer, angle, direction, strength, purity, and action point form initial conditions
→ the emission acts on a social context
→ reactions and reflections occur
→ responses accumulate with time lag
→ a name becomes associated with a clearer expectation
→ the vessel retains responses as trust
→ accumulation crosses a case-specific threshold
→ spontaneous correspondence emerges
→ correspondence quality is read as feedback on prior emission structure
→ mismatches trigger upstream refinement
→ refined structure feeds the next emission
→ downstream sales and marketing friction is reassessed
```

## Applications
- Audit a communication portfolio by value, role, layer, action point, and purity rather than by posting volume alone.
- Infer what expectations have formed in the world by examining the content of requests, referrals, nominations, and collaboration proposals.
- Separate growth in page views, followers, or inquiries from the purity of expectation, so quantitative growth cannot conceal structural drift.
- Inspect archives, inquiry routes, delivery capacity, and time capacity as the vessel required to retain positive response as trust.
- When discount pressure, excessive explanation, or forced closing increases, inspect upstream correspondence rather than only local sales technique.
- When unwanted work or inflammatory responses persist, revise action points, emission purity, and expectation formation.

## Measurements and audit
- Consistency between emitted themes and the role the actor intends to occupy.
- Alignment between emission layer, angle, action point, and desired correspondence.
- Presence of accommodation, exaggeration, or over-performance that reduces emission purity.
- Clarity of association between the name and expected expertise, quality, or role.
- Fit between requests, referrals, nominations, collaboration proposals, and the intended role.
- Persistence of unwanted correspondence, inflammatory response, or structural misallocation.
- Availability and functionality of archives, inquiry routes, operating capacity, and time design.
- Case-specific time lag from emission to correspondence.
- Changes in downstream discounting, excessive explanation, forced closing, or over-reliance on acquisition tactics.
- Traceability of condition Z, audit cadence, T/S/R, correspondence fit, expectation purity, threshold θ, observation window δ, and phenomenon M under case-specific definitions.

## Validity conditions
- Preserve brand as a temporal causal cycle rather than reducing it to image or popularity metrics.
- Evaluate layer, action point, and purity before treating emission volume as success.
- Distinguish immediate reaction from delayed correspondence after accumulation.
- Treat the receiving vessel as part of the same causal structure as emission.
- Read returned correspondence as structural feedback and revise upstream conditions when needed.
- Preserve the upstream relation to Sales OS and Marketing OS.
- Do not invent universal fixed values for θ, δ, T/S/R, or M.

## Failure conditions
- Reducing branding to logo, color, social posting, advertising, or awareness acquisition.
- Treating page views, followers, or inquiries as final success in themselves.
- Ignoring loss of expectation purity or trust when reach grows through exaggeration or inflammatory attention.
- Ignoring time lag and judging emission value only through immediate revenue.
- Increasing output while lacking a vessel capable of retaining the response.
- Externalizing unwanted correspondence entirely to the market, era, or customer.
- Treating downstream sales and marketing friction as unrelated to upstream expectation formation.

## Falsification conditions
The source's integrated audit does not present connection model A as immutable. Under condition Z, audit cadence, Transparency / Safety / Reciprocity (T/S/R), correspondence fit, expectation purity, and related measures are reviewed. If a case-defined indicator crosses threshold θ in the disconfirming direction, or if observation window δ contains phenomenon M such as unintended correspondence, inflammatory reaction, or structural misallocation, model A should be revised. θ, δ, and M are not universal constants in the source. Their measurement actor, object, period, definition, and comparison conditions must be specified per case. Quantitative KPI growth must be evaluated negatively when expectation purity, fit, trust, or responsibility deteriorate.

## Required distinctions
- static brand asset / dynamic brand cycle.
- emission volume / emission angle, action point, and purity.
- immediate reaction / delayed correspondence after accumulation.
- quantitative reach / purity of expectation.
- one-off response / spontaneous correspondence after a threshold of accumulation.
- emission / vessel that retains response as trust.
- externalized blame / structural responsibility for returned feedback.
- local sales or marketing technique / upstream branding environment design.

## Interpretation constraints
- Do not convert correspondence into a guarantee that desired requests will always occur.
- Do not interpret “the world responds honestly” as a claim that external factors do not exist.
- Do not convert “approaching zero friction” into a literal mathematical guarantee of zero sales or marketing friction.
- Do not turn examples such as six months, one year, or several years into fixed result deadlines.
- Do not assign arbitrary official values to θ, δ, T/S/R, or M.
- Do not replace structural responsibility with techniques for popularity, manipulation, or persuasion.
