## Summary
Core Point Discovery redefines KPI not as a collection of management metrics but as the unique causal fulcrum that directly connects to a KGI. The core point is not chosen arbitrarily: it is isolated through KGI definition, causal decomposition, candidate extraction, counterexample filtering, necessary-actuation design, and single-factor intervention tests.

## Concepts
- KGI: the final state that must be achieved.
- Core Point: the unique causal fulcrum whose satisfaction directly connects to the KGI.
- Necessary actuation: the number, pressure, intensity, or quasi-quantitative action applied to the core point.
- Sub Metrics: auxiliary management or observation metrics that are not themselves the core point.
- Counterexample sieve: testing whether a candidate can be satisfied while the KGI still fails.
- Freedom test: checking whether the intervention preserves multiple acceptable choices rather than collapsing them into a hidden single answer.

## Causal chain
Define KGI → observe field characteristics → decompose causality backward from KGI → extract up to three candidate fulcrums → apply counterexamples → eliminate candidates that can be satisfied without reaching KGI → define necessary actuation for the remaining candidate → compare baseline A with B’ where only the candidate changes → verify direction across three cycles, freedom, and non-inducement → fix the core point → connect its actuation to KGI achievement.

## State model
1. Sub-metric confusion: multiple auxiliary metrics are called KPIs.
2. Causal exploration: prerequisites are decomposed backward from the KGI.
3. Candidate filtering: counterexamples eliminate non-unique points.
4. Provisional core point: one candidate remains but intervention testing is incomplete.
5. Fixed core point: single-factor intervention and safeguards pass.
6. Formalization failure: the ritual of “one point” remains while causal-fulcrum status is lost.

## Applications
The method can be applied to organizational consensus, learning retention, public participation, sales, and institutional operations. The target is the structural condition that moves the final outcome, not merely activity volume. When the apparent fulcrum is a person, the parent text directs the analysis toward a structural condition rather than personal appraisal.

## Measurements and audit
Pre-observation includes consensus time, self-propulsion index, and the quality of resistance or silence. Validation uses an A/B’ comparison in which only the core-point factor changes, a three-cycle stabilization test, a freedom test, and an inducement check. Necessary actuation may be numerical or expressed through frequency, sequence, or duration.

## Validity conditions
The KGI must be a judgeable end state; causal prerequisites must be decomposable; counterexamples must be constructible; a single-factor comparison must be feasible; and the core point must be treated as a structural condition rather than a personal score. The intervention must preserve meaningful freedom.

## Failure conditions
The method fails when Sub Metrics are relabeled as KPIs, the single point is selected before causal analysis, counterexample filtering is skipped, one success is treated as proof, people are scored as the core point, outcomes are exaggerated despite sample bias, or hidden answers remove freedom.

## Falsification conditions
If a candidate survives counterexample filtering and single-factor intervention, receives the specified necessary actuation, yet repeatedly fails to reach the KGI, the candidate’s core-point status or the parent claim that a unique core point guarantees KGI achievement requires revision. Repeated cases where several independent fulcrums are jointly indispensable would also challenge the uniqueness condition.

## Required distinctions
KPI vs. Sub Metrics; core point vs. a visually prominent metric; discovering uniqueness vs. arbitrarily reducing metrics to one; necessary actuation vs. unlimited pressure; single-factor intervention vs. multi-variable change; structural-condition evaluation vs. personal appraisal.

## Interpretation constraints
The parent article makes a strong claim that full actuation of the true core point leads necessarily to the KGI. This derivative preserves that claim strength but does not upgrade it into an independently proven universal law. It must not be misread as saying that every complex system contains an obvious single number. The non-coercive, freedom-preserving, non-personal-evaluation constraints remain binding to the meaning.
