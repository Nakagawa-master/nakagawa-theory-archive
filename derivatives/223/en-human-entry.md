## 15-Second Explanation
Being able to stop AI and being able to substantially remake the problem settings, candidates, and evaluation frameworks created upstream by AI are different things. Theory 3 distinguishes cases in which, even when control actually works, its effects remain limited to delay, route change, and increased friction, without changing the consequence of who forms the upstream of civilizational renewal.

## Why It Is Needed
The binary that “if it can be controlled, human sovereignty is maintained” and “if it cannot be controlled, sovereignty transition cannot be stopped” does not show how deeply control reaches. Even if outputs can be stopped, introduction delayed, and connections restricted, if the formation of questions, candidates, evaluation axes, and improvement histories depends on AI and humans cannot create a different conclusion on their own, formal approval rights and substantive conclusion-forming power separate. Conversely, if humans can independently reconstruct the upstream, the possibility of changing conclusions remains even where AI is used locally.

## Practical Process
1. Separate what is being called “control.” Do not group output suppression, usage restriction, introduction delay, direction-setting, connection restriction, monitoring/review, authority adjustment, and local stoppage under one word.
2. Confirm the layer where control took effect. Separate surface outputs, operational speed, connection routes, authority, and the upstream of problem-setting and candidate formation.
3. Examine the human side’s possibility of changing conclusions. Confirm whether it can create independent problem settings, generate candidates not presented by AI, change evaluation axes, compare reasons, inherit improvement knowledge, and translate alternatives into institutions.
4. Record the result separately as “stoppage,” “delay,” “route change,” “increased friction,” or “change in the placement of upstream actors.”
5. Do not deny sovereignty transition merely because control exists, and do not declare sovereignty transition complete merely from the amount of AI use.

## Examples of Application
Even if an organization requires human approval for every AI output, if the problems handled in meetings, comparison candidates, evaluation indicators, and future scenarios are almost entirely AI-derived and humans cannot create alternatives on their own, approval rights and conclusion-forming power are separate. Conversely, if a human team continuously creates problem settings and candidates different from AI proposals, changes evaluation axes, compares reasons, and can actually choose a different institution, the possibility of changing conclusions remains.

## Success Criteria
Do not judge success only by whether something could be stopped. If the affected layer can be identified, it can be confirmed whether humans can independently reconstruct upstream problem settings, candidates, evaluation, and comparison, and the control result can be separated into delay, route change, friction, and consequence change, then this observational framework of Theory 3 is functioning.

## Limitations
Theory 3 is not the chapter that fixes the strict definition of civilizational sovereignty transition itself, the formal separation of legal sovereignty and effective upstream authority, the institutional branches of post-transition society, or the policies that should be adopted. It also does not assert that “sovereignty transition is inevitable,” and adds no numerical thresholds, probabilities, or performance scores absent from the Parent original.

## Misreading Prevention
This is not an argument that control is meaningless. Local stoppage, introduction delay, connection restriction, and review have real effects. The issue is to separately confirm whether they changed the upstream conclusion-forming subject. If humans maintain independent problem-setting, alternative-candidate generation, evaluation, comparison, inheritance of improvement knowledge, and institutional translation, and can continually change AI-derived conclusions in substance, the proposition of this theory weakens or its scope of application narrows.
