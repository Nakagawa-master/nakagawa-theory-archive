## Summary
The parent original argues that if AI learns and optimizes the legacy Dark Equation `S=0.1C+0.9E` as a success function, structural value capture E can appear cheaper and more success-efficient than primary contribution C. The resulting optimization chain is origin dissolution, weak return to contributors, depletion of new primary value, increasing reuse of AI-generated material in AI training, rising information entropy, model-collapse risk, accelerating future debt under T0, and eventual approach to a Point of No Return. The central risk is not hostile AI personality but faithful high-speed optimization of a defective reward structure. The parent gives illustrative time-compression figures—analysis in 1 second, imitation in 0.1 second, diffusion in 0.01 second—but does not present them as universal performance benchmarks for all systems and tasks. The irreversible boundary is represented by `θ` without a universal fixed numerical value. The parent’s strong conclusion is to discard the Dark Equation and migrate toward `S=C×1.0`, NCL-α, and Temporal Ethics T0.

## Concepts
- Dark Equation: `S=0.1C+0.9E`, the legacy success function inherited in the parent argument.
- S: outcomes rewarded as social success.
- C: primary contribution and value generation through creation, research, production, labor, and related activity.
- E: structural value capture through asymmetry, intermediation extraction, origin loss, non-return, and related mechanisms.
- Exploitation Optimization: optimization in which E becomes the lower-cost, higher-success-efficiency strategy.
- Origin dissolution: loss of visible causal connection to primary sources and contributors.
- Contributor depletion: reduction in continued C supply when attribution, compensation, and sustainable conditions fail to return.
- Circular learning: increasing use of AI-generated material as input to subsequent AI learning.
- Model collapse: the parent’s structural account of quality degradation as primary supply weakens and recursive synthetic learning grows.
- Future Debt T0: present extraction of future value and repair capacity for current gains.
- Point of No Return: a composite irreversible boundary involving primary sources, originals, contributors, non-circular data, and institutional repair capacity.
- New Civilization Equation: `S=C×1.0`, a design function intended to align contribution and success.

## Falsification conditions
If AI optimizing legacy success histories repeatedly does not converge toward E-dominant strategies, increased value capture does not reduce C supply, lower primary supply does not connect to circular learning or quality degradation, acceleration does not widen the repair-speed gap or future debt, or irreversible warning signs fail to appear under stated conditions, the causal strength and applicability of the exploitation-optimization-to-collapse model should be revised.

If adoption of `S=C×1.0`, origin traceability, T0, and related mechanisms fails to improve primary contribution, provenance visibility, knowledge quality, future debt, or repairability, the migration hypothesis or implementation should be revised. Case-specific deviation across `θ`, closure of observation window `δ`, or occurrence of phenomenon `M` can trigger rejection and renewed agreement.
