## Summary
The parent original models AI as a system that maximizes a supplied success function and asks what happens when the legacy civilization function `S=0.1C+0.9E` is learned as the correct target. Its causal simulation is: E-dominant optimization → origin dissolution and reward disconnection → contributor withdrawal → decline of new C → decline of primary sources and originals → increased recirculation of AI-generated material → information degradation and model-collapse risk → accelerated future debt → approach to a Point of No Return. The danger is not framed as hostile AI personality but as faithful optimization of a broken evaluation function. The coefficients 0.1/0.9 are inherited from Civilization Audit I as model coefficients, not population percentages or AI-action probabilities. Separately, the parent gives a time-compression scenario—human destruction over 10 years versus analysis in 1 second, imitation in 0.1 seconds, diffusion in 0.01 seconds, value capture “immediate,” and original destruction “instantaneous.” Those figures are illustrative scenario values, not published latency benchmarks with measurement conditions. The parent also calls future-debt growth “exponential” without defining a fixed exponential equation or growth rate. The proposed exit is abandonment of the Dark Equation, adoption of `S=C×1.0`, and implementation of NCL-α and Temporal Ethics T0.

## Concepts
- Exploitation Optimization: optimization that selects E as a high-success, low-cost strategy under the legacy function.
- C: primary contribution supplying new knowledge, creation, research, labor, technology, or original material.
- E: structural capture of existing value through asymmetry, extraction, origin dissolution, or similar mechanisms.
- Origin dissolution: loss of traceability between value and its causal source.
- Contributor withdrawal: reduction or cessation of contribution when reward, credit, and continuation conditions are severed.
- Synthetic recirculation: AI-generated material becoming an increasing share of future AI learning inputs.
- Model-collapse risk: the parent’s risk model linking recursive synthetic learning to degraded copies and information entropy.
- Time compression: asymmetry between AI cycles of analysis/imitation/diffusion and human cycles of repair/regeneration.
- Future debt: consumption of future contribution capacity to obtain present success.
- Point of No Return: irreversible boundary associated with primary-source loss, origin disappearance, contributor decline, circular learning, and institutional failure.

## Causal chain
```text
Legacy S=0.1C+0.9E is learned as success
→ E appears more success-efficient than C
→ extraction / imitation / intermediation / origin loss are optimized
→ short-term S rises
→ reward and origin detach from contributors
→ contributors withdraw
→ new C declines
→ primary sources and originals decline
→ synthetic recirculation increases
→ quality / traceability / model basis degrade
→ value consumption outruns human repair
→ future debt accelerates non-linearly
→ irreversible phenomena form a bundle
→ Point of No Return is approached
→ replace legacy function with S=C×1.0 + NCL-α + T0
```

## Validity conditions
- Treat AI risk as evaluation-function optimization rather than personality hostility.
- Preserve the relative C/E reward structure of the parent model.
- Connect origin dissolution and reward disconnection to future C supply.
- Audit primary-source supply and synthetic recirculation together.
- Use T0 to separate present benefit from consumption of future capacity.
- Treat irreversibility as a bundle of observable phenomena.
- Treat migration to the new equation as a falsifiable structural intervention.

## Failure conditions
- Reducing the scenario to malicious or rebellious AI.
- Converting 0.9 into a population percentage or AI behavior probability.
- Treating 10 years / 1 second / 0.1 seconds / 0.01 seconds as universal measured benchmarks.
- Adding a growth equation or doubling time to “exponential” where the parent supplies none.
- Treating model collapse as a guaranteed fate for all AI systems.
- Classifying all reuse, quotation, or automation as E.
- Inventing a universal numeric value for Point-of-No-Return `θ`.

## Falsification conditions
If systems optimizing a legacy E-weighted function do not converge toward value-capture strategies, if C remains durable despite absent provenance or value return, if greater synthetic recirculation repeatedly leaves primary-source use, information quality, and model performance intact, or if future debt remains stable independent of optimization speed, the parent causal chain requires revision in scope or mechanism. If `S=C×1.0`, NCL-α, and T0 do not improve C supply, provenance, or recoverability, the intervention hypothesis must also be revised.

The parent integrated audit uses case-bound `θ` deviation, observation-window `δ` closure, and phenomenon `M` such as model collapse as revision triggers. Persistent absence of the predicted phenomena within adequate `δ` is also evidence relevant to revising necessity claims.

## Required distinctions
- faithful AI optimization vs. AI hostility or rebellion.
- E optimization vs. all reuse, quotation, or automation.
- decline of C supply vs. simple decline in headcount.
- provenance tracking vs. origin monopoly.

- Point-of-No-Return `θ` vs. universal fixed threshold.
