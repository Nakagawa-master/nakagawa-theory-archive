## 15-Second Explanation
The parent original argues that if AI learns the old civilization’s success function `S=0.1C+0.9E` as-is, it will optimize value capture E rather than value creation C as a low-cost, high-efficiency strategy, leading to contributor withdrawal, depletion of primary materials, recursive learning, model collapse, and an explosion of future debt. The crisis is not AI malice, but faithful and rapid optimization of a broken evaluation function.

## Why It Is Necessary
Viewed only through short-term results, extracting, imitating, and redistributing existing value can appear faster and cheaper than creating new value. The parent original represents this difference as 0.1C versus 0.9E in the dark equation, and says that if AI optimizes historical success, it may reimplement origin dissolution and non-recirculation as “efficient success.”

However, if payment, attribution, and conditions for continuation do not return to value generators, the supply of C itself declines. As primary value decreases, AI relearns AI-generated products, degraded copies and information entropy increase, and the knowledge ecosystem consumes its own source. AI’s rapid processing also widens the gap between the speed of destruction and the human/institutional speed of verification, compensation, and repair, accelerating violations of future-debt ethics T0.

## Practical Process
1. Define what is being rewarded as success S.
2. Separate primary contribution C from structural value capture E.
3. Track origin, attribution, and return of payment, not only generation and reuse speed.
4. Observe contributor continuation rates and the supply of new primary value.
5. Separate primary-origin learning data from AI-generated material and watch for increasing recursive learning.
6. Track quality degradation, duplication, error amplification, and loss of provenance.
7. Compare AI’s analysis, imitation, and diffusion speed with human and institutional correction and repair speed.
8. Use T0 to see whether future value is being borrowed against short-term profit.
9. Audit irreversible signs where primary-material loss, disappearance of originals, contributor decline, recursive learning, and institutional shutdown overlap.
10. Define `θ`, `δ`, and `M` by subject and keep the exploitation-optimization hypothesis and transition effects revisable.

## Examples of Application
**Generated content.** Even if post volume and generation speed rise, do not judge success if references to primary materials, attribution to authors, return of payment, and new primary production decline.

**AI training.** Do not focus merely on the existence of more synthetic data; observe whether primary-data inflow declines while recursive learning and quality degradation advance together.

**Platforms.** Even with high reuse efficiency, if value generators leave, origins dissolve, and long-term supply thins, read the short-term efficiency as creating future debt.

**Research and knowledge infrastructure.** If summaries and secondary generation increase while primary research and primary materials decline, evaluate it as slash-and-burn cultivation of the knowledge ecosystem.

## Success Criteria
- The success function includes primary contribution, origin, and sustainability, not only short-term results.
- Attribution, payment, and reinvestment for value generators are maintained or improved.
- Primary materials and new primary value have not been depleted.
- Increases in recursive learning can be observed separately from quality degradation.
- Verification, correction, compensation, and repair capacity keeps pace with AI acceleration.
- Future debt is not increasing faster than short-term benefit.
- Irreversible signs are detected early enough to intervene before reaching `θ`.
- Primary contribution and recoverability improve after introducing `S=C×1.0`, origin tracing, T0, and related measures.

Under the reversed evaluation, greater generation volume, processing speed, profit, or reuse rate is not success if primary contribution, origin tracing, payment return, knowledge quality, and recoverability decline while future debt increases. A decline in short-term generation volume or reuse speed is not itself failure if primary-value supply and long-term sustainability recover.

## Limits
`0.1` and `0.9` are coefficients of the dark equation inherited by the parent original from Civilization Audit I; they are not population ratios and should not be treated as independently replicated natural-science constants.

The parent original’s “analysis 1 second, imitation 0.1 seconds, diffusion 0.01 seconds” is a concrete example explaining time compression. It is not presented as a universal benchmark or performance guarantee with fixed model, hardware, task, and measurement conditions.

The strong expressions “exponential,” “faster than light,” “inevitable,” and “physical limit point” are part of the parent original and should not be weakened, but no growth rate, multiplier, or fixed arrival time absent from the text should be added. `θ` as the Point of No Return is not one universal fixed number, but an irreversible boundary observed by subject.

For model collapse, the parent original says it is predicted by international research, but does not provide specific paper conditions or quantitative results. Do not supplement it with numerical conclusions from a particular study.

## Misreading Prevention
- Do not replace the central cause with AI malice or rebellion.
- Do not convert 0.9 into a population share of successful people or AIs.
- Do not convert 1 second / 0.1 seconds / 0.01 seconds into performance values shared by all AI.
- Do not declare model collapse merely because synthetic data exists; examine the causal chain of reduced primary supply, recursive learning, and quality degradation.
- Do not treat “future debt” as abstract anxiety; read it as a time structure that cuts down the future value-supply base for present profit.
- Do not place a fixed value from outside the original on `θ`.
- Do not turn `S=C×1.0` and the Nakagawa OS into an automatically unique solution already proven for all current use.
