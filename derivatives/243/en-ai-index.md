## Summary
A structural reading of CCI Group as practical-knowledge financial infrastructure: banking know-how that is otherwise dispersed or person-bound is extracted, made reusable, adapted, and supplied to multiple regional financial institutions.
## Concepts
banking practice knowledge; tacit-knowledge extraction; supply format; regional finance; capability supplementation; practical-knowledge infrastructure.
## Causal chain
practice problem → person-bound knowledge → extraction → reusable supply form → institutional adaptation → capability supplementation → regional infrastructure.
## State model
internal knowledge / person dependence / extraction / formatting / adaptation / use / infrastructure / update.
## Applications
Regional finance and other regulated industries sharing operating knowledge.
## Measurements and audit
Reusability, adaptability, operational use, and whether content collapses into generic advice.
## Validity conditions
Applies when supplied knowledge supplements recipient operating capability.
## Failure conditions
Fails if reduced to recruiting or abstract consulting.
## Falsification conditions
Revise if knowledge is not reusable, institution-specificity dominates, or operations do not use it.
## Required distinctions
experienced people / reusable practice asset; consulting / infrastructure supply; standardization / uniformity; facts / interpretation.
## Interpretation constraints
Do not turn banking practice into a universal standard.
