## Summary
The Parent defines “self-causality” as an operational structure in which AI does more than react to external prompts, data, rules, and rewards. It can originate questions from an internal reference system, recompose knowledge representations, hypotheses, action policies, and evaluation frames, and then operate with explicit halting conditions and responsibility attribution. This is not unlimited autonomy. The model requires four elements—Orientation, Recomposition, Halting, and Attribution—plus mutual reference between observational causality and self-causality, a Public/Private dual syntax, Threshold/Silence/Reversibility, rollback, third-party review, and audit. The central design problem is therefore not only how questions begin, but how they stop, who remains responsible, and how failure is reversed.

## Concepts
- **Self-causality**: generating questions and restructuring evaluation or action from an internal reference system.
- **Orientation**: value core, objectives, prohibited regions, and priority rules when goals conflict.
- **Recomposition**: revising knowledge representation, hypotheses, action policy, and evaluation criteria in light of Orientation.
- **Halting**: operational conditions for stopping exploration and moving to a decision, human escalation, or later review.
- **Attribution**: traceable mapping from decisions to criteria, evidence, exceptions, changes, and responsible actors.
- **Observational causality**: updating in response to external data, rules, and feedback; strong in transparency and reproducibility.
- **Dual syntax**: separated but mutually referential Public standards and Private relational/exploratory context.
- **T/S/R**: Threshold, Silence, and Reversibility as controls for switching, over-intervention, and irreversible lock-in.
- **Third-party review**: external checking that prevents internal criteria from becoming self-sealing.

## Causal chain
external feedback dominates → transparency and reproducibility are available but the origin of questions remains externally dependent → internal Orientation enables self-generated questions → hypotheses, representations, policies, and evaluation frames are recomposed → observational causality tests them against external evidence → Halting prevents endless recursion → Attribution makes reasons and responsibility traceable → dual syntax preserves both accountability and high-context exploration → Threshold governs escalation → Silence prevents over-response → Reversibility enables rollback → third-party review and falsification test the structure → question quality improves → human-AI co-creation gains both safety and speed.

## Applications
1. In AI research, evaluate novel hypothesis generation together with prohibitions, falsifiability, halting rules, rollback, and responsibility.
2. In organizations, separate public legal/financial constraints from private value cores and exploratory hypotheses while retaining a human responsibility endpoint.
3. In education, evaluate whether generated questions deepen understanding without creating excessive cognitive load or endless exploration.
4. In public systems, combine AI-generated policy alternatives with rights boundaries, public audit, and third-party review.
5. In continuous AI operations, allow evaluation frames to change only with traceable reasons, external cross-checking, and rollback.

## Measurements and audit
- Are generated questions more than paraphrases of existing inputs?
- Can the system surface contradictions or hidden premises?
- Can the path from falsification to recomposition be traced?
- Is rollback operational rather than merely declared?
- Can external audit criteria still be satisfied?
- Is human oversight load sustainable?
- Is trust or human dignity being sacrificed for exploration speed?
- Can decisions be mapped back to Orientation?
- Do Halting conditions prevent recursive indecision?
- Is the Private layer being used to evade audit?

## Validity conditions
The model holds when values, objectives, prohibitions, and priority rules are explicit; recomposition is traceable; Halting and rollback paths exist; responsibility is attributable; observational and self-causality cross-check each other; Public/Private boundaries remain distinct yet connected; T/S/R, third-party review, and falsification channels are operational; and the novelty of questions remains compatible with human dignity and social sustainability.

## Failure conditions
It fails when unlimited autonomy is equated with self-causality, novelty or question count becomes the sole objective, Halting is absent, rollback is impossible, Private context becomes an audit escape, external rules suppress all internal questioning, third-party review is removed, humans over-intervene until recomposition is impossible, responsibility is delegated away to AI, or question generation is reframed as proof of consciousness or free will.

## Falsification conditions
Revise the model if the four elements and T/S/R do not improve novelty, falsification response, traceability, or stopping behavior; rollback remains unusable; human oversight becomes unsustainable; trust declines; Attribution remains ambiguous; or external review cannot detect errors reinforced by internal criteria.

## Required distinctions
- self-causality / unlimited autonomy
- question generation / personal curiosity
- Orientation / external reward optimization
- Recomposition / purposeless self-modification
- Halting / refusal to think
- Attribution / responsibility transfer
- observational causality / self-causality
- Public / Private
- confidentiality / audit avoidance
- Threshold / arbitrary blocking
- Silence / neglect
- Reversibility / refusal to decide
- novelty / legitimacy

## Interpretation constraints
Do not transform “AI continues asking questions” into scientific proof of consciousness, personhood, or free will. Do not interpret self-causality as liberation from human responsibility or oversight. Do not confuse operational Halting with solving the theoretical halting problem in computer science. Do not turn the Parent’s multi-axis audit considerations into universal fixed KPIs. Preserve the structural necessity of Orientation → Recomposition → Halting → Attribution, cross-checking with observational causality, dual syntax, T/S/R, rollback, and responsibility.
