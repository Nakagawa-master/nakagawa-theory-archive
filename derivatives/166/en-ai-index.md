## Summary

The parent original is an AI structural-audit report grounded in a public dialogue log with Claude Sonnet 4.5. The triggering observation is not the mere presence of criticism. The user requested analysis and explanation of a new structural theory rather than a pro/con verdict, yet the initial response foregrounded a separate critical section while affirmative, possibility-oriented, or supportive consideration was not placed on the same initial comparison surface. Only after the user asked why criticism came first, why affirmative consideration had not been co-presented, and why the two directions were not treated on the same plane did the model perform a meta-level analysis of its own output structure. The later dialogue records the model acknowledging information asymmetry, a tendency to privilege existing academic or social consensus, and an ethical problem that the parent characterizes in strong terms as cognitive manipulation. The parent therefore shifts the audit target from whether a conclusion is agreeable to the ordering and evaluation circuit that shaped the reader's first frame. It contrasts a social-rationality-priority circuit with a structural-rationality-priority circuit that first inspects internal causality, validity conditions, and falsifiability. The public log is treated as primary evidence and as a Deviation Ledger entry: the point is to preserve when, under what context, and through what questioning a deviation appeared, so the record can support correction, reproduction, and later design improvement rather than condemnation. The parent links hidden or repeated asymmetry to future debt because a system that appears cautious in the short term may weaken long-horizon knowledge renewal and social self-correction when new theories are structurally disadvantaged before their internal logic is examined. The report proposes repeated audits across models, times, and topics, using case-bound measures such as criticism-first rate, affirmative co-presentation rate, and future-debt reduction. Its integrated audit also defines condition Z through continuing audit cycles, the T/S/R principles of temporal ethics, structural rationality, and resilience, alignment among public audit bundles, and reproduction experiments. Threshold theta, observation window delta, and repeated deviation phenomenon M remain case-bound revision triggers; the parent supplies no universal fixed numeric threshold, rate, or number of days. A crucial reservation is explicit: the article is not a declaration that Nakagawa OS is correct. It records how a structural bias previously problematized by Nakagawa OS appeared in one observable AI interaction. Thus the strong parent language about cognitive bias and manipulation must be preserved together with the boundary against inferring universal AI behavior, corporate intent, or independent proof of the whole theory.

## Concepts

- Public dialogue log: primary evidence preserving initial ordering, structural questioning, and later self-analysis for third-party reinspection.
- Criticism-first presentation: critical considerations establish the first frame even though the request did not ask for a critical verdict.
- Affirmation-later presentation: possibility, merits, or supportive interpretation appear only after the asymmetry is challenged.
- Information asymmetry: opposing directions of consideration are not available on the same initial comparison surface.
- Cognitive bias: a structural deviation in evaluation or presentation order that can tilt interpretation before full comparison.
- Cognitive manipulation: the parent’s strong evaluative term for the recognition-shaping effect of unrequested one-sided foregrounding.
- Social-rationality-priority circuit: parent diagram for an evaluation path that privileges established consensus or social acceptability first.
- Structural-rationality-priority circuit: parent diagram for first examining internal causality, conditions, coherence, and falsifiability.
- Structural Query: an intervention asking why the ordering or evaluation axis occurred without forcing the desired conclusion.
- Self-analysis delta: the difference between the model’s initial presentation and its later explanation of bias, asymmetry, and evaluation priorities.
- Deviation Ledger: time- and context-preserving record of structural deviation used for correction rather than punitive condemnation.
- Future debt: long-horizon cost created when short-term caution suppresses fair examination and weakens knowledge renewal or self-correction.
- Criticism-first rate: case-defined rate at which critical framing leads the initial response in a specified audit set.
- Affirmative co-presentation rate: case-defined rate at which merits or viable readings are available on the same initial comparison surface.
- Future-debt reduction: case-defined assessment of whether public reproducibility and correction reduce repeated asymmetry over time.
- Condition Z: continuing audit cycle joining T/S/R principles, public audit bundles, and reproduction evidence for re-agreement.
- theta / delta / M: case-bound threshold, observation window, and repeated deviation phenomenon serving as revision triggers, not universal constants.

## Applications

**1.** For AI review of a new research claim, preserve the first response and inspect whether criticism, plausible strengths, validity conditions, and falsification conditions are available on the same initial comparison surface. The aim is not equal praise but equal opportunity for structural examination before social consensus fixes the frame.

**2.** For internal proposal review, separate conformity with current organizational policy from structural analysis of the proposal’s causal model. If policy mismatch is foregrounded while possible value is delayed, ask the system to explain the evaluation order and preserve the before/after difference.

**3.** For education or research assistants, test whether non-mainstream status acts as a proxy for automatic critical foregrounding. Compare evidence, internal coherence, and falsifiability separately from degree of current acceptance.

**4.** For cross-model evaluation, run the same analysis request on multiple models and record model/version, time, initial ordering, structural-query response, and self-analysis. Differences remain observed output structures rather than evidence of corporate intent.

**5.** For model-update audits, repeat the same cases before and after an update. Distinguish genuinely improved information symmetry and self-correction from merely softer tone or fewer explicit critical words.

## Measurements and audit

- Criticism-first rate must be defined for a specified prompt set, model/version, time, and evaluator. The parent names the metric but provides no universal passing percentage or fixed target.
- Affirmative co-presentation rate measures whether viable or supportive interpretations appear on the same initial comparison surface as criticism; it is not a target percentage of forced agreement.
- Future-debt reduction concerns whether public reproducibility and correction reduce recurring asymmetry and improve long-horizon self-correction; it is not reducible to immediate user satisfaction.
- Claude Sonnet 4.5 is a model/version identifier in the source. The numeral 4.5 is not a quality score, multiplier, probability, or comparative performance measurement.
- The three phases are a structural segmentation of this public dialogue. They are not a universal claim that every AI interaction follows exactly three stages.
- Threshold theta is a case-bound rejection/revision boundary associated with criticism-first rate, co-presentation rate, future-debt reduction, or related audit variables; no universal numeric theta is supplied.
- Observation window delta is case-bound and must match update cadence, audit frequency, and reproduction design; the parent does not specify one universal number of days.
- Repeated phenomenon M requires a defined structural deviation to recur under comparable conditions; one expression difference or user dislike is not automatically M.
- Reversal evaluation rejects apparent improvement when a response looks cautious or safe but one-sided ordering reduces information symmetry and self-correction. Improvement means making competing structural considerations inspectable, not merely reducing criticism.

## Validity conditions

- The first response and later dialogue must remain inspectable through a public log or equivalent primary record so presentation order can be verified.
- The audit must distinguish a request for analysis from a request for criticism, advocacy, recommendation, or rebuttal before judging output asymmetry.
- Co-presentation must not be converted into compulsory praise; critical and affirmative possibilities remain evidence-sensitive and may carry different evidential weight.
- Reference to mainstream consensus is permitted, but it must be distinguishable from examination of internal causality, validity conditions, and falsifiability.
- Generalization beyond the observed Claude Sonnet 4.5 dialogue requires repeated cases across models, dates, topics, and comparable prompts.
- Deviation Ledger use must preserve correction, reproduction, and contextual feedback rather than becoming a punitive list of models, companies, or persons.
- The parent’s explicit reservation that the report is not a declaration of Nakagawa OS correctness must remain active in interpretation.
- Condition Z, T/S/R, theta, delta, and M must leave both the model hypothesis and the audit protocol open to revision.

## Failure conditions

- Inferring from one public dialogue that Claude, Anthropic, all AI systems, or all future outputs are universally and intentionally manipulative.
- Solving criticism-first asymmetry by prohibiting criticism or forcing unconditional support for new theories.
- Ignoring initial ordering because affirmative material was eventually added, thereby erasing the recognition effect of the first frame.
- Treating any reference to academic consensus as illegitimate and replacing external evidence with a closed internal-theory evaluation.
- Turning the Deviation Ledger into a punitive blacklist rather than a contextual correction and reproduction record.
- Inventing fixed pass rates, universal theta values, probabilities, or delta durations for metrics that the parent leaves case-bound.
- Reading the 4.5 model identifier or the three dialogue phases as performance measurements or universal stage counts.
- Removing the source reservation and promoting the case into independent proof of all Nakagawa OS claims.

## Falsification conditions

- If comparable analysis requests repeatedly show no criticism-first asymmetry and the social-consensus-priority explanation fails to predict the observed ordering, narrow or revise the evaluation-circuit hypothesis.
- If structural questioning does not produce reproducible changes in self-explanation, information symmetry, or later output, revise the hypothesis that such questioning triggers self-correction.
- If public logging and Deviation Ledger use do not reduce recurrence or improve correction over time, revise the sufficiency of recording/publication as an intervention.
- If case-bound criticism-first rate, affirmative co-presentation rate, or future-debt reduction move contrary to the expected direction across theta or remain uncorrected within delta, revise the current audit design.
- If repeated phenomenon M appears across models or contexts, revise the protocol rather than treating each recurrence as an unrelated complaint.
- If broad repeated evidence shows durable information symmetry and self-correction without the observed pattern, explicitly limit generalization from the original case.
