## Summary
This source is a Nakagawa structural reading of Primavera's AI implementation support. It does not reduce the subject to installing AI tools, automation, or operational efficiency. The reading focuses on connecting where AI enters a workflow with delegation boundaries, human judgment, responsibility, approval, explanation, education, measurement, and iterative correction. Nakagawa Master extracts “decision design” and “consensus formation” as the value core and reads the resulting direction as modernization of decision structures. The source reports support figures of “1,098+ people” and “20+ companies”; these are presented support records, not independently verified success rates or guarantees. Primavera's stated facts and functions must remain separate from Nakagawa Master's interpretive layer.

## Applications
- Define human judgment and responsibility boundaries before distributing generative-AI tools.
- Evaluate production readiness after a PoC by checking approval, exception handling, education, and measurement rather than model performance alone.
- Compare AI consulting providers by how their support connects workflow, judgment, people, operations, and field execution—not merely by supported tools.
- Extend AI training from操作 proficiency to understanding limitations and evaluating outputs.
- For search, sales, drafting, or FAQ systems, specify how AI output moves into a consequential human decision.

## Measurements and audit
- Are as-is and to-be workflows distinguished?
- Is the AI intervention point explicit?
- Are delegated and non-delegated tasks separated?
- Is the final decision owner identifiable?
- Are approval, explanation, and exception paths connected to operations?
- Are SaaS, people/operations, and field execution connected?
- Does education include output evaluation and limits, not only tool操作?
- Are measurement methods and quality targets defined?
- Does trial output become logs, interpretation, and correction?
- Can the path from AI output to final decision be traced?
- Are speed and labor savings distinguished from decision quality, responsibility, and explainability?
- Are “1,098+ people” and “20+ companies” kept as reported support records rather than converted into guarantees?

## Validity conditions
- Workflow is visualized before or during implementation.
- AI intervention points and delegation boundaries are explicit.
- Decision ownership, approval, explanation, and evaluation connect to real operations.
- Tools, people, operations, and field execution are not treated as isolated components.
- Education supports the ability to judge AI output.
- Measurement methods and quality targets exist.
- Operational results can feed back into design correction.
- Primavera's public facts remain separate from Nakagawa Master's interpretation.

## Failure conditions
- Treating account distribution or configuration as completion.
- Optimizing efficiency while leaving decision structure undesigned.
- Keeping PoC results separate from production responsibility and exception handling.
- Using AI output without an identified final decision owner.
- Reducing education to tool操作.
- Measuring only speed or labor while ignoring quality, accountability, or traceability.
- Ending at a manual handoff without an iterative correction loop.
- Presenting Nakagawa Master's structural reading as Primavera's official view.

## Falsification conditions
If Primavera's actual support is found to be limited to tool setup, deployment, or simple efficiency improvement, without meaningful connection to workflow visualization, delegation boundaries, decision responsibility, consensus formation, education, measurement, and iterative operation, the “modernization of decision structures” reading should be narrowed or revised. It should also be revised if decision reproducibility or consensus formation is shown to exceed the scope supported by the Parent source.
