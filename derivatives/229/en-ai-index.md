## Summary
This Parent is a Nakagawa structural reading, not a corporate description of AI training or implementation support. It separates target-side facts such as AI implementation support, practical sessions, SaaS, people & operations, and field connection from Nakagawa Master’s extracted value kernel: an implementation apparatus that modernizes decision structure. AI adoption can expose ambiguity in who decides, what AI is delegated, what becomes a work product, who owns quality, and how results are returned to improvement. The reading therefore centers on making current/target workflows visible, separating AI and human roles, turning output into operational work products, and connecting operation to verification and improvement. It also preserves why this axis was selected for the external article.

## Concepts
- Target-side facts: AI implementation support, practical sessions, SaaS, people & operations, field connection, and related support scope.
- Surface classification: AI training, tool adoption, operational efficiency.
- Structural exposure: AI adoption making hidden ambiguity in decisions, responsibility, work products, and quality visible.
- As-is / to-be: explicit representation of current workflow and intended workflow.
- Role separation: distinguishing AI-delegated processing from human judgment and responsibility.
- Work-product formation: converting AI output or learning into reusable operational artifacts.
- Decision-structure modernization: Nakagawa Master’s value kernel in which judgment, execution, verification, and improvement become connected and reproducible.

## Causal chain
AI adoption → ambiguity in decisions/responsibility/work products becomes visible → as-is/to-be visualization → AI/human role separation → explicit judgment and responsibility points → work-product formation → operational connection → quality/result verification → improvement feedback → modernization of decision structure.

## State model
pre-adoption ambiguity / structural exposure / visualization / role design / work-product formation / operational connection / verification and improvement / modernized decision structure.

## Applications
When assessing AI training or implementation programs, inspect who makes decisions, what AI handles, what humans remain accountable for, what work products enter operations, and how quality and outcomes are verified, rather than treating user counts or speed gains as sufficient evidence. Apply the comparison elsewhere only where similar structural ambiguity is actually present.

## Measurements and audit
Separate target-side facts, surface classification, decision owner, AI/human roles, responsibility points, work-product definition, operational integration, quality/result verification, improvement loop, Nakagawa Master’s value kernel, the reason for selecting that kernel, the reason it became the external-article axis, and the boundary from company-official claims. Do not invent ROI, productivity, or time-saving thresholds absent from the Parent.

## Validity conditions
The reading applies where judgment, role, work product, operation, verification, and improvement form a connected structure that explains implementation value better than AI usage or speed alone. The structural-reading meta layer must preserve target facts separately from Nakagawa Master’s interpretation and external-article construction decision.

## Failure conditions
It fails if flattened into generic AI training, tool adoption, or efficiency; if training completion is equated with operational success; if work-product and verification layers disappear; if Nakagawa Master’s reading is presented as Primavera’s official self-definition; or if the reasons for kernel selection and external-article framing are omitted.

## Falsification conditions
Narrow or revise the kernel if sustained implementation value is repeatedly explained by mechanisms unrelated to decision/role/work-product/verification structuring, or if formalizing those structures does not connect to operational quality or reproducibility.

## Required distinctions
target-side facts / Nakagawa Master interpretation; AI implementation support / decision-structure modernization; speed gain / judgment quality; AI output / operational work product; learning completion / operational integration; adoption / verification-improvement loop; value kernel / company-official view; reason for kernel selection / reason for external-article axis.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not state that Primavera officially defines itself as an apparatus for modernizing decision structure. Do not infer automatic organizational modernization from AI adoption. Do not deny the practical value of efficiency; separate it from decision, responsibility, and verification structure. Do not replace the specific external-article reason with a generic phrase such as “social translation.”
