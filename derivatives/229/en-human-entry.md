## 15-Second Explanation
This Parent is not an introduction to AI training or tool implementation. It is Nakagawa Master’s structural reading of the value of AI implementation support as **an implementation device that modernizes decision-making structures**. It retains as the value core the structure that makes visible the ambiguity of judgment, responsibility, deliverables, and evaluation exposed by introducing AI, then returns it to role design, deliverable creation, operation, verification, and improvement, including why this axis was used for the external article.

## Why It Is Needed
Even if introducing AI increases work speed, if it remains unclear who adopts the output, what quality is considered acceptable, what is returned to the work as a deliverable, and who corrects errors, the implementation effect will remain personal and non-reproducible. The Parent reads AI implementation as an opportunity to make visible and redesign an existing organization’s decision-making structure.

## Practical Process
1. Separate the subject-side facts—AI implementation support, practical sessions, SaaS, and so on—from Nakagawa Master’s reading.
2. Visualize current and desired work as as-is / to-be.
3. Separate the processes entrusted to AI from the processes for which humans retain judgment and responsibility.
4. Turn AI outputs and learning outcomes into deliverables usable in practice.
5. Connect the deliverables to the practical workflow.
6. Verify quality and operational results, and return improvement to the next operation.
7. State why this structure was made the value core and the central axis of the external article.

## Application Example
Even if each person can use AI after training, if the judge of output quality and the point of responsibility are unclear and deliverables do not return to the work process, implementation remains temporary efficiency improvement. On the other hand, if roles, judgment, deliverables, and verification are established and improvement can be repeated, it can be read as the modernization of decision-making structures, the Parent’s value core.

## Success Criteria
Success means being able to explain not only whether AI was introduced or how fast it is, but who judges, how AI and humans divide the work, what becomes a deliverable, how it returns to the work, and how it is verified and improved—while also preserving why the external article adopted this value core.

## Limitations
Systematization alone does not guarantee implementation results. Work characteristics, data quality, expertise, organizational culture, and other factors may also exist. This derivative does not independently guarantee the subject company’s results and does not add productivity gains, ROI, or other official conditions not present in the Parent Original.

## Preventing Misreading
“An implementation device that modernizes decision-making structures” is Nakagawa Master’s structural reading, not Primavera’s official corporate definition. It is not an argument denying the value of AI training or efficiency improvement; it is a reading that examines the connection among judgment, deliverables, operation, and verification separately because those alone do not sufficiently explain support quality.
