## 15-Second Explanation
If Mysurance is seen only as a “digital insurance company” or a “company for oshikatsu insurance,” attention goes to product names. Nakagawa Master’s structural reading looks at the design before that: **put daily behavior such as traveling, going on a trip to support someone, or using a smartphone first; translate the financial anxiety that arises along the way into insurance; and make it selectable within the necessary service path**. The value core is “behavior-origin insurance translation.”

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Even if insurance is correctly designed as a system, it becomes a distant product if people cannot understand “which of my behaviors it relates to.” On the other hand, if coverage conditions are made vague by leaning too far into behavioral context, trust as a financial product is lost.

This structure is necessary because a translation layer is needed between people’s concrete situations and insurance’s specialized contractual structure. The symbolic nature of oshikatsu cancellation insurance is not because it is hobby culture. It is because the behavior of traveling to an event, the anxiety of cancellation costs, coverage conditions, and the enrollment path connect in one context.

**Why this value core was selected.** Product categories such as “digital insurance,” “small-amount short-term insurance,” and “oshikatsu insurance” do not show how anxieties are converted into financial functions or why they can be selected at the necessary point. Nakagawa Master values the translation order **daily behavior → concrete anxiety → insurable loss → understandable coverage → selection path at the point of behavior**, rather than placing the insurance product first.

**Why this core was foregrounded in the external article.** If external readers are told only “new insurance for oshikatsu,” their understanding leans toward topicality or product ideas. By making the structure that translates anxiety in daily behavior into finance and makes it selectable when needed the main axis, the Parent socially translates the individual product as a design problem of Embedded Insurance and understanding people’s lives. This is not Mysurance’s official definition, but the value core Nakagawa Master selected from the subject facts.

## Practical Process
1. First identify the behavior people actually engage in. Do not start with an insurance category.
2. Break down where financial anxiety or opportunity loss occurs in that behavior.
3. Separate the parts of that anxiety that can be underwritten as insurance.
4. Translate coverage, exclusions, and conditions into words people can understand.
5. Place a selection path where the behavior occurs, such as booking, purchase, or service use.
6. Align meaning not only at enrollment but through the claims experience when an accident or cancellation occurs.
7. Correct gaps in understanding from actual use, inquiries, and claims.

## Application Example
For travel bookings, instead of making people look for insurance separately after selling the trip, cancellation-cost anxiety can be presented understandably within the booking behavior. For oshikatsu travel, translate not only the feeling that “the event matters,” but into concrete losses such as cancellation costs for transportation and lodging. For smartphones, connect the behavior of carrying a device daily to risks such as damage or theft.

What these share is the order of moving from daily behavior to the necessary financial function, rather than placing an insurance product first and fitting it afterward.

## Success Criterion
Success is not judged only by enrollment numbers. At minimum, users must understand the relationship between their behavior and coverage, have few misunderstandings including exclusions, be able to choose at the necessary point, and not encounter a major discrepancy at claims time from what they understood at enrollment.

Even if topicality is high, if the connection between behavior and coverage is weak, conditions are difficult to understand, or meaning reverses at claims time, it is difficult to call the structure a success.

## Limitations
Designing from behavior does not mean every anxiety can become insurance. Underwriting feasibility, laws and regulations, rates, the definition of loss, information management, responsibilities in sales and solicitation, and division of roles with partners are required. Also, embedding something in daily context is different from enrolling people automatically and unconsciously.

This derivative does not recommend enrollment in any individual product or guarantee payment. Confirm specific conditions in Mysurance’s official information, policy terms, and so on.

- Do not read “easy to understand” as “omit conditions.”
