## Summary
The Parent reads Kurashiteku and “Houkan AI Ope” not merely as home-visit-nursing AI, medical DX, document generation, or office-work efficiency. Nakagawa Master identifies a deeper structure: capturing tacit institutional judgment held by experienced workers, converting it into reproducible execution assets that AI can use, and connecting those assets to completed operational work and economic recovery. Home-visit nursing is treated as a first implementation, not as proof of universal transfer.

## Concepts
- institutional industry: a domain where accuracy of connection to rules, claims, and administrative processes materially affects continuity and economic recovery.
- tacit knowledge: case judgment, exception handling, wording, check sequences, and return-handling know-how held by experienced workers.
- tacit-knowledge capture: making expert judgment articulable and reviewable.
- AI assetization: converting captured judgment into data, evaluation criteria, and workflows.
- execution asset: knowledge represented in a form that can drive real operational completion rather than remaining documentation.
- completion responsibility: moving value from advice to verified completion points such as checking, transmission, billing linkage, and return handling.
- first implementation: home-visit nursing as an initial test domain rather than an automatic endpoint.

## Causal chain
institutional complexity → case and exception judgment required → judgment becomes person-dependent → accuracy gaps in applications and claims → omissions, returned claims, uncollected revenue, billing delays → effects on revenue, cash flow, and operational continuity → labor shortages make expertise harder to retain → tacit judgment must be captured → articulation and expert review → conversion into training data, criteria, and workflows → AI execution connected to real workflow → documents checked, sent, billed, and returns handled → execution results feed the next improvement cycle.

## Applications
1. Audit home-visit-nursing AI by whether institutional work actually closes, not only by time saved.
2. Design medical and care DX while preserving human care, relationships, and professional judgment as separate responsibilities.
3. Test analogous structures in pharmacy, construction, professional services, or administrative procedures where expert judgment materially affects economic outcomes.
4. Design AI products around closed loops of checking, external-system linkage, exception handling, and completion confirmation rather than chat accuracy alone.
5. Separate company/service facts from Nakagawa Master’s structural reading so interpretive language is not misattributed as an official corporate claim.

## Measurements and audit
- Do not measure value only through task efficiency; trace institutional-connection accuracy and economic effects.
- Track omissions, returned claims, uncollected revenue, billing delays, and related workflow losses where supported by the Parent.
- Identify specific person-dependent judgments rather than labeling all expertise as tacit knowledge.
- Check whether captured knowledge becomes executable criteria and workflows rather than static documentation.
- Verify that AI reaches required completion points rather than stopping at advice or drafts.
- Verify human review for exceptions, rule changes, and incorrect execution.
- Verify that execution results feed back into data, criteria, and workflow updates.
- Preserve responsibility boundaries between institutional processing and human care or professional judgment.

## Validity conditions
The structure is valid when institutional-connection accuracy materially affects economic recovery or continuity; important judgments and exceptions are person-dependent; domain experts can expose and review them; the judgments can be converted into reproducible criteria, data, and workflows; AI execution can safely connect to real completion points; human review handles exceptions, rule changes, and corrections; and execution results feed continuous improvement.

## Failure conditions
The structure fails when AI stops at advice or text generation, tacit-knowledge capture is too shallow to reproduce important exceptions, assets fail to track institutional change, review structures cannot detect incorrect execution, AI increases rework or responsibility ambiguity, relevant losses do not improve, or transfer is attempted where equivalent structural conditions are absent.

## Falsification conditions
The structural hypothesis should be narrowed or revised if persistent evidence shows that person-dependent expertise has little relationship to claim or application accuracy, deeper AI execution does not improve workflow completion or relevant economic losses, institutional change and exceptions make judgment assets unmaintainable, or comparable transfer conditions do not exist in other institutional industries.
