## 15-Second Explanation
This Parent is not reading the company as “a company that makes administrative work easier with AI for home-visit nursing.” It reads a structure that extracts judgments accumulated in skilled practitioners' minds in regulated industries, converts them into reproducible forms that AI can handle, and connects them to actual work completion and business recovery. The value core selected by Nakagawa Master is **turning implicit knowledge into executable assets**.

## Why It Is Needed
In regulated industries such as home-visit nursing, profitability and cash flow depend not only on the quality of field service, but also on correctly connecting to government administration, insurance, billing, add-on fees, rejected claims, and submitted documents. Processing systems include areas dependent on experienced judgment: case decisions, exception handling, submission wording, checking order, and how to correct a rejected claim.

When judgment becomes person-dependent, whether a skilled person is available becomes a difference in application accuracy; through missed add-on fees, rejected claims, uncollected revenue, and delayed billing, that difference becomes a management difference. The Parent therefore reads the conversion of skilled judgment into reproducible executable assets, rather than a mere manual, as the value core.

**Why this value core was selected.** “AI for home-visit nursing,” “document generation,” and “administrative efficiency” alone do not show the difference between AI answering quickly and reproducing the judgments required by the system to close work all the way through. Because exception judgment and checking order connect to application, billing, and collection accuracy in regulated industries, Nakagawa Master selected **conversion into executable judgment assets**, not knowledge presentation, as the value point.

**Why this core was foregrounded in the external article.** Merely listing AI functions for outside readers would confine the case to home-visit nursing. The Parent therefore centers the transferable structure of recovering person-dependent implicit knowledge and turning it into assets AI can execute, translating the first home-visit-nursing implementation into a question that can be considered across regulated industries. This is not Kurashiteku's official self-definition; it is the interpretive axis Nakagawa Master selected from the target facts.

## Practical Process
```text
Identify the loss point in the regulated industry
→ Identify judgments dependent on skilled practitioners
→ Verbalize judgments, exception handling, and checking order
→ Have domain experts review them
→ Convert them into training data, evaluation criteria, and workflows
→ Connect the AI agent to the actual work process
→ Complete document creation, checking, sending, billing, rejected-claim handling, etc.
→ Return execution results, exceptions, and correction history to the next improvement
```

The important point is not only that AI answers intelligently, but that skilled judgment becomes an “executable asset.”

## Applications
**1. Home-visit nursing.** Look not only at reduced time after AI introduction, but at how far system connections such as missed add-on fees, rejected claims, delayed billing, and document sending have been closed.

**2. Medical and care DX.** While preserving care and relationships handled by people, design separately how to reduce person-dependence in system processing.

**3. Other regulated industries.** In dispensing, construction, professional services, and administrative procedures, check whether the same conditions exist: heavy system connections, important submissions, skilled judgment, and management impact.

## Success Criteria
- Skilled-person-dependent judgments can be concretely recovered.
- Judgments are converted into reproducible forms such as evaluation criteria and workflows.
- AI connects beyond advice to the required work-completion point.
- Human review functions for exceptions, system changes, and error correction.
- Losses such as rejected claims, missed add-on fees, uncollected revenue, and delayed billing move toward improvement.
- Execution results return to the next improvement.
- The responsibility boundary with care performed by people is maintained.

## Limits
The Parent does not claim that AI magically solves labor shortages or fully replaces interpersonal care. Human domains remain, including contact with users, relationships, care quality, and clinical or specialist judgment.

Nor does it assert that the structure established in home-visit nursing can be transplanted unchanged to every regulated industry. Conditions of the same type are needed: heavy system connections, important submissions, strong dependence on skilled people, judgment quality directly affecting management, and difficulty maintaining person-dependence under labor shortages.

## Preventing Misreading
- Do not confuse public facts about Kurashiteku / Houkan AI Ope with Nakagawa Master's structural reading.
- Do not treat “implicit-knowledge conversion company” or “turning implicit knowledge into executable assets” as the company's official definition.
- Do not expand “AI that finishes the work, not AI that answers” into total replacement by AI.
- Do not generalize the home-visit-nursing structure unconditionally to other regulated industries.
- Do not drop the reason for selecting the value core in the external article and return to a mere AI feature introduction.
- Do not guarantee success from adoption counts or technical terms alone.
