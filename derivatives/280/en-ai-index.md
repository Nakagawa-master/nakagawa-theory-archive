## Summary
The Parent reframes social memory from a static archive into dynamic reconstruction. When principles, actions, and records repeat in phase, and periodicity, temperature, margin, observability, and counterexample tolerance remain stable, observers may develop a provisional-completion perception that “the same coherence will come again.” Future alignment can then be brought forward as present agreement. This is not historical revision or direct memory manipulation. It is a nonverbal structural device based on public traces, repair rhythm, primary logs, vocabulary dictionaries, reversibility, criticism, and T/S/R.

## Causal chain
past-record-centered legitimacy -> slow recognition of change -> daily nonverbal repetition shapes collective assumptions -> principles/actions/records repeat in phase -> periodicity, temperature, and margin stabilize -> coherence becomes externally observable -> the next coherent state is anticipated -> rhythm × observability × counterexample tolerance -> provisional completion / silent consensus -> future alignment is brought forward as present agreement -> social memory shifts to dynamic reconstruction -> counterexamples trigger visible repair rhythm -> primary logs, dictionaries, public abstracts, and T/S/R preserve provenance and reversibility -> memory coherence is re-audited -> a repeatable social habit emerges.

## Applications
Organizations can standardize the rhythm of decision, handoff, repair, and explanation so new members can replay judgment from dictionaries and primary logs. Public systems can preserve pre-change records while layering current explanations. AI knowledge systems can update current interpretations without erasing origin, terminology, and primary differences. Brands, education, and communities can audit whether conduct, records, and closure remain at the same temperature rather than optimizing message volume.

## Measurements and audit
Audit memory coherence, rhythm compliance, phase stability S, time-to-reagreement, leakage/misallocation, memory discontinuity, reputation cascades, reproducibility of counterexample repair, vocabulary alignment, provenance across primary logs and public abstracts, and effectiveness of T/S/R as a bundle. Do not invent unpublished theta thresholds, internal equations, or direct memory-modification procedures.

## Validity conditions
Principles, conduct, and records remain in phase; update/publication/response cadence is externally comparable; tone and editing density remain stable; silence and margin are structurally available; repetition is observable; counterexamples can be answered with reproducible repair; primary logs, dictionaries, role boundaries, and public abstracts remain traceable; criticism, withdrawal, re-agreement, and T/S/R are usable.

## Failure conditions
Social memory is reduced to storage volume; past records are deleted and called reconstruction; the framework is used for direct manipulation of personal or group memory; coercive conformity or reputation engineering is justified as rhythm; one of the three layers is replaced by performance; counterexamples are removed; public abstracts become propaganda; unpublished thresholds or manipulation procedures are inferred.

## Falsification conditions
Revise the social-memory reconstruction hypothesis if in-phase repetition is followed by worsening memory coherence, falling rhythm compliance or phase stability, longer re-agreement, more leakage or misallocation, irreproducible repair, increased memory discontinuity, reputation cascades, or coercive conformity.

## Required distinctions
social memory / record volume; dynamic reconstruction / historical revision; rewriting / deletion; structural rhythm / publicity repetition; provisional completion / prophecy; silent consensus / forced conformity; observability / achievement display; counterexample tolerance / hiding failure; repair rhythm / impression management; public abstract / internal log.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not translate “rewriting social memory” into alteration of history, evidence, or personal memory. The Parent addresses how repeated coherence updates collective assumptions while preserving traces and counterevidence. Preserve the strong direction toward future agreement being brought into the present, but do not strengthen it into prophecy, universal inevitability, or direct consciousness control. Do not infer secret thresholds, memory-modification algorithms, or private logs.
