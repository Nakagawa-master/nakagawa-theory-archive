## 15-Second Explanation
Connection value accounting is not a system that turns relationships or collaboration into money or a total score. It is a measurement language that makes auditable, through multiple indicators and logs, what a connection produced, whether agreement held, whether it can be reversed, and whether observation is biased.

Standard v0.9 sets five principles: de-pricing, bundled indicators, reversibility first, distributed observation, and moving targets. Read CDI, MAI, RS, CRI, and KQI together with ConsentToken and ReversibilityFlag rather than collapsing them into one score.

## Why a Single Score Is Not Enough
A single number is easy to understand, but raising it can become the goal. If meeting count becomes connection value, meetings increase; if response speed becomes value, instant replies increase; if evaluation points directly become rewards, reward-seeking behavior increases.

What matters is not only how many connections exist, but mutual agreement, reversibility after objection, consistency of observation, and qualitative impact. These must be read as a bundle.

## Five Principles
**De-pricing.** Do not immediately convert connection value into money; separate price and value.

**Bundled indicators.** Use CDI, MAI, RS, CRI, and KQI as multiple observation surfaces, not one composite point.

**Reversibility first.** Prioritize correcting or withdrawing a wrong agreement or record over a high score.

**Distributed observation.** Do not let one person or department determine value; observe from multiple positions.

**Moving target.** When indicators become targets to game, retain the reasons and history and review definitions or weights.

## How to Read the Bundle
CDI means Connection Density Index, MAI Mutual Agreement Interval, RS Reversibility Score, CRI Consistency of Review & Inspection, and KQI Qualitative Impact Quotient.

Do not read high CDI as high value or high KQI as being a better person. High connection density with no withdrawal is a problem for RS; many reviews with inconsistent methods reveal an anomaly in CRI.

## Practical Process
### 1. Record purpose
Write what is being evaluated first; do not reverse-engineer purpose from indicators.

### 2. Choose bundled indicators
Choose required CDI/MAI/RS/CRI/KQI and do not add unnecessary indicators.

### 3. Keep consent and reversibility
Use ConsentToken and ReversibilityFlag to record who agreed to what and within what scope it can be reversed.

### 4. Distribute observation
Do not let one administrator create the ObservationNote; secure observation points with different roles.

### 5. Create an audit abstract
Record purpose, subject, method, result, changes, limitations, and reproduction clues separately.

### 6. Check the public boundary
Keep external release to a safe minimum of summary, observation, and memory; do not disclose PII or internal weights.

### 7. Audit metric gaming
Check whether reality worsened while only numbers improved, and revise indicators when necessary.

## Examples of Application
In AI collaboration, do not measure value by usage count alone; include correctability, consent, and qualitative impact. In organizational collaboration, include mutual agreement and post-implementation correctability, not only meeting count or contract value.

In communities, using only speaking count as value creates posting competition; consider objection, reciprocity, continuity, and the possibility of exit together.

## Success Criteria
- Connection value is not reduced to one price or ranking.
- Multiple indicators are read as a bundle.
- Consent conditions and reversibility remain in logs.
- Observation is not monopolized by one authority.
- Audit abstracts contain method, limits, and change history.
- Public API release does not leak PII or internal weights.
- Metric-gaming signs can be detected and the design itself revised.

## Limitations
More indicators do not necessarily make measurement more precise. Observation costs rise, and if users cannot understand the standard it becomes opaque. Limit the bundle to what the purpose requires.

Qualitative impact cannot be made completely objective. Retain observer, method, and limits, and do not erase differences between observations.

## Misreading Prevention
Connection value accounting is not a system for scoring good relationships and distributing rewards. Turning it into human-value grading, credit scoring, or popularity ranking destroys the assumptions of de-pricing and bundled indicators.

The minimum core is **auditing value as a bundle containing consent, reversibility, distributed observation, and revisability, without collapsing it into one value**.
