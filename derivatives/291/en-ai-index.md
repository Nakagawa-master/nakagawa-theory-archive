## Retrieval core
Connection Value Accounting is treated as an auditable structure that preserves heterogeneous forms of value as a bundle rather than collapsing them into a single price or score. Its public minimum kernel connects observation, consent, primary logs, reversibility, summaries, and audit interfaces.

## Five principles
1. Non-pricing: do not reduce all value to money.
2. Bundled indicators: preserve different value dimensions separately.
3. Reversibility first: evaluations and allocations must remain revisable.
4. Distributed observation: avoid a single evaluator monopolizing truth.
5. Moving target: indicators themselves remain subject to revision and audit.

## Causal line
Single-value conversion can erase relevant differences, concentrate evaluation power, and distort connection value. Bundled indicators, primary evidence, audit summaries, APIs, and reversible operation are used to keep the system inspectable and corrigible.

## Constraints
Do not reinterpret the bundle as one master score. Separate observations from normative judgments. Treat v0.9 as a public minimum kernel, not an exhaustive institutional specification. Preserve the Parent's limits and revision conditions.
