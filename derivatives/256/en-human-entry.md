## 15-Second Explanation
This Parent Original reads the future not as a simple statistical forecast but as the result of current structural contradictions accumulating over time. If friction between monetary causality and connection causality approaches a critical point, the design is to operate old and new functions in parallel and use a policy window and reversibility to advance “ordered replacement” in stages, rather than destroying the old order all at once.

## Why It Is Needed
If discussion of the future becomes only a prediction of what will happen in which year, design knowledge is lost when the prediction misses. The Parent’s value lies not in the dates themselves, but in showing the causal structure of friction accumulation → critical point → policy window → dual operation → functional replacement. By observing this structure, transition design can be improved while revising timing and intensity.

## Practical Process
1. Identify current structural contradictions.
2. Observe symptoms and root causes separately.
3. Check whether partial correction of the old system reduces friction.
4. Run alternative functions in parallel within a limited domain.
5. Treat the critical point and policy window as observation conditions, without asserting them.
6. Record differences and divergence between old and new functions in double ledgers and primary logs.
7. Replace functions one at a time while retaining rollback and renewed agreement.

## Application Examples
In institutional reform, do not immediately abolish old rules; run new rules in parallel within a small domain. In organizational transformation, observe old KPIs alongside new relationship and re-reference indicators for a set period. In regional measures, retain external rights while introducing connection-based operation on a limited basis, and check social acceptance and side effects.

## Success Criteria
- Structural friction can be observed.
- The effects and limits of partial correction can be distinguished.
- Old and new functions are operated in parallel.
- The transition includes self-directed adoption rather than coercion.
- Ledger divergence and side effects can be detected.
- Rollback and renewed agreement are possible.
- The date and inevitability hypotheses remain falsifiable.

## Limitations
Do not use the years 2030, 2035, 2040, and 2050–2055 as certain predictions. The Parent Original presents strong future hypotheses, but its audit summary also retains falsification and revision conditions. Also, ordered replacement does not mean the immediate abolition of money or exclusion of outsiders by connection communities.

## Preventing Misreading
Do not turn the word “inevitability” into a meaning of no need for observation or impossibility of falsification. The Parent’s design value lies in making structural friction observable and designing alternative functions and rollback in advance for the critical point. Read it not as a timeline for waiting for the future, but as a roadmap for designing transition while testing future lines.
