## 15-Second Explanation
This Parent Original is a design theory for a Civilization OS. It argues that even if individuals and organizations take good actions, downstream efforts will continue to be pulled in the wrong direction if civilization’s evaluation function—what it counts as success—continues to reward exploitation and excessive burden. It therefore seeks to resynchronize the L7 value function with Tao, a circulation channel of minimum resistance, and to create a structure in which contribution C can pass into outcome S by placing a connection-reward society responsible for “recording → linking → transforming” at L6.

## Why It Is Needed
The Parent Original’s problem setting is that even if inequality, environmental destruction, institutional fatigue, and similar issues are handled separately, the same distortions will recur unless the evaluation function above them changes. It organizes the deep distortions into five forms: “single-objective convergence,” “discounting the future,” “absolutizing ownership,” “severing correspondence,” and “feedback deficiency.”

The dark equation `S = 0.1C + 0.9E`, placed to make the result highly visible, expresses an in-text model of a civilization structure in which exploitation and excessive burden E are more readily converted into success and reward S than contribution C. It is not a universally measured statistical coefficient.

The Parent Original does not try to reverse this structure through moral condemnation alone. It places `S = C × 1.0` as a new L7 and tunes the value function toward converting contribution into outcome. However, reality will not move merely by stating an equation, so it says that a connection infrastructure must be built at L6.

## Practical Process
**1. See what current S rewards.** Check whether only sales, authority, name recognition, and short-term results are highly valued, while future debt, burdens on others, environmental burdens, and invisible care are buried underneath.

**2. Record contribution C as causality.** Make it traceable who changed what in which structure, and how that change affected subsequent judgments, options, and burdens, rather than merely saying someone “worked hard” or is “well regarded.”

**3. Link to the necessary nodes.** Do not close contribution inside an organizational evaluation table; connect it to the individuals, organizations, projects, communities, and other nodes where it can work most effectively. The resonance market in the Parent Original considers this connection not only through price but also through structural fit.

**4. Check purity across layers.** Trace back not only to L1 results but also to the L2 process, L3 institutions, L4 cognition, and L5 design, and check whether E has entered the outcome. The audit is for tuning, not punishment.

**5. Separate harmful friction from healthy friction.** Remove opaque procedures, arbitrary evaluation, and meaningless competition. Retain expert review, consensus building, risk assessment, and confirmation of long-term effects. The Parent Original seeks “safe friction,” not “zero friction.”

**6. Transform from L6 to L7.** Convert recorded and linked C into S by checking it against a value function that asks how much it amplifies circulation, organizes the structure over the long term, and reduces E.

**7. Return feedback upward.** Secure a route by which field-level discomfort and anomalies are not simply processed at L1–L3 and ended, but can revise the premises, institutions, and evaluation function at L4–L7 when necessary.

## Examples of Application
### Organizational Evaluation Systems
If evaluation is based only on short-term sales, actions that externalize future customer loss, burdens on other departments, and quality deterioration may receive high S. In the Parent Original’s reading, separate C and E across the process, institutions, cognition, and structural design—not only the L1 result called sales—and connect and evaluate actions that truly increase circulation.

### Research, Education, and Care
There is C that is difficult to reflect immediately in market prices but expands future options or reduces debt. Rather than treating it as low value because “the amount is small,” record its causal contribution and examine whether it can be connected to the necessary nodes.

### Governance
If a scandal is closed with individual punishment, the structure producing the same anomaly remains. Return the anomaly signal upstream and correct wherever the cause lies among institutions, evaluation, ownership, information routes, and the value function.

## Success Criteria
Success is not the declaration of an ideal equation. Following the Parent Original’s causality, at least the following need to be observable.

- C can be tracked as a causal sequence without disappearing.
- C is connected to the necessary structures.
- E is not increasing together with the increase in S.
- Long-term debt and external burdens have not disappeared from evaluation.
- An audit spanning L1–L5 is functioning.
- Harmful friction decreases while healthy friction remains.
- Anomaly signals from the field return upward.
- Circulation among individuals, organizations, and civilization becomes harder to sever.

In the Parent Original’s audit summary, transparency, correspondence, and circulation are reevaluated. If declining circulation, a rising exploitation coefficient, broken feedback, failed correspondence, connection saturation, L6 congestion, and local collapse of the value function continue, the design hypothesis should be revised or rejected.

## Limitations
- `0.1`, `0.9`, and `1.0` cannot be upgraded by the official derivative into empirically measured coefficients.
- Tao is defined structurally within the Parent Original and should not be treated as an established law of physics in general.
- A resonance market does not mean the complete abolition of price markets.
- Autonomous decentralization does not mean abandoning responsibility or management.
- Multi-layer auditing is not an omnipotent value judge and presupposes complementarity between AI and humans.
- The Civilization OS is not Tao itself; the Parent Original describes it as one partial structure within Tao.

## Preventing Misreading
If this Parent Original is reduced to the moral claim that “good people should be rewarded,” the design of the L6 connection infrastructure disappears. Conversely, reading it as “there is an equation, therefore it has been scientifically proven” goes beyond the model character of the Parent Original.

Likewise, a connection-reward society does not directly mean a “kind community” or a society where “everyone gets along.” What the Parent Original emphasizes is civilization infrastructure that records contribution as causality, links it to the necessary structures, audits it including long-term burdens, and transforms it into outcomes in line with the value function.
