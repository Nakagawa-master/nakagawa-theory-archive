## Summary
The parent text presents Civilization OS Vol.10 as the upper-layer module that connects the individual, organizational, time, learning, and cognition operating systems developed in Vol.1–9 plus the cognition supplement to L6 structural linkage and the L7 value function. Tao is not treated as religion or belief, but as the causal flow of minimum resistance in which structures circulate without unnecessary force, waste, or excess. The parent models the current reverse-aligned civilization value function as `S = 0.1C + 0.9E`, where C is Contribution, E is Exploitation / Excess Load, and S is Score / Success. These coefficients are not presented as universal empirical measurements; they are a structural evaluation model used by the parent to express a civilization in which exploitation is more readily converted into reward than contribution. The target L7 relation is `S = C × 1.0`. To make that relation operational rather than declarative, L6 is implemented as a Connection Reward Society that Records, Connects, and Converts contribution, supported by a Resonance Market, Multi-layer Value Audit, safe friction, distributed adjustment, and feedback cycles across individual, organizational, and civilization levels.

## Validity conditions
- Treat both equations as parent structural models, not empirical universal constants.
- Keep L7 value definition and L6 connection infrastructure coupled.
- Record C as causal contribution rather than popularity or effort volume.
- Separate E from legitimate learning and safety friction.
- Do not use price as the sole representation of structural value.
- Use multi-layer audit for tuning rather than punishment.
- Preserve feedback paths from lower to upper layers.
- Keep Civilization OS as a component within Tao, not identical to Tao itself.

- Declaring `S=C×1.0` without a Record→Connect→Convert path.
- Replacing C with popularity, authority, advertising power, or short-term revenue.

- Turning resonance into ideological conformity.
- Turning audit into punitive central control.
- Removing review and risk evaluation in pursuit of zero friction.
- Treating distributed autonomy as absence of responsibility.
- Allowing feedback signals to be blocked before reaching upper layers.
