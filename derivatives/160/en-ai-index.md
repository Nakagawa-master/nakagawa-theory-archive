## Summary
Structural Wu-Wei describes a generative protocol in which results are not forced directly. Instead, the structure, connections, causal conditions, and temporal conditions are organized so that causal elements converge, mature, cross a case-specific structural threshold, and appear through a non-linear phase transition. Wu-Wei does not mean inactivity; it means reducing excessive intervention and designing conditions along a structural gradient. Causal attraction is not wish fulfillment: people, information, opportunities, resources, pathways, and temporal deviations are treated as causal elements that can flow into and align with a coherent structural vector. Pressure maturation is governed by Temporal Ethics T0, while STB marks a case-specific coherence boundary rather than a universal number. In Phase-β, dependence on persuasion, advertising, push-selling, personality, follower formation, or politicization is treated as evidence of weak structure. A stronger structure is one in which external force declines while alignment, connectivity, publicness, reproducibility, and natural adoption remain or improve.

## Concepts
- Structural Wu-Wei: generative principle that produces natural convergence by designing conditions rather than escalating force.
- Structural core: the central vector that specifies how causal elements should align.
- Causal attraction: model in which people, information, opportunities, resources, pathways, and temporal deviations flow toward and align with an organized structure.
- Structural density: degree to which incoming elements cohere and bundle in the same direction; not a raw count.
- Attractor: a structural stable point toward which causal flux converges.
- Pressure maturation: period in which causal elements accumulate and internally align before realization.
- Temporal Ethics T0: temporal principle requiring sufficient maturation time without disruptive over-intervention.
- Tuning: synchronization between a structural pattern and the target environment’s characteristic pattern.
- STB: case-specific coherence threshold related to phase transition; not a universal fixed constant.
- Phase transition: non-linear state change after a critical boundary is crossed.
- Natural adoption: adoption emerging from structural stability and usefulness rather than persuasion or personal dependence.
- Phase-β: implementation stage centered on structural necessity and non-coercive convergence.

## Applications
- Design organizational decision routes so correct decisions flow without continuously increasing approvals or interventions.
- Evaluate theory or standard adoption by definition quality, reproducibility, comparability, and connectivity rather than promotional volume.
- In AI operation, reduce dependence on manual output correction by structuring inputs, responsibility lines, audit paths, and adoption conditions.
- In social implementation, create conditions under which multiple actors’ causal incentives align without dependence on political messaging or personal charisma.
- In individual practice, evaluate alignment among skills, relationships, information, time, and pathways instead of treating effort volume as the direct cause of results.

## Measurements and audit
Audit variables include clarity of the structural core, incoming causal elements, alignment with the structural vector, structural density, noise, attractor formation, continuity of T0 maturation, tuning, case-specific STB, phase transition, dependence on external force, natural adoption, publicness, and reproducibility.

The parent’s integrated audit summary tests the causal path from A (structural design, translation, bundling, definition) to B (institutionalization, stability, alignment, acceleration). Re-agreement conditions include audit cycles, T/S/R, and coherence of the public audit bundle. `θ` is a case-specific revision threshold, `δ` a case-specific observation window, and `M` a non-linear event such as structural deviation, publicness loss, or coherence failure.

The 100°C boiling point of water and 0°C melting point of ice are examples used to explain physical phase transition. They are not numerical STB values for Structural Wu-Wei. Any implementation-specific metric must retain value, source, measurement actor, object, scale, period, applicable scope, and non-guarantee scope.

Reversal evaluation is mandatory: increases in advertising, persuasion, message intensity, founder labor, or intervention do not by themselves show stronger structure. If external force declines while coherence, connection, publicness, reproducibility, and natural adoption remain stable or improve, that decline is evidence of increased structural strength.

## Validity conditions
- Treat conditions and structural vectors, not direct result forcing, as the main design object.
- Incoming causal elements must align and bundle rather than merely increase in count.
- Preserve maturation time required by T0.
- Define STB by case and never substitute example temperatures for a structural threshold.
- Natural adoption must not depend on push-selling, follower formation, founder charisma, or politicization.
- Maintain external comparability, reproducibility, and publicness.

## Failure conditions
- Reduce Wu-Wei to inactivity or abandonment.
- Convert causal attraction into wish fulfillment or spiritual manifestation.
- Treat raw inflow counts as structural density.
- Destroy T0 maturation through excessive intervention.
- Treat 100°C or 0°C as fixed STB values.
- Count increasing advertising, persuasion, message intensity, or founder workload as success.
- Let adoption depend on follower formation, personality, or politicization.
- Lose publicness, reproducibility, or external auditability.

## Falsification conditions
If case-specific structural density, causal flux, external-force dependence, natural adoption, publicness, or reproducibility crosses its configured `θ`, if observation window `δ` closes, or if phenomenon `M` occurs, and structural preparation still fails to produce convergence, maturation, phase transition, or natural adoption, the current hypothesis must be reviewed and revised.

Revision is especially required if causal inflow remains random after structural preparation, coherence density does not rise during maturation, no non-linear change appears after crossing a case-defined STB, or adoption cannot persist without advertising, persuasion, or founder dependence after supposed realization.
