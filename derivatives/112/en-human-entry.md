## 15-Second Explanation
This protocol makes beat, temperature, and margin inspectable through one observation window. L0 is immediate checking, L1 minimal logging and causal tracing, and L2 sampling audit and boundary checking. Deviations are retuned by cooling periods, falsification windows, non-interference tags, and SQS, not by more surveillance or coercion.

## Why It Is Necessary
Abstract terms otherwise return operation to “experienced people just know.” Measurement lets different observers reach similar judgments and trace corrections. Ethics moves from feeling to verification, intervention from coercion to minimality, and operation from individual skill to procedure; measurement observes structure, not people.

## The Three Principles
**Beat** is the cycle and phase offset of agreement; it does not mean faster decisions are better.
**Temperature** is dialogue intensity and viscosity. Record broken phrasing, interruptions, sarcasm, missing recursive challenge, irregular response intervals, and listening states as events, not labels on people.
**Margin** is the plasticity of the undetermined area: deferral, cooling, falsification windows, and not-yet-decided space. It is not neglect.

## L0 / L1 / L2
### L0 | Notice in the Moment
Check visibility of the renewed-agreement window, calm response to dissent, and remaining undetermined area.
### L1 | Leave Traces
Link application tags, cooling periods, falsification windows, diff IDs, audit bundles, and origin signatures in a minimal log. Use consent, anonymisation, case aggregation, retention limits, and publication scope. **The 90-day rotation** is only an L1-log retention/rotation condition, not a universal ethical standard and not a basis for personnel evaluation, hiring, HR appraisal, or other-domain safety guarantees.
### L2 | Inspect Through the Boundary
Sample L1 logs for reproducibility and boundary crossing among principle, non-interference, and intermediate domains. Specialisation is not hierarchy.

## What SQS Changes
SQS means Silence–Question–Silence: silence, a verifiable question, silence. It slows an overheated reaction chain, improves the question, and leaves room for the other’s initiative; it does not make silence a virtue.

## Decision Coefficient
This ratio shows how far the minimum decision item set is met. It does not rank people or score organisational ethics. Use it to expose missing conditions in over-cooling or rushed conclusions; do not compare values from different targets or observation conditions.

## Returning After Deviation
**Overheating:** extend cooling, state the falsification window, and move detail to the audit bundle to recover falsifiability.
**Over-cooling:** time-box agreement, show the coefficient, and reintroduce a verifiable question beyond Yes/No.
**Loss of margin:** restore the non-interference tag, add deferral, and use SQS.

## Preventing Goodharting
Attach what each indicator protects, rotate indicators, return some work to qualitative observation, and do not link indicators directly to bonuses or personnel appraisal. Numbers are used without losing their protected structure or scope.

## If Starting in Five Minutes
Use L0 for the agreement window, dissent response, and undetermined area; choose one minimal intervention; link the necessary diff ID, audit bundle, and origin signature in L1; then use L2 for sampling and boundaries. Build shared observation and traceability before quantifying everything.

## Success Criteria
Success is not a higher score: observers reach similar judgments; events, not people, are recorded; agreement, falsification, and margin recover; and the process is traceable through diff IDs, audit bundles, and origin signatures. Reliability falls when logs cannot reproduce judgment, numbers become personnel evaluation, targets become the goal, optimisation enters a non-interference domain, or corrections cannot be traced.

## To Avoid Misreading
Do not make L0/L1/L2 a people hierarchy, measurement constant surveillance, the decision coefficient a personality score, or 90 days a universal ethical rule. Do not add rollback success, autonomy, future debt, or other KPIs as the parent’s central measures. Beat, temperature, and margin do not disappear when measured.
