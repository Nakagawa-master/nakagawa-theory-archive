## Summary
Instrumentation for beat, temperature and margin uses a staged L0/L1/L2 protocol: low-cost observation, comparative operational measurement, and focused causal testing. It rejects both vague intuition-only governance and false numerical precision.

## Central thesis
Measure only as deeply as necessary, keep proxies distinct from the constructs they represent, and combine quantitative traces with qualitative evidence and falsification.

## Failure conditions
Single-score reduction, surveillance incentives, correlation treated as causality, universal thresholds, hidden minority pressure.

## Falsification conditions
If staged measurement does not improve causal diagnosis or reproducible repair compared with simpler qualitative practice, reduce or redesign it.

## Interpretation constraints
Measurement ≠ truth. Proxy ≠ construct. Precision ≠ accuracy. L2 ≠ always necessary.
