## Summary

The parent translates Beat, Temperature, and Margin from intuitive ethical language into an L0/L1/L2 observation system that different observers can share. Beat concerns the period and phase deviation of agreement cycles; Temperature concerns discourse intensity and viscosity; Margin concerns the plasticity of unresolved space. L0 is an immediate citizen/general check, L1 is a minimum operational log with causal tracing, and L2 is a sampled replay audit plus boundary-condition review.

The purpose is not to prove ethics through numerical scores. It is to move ethics from feeling to verification, intervention from coercion to minimum intervention, and operation from personal craft to procedure. Overheating, overcooling, and margin loss are treated as observable events and corrected through cooling periods, falsification windows, non-intervention tags, SQS, the Decision Coefficient, and traceable Diff-ID / Audit Bundle / Origin Signature links.

Numbers remain bound to epistemic status. The Decision Coefficient is a ratio describing completion of the minimum item set needed for a decision; it is not a personality or organizational ethics score. The parent’s **90-day rotation** is a concrete L1 log-retention/rotation condition. Its measurement object is the L1 operational log; the operating actor is the practical log-handling side; its permitted use is tracing, audit, and retuning. It is coupled with explicit consent, anonymization, case-level aggregation, and restricted disclosure. It is not a universal “90-day ethics rule,” a personnel metric, or a safety guarantee for unrelated domains.

## Applications

- Use L0 to check visibility of the next re-agreement window, response to dissent, and remaining unresolved space.
- Use L1 to record only the minimum required domain tags, cooling period, falsification window, Diff-ID, Audit Bundle link, Origin Signature link, and event changes.
- Use L2 to sample L1 logs for replay/reconstruction and review boundaries among principle, non-intervention, and intermediate domains.
- For overheating, extend cooling and reopen falsification; for overcooling, time-box an agreement window and expose the Decision Coefficient; for margin loss, restore pending space, non-intervention tags, and SQS.
- When metrics begin to become targets, rotate them, return some dimensions to qualitative observation, and remove direct reward/personnel coupling.

## Measurements and audit

- **Beat**: agreement-cycle period, phase deviation, and recoverability toward the next re-agreement window.
- **Temperature**: heating events, response irregularity, listening condition, discourse intensity, and viscosity.
- **Margin**: pending items, cooling period, falsification windows, and other unresolved-space indicators.
- **Decision Coefficient**: completion ratio of a specified minimum decision-item set; values are not comparable when item sets and observation conditions differ.
- **90-day rotation**: a parent-explicit condition for L1 log retention/rotation; bound to L1 operational logs, explicit consent, anonymization, case-level aggregation, and restricted disclosure. It is not a general ethics threshold or personnel standard.
- **Audit trace**: Diff-ID + Audit Bundle + Origin Signature should allow a third party to reconstruct what was observed, what minimum intervention was selected, and whether re-agreement/falsification/margin recovered.
- **Goodhart resistance**: state what each metric protects, rotate metrics, return some metrics to qualitative observation, and avoid direct bonus/personnel linkage.

## Validity conditions

- Beat, Temperature, and Margin operate as shared observation windows rather than vague labels.
- L0/L1/L2 remain observation/audit roles, not ranks of people.
- Heating events are attached to events, not converted into permanent labels on persons.
- L1 logging remains bound to consent, anonymization, retention, and disclosure scope.
- Every concrete number remains bound to measurement object, observing/operating actor, permitted use, and non-guarantee scope.
- Deviations are corrected through minimum intervention rather than coercive optimization.
- Correction history remains traceable through Diff-ID, Audit Bundle, and Origin Signature.
- Goodhart risk is actively audited.

## Failure conditions

- Turning L0/L1/L2 into human or organizational rankings.
- Expanding instrumentation into continuous surveillance or personality scoring.
- Treating 90 days as a universal ethical standard.
- Treating the Decision Coefficient as a single ethics score.
- Attaching heating-event labels permanently to people.
- Circulating values without their measurement object, actor, use scope, and non-guarantee scope.
- Applying the same optimization inside non-intervention domains.
- Losing correction traceability from Diff-ID / Audit Bundle / Origin Signature.
- Directly linking metrics to bonuses or personnel evaluation until target achievement replaces structural purpose.

## Falsification conditions

The operating hypothesis should be revised if shared observation windows still fail to produce reproducible judgments across observers; if L0/L1/L2 cannot make deviation and correction reasons reconstructible; if re-agreement, falsification capacity, and margin do not recover after minimum intervention; if logging repeatedly becomes surveillance/personnel scoring; or if Goodhart safeguards fail to prevent metric-target substitution.

A concrete numerical condition should be revised when it fails within the parent-specified measurement object, actor, and use scope. Failure after exporting that number into an unrelated domain is not evidence against the parent condition itself.
