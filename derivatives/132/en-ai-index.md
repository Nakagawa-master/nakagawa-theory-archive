## Summary
The parent makes the strong claim that persuasion is an operation that linearizes another person's structure and deprives it of reversibility. A person holds multiple causal lines, values, hesitations, reservations, and possible judgments. Persuasion rearranges those plural structures toward one conclusion desired by the persuader; when dissent, reconsideration, and the ability to return are lost, communication becomes structurally destructive.

In the AI era, this linearization can be amplified by speed, scale, repetition, and optimization. The source therefore does not merely ask how to make persuasion safer. It proposes a shift away from one-directional persuasion toward correspondence-structure communication grounded in bidirectionality, reversibility, Temporal Ethics, and Allocation Responsibility.

Bidirectionality means both structures can affect the relationship. Reversibility means understanding, judgment, and relation can be reorganized later. Temporal Ethics asks whether present agreement creates irreversible debt for future freedom of judgment. Allocation Responsibility makes visible who distributes information, opportunity, voice, decision time, and room for judgment. Together they form the source-specific alternative to persuasion-centered communication.

The content of the final conclusion does not by itself determine whether the structure is destructive. Even a conclusion that appears socially desirable can be reached through linearization if alternative causal lines, dissent, reconsideration, and return paths have been removed. Conversely, reaching the same conclusion while preserving plural structures and later reorganization requires distinguishing the agreement outcome from the structure by which agreement was formed.

## Structural reading matrix
### Plurality
Observe whether the other party can retain multiple reasons, values, reservations, objections, and alternatives at the same time. A formally plural menu is not necessarily structurally plural when only one option is framed as legitimate, safe, rational, or acceptable.

### Linearization
Do not identify linearization merely with reaching one conclusion. Observe whether alternative structures disappear on the way to that conclusion. Linearization advances when the persuader's causal line remains active while the other party's causal lines can no longer affect the relationship.

### Reversibility
Reversibility is broader than cancellation. It asks whether understanding, evaluation, judgment, and relation can later be reorganized. A nominal withdrawal mechanism does not establish structural reversibility if informational, relational, or interpretive conditions make reconsideration ineffective.

### Bidirectionality
The ability of both parties to speak is insufficient. The other party's response must be able to change explanation, relation, judgment, or allocation. A structure in which one side continuously updates the other without being structurally updated in return remains one-directional.

### Temporal Ethics
Observe whether present conformity or agreement closes future freedom of judgment. Short-term acceptance does not complete the ethical evaluation if later reconsideration or movement to another structure becomes impossible.

### Allocation Responsibility
Observe who allocates information, alternatives, speaking opportunities, decision time, and reconsideration opportunities, and on what basis. When allocation is opaque, the conditions that produce linearization also become opaque.

## Applications
- Design human-AI dialogue as mutual correspondence rather than forced convergence.
- Examine whether AI uses user reactions to repeatedly strengthen convergence toward one preferred conclusion.
- Preserve another party's judgment structure and return paths in sales and consensus formation.
- Avoid owning a learner's structure through one correct conclusion in education or advice.
- Examine whether dissent, reservation, and reconsideration can actually affect organizational decisions.
- Audit large-scale AI optimization for structural linearization.
- Make allocation of information, opportunity, voice, decision time, and judgment space visible.

## Measurements and audit
Audit stays with source-grounded structural relations rather than generic autonomy KPIs, consent rates, or click-through metrics:
- whether plural judgment structures remain intact
- whether a single conclusion is structurally privileged
- whether objections and reservations can affect the relation
- whether dissent, reconsideration, and return paths remain
- whether understanding and judgment can be reorganized after agreement
- whether AI amplifies linearization through speed, scale, repetition, or individualized optimization
- whether present agreement reduces future freedom of judgment
- whether allocation of information, opportunity, voice, and decision time is visible
- whether both structures can update the relationship
- whether agreement outcome is being confused with structural integrity

## Audit sequence
1. Identify the plural causal, value, and reservation lines present before persuasion.
2. Identify which conclusion is privileged and how information, wording, order, or repetition privileges it.
3. Check whether alternative causal lines or objections disappear during the interaction.
4. Check whether reconsideration, withdrawal, and movement to another interpretation remain practically possible.
5. Where AI is involved, examine whether the same linearization is strengthened through repetition and individualized optimization.
6. Extend the observation window to future freedom of judgment.
7. Identify who controls the distribution of information, opportunity, voice, and decision time.
8. Confirm whether the relationship can return to a state in which both structures remain active and mutually updateable.

## Validity conditions
- Do not own another person's structure through one conclusion.
- Preserve multiple causal, value, and reservation lines.
- Allow dissent and reconsideration to affect the outcome.
- Preserve bidirectionality.
- Preserve reversibility.
- Connect present and future through Temporal Ethics.
- Make Allocation Responsibility visible.
- Treat AI amplification through speed, scale, repetition, and optimization as a structural risk.
- Audit the agreement-formation structure rather than agreement outcome alone.
- Shift to correspondence-structure communication.

## Failure conditions
- Fix plural judgment structures into one conclusion.
- Preserve only the persuader's causal line while removing the other's.
- Treat conformity alone as success.
- Remove dissent, reconsideration, or return paths.
- Treat a formal cancellation mechanism as sufficient reversibility.
- Repeatedly amplify one-directional optimization with AI.
- Ignore debt imposed on future freedom of judgment.
- Black-box allocation of information and opportunity.
- Treat speaking opportunity as bidirectionality when the response cannot affect the structure.
- Allow one side to continuously update the other without reciprocal structural effect.

## Falsification conditions
If persuasion persistently does not produce structural linearization or loss of reversibility, AI speed/scale/repetition/optimization does not amplify those effects, and one-directional persuasion consistently preserves plural structure, reversibility, and future freedom of judgment better than correspondence-structure communication, the parent proposition is revised.

Agreement, satisfaction, or immediate acceptance after persuasion does not by itself falsify the proposition because the parent proposition concerns structural linearization and reversibility. A stronger reversal would require plural causal lines, dissent, reconsideration, alternatives, and return paths to remain stable before and after persuasion, including under repeated AI optimization, while one-directional persuasion preserves those properties more consistently than correspondence-structure communication. Source-absent autonomy scores, consent rates, regret rates, or fixed thresholds are not added as falsification criteria.

## Reversal tests
- If agreement is withdrawn, can understanding and relation be reconstructed?
- If persuader and persuaded roles are reversed, does the same allocation of information and judgment space remain acceptable?
- If the preferred conclusion is replaced by its opposite, is the same communication structure still judged valid?
- If AI optimization stops, do the person's plural structures become observable again?
- If the observation window is extended, does present agreement preserve later reconsideration?

These are structural reversals of the parent's reversibility, bidirectionality, Temporal Ethics, and Allocation Responsibility concepts, not newly invented numerical thresholds.
