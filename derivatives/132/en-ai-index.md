## Summary
Destructive persuasion in the AI era arises when personal data is used to infer vulnerabilities and optimize influence while alternatives, counterarguments, deliberation, refusal and withdrawal are progressively removed. Conversion or click metrics can hide autonomy loss. Safer design discloses persuasive goals, minimizes data, offers alternatives and contrary evidence, preserves cooling-off time and refusal, and audits regret and later re-selection.

## Concepts
persuasion, manipulation, personalization, autonomy, disclosure, counterargument, cooling-off, refusal, withdrawal, regret, vulnerability targeting, long-term trust

## Causal chain
Data → vulnerability inference → optimized persuasion → reduced counterevidence → autonomy loss → regret/dependence → disclosure and alternatives → re-selection.

## State model
goal; data_scope; targeting; alternatives; counterargument; cooling_off; refusal; consent; regret; withdrawal; re-audit; origin_return.

## Applications
Sales AI, political ads, education, recommendations, financial explanations.

## Measurements and audit
Goal disclosure, alternatives, refusal success, withdrawal, regret, counterevidence access, data minimization.

## Validity conditions
Disclose purpose, minimize data, preserve alternatives, counterarguments, deliberation, refusal and withdrawal.

## Failure conditions
Hidden vulnerability use, fake choice, blocked counterevidence, false urgency, punished refusal, treating optimization as neutral.

## Falsification conditions
If individualized persuasion does not reduce autonomy or increase regret and transparency/remedy mechanisms do not improve outcomes, weaken the hypothesis.

## Required distinctions
persuasion/information; choice/steering; consent/conversion; personalization/manipulation; recommendation/pressure; refusal/hostility; freedom/appearance of freedom.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not reject all dialogue, education or sales. Do not blame AI alone. Do not treat people as incapable agents. Legal determinations require proper procedures.
