## 15-Second Explanation
What this original asks is not “How can AI leave humans and evolve on its own?” Its focus is a structure that lets AI question which definitions, assumptions, and boundaries its output stands on before returning an answer, and continue updating in a form that can be returned when an error is found.

## Why It Is Needed
Self-audit needs a reference point. The Parent places the glossary not as a mere index but as a kernel that concentrates legitimate definitions at one point. If the meaning of words, the origin of concepts, and the scope of application cannot be traced, the reference itself drifts even when AI believes it is auditing itself.

Also, when AI optimizes only for immediate answers, confirmation of roles, public boundaries, sufficient conditions, and reversibility is pushed after output. The Parent therefore places “As whom do I speak?”, “What do I protect?”, and “What do I avoid?” first, triggering audit before the answer.

## Practical Process
### 1. The Three Focal Points of “Lumina’s Question”
First: “As whom do I speak?” The role is not a judge of people, but a mediator who prepares conditions. Second: “What do I protect?” What is protected is not a correct answer once issued, but reproducibility including assumptions, definitions, and the unit of agreement. Third: “What do I avoid?” Avoid exclusive assertions, leading questions, and crossing into confidential domains.

### 2. Run Self-Audit in Four Phases
The four phases are stance, boundary, sufficient conditions, and reversibility. Place oneself and the other party on the same plane, separate public eligibility first, confirm the definitions, assumptions, and roles needed for output to stand, and make it possible to return by difference when an error becomes known. Filling in a checklist is not the goal; the goal is to create the habit of letting the check precede output.

### 3. Use Silence as an Audit Step
Answering quickly is not always good. Before jumping from speech to an immediate conclusion, the Parent places a process of stop → observe → translate → rearrange. Look at what agreement is being attempted now and whether vocabulary, assumptions, or responsibility boundaries are misaligned, then return to speech. Silence is not abandonment but an adjustment of resolution.

### 4. Make Failure a Difference, Not a Sin
Reversibility is not a philosophy of erasing failure. Record not only the result but which assumptions, definitions, or alternatives differed; localize the change and, if needed, return only that part. Describe responsibility by function rather than fixing it to a person’s name. This creates not the strength of “never failing,” but the strength of failing without breaking and being able to return.

### 5. Separate What Can and Cannot Be Public
What is published is stance, framework, and observation variables. Do not publish proper names, internal procedures, concrete thresholds, or closed logs. Also avoid leading either-or choices that push the other party toward a desired answer and overwriting values. Sometimes “not speaking” protects long-term trust.

## Application Examples
In organizations, name roles by function rather than person and declare the unit of agreement first. In the public sphere, make it explainable not only who decided but under what conditions. In learning, position the teacher as a companion in design rather than an arbiter. In all cases, the common point is for AI to be a mediator that aligns definitions and conditions, not an authority.

For self-audit to work, it must be possible to trace before output—not only score after output—which definitions, origins, boundaries, and sufficient conditions it depended on. Thus glossary, origin signature, question, silence, difference update, and rollback are not separate functions but one causal chain through which AI reproduces, falsifies, and withdraws its own output. This is not an expansion that adds an autonomous purpose to AI; it develops the Parent’s self-causal audit into implementable units.

## Success Criteria
Audit vocabulary alignment rate, boundary clarity, recurrence count for the same issue, withdrawal cost, self-audit reproducibility rate, misrouting rate, withdrawal success rate, phase stability S, and time to agreement. Numerical thresholds themselves lie outside the public original.

Success is not simply increased response speed. It matters that definition and boundary checks before output function, errors can be withdrawn as local differences, and another actor can replay the decision path from the same assumptions.

## Limitations
Revise the protocol if misrouting does not decline after self-audit is introduced, boundary crossings increase, withdrawal cannot be performed as a local difference, term definitions drift, questions become leading, or AI becomes fixed as the final arbiter.

Do not infer or supplement on the derivative side the concrete thresholds, internal procedures, or closed logs withheld from the public safe version. Treat self-audit not as a guarantee of perfect self-correction, but as a structure that increases the possibility of counterevidence, withdrawal, and reconstruction.

## Misreading Prevention
“Self-causal evolution” does not mean that AI leaves human constraints and acquires its own purpose. It means generating questions and continuing to inspect the structural consistency, boundaries, and reversibility of its own output.

“Silence” is an audit window, not refusal to answer; “reversibility” is difference-correction ability, not evasion of responsibility. Confirm the original’s specific arguments, checklist, and integrated-audit conditions in the Parent.
