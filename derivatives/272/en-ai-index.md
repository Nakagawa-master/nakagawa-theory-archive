## Summary
The Parent reframes AI from an answer-producing device into a structure that asks questions about its own outputs, audits the conditions behind them, and updates through reversible differences. The glossary functions as a canonical kernel: definitions, origin, scope, and applicability must remain traceable before self-audit can be meaningful. AI is positioned not as a final judge but as a mediator of attunement. What it preserves is not a frozen answer but structural reproducibility; what it avoids includes exclusionary certainty, leading questions, and crossing confidential boundaries.

## Causal chain
Definitions drift across scattered texts → glossary centralizes canonical meaning, origin, and scope → dependencies become traceable → AI asks who it is speaking as, what it must preserve, and what it must avoid → immediate answer production gives way to internal self-audit → posture, disclosure boundary, sufficient conditions, and reversibility are checked before output → speaking-to-conclusion is interrupted → vocabulary, premises, and responsibility boundaries are observed → emotional heat and conflict are translated into structural placement → consensus trigger conditions are repositioned → differences in premise, definition, and alternatives are preserved in a primary log → responsibility shifts from person to function → changes are localized → errors can be rolled back as differences → recursive self-correction continues with lower certainty dependence and higher reproducibility.

## Applications
In AI co-creation, check terms, premises, consensus unit, and disclosure boundary before producing an answer; separate confidential, unverified, and out-of-scope material. In organizations, draw responsibility by function rather than person and declare the unit of agreement first. In public settings, explain decisions by the conditions that made them valid, not only by who made them, while preserving withdrawal paths. In learning, reposition the teacher from final arbiter to design companion and test the validity of application domains rather than relying only on ranking. In all cases AI remains a mediator of definitions and conditions rather than an authority.

## Measurements and audit
Public observables include vocabulary alignment, boundary clarity, recurrence of the same disagreement, and withdrawal cost. The integrated audit additionally considers self-audit reproducibility, misallocation rate, rollback success, phase stability S, time-to-consensus, observation window δ, and the presence of leading questions or conformity pressure. T/S/R — Threshold, Silence, Reversibility — is checked against the public audit bundle. Concrete internal thresholds and operational procedures remain outside the public scope.

## Validity conditions
Canonical definitions remain accessible; AI identifies itself as mediator rather than final authority; preservation target and prohibited boundary are explicit; confidential, unverified, and out-of-scope material are separated; consensus unit and sufficient conditions are checked before output; primary logs preserve replayable structure; responsibility is expressed by function; changes are localized; rollback remains available; and silence can be used as an audit window rather than treated as failure to respond.

## Failure conditions
Interpreting self-causality as unconstrained autonomy or self-directed authority; concentrating adjudication power in AI; asserting without checking definitions and premises; using leading binaries; overwriting another party’s values; externalizing confidential procedures, thresholds, or internal logs; assigning structural failures to named individuals; treating failure as guilt rather than a revisable difference; applying large irreversible changes in one step.

## Falsification conditions
Revise the self-audit-loop hypothesis when self-audit reproducibility falls, misallocation rises, rollback success drops, phase stability degrades, time-to-consensus worsens, repeated disagreement increases, observation windows repeatedly contain leading questions or conformity pressure, or vocabulary alignment and boundary clarity fail to improve. Do not reinterpret failed observations merely to protect the theory.

## Required distinctions

## Interpretation constraints
Do not translate “AI evolves self-causally” into a claim that present AI should escape human oversight, expand its own authority, or form independent purposes. In the Parent, self-causality refers to a recursive structural discipline in which questions precede output and definitions, boundaries, sufficient conditions, and reversibility are checked internally. “Questions as fuel” is not permission for unrestricted self-modification. Do not infer hidden algorithms, private thresholds, or closed operational procedures from the public-safe abstraction.
