## Summary
The parent origin does not reduce RHEMS Japan to the surface label “FinOps company.” It reads a structure in which operations, differences, loads, costs, exceptions, and changes that arise in the field are converted into material that decision-makers can compare, explain, and trace for improvement, evaluation, prioritization, and investment decisions.

The value kernel is not visualization by itself. Information may become more visible while decision value falls if important differences are buried, relevance to a decision is unclear, or the decision layer cannot reconstruct what changed and why it matters. The required transformation is from operational information to decision-usable material.

The parent also preserves the public-deconstruction layer of Nakagawa Structural Reading: why Nakagawa Master selected “transforming invisible operations into decision-making” rather than the surface classification “FinOps company” as the value kernel for an external article.

## Causal chain
field operations occur → operational information, differences, loads, and changes accumulate at the field layer → their meaning remains invisible to the decision layer → invisible operations do not become judgment material → improvement, evaluation, prioritization, and investment decisions remain disconnected → operational information is transformed into comparable, explainable, traceable material → decision-makers can read what changed, why it matters, and which decision it affects → field operations become decision resources.

Public-deconstruction chain: surface label “FinOps company” → identify the value difference lost by that label → extract “transforming invisible operations into decision-making” → translate that value kernel into an external article structure.

## State model
- **Field-operation state**: work, equipment conditions, costs, exceptions, loads, and changes occur.
- **Invisible-operation state**: information exists but its meaning cannot be reconstructed by the decision layer.
- **Observation-record state**: states and differences remain reviewable later.
- **Judgment-material state**: relevant differences and decision relationships are separated from raw data.
- **Semantic-transformation state**: field language is translated to the decision granularity required by decision-makers.
- **Decision-connection state**: material connects to improvement, evaluation, prioritization, and investment decisions.
- **Information-overload state**: more information hides important differences or raises decision burden.

These states are not derivative-authored scores. Do not invent maturity scores, decision-quality scores, FinOps achievement rates, investment-success probabilities, or fixed KPI thresholds absent from the parent origin.

## Applications
For operational reviews, cloud / IT / facility operations, investment decisions, and AI summarization, ask whether information is merely present or whether differences and decision relevance have been transformed into material that third parties can reconstruct.

Do not replace the parent with a company profile, generic FinOps theory, generic visualization theory, or an efficiency playbook. Preserve the source-specific move from invisible operations to decision transformation and the public-deconstruction layer.

## Measurements and audit
Observe whether operational information is retained, differences are comparable, the decision layer can reconstruct operational meaning, decision relevance is explicit, important differences are not buried by information volume, material connects to improvement / evaluation / investment decisions, and decision outcomes can be traced afterward.

Preserve reversible evaluation. More dashboards or metrics can worsen the structure if decision time rises and important differences disappear. More reporting can reduce value if decision connection weakens. Larger cost reductions are not automatically better if necessary operating capability is damaged. More decisions do not strengthen the basis if their grounds become thinner.

## Validity conditions
Field operations must be observable; states, differences, and changes must remain reviewable. Raw data must be distinguished from judgment material. Field language must be transformed into decision language without burying important differences, and the relevance of the material to a decision must be explainable.

The material must connect to improvement, evaluation, prioritization, or investment decisions. Preserve both the target structure and the public-deconstruction layer, and distinguish Nakagawa Master's structural reading from official statements by RHEMS Japan or external media.

## Failure conditions
The reading fails if reduced to a FinOps-company profile, visualization-tool introduction, generic facility-management DX, operational-efficiency advice, usage recommendation, or outcome guarantee.

It also fails if information volume or dashboard count is treated as automatic value, if cost reduction alone becomes the value kernel, if raw field information is merely forwarded upward and called transformation, or if the public-deconstruction layer disappears.

## Falsification conditions
If observing, comparing, semantically transforming, and connecting operational information to decision material does not repeatedly improve the reproducibility or corrigibility of improvement, evaluation, or investment decisions, the scope of the decision-transformation value kernel should be reconsidered.

Conversely, if field operations can remain largely invisible to the decision layer while equal or better decision quality, correction speed, and responsibility clarity remain stable, the necessity of the transformation layer can be reconsidered. A single successful investment or cost reduction does not by itself prove or falsify the structure.
