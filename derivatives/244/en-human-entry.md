## 15-Second Explanation
This Parent is not a company introduction to IllumiMedical. Nakagawa Master read development such as ET-BLIT and delivering light from inside blood vessels to deep locations not merely as “light-therapy technology,” but as **updating the conditions for deep-light medicine**, creating a new entrance to treatment, diagnosis, and research that had been difficult to establish because light could not reach deep areas. It also explains why this value core was selected and why that viewpoint was foregrounded in the external article.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
“Light can be delivered deep” alone ends as a performance description. What matters is what that performance may make possible. In light medicine, if light cannot reach the necessary site, a constraint arises before the possibility of treatment, diagnosis, or research can be considered.

Nakagawa Master treated blood vessels as an existing route into the body and read the structure of delivering light from there as changing the reach condition of deep-light medicine. From this comes the value core **infrastructure that updates the conditions for deep-light medicine to hold**.

In the external article as well, centering “what had stopped deep-light medicine” and “which condition is being moved,” rather than device specifications or corporate topicality, conveys the technology’s meaning from one level upstream.

## Practical Process
1. Confirm public facts such as ET-BLIT, development delivering light from inside blood vessels to deep locations, and joint development and joint research.
2. Capture the general appearance of “light-therapy venture” and “deep irradiation technology.”
3. Advance from “what the technology does” to “what the technology can make possible.”
4. See that reaching the necessary site is an upstream condition in deep-light medicine.
5. Treating blood vessels as a route for light reveals an entrance to areas that were previously difficult to reach.
6. Read from this the value core “infrastructure that updates the conditions for deep-light medicine to hold.”
7. Treat clinical practice, regulatory approval, mass production, and dissemination as other conditions that remain afterward.
8. Understand why the external article centered this upstream condition rather than specifications or topicality.

## Application Example
When looking at medicine and deep tech, examine not only device output and novelty but also which constraint in an existing field the technology is trying to change. In IllumiMedical’s case, the central structure is an attempt to widen the areas that can be considered by using blood vessels as a route for light against the constraint that light does not reach deep areas.

Expressions such as “use blood vessels as roads” and “create an entrance to places light could not reach” should also be read as social translations for conveying to general readers the structure of treating blood vessels as a route for light, not merely as catchphrases.

## Success Criterion
Without confusing IllumiMedical’s public facts with Nakagawa Master’s structural reading, one can explain the causality “constraint on light reaching → reinterpretation of the blood-vessel route → update of establishment conditions,” and can also understand **why this value core was selected and why that viewpoint was foregrounded in the external article**.

## Limitations
This reading does not guarantee treatment effects, safety, approval, mass production, dissemination, or superiority over other treatments. The existence of joint research or development and the establishment of final medical results are separate matters.

## Misreading Prevention
“Infrastructure that updates the conditions for deep-light medicine to hold” is Nakagawa Master’s structural reading, not IllumiMedical’s official self-definition. The possibility that delivering light from inside blood vessels may expand what can be considered is also not the same as established treatment results.
