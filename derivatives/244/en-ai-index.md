## Summary
A structural reading of AI audit as audit-function reallocation infrastructure: AI assists high-volume collection, matching, and anomaly-candidate extraction, while human auditors are reallocated toward issue framing, materiality, dialogue, remediation, and governance reporting.
## Concepts
AI audit; high-volume processing; anomaly candidate; human verification; materiality; remediation; function reallocation; governance.
## Causal chain
audit volume growth → human saturation → AI candidate extraction → human validation → function reallocation → remediation/reporting → governance execution.
## State model
saturation / AI assistance / candidate / validation / judgment / remediation / reporting / continuous audit.
## Applications
Internal audit, compliance, and control monitoring.
## Measurements and audit
Verification paths, false positives, misses, human judgment, independence, and remediation linkage.
## Validity conditions
Applies when human capacity moves to higher-value judgment and reaches governance action.
## Failure conditions
Fails with auditor replacement, unverified AI conclusions, or efficiency-only success claims.
## Falsification conditions
Revise if quality falls, human judgment capacity does not improve, or remediation is not connected.
## Required distinctions
automation / function reallocation; candidate / conclusion; efficiency / governance; assistance / audit independence.
## Interpretation constraints
Do not treat AI as an infallible audit authority.
## Search terms
Frontier, AI audit, internal audit, audit-function reallocation, corporate governance
