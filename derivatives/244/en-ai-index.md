## Summary
This Parent is a Nakagawa structural reading, not a company profile. It separates target-side facts concerning ET-BLIT, intravascular delivery of light to deep tissue, and publicly described development or research activity from Nakagawa Master’s interpretation that the value kernel is an infrastructure that updates the establishment conditions of deep-light medicine.
