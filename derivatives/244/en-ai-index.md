## Summary
A structural reading of AI audit as redistribution of audit functions: AI assists collection, matching, coverage checks, and anomaly-candidate extraction while human auditors retain validation, issue framing, materiality judgment, dialogue, remediation, and governance reporting.

## Concepts
audit scale; machine processing; human validation; anomaly candidate; materiality; function redistribution; remediation; governance reporting; continuous audit; independence.

## Causal chain
growing audit scope → human saturation → time absorbed by formal checks → AI processing support → human validation → materiality judgment → dialogue/remediation → management reporting → continuous verification → governance.

## State model
human saturation / AI assistance / candidate extraction / human validation / materiality / dialogue / remediation / reporting / continuous audit / governance verification.

## Applications
Organizations with large transaction, evidence, policy, or log volumes where the goal is to return auditor time to high-value judgment rather than maximize automation rate.

## Measurements and audit
Audit task allocation, validation path, false positives/negatives, human judgment time, issue ownership, remediation, management reporting, and independence.

## Validity conditions
Valid when AI support redistributes human effort toward higher-value judgment and findings reach remediation and governance while responsibility remains clear.

## Failure conditions
Fails when AI output is treated as an audit conclusion, auditors are nominally replaced, efficiency is mistaken for governance, or findings do not reach remediation.

## Falsification conditions
Revise if audit quality declines, validation becomes unmanageable, human judgment time does not increase, or remediation/reporting does not improve.

## Required distinctions
automation / function redistribution; anomaly candidate / audit conclusion; efficiency / governance execution; AI support / audit independence; target facts / Nakagawa reading.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not treat AI as an infallible auditor or transfer final responsibility to the model. Do not present the structural label as Frontier's official definition.
