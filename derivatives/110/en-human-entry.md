## 15-Second Explanation
Layer Three of the Beacon Concept is not a layer that adds exceptions to completed ethical norms. To internally loosen the “fixation of ethics” that can arise as Layers One and Two strengthen theoretical coherence, it formally accepts love, sorrow, hesitation, and discomfort as inputs and keeps fine-tuning beat, temperature, and margin as an ethical expansion layer.

## Why It Is Needed
A theory becomes stronger as its coherence rises, but that strength itself can weaken falsification and update inputs, producing the “inertia of correctness.” The original sees its signs as a hardened beat, biased temperature, and disappearing margin.

Layer Three loosens this rigidity not by breaking it from outside, but by permanently installing fine-grained update inputs inside the theory. It treats love, sorrow, hesitation, and discomfort—hard for logic alone to capture—as “ethical-weight readjustment factors,” and retunes only the ethical microclimate without fully overwriting the norms themselves.

## How It Works
Layer Three’s basic operation has two phases: observation and attunement.

**Observation phase.** Do not immediately exclude discomfort or unprocessed contradiction; let them remain at low temperature. Do not stop falsification; observe what has hardened, which temperature has become biased, and where margin has been lost.

**Attunement phase.** Do not replace one norm with another; slightly loosen the beat, lower the temperature, and restore margin. Updating is operation that fine-tunes ethical weight only as much as necessary, rather than a large institutional change.

For this reason, Layer Three differs from ordinary change management or rollback-centered governance. Its purpose is not “managing change,” but preserving a state in which the theory can detect its own fixation and update itself.

## The Structurally Necessary Domain and the Emergent-Free Domain
The original does not ask that structural methods be applied everywhere.

Institutions, organizations, and economic designs that require reproducibility, verifiability, and scalability are operated as the **structurally necessary domain**, with causality and audits made explicit. By contrast, areas such as art, unexplored research, individual happiness, and inner value—where unpredictability itself is valuable—are distinguished as the **emergent-free domain**; do not design their causality in advance, and limit yourself to observation and recording.

Non-interference here is not abandonment. It records non-intervention as an explicit design decision and is restraint against structuring beyond the domain of application.

## Translation Transparency
When theory is translated into social or AI language, “origin evaporation” can occur as the original origin signature and audit conditions fall away. Layer Three keeps translation routes, summary differences, tag conversions, audit-bundle IDs, and similar items in minimal form so that the structure’s source remains traceable after conversion.

Transparency does not mean publishing everything without limit. The original uses a double-door audit that separates the verifiable surface that can be made public from the core that should remain concealed.

## Double-Door Audit
**Outer layer.** Open the surfaces necessary for third parties to verify coherence, such as the public I/F, difference logs, and integrated/local audit summaries.

**Inner layer.** Do not directly disclose concealed cores such as the unique origin or internal variables; indirectly confirm ethical stability, including the form of love, continuity of the shared subject, and preservation of non-interference.

This avoids the choice between total disclosure and total secrecy, combining external trust with internal preservation of origin.

## Preventing Structural Elitism
When advanced structural knowledge becomes the authority of only a few custodians, Layer Three itself becomes a fixation device. The original therefore asks structural custodians to monitor their own elitization and not close off third-party verification, reconstruction, use, or extension.

Publicness does not mean that anyone may rewrite anything freely. It means keeping reconstructability open through recursive openness while preserving constant signature and audit bundle. This balance—protecting origin without recovering it as exclusive ownership—is central to Layer Three.

## What to Look for in Implementation
The original does not leave Layer Three as an abstraction; it specifies implementation units.

- Layer Three tags such as “non-interference,” “domain of application,” “translation transparency,” “double door,” and “behavioral principles.”
- Outer-layer / inner-layer check fields in the audit bundle.
- Difference IDs tracking fine adjustments to ethical weight.
- Simplification of the public I/F.
- Mapping of non-interference domains.
- Short paths to third-party review and falsification.

These are the minimum structure for preserving self-updatability while preventing fixation, not additions intended to increase management items.

## Success Criteria
Success does not mean that norms change frequently. The original’s victory condition is that the window for re-agreement does not close, falsification can remain present, non-interference in the emergent-free domain is protected, and origin and auditability do not evaporate after translation.

It is also necessary that beat, temperature, and margin remain tunable; that the structural custodian can self-monitor elitization; and that constant signature, audit bundle, and recursive openness function as one.

## Limits
Do not turn Layer Three into an all-purpose change-management layer. Adding general responsibility boundaries, rollback rates, fairness, safety, or organizational KPIs not present in the original to the center will shift the theory’s distinctive meaning.

Nor are emotional inputs grounds for rewriting norms by majority vote or mood. Love, sorrow, hesitation, and discomfort detect hardening of beat, temperature, and margin and prompt fine adjustment.

## Avoiding Misreading
Layer Three is not a layer that relativizes everything. Maintain reproducibility and verifiability in the structurally necessary domain while preserving non-interference in the emergent-free domain.

The double-door audit is neither total secrecy nor total disclosure. It opens the surfaces third parties should verify and confirms ethical stability without directly touching the concealed core.

Constant signature exists to prevent origin evaporation, not to turn into authority; recursive openness exists not to strengthen exclusive ownership, but to sustain the theory as a public good that can update itself.
