## Summary
The parent treats questions not as mere requests for information but as structural operations that arrange attention, relation, judgment, causality, institutionalization, and origin. PQ-5 specifies Purpose, Hypothesis, Medium, Constraints, and Origin. D0–D5 classifies six inquiry depths, while SQS places Silence–Question–Silence around the inquiry. D-gap compares designed and actually reached depth, and R-index observations support revision of the question and its context. Greater depth is not inherently better; fit to purpose, non-coercion, factual verification, and origin return are required.

## Concepts
- PQ-5: five source-explicit components of inquiry design: Purpose, Hypothesis, Medium, Constraints, Origin.
- D0 Request: asks for response or task start.
- D1 Fact check: confirms information or state.
- D2 Relation design: examines roles, value, expectations, and relationships.
- D3 Causal perturbation: asks how changed conditions affect outcomes.
- D4 Institutionalization: connects inquiry to responsibility, standards, audit, stopping, and withdrawal.
- D5 Origin reference fixation: preserves return paths to purpose, source, version, and correction route.
- SQS: Silence–Question–Silence, preserving observation and judgment space before and after a question.
- D-gap: difference between designed inquiry depth and depth actually reached.
- R-index: observations of silence, re-answering, re-questioning, restart and related states; not a score of human worth.
- Inquiry Canvas: shared representation of purpose, hypothesis, target, medium, constraints, origin, depth, measures, stop conditions, and endpoint.

## Causal chain
```text
purpose, hypothesis, scope, authority, and origin are unclear
→ response scope diffuses and judgment responsibility shifts to the respondent
→ shallow responses, over-intrusion, repeated questions, or formal agreement appear
→ compose PQ-5
→ select the D0–D5 depth required by purpose
→ observe before the question and preserve silence after it
→ observe response, silence, dissonance, and re-questioning
→ evaluate D-gap
→ revise depth, order, content, constraints, or stop
→ record outcome, falsification, failure, withdrawal, and restart conditions
→ institutionalize only when reproducibility and boundaries are supported
```

## Applications
- Sales: ask about value, adoption conditions, approval structure, and failure handling only to the depth needed for a valid decision.
- AI collaboration: specify canonical source, purpose, hypothesis, causal kernel, prohibited boundaries, falsification conditions, and output form.
- Meetings: ask not only whether to approve, but who owns implementation, start/stop conditions, withdrawal, audit, and source basis.
- Research: use D1 for a fact when deeper relational or value questions are unnecessary.
- Institutional design: place reproducible inquiry structures into an Inquiry Canvas with stop and origin-return conditions.

## Measurements and audit
PQ-5’s “5” and D0–D5’s six levels are source-explicit enumerations/classifications, not performance scores or probabilities.

  measurement_object: functional depth of an inquiry
  source_modality: SOURCE_EXPLICIT_SIX_LEVEL_CLASSIFICATION
  permitted_use_scope: matching required and actual depth
  non_guarantee_scope: D5 is not inherently superior to D1; not a human or question ranking
- value: D-gap
  source: parent original
  measurement_actor: inquiry design/operation evaluator
  measurement_object: difference between designed and reached depth
  source_modality: structural difference observation
  permitted_use_scope: revising depth, wording, medium, constraints, or stopping
  non_guarantee_scope: zero gap alone does not establish truth, ethics, or quality
- value: R-index
  source: parent original
  measurement_actor: observer of inquiry and context
  measurement_object: silence, re-answering, re-questioning, restart and related process states
  source_modality: operational observation family

  non_guarantee_scope: not a credit, obedience, or human-value score

Reversible evaluation: more questions, longer answers, or greater depth transition do not establish success when burden, intrusion, coercion increase or objection, withdrawal, and factual verification weaken.

## Validity conditions
- Purpose, Hypothesis, Medium, scope, authority, time, and prohibited conditions are explicit.
- Inquiry depth is proportionate to purpose and burden.
- Refusal, hold, objection, and withdrawal remain possible.
- SQS silence functions as judgment space rather than pressure.
- Factual and expert claims are verified through appropriate evidence outside the inquiry itself.
- Designed depth, reached depth, D-gap, and re-question reasons can be recorded.
- Metrics audit the inquiry/context rather than the value of a person.
- Origin, version, Diff-ID, and correction route remain traceable.

## Failure conditions
- Greater depth is treated as inherently better.
- Simple tasks demand unnecessary personal information or values.
- SQS becomes psychological pressure or forced response.
- Silence is immediately classified as consent, refusal, or incapacity.
- D-gap is blamed solely on respondent ability.
- Questions are used to determine truth, medical/legal responsibility, or expert conclusions without evidence.
- R-index becomes a credit or obedience score.
- D5 becomes author worship, monopoly, or prohibition of criticism.
- Correction, withdrawal, or non-participation routes disappear.

## Falsification conditions
- Explicit PQ-5 repeatedly fails to clarify scope, responsibility, or origin.
- Selected depth repeatedly produces large D-gap that revision does not improve.
- SQS repeatedly produces pressure, inhibition, or delay rather than judgment space.
- R-index observations fail to support redesign or stopping decisions.
- D4 institutionalization creates harmful rigid questions across differing contexts.
- D5 origin recording fails to improve correction, criticism, or reusable traceability.
- Persistent failure requires limiting or revising the depth model, operating method, measures, or institutionalization scope.

## Required distinctions
- deep inquiry / purpose-fit inquiry
- five PQ-5 components / five-point score
- D0–D5 classification / ranking of people or questions
- request / fact check
- relation design / intrusion into inner life
- causal hypothesis / causal proof
- institutionalization / unconditional standardization
- origin reference / authority worship
- respectful silence / pressure through silence
- R-index / human ranking
- D-gap / respondent defect

## Interpretation constraints
Do not interpret claims that inquiry can “move future causality” as unlimited control over people or future outcomes. Questions arrange conditions for attention, comparison, judgment, and choice; they do not automatically determine facts or outcomes. D0–D5 is a purpose-fit classification, not a hierarchy of worth. SQS is not a pressure technique. D5 is for responsibility, version, and correction traceability, not an authority mechanism that blocks criticism or derivatives.
