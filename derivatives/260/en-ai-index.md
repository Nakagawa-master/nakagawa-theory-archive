## Summary
The Parent defines a structural unification principle for heterogeneous phenomena without flattening them into one explanatory plane. It separates three interacting layers: subjective orientation, objective verification, and an integrative reference frame called the true-self layer. The subjective layer supplies meaning, expectation, fear, values, and directional attention; the objective layer supplies public criteria, evidence, auditability, repeatability, and braking against subjective overreach; the integrative layer coordinates value cores, boundaries, exceptions, and priorities. In practice, a Public/Private dual syntax preserves both transparency and relationship-specific density, while Threshold, Silence, and Reversibility govern switching, over-intervention, and rollback. Unification therefore means connected difference, not homogenization.

## Concepts
- **Subjective layer**: context-dense intentions, values, expectations, fears, responsibilities, and linguistic framing that orient action.
- **Objective layer**: publicly inspectable laws, procedures, evidence, records, contracts, and audits that provide verification and braking.
- **True-self / integrative reference layer**: not a superior person, but a framework for reconciling value cores, boundaries, exceptions, and priorities.
- **Causality of observation**: a social-operational metaphor for how questions, vocabulary, expectations, evaluation criteria, and silence shape attention and decision pathways.
- **Dual syntax**: separation and mutual reference between Public transparency and Private high-context relational operation.
- **Tension as resource**: contradiction is retained and operated through sequence, spacing, placement, and timing rather than erased.
- **Threshold**: predefined conditions for intervention, stopping, switching, or reopening a decision.
- **Silence**: deliberate non-response used as a fail-safe against over-intervention, not as neglect.
- **Reversibility**: practical rollback capacity that allows experimentation without irreversible lock-in.
- **Falsification window**: a route for new evidence, objections, and failure signals to reopen a decision.

## Causal chain
heterogeneous phenomena forced onto one plane → subjective and objective functions blur → context loss, conformity pressure, over-explanation, and institutional fatigue rise → subjective/objective/integrative roles are separated → subjective orientation becomes explicit → objective verification and braking are added → value cores, boundaries, exceptions, and priorities are reconciled at the integrative layer → Public and Private are separated yet mutually referential → contradictions are kept as operational tension → Threshold/Silence/Reversibility bound updates → small-scale testing and falsification repeat → heterogeneous order converges without flattening difference.

## Applications
1. In organizational decisions, treat intuition as a subjective hypothesis, validate constraints and evidence objectively, then reconcile the option with value cores and unacceptable boundaries.
2. In AI use, do not treat model output as objective reality; separate data, prompts, evaluation criteria, evidence, and human responsibility boundaries.
3. In education, separate public evaluation standards from protected exploratory and relational context, while keeping retry paths open.
4. In medicine, separate guidelines and measurable evidence from patient values and family context, then reconcile priorities explicitly.
5. In public governance, combine auditability and transparency with protected high-context stakeholder knowledge through dual syntax and plural logs.

## Measurements and audit
- Can subjective interpretation be distinguished from verifiable facts?
- Can decision conditions, evidence, and responsible actors be traced?
- Has the integrative layer become a personal authority or mystical trump card?
- Are Public/Private boundaries and their interfaces explainable?
- Are orientation and verification balanced rather than dominated by one side?
- Are trigger thresholds defined before a switch or escalation?
- Is silence functioning as a bounded fail-safe rather than accountability avoidance?
- Can rollback actually be executed?
- Do objections and new evidence reopen review?
- Does “unification” preserve minority viewpoints and contextual difference rather than erase them?

## Validity conditions
The model holds when the three layers remain functionally distinct yet mutually referential; subjective meaning is preserved without replacing evidence; objective verification remains available without erasing context; value cores, boundaries, exceptions, and priorities can be reconciled explicitly; and Public/Private dual syntax, T/S/R, falsification windows, bounded trials, third-party review, and rollback are operational.

## Failure conditions
It fails when subjective certainty is promoted into fact without verification, numerical outputs or majority opinion are treated as objective truth, the true-self layer becomes personal authority, dual syntax collapses into secrecy or indiscriminate exposure, contradiction is erased through forced homogenization, switching occurs without thresholds, silence becomes avoidance, or irreversible changes are imposed without rollback.

## Falsification conditions
Revise the implementation if separating the three layers does not improve traceability or decision quality, dual syntax increases responsibility fragmentation, the integrative frame legitimizes arbitrary authority, T/S/R does not reduce switching failures or irreversible loss, falsification channels are functionally closed, or preserving heterogeneity simply produces persistent paralysis rather than accountable coordination.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not transform the Parent’s “observation” into a direct claim about quantum mechanics or physical observer effects. Do not interpret the true-self layer as a spiritual or superior person. Do not reduce unification to making the three layers identical. Do not invent universal fixed KPIs, thresholds, or equations absent from the Parent. Preserve the original structural necessity: subjective orientation, objective verification/braking, and integrative reconciliation must remain distinct but connected.
