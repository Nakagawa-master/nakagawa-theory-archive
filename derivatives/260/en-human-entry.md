## 15-Second Explanation
This Parent Original does not erase subjectivity in favor of objectivity alone, nor decide the world through subjectivity alone. It separates and mutually refers three layers as **subjectivity = direction-setting, objectivity = verification and braking, and true self = an integrated reference frame that binds the value core, boundaries, and priorities**. What matters is not which is most correct, but connecting three layers with different roles without mixing them.

## Why It Is Needed
People do not receive events exactly as they are. What they regard as a problem, the words they use, and what they fear or expect change the direction of attention, judgment, and action. Subjectivity is therefore the starting point of decision-making.

But subjectivity alone easily mixes conviction with fact. The objective layer returns to conditions and evidence through records, public standards, third-party review, and auditing. Yet objective data alone cannot automatically decide what to protect, which exceptions to allow, or what to prioritize. An integrated reference frame is therefore needed.

## The Three Layers in One Sentence
- **Subjective layer**: Directs what to look at and what to regard as meaningful.
- **Objective layer**: Verifies whether that view is consistent with conditions, facts, and evidence.
- **True-self layer / integrated reference frame**: Integrates the value core, boundaries, exceptions, and priorities at a higher level.

The true self is not a person or transcendent authority. It is an operational point of integration.

## Caution About “Observation”
The observation discussed in the Parent Original does not claim the rigorous observation problem of natural science or quantum theory. It is a metaphor for social operation.

For example, asking “Who is at fault?” gathers information for assigning blame, while asking “Which conditions for success were missing?” gathers information about conditions and design. Vocabulary, questions, evaluation axes, and Silence affect what is brought to the foreground and which paths of action are opened. In this sense, observation has direction-setting power.

## Dual Syntax
In operation, separate a publicly shareable external layer from a relationship-specific internal layer.

The **external layer** contains laws, rules, standards, accountability, and auditable records.

The **internal layer** contains trust, tacit knowledge, agreement histories, emotional protection, role rotation, and exceptional context.

Mixing both leaks sensitive information and constricts internal relationships. Completely separating them makes the internal layer irresponsible and the external layer merely formal. A connection is therefore needed that allows validity to be checked externally while context is retained internally.

## Practical Process
1. Put into words the subjective expectations, fears, and preferences contained in the current judgment.
2. Separate them from facts, evidence, and conditions.
3. Decide who can verify what.
4. Make explicit the value core that must never be damaged in this setting.
5. Decide exceptional norms and responsibility boundaries.
6. Separate the public layer from internal context.
7. Keep the judgment history in the vocabularies of multiple positions.
8. Preserve a period and route for receiving falsification.
9. Try on a small scale so it can be restored after failure.
10. Make explicit the thresholds for stopping, changing, and re-reviewing.

## Examples
### New Business in an Organization
Do not deny that an executive feels “this will grow.” Treat that intuition as a hypothesis in the subjective layer, verify customer behavior, costs, and legal conditions in the objective layer, and compare it with the company’s value core and unacceptable risks in the integrated reference frame.

### Use of AI
An AI answer is not objectivity itself. The data, instructions, and evaluation axes supplied to it give it direction. Use AI for structuring while separately holding human values and responsibility boundaries, external evidence, and routes for objection.

### Conflict Adjustment
Do not dismiss the parties’ subjectivity as invalid because it is emotion; bring out each side’s meaning and fears. At the same time, place shareable facts in the objective layer, and organize the rights, boundaries, and priorities to protect in the integrated reference frame.

## Successful State
- Subjective interpretations and facts can be distinguished.
- Conditions, evidence, and responsible parties for important judgments can be traced.
- The value core and priorities can be explained.
- The boundary between externally public information and internal context is clear.
- The judgment process can be reopened even when objections or falsification arise.
- Trials can be made rollback-capable rather than an irreversible all-or-nothing gamble.
- None of the three layers erases the others.

## Signs of Failure
- “I feel that way” is replaced by fact.
- “The majority,” “the numbers,” or “the AI answer” becomes a synonym for objectivity.
- The term true self becomes authoritative or personalized.
- Observation causality is spoken of as though it were a fact of quantum theory.
- Accountability is refused on the grounds of internal context.
- Sensitive information is all disclosed in the name of transparency.
- Objections and falsification after a decision are blocked.
- A rollback-impossible change is fully implemented from the start.

## Falsification and Revision
If introducing the three-layer operation does not improve traceability of judgments, dual syntax increases divided responsibility, the integrated standard becomes mere authority, or the falsification window cannot actually be used, the implementation has failed. At that point, revise layer boundaries, the scope of disclosure, responsible parties, and thresholds without ignoring reality in order to preserve the principle.

## Preventing Misreading
- Do not treat subjectivity as bad and objectivity as good.
- Do not equate objectivity with majority vote.
- Do not assert the true self as a spiritual or personal subject.
- Do not think integration means mixing the three layers.
- Do not expand observation into a claim of physics.
- Do not make the internal layer secretive.
- Do not regard reversibility as avoiding decisions.
