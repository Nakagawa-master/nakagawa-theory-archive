## 15-Second Explanation
The parent original’s central proposition is that “power cannot defeat structure.” Law, money, status, commands, and exposure are outputs that apply force to phenomena, but how that force flows is determined by the structural wiring of information, roles, incentives, time, and boundaries. The more output is intensified while the wiring remains distorted, the more friction costs increase, and counterforces return as delay, formalism, sabotage, defection, and counter-propaganda.

## Power and Structure
Power consists of visible levers such as law, money, violence, status, and media exposure. Structure consists of causal wiring such as role architecture, incentives, information flows, time arrangements including reversible/irreversible choices, and boundary setting. If phenomenal operation is increasing the current, structural design is rewiring the circuit itself.

As higher-order constraints governing power, the original identifies information asymmetry, role architecture, and time structure. When information reaching decision-makers is distorted, roles overlap, are vacant, or conflict, or short-term gains conceal long-term losses, long-term alignment cannot be maintained even with strong output.

## The True Principle of Operation
The alternative presented by the original is syntactic operation. It binds mind, causality, structure, and time into one design philosophy, lowering circuit resistance instead of adding output. Through the three principles of connecting mind and structure, tuning friction, and recursively designing time structure, it prepares conditions in which self-running and re-reference arise without relying on explanation or coercion.

## Authority with Minimal Resistance
The original contrasts: coercion produces obedience, and obedience prepares revenge; alignment produces assent, and assent prepares re-reference. Through three points—placing the meaning axis first, making boundaries variable, and the right to silence—it establishes authority as internal agreement rather than external pressure.

When placing the meaning axis first, the field is arranged in the order of evaluation criteria → options → free voting. Boundaries declare time, budget, and roles as variable ranges while leaving freedom. The right to silence using SQS lowers pressure for an immediate answer and secures a sense of self-determination.

## Ethical Conditions
Structural tuning requires legitimacy not only in results but also in means. The original explicitly sets a non-command principle, coexistence of the individual and the public, transparent observation indicators, advance presentation of counterexamples, and a freedom test that does not close into Yes/No. Tuning that loses ethics is placed on the boundary of becoming sophisticated manipulation.

## Observation and Verification
The main observations are time to agreement, re-reference rate, self-running rate, and friction coefficient. Structural modifications are made one factor at a time, comparing conventional condition A with B’, which changes only one element, under the same conditions. Combine three-cycle stabilization, a freedom test, and advance presentation of counterexamples to examine reproducibility.

The 20-minute operating framework is two minutes for placing the meaning axis first, five minutes for issue placement, five minutes for SQS, five minutes for extracting the core point, and three minutes for confirming freedom. This time allocation and the three cycles are public operating specifications specific to the original, not universal success thresholds or statistical guarantees.

A shorter time to agreement is not always better; if coercion shortened it, that is failure. A high re-reference rate is not necessarily correct; erroneous decisions may also be fixed in place. Self-running must be distinguished from abandoning responsibility, and increased silence in the friction coefficient must be read as either maturation or suppression.

## Breakdown Patterns
The original’s five anti-patterns are simultaneous multi-factor intervention, numerical fixation/language anemia, omission of silence, rigid boundaries, and layering more power on top. They fail by making it impossible to know what worked, making counting the purpose, destroying depth through pressure for immediate answers, eliminating freedom, and increasing output while leaving the cause of friction intact.

## Applications
In organizations, examine decision time and re-reference rate through evaluation axes, issue placement, and SQS. In public life, turn opposing views into questions and share period and cost boundaries as variable. In products, design retention before short-term registration. In AI collaboration, declare evaluation criteria, freedom, and the right to silence before adding more command text. Each is an example of rewriting the wiring diagram.

## Falsification
If a single-factor change makes no difference in the main observations, restoring the change does not alter the result, the direction does not stabilize over three cycles, or freedom declines and friction costs increase, revise the structural hypothesis. Do not escape to another indicator to preserve the appearance of the numbers.
