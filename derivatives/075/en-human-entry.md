## 15-Second Explanation
The memory of consensus is a structure that preserves not only “what was decided,” but also “why it was decided,” “who was involved in which role, with what interests and authority,” “what objections or reservations existed,” and “under what conditions it can be reconsidered, withdrawn, or agreed again.”

The purpose is not to freeze decisions forever. It prevents mistaken judgments from becoming fixed through volume, authority, popularity, or speed of dissemination, and enables society to correct, reach agreement again, or roll back after taking action while retaining responsibility and provenance.

## Why It Is Needed
In meetings and social decision-making, only the final conclusion often remains, while the assumptions, opposing views, reservations, responsibility, and time conditions that produced it disappear. As a result, a decision that was originally provisional is treated as a permanent truth and can no longer be corrected even when circumstances change or new evidence appears.

The parent original treats this malfunction not as a problem of “weak consensus,” but as a problem of insufficient formats for remembering consensus. By preserving causality, relationships, and the capacity for renewed agreement, and by making non-coercion, renewed agreement, and reversibility genuinely usable, it maintains consensus as a living structure.

## Practical Process
1. Separate the subject of judgment, primary facts, interpretations, and unverified matters.
2. Record participants, affected people, roles, authority, and conflicts of interest.
3. Preserve not only reasons for adoption, but also reasons for opposition, minority views, reservations, and possible disconfirming evidence.
4. Distinguish consensus states such as provisional, in operation, awaiting review, corrected, and withdrawn.
5. Make deadlines, review conditions, withdrawal conditions, and rollback conditions explicit.
6. Maintain an audit summary through which a third party can understand causality, relationships, dissent, and update conditions.
7. When conditions change or an error is confirmed, correct or renew the agreement without erasing the old decision.
8. Do not repurpose records for personality scoring, surveillance, or loyalty assessment.

## Examples of Application
**Internal meetings.** Recording the reasons for adopting a new initiative, rejected alternatives, the trial period, stop conditions, and the reconsideration date makes it possible to reconstruct the decision’s provenance even when the person in charge changes.

**Local government and public policy.** Setting deadlines and review conditions for provisional emergency measures, while preserving minority views, affected groups, and routes to remedy, prevents crisis responses from becoming permanent institutions.

**AI-assisted decisions.** Separating and preserving the AI’s proposal, the information used, assumptions, counterexamples, and human judgment prevents AI output from being treated as absolute and enables later correction and reevaluation.

**Contracts and joint research.** Preserve conditions for changes of purpose, reuse of data, attribution of results, withdrawal, and renewal; do not turn the initial signature into blanket consent for everything in the future.

## Criteria for Success
What matters is being able to return from the conclusion to its grounds, dissent, reservations, responsible actors, and review conditions. Even when the person in charge or an authority figure changes, it must be possible to reconstruct the decision’s provenance from the structure rather than from a person’s reputation.

It is also important that correction, withdrawal, renewed agreement, and rollback are actually available. This is not a simple evaluation in which more or fewer uses are always better. Examine together with causality whether necessary corrections are being blocked or, conversely, formal changes are being issued indiscriminately.

## Limitations
Simply increasing the amount of record-keeping will not make this work. In environments where objections cannot be freely voiced, the log itself can become a justification device for those in power.

The purpose is not to preserve every conversation forever. The scope of preservation, retention period, access rights, and deletion or correction conditions must be limited to what is necessary.

Rates, preservation rates, explicitness rates, time to renewed agreement, states of acceptance, numbers of corrections, and similar measures are observed variables; the parent original does not define universal pass rates, fixed thresholds, or guaranteed times. If shortening time removes consideration of dissent, or if a higher preservation rate is accompanied by conversion into surveillance, it cannot be called an improvement.

## Preventing Misreading
The memory of consensus is not a theory that rejects majority voting or democracy. It adds layers of causality, relationships, dissent, and the capacity for renewed agreement to agreement by numbers.

Renewed agreement does not mean breaking promises without responsibility. It is a procedure for responding to changed conditions while retaining the old decision and its effects. Reversibility does not mean erasing harm or responsibility; it is the property of returning a state as far as possible and connecting that return to remedy and prevention of recurrence.
