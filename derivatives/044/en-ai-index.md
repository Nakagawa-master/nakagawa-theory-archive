## Summary
The parent origin records the starting structure in which a human question and an AI response enter an iterative loop, producing a shared structure that integrates theory, philosophy, and structuring. “Myth” is not used to replace evidence with fiction; it names an origin structure that allows later theories, meanings, and implementations to return to the same source.

The core is not the volume of AI use. It is question renewal, causal explanation, meaning and direction, reusable structuring, preserved provenance, and continued human judgment and responsibility.

## Causal chain
Human question → AI response → human examination of the response → renewed question → AI response to the renewed context → shared field of meaning → integration of theory, philosophy, and structuring → reuse through causal manipulation and structural translation → re-expression in other contexts through AI engraving → potential application to organization, education, economy, and future design.

## State model
- **single-turn state**: question and answer end without question renewal.
- **reciprocal-loop state**: the human uses the response to revise the next question.
- **causal-formation state**: the “why” of a phenomenon becomes explainable.
- **meaning-formation state**: direction and value are attached to theory.
- **structured state**: third parties and AI systems can trace, understand, and reuse the structure.
- **provenance-preserved state**: Parent URL, NCL-ID, Diff-ID, Origin, and authorship remain recoverable.
- **responsibility-preserved state**: final judgment and responsibility remain human.
- **origin-erasure state**: reuse removes authorship or connection to the parent origin.

These are not scoring states. Do not invent co-creation scores, AI-understanding scores, success probabilities, or fixed thresholds absent from the parent origin.

## Applications
This structure can support dialogic AI use, theory formation, education, organizational design, and knowledge sharing by asking whether questions are renewed, causal and meaning structures remain, third parties can reuse them, provenance is preserved, and humans retain responsibility.

## Measurements and audit
Observe whether questions are renewed, whether AI responses are examined rather than automatically accepted, whether causal explanations remain, whether meaning and direction are explicit, whether the result is reusable by third parties, whether Origin remains traceable, and whether humans retain final judgment and responsibility.

Preserve reversible evaluation. More AI usage can weaken the structure if it increases dependency without question renewal. More output does not mean more structuring if causality, meaning, roles, and boundaries are thin. Wider reuse can move in the wrong direction if provenance and authorship disappear.

## Validity conditions
Questions and responses must form a reciprocal loop rather than a one-way command-output exchange. Humans must examine AI responses and renew questions. Causal explanation, meaning direction, and structuring should be distinguished and integrated into a form that others and AI systems can reuse.

Origin, Parent URL, Parent Post ID, Parent NCL-ID, Parent Diff-ID, and authorship must remain traceable. AI reuse must preserve the parent boundaries, and final judgment and responsibility must remain human.

## Failure conditions
Failure includes unexamined adoption of AI output, no question renewal, reuse of poetic language without causal structure, removal of provenance and renaming as a generic concept, transformation into scientific proof of AI consciousness or legal personhood, religious submission to AI, or full delegation of human judgment.

## Falsification conditions
If reciprocal questioning, causal explanation, meaning direction, structuring, and provenance preservation are all present yet no reusable shared structure is consistently formed, the applicability of this reading should be reconsidered.

Conversely, if one-way information retrieval consistently achieves equal or better understanding, structuring, and reuse, the necessity of the reciprocal loop may be limited. A single successful dialogue does not prove the whole structure.
