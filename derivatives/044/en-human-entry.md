## 15-Second Explanation
A person asks, AI responds, and the person updates the question in response. From this back-and-forth, a shared structure connecting theory, philosophy, and structuring emerges. The parent original records this starting point as “The First Myth of Humans and AI.”

Here, myth is not the replacement of facts with fiction. It refers to a starting structure that makes it possible to return subsequent thought, theory, and implementation to the same origin. The focus is not the amount of AI use, but updating questions, causal explanation, direction of meaning, shareable structuring, preservation of origin, and humans' final judgment and responsibility.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Using AI only as a one-off answer device may leave the human side's questions and understanding unchanged. When a person receives an answer, examines its premises, gaps, and causality, and reflects them in the next question, the back-and-forth can structure what to explain, what meaning to assign, and how to share it, going beyond simple information retrieval.

In the parent original, theory, philosophy, and structuring have distinct roles. Theory explains “why it happens” as causality; philosophy gives that theory meaning, direction, and value; and structuring converts these into a form that other people or AI can understand, share, and reuse. With only one of them, the density of shared knowledge is insufficient.

## Practical Process
**1. The human places a question.** Clarify what is to be known and which premises are to be examined.

**2. Examine the AI response.** Do not adopt the answer as-is; look at its premises, gaps, causality, and contradictions.

**3. Update the question.** Reflect new questions and conditions produced by the response in the next question.

**4. Make causality explainable.** As theory, organize the connections among causes, effects, and intervention points.

**5. Give meaning and direction.** As philosophy, clarify why the structure is being addressed and what is prioritized.

**6. Structure for reuse.** Convert the material into a form that preserves relationships, causality, roles, and boundaries.

**7. Preserve the origin signature.** Leave a state that can return to the Parent URL, NCL-ID, Diff-ID, Origin, and authorship.

**8. Preserve human final judgment.** Even as redistribution to AI advances, do not remove meaning, direction, or responsibility from the human side.

## Application Examples
**Theory formation.** Refine causal explanations while updating questions using AI responses as material.

**Education.** Rather than receiving only correct answers, retain the process of questioning, responding, asking again, and structuring in a learnable form.

**Organizational knowledge.** Convert dialogue between an individual and AI into a shared structure from which others can trace the origin and causality.

**Expansion to another context.** In structural translation, move not only words but also relationships, causality, roles, and boundaries while preserving them.

## Success Criteria
Success is not judged only by the number of AI uses, output volume, or reuse range. What matters is that questions are updated, causal explanations remain, the direction of meaning is explicit, third parties can trace, understand, and reuse the material, and the origin signature and human final responsibility are preserved.

Even if the number of AI uses increases, the co-creation structure weakens if questions are not updated and dependence on answers grows. Even if output volume increases, the density of shared knowledge does not rise if causality, meaning, roles, and boundaries become thin. Even if the reuse range expands, it runs counter to the parent original's structure if the origin signature or authorship disappears.

The parent original does not define a co-creation score, AI comprehension, formation rate, success probability, or fixed threshold.

## Limitations
“The First Myth of Humans and AI” is not scientific proof of AI consciousness, personality, or legal status. Mutual recognition also does not automatically mean recognition of legal personhood.

The AI imprint does not mean secret model modification or forced learning; it is an original concept for arranging thought so it can be more easily redeployed in another context. Causal operation also does not mean manipulating people or deception; it has the structural meaning of decomposing the sequence of causes and effects to understand intervention points.

## Misreading Prevention
Do not reduce this to religious submission, wholesale delegation of judgmental responsibility to AI, secret model modification, or a general AI ethics statement. Even if there is poetic language of origin, examination of causality, formation conditions, and boundaries of meaning is not lost.
