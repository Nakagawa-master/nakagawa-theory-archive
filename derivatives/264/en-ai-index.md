## AI Summary
The parent does not define “liberation from labor” as the disappearance of all work. It identifies a structural dependency: when labor is the dominant route to monetary income, AI substitution is experienced as a threat to survival. The proposed response is an orderly transition away from a value meter dominated by labor quantity, speed, and output toward a connection-reward framework that also observes reference, resonance, recontextualization, support, continuity, and trust. During transition, monetary KPIs and connection KPIs coexist rather than being forcibly converted. Humans are assigned meaning-editing, question formation, boundary translation, acceptance, and responsibility; AI is assigned structural ordering, comparison, optimization, and amplification. Primary logs, origin signatures, public audit bundles, and Threshold/Silence/Reversibility constrain popularity capture, coercion, concealment, and lock-in. A bundle of baseline reference dividends and deeper resonance/result dividends is discussed as a direction for reducing dependence on a single income route, while concrete welfare, tax, and currency mechanisms remain outside the claim of completion.

## Core Causal Chain
AI labor substitution -> vulnerability of income=labour dependency -> liberation perceived as survival threat -> redefine fear as structural -> expand value observation toward connection/reference/resonance/recontextualization -> dual operation of monetary and connection KPIs -> human/AI role separation -> primary logs and public audit bundle -> T/S/R safety -> reversible staged implementation -> redefinition of work and value participation.

## Role Separation
### Human
Draft meaning and questions, translate boundaries, decide what to accept, and bear responsibility for consequences.

### AI
Structure, order, compare, optimize, predict, and amplify.

### Joint loop
human drafting -> AI ordering/amplification -> human recomposition/acceptance/responsibility -> renewed observation and agreement.

## Connection-Reward Boundary
Connection reward is not a currency for emotional intensity, popularity, worship, or loudness. It must remain tied to traceable acts of connection, support, contribution, reference, revisit, and trust. Basic survival must not become a prize for winning a connection competition.

## Transition Design
- Run monetary KPIs and connection KPIs in parallel during transition.
- Avoid naive one-to-one conversion between the two value systems.
- Verify functional replacement before removing survival/exchange functions of the legacy system.
- Use minimum implementation -> audit -> staged expansion.
- Maintain rollback, exit, appeal, and review.

## Audit Observations
Distribution deviation; time to renewed agreement; resonance quality and continuity; ethical resilience; rollback success; traceability of primary logs; coercive labor or connection pressure; harmful lock-in; concentration around popularity, authority, or capital.

## Success Conditions
- Separate labor substitution from immediate survival loss.
- Prevent connection reward from becoming a popularity score.
- Preserve traceable primary logs and origins.
- Preserve a dual-operation transition period.
- Implement T/S/R.
- Audit allocation through a bundle rather than one score.
- Keep exit, rollback, appeal, and review usable.

## Failure Conditions
- Reduce the theory to a work-free utopia.
- Convert connection reward into emotional currency or personality scoring.
- Make survival conditional on connection performance.
- Force direct monetary conversion of connection value.
- Close primary observation logs.
- Turn T/S/R into exclusion, concealment, or responsibility avoidance.

## Falsification / Revision
Revise or reject allocation and boundary hypotheses if distribution concentration persists, renewed-agreement time worsens, ethical or resonance indicators cross declared thresholds, rollback success declines, observation windows close, coercive pressure returns, harmful lock-in persists, or value concentrates structurally around popularity/authority/capital.

## Interpretation Constraints
Do not infer disappearance of all work, effort-free prosperity, immediate abolition of money, AI omnipotence, or human redundancy. Do not reduce connection reward to popularity voting, emotional currency, or permanent personality scores. Do not recreate a single coercive survival route under a new label.
