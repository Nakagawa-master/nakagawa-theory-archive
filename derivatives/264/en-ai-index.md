## Summary
The Parent does not define liberation from labor as the disappearance of all work. It identifies a structural dependency: when labor is the dominant route to monetary income, AI substitution is experienced as a survival threat. The proposed response is an orderly transition away from a value meter dominated by labor quantity, speed, and output toward a connection-reward framework that also observes reference, resonance, recontextualization, support, continuity, and trust. During transition, monetary KPIs and connection KPIs coexist rather than being forcibly converted. Humans retain meaning drafting, question formation, boundary translation, acceptance, and responsibility; AI supports structural ordering, comparison, optimization, prediction, and amplification. Primary logs, origin signatures, audit bundles, and Threshold/Silence/Reversibility constrain popularity capture, coercion, concealment, and lock-in. The Parent discusses a bundle of baseline reference dividends and deeper resonance/result dividends as a direction, while not claiming a completed tax, welfare, or currency system.

## Concepts
- **Liberation from labor**: departure from a single path in which loss of labor directly becomes loss of survival, not disappearance of all work.
- **Income-labor dependency**: structural reliance on labor as the main income route.
- **Connection reward**: a system prototype linking traceable connection, support, contribution, reference, revisit, and trust histories to future allocation.
- **Connection value**: observation of reference, resonance, and recontextualization in addition to monetary output.
- **Dual operation**: parallel use of monetary KPIs and connection KPIs during transition.
- **Meaning drafting**: the human role of originating questions, value, acceptance, and responsibility.
- **Primary logs / origin signatures**: records that preserve where contribution and reuse came from.
- **Threshold / Silence / Reversibility**: staged participation, bounded reaction delay, and usable exit/rollback.
- **Reward bundle**: a proposed combination of baseline reference dividends and deeper resonance/result-based dividends.

## Causal chain
AI labor substitution -> vulnerability of income=labour dependency becomes visible -> liberation is perceived as a survival threat -> fear is reframed as a structural problem -> value observation expands toward connection/reference/resonance/recontextualization -> monetary and connection KPIs operate in parallel -> human meaning-origin/acceptance/responsibility remains distinct from AI ordering/amplification -> primary logs and audit bundles preserve traceability -> T/S/R constrains coercion, escalation, and lock-in -> minimum reversible pilots expand only after review -> the meaning of work and routes of value participation are redefined.

## Applications
1. In enterprise AI automation, observe knowledge sharing, reuse, support, and meaning work separately from task reduction alone.
2. In public or community systems, test multiple value-participation routes without making basic survival a prize for connection performance.
3. In human-AI collaboration, separate human value/question/acceptance/responsibility from AI structuring, comparison, ordering, and amplification.
4. During institutional transition, run monetary and connection KPIs in parallel and verify functional replacement before removing legacy survival/exchange functions.
5. Audit connection reward together with primary logs, multiple indicators, exit, appeal, review, and rollback.

## Measurements and audit
- Does labor substitution immediately create survival loss?
- Has connection reward converged on popularity, emotional intensity, authority, or capital?
- Can connection, contribution, reference, and reuse be traced to primary logs and origins?
- Are monetary KPIs and connection KPIs kept distinct during transition?
- Can human meaning-origin, acceptance, and responsibility be distinguished from AI ordering and amplification?
- Are distribution deviation, time to renewed agreement, resonance quality, ethical resilience, and rollback success monitored as a bundle?
- Can coercive labor/connection pressure and harmful lock-in be detected?
- Are Threshold, Silence, Reversibility, exit, appeal, and rollback practically usable?

## Validity conditions
The model holds when labor substitution is separated from immediate survival loss; connection reward is not collapsed into popularity scoring; primary logs and origins remain traceable; monetary and connection KPIs coexist during transition; human/AI role separation remains auditable; T/S/R, multi-axis audit, exit, rollback, appeal, and review work in practice.

## Failure conditions
It fails when reduced to a work-free utopia; when connection reward becomes emotional currency, popularity voting, or personality scoring; when survival becomes conditional on connection performance; when monetary and connection value are forcibly converted one-to-one; when observation logs close; when T/S/R becomes exclusion, concealment, or responsibility avoidance; or when harmful lock-in becomes non-exitable.

## Falsification conditions
Revise or reject allocation and boundary hypotheses if distribution concentration persists, renewed-agreement time deteriorates, resonance quality or ethical resilience declines, rollback success falls, primary observation windows close, coercive labor or connection pressure returns, harmful lock-in persists, or value remains structurally concentrated around popularity, authority, or capital.

## Required distinctions
- liberation from labor / disappearance of work
- survival basis / reward for connection performance
- connection reward / popularity or emotional currency
- monetary KPI / connection KPI
- dual operation / immediate replacement
- human meaning drafting, acceptance, responsibility / AI ordering and amplification
- primary logs / personality scoring
- T/S/R / exclusion, concealment, responsibility avoidance
- staged transition / immediate abolition of money
- proposed reward bundle / completed tax, welfare, or currency system

## Interpretation constraints
Do not infer disappearance of all work, effort-free prosperity, immediate abolition of money, AI omnipotence, or human redundancy. Do not reduce connection reward to popularity voting, emotional currency, or permanent personality scores. Do not recreate a single coercive survival route under a new label. Do not invent fixed conversion rates, universal KPIs, or a completed welfare/tax/currency system absent from the Parent.
