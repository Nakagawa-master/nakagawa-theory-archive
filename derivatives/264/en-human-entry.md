## 15-second explanation
What makes people afraid of AI replacing their jobs is not that work itself is the sole meaning of life, but that under the current system, **the stoppage of labor leads directly to the stoppage of income and anxiety about survival**. The Parent Source unravels this structure and designs connection rewards that broaden the instrument for measuring value beyond labor volume to connection, reference, resonance, and recontextualization, as a "testable, reversible system" equipped with T/S/R and audits.

## Why is it needed?
In today's society, labor is the main route to monetary income. That is why the claim that AI will replace labor creates the fear that "income will disappear and we will no longer be able to live" before the hope that "free time will increase."

What the Parent Source considers important is not turning this fear into an individual's weakness. The problem lies in the institutional structure that strongly fixes **income = labor**. If the structure is the cause, the solution can also be considered as institutional design.

## What changes?
What changes is not the binary choice of "work or do not work," but **what is used to measure value**.

Traditionally, working hours, quantity of outputs, speed, sales, and so on were central. Connection rewards additionally observe whether someone's knowledge was deeply referenced, whether a relationship continued, whether it was reused in another context, and where trust or support arose.

## Connection rewards are not a popularity vote
This boundary is important. Connection rewards do not directly turn emotional intensity or enthusiasm into money. It would fail if the system became one in which only loud, popular, or worshipped people benefit.

What is needed is to record in primary logs how connection, support, contribution, and re-reference actually arose, so that the grounds for evaluation can be traced.

## Will work disappear?
The Parent Source does not define "liberation from labor = the end of work." Meaning compilation, boundary translation, and question design will remain as work.

What changes is the measuring stick for the value of work. Alongside the speed and quantity of outputs, reference, resonance, and recontextualization become parts of value.

## Roles of humans and AI
Humans take responsibility for what is considered good, what questions are asked, which meanings are adopted, and who bears the consequences. AI handles structuring, ordering, comparison, optimization, and amplification.

```text
Humans draft meanings and questions
↓
AI structures, orders, and amplifies
↓
Humans recompile, and bear the decisions and responsibility
```

## How is the basis for survival considered?
The Parent Source indicates a direction in which survival is not made dependent on a single income source. It considers basic reference dividends derived from public contribution and shared learning, together with dividends according to deep resonance and outcomes, as a **bundle of connection rewards**.

However, the complete form of a specific benefit system or currency system is not settled by this Parent Source alone.

## The transition is not made all at once
This does not mean discarding monetary KPIs today and switching to connection KPIs alone tomorrow. During the transition, both run in parallel: the survival and exchange functions supported by the old system are not broken, and whether the new system truly works is checked.

In other words, this is **an orderly replacement**, not a revolution.

## Three safety principles
### Threshold｜閾値
Participation, connection, allocation, and authority are staged to prevent overheating and abrupt replacement.

### Silence｜沈黙
Time is secured for asynchronous organization without immediately reacting or evaluating. However, without clearly stating the purpose, period, and responsibility, it becomes concealment.

### Reversibility｜可逆性
Deadlines, re-review, exit, rollback, and compensation are prepared. It is testable because it can be reversed; when reversing it, learning and responsibility are also retained.

## What audits examine
Whether connection rewards truly work is examined through multiple audit bundles rather than a single score. We check whether distribution is biased, whether re-agreement takes too long, whether withdrawal is possible, whether a third party can trace the primary logs, and whether forced labor or forced connection has reappeared.

## Practical process
1. Inventory which income and survival functions the stoppage of labor directly affects.
2. Define the histories of connection, reference, resonance, and recontextualization to observe beyond labor volume.
3. Design separately the allocation routes for the survival basis and value participation.
4. Run monetary KPIs and connection KPIs in parallel during the transition.
5. Separate human meaning-drafting, acceptance/rejection, and responsibility from AI ordering and amplification.
6. Leave connection, contribution, and re-reference in primary logs.
7. Prepare Threshold / Silence / Reversibility, exit, appeals, and rollback in advance.
8. Test at minimum scope and confirm side effects and actual functional replacement through audit bundles.
9. Expand in stages only within the range where the conditions for viability are maintained.

## Examples of application
**Companies.** Do not treat only the amount of work reduced by AI automation as the outcome; observe value participation such as knowledge sharing, re-reference, support, and meaning compilation on separate axes.

**Regions and the public sector.** Do not fix the survival basis to a single employment relationship; verify multiple participation routes such as public contribution and shared learning while retaining exit and appeals.

**Human-AI co-creation.** Humans hold meaning, questions, boundaries, acceptance/rejection, and responsibility, while AI handles structuring, comparison, ordering, and amplification. Do not replace total value with AI's production volume as-is.

## Success criteria
- Weaken the single route by which losing labor immediately makes survival impossible.
- Ensure connection rewards do not become proxies for popularity, enthusiasm, or authority.
- Make contribution, connection, and re-reference traceable in primary logs.
- Run monetary KPIs and connection KPIs in parallel during the transition.
- Make the boundary between human and AI roles traceable.
- Make T/S/R, exit, appeals, and rollback actually usable.
- Make staged introduction and re-review function.

## Limitations
This Parent Source does not predict that "AI will eliminate all work." It is neither a policy document that immediately abolishes money nor a document guaranteeing that people can become unconditionally prosperous without working. The specific tax, benefit, and currency design of connection rewards requires separate verification.

## Preventing misreading
- Do not turn liberation from labor into the disappearance of work.
- Do not make survival security a prize for connection records.
- Do not turn connection rewards into emotional currency or a popularity vote.
- Do not simply convert money and connection value.
- Do not turn this into AI omnipotence or human dispensability.
- Do not turn T/S/R into concealment, exclusion, or evasion of responsibility.
- Do not turn primary logs into personality scores.
