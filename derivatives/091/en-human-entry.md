## 15-Second Explanation
If the future is predicted only as an extension of current data, the moment when the structure changes is missed. The parent original reads three defects—hardening information asymmetry, destruction of role architecture, and cessation of temporal recursion—as accumulating civilizational friction, with stronger control strengthening the conditions for reorganization. However, the phase after reorganization is not singular; tuning can change the branches among hardening progression, partial tuning, and overall tuning.

## Why Strengthening Control Can Backfire
Increasing monitoring increases observation costs, while approval layers and exception handling consume the bandwidth of information circuits. As exception handling increases, responsibility boundaries become ambiguous, and as penalties intensify, over-adaptation to short-term indicators occurs. The result is the advance of “formal compliance / substantive hollowing,” in which formal rules are followed while the ability to adjust to reality is lost.

This is not a story about insufficient effort. It is a story about structure: increasing effort in a field with poor circuit design turns that effort itself into friction.

## Three Defects
**Hardening information asymmetry** means that those who know and those who are not informed become fixed. The problem is not a temporary information gap, but fixed access rights, recording formats, and reuse standards that continue to produce observational hollowness and delayed correction.

**Destruction of role architecture** means that the starting and ending points of responsibility for humans, organizations, and AI become ambiguous. Although filling the gap with person-dependent skill may look fast in the short term, institutional learning is not retained and reproducibility falls.

**Cessation of temporal recursion** means that short-term evaluation consumes long-term structural investment, past precedents become shields for justification, and the future becomes an after-the-fact explanation.

## Four Principles of Tuning
The parent original presents a single line of purpose–standard–observation, simplification of circuits, redesign of boundaries, and temporal recursion. Do not end purpose at declaration; translate it into standards and support standards through observation. Reduce inputs and increase reuse, and redraw the human/AI/institution boundary by the starting and ending points of responsibility. Place future standards first, connect the present through minimum change, and reorganize the past as a learning asset.

## 20-Minute Public-Safe Frame
The frame specifies two minutes for the meaning axis, five for issue arrangement, five for SQS, five for minimum change, and three for the freedom test: a 2/5/5/5/3-minute form. It totals 20 minutes, but does not mean that a civilizational problem can be solved in 20 minutes. It is the implementation frame published by the original.

Compare intervention A with B’, changing only one factor, and confirm whether an effect in the same direction continues for three cycles. Record side effects together as changes in freedom. Three cycles are not a universal statistical guarantee but a verification specification specific to the original.

## Three Future Phases
**Hardening progression phase:** control strengthens while asymmetry and ambiguous responsibility boundaries remain, accumulating delay, inhibition, and exception handling behind short-term order.

**Partial tuning phase:** rereference rate and self-running degree improve in limited areas, but standard mismatch with adjacent areas becomes new friction.

**Overall tuning phase:** purpose–standard–observation, circuit simplification, and boundary clarification work across a broad area, allowing reorganization to be reread not as “uncontrollable collapse” but as a transition of natural alignment.

## What to Measure
Agreement time, self-running degree, rereference rate, friction ratio, and the D-gap are the main indicators. Rereference rate is presented as the number of secondary uses of documents or standards / total uses. Friction ratio is explanation, exception-handling, and renewed-approval time / total time.

However, do not automatically judge short agreement time, high self-running degree, or high rereference rate as good. Suppressing dissent makes agreement faster, and fixing an incorrect standard raises rereference rate. Read them together with freedom, responsibility boundaries, and consistency with primary records.

## Counterevidence and Limits
If tuning produces no reproducible difference in agreement time, rereference rate, friction ratio, or boundary coherence; the direction does not stabilize over three cycles; freedom worsens; or partial tuning constantly increases friction with adjacent areas, revise the tuning hypothesis.

In the integrated audit summary, agreement time, rereference rate, friction ratio, boundary-redesign coherence, RC/ER/CEA/S, Rollback Cost, and threshold θ and observation window δ are placed in counterevidence relations. The original does not provide concrete values for θ or δ, so do not add numbers.

## Preventing Misreading
Do not turn the strong expression “reorganization will come” into a prediction of collapse in a particular month or a probability forecast. Structural necessity does not mean that one specific phase is uniquely determined; it is not determinism. Do not turn tuning into hidden operation, circuit simplification into information deletion, or boundary redesign into evasion of responsibility.
