The source argues that hardened information asymmetry, broken responsibility architecture, and stopped temporal recursion can make stronger control accelerate reorganization. Structural tuning reconnects purpose, standards, and observation, simplifies information circuits, redraws responsibility boundaries, and restores future-to-present recursion so that the phase of reorganization becomes selectable rather than merely suffered. ## Applications

**1.** Public administration can replace additional approval layers with a shared standard and reusable primary record while measuring agreement time and exception work.

**2.** AI adoption can redraw proposal, decision, and verification responsibility instead of merely replacing data entry and then test one change at a time.

**3.** Research and education can connect future capability standards to one current evaluation change rather than optimizing only short-term output metrics.

**4.** Market or regional transition programs can audit friction transfer across adjacent domains before scaling a local improvement into a system-wide rule.

## Measurements and audit

- agreement-time delta across repeated cycles
- autonomy without additional explanation
- re-reference rate of primary records and standards
- friction ratio of explanation, exceptions, and reapproval
- D-gap between expected and reached structural depth
- repeatability of one-factor A/B-prime changes
- degrees of freedom and rollback cost

## Validity conditions

- observable friction and reusable primary records exist
- at least one structural parameter can be changed without masking other factors
- responsibility start and end points can be named by participants
- a one-factor comparison window is available under comparable conditions
- adjacent and long-term effects can be observed rather than assumed
- rollback and re-agreement remain possible after the intervention

## Failure conditions

- adding control without repairing information circuits
- changing multiple factors simultaneously and losing effect attribution
- relying on exceptional individuals or AI to absorb boundary failures
- optimizing only precedent and short-term KPIs
- declaring local speed gains while exporting friction to adjacent domains
- substituting explanatory volume for primary observation and reuse

## Falsification conditions

- metrics fail to improve over repeated cycles
- boundary redesign increases double responsibility or hidden work
- one-factor gains fail to reproduce under comparable conditions
- freedom falls while rollback cost and exception work rise
- the predicted relation between the three defects and reorganization is not observed
