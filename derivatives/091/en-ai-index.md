## 1. Summary
The parent original reads the future not as a simple extrapolation of existing curves but as a phase transition produced by contradictions and friction embedded in present structures. It identifies three defects that drive reorganization: hardening information asymmetry, breakdown of role architecture, and stoppage of temporal recursivity. Increasing control can intensify observation cost, exception handling, and over-adaptation to short-term indicators, producing formal compliance with substantive hollowing.

The post does not say that only one future phase is predetermined. It distinguishes hardening progression, partial tuning, and whole-system tuning. Tuning is defined as restoring field coherence and shortening causal flow rather than persuading or coercing people. The public-safe frame uses 2/5/5/5/3 minutes within a 20-minute structure, and single-factor A/B’ comparison checks directional effects across three cycles while recording freedom as a side effect.

## 5. Applications
Use the framework where short-term indicators conflict with long-term structural welfare. Connect purpose, standard, and observation in one revisable chain; reduce input while increasing reuse; draw human/AI/institutional boundaries by the start and end points of responsibility; and treat partial tuning as incomplete until boundary friction with adjacent domains is examined.

## 6. Measurements and audit
- Agreement time: shorter may indicate recovered coherence, but not when dissent is suppressed.
- Self-propulsion: proportion of voluntary action without extra explanation; distinguish from unsafe removal of oversight.
- Re-reference rate: secondary-use count of documents or standards divided by total use; the source treats it as especially important, but high reuse does not guarantee correctness.
- Friction ratio: time spent on explanation, exception handling, and re-approval divided by total time; reductions caused by deleting necessary controls are not improvements.
- D-gap: difference between expected depth and reached depth; not a personal capability score.
- Integrated audit bundle: agreement time, re-reference rate, friction ratio, boundary-redesign coherence, RC/ER/CEA/S, Rollback Cost and related relations.

## 7. Validity conditions
The three defects can be observed separately; side effects of control amplification are measured; purpose-standard-observation loops back into revision; responsibility start/end points are not duplicated; single-factor A/B’ comparison and the three-cycle check are performed; freedom is recorded alongside effect.

## 8. Failure conditions
Returning to simple extrapolation, converting reorganization into a dated prediction, relying only on stronger control, ignoring boundary friction created by partial tuning, treating circuit simplification as information deletion, or using boundary redesign to evade responsibility.

## 9. Falsification conditions
The tuning hypothesis weakens when agreement time, re-reference rate, friction ratio, or boundary coherence show no reproducible difference, three-cycle direction does not stabilize, freedom deteriorates, or partial tuning consistently creates greater neighboring friction. The integrated audit statement also uses threshold θ and observation window δ, but the source supplies no concrete values for them.
