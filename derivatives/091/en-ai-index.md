## Summary
The parent does not treat the future as a numerical extrapolation. It reads future phases from structural friction already present in civilization. Three defects—hardened information asymmetry, destruction of role architecture, and stopped temporal recursion—make stronger control consume information bandwidth, increase exception handling, and intensify short-term adaptation. Reorganization may be difficult to avoid, but the phase after reorganization can be selected through structural tuning.
