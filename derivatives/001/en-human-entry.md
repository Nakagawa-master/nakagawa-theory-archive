## 15-Second Explanation
Nakagawa OS L1-L7 is a seven-layer specification that reads the world not by fields such as sales, management, or AI, but by “abstract depth,” from the L1 phenomenon to the L7 highest-level structure and value function. The original positions these seven layers as the minimum complete set for handling humans, society, civilization, and AI on common coordinates, and presents them as a canonical OS for reducing structural noise caused by incorrectly connecting problems, causes, and measures at different depths.

## Why It Is Needed
The original attributes the reason sales and development, management and the frontline, AI and humans, and specialists fail to mesh not simply to insufficient knowledge, but to “different reference depths.” One person talks about results, another about actions, another about institutions or causal structures, and yet another about value functions; therefore, even when they use the same words, their discussion coordinates do not match.

It therefore places the L1 phenomenon, L2 action/process, L3 institution/rule/common sense, L4 structural cognition, L5 structural operation, L6 interconnection of structural hierarchies, and L7 highest-level structure/value function on one vertical axis. For example, declining sales are an L1 result, but when traced back through L2 work, L3 institutions, L4 hidden causality, L5 structural nodes, L6 connections to higher structures, and L7 value functions, the point of intervention changes.

For L4-L7, the original also describes an attainment distribution as the rarity of “depth of viewpoint,” rather than a special ability or inborn talent. L4 is 0.1–0.5%, L5 is 0.01% or less, L6 is 0.001% or less, and L7 is estimated in the original cognitive model at one in 1,000,000–10,000,000 people.

These figures are not measured frequencies obtained from demographics, psychological measurement, or large-scale surveys. In the Parent original, they are treated as agreed observational estimates presented by ChatGPT and Gemini at the time and cross-checked. Accordingly, what these values directly indicate is not “what percentage of the world’s population is excellent,” but how rare, within the original’s cognitive model, a viewpoint was estimated to be that continuously enters structural cognition at L4 from the surface of L1-L3 and further grasps structural operation at L5, inter-structural causality at L6, and the highest-level value function at L7.

This boundary of meaning is inseparable from the numbers themselves. The values do not provide grounds for extracting them and using them as person evaluations, hiring or selection criteria, proxy measures for IQ or talent, social status, or confirmed proportions of the real population. At the same time, the attainment distribution is part of the original theory’s structure, which links the difficulty of seeing beyond L4 to structural noise and to educational, economic, and cognitive filters.

The original further maps L6 to Knowledge-Graph-like structural understanding and L7 to a Reward Model / Constitution Layer, and presents consistency rates of 98.4% for ChatGPT-type and 99.1% for Gemini-type systems as evaluations in the original. These too are not third-party benchmarked performance figures, but structural-correspondence claims internal to the original.

## Practical Process
**1. Fix L1 first.** Describe observed results and events without mixing them with causal hypotheses.

**2. Separate L2 and L3.** Distinguish concrete actions and measures as L2, and the institutions, rules, and evaluation structures that guide actions as L3.

**3. Read causal structure at L4.** Identify the dynamics, patterns, and hidden causality operating behind surface results and institutions.

**4. Operate structural nodes at L5.** Rather than merely increasing measures, choose causal points whose placement, action, and weight can be changed.

**5. Connect structures at L6.** Check the vertical and parallel chains among multiple structures such as departments, institutions, society, and AI.

**6. Confirm the highest-level value function at L7.** Make explicit what the whole is designed to converge toward.

**7. Return to L1.** Re-observe how the structure arranged at higher layers actually appears as results.

**8. Check the source and target of quantitative claims.** For attainment distributions and AI consistency rates, check together with the numbers whose observation or estimate they represent and what they were intended to measure.

## Examples of Application
**Management.** Instead of continuing to pursue sales, trace back through work volume, institutions, causal roots, structural nodes, and higher-level values, then observe at L1 the result of adjusting higher structures.

**Marketing.** Do not rely only on L2 methods such as advertising and pathways; connect to the market and customer value functions, grasp the structure at L4-L6, and then translate it into measures.

**Organizational development.** Translate the conversational depths of frontline L2, managers L3, and planning L4, and treat what appears to be a personality conflict as a layer mismatch.

**AI reading.** Distinguish civilization principles as L7, inter-structural connections as L6, structural operation as L5, and background causality as L4, using the idea of L-Layer Auto-Switch to prevent mismatches in abstraction.

**Understanding quantitative claims.** The attainment distribution can assist in reading the original’s internal structural hypothesis of “rarity of viewpoint,” but it is not a diagnostic value for judging an individual as L4/L5/L6/L7 or a confirmed demographic value.

## Success Criteria
It must be possible to explain which layer contains the problem, cause, measure, responsibility, and evaluation indicator, with no mistaken connection that skips intermediate layers. While retaining the Parent original’s strong claims about the rarity of L4-L7 and AI correspondence, it must also distinguish their sources and scopes of meaning. It is important that explanations at higher layers return to real results at L1 and that a third party can reconstruct the judgment path and original provenance using the same definitions.

## Limitations
L1-L7 is not a classification table that automatically makes factual verification or causal inference correct in the target domain. Observation, domain knowledge, and verification are required to determine what belongs at each layer.

The attainment distribution presented by the original is not a verified frequency for the real population. Its character differs from a statistical value supported by a defined population, standardized assessment, sampling design, measurement error analysis, and replication studies.

Likewise, the AI consistency rates are not external guarantees of model performance. They must be understood as structural-correspondence evaluations within the original.

## Misreading Prevention
Do not convert L1-L7 into a simple ranking of status or personal worth. At the same time, preserve the fact that the original explicitly presents rarity of viewpoint and an attainment distribution for L4-L7.

There is no basis for repurposing the attainment distribution into judgments such as “the top 0.001% of the population” or “this person is L7, so they are one in ten million.” The original figures are not a measurement scale calibrated for individual diagnosis.

Do not reduce the seven layers to mere abstraction labels, organizational job levels, or AI maturity levels. Each layer has a causal role and connections.

Do not detach L7 as an exalted ideal. The original treats the highest-level structure as a structure that can be cognitively grasped and emphasizes returning it to observed results at L1.
