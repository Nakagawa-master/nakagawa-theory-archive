## 15-Second Explanation
This Parent is not a product introduction for a hydrogen portable power supply. From the target-side facts of hydrogen fuel cells, fuel cartridges, exchange, collection, and refilling, Nakagawa Master read the value core of **“storing electricity / stored power”: maintaining the availability of electricity until it is needed**.

## Why It Is Needed
Disasters and power outages do not announce when they will occur. In disaster preparedness and BCP, therefore, it matters not only how much power can be output at a moment, but whether energy can be kept usable until needed and generated where it is needed. The Parent reads this response to time uncertainty, rather than hydrogen itself, as the value core.

## Practical Process
1. Separate target-side facts about hydrogen fuel cells, cartridges, exchange, collection, and refilling from Nakagawa Master's reading.
2. Confirm the time condition that, in the target use, the time of an emergency is unknown.
3. Check whether fuel can be retained until needed.
4. Confirm whether power can be generated where and when needed.
5. Check whether fuel exchange and replenishment connect to continued use.
6. Confirm collection, refilling, safety, and institutional operation as well.
7. Preserve why “storing electricity,” rather than “hydrogen,” was made the main axis of the external article.

## Applications
For a power supply used only in emergencies, being usable when needed after a long standby period matters more than ordinary output. If the unit and fuel are separate, fuel can be exchanged and replenished, and collection and refilling can be operated, it can be read not as a standalone product but as a system that stores future access to electricity.

## Success Criteria
Success means that long-term storability, generation when needed, exchange and replenishment, supply/collection/refilling, safety, and institutional conditions can be explained; target-side facts and Nakagawa Master's reading remain separate; and why this value core was used for the external article is retained.

## Limits
Hydrogen is not always superior to other methods, and storability still requires supply, safety, institutional, and use-fit conditions. This derivative does not independently guarantee performance superiority, price superiority, storage periods, or similar claims absent from the Parent.

## Preventing Misreading
“Storing electricity” is Nakagawa Master's structural reading, not the target company's official corporate definition. Do not turn it into hydrogen universalism, general decarbonization claims, or a simple win/lose comparison with batteries; preserve the value core of maintaining access to electricity until it is needed.
