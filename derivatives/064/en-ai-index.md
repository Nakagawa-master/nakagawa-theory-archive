## 1. Summary
First impressions are treated as provisional hypotheses about expectation, concern, role, responsibility, and choice rather than as consent. Existence, meaning, conditions, evidence, confirmation, and memory are separated so that understanding, limited agreement, non-agreement, hold, revision, and withdrawal remain jointly rereadable.

## 2. Concepts
- First impression: a provisional post-contact hypothesis, not consent.
- Consensus memory: jointly rereadable understanding, agreement, hold, refusal, and withdrawal states.
- Six layers: existence, meaning, conditions, evidence, confirmation, memory.
- State set: CONTACT / PROVISIONAL_UNDERSTANDING / PROVISIONAL_AGREEMENT / CONFIRMED / REVISED / WITHDRAWN.
- Joint rereadability: both parties can review the same record.
- Power-asymmetry protection: cooling time, third-party confirmation, protected refusal.

## 3. Causal chain
```text
contact
→ first impression and assumptions
→ implicit continuation
→ false consent and responsibility mismatch
→ six-layer separation
→ disclose conditions/disadvantages/withdrawal
→ counterparty restates understanding
→ separate agreement/non-agreement/hold
→ reconfirm/revise/withdraw
→ consensus memory
```

## 5. Applications
- Sales: do not convert interest into contractual intent.
- Hiring/collaboration: separate positive impression from role fit.
- Meetings: distinguish support, non-objection, hold, unconfirmed, and opposition.
- Human-AI work: separate proposal, judgment, approval, execution, and audit.
- Power asymmetry: add cooling periods, third-party review, and protected refusal.

## 7. Validity conditions
- Questions, refusal, hold, and correction remain free.
- Cost, deadline, responsibility, disadvantage, non-applicability, and withdrawal paths are visible.
- First-impression assumptions are not treated as facts.
- Records are jointly rereadable.
- Silence, nodding, or non-objection are not treated as consent.
- Power asymmetry receives additional protection.

## 8. Failure conditions
- Agreement obtained through charm, authority, fear, or haste.
- Silence or ambiguous responses treated as consent.
- Conditions, disadvantages, or exit disclosed only later.
- Records used for surveillance, binding, or blame shifting.
- One-time consent made permanent.
- Reconfirmation fails to reproduce understanding but the process continues.

## 9. Falsification conditions
- Repeated confirmation still cannot reproduce understanding.
- Condition mismatch and responsibility shifting do not improve.
- More recording causes fewer questions or refusals because of surveillance pressure.
- Reconfirmation becomes forced convergence.
- Protected refusal still does not work under power asymmetry.
- Faster agreement is achieved by removing explanation, comparison, or cooling time.

## 10. Required distinctions
- first-impression design / impression manipulation
- consensus memory / memory fixation
- six layers / maturity score
- state set / person evaluation
- limited agreement / comprehensive consent
- silence / consent
- non-objection / support
- record / surveillance
- continuation / lock-in
- reconfirmation / forced convergence

## 11. Interpretation constraints
Do not reduce the framework to sales closing, psychological manipulation, implicit consent, record-based binding, or responsibility shifting. The six layers and state set are classifications, not scores. Do not optimize agreement speed or rate alone; read them with questions, refusal, holds, correction, and withdrawal. Do not invent thresholds absent from the parent source.
