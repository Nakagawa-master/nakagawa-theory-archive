## Summary
The parent does not read REMODELA merely as an “AI move-out inspection” product. It reads a structure in which the condition at move-out is observed, recorded, converted into judgment material, made comparable across landlord, tenant, management, and other relevant actors, and connected to an explainable path toward a restoration judgment. The value core is therefore a validity basis for restoration judgment, not automated authority.

A conclusion alone does not establish validity. What was observed, when and how it was recorded, which record became relevant judgment material, where stakeholder recognition diverged, and why the final judgment was reached all remain traceable elements. AI may assist recording, organization, comparison, and transformation into usable material, but its output is not identical to legal, contractual, or factual correctness.

The parent also contains a second layer of Nakagawa Structural Reading: it publicly explains why Nakagawa Master selected “validity basis for restoration” rather than the surface category “AI move-out inspection” as the value core for an external article.

## Causal chain
```text
observe the condition at move-out
↓
record the observation in a later-verifiable form
↓
convert the record into judgment material
↓
allow relevant stakeholders to refer to and compare the same material
↓
make recognition gaps and different judgment premises visible
↓
trace why a judgment was reached
↓
connect the conclusion to evidence and reasoning
↓
make restoration judgment examinable
↓
establish a validity basis rather than an automatic correctness guarantee
```

## Applications
Move-out condition review examines not only whether photographs or records exist, but which place, time, and condition they represent and how they enter later judgment. Stakeholder disagreement can be examined by whether landlord, tenant, and management can return to common observation material and identify where recognition or premises diverge. AI remains one component for organizing records, comparing states, or producing judgment material, rather than final contractual, legal, or factual authority.

The causal chain observation → record → judgment material → shared comparison → recognition-gap explanation → reason traceability → validity basis remains source-specific, together with the public-deconstruction layer explaining why “validity basis” was selected as the external-article value core.

## Measurements and audit
The parent does not define a universal validity score, AI accuracy rate, dispute-reduction percentage, cost-validity percentage, responsibility probability, or fixed pass threshold.

Observable relations include whether the observation target and time are explicit, records remain verifiable, records connect to judgment material, relevant actors can compare the same material, recognition gaps can be explained, judgment reasons can be traced, and AI output has not become unquestioned authority.

Reversal evaluability remains important. More records can be structurally worse when important differences are buried in volume. Faster AI processing can be worse when the reasoning path becomes less traceable. More explanation fields do not improve explainability if evidence and conclusion become more weakly connected. Fewer disputes are not automatically proof of better validity if disagreement is merely suppressed or claims become harder to raise.

## Validity conditions
- The move-out condition is observed rather than assumed.
- Observations remain available in a later-verifiable record.
- Records connect to the judgment material for which they are relevant.
- Stakeholders can refer to and compare common material.
- Recognition gaps and judgment premises remain explainable.
- Traceability from conclusion back to evidence and reasons is preserved.
- AI output is not used as proof of correctness by itself.
- Contractual, legal, deterioration-over-time, responsibility, and case-specific conditions remain visible outside the record-processing layer.
- Both the target-service structure and the public-deconstruction layer remain present.

## Failure conditions
The reading is reduced to a product profile, usage recommendation, implementation-success guarantee, case-specific quotation assessment, legal advice, or a guarantee that AI output proves a charge or restoration responsibility correct. It also fails if more records are treated as monotonically higher validity, if generic transparency language replaces the specific observation-to-judgment-material chain, or if the public-deconstruction layer is deleted.

## Falsification conditions
If improving observation, recording, conversion into judgment material, shared comparison, and reason traceability repeatedly fails to improve the explainability of recognition gaps or the traceability of judgment grounds, the validity-basis reading can be revised within the parent’s scope.

Conversely, if equally strong or stronger explainability, reproducibility, and responsibility clarity repeatedly remain stable without shared records or traceable judgment material, the claimed necessity of the record-based layer can be reconsidered. A single agreement or dispute does not by itself settle the structure.
