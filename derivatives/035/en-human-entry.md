## 15-Second Explanation
The parent original is not an article introducing REMODELA as a “service that makes move-out inspections more efficient with AI.” It reads REMODELA as a structure that creates a basis for examining the validity of restoration judgments by observing and recording the condition at move-out, turning those records into judgment material, enabling relevant parties to compare the same material, and making the reasons for judgments traceable.

At the same time, the parent original publicly deconstructs why Nakagawa Master adopted “a validity basis for restoration” rather than “AI move-out inspection” as the value core and entry point in an external article. The structure of the target service and the reason for using that value core as the entry point of the external article form one integrated two-layer structure.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
In restoration at move-out, the landlord, tenant, management side, and others may not share the same recognition of the condition or the same premises for judgment. If what was seen, when the condition was observed, and which record was used as the basis are not retained, it is difficult to verify the reasons for a judgment later.

What therefore becomes important is turning observations into records, turning records into judgment material, and enabling relevant parties to refer to the same material. This moves the system closer to a state where the path leading to a conclusion can be followed later, rather than merely sharing the conclusion.

Even if AI has meaning, that does not mean “it is correct because AI judged it.” AI has value when it assists with organizing and comparing condition records and converting them into judgment material, and connects them to an information basis in which validity can be examined.

## Practical Process
1. Clarify which locations, at what time, and in what condition are to be checked.
2. Turn observations into records that can be checked later.
3. Do not merely increase the volume of records; organize which information relates to which judgment.
4. Create a state in which the landlord, tenant, management side, and others can return to the same material.
5. Make differences in recognition and in the premises for judgment visible.
6. Make it possible to trace not only the conclusion, but also the grounds, rules, and judgment path.
7. Do not make AI the final authority; retain the contract, laws and regulations, deterioration over time, and individual circumstances.
8. Confirm through the Parent URL why “a validity basis for restoration” was adopted as the value core of the external article.

## Applications
In checking the condition at move-out, do not stop at whether photographs or records exist; confirm how they are used in which judgment. When explaining a recognition gap, if the landlord and tenant make different claims, check whether they can return to the common records and identify the difference.

For AI assistance, do not treat AI as the final judge; treat it as one element that assists with organizing records, comparison, and turning records into judgment material.

## Success Criteria
Success is not judged only by the number of records, processing speed, or AI usage rate. It is important that the observation target is clear, records can be checked later, records connect to judgment material, relevant parties can compare them, and recognition gaps and judgment reasons can be explained and traced.

Even if the volume of records increases, validity does not increase if important differences are buried. Even if AI processing becomes faster, the validity basis can worsen if the reasons for judgment can no longer be traced. Even if explanation items increase, explainability does not improve if the connection between grounds and conclusions is weak.

The parent original does not define fixed numerical models such as a validity score, AI accuracy rate, dispute-reduction rate, cost-appropriateness rate, or fixed pass threshold.

## Limitations
This original does not automatically determine the validity of an individual charge, contract interpretation, or legal responsibility. The specific burden of restoration depends on the contract, laws and regulations, deterioration over time, condition, individual circumstances, and other factors. The existence of records alone does not make a judgment correct.

The functional explanation of REMODELA, the explanation in external media, and Nakagawa Master's structural reading are distinct.

## Misreading Prevention
Do not reduce this to “it is correct because AI judged it,” “validity increases with more records,” or a general discussion of transparency. Preserve both the structure of the target service and the second layer of the public deconstruction explaining why “a validity basis for restoration” was adopted as the value core of the external article.

The causality of observation, recording, conversion into judgment material, shared comparison, and reason traceability, together with the reason for selecting that value core, can be confirmed by returning to the Parent URL, Parent Post ID 4327, Parent NCL-ID, Parent Diff-ID, and Origin.
