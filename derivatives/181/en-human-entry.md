## 15-Second Explanation
The Parent Source holds that judging complex accidents and social problems only by “who is at fault” will not prevent recurrence if the institutions, evaluations, connections, and definitions of success that produced the same result remain. It therefore redefines justice from a mechanism for condemning individuals into a “civilization’s immune OS” that audits misalignment in causal wiring across L1–L7. The audit target is not personality but connection, and the standards are transparency, reproducibility, safety, and coherence. Non-aligned circuits are treated as “sedimentation” in the Parent model, while aligned circuits are treated as “confluence.”

## Why It Is Needed
Modern problems often do not end with a single actor. When multiple wirings overlap—evaluation systems encouraging short-term results, organizations hiding bad news, social media amplifying provocative reactions, and AI accelerating existing standards—one result may be produced. Punishing the person who appears on the surface leaves the generative conditions intact.

The Parent Source audits this recurrence structure by tracing vertically through L1 phenomena, L2 behavior, L3 institutions, L4 premises, L5 design philosophy, L6 hierarchical connections, and the L7 value function. The top-level Parent model is \`S=C×1.0\`; C is not moral “good-person-ness,” but a contribution that increases structural sustainability, reversibility, and coherence.

This is not an alternative description of current law. Actual crimes, harm, punishment, due process, and legality each have separate legal frameworks. “Sin,” “justice,” and “sedimentation” in the Parent Source include terms from its own theory and must not be transferred directly into real-world sanction procedures.

## Practical Process
**1. Fix the facts at L1.** Record what happened and what losses, gains, and reactions appeared. Do not substitute anger or impressions for facts.

**2. Trace L2 behavior.** See who chose what, but do not make the actor’s personality the endpoint of the cause.

**3. Examine L3 institutions.** Check which behaviors rules, KPIs, authority, procedures, and rewards made easier to choose.

**4. Put L4 premises into words.** Reveal the worldviews supporting judgment, such as “faster is better,” “do not show failure,” or “higher sales means success.”

**5. Check direction at L5.** See whether the design philosophy amplifies C or E. Handle the direction of the wiring, not people.

**6. Trace externalization at L6.** Check whether one department’s or person’s success is pushing irreversible damage onto other departments, customers, society, or the future.

**7. Read L7 through its substance.** Look not at ideals but at where evaluation, funds, authority, and attention gather. The Parent Source treats this as the implementation of the definition of success.

**8. Audit with T/R/S/A.** Check whether the process is transparent, reproducible under the same conditions, safe without damaging other layers, and aligned with the Parent value function.

**9. Stop amplification of misaligned circuits.** In the Parent Source, sedimentation is a structural concept for preventing amplification resources from continuing to flow into reverse-direction circuits, not a personal attack. Do not connect it directly to real-world rights restrictions or private punishment.

**10. Confluence and re-observation.** Confirm that connection flows toward circuits amplifying C and that recurrence, division, and E decrease as a result.

## Examples of Application
**Organizational quality incident.** Do not close the case as “the person in charge was careless.” Examine connections including deadline evaluations, suppression of reports, omitted reviews, insufficient authority, and short-term KPIs. Handle individual responsibility and structural recurrence prevention together without conflating them.

**Social-media backlash.** Do not immediately treat collective anger as justice. Separate facts, diffusion algorithms, attention rewards, clipped content, and delays in organizational response. What the Parent Source seeks to avoid is reproduction of mob punishment.

**Personnel evaluation.** If valuing short-term sales alone worsens long-term customer value, quality, and the burden on other departments, re-audit the connections of the success indicators.

**AI use.** Do not make AI decide “who is at fault.” Audit transparency of decision criteria, data, rewards, authority, and recoverability when an error occurs. This is not a design in which AI arbitrarily determines the Parent’s L7.

## Success Criteria
- The L1–L7 connections producing recurrence can be identified in addition to condemning an individual.
- An audit can satisfy all four T/R/S/A standards at once.
- Explanations can center on observable causal wiring rather than guesses about motives or personality.
- Mechanisms protecting C and preventing amplification of E work without increasing division or mob punishment.
- “Sedimentation” has not mutated into retaliation, exclusion, or deprivation of rights.
- “Confluence” has not mutated into majority conformity or thought control.
- After upper-level structures change, outcomes such as recurrence rates, irreversible damage, and division improve.
- When a condition-Z falsification condition is encountered, success can be withdrawn and the design revised.

## Limitations
The Parent Source is a normative and structural theory that broadly redefines justice; it does not describe the current legal system as it stands. \`S=C×1.0\`, sedimentation, confluence, immunity, and automation must be read as components of the Parent’s civilization-design model.

The Parent design of not making motives and emotions the final standard of structural audit does not mean that intent, subjectivity, or victims’ experiences are legally irrelevant in actual criminal, civil, labor, or administrative matters. This derivative does not provide individual legal judgments.

## Misreading Prevention
- “Do not judge sin” does not mean leaving actual crimes unattended.
- “Do not judge people” does not mean erasing responsibility. The Parent shifts the focus of structural audit to the wiring.
- “A bug even if it is not illegal” does not mean private citizens may punish by their own standards.
- “Sedimentation” is not expulsion, cancellation, or deprivation of rights.
- “Confluence” is not obedience to those in power or to the majority.
- “Automation” does not mean AI adjudicating without procedure.
- \`S=C×1.0\` is not a higher norm of current law or a universal scientific formula.
- T/R/S/A is not a score of human goodness or badness.
- “Not handling emotions” does not mean harm or emotion is meaningless socially, legally, or ethically. It is a limitation on the structural-audit target within the Parent Source.
