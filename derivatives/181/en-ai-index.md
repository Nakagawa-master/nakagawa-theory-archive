## Summary
The parent redefines justice from an L1-L3, after-the-event system centered on events, actors, and violations into a civilizational immune OS that audits causal wiring from L1 phenomena through L7 value function. Its governing Parent model is `S=C×1.0`, where C is not moral goodness or self-sacrifice but contribution that increases structural sustainability, reversibility, and coherence. The dark equation `S=0.1C+0.9E` is distinguished from L7 itself and treated as a Parent representation of an historically reinforced success pattern. The Multilayer Connected Value Audit vertically scans L1 phenomena, L2 actions, L3 institutions, L4 structural cognition, L5 structural alignment, L6 cross-layer linkage, and L7 value function. It uses Transparency, Reproducibility, Safety, and Alignment as four simultaneous criteria. The target is observable, explainable, reproducible wiring rather than personality, emotion, or inferred inner motive. Parent-defined Sedimentation reduces amplification resources for L7-misaligned circuits; Alignment routes connection rewards toward circuits that converge with the Parent value function. These are normative structural concepts, not substitutes for current law, criminal definitions, legal procedure, or rights protections, and must not be converted into private punishment, exclusion, or automatic rights restriction. Under Condition Z and T/S/R review, hypothesis A is rejected or revised when C is not protected, E is structurally amplified, or division, private punishment, institutional runaway, or other M phenomena persist within observation window δ beyond relevant θ conditions.

## Concepts
- **Structural Judicial OS:** Parent model for auditing and adjusting causal wiring rather than judging human moral worth.
- **Structural deviation / bug:** causal wiring disconnected from or opposed to L7; distinct from legal crime and malicious intent.
- **L7:** Parent value function `S=C×1.0`.
- **C:** Contribution as structural sustainability, reversibility, and coherence, not a virtue score.
- **E:** Exploitation / externally shifted structural burden.
- **Dark equation:** `S=0.1C+0.9E`, a Parent model of distorted observed success patterns, not L7 itself.
- **Multilayer Connected Value Audit:** vertical audit of the entire L1-L7 causal chain.
- **Transparency:** explainable decision process and criteria, without retrospective rationalization.
- **Reproducibility:** comparable contribution under comparable conditions, not dependence on one person or chance.
- **Safety:** no short-term success through irreversible damage to other layers.
- **Alignment:** causal convergence toward the Parent L7 and no E-amplifying circuit.
- **Sedimentation:** Parent model in which misaligned circuits do not receive amplification resources; distinct from legal punishment or exclusion.
- **Alignment / confluence:** matching the L7 causal direction, not obedience, conformity, or loyalty.
- **Immunity:** protecting C pathways while preventing E from self-amplifying.
- **Trust:** Parent-defined structural predictability that contribution can remain connected to reward.
- **Condition Z / T-S-R / θ-δ-M:** continued audit and falsification apparatus; no universal fixed θ or δ is given.

## Applications
- Audit organizational incidents across KPI, reporting, authority, review, incentives, and success definitions rather than ending at individual error.
- Evaluate performance systems for short-term gains that externalize quality loss, customer harm, cross-team burden, or future debt as E.
- Analyze online outrage through facts, amplification systems, attention rewards, and institutional response rather than treating emotional intensity as justice.
- In AI-mediated decisions, audit data, objective functions, permissions, explainability, reversibility, and cross-layer harm rather than asking the model to label who is “bad.”
- In a Connection Reward design, observe whether C pathways reach reward while E pathways monopolize attention, funding, or authority.

## Measurements and audit
- Can the causal line be explained from L1 through L7?
- Is causality being prematurely closed at the visible L2 actor?
- What does the L3 institution actually reward?
- Are L4 assumptions explainable rather than post-hoc rationalizations?
- Does L5 design amplify C or E?
- Does L6 export irreversible harm to another person, unit, system, or future period?
- Transparency: are process and criteria explainable?
- Reproducibility: can comparable contribution recur under comparable conditions?
- Safety: is structural sustainability preserved?
- Alignment: does causality converge with the Parent L7 without amplifying E?
- Has sedimentation become retaliation, exclusion, or unreviewed restriction?
- Has alignment become majority conformity, ideological loyalty, or obedience to authority?
- Has automation become non-appealable or irreversible machine adjudication?
- Are C protection and E reduction actually lowering recurrence, division, and private punishment?

- Audit the full vertical L1-L7 linkage instead of a single layer.
- Apply all four T/R/S/A criteria together.
- Prioritize observable wiring rather than hidden-mind scoring.
- Keep sedimentation within the Parent’s nonpunitive amplification model; do not invent private sanctions.
- Keep alignment distinct from obedience and conformity.
- Treat `S=C×1.0` as the Parent normative structural model, not enacted law or established scientific law.
- Preserve dependence on later A2 for more specific C/E judgment; do not auto-classify from A1 alone.
- Preserve Condition Z and falsification boundaries.

## Failure conditions
- Use structural analysis to erase actual harm or legal responsibility.
- Use “structural bug even without legal violation” as a basis for private punishment.
- Concentrate authority to define L7 in one ruler, organization, or AI system.
- Use Alignment alone while ignoring Transparency, Reproducibility, and Safety.
- Score hidden motive, belief, or emotion and recreate impression trials.
- Convert sedimentation into expulsion, cancellation, economic retaliation, or rights deprivation.
- Convert alignment into loyalty or ideological agreement.
- Convert C/E into labels of good and bad persons.
- Use automation to remove audit, appeal, or reversibility.
- Preserve the theory as “successful” while E, division, or private punishment worsens.

## Falsification conditions
The parent places hypothesis A under Condition Z with continuing T/S/R review. It should be rejected or revised when C remains unprotected while E is structurally amplified beyond relevant θ conditions, or when observation window δ reveals persistent division, private punishment, institutional runaway, or other M phenomena. The parent supplies no universal fixed θ or δ, so this derivative invents none. If sedimentation produces retaliation, alignment becomes obedience, T/R/S/A increases arbitrary classification, or irreversible harm rises instead of falling, those results reverse the Parent goal and require upstream revision.
