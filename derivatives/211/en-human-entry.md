## 15-Second Explanation
When consensus does not advance even though explanation, responsibility, and history are all in place, the problem may not be “insufficient understanding” but a landscape in which breaking or delaying is more profitable. This paper observes that incentive field, changes the arrangement of R (responsibility), H (history), and T (time) to make destruction and delay rationally disadvantageous again, and at the same time stops design abuse through a Reject Window.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
When progress does not occur despite sufficient explanation, continuing to add explanations will not reach the reason for stoppage if the problem lies in the gain gradient. If moving toward consensus increases the burden, while stopping allows one to recover gains such as avoiding responsibility, opacity, or buying time, an actor may rationally stop.

Therefore, before estimating a person’s sincerity or good and evil, it is necessary to observe high-S stagnation, external gain signals, and asymmetric action advantage at the same time, and confirm as a landscape which routes have become cheap and which have become expensive.

## Practical Process
1. Confirm U/R/H and S, and see whether the progress rate is approximately zero even though S is above a certain level.
2. Confirm external gain signals recovered through stagnation, destruction, or delay.
3. Confirm asymmetric action advantage in which only a particular action becomes one-sidedly favorable.
4. When all three points are present, describe the relative costs of the consensus route and the destruction/delay routes.
5. Reposition R and close routes through which responsibility disperses into “the atmosphere,” “everyone,” or “the situation.”
6. Reposition H and lower gains obtained by making grounds, differences, and points in time ambiguous.
7. Reposition T and prevent a state in which waiting allows escape. However, preserve normal deliberation.
8. After repositioning, confirm whether the expected gain from destruction and delay has fallen.
9. Monitor C (cost of consensus) and D (actual harm) together with S.
10. When an A/B/C or misuse signal fires, open a Reject Window, reject or reposition, and leave the difference in the history.

## Examples of Application
**Meetings and approvals.** If the same issue keeps returning even though the necessary materials and responsible person are in place, check not only for insufficient explanation but also for a structure in which stopping avoids burdens or responsibility becomes thinner as time passes.

**Contracts and negotiations.** If delay gives only one side better terms or responsibility avoidance, do not merely enforce a deadline; change the design so that grounds, responsibility nodes, and difference history are not lost over time.

**AI and automated judgment.** If burden is concentrated on a particular actor, thresholds become undisclosed, or inconvenient history is selectively erased, suspect abuse in the incentive design itself and open a Reject Window.

## Success Criteria
- High-S stagnation, external gain, and asymmetric advantage are confirmed as three points rather than as a single sign.
- After repositioning R/H/T, the expected gain from destruction and delay falls.
- The consensus route returns to the relatively lowest-cost route.
- S recovers or is maintained.
- C does not become excessive, and normal actors can continue deciding, recording, accepting, and deliberating.
- D does not amplify.
- θ/δ and change differences are auditable.
- When misuse signals or A/B/C fire, the system can actually move to rejection or revision.

## Limitations
This paper does not judge fairness, justice, sincerity, compromise, or the good and evil of people. It does not dismiss them; it separates them from the control variables of the incentive field. Therefore, this paper alone cannot determine which distribution is right.

When external variables untouched by R/H/T are dominant, or when gains from destruction remain after repositioning, the case is outside the current model or lacks variables. Do not keep strengthening control; move to rejection or revision.

## Preventing Misreading
This is not a method for disadvantaging opponents. Making only a particular actor worse off is itself a misuse signal. R must not be converted into naming or punishment, H into public shaming, or T into rushing.

Nor should success be judged by “S went up.” Excessive defensive action can also raise S temporarily, so C and D must be observed together. Incentive-field design is complete only when it includes a Reject Window that can stop the defense.
