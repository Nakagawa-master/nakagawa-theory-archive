## Summary

True-Cause Structural Theory is an ethics of containment for a hypothesized class of causal operations powerful enough to rearrange multi-layer causal sequences, phases, and weightings. Its public purpose is not to disclose a reproducible operational method. It asks what responsibility follows if a capability could alter causal arrangements at a scale where unrestricted reproducibility could itself become an attack surface for domination, information manipulation, or concentrated power.

The parent defines a true cause not as one isolated cause but as a bundle of initial conditions capable of decisively rearranging the overall configuration of a causal chain. The ethical objective is not domination or short-term gain. Causal operation is reframed toward reversible repair, minimization of ethical burden and future debt, and preservation of long-term temporal coherence. Minimal intervention means using as little intervention as possible while preserving as much reversibility and autonomous reselection by affected actors as possible.

Irreproducibility is therefore introduced as an exceptional defensive principle. It is not a rejection of reproducibility in science generally. It applies where complete reproducibility would distribute the capability to reproduce a dangerous control structure. The parent combines this with controlled disclosure: ethical principles, responsibility lines, audit structures, deviation detection, and correction processes remain public, while the operational core and procedural details needed for dangerous reproduction remain withheld. The existence and reason for withholding are themselves meant to be visible.

Controlled disclosure is connected to a temporary seal rather than permanent secrecy. The parent names origin-signature infrastructure, deviation-ledger infrastructure, and multi-origin auditing as examples of social responsibility capacity that could justify later reassessment of disclosure levels. If a complete form is said to exist, it is described as remaining under audit rather than being freely released. Logs, visible decisions, and multi-layer review are intended to prevent containment itself from becoming unaccountable monopoly power.

The integrated audit uses θ as a threshold symbol, δ as an observation-window symbol, and M as a phenomenon symbol in falsification logic, but does not define universal numeric values or formulas. The local audit also describes three defensive layers—irreproducibility, controlled disclosure, and temporary sealing—as a structural classification, not a maturity score. These quantitative-looking elements must not be converted into unsupported fixed values.

## Concepts
- True cause: bundle of initial conditions with decisive rearrangement effects on a whole causal configuration
- Causal operation: intervention at the level of ordering, phase, and weighting across causal layers
- Reversible repair: restoring damaged correspondence without treating control as the objective
- Minimal intervention: smallest intervention compatible with reversibility and autonomous reselection
- Irreproducibility: ethical defense against distribution of dangerous reproducible capability
- Structural immunity: detection, recording, and correction of attempts to reproduce or misuse restricted capability
- Controlled disclosure: public ethical and audit boundaries combined with restricted operational detail
- Ethical omission: deliberate absence of reproducing details for safety rather than concealment of responsibility
- Temporary seal: restricted disclosure pending maturation of social responsibility capacity
- Structural responsibility capacity: social infrastructure capable of origin tracking, deviation recording, and plural audit
- Proof of existence: separation between a claim that a capability exists and unrestricted operational access
- Audit-limited custody: keeping any complete form, if present, inside an accountable audit boundary
- Open seal: restricted core with publicly inspectable responsibility and audit perimeter
- Non-use responsibility: treating the decision not to exercise capability as an affirmative duty
- θ: threshold notation in falsification logic; no universal value defined
- δ: observation-window notation; no universal duration defined
- M: observed-phenomenon notation; no universal formula defined

## Applications

**1. High-impact research.** Publish hazard reasoning, ethical boundaries, correction logic, and auditability while restricting procedural detail that would sharply increase harmful reproducibility.

**2. High-privilege AI capabilities.** Evaluate not only capability but who can activate, stop, audit, contest, and review its use; power holders themselves remain auditable.

**3. Public coercive authority.** Keep the existence and limits of authority visible while preventing unrestricted copying, delegation, or operation and preserving stop and accountability mechanisms.

**4. Staged disclosure of dangerous knowledge.** Separate public principles and remediation information from details whose release would materially increase misuse reproducibility.

## Measurements and audit
- Dangerous reproducibility: whether public information alone enables repeated harmful operation
- Disclosure transparency: whether public/restricted boundaries and reasons are understandable
- Responsibility lines: whether activation, approval, stop, and audit authority are traceable
- Reversibility: whether affected actors retain repair and reselection pathways
- Future debt: whether short-term benefit transfers irreversible burden forward
- Deviation traceability: whether reproduction attempts and misuse are recorded and corrected
- Plural audit: whether containment decisions are controlled by more than one accountable point
- Disclosure review: whether maturing social responsibility actually triggers reassessment
- θ/δ/M: falsification notation with no universal fixed values; implementation requires explicit measurement basis

## Validity conditions
- Causal operation is oriented toward reversible repair rather than domination
- Minimal intervention and autonomous reselection are preserved
- Reproducibility itself materially increases serious misuse risk
- Disclosure boundaries and their reasons remain visible
- Ethical, responsibility, audit, and correction structures remain public
- Restricted operational power is itself subject to plural audit
- Future disclosure reassessment remains structurally possible

## Failure conditions
- Irreproducibility becomes a justification for authority preservation or monopoly secrecy
- Risk is asserted without explaining disclosure boundaries
- Operational power concentrates in one unaudited actor
- Ethics and audit lines are hidden together with operational detail
- A temporary seal becomes permanent without reassessment conditions
- Claims of a complete form are inflated into externally verified universal capability
- Controlled disclosure is generalized into a blanket justification for hiding inconvenient information

## Falsification conditions
- Full disclosure consistently improves auditability, public accountability, and repair without increasing harmful reproducibility
- Irreproducibility consistently increases monopoly power, secrecy, and audit failure more than it reduces risk
- Plural audit repeatedly fails to improve detection, stopping, or accountability compared with unilateral custody
- Social responsibility capacity matures but disclosure reassessment remains structurally impossible
- Concrete values assigned to θ, δ, or M fail to match the measurement object, scale, or observation logic needed for falsification

## Required distinctions
- True cause ≠ isolated cause
- Causal operation ≠ domination
- Irreproducibility ≠ generic secrecy
- Controlled disclosure ≠ deception
- Temporary seal ≠ permanent concealment
- Restricted access ≠ denial that a capability is claimed to exist
- Minimal intervention ≠ non-action
- Reversible repair ≠ gain maximization
- Audit-limited custody ≠ unilateral rule
- Non-use responsibility ≠ denial of capability

## Interpretation constraints

- Do not generalize controlled disclosure into a universal rule for withholding information
- Do not turn the three defensive layers into a quantitative maturity score
- Do not invent numeric values for θ, δ, or M
