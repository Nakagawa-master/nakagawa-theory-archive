## 15-Second Explanation
The “threshold of structural sensitivity” is the phenomenon in which even the most coherent structure is rejected when it is expected to disrupt present stability, roles, or relational benefits. Rejection is not treated as ignorance or malice, but as natural defense protecting current psychological equilibrium. Cognitive friction is reduced and an acceptance space designed through **immediate visualization of Correspondence, presenting the beat (Temporal Ethics) in advance, and presenting roles in separated units**.

## Why Can Coherent Structures Be Rejected?
Higher-order proposals reorganize causality, roles, allocation responsibility, and Temporal Ethics at once. Even a logically correct proposal feels threatening if the receiver expects “my role will disappear,” “the relationship’s balance will collapse,” or “my past decisions will be denied.”

Three representative reactions are:

- **Threatening:** the improvement proposal is received as “a denial of me.”
- **Inversion:** discussion leaves the structure and shifts to motive or personality—“Why say that?” or “Who are you?”
- **Contraction:** people avoid new roles or responsibility allocation and return to established practice.

These are also defenses protecting present stability, roles, and relational benefits. Pushing a more correct explanation can therefore increase friction.

## Three Design Principles
### 1. Immediate Visualization of Correspondence
Show first whose burden changes and how, which responsibility moves where, and what is returned on which beat. Before/After causal lines, an allocation-responsibility map, and a repayment schedule make correspondence of speech and substance visible.

### 2. Present the Beat (Temporal Ethics) in Advance
When structural change looks like short-term loss, showing when burden is verified, repaid, or rolled back can shift rejection from total refusal to conditional holding. Show short-, medium-, and long-term evaluation, rollback after failure, renegotiation after threshold excess, and the observation window before introduction. Without time design, only present burden appears certain.

### 3. Present Roles in Separated Units
Split a large rearrangement into a minimum movable part (Unit of Change) and test it locally. Show the unstable period, alternative provision during it, and the window for inquiry, verification, and revision. Acceptance blockage comes not only from “not understanding” but from “having nowhere to go,” so objections and anxiety need a structural place.

## The Original’s Presentation Sequence
1. **Correspondence of the pain point:** show current burden, contradiction, or wear.
2. **Rearrangement of causality:** show an improvement loop in the smallest unit.
3. **Beat contract:** establish cycle, indicators, re-agreement, and observation window.
4. **Allocation responsibility:** state access rights, approval routes, and objection channel.
5. **Reversibility:** document correction rights and rollback conditions.

This order creates an **acceptance space** that receives mental defenses through institutional lines; it is not for pushing past emotion.

## What Is Observed?
The original discloses observable principles, not its internal formula or thresholds.

### Behavioral Indicators
- Where questions concentrate: causality, allocation, or time.
- Whether alternatives can be generated rather than mere denial.
- Whether a small reversible experiment is tolerated.

### Vocabulary Indicators
- Whether evaluation words name “people” or “structure.”
- Whether tense is “this instant” or “a cycle.”
- Whether explanation, allocation, redo, and responsibility appear.

These are not for ranking individuals, but for estimating Correspondence depth and the route toward acceptance.

## Audit and Inverted Evaluation
A→B means: A defines the threshold of structural sensitivity and designs a presentation order premised on Correspondence, Temporal Ethics, reversibility, and allocation responsibility; B attenuates rejection, amplifies acceptance, cycles verification, and stabilizes operation.

Re-agreement condition Z is consistency among audit cycle, T/S/R, and public audit bundle. T is Correspondence, S structural sustainability, and R recursion. If M is confirmed—an indicator falls below or rises above `θ`, or `δ` closes—hypothesis A is subject to rejection or revision.

`θ`, `δ`, and M label no person. They evaluate whether presentation design attenuates rejection and enables acceptance and re-verification.

## Conclusion
Whether a structure passes through is determined not only by likes and dislikes but by **whether it can be designed**. Rejection can be attenuated and acceptance amplified by design. Structural-sensitivity design passes causal coherence through while protecting the dignity of feeling.

## Misreading Prevention
This is not the general advice “be considerate of feelings,” nor a theory turning structural sensitivity into a diagnosis, personality evaluation, or AI psychological assessment. Its center is the structural treatment of threatening, inversion, and contraction, and acceptance-space design through the three principles, presentation sequence, observation principles, T/S/R, `θ`, `δ`, and M.
