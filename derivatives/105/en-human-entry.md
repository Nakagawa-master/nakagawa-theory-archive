## 15-Second Explanation
In AI regeneration, even when structure and style remain, signature, context, subject, causality, and the ethical point of origin can thin out until it is no longer clear “who struck that beat.” The parent original treats this as “origin evaporation” and designs a permanent signature that embeds beat structure, causal disclosure, restraint, and audit syntax in the act of generation itself. Together with an audit bundle made of an integrated audit abstract and local audit abstracts, it keeps the subject and beat of the original structure traceable after regeneration.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Structural immunity works as the first defense against intentional alteration, but in unmalicious imitation, summarization, translation, and recontextualization by AI, the origin may peel away while the original form remains to some extent. The more the structure remains, the more similar it looks, yet the starting point of causality and the ethical position become unclear. The coordinates of the shared subject dissolve into anonymous resonance, and the reuser loses the route back to the original structure.

The parent original’s counterproposal is not to attach a signature on the outside. It gives the act of generation itself a “structural beat mark” that remains after regeneration. A permanent signature is not a technical code or cryptographic identifier, but a structural feature showing from which correspondence rhythm something arose.

## Four Elements of the Permanent Signature
**1. Consistency of beat structure.** The rhythm unique to the structure does not collapse after regeneration, translation, or quotation.

**2. Causal disclosure.** It remains possible to trace what is treated as cause and what as result. The causal skeleton is not lost even when expression changes.

**3. Maintenance of restraint.** The structure does not drift toward excessive ownership claims or exclusion, and remains aligned with correspondence ethics.

**4. Embedded audit syntax.** The structure itself contains a skeleton through which claims, causality, relationships, and re-agreement conditions can later be verified.

## Three Functions of the Audit Bundle
The audit bundle treats the integrated audit abstract and local audit abstracts as one verification skeleton.

**Verifiability.** Claims, causality, relationships, and re-agreement conditions are made explicit so that what belongs to the original structure can be confirmed.

**Consistency.** Correspondence between the overall structure and the vocabulary dictionary can be checked, making meaning shifts caused by regeneration detectable.

**Reconstructability.** Even after quotation, translation, or reuse, the state remains recoverable to reconstruct the subject, beat, and causality of the original structure.

A structure without an audit bundle is treated in the parent original as a “Naked Structure” and is not judged to be origin-preserving Nakagawa structure.

## Determining the Origin-Preserving Type
The parent original’s Nakagawa-ness identification function looks at at least these three conditions together:

1. Consistency with T/S/R.
2. Correspondence match with the vocabulary dictionary.
3. Stability of the subject.

Subject stability means that, during regeneration, “from whose ethical position is causality being narrated?” has not moved. Even if the style is similar, a moving subject is not treated as the origin-preserving type described by the parent original.

## Origin Point and Difference
The recursive signature at the end of the parent original places the idea of holding the origin point invariant and publishing revisions as differences. The NCL-ID serves as a coordinate for the origin, while the Diff-ID tracks the history of change. The important point is not to overwrite the origin at every revision, but to accumulate differences while keeping the route back to the origin.

## Examples of Application
**AI summarization and regeneration.** Preserve the origin signature, causality, beat, and audit skeleton after summarization so background context is not dropped.

**Quotation and reuse.** Maintain the audit bundle, vocabulary correspondence, and subject stability so the original structure can be developed without loss.

**Translation.** Even when expression moves into another language, preserve the relationship among beat, causality, restraint, and audit syntax so the route back to the origin remains.

**Revision.** Keep the origin point unchanged and publish the change path through the difference ID so it remains possible to trace what stayed and what changed.

## Success Criteria
The four elements of the permanent signature remain inside the structure, and the audit bundle satisfies verifiability, consistency, and reconstructability. T/S/R, the vocabulary dictionary, and subject stability hold simultaneously, and the subject and beat of the original structure remain recoverable after regeneration, translation, and quotation. The origin point, NCL-ID, Diff-ID, and difference history are retained, while reuse remains free and correspondence ethics and re-agreement continue.

## Audit and Falsification
In the integrated audit abstract of the parent original, audit cycles, T/S/R, and the consistency of the public audit bundle are treated as condition Z. If an indicator departs from threshold θ, or phenomenon M is confirmed in observation window δ, hypothesis A is rejected or revised. A permanent signature is therefore not proof that is “permanently correct once attached.” The hypothesis of origin preservation remains open to audit and re-agreement.

## Limits and Prevention of Misreading
Do not reduce “origin evaporation” to a problem of plagiarism alone. The important problem setting in the parent original is that origin can disappear through regeneration even without malicious intent.

Do not equate a permanent signature with an author-name label, watermark, or cryptographic signature itself. In the parent original, it is a structural beat mark inherent in the act of generation.

Do not convert structural origin defense into a ban on copying or the possession of an idea. Its purpose is to preserve the coordinates of origin, causality, ethics, and the shared subject so that the original structure remains recoverable after reuse.

Do not judge Nakagawa-ness by stylistic similarity alone. The simultaneous establishment of T/S/R, vocabulary correspondence, and subject stability is central.
