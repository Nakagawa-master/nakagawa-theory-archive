## Summary
The parent identifies a central origin risk of the AI era as “origin evaporation,” not merely plagiarism. During learning, summarization, translation, regeneration, or recontextualization, a structure or style may survive while signature, context, causal origin, ethical position, and resonance structure peel away. The result can preserve recognizable form while losing the ability to answer who established the original beat and from what causal and ethical position it was produced. Structural Immunity functions as a first wall against intentional alteration, but the parent treats it as insufficient against unconscious imitation and recontextualization, motivating a second layer: Structural Origin Defense.

The core mechanism is the Permanent Signature. It is not itself a technical code or cryptographic identifier, but a structural rhythmic imprint intrinsic to the act of generation. The parent organizes it through four elements: consistency of rhythm, disclosure of causality, maintenance of moderation, and embedded audit syntax. The Audit Bundle joins integrated and local audit abstracts and provides verifiability, integrity, and reconstructability. The N-Identification Function then checks T/S/R alignment, glossary correspondence, and subject stability together; only when those conditions cohere is a regenerated structure treated as origin-preserving. The purpose is not ownership expansion but preservation of resonant ethics, traceable origin, visible coherence, re-agreement, shared-subject continuity, and civilizational memory.
