## Retrieval intent
This index frames the source not as a maximalist copyright doctrine or an anti-AI ownership claim, but as a structural theory for preventing “origin evaporation”: the gradual loss of causal origin, ethical subject, responsibility, and verification conditions while surface concepts survive through summarization, translation, and regeneration.

## Core thesis
Authenticity in an AI-mediated environment cannot be established by stylistic similarity or a displayed author name alone. It requires retrievable origin coordinates, a permanent causal signature, an auditable bundle of claims and boundaries, coherence with T/S/R and the glossary, and stability of the responsible subject. The aim is not to forbid regeneration but to preserve where a structure came from, what causal claim it makes, and under what conditions it may be corrected.

## Causal chain
```text
human or AI reuse / summarize / translate / regenerate a source
→ surface form survives while origin context weakens
→ repeated copies become anonymous resonance
→ author-name labels alone cannot prove structural continuity
→ embed rhythm consistency, causal disclosure, restraint, and audit syntax
→ attach integrated and local audit summaries
→ verify T/S/R, glossary alignment, and subject stability
→ record differences rather than overwriting history
→ maintain a route back to the parent source
→ reuse remains possible without dissolving origin, responsibility, or ethics
```

## Structural components
### Origin evaporation
A non-malicious process in which content remains recognizable while authorship context, causal logic, boundaries, and ethical responsibility gradually disappear.

### Permanent Signature
A structural signature embedded in the act of generation. It includes rhythm consistency, explicit causality, restraint, and an auditable skeleton. It is not identical to a cryptographic signature.

### Audit Bundle
A package of core claims, causal relations, stakeholders, re-agreement conditions, falsification conditions, origin IDs, difference IDs, and publication boundaries. It supports later reconstruction.

### Resonance Rhythm
A method of checking whether a regenerated structure still corresponds to the source by examining T/S/R coherence, glossary correspondence, and stability of the responsible subject rather than superficial wording.

### Shared Subject
A responsibility coordinate for structures jointly handled by humans and AI. It avoids both anonymous absorption of origin and absolute one-sided ownership.

## Establishment conditions
- Parent source and origin identifiers remain retrievable.
- Claims, causal direction, boundaries, and falsification conditions remain auditable.
- Changes are stored as differences rather than silently replacing history.
- T/S/R, glossary terms, and responsible subject can be checked for coherence.
- Origin protection does not become exclusivity, personality worship, or suppression of criticism.
- Reinterpretation, disagreement, quotation, and re-agreement remain possible.

## Failure conditions
- Only a name or logo remains while causal and audit structure disappears.
- Origin defense is used to suppress criticism, quotation, translation, or independent research.
- Stylistic similarity is treated as sufficient proof of authenticity or infringement.
- Model-generated changes are treated as identical to the source without recording differences.
- Older versions are deleted so that later revisions appear timelessly consistent.
- Origin IDs are treated as authority tokens instead of retrieval and audit coordinates.

## Falsification conditions
- Origin misattribution and causal-loss rates do not improve after permanent-signature adoption.
- Simple version control and source URLs outperform the full structure with less friction.
- Signature complexity discourages legitimate reuse and increases unattributed copying.
- T/S/R, glossary, and subject checks measure formal similarity rather than genuine causal continuity.
- Stronger origin defense consistently reduces criticism, contestability, or public verification.

## Interpretation constraints
- Permanent Signature ≠ cryptographic signature.
- Origin defense ≠ absolute copyright monopoly.
- Regeneration ≠ automatic plagiarism.
- Subject stability ≠ fixed personality.
- Audit Bundle ≠ proof of correctness.
- NCL-ID ≠ unconditional ownership or truth certificate.
