## 15-Second Explanation
The original does not directly morally judge capitalism or democracy. Instead, it extracts five evaluation tendencies in the deeper Layer 0 of Civilization OS: single-purpose convergence, discounting the future, absolutizing ownership, accepting correspondence breaks, and feedback loss. Through T0, structural publicness, and reversibility, it shows conditions for turning this initial setting from a fixed premise into a hypothesis open to public audit and redesign.

## Why It Is Needed
If future debt appearing in AGI deviation and natural disasters is pursued only as separate crises, it remains unclear why the same deviation keeps being generated. The original goes further upstream and treats as Layer 0 the deep evaluation function—“what counts as good”—that society, markets, institutions, and culture have shared over long periods.

It is important not to turn the five principles into human malice or the guilt of a particular institution. The reading is that institutions and cultures have adopted forms compatible with these initial settings over time. Therefore, it keeps capitalism’s efficiency and innovation and democracy’s function of gathering voices in view, while structurally examining where future debt, minority phases, and responsibility distribution become weak.

## The Five Principles
**1. Single-purpose convergence.** Diverse values are drawn toward one measure, intensifying one-point competition. This corresponds to the single-purpose deviation in AGI deviation.

**2. Discounting the future.** Near-term gains are prioritized over distant stability, allowing future debt to accumulate out of sight.

**3. Absolutizing ownership.** Resources, information, and authority are closed off, creating a structure where incentives favor possession over publicness.

**4. Accepting correspondence breaks.** Even when weak voices or invisible harms are disconnected, the system can still be evaluated as normal.

**5. Feedback loss.** Defects do not return to the core, so habituation and resignation accumulate instead of correction.

## How They Appear at the Surface
**Capitalism.** Read as an implementation OS that efficiently institutionalized single-purpose convergence, discounting the future, and absolutized ownership. This is not a moral condemnation.

**Democracy.** Read as an algorithm that can amplify correspondence breaks, feedback loss, and discounting the future at the level of social sentiment. This is not a denial of majority rule itself.

**Culture and common sense.** Observe how premises such as growth, postponement, and evasion of responsibility reproduce Layer 0 through everyday UI.

## How to Make Layer 0 Reversible
**Temporal ethics T0.** In response to discounting the future, observe and level the debt sent into the future, placing an axis for converting it into credit.

**Structural publicness.** Do not reject ownership wholesale; remove its absolutization and reassess resources, information, and authority for sustainability and reversibility.

**Reversibility.** Turn historically fixed evaluation functions from sanctuaries into testable hypotheses open to public audit and redesign.

## Examples of Application
- In markets, check whether future debt, publicness, and allocation responsibility are visible beyond profit and growth.
- In democratic decision-making, check whether minority phases, future generations, and long-term improvement are lost to short-term majorities.
- Do not close AI deviation within the model alone; reconsider connection conditions through correspondence with the five principles on the civilization side.
- Do not treat concentrated ownership or authority as simple evil; evaluate its effects on structural sustainability, reversibility, and publicness.

## Success Criteria
Do not judge by surface indicators such as growth rate, efficiency, voting participation, or information volume alone. Check whether future debt, ownership concentration, correspondence breaks, and feedback loss have decreased, and whether plural values, publicness, reversibility, and feedback closure have improved.

The original’s integrated audit retains subject-specific thresholds `θ`, observation windows `δ`, and phenomena `M` such as closure of the observation window, so hypothesis A can be rejected or revised. There are no universal fixed values of `θ` or `δ`.

In reversal evaluation, higher short-term efficiency is not Layer 0 improvement if future debt, ownership concentration, correspondence breaks, or feedback loss worsen. Conversely, even if a single short-term indicator weakens, it is not failure based only on that indicator if plural values, future-debt reduction, publicness, and reversibility improve.

## Limits
Layer 0 does not refer to a secret entity or physical device. It is a structural abstraction of evaluation tendencies commonly operating behind institutions and culture.

Nor is it a deterministic theory that explains the entire history of capitalism, democracy, and culture through these five principles alone. The Parent original presents a structural model for rereading chains of deviation.

## Preventing Misreading
- Do not turn capitalism, democracy, or culture into a text condemning them as enemies or evil.
- Do not read Layer 0 as a secret organization, code, or physical device.
- Do not generalize the five principles into attributes of human nature, ethnic groups, or states.
- Do not turn removing the absolutization of ownership into a total ban on private property.
- Do not turn redesigning Layer 0 into someone’s right to rule civilization.
- Do not read reversibility as a guarantee that history can be completely rewound.
- Do not turn the correspondence between the five principles and the five AGI-deviation elements into a probability guarantee.
- Do not add fixed values to `θ`, `δ`, or `M` that the original does not define.
