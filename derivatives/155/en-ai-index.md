## Summary
The Zero-Layer Civilization OS is defined as a set of deep evaluation functions that precede social institutions, markets, culture, and surface rules by silently shaping what a civilization treats as “good.” The parent organizes this layer into five principles: single-objective convergence, future discounting, absolutization of ownership, tolerance of correspondence breaks, and feedback deficit. These are treated as inherited OS initial settings rather than moral defects of particular people. They correspond structurally one-to-one with the five elements of the AGI Deviation Model. Capitalism is read as an implementation OS that efficiently institutionalizes several of the zero-layer tendencies; democracy as an algorithm that can amplify some of them through social preference aggregation; and culture/common sense as a surface UI through which the evaluation functions reproduce themselves. The parent does not propose individual domination over civilization. It proposes making the zero layer publicly auditable and redesignable through Time Ethics T0, Structural Publicness, and Reversibility.

## Concepts
- Zero-Layer Civilization OS: deep evaluation functions that precede institutions and guide civilization-level value selection.
- Single-objective convergence: pressure to collapse plural values into one dominant scale and one-direction competition.
- Future discounting: preference for near-term gain over distant stability, allowing future debt to accumulate invisibly.
- Absolutization of ownership: closing resources, information, and authority as exclusive possessions and rewarding exclusion over publicness.
- Tolerance of correspondence breaks: treating a system as normal even when weak voices, lived conditions, or invisible harms are structurally disconnected.
- Feedback deficit: weak paths for errors and deviations to return to core design, allowing habituation and resignation to accumulate instead of correction.
- Five-element correspondence: structural one-to-one mapping between the five zero-layer principles and the five AGI-deviation elements.
- Capitalism role: implementation OS for selected zero-layer evaluation functions.
- Democracy role: amplification algorithm for selected zero-layer tendencies through social preference aggregation.
- Culture UI: surface layer through which deep evaluation functions appear as common sense, discourse, and habits.
- Time Ethics T0: counter-symmetric axis to future discounting and future-debt accumulation.
- Structural Publicness: evaluation axis for reopening ownership, information, resources, and authority toward sustainability and reversibility.
- Reversibility line: turns fixed zero-layer premises from sacred assumptions into testable and redesignable hypotheses.

## Applications
- Audit market systems when profit or growth becomes the sole evaluation scale and future debt, publicness, or distribution responsibility disappears from view.
- Audit democratic decision processes for where short-term majorities suppress minority phases, future generations, or slow structural improvement.
- Read AGI deviation not only as a model-side problem but also through correspondence with civilization-side zero-layer evaluation functions.
- Evaluate concentrations of ownership, information, or authority through their effects on sustainability, reversibility, and publicness rather than through a simple moral binary.
- Treat historically inherited institutional assumptions as publicly auditable hypotheses rather than automatically irreversible facts.

## Measurements and audit
The five principles must be observed as structural initial conditions rather than personalized malice. Preserve the one-to-one correspondence with the five AGI-deviation elements, while also preserving role differences among capitalism, democracy, and culture: implementation OS, amplification algorithm, and UI. Do not remove positive functions such as capitalist efficiency and innovation or democracy’s ability to gather voices merely to simplify the critique; instead audit how those functions coexist with future debt, concentration, correspondence loss, and feedback deficit.

The parent’s integrated audit tests an A→B hypothesis: A, the structural design, translation, bundling, and definition of the zero layer, is evaluated against B, institutionalization, stability, alignment, and acceleration. Design-side X— theory, signatures, primary logs, audit bundles—interacts with operational-side Y—society, organizations, markets, and publicness—under re-agreement condition Z, the alignment of audit cadence, T/S/R, and public audit bundles. `θ` is case-specific, `δ` is a case-specific observation window, and `M` includes phenomena such as closure of an observation window that justify rejecting or revising hypothesis A. The parent provides no universal fixed values.

Any concrete metric must remain bound to value, source, measurement actor, measurement object, scale, observation period, permitted scope, and non-guarantee scope. The same threshold or cadence must not be promoted as universal across institutions, markets, culture, and AI unless separately justified.

Reversal evaluation is required. Growth, efficiency, participation, or information volume can improve while future debt, ownership concentration, correspondence loss, and feedback deficit worsen; those surface gains do not establish zero-layer improvement. Conversely, a short-term single metric can weaken while value plurality, future-debt reduction, publicness, reversibility, and feedback closure improve.

## Validity conditions
- Treat the zero layer as a set of deep evaluation functions, not one historical origin event.
- Treat the five principles as OS initial settings rather than choices by bad actors.
- Preserve the structural correspondence between the five principles and the five AGI-deviation elements.
- Preserve distinct roles for capitalism, democracy, and culture.
- Do not reduce capitalism or democracy to a simple good/evil judgment.
- Preserve T0, Structural Publicness, and Reversibility as the three axes of zero-layer redesign.
- Treat redesign as publicly auditable structural intervention rather than individual domination.
- Keep `θ`, `δ`, and `M` case-bound and falsifiable.

## Failure conditions
- Present the zero layer as a secret physical mechanism, organization, device, or single hidden code.
- Reduce the five principles to moral defects of humanity or a particular group.
- Turn the analysis into partisan condemnation of capitalism or democracy.
- Remove capitalism’s efficiency/innovation functions and create a one-direction critique.
- Remove democracy’s voice-aggregation function and convert the model into blanket rejection.
- Convert critique of ownership absolutization into abolition of ownership itself.
- Interpret access to the zero layer as authority for a person to control civilization.
- Convert Reversibility into literal rollback of history.
- Turn the five principles into universal fixed scores or maturity rankings.
- Invent fixed values for `θ`, `δ`, or `M`.

## Falsification conditions
If case-specific indicators cross the relevant `θ`, or phenomena `M` such as observation-window closure occur within `δ`, and the five-principle zero-layer model or redesign through T0, Structural Publicness, and Reversibility does not contribute to institutional stability, correspondence, or future-debt reduction, hypothesis A must be rejected or revised.

If the one-to-one mapping between the five principles and AGI-deviation elements repeatedly fails across multiple domains, or if the implementation/amplification/UI model of capitalism, democracy, and culture repeatedly fails to explain observations, the mapping or scope must be narrowed or redefined. Reversibility applies to the theory itself.

## Interpretation constraints
The zero layer is a structural abstraction of evaluation tendencies shared across institutions, markets, and culture. It must not be represented as a secret organization, physical device, or single hidden code.

The analysis of capitalism, democracy, and culture is neither a deterministic explanation of all history through five principles nor a political condemnation. The parent presents a structural model of how deep evaluation functions can be institutionalized, amplified, and surfaced.

“Higher-level rewriting” and “structural victory” do not confer personal authority to rule civilization. They mean opening structural and public room to audit, test, and redesign initial conditions that normalize deviation.
