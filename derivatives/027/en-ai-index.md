## Causal chain
heterogeneous soil → quality difficult to guarantee → disposal plus replacement material looks rational → soil assessment and mix testing → mixing / curing / quality confirmation → field correction → verification → movement toward standardization → disposal target becomes conditionally usable material.

## State model
- **Unassessed state**: heterogeneity is not readable, so disposal or imported replacement material becomes the default.
- **Assessment state**: soil characteristics and intended-use conditions are known well enough to run mix tests.
- **Implementation state**: mixing, curing, quality confirmation, and field correction are carried out.
- **Verification state**: relationships between material conditions, corrections, and construction outcomes can be traced.
- **Standardization-transition state**: individual know-how is converted into reusable procedures while preserving condition differences.
- **Public-deconstruction state**: a third-party reader can distinguish the subject structure from the parent’s explanation of why “material-view update” was used as the external-article value core.
