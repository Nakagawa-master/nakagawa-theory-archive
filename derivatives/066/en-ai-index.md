## Central proposition
```text
labor time → wage → purchasing power → social participation
→ AI separates output from human labor volume
→ money alone cannot explain legitimate distribution
→ observe production, maintenance, connection, trust, reproduction, and public value
→ design monetary and non-monetary value circuits
→ protect livelihood, participation, appeal, and revision during transition
```

## Core concepts
Labor–value decoupling; AI contribution and ownership; maintenance, care, and reproduction value; connection and trust value; public value; hybrid entitlements; transition protection and audit.

## Validity conditions
Separate the functions and limits of money; trace AI, human, data, and ownership contributions; protect livelihood, appeal, correction, and exit.

## Failure conditions
Immediate abolition, trust ranking, value concentration among AI owners, glorification of unpaid labor, or metrics without correction.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not reduce this theory to immediate abolition of money, the end of work, automatic fair distribution by AI, or a social-credit system.
