## Central proposition
When AI substitutes for or amplifies direct human labor, output may rise while human work hours and wage opportunities do not. The single circuit of labor time → wage → purchasing power → livelihood and participation therefore loses explanatory power.

```text
human labor time as the main basis of value and income
→ AI separates output from human labor volume
→ productivity rises while wage access may contract
→ livelihood remains tied to labor-market participation
→ maintenance, care, connection, trust, reproduction, and public value stay invisible
→ design plural value circuits, entitlements, livelihood protection, appeal, and correction
→ retain money while reducing sole dependence on money
```

## Application domains
- Distribution of automation gains inside AI-enabled firms.
- Recognition and protection of care, education, maintenance, and community work.
- Attribution, reuse, and public spillovers in creative and research work.
- Public infrastructure, safety, environmental maintenance, and disaster response.
- Employment transition, shorter work time, retraining, and livelihood security.

## Measurement and audit
- Gap between AI-enabled output growth and human income growth.
- Distribution of automation gains among owners, workers, data contributors, users, and the public.
- Whether care, maintenance, connection, and public value lead to enforceable rights or support.
- Whether trust and connection records become ranking or surveillance systems.
- Effectiveness of appeal, correction, deletion, withdrawal, and exit.
- Changes in unpaid work, livelihood insecurity, and ownership concentration.

## Validity conditions
- Separate the functions and limits of money.
- Trace AI, human, data, equipment, ownership, and supervisory contributions.
- Do not treat care, maintenance, connection, or public value as naturally unpaid.
- Do not convert value records into human worth scores.
- Connect recognized value to livelihood, participation, use, or learning rights.
- Make allocation criteria and reasons explainable.
- Preserve appeal, correction, withdrawal, deletion, and exit.
- Protect law, taxation, social security, and safety during transition.

## Failure and falsification conditions
- Immediate abolition of money without replacement for exchange and settlement.
- Concentration of value and authority among AI or platform owners.
- Celebration of care and community work while leaving it unpaid.
- Reduction of connection, popularity, or trust to one score.
- Metrics that cannot be challenged or corrected.
- Increased exclusion and insecurity after plural circuits are introduced.
- Value records that never become real rights or distributions.
- Use of public value as an opaque justification for authority.

## Required distinctions
```text
limits of money ≠ immediate abolition of money
labor–value decoupling ≠ human labor becoming meaningless
recording non-monetary value ≠ ranking human worth
connection value ≠ contact count or popularity
trust value ≠ unverified belief
livelihood protection ≠ coercive dependency
AI contribution ≠ full value ownership by AI owners
public value ≠ whatever authorities declare to be good
```

## Interpretation constraints
Do not reduce the theory to immediate abolition of money, the end of work, automatic fair distribution by AI, basic-income-only solutions, social-credit scoring, glorification of unpaid labor, or the elimination of markets, taxation, law, and social security.
