## Structural summary
The source defines decision support not as accumulating more reasons to say yes, but as identifying and structurally resolving the remaining reasons not to choose. Five recurring blockers are price anxiety, execution anxiety, risk anxiety, competitive comparison, and unclear priority. A decision should emerge from completed understanding and comparability, not pressure. Constraints that cannot be resolved require a designed withdrawal boundary and a record for possible future reconnection.

## Causal chain
```text
proposal understood
→ remaining No factors detected
→ client articulates them
→ evidence, implementation, risk and comparison become reviewable
→ resolvable blockers are removed
→ structural constraints trigger withdrawal
→ self-owned decision or future reconnection
```

## Required distinctions
- eliminating No is not denying concerns
- decision support is not coercion
- withdrawal is not defeat
- client self-decision is not salesperson control
