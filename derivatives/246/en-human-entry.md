## 15-Second Explanation
This essay does not treat understanding as the same thing as “knowing” or “being able to explain.” It defines understanding as **grasping the conditions required for an object to be established, within the scope and level of detail necessary for the purpose**.

## Why It Is Needed
Even when we possess a large amount of information, we may not know which conditions change the result. Even when an explanation is fluent, we may become unable to judge when the conditions change. Even with long experience, we may judge incorrectly if we cannot distinguish the differences between past and present conditions.

Understanding is therefore considered not as information volume or explanatory ability, but as grasping establishment conditions. In addition, the causal scope being viewed and the level of detail at which that scope can be distinguished are treated as separate axes.

## Practical Process
1. Learn the name and outline of the object.
2. Distinguish the feeling of “I understand” from actually grasping the establishment conditions.
3. Find the conditions necessary for the object to be established.
4. Connect the premises, dependencies, order, and exceptions among the conditions.
5. Decide how far the causal scope must be viewed according to the purpose of the judgment.
6. Decide to what level of detail that scope must be distinguished.
7. Consider how establishment or non-establishment changes when a condition changes.
8. Leave conditions that are not understood as conditions that are not understood.
9. Use the understanding obtained for design, judgment, explanation, and verification.

## Examples of Application
Even if one can explain the purpose of a system, if one does not know who inputs what, under which conditions decisions are made, and what happens in exceptional cases, it is difficult to call that understanding usable in operation.

The same applies to AI. Even if it can summarize text naturally, that understanding is not necessarily usable for real-world judgment if necessary conditions or exceptions have been omitted. Smoothness of a summary and grasping establishment conditions are different things.

## Success Test
If you can list the conditions under which the object is established, explain the causality among the conditions, distinguish the scope and granularity necessary for the purpose of judgment, and consider the result of changing one condition, you are approaching understanding as defined here. It is also important not to treat an area you do not understand as understood.

## Limitations
The necessary scope and granularity of understanding are not fixed. The required level of detail differs between understanding used to grasp an outline and understanding used for design or safety confirmation. More detail is not always better; what matters is why that granularity is necessary for the judgment.

## Misreading Prevention
It is not appropriate to simplify this as “understanding means knowing things in detail.” Judging understanding only by information volume, explanatory ability, years of experience, or AI fluency also differs from this essay’s definition.
