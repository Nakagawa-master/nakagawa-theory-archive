## 15-Second Explanation
Syntactic operation is not a method of directly pushing the person or result in front of you to move it. It is a way of reweaving the boundary where the mind becomes stable, the placement of information and responsibility, the order of questions and silence, and the time design that returns from the future to the present, thereby creating a field in which causality flows with little friction.

## Difference from Phenomenal Operation
Phenomenal operation applies force to downstream phenomena: increase activity when sales are insufficient, or increase explanation and control when agreement is slow. It may work in the short term, but if structural contradictions remain, counterforces accumulate and lead to institutional fatigue, declining trust, and an inability to learn.

Before that, syntactic operation asks “Why are the same failures reproduced?” and returns to relationship placement, order, boundaries, and time design. Rather than changing people’s qualities, it designs a grammar in which mind, structure, and time operate stably.

## Three Integrating Principles
The first is **the connection between mind and structure**. The mind is understood as a circuit of self-reference and recursive evaluation, connected to roles, responsibilities, and information flows. The subjective core is not constantly exposed; on the objective side, standards, observation, and agreement are placed first.

The second is **the tuning of friction**. It looks at structural friction rather than lack of ability, and edits order, intervals, and placement. The order of evaluation criteria → options → free voting, the silence window after a question, and the proximity/opposition of issues are basic operations in the original.

The third is **recursive design of time structure**. It works backward from desirable terminal conditions and updates present standards and interpretations of the past. It does not react reflexively to short-term KPIs, but guarantees input to the core point directly connected to the KGI.

## Public Implementation Form
The original’s 20-minute protocol consists of two minutes for the meaning axis, five minutes for issue cards, five minutes for SQS, five minutes for a single-factor change, and three minutes for the freedom test. Twenty minutes and the individual time allocations are implementation specifications from the original, not a guarantee of success for every meeting.

On the outer shell, standards, observation, records, and responsibility boundaries are made public. In the inner shell, value axes and origin context are protected, and disclosure is limited to conditional point events. Only deliverables pass from the inner shell to the outer shell; intentions and motives are not kept public permanently.

## Core Point and Cycles
KPIs are not multiplied into many items but are brought together around one or two core points. An operating example guarantees input daily and evaluates output weekly. This is a design specific to the original; one or two points, daily, and weekly are not made into universal standards for every field.

## What Is Measured
The seven indicators are time to agreement, degree of self-running, re-reference rate, friction ratio, silence-compliance rate, limited-disclosure compliance rate, and recovery time. Compare A with B’, which changes only one factor, under the same conditions; confirm whether an effect in the same direction appears for three consecutive cycles, and also record reduced freedom and increased pressure to conform.

Short time to agreement, high self-running, and high silence-compliance are not automatically converted into high scores. Agreement becomes faster if dissent is suppressed, and compliance rises if silence is forced. Numbers are windows for learning, not scales for scoring a person’s value.

## Six Failure Types
The six types are simultaneous multi-factor input, advertising the subjective, dissolution of boundaries, explanatory universalism, excessive KPIs, and failure to carry out the silence window. They share the failures of making it impossible to know what worked, subordinating the subjective to external evaluation, making responsibility ambiguous, increasing explanation instead of observation, burying the core point, and destroying the formation of depth.

## Falsification
If the main indicators do not change after changing to B’, there is no difference after returning to the original, the direction does not stabilize across three cycles, the effect disappears when the person in charge is replaced, or freedom declines and pressure to conform worsens, revise the syntactic hypothesis. Do not escape to another indicator to protect a success story.

## Preventing Misreading
Syntactic operation is not invisible steering. Its purpose is to expand freedom, and it includes a design that avoids closing off choices. Preserving the subjective is not the elimination of accountability; it is a separation of roles that strengthens the objective outer shell. “Weaving the future” does not mean prediction or a method that guarantees victory.
