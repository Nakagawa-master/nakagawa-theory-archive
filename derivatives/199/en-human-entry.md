## 15-Second Explanation
Real Estate Market OS Vol.2 addresses “tension equilibrium,” which keeps a market operating without breaking it by treating friction as a premise rather than eliminating friction through transparency. It places the owner’s Fear as the brake; the Greed of investors, businesses, and prospective buyers as the driving force; and public Justice as the reason for existence and the boundary, all at the same time. It does not erase backlash or complaints as failures; it reads them as signals of design misalignment and returns them to the cycle through Exit and the Ethical Shield.

## Why It Is Needed
Transparency in real estate is not merely an act of showing prices. Because it makes visible declining asset value, repair, tax, and inheritance burdens, past choices, and future anxiety, defensive reactions arise in owners. At the same time, the more visible demand and acquisition conditions become, the more the profit motives of investors, businesses, and prospective buyers also come to the surface.

By themselves, these two forces turn the market into a collision between pressure and profit-seeking. Therefore, the original places public purposes such as disaster prevention, land conservation, and regional sustainability above them as Justice, giving legitimacy to why transparency is used and how far it is permitted. Friction still does not disappear, so hate and complaints are treated as observational signals for correcting the design.

## Practical Process
1. **Anticipate the Fear transparency creates.** Abandon the premise that showing numbers will produce agreement.
2. **Place an Exit alongside Fear.** Lay out renting, renovation, consultation/deferral, and rational holding, not only selling.
3. **Do not hide Greed.** Make interests such as wanting to buy, wanting to acquire cheaply, and wanting yield visible as market fuel.
4. **Do not leave Greed uncontrolled.** Establish conditions, access rules, and accountability.
5. **Explicitly state Justice as the higher purpose.** Explain why transparency is socially acceptable through disaster prevention, land conservation, regional sustainability, and similar purposes.
6. **Observe hate without suppressing it.** From where anger, rejection, or complaints arise, inspect boundary violations, insufficient explanation, and insufficient exits.
7. **Implement the Ethical Shield.** Distinguish unjustified attacks, malicious intrusion, and legitimate criticism, and send legitimate questions into the dialogue circuit.
8. **Avoid partial implementation.** Operate Fear / Greed / Justice / Ethical Shield as one system.
9. **Do not fix the equilibrium point.** Continuously adjust it in response to public opinion, disasters, economic conditions, and institutional change.
10. **Protect the responsibility boundary between the private sector and government.** The private sector handles voluntary participation and consensus-building; it does not privately possess state coercive power.

## Use Cases
**Explaining to owners.** Do not show only future losses and press them to “sell immediately.” Show selling, renting, renovation, consultation/deferral, and rational holding at the same time, treating numbers as conditions rather than correct answers.

**Investors and businesses.** Do not hide the pursuit of profit itself; state desired prices, yields, conditions, and risks. Do not drive desire underground; place it under accountability.

**Complaints.** Do not treat “Why publish information this extensive?” as mere crisis response. Inspect where the misalignment lies: publication scope, access, explanation, exit, or public purpose.

**Connection to government.** Separate areas requiring state guarantees or coercive power—identity verification, registration, taxation, public safety, and so on—as connection points to the legal system, rather than absorbing them as the authority of a private service.

## Success Criteria
Success is not “zero backlash.” It is a state in which Fear, Greed, and Justice coexist; the market does not break even when backlash occurs; correction points can be obtained from complaints; legitimate criticism goes to dialogue; malicious intrusion is controlled at the boundary; and participants retain an Exit.

In addition, the equilibrium point must not be fixed but continuously adjusted to change, and the voluntary-participation domain of the private sector must not become mixed with the coercive-power domain of government and legal institutions.

## Limits
- Treating hate as an observational signal does not mean permitting abuse, harassment, discrimination, threats, or violence.
- Using Greed as a driving force does not excuse lowballing or exploitation.
- Declaring Justice does not permit unlimited access to personal information.
- The Ethical Shield is not a wall that blocks legitimate criticism.
- Tension equilibrium is not a fixed mathematical solution or an empirically established natural law, but a market-structure model in the original.
- The original does not present universal fixed θ values or δ periods.

## Preventing Misreading
Do not turn the statement “friction is evidence that the system is alive” into a recommendation to intentionally increase conflict or outrage. The original designs for friction to be observed, controlled, and routed into correction without erasing it.

Also, do not turn placing publicness as Justice into a claim that invoking a public purpose allows one to ignore an individual’s choices, assets, or privacy. The original instead requires access boundaries, accountability, Exit, and separation of authority between the private sector and government.
