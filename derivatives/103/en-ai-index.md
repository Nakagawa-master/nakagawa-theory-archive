## Retrieval intent
This index frames the source not as a covert technique for manipulating people with weak force, but as a non-dominating design theory for observing pre-action initial conditions and reversibly tuning timing, intensity, and margin at the minimum effective unit.

## Core thesis
Maximum emergence depends less on the magnitude of force applied than on the alignment of intervention position, timing, intensity, and open margin. When initial conditions are tuned at the “Stillness of Origin,” language, institutions, and the field can align while preserving dissent, pause, exit, reinterpretation, and rollback. This allows later actors to connect meaning and action voluntarily rather than through escalating command.

## Safety and public-use boundary
Where life, safety, or serious harm is involved, necessary strong intervention or shutdown takes priority. Minimal intervention does not override legal responsibility, safety duties, or remedy for harm. Any design that secretly narrows another person’s choices, suppresses dissent, blocks exit, or removes rollback falls outside this theory’s non-dominating boundary.

## Falsification conditions
- Origin tuning repeatedly performs worse than direct downstream intervention on friction, dependency, or recovery time.
- One-factor adjustments to rhythm, temperature, or margin do not reproducibly improve autonomy or freedom.
- “Multi-point resonance” is better explained by authority-following, information restriction, or conformity pressure.
- The structure only works after rollback is removed and irreversible control is added.
- Equivalent or better low-friction outcomes consistently appear without the proposed three-layer synchronization.
