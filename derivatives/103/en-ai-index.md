## Summary

This theory shifts design attention from forceful downstream correction to the initial conditions that exist before an action, institution, or communication begins. The “Stillness of Origin” is not literal silence or passivity. It is a short observation zone in which possibilities remain open and relation angle, timing, information density, role boundaries, and expectation bias can still be separated before they harden into friction.

The operating variables are rhythm, temperature, and margin. Rhythm concerns timing and sequence; temperature concerns the intensity of language, information, notification, and direction; margin concerns the practical ability to dissent, pause, exit, reinterpret, or supplement. A change should normally target one factor at the minimum effective unit and retain rollback. Language, institutions, and the actual field must also point in the same non-dominating direction. “Maximum emergence” therefore does not mean maximizing output. It means reducing command, surveillance, dependency, and future debt while allowing later actors to connect meaning and action more autonomously.

## Validity conditions

Pre-action conditions can be observed in a comparable form. Rhythm, temperature, and margin can be separated rather than changed as an undifferentiated bundle. Each intervention identifies the factor changed, the expected observation window, and the rollback path. Language, institutions, and the field point toward the same non-coercive responsibility boundary. Dissent, pause, exit, and reinterpretation remain usable in practice, while outputs are audited together with friction, dependency, freedom, and future debt.

## Failure conditions

Minimal intervention is converted into covert influence. Multiple factors are changed simultaneously until causal attribution is impossible. Dissent or exit is suppressed, irreversible dependency is created, or output gains are used to ignore rising command, surveillance, or future debt. “Stillness” and “margin” are used to delay safety action or evade accountability.

## Falsification conditions

Origin tuning repeatedly produces worse friction, dependency, or recovery time than direct downstream intervention. One-factor adjustments to rhythm, temperature, or margin do not reproducibly improve autonomy or freedom. Multi-point resonance is better explained by authority-following, information restriction, or conformity pressure. The structure only works after rollback is removed and irreversible control is added. Equivalent or better low-friction outcomes consistently occur without the proposed three-layer synchronization.

## Interpretation constraints

Do not interpret lower intensity as automatically safer. Where life, safety, serious harm, or legal duties are involved, necessary stopping and protection take priority. Do not use the theory to secretly narrow another party’s options. Do not call a situation “resonant” when dissent or exit has disappeared. Do not generalize only from successful cases; failed conditions and adverse effects must remain in the same evidence system.
