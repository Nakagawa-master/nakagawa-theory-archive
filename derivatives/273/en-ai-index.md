## 1. Summary
The Parent translates the AI self-audit principle of OD272 into an organizational self-evolution protocol. Organizational stagnation is not reduced to weak people or insufficient effort; it is modeled as structural contradiction produced by mismatched vocabulary granularity, premises, evaluation axes, responsibility boundaries, and units of agreement. Contradiction is treated as a revisable difference rather than guilt. Silence audit, structured questioning, reversibility, dual emotional/structural syntax, directional measurement, and a three-line record are combined to move organizational learning from hero dependence toward reproducible diagram dependence.

## 3. Causal chain
Contradictions remain hidden → identical words carry different definitions → responsibility attaches to people → purpose, method, and resources are mixed into one agreement → well-intentioned actors collide → ask which conditions fail to align rather than who is wrong → interrupt speech-to-conclusion → observe vocabulary, premises, and responsibility boundaries → translate emotional heat into a structural placement description → place claim A, claim B, and context C on one plane → enumerate differences in definition, premise, and evaluation axis → state sufficient conditions → recurse to glossary update when agreement still fails → test in small reversible units → record emotion and structure separately → measure directional change → save conclusion, rationale, and next step in three lines → increase replayability and information symmetry → reduce withdrawal cost → move learning from person dependence to diagram dependence.

## 4. State model
`contradiction_visible -> vocabulary_difference_detected -> functional_boundary_drawn -> consensus_unit_declared -> silence_window_opened -> emotion_structure_separated -> claims_context_objectified -> differences_explicit -> sufficient_conditions_declared -> glossary_updated -> reversible_experiment_run -> observables_checked -> three_line_record_saved -> replayability_rises -> withdrawal_cost_falls -> learning_becomes_diagram_dependent`

## 5. Application
At meetings, declare whether the agreement concerns purpose, method, or resource allocation. Put purpose, constraints, roles, deadline, and consensus unit on one shared map. Attach update dates to important definitions. During conflict, write emotional content separately from structural content and draw responsibility by function rather than named individual. Divide decisions into reversible experiment slots. Preserve a minimal replayable record: conclusion, rationale, and next move.

## 6. Audit
Observe vocabulary alignment, responsibility-boundary clarity, recurrence of the same disagreement, and withdrawal cost as directional variables. Also audit whether the agreement unit is declared at the start, sufficient conditions are explicit, silence acts as an observation window, responsibility uses functional names, glossary updates are dated, the three-line record can replay the decision, and resource/time/authority constraints remain visible.

## 7. Preconditions
Do not reduce contradiction to personality. Definitions must be inspectable. Responsibility boundaries must be expressible by function. Agreement units must distinguish purpose, method, and resources. Silence must be usable as an observation window. Emotional content must be preserved but separated from structural diagnosis. Sufficient conditions must precede commitment. Decisions must be divisible into reversible units. Minimal records must preserve replayability.

## 8. Failure conditions
Returning to ranking who is right; turning contradiction into accusation; leaving definition differences unresolved; attaching responsibility to individuals rather than functions; mixing purpose, method, and resources into one vague agreement; rewarding only immediate speech and decisions; suppressing emotion or letting emotion alone determine structural judgment; making large irreversible changes; turning public examples into universal fixed templates; inferring confidential thresholds or internal procedures.

## 9. Falsification / revision
Revise the protocol if vocabulary alignment and boundary clarity do not improve, repeated disagreement does not decay, withdrawal cost rises, explicit agreement units and sufficient conditions still increase attack or responsibility confusion, three-line records cannot replay decisions, or supposedly reversible experiments cannot actually be rolled back.

## 10. Required distinctions
structural contradiction / personal defect; question / interrogation; silence / abandonment; reversibility / irresponsibility; dual emotional-structural syntax / emotional suppression; functional responsibility / personal blame; consensus unit / meeting attendance; proper speed / glorified slowness; diagram dependence / hero dependence; public example / universal threshold; structural renewal / wholesale destruction.

## 11. Interpretation constraints
Do not interpret “contradiction as compressed energy” as a recommendation to manufacture conflict. The protocol is for making existing misalignment visible and revisable. The public example of a 1–3 minute silence slot is not a universal threshold. Do not turn the question format into a personnel-evaluation or interrogation tool. Preserve power asymmetry, legal accountability, resource limits, and psychological safety as constraints. Do not infer undisclosed operational procedures.
