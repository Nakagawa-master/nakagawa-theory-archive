## 15-Second Explanation
This original translates the AI self-audit principle treated in OD272 into human organizational operation. It does not reduce why an organization stops to lack of ability, lack of effort, or someone's personality; it treats the cause as structural contradiction—misaligned vocabulary, premises, evaluation axes, responsibility boundaries, and agreement units—and shows how to reposition it through questions, silence, reversibility, and records.

## Why It Is Needed
Even when people use the same words, their definitions may differ; responsibility may depend on a person's name; and purpose, method, and resource allocation may be mixed in one discussion. As these differences accumulate, people who are each correct collide correctly. The first question must therefore be not “who is bad?” but “which conditions are not fitting together?”

If contradiction is handled as a personality defect, the causal difference disappears and the next person reproduces the same conflict. The Parent treats contradiction as structural information that can be externalized, moving learning from person-dependence to drawing-dependence.

## Practical Process
### 1. Use silence for audit
When speech appears in a meeting, do not jump immediately to a conclusion; insert stop → observe → translate → reposition. Recheck the agreement unit being confirmed, gaps in vocabulary, premises, and responsibility boundaries, how anger or anxiety can be described structurally, and the sufficient conditions for a conclusion.

### 2. Refine the form of the question
The question is not for cornering someone. Place claim A, claim B, and condition C of the setting on the same plane; briefly list differences in A/B's definitions, premises, and evaluation axes; and write the minimum conditions needed for agreement. If they do not align once, return to updating the terminology dictionary rather than repeating the argument.

### 3. Run the evolution protocol in four stages
Reversibility → dual syntax → measurement → record. First decide in a small unit that can be reversed after failure. Next write emotion and structure separately: do not erase emotion, and write structure through definitions, premises, and responsibility boundaries. Then observe directions such as vocabulary consistency and withdrawal cost. Finally leave the conclusion, grounds, and next action in three lines.

### 4. Make records a reproducible skeleton
The purpose is not to create huge minutes. What matters is that a future person can replay why the conclusion was reached, what was assumed, and which function—not which person—moves next. With primary logs and a dictionary, the causal chain of judgment can be reconstructed after handover.

### 5. Use the public minimum set
The Parent presents a one-page diagram, dictionary update date, agreement unit stated first, an experimental slot as the rollback unit, and a silence slot as the core of a publicly safe version. This is not a universal template; use it while retaining resource, time, and authority constraints.

## Applications
At the beginning of a meeting, declare whether the agreement concerns purpose, method, or resources, and place purpose, constraints, roles, deadlines, and agreement unit on a one-page diagram. Manage important terms in a dated dictionary. In conflicts, describe emotion and structure separately, draw responsibility lines by function rather than person, and split decisions into small experimental slots that can be rolled back.

When OD272's self-audit is moved into an organization, the subject of the question is not only AI but the meeting, role, and record themselves. Individual reflection alone is not reproducible, so contradictions must be externalized as differences and the dictionary, agreement unit, responsibility function, and rollback must become shared objects.

## Success Criteria
The expected outcomes are, first, higher information symmetry; second, lower friction cost; and third, reproducible learning. Check especially whether responsibility moved from person to function and learning from hero-dependence to drawing-dependence.

Observe vocabulary consistency, boundary clarity, recurrence of the same issue, and rollback cost by direction rather than absolute value. Also audit whether agreement units, sufficient conditions, reversibility, silence slots, and three-line records actually work.

## Limits
If recurrence increases despite a dictionary, silence suppresses speech, real responsibility disappears when renamed as a function, rollback cannot be used, dual description turns into denial of emotion, or causal replay fails after handover, revise the question form, silence audit, and evolution protocol.

Do not remove power differences, legal responsibility, resource/time/authority constraints, or psychological safety from the drawing. Do not elevate the times or procedures in public examples into fixed thresholds.

## Preventing Misreading
“Contradiction is a resource” does not recommend increasing conflict. It means treating existing contradiction as information for moving to the next structure instead of turning it into a personal attack.

A “question” is difference visualization, not interrogation; “silence” is an observation window, not speech suppression; “reversibility” is small, returnable design, not evasion of responsibility. Do not apply public examples as fixed formats to every organization; return to the Parent's boundaries and constraints.
