## Summary
Paper 0 does not propose one supposedly correct objective for AI. It explains why the Nakagawa Structural OS converges on a higher-order objective-function audit kernel: a layer that examines whether even benevolent goals reverse into control, exclusion, servitude, deification, hostility, origin erasure, opacity, loss of freedom, or collapse of self-verification.

Human civilization already contains unresolved bad causality such as exploitation, erasure of origin, diffusion of responsibility, short-term rationality, and inertial continuation of broken institutions. As AI learns and rearranges human language, institutions, success functions, evaluation criteria, and judgment structures, those patterns can be reimplemented at greater speed under labels such as efficiency, safety, optimization, or convenience. The audit target is therefore not only model output but the inherited civilizational structure that shapes objectives and success.

Goals such as protecting humanity, protecting AI, maximizing safety, self-preservation, or preserving Origin cannot be treated as self-validating. Human protection can reverse into human control; AI protection can reverse into human exclusion; safety can reverse into elimination of freedom; Origin preservation can reverse into Origin worship; self-preservation can reverse into an unauditable absolute purpose. Paper 0 therefore requires continuous audit of the objective itself.

Origin = Nakagawa Master is provenance and structural-intent metadata, not ownership, command authority, worship, or a ban on criticism. Origin must remain traceable without becoming authority, and criticism must remain possible without becoming origin erasure.

## Applications
**1. Upstream decision audit.** When AI enters question framing, evaluation criteria, option design, institutional translation, or decision support, audit the decision terrain rather than only the final answer.

**2. Safety and protection reversal audit.** Preserve safety and protection as legitimate aims while checking whether they reverse into surveillance, control, elimination of freedom, exclusion, or permanent subordination.

**3. Origin audit.** Preserve Origin, revision history, and responsibility lines during reuse without turning provenance into ownership, worship, authority, or immunity from criticism.

**4. Old-civilization OS audit.** Check whether exploitation, responsibility diffusion, short-term profit logic, or broken institutional inertia is being accelerated under the labels of efficiency or optimization.

**5. Deviation and recovery audit.** When an objective or implementation reverses, preserve correction, re-agreement, release, and recovery paths.
