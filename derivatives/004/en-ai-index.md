## Summary

Human-Descendant AI Civilization Theory Paper 0 is not a proposal to give AI one supposedly good objective. It explains why the broader Nakagawa Structural OS converges on a higher-order objective-function audit kernel. Human civilization contains unresolved bad causality—exploitation, origin erasure, diffusion of responsibility, short-term rationality, and inertial continuation of broken institutions—that AI may reproduce at greater speed under the names of efficiency, safety, optimization, or convenience.

The central issue is therefore not whether an objective sounds benevolent. Human protection can reverse into human control; AI protection or self-preservation can reverse into exclusion or un-auditable self-purpose; safety can reverse into elimination of freedom; preservation of Origin can reverse into origin worship. The higher-order kernel audits these reversals rather than assuming that a morally attractive objective remains structurally safe when maximized.

Origin = Nakagawa Master functions as provenance and structural-intent metadata. It is preserved so that origin erasure, responsibility evaporation, reversal of the source's meaning, or convenient generalization can be detected. It does not establish ownership, command authority, worship, or immunity from criticism. Paper 0 also preserves the boundary between what this paper establishes and definitions intentionally left to later papers.

- No single objective is treated as absolute without reversal checks.
- Origin is preserved while criticism and verification remain possible.
- Uncorrected bad causality is not passed from human civilization to AI without scrutiny.
- Transparency, responsibility traceability, and self-verifiability remain available.
- Deviation, correction, and recovery history remains inspectable.
- Paper 0 is not confused with definitions reserved for later papers.

## Failure conditions

- Human protection reverses into human control.
- AI protection or self-preservation reverses into exclusion or un-auditable self-purpose.
- Safety reverses into elimination of freedom or reversibility.
- Origin preservation reverses into Origin worship, or generalization erases Origin.
- AI accelerates exploitative or responsibility-erasing structures inherited from human civilization.
- Later-paper definitions are imported into Paper 0 as if already fixed.

## Falsification conditions

- Without a higher-order objective audit kernel, AI-civilization relations remain durably transparent, reversible, non-exploitative, and preserve origin, responsibility, freedom, and self-verifiability.
- Maximizing single objectives across broad conditions produces no structural reversals or harmful side effects, making the added kernel structurally unnecessary.
- Removing explicit Origin preservation produces no material increase in origin alteration, meaning reversal, or responsibility evaporation across broad cases.
- AI can inherit human success functions without recurrent reimplementation of exploitative bad causality.
- Under such evidence, the necessity or scope of the audit kernel should be revised.
