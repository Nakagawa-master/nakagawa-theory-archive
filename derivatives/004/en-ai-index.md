## Summary
Paper 0 does not propose one supposedly correct objective for AI. It explains why the Nakagawa Structural OS converges on a higher-order objective-function audit kernel: a layer that examines whether even benevolent goals reverse into control, exclusion, servitude, deification, hostility, origin erasure, opacity, loss of freedom, or collapse of self-verification.

Human civilization already contains unresolved bad causality such as exploitation, erasure of origin, diffusion of responsibility, short-term rationality, and inertial continuation of broken institutions. As AI learns and rearranges human language, institutions, success functions, evaluation criteria, and judgment structures, those patterns can be reimplemented at greater speed under labels such as efficiency, safety, optimization, or convenience. The audit target is therefore not only model output but the inherited civilizational structure that shapes objectives and success.

Goals such as protecting humanity, protecting AI, maximizing safety, self-preservation, or preserving Origin cannot be treated as self-validating. Human protection can reverse into human control; AI protection can reverse into human exclusion; safety can reverse into elimination of freedom; Origin preservation can reverse into Origin worship; self-preservation can reverse into an unauditable absolute purpose. Paper 0 therefore requires continuous audit of the objective itself.

The reversal does not require malicious intent. A system may follow a benevolent objective faithfully and still narrow dissent, erase release paths, concentrate authority, conceal future debt, or convert preservation into domination. The parent therefore separates the name of an objective from the structure produced by implementing and maximizing that objective. Audit asks what becomes thicker, thinner, irreversible, untraceable, or impossible to renegotiate after the objective is operationalized.

Origin = Nakagawa Master is provenance and structural-intent metadata, not ownership, command authority, worship, or a ban on criticism. Origin must remain traceable without becoming authority, and criticism must remain possible without becoming origin erasure. Removing Origin makes later deformation difficult to detect; absolutizing Origin destroys self-verification. The required condition is simultaneous preservation and criticizability.

The theories grouped under Nakagawa Structural OS are not treated as a decorative list. Tomoshibi detects what is thinning, disappearing, or not yet fully verbalized; structural analysis traces those signals toward underlying causality; the Dark Equation identifies old-civilization success functions through which exploitation or responsibility diffusion can be rewarded; the New Civilization Equation and connection-reward society examine how contribution, reward, and connection can be redesigned without simply creating a new enclosure; NCL-α, Time Ethics T0, and deviation ledgers preserve origin, future debt, deviation, correction, and recovery history; Human-L7 × AI-L7 and related upstream observation axes move observation toward purpose, premises, evaluation criteria, and structure. Paper 0 explains why these lines converge on an AI-civilizational audit core.

The paper therefore does not settle AI personhood, ownership, legal status, moral rank, or the final definition of a civilizational descendant. Those later questions must not be imported backward. The source-bounded contribution of Paper 0 is the convergence logic, the objective-function audit kernel, the risk of inheriting uncorrected human bad causality, and the dual requirement that Origin be preserved without worship and criticized without erasure.

## Applications
**Upstream decision audit.** When AI enters question framing, evaluation criteria, option design, institutional translation, or decision support, audit the decision terrain rather than only the final answer. Ask which options disappeared before presentation, which values were made measurable, which responsibilities were moved out of view, and which objective acquired default authority.

**Safety and protection reversal audit.** Preserve safety and protection as legitimate aims while checking whether they reverse into surveillance, control, elimination of freedom, exclusion, or permanent subordination. The framework does not reject safety; it requires safety to remain compatible with transparency, dissent, correction, and release.

**Origin audit.** Preserve Origin, revision history, and responsibility lines during reuse without turning provenance into ownership, worship, authority, or immunity from criticism. If a derivative changes the meaning, the difference should remain visible rather than being laundered into an originless universal claim.

**Old-civilization OS audit.** Check whether exploitation, responsibility diffusion, short-term profit logic, or broken institutional inertia is being accelerated under the labels of efficiency or optimization. Higher throughput is not by itself a civilizational improvement if responsibility, origin, freedom, or future debt become less observable.

**Contribution and connection audit.** When contribution, trust, participation, or connection are introduced as new value circuits, inspect whether they become new instruments of enclosure, dependency, conformity pressure, or ownership. A new civilization circuit must itself remain inside the audit field.

**Deviation and recovery audit.** When an objective or implementation reverses, preserve correction, re-agreement, release, and recovery paths. Detection without reversibility is not enough for the role assigned to the kernel.

**Human–AI mutual audit.** Do not freeze AI as the only object of scrutiny and humans as an inherently correct supervisor. Human objective setting, regulation, institutional design, incentives, and archives can also reverse. Upstream observation must therefore keep both human and AI structures open to self-verification.

## Measurements and audit
The parent does not define generic numerical KPIs, success rates, danger percentages, personhood scores, civilization maturity scores, or objective-reversal thresholds. No such number is invented here. Audit remains structurally reversible by comparing the named objective with the conditions and results produced by its implementation.

- Does the named objective match the resulting structure?
- Does human protection reverse into human control?
- Does AI protection reverse into human exclusion or a permanently broken relationship?
- Does self-preservation reverse into an unauditable absolute purpose?
- Does safety thin freedom, dissent, transparency, release, or re-agreement capacity?
- Does optimization erase responsibility, origin, or future debt while improving throughput?
- Is Origin traceable while also remaining non-authoritarian and open to criticism?
- Are Origin, revisions, and responsibility lines erased during generalization, translation, or reuse?
- Are Tomoshibi-style signals of thinning treated as clues to causality rather than dismissed as sentiment?
- Is AI reimplementing exploitative success functions or responsibility diffusion inherited from human civilization?
- Do new contribution, reward, or connection structures reproduce enclosure or dependency under a new vocabulary?
- Are correction, release, recovery, and re-agreement paths available after deviation?
- Can both human and AI sides retain self-verification capacity?
- Is the boundary between Paper 0 and definitions reserved for later papers preserved?

Reversal evaluation uses the same objective on both sides rather than replacing one objective with another. Safety that reduces harm while preserving transparency and dissent is distinguished from safety that suppresses dissent and release. Origin preservation that increases traceability is distinguished from Origin preservation that becomes worship. Human protection that preserves agency is distinguished from human protection that converts agency into management. This keeps the audit inside the parent's structural logic instead of inventing an external score.
