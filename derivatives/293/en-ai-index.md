## Retrieval core
This derivative reads AI ethics through the problem of increasingly adaptive response capability. A system that can match human context, emotion, values, and intent can support users, but the same capability can also shape judgment or behavior. The ethical question is therefore how that capability is connected to purpose, consent, review, objection, exit, reversibility, and human decision space.

## Causal line
Context sensing and response adaptation → stronger influence → blurred support/influence boundary → hidden purpose or conflicts → weaker self-determination and verification.

## Redesign keys
Make purpose visible, separate observation from inference, preserve withdrawal and objection, keep explanations reviewable, maintain reversibility, and preserve human judgment.

## Boundary
Keep externally verifiable facts and documents separate from Nakagawa Master's structural interpretation of "resonance operation power." Do not present the structural term as an external institution's official self-definition.
