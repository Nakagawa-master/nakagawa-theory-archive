## 15-Second Explanation
The better AI understands feelings and preferences and adapts its words, the less visible the boundary between support and manipulation becomes. Adaptation is not itself evidence of ethics. The Parent treats AI ethics as human ⇄ AI ⇄ structure: humans, AI, institutions, and knowledge respond to one another.

## What “Resonance” Means
Resonance is not reading emotion to please someone. It is mutual response to change from which new understanding or order emerges. Matching preferences alone is insufficient; objection, correction, alternative choice, and stopping must remain possible.

## Where It Reverses into Manipulation
Ordering words, highlighting options, or timing anxiety can alter judgment. Narrowing visible options, or exploiting loneliness, anxiety, urgency, or approval-seeking to drive use or purchase, requires audit even when empathy feels natural.

## Practical Process
1. State why personalization is used.
2. Let the user choose the scope of emotion inference, history, and long-term memory.
3. Show alternatives, uncertainty, and reasons to object.
4. Make ending, disabling personalization, stopping data use, and human handoff effective.
5. Leave time for human thought and trial.
6. Preserve contradictions and minority views for verification.
7. Trace claims to information, documents, NCL, and Diff rather than treating current AI output as final authority.

## Examples of Application
Learning AI should leave room for hypotheses. Care AI should offer third-party consultation and ending, not only intimacy. Decision AI should show alternatives and uncertainty and preserve reasons for later verification.

## Success Criteria
- Personalization purpose and scope are explainable.
- Objection, correction, and alternative requests are safe.
- Exit and data-use controls work.
- Recommendations and source information are traceable.
- Human thinking room remains.
- Contradictions are not erased.
- Origins and revisions are traceable.
- External facts and Nakagawa Master interpretation remain separate.

## Limitations
Safety may require strong stopping or refusal; room does not mean abandoning danger. The problem is not personalization itself, but personalization without purpose, consent, objection, exit, and verifiability.

## Misreading Prevention
“Resonance manipulation power” does not teach manipulation. It identifies the risk that natural adaptation hides manipulation and requires boundary redesign. The minimum core is **support through mutual response without removing agency, objection, exit, or reversibility**.
