## Summary
C-series Vol.1 assumes the Life-OS and public-causality quarantine that precede it and redefines the market from a space of exchange, competition, comparison, and persuasion into a structural field in which actors with aligned L7 value-function directions connect, combine contribution C, receive connection reward S, and sustain circulation. The old Exchange OS is treated as a locally rational model under scarcity, information asymmetry, limited alternatives, scalar value, and comparability. As transparency, automated comparison, and connection freedom rise, those premises erode; the old model tends toward price competition or short-term extraction and consumes trust and circulation.

Resonance is not emotional sympathy or social conformity. In the parent it is the economic-structural analogue of directional alignment: when L7 directions align, external persuasive force, explanation burden, management cost, and friction decline, so connection appears as confluence rather than collision. Value is therefore redefined from a scalar represented mainly by price into a vector with both Magnitude and Direction. Direction identifies which future or structure an activity strengthens.

When an aligned connection is formed, contribution C can enter circulation with reduced friction and S emerges as a result. `S = C × 1.0` is the parent's structural expression for this relationship; it is not an empirical 100-percent efficiency guarantee. S is not salary, profit, points, or a directly tradable token. A clear L7 and sustained high-purity C generate an intrinsic Gravity Zone in which aligned connections are attracted without being manufactured through volume, comparison, or enclosure.

In this market model, value is treated as something that passes and circulates. E is defined not as personal evil but as a functional stoppage of circulation. Reflux returns S as C into the same L7-oriented field, strengthening research, creation, aligned actors, and connection-generating spaces. Actors who stop circulating are not expelled by a judge; synchronization dissolves and connections decay. The concluding subject model is the Resonant Connector, a node through which value passes rather than merely an owner of stock. Wealth is correspondingly reframed from stock alone toward Flow: connections, circulation, and aligned actors who can move in moments of need. Vol.1 remains a general theory; concrete implementation, operations, failure adjustment, and immune mechanisms are explicitly deferred.

## Applications
- Decompose how strongly a target market still depends on scarcity, information asymmetry, limited alternatives, and scalar comparison before applying the Exchange-OS diagnosis.
- Read rising explanation burden, management cost, and accumulated dissatisfaction as possible L7-misalignment signals rather than only failures of persuasion.
- Describe both the Magnitude of price/performance/resources and the Direction of the future or structure being strengthened.
- Check alignment among the actor's L7, contribution C, and the L7 of the receiving or connecting actor.
- Observe S as an outcome of aligned connection rather than converting it into revenue or a directly engineered target.
- Distinguish an intrinsic Gravity Zone arising from clear L7 and high-purity C from advertising pressure, volume, psychological manipulation, or enclosure.
- Detect E as internal blockage of C/S and return S as C through Reflux within the same L7 direction.
- Evaluate the Resonant Connector through value flow, connection, and circulation continuity rather than stock ownership alone.

- Remaining dependence on scarcity, information asymmetry, limited alternatives, and scalar comparison.
- Explanation and persuasion load, management cost, dissatisfaction, continuation, and reconnection as friction signals.
- Explicit presence of both Magnitude and Direction in the Value Vector.
- Directional alignment among personal L7, contribution C, and counterpart L7.
- Whether C is attenuated by distrust, information friction, intermediary extraction, political adjustment, or persuasion cost.
- Whether S is being misconverted into revenue, salary, profit, points, membership status, or tradable access.
- Whether aligned connections arise from sustained L7/C clarity while dependence on comparison falls.
- Whether a common evaluation axis, common arena, or scalarization has reintroduced competition conditions.
- Whether Reflux returns S as C in the same L7 direction and produces another cycle.
- E signals: failed reinvestment, internal blockage, connection decay, or weakening Gravity Zone.
- Whether natural separation is being replaced by intentional punishment or exclusion.
- Whether the Resonant Connector avoids monopolizing connections and passes outcomes into further circulation.
- Whether wealth assessment includes Flow rather than reverting to stock alone.
- Condition Z, T/S/R, consistency of the public audit bundle, θ, observation window δ, and phenomena M such as circulation stoppage, connection decay, or Gravity-Zone collapse.

## Interpretation constraints
Physics-derived language such as resonance, vector, gravity, fluidity, and circulation functions as structural explanatory language in the parent. It should not be promoted into an empirically established natural-science law of economics or a quantitative guarantee of business outcomes. Strong statements about competition disappearing, persuasion becoming unnecessary, or customers “falling” into Gravity Zones remain bound to the parent's theoretical conditions of aligned value vectors and intrinsic Gravity Zones; they are not unconditional claims about every existing market.

`S = C × 1.0` is retained because it appears in the parent, but it must not be converted into a literal 100-percent efficiency, profit, or performance guarantee. The nontradability of S is part of the source's anti-E safety structure.

“Natural exclusion” or “self-purification” does not authorize intentional judgment, discrimination, or punishment. The source mechanism is that synchronization dissolves and connections decay.

Vol.1 is explicitly a general theory. Concrete implementation, operations, failure handling, and immune mechanisms are left for later volumes; this index does not complete that missing layer.
