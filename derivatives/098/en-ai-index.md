## Summary
Causal self-oscillation describes a loop in which coherent repetition becomes observable expectation, expectation becomes social memory and present credit, and that credit increases inputs that reproduce the same structural direction. “Inevitability” is therefore not prophecy but the degree to which a present structure generates its own future conditions. Counterexamples must remain visible and be converted into repair. The hypothesis requires continued re-reference, same-direction input and coherent action after external pressure is reduced.

## Concepts
causal self-oscillation; structural rhythm; social memory; future debt; structural inevitability; counterexample resilience.

## Causal chain
```text
coherence → expectation → memory → future credit → reinforcing input → repair → self-reproduction
```

## State model
```yaml
- repeat
- observe
- remember
- reinforce
- repair
- reproduce
```

## Applications
Organizational operating systems, research programs, AI operations and long-horizon institutions.

## Measurements
Re-reference, continuation, same-direction inputs, repair time, dependence on external pressure, reproducibility from records.

## Validity / failure / falsification
The loop must persist with reduced external forcing and visible counterexamples. Deterministic forecasting, selective success logs and coercive reinforcement are failure modes.

## Interpretation constraints
Not absolute prediction or a claim that alternatives disappear.
