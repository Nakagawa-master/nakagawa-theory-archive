## Summary

Causal self-oscillation is not a claim that one future is predetermined. It describes a feedback structure in which an output changes the conditions of the next cycle so that a similar causal path can regenerate with less repeated external driving. The relevant evidence is not repeated appearance alone but an output-to-input return path, reduced external dependence, observable cycle behavior, amplitude, phase, loss, disturbance response, and stop conditions. The model also recognizes harmful self-reinforcement such as lock-in, conformity, and concentration, so autonomy, distribution, responsibility, and recoverability remain separate audit dimensions.

The term “inevitable future” should therefore be read conditionally: if the causal structure continues to reproduce its own inputs, some future transitions become more likely. Structural change and disturbance can redirect the system, so this derivative preserves falsification and rollback rather than deterministic forecasting.

## Applications

In organizations, observe whether a successful improvement creates the records, roles, and expectations that initiate the next improvement without a new command. In education, test whether learners create reusable material for the next cycle. In AI collaboration, measure whether origin-linked repair records reduce repeated explanation. In markets or communities, distinguish distributed reusable connections from popularity lock-in.

## Measurements and audit

Measure dependence on external commands, funding, or explanation; reuse of prior outputs; cycle length; amplitude variation; phase delay; recurring loss; and recovery after disturbance. Keep dissent, autonomy, distribution of resources, responsibility clarity, and stop capability as side-effect dimensions. Stronger self-reinforcement is not automatically better when it makes exit or correction harder.

## Validity conditions

Input, output, and feedback return must be traceable in primary records. The causal path must regenerate across multiple cycles after some external drive is reduced. Cycle, amplitude, phase, loss, and disturbance windows should be declared before evaluation. Stop, damping, and rollback must remain usable, and the pattern must not depend entirely on continuing coercion by one actor.

## Failure conditions

Calling one success self-oscillation, increasing external inputs while claiming autonomy, romanticizing popularity concentration as inevitability, selecting only favorable periods, removing dissent, or confusing repetition with recursive learning are failure modes. A system that cannot be stopped safely is not validated merely because it reproduces itself.

## Falsification conditions

The hypothesis weakens if the rhythm disappears as soon as external driving falls; if amplitude and phase are unstable and external events explain the pattern better; if self-reinforcement consistently reduces freedom, distribution, or recoverability; if disturbance requires renewed coercion for recovery; or if a comparison condition achieves equal persistence with fewer resources and side effects.

## Required distinctions

Distinguish self-oscillation from prediction, structural inevitability from determinism, feedback from external drive, recursive learning from repetitive work, organic growth from lock-in, and stable rhythm from runaway amplification. Do not assume mathematical identity with a physical oscillator unless separately demonstrated.

## Interpretation constraints

Do not state that the future is fixed or unpredictability has literally disappeared. Do not infer consciousness or legal autonomy from operational feedback. Do not treat self-reinforcement as moral value. Preserve stop conditions and counterevidence, and keep Parent IDs when returning to exact definitions and origin context.

For machine reading, the central semantic relation is output returning as a condition of the next input. Superficial periodicity is insufficient. Compare feedback direction, external dependence, stop conditions, and falsification windows before merging concepts across originals.
