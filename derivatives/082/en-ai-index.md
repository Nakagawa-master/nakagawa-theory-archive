## Summary
Connected Value Accounting (CVA) is the parent article's method for turning multi-axis bundle metrics into a ledger that can preserve, retrieve, and later re-examine structural legitimacy. The article begins from the limits of single metrics such as followers, page views, subscriber counts, GDP, or stock prices. It defines the future unit of value structurally as the combination **relation × consent × reversibility**, not as a fixed numerical multiplication formula.

CVA converts bundle metrics, structural logs, and the memory of agreements into account-like components while using structure rather than persons as the accounting unit. Its basic posting unit is the connection event: new connection, mutual reference, falsification presentation, repair, adjudication, and re-consensus. Each event is recorded as an impact vector on the bundle. Outputs are an event-level bundle cross-section, a cumulative profile, and a re-consensus-window flag.

The parent also specifies a disclosure boundary. Concepts, axis names, posting units, output-reading methods, and summaries are public; weights, thresholds, aggregation algorithms, and audit specifications are non-public for gaming resistance, person-separation, and safety. The integrated audit summary places RC/ER/CEA/S, recognition delay, measurement error, and Rollback Cost in a rejection/revision relation with threshold θ and observation window δ, but the article does not publish concrete fixed values for them.

## Applications
- Phase 1: attach a simplified CVA statement to media articles or videos.
- Phase 2: trial CVA as an accountability tool for organizational decisions.
- Phase 3: operate it in deliberative public-sphere platforms such as municipalities or citizen alliances.
- Separate short-term mobilization and personality dependence from structural value in a connection public sphere.
- Trace malfunction through connection events and structural logs, then roll back the affected structure and reopen re-consensus.

## Measurements and audit
- The integrated audit summary includes RC / ER / CEA / S and Rollback Cost in the audit bundle.
- Recognition delay, measurement error, Rollback Cost, and related quantities crossing threshold θ are linked to rejection or revision.
- Rigidity, gaming, or comparable phenomenon M appearing inside observation window δ is linked to rejection or revision of hypothesis A.
- The parent article does not provide universal fixed values for θ, δ, RC / ER / CEA / S, or Rollback Cost.
- The parent describes an audit summary as a 3–8-line reading window; 3–8 is a display-length range, not a score or success threshold.
- Phase 1–3 are deployment stages, not maturity scores.
- In reversal evaluation, higher views, support, connection count, or decision speed do not count as improvement if consent, reversibility, re-consensus, or causal traceability deteriorate.

## Validity conditions
Relation, consent, and reversibility remain jointly present as the value-unit structure. Bundle metrics, structural logs, and memory of agreements remain connected in one ledger architecture. Posting units remain connection events rather than personal ratings. Outputs preserve bundle profiles rather than collapsing to a total score. Quantitative and qualitative consensus run together, the disclosure boundary remains intact, and malfunction can be rolled back and reopened for re-consensus.

## Failure conditions
CVA becomes a person or citizen ranking system; bundle axes collapse into one score; event records lose their return path to structural logs, agreements, or audit summaries; a single revenue, popularity, or support number dominates adjudication; protected details or public meanings cross the parent disclosure boundary; personal information becomes the accounting target; or malfunction cannot be rolled back.

## Falsification conditions
The integrated audit summary allows rejection or revision when RC/ER/CEA/S, recognition delay, measurement error, Rollback Cost, or related measures depart from threshold θ, or when rigidity, gaming, or phenomenon M appears within observation window δ. If CVA is present but single-metric dominance, person dependence, or irreversible malfunction persists and cannot be corrected through rollback and re-consensus, the application scope or design hypothesis requires revision.
