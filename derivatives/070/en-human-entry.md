## 15-Second Explanation
The Connection Basic Rights Charter is a minimum system of rights for guaranteeing, as procedures that can actually be used, not only the freedom to connect but also the freedom not to connect, scope limitation, explanation, objection, correction, exit, forgetting, reconnection, human review, and remedy in a society where connection histories and evaluations affect allocation, trust, and opportunities to participate.

## Why It Is Necessary
As connection value, connection governance, and connection protocols spread, who someone connected with, what evaluations they received, and what records remain may affect employment, education, public services, allocation, and other areas.

Even if opportunities for connection increase, it is not free connection if a person suffers disadvantage for refusing, limiting scope, or exiting. Therefore, with the same weight as the right to connect, the rights not to connect, to explanation, to objection, correction, exit, forgetting, reconnection, and remedy are necessary.

## Rights and Procedures
- **Self-determination**: Choose with whom, for what purpose and scope, and for what period to connect.
- **Explanation**: Be able to understand what is recorded, who uses it, and what affected a decision.
- **Objection and correction**: Raise an objection to incorrect records, incorrect evaluations, use outside the purpose, and disadvantageous decisions, and request correction.
- **Exit**: Stop unnecessary connections, authority, and use.
- **Forgetting**: Request deletion or deactivation of unnecessary personal information and connection records.
- **Reconnection**: Rebuild the relationship under new conditions and consent.
- **Human review**: Request human re-review of AI or automated decisions.
- **Remedy**: Request provisional suspension, correction, deletion, recalculation, restoration of rights, compensation, and prevention of recurrence.

## Status of a Rights Case
The parent original treats `REQUESTED / PROVISIONALLY_PROTECTED / UNDER_REVIEW / CORRECTED / REMEDIED / CLOSED / REOPENED` as the procedural statuses of a rights case.

These are not scores showing the person’s trustworthiness or value. They distinguish the current position in the procedure: filing, provisional protection, review, correction, remedy, closure, and reopening.

## Concrete Examples
**Connection value system.** Even when an evaluation result is used for allocation, the person can learn the reason and request an objection, correction, provisional suspension, and recalculation.

**Online community.** Make anonymous or pseudonymous use, moderation objections, data processing after exit, and conditions for rejoining explicit, and do not treat someone who exited as a low-trust person.

**AI agents.** Guarantee the scope of delegation, the right to stop, the right to explanation, human review, correction, and a responsible party, and do not refuse remedy on the grounds that the decision was made by AI.

**Government, employment, and education.** Do not use connection histories or network indicators for improper selection affecting people’s lives or basic opportunities.

## How to Read the Measurements
Initial response time, time to provisional suspension, and final resolution time are observations for seeing the responsiveness of rights remedies. Shorter is not always better; they must not be shortened by omitting explanations or independent review.

The execution rate of correction, deletion, deactivation, and exit requests, and the rate of transfer to human review, are observations for seeing whether rights can be used in practice rather than merely declared. Do not make a high rate the objective itself, and read these measures while ensuring that they do not induce unnecessary claims or merely increase formal processing.

Disadvantage to people who refuse or limit scope, and recurrence of discrimination, exclusion, or use outside the purpose after remedy, are also important. Even when the number is low, distinguish whether it appears low because claims cannot be made, incidents cannot be detected, or people are deterred from speaking up.

## Inversion Evaluation
A system is not healthy when the number of rights exercises is low because people do not know where to apply, the procedure is difficult, or they fear retaliation. A shorter remedy time is not an improvement if explanation, independent review, or compensation has been omitted. A low exit rate is not rights protection if exit carries disadvantage.

## Conditions for Validity
- Rights are implemented as procedures that can be operated.
- There is no retaliation for refusing connection, limiting scope, exiting, or objecting.
- The reviewing body is independent to a certain degree from the original decision-maker.
- Explanations are understandable in accordance with the person’s language, abilities, and situation.
- Provisional remedy, human review, correction, deletion, and compensation are available.
- Correction, deletion, and exit are reflected across all connected parties.
- The responsible party does not disappear even when AI or contractors are used.
- Reasonable accommodation is provided to children, people with disabilities, and people who need language or representative support.

## Limits and Misreading Prevention
Forgetting and audit evidence are in tension. If everything is erased, harm and misconduct cannot be verified; if everything is retained, it becomes surveillance. Design these separately by distinguishing the purpose and period of retention, anonymization, access rights, and objection procedures.

Do not reduce this to a statement of principles that merely lists rights, or assume that providing connection opportunities is sufficient. Do not treat people who exit or object as low-trust people, justify indefinite surveillance in the name of safety, or make an AI automated decision the final decision.
