## Summary
The Parent defines resonance as voluntary external alignment emerging when an internal structure reaches high ethical coherence through non-coercion, reversibility, silence, and dual-layer design. It rejects short-term compliance as the measure of success and instead requires autonomous choice and reversible consensus.

## Concepts
Resonance; non-coercion; alignment pressure differential; silence; reversibility; ethical density; phase mismatch; quiet intervention; silence slot; staged threshold; boundary translation; dual logs; R-Conv; EDI; stabilization S; parallel audit.

## Causal chain
Coercion → friction and reaction → internal redesign → silence creates autonomous workspace → contradiction is repositioned → exit/time limits/rollback → fear decreases → ethical density rises → voluntary alignment → reversible consensus → audit and re-consensus.

## State model
`coercion → friction → internal_alignment → silence → repositioning → reversibility → fearless_choice → voluntary_resonance → reversible_consensus → audit`

## Applications
Cooling windows and pilot ordinances in public systems; debt-repayment weeks, staged authority, feature flags and rollback in firms; contemplation and resubmission in education; reflection steps, human final responsibility and full logs in AI operations.

## Measurements and audit
Use a bundle including R-Conv, EDI, stabilization, resonance density, vocabulary consistency, time to re-consensus, withdrawal success, phase stability, defection, intervention cost, and observation-window integrity.

## Validity conditions
Preserve autonomous choice, exit, time-limited agreements, staged rollout, rollback, silence with purpose and duration, transparent thresholds, boundary translation, and multiple independent audits.

## Failure conditions
Turning non-coercion into neglect, silence into concealment, resonance into manipulation, thresholds into exclusion, reversibility into a nominal label, or resonance into popularity or homogenization.

## Falsification conditions
Revise or reject if resonance, vocabulary consistency, re-consensus, withdrawal, or phase stability worsens, coercion recurs, the observation window closes, or exit becomes unusable.

## Required distinctions
Non-coercion / neglect; resonance / persuasion or control; silence / concealment; threshold / exclusion; reversibility / irresponsibility; ethical density / popularity; resonance / homogenization; causal operation / manipulation of others.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not turn “necessity” into inescapable manipulation. Do not invent fixed formulas for R-Conv, EDI, or S. Do not promote the Parent’s strong theoretical conclusion into a proven universal natural law.
