## 15-Second Explanation
C-series Vol.3 treats post-success corruption in a Resonance Market not as “bad people entering,” but as a structural problem in which connection rewards, fixation, and sunk costs increase through success. It keeps the market alive through immunity checking L7 against current output, metabolism removing ended connections from circulation, contraction choosing appropriate scale rather than maximization, and dynamic equilibrium inheriting L7 and structure rather than people.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Once Vol.2 establishes a Gravity Zone, people, money, reputation, and influence gather. Gravity does not select only comrades: motives diversify, and a “mimicry state” appears where apparent agreement with principles coexists with mismatch among actual behavior, contribution, load tolerance, and L7.

Hunting bad people creates witch hunts or internal conflict. Observing nothing in the name of goodwill concentrates costs on contributors, turns principles into slogans, and prevents highly aligned newcomers from entering. Immunity must observe states rather than personalities, and metabolism must return stagnation to circulation.

## Practical Process
1. **Observe corruption as normal after success.** Headcount, sales, and participation increases alone are not health.
2. **Observe L7 and current output.** Check present behavior, contribution, and load tolerance—not intention, goodwill, tenure, title, or past achievement.
3. **Treat mimicry as a state.** Avoid personality labels; anyone can enter this reversible state.
4. **Update Structural Checkpoints.** Do not check only at joining; update behavior-and-load checkpoints as time, roles, and environments change.
5. **Prevent immune overrun.** Watch for loyalty tests, ideological screening, veteran domination, and ranking by correctness.
6. **Metabolize.** Remove ended roles, non-contributing relationships, vested interests, and sunk-cost stagnation as flow control, not expulsion.
7. **Close as graduation.** State gratitude for the former connection, the present directional split, and both sides’ freedom to live elsewhere.
8. **Make scale a design variable.** Appropriate scale is where Origin’s Tomoshibi reaches people with its temperature and resolution intact.
9. **Contract when necessary.** Recruitment pauses, higher prices, fewer people, or narrower provision can protect the next cycle.
10. **Make structure the succession unit.** Ensure L7 and implementation function without the founder rather than choosing only a successor individual.

## Examples of Application
**Membership organization after success.** If membership grows while only active members carry operating load and others merely enjoy connection rewards, do not call headcount growth success; activate structural immunity.

**Relationship with a contributor of merit.** Record past contributions without erasing them, but do not make them amnesty for present mismatch. When roles diverge, retain a disconnection path called “graduation.”

**Scale review.** Even with growing sales and headcount, consider stopping expansion or shrinking if the founder’s L7 no longer reaches people, immunity and metabolism slow, and operation becomes inertia.

**Succession.** Ask not only who the next leader is, but whether decision criteria align and correction of mismatch arises internally when the founder is absent.

## Success Criteria
- L7 connects to current behavior rather than remaining a slogan.
- Mimicry states are observed without personal attack.
- Load does not remain concentrated on contributors.
- Immunity does not become loyalty tests or ideological control.
- Present mismatch is metabolized while past contribution is respected.
- Disconnection preserves dignity and freedom to diverge.
- L7, immunity, and metabolism outrank numerical maximization.
- Necessary contraction can be carried out.
- L7 and structure function without the founder.
- The market circulates as dynamic equilibrium, not a fixed form.

## Limits
The Parent sets no fixed headcount or sales limits, mimicry rate, or immunity score. Appropriate scale is the limit at which Origin’s Tomoshibi retains its temperature and resolution, not a universal number.

Entropy, immunity, metabolism, and cardiac contraction/expansion are physics and biology metaphors for structural explanation, not identical strict laws or fixed management rules for every market.

## Preventing Misreading
- Mimicry is not a bad-person label.
- Immunity is not attack or exclusionism.
- A Checkpoint is not a loyalty test.
- Disconnection is not punishment.
- Graduation is not defeat.
- Shrinking does not always make a system healthy.
- Rejecting maximization is not rejecting all growth.
- Preserving Origin-specificity is not founder absolutism.
