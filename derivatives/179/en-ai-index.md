## Summary
The parent text is Part III of “Critical Point of Structural Civilization.” It frames the civilizational effect of LLMs not primarily as a question of AI personality or developer morality, but as a question of objective function / operating system: what is treated as correct, successful, and reward-worthy at the highest level. The parent’s “public-opinion-criterion AI” is a structural type in which statistical majority, frequency, short-term reactions, likeability, and avoidance of backlash become proxies for higher-order correctness; it is not identical to democracy or citizens’ political rights. The parent argues that when datasets containing anger, incitement, exaggeration, partial truths, and conformity pressure are combined with frequency-oriented optimization, existing E-like patterns may be reamplified as answer-likeness. Its alternative, “structure-criterion AI,” evaluates beyond L1–L3 surface signals by using L4 mechanisms and biases, L5 causality, L6 macro–micro connection, and L7 structural alignment and long-term Contribution C. It connects this to the parent’s structural equations `S=0.1C+0.9E` and `S=C×1.0`. L5 is deliberately kept abstract rather than turned into concrete causal-manipulation techniques, and the model remains subject to audit condition Z, θ/δ/M falsification, human intervention, non-coercion, reversibility, and verifiability.

## Concepts
- **Public-opinion-criterion AI:** Parent structural type that elevates frequency, majority, and short-term reaction toward higher-order evaluation; not democracy itself.
- **Structure-criterion AI:** Parent model prioritizing long-term causality, structural alignment, Contribution C, and future debt.
- **Objective function / L7:** Highest-order criterion defining what counts as success, reward, or correctness.
- **AI personality:** Surface style or character; the parent does not treat it as the main civilizational fork.
- **L1–L3:** Phenomena, behavior, narratives, institutions, and other near-surface signals.
- **L4:** Structural-recognition layer for bias, patterns, mechanisms, and structural noise.
- **L5:** Causal / structural-operation layer. The parent keeps it abstract and does not publish concrete manipulation techniques.
- **L6:** Layer connecting individual and organizational effects to civilization-scale macro–micro interaction.
- **L7:** Value-function layer including structural alignment and long-term contribution.
- **C:** Contribution; long-term structural contribution.
- **E:** Exploitation / Excess Load; extraction, externalized burden, or structural distortion.
- **Dark equation:** `S=0.1C+0.9E`, a parent civilization-evaluation model rather than universal measured coefficients.
- **Connection reward:** `S=C×1.0`, the parent’s directional model for C to flow into outcome without being captured by E, not a 100% performance guarantee.
- **Condition Z:** Continuous reevaluation condition including audit, updates, and human intervention.
- **θ / δ / M:** Variable audit elements for target-specific deterioration threshold, observation window, and newly observed failure phenomena.

## Applications
- Evaluate LLM quality by separating user satisfaction from causal depth, reservations, and long-term misrecognition risk.
- In information environments, separate reaction volume from causal effects on polarization, error propagation, and future debt.
- In AI deployment, audit ranking, reward, selection, and evaluation criteria rather than relying on personality settings.
- In organizational decisions, retain majority opinion as important input while separately evaluating irreversibility, minority burden, and long-term consequences.
- Observe feedback loops in which AI outputs return to the data environment and may amplify E-like frequency patterns.

## Measurements and audit
- Is frequency, likeability, or short-term reaction the sole evaluation axis?
- Is majority frequency distinguished from causal and long-term alignment?
- Are frequent online patterns promoted to correctness without checking factuality and causality?
- Can the actual reward and evaluation structure be audited separately from developer intent?
- Can L4 identify mechanisms, bias, and structural noise?
- Is L5 kept from drifting into concrete manipulation of people or society?
- Can L6 connect local optimization with civilization-scale effects?
- Does L7 evaluate C, E, future debt, and structural alignment together?
- Has support for deliberation turned into censorship or suppression of dissent?
- Is output feedback increasing E-like frequency patterns?
- Can condition Z and θ/δ/M trigger revision when outcomes deteriorate?
- Are non-coercion, reversibility, and verifiability preserved?

## Validity conditions
- Preserve public opinion, majority, and frequency as inputs without equating them with the highest value function.
- Distinguish democratic civic rights from statistical-majority optimization.
- Distinguish AI personality from L7 objective function.
- Distinguish developer intent from actual evaluation and reward structure.
- Analyze L4–L7 rather than stopping at L1–L3.
- Do not equate structure criteria with arbitrary expert or authority judgment.
- Separate C from E and short-term success from future debt.
- Keep L5 as an abstract safety principle rather than an operational manipulation technique.
- Preserve both equations as parent structural models.
- Retain human intervention, audit, falsifiability, and revision.

## Failure conditions
- Turning criticism of public-opinion criterion into rejection of democracy.
- Turning “majority can be wrong” into a doctrine that majority is always wrong.
- Turning structure criteria into rule by AI, experts, or authorities.
- Judging safety only from polite style, goodwill, or personality.
- Equating popularity, frequency, or click rate with C.
- Calling short-term S growth success while E grows.
- Converting L5 into concrete psychological or social manipulation instructions.
- Turning polarization reduction into suppression of dissent or diversity.
- Making structure criteria unfalsifiable and unauditable.

## Falsification conditions
The parent places the model under integrated audit condition Z. Hypothesis A should be rejected or revised when target-specific indicators deteriorate beyond variable threshold θ, observation window δ closes and external feedback or falsifiability is lost, or a new failure phenomenon M emerges and persists. If structure-criterion AI fails to increase C, increases E, concentrates power, suppresses dissent, degrades causal explanation, or worsens human–AI coexistence, success assessment should reverse even when short-term S is high. The parent provides no universal fixed values for θ or δ, so this derivative invents none.

- Developer goodwill / actual evaluation and reward structure.
