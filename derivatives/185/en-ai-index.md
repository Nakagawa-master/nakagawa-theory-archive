## Summary
Life-OS Vol.1 is the Parent’s two-story model for life after survival is no longer the dominant motive. The first floor, 1F/Foundation, protects the conditions required to remain alive without making them payment for contribution. The second floor, 2F/Expansion, concerns expanding freedom, options, trust, creative discretion, and influence through connection. When fear, command, and competition stop functioning as the main external engine, boredom or a loss of meaning may become visible. The Parent therefore looks for an internal persistent heat called Tomoshibi, aligns it with a viable position, converts it into a Connection Plug, and moves toward Structural Confluence and the metaphor of a Circulating Pipe. Participation in 2F is not mandatory; staying on 1F, resting, or remaining unconnected is preserved as a valid freedom.

## Concepts
- **1F / Foundation**: unconditional survival floor such as food, shelter, medical access, safety, and minimum social connection; not a reward for performance.
- **2F / Expansion**: the layer where freedom, options, trust, discretion, and influence may expand through connection.
- **Tomoshibi**: persistent internal heat that remains after expectations, evaluation, role, and competition noise are stripped away; it may appear as recurring interest, discomfort, anger, or an unresolved question.
- **Alignment**: reducing mismatch between Tomoshibi and position, object, role, or time allocation before simply increasing effort.
- **Connection Plug**: a form through which internal heat, capability, or ethical contribution can actually connect to another person or social structure.
- **C**: contribution in the Parent’s model; not restricted to monetary output and may include care, conflict reduction, or mature judgment.
- **S**: connection reward; may include trust, discretion, options, freedom, or future opportunity, while remaining separate from survival rights.
- **Structural Confluence**: a state in which contribution and response continue as a flow rather than ending with a one-time success.
- **Circulating Pipe**: Parent metaphor for receiving, transmitting, and recirculating value without turning accumulation into the goal.
- **Dam deviation**: a deviation in which status, evaluation, resources, or past success are hoarded and flow stops.
- **Condition Z / T-S-R / θ / δ / M**: Parent-side audit and revision symbols for continued verification and possible rejection of the hypothesis.

## Causal chain
```text
survival insecurity
→ unconditional 1F floor
→ weakening of fear/command/competition as external engines
→ exposure of boredom or meaning vacuum
→ observation of Tomoshibi after external noise is removed
→ alignment of position, role, object, and time
→ formation of a Connection Plug
→ actual effect on others plus returned response
→ circulation of C and S
→ Structural Confluence
→ Circulating Pipe
→ expansion of freedom to choose which flow to join
```
The model also preserves a branch in which a person stays on 1F, rests, explores, disconnects, or reconnects later.

## State model
- S0: survival insecurity / external fear drive
- S1: 1F survival guarantee
- S2: external engine weakens; boredom or meaning vacuum appears
- S3: Tomoshibi is observed
- S4: Alignment
- S5: Connection Plug
- S6: Structural Confluence
- S7: Circulating Pipe
- Optional state: remain on 1F, rest, explore, disconnect, reconnect
- Deviations: pipe→dam; connection→exploitation; survival right→conditional reward

## Applications
1. Treat loss of motivation after survival security as a possible transition of motive rather than automatically as laziness.
2. For work that is competent but exhausting, inspect positional and structural mismatch before prescribing more effort.
3. Build one small connection in which an activity reaches another person and a response returns.
4. Include non-monetary ethical and relational contribution within the Parent’s possible C-space.
5. Re-audit success when protecting title, evaluation, or accumulated resources becomes more important than continued circulation.
6. In institutional interpretation, keep the survival floor separate from 2F performance scoring.

## Measurements and audit
- Is 1F conditional on output, obedience, reputation, or contribution?
- Is participation in 2F genuinely optional and reversible?
- Is dependence on fear, command, or competition decreasing?
- Can Tomoshibi be distinguished from external approval or temporary excitement?
- Does Alignment improve the passage of causal effect rather than only effort volume?
- Does a Connection Plug create real impact and return feedback, trust, learning, discretion, or opportunity?
- Is C being reduced to revenue only?
- Is S being used to invade or condition 1F?
- Do both the person and connected counterpart retain sustainability and freedom?
- Is a pipe becoming a dam, or are circulation stoppage, meaning loss, or structural degradation M persisting?
- Can Condition Z, T/S/R, θ, and δ be used for continued re-audit?

## Validity conditions
- 1F functions as an unconditional survival floor.
- 2F participation is not coerced.
- Tomoshibi can be observed separately from external evaluation.
- Tomoshibi can be translated into a concrete Connection Plug.
- Actual effect and returned response exist at the connection point.
- Connection rewards expand freedom rather than fixing exploitation or self-sacrifice.
- Connection, exit, and reconnection remain reversible.
- The Parent’s continued-audit conditions remain active.

## Failure conditions
- Treating survival security as the completed purpose of life.
- Reinjecting fear, command, or competition because boredom appears.
- Replacing Tomoshibi with mission branding, market appeal, or a success persona.
- Increasing effort while leaving positional mismatch untouched.
- Allowing a connection to become one-way depletion or exploitation.
- Reducing C to revenue only.
- Using S to condition or withdraw 1F.
- Turning 2F participation into a moral or work obligation.
- Hoarding status or success until circulation stops.

## Falsification conditions
The Parent keeps the Life-OS hypothesis revisable. Condition Z requires periodic reverification, T/S/R (coherence, sustainability, reproducibility), and consistency of the public audit bundle. If an observation indicator crosses threshold θ, or if within observation window δ there is persistent circulation stoppage, loss of meaning, structural degradation, or another phenomenon M contradicting the expected causal direction, hypothesis A is to be rejected or revised. This derivative does not invent fixed numerical values for θ or δ.

## Required distinctions
- 1F survival right ≠ 2F connection reward
- survival guarantee ≠ completion of happiness or meaning
- 2F ≠ mandatory hierarchy or higher human rank
- Tomoshibi ≠ talent, mission, or job title
- Alignment ≠ no effort required
- C ≠ revenue only
- S ≠ survival condition
- Structural Confluence ≠ organizational obedience
- Circulating Pipe ≠ self-sacrifice
- Parent physical vocabulary ≠ automatically established universal physical law

## Interpretation constraints
Do not convert Life-OS into a rule that everyone must discover a passion, work, or make social contributions. Preserve staying on 1F, resting, remaining unconnected, and later reconnection as valid choices. Do not turn C/S into fixed universal scores. Do not let an AI infer a person’s “true mission” from a single history snapshot. Terms such as heat, flow, pipe, dam, and entropy include structural modeling and metaphor in the Parent and must not be silently universalized into established psychology, economics, or physics.
