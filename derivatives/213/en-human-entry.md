## 15-Second Explanation
Endurance civilization is a concept that defines the state in which society does not stop even though it has broken down, not with emotional terms such as “stagnation” or “decay,” but as an operating phase of civilization. Even when the core of consensus formation—understanding, responsibility, and history—has weakened, the outward forms of institutions, administration, markets, and daily life remain. Society therefore prolongs itself not through consent, but by using fear, profit, and surveillance as primary mechanisms, with myth and inertia as amplification devices.

What matters is that this does not mean “it is healthy because it is still alive,” nor that “it will collapse soon.” Endurance civilization is a civilizational phase of **survival without regeneration**: it is moving, but has lost its capacity for self-repair, and reform or repair is liable to shift from removing causes to postponing stoppage.

## Why It Becomes Necessary
Society does not necessarily stop as soon as its institutions break. Massive structures—administrative procedures, logistics, markets, schools, hospitals, organizations, meetings, salaries, evaluations, and urban life—have inertia. Even when understanding collapses, responsibility becomes invisible, and history is severed, that body continues moving for a while.

This makes two misrecognitions likely. First, people think it is healthy because it is still moving. Second, they think it will end soon because it is this broken. The original places between these two the endurance phase: broken, yet continuing for a long time.

Furthermore, when consensus formation weakens, the driving sources of order also change. Rather than people understanding, consenting, and then complying, fear of the losses of noncompliance, connection to allocation and status through profit, and surveillance pressure under which deviations are visible maintain social participation. Myth legitimizes that operation, and inertia automates it through repetition.

## Practical Process
1. **Separate outward form from vital functions.** Examine not only whether institutions, administration, and markets are operating, but also whether understanding, responsibility, and history are functioning.
2. **Examine understanding.** Confirm whether the same words are shared with nearby meanings and whether multiple actors can explain institutional purposes and reasons for decisions.
3. **Examine responsibility.** Confirm whether it is possible to trace who decided, who carried out the decision, and who accepts the result.
4. **Examine history.** Confirm whether the course of institutional changes and decisions can be followed as a continuous line rather than as fragments.
5. **Examine operating force.** See whether fear, profit, and surveillance have become the main reasons for connection rather than consent.
6. **Examine amplification devices.** See whether myth makes operation appear inevitable and legitimate, and whether inertia turns institutions without a known reason into autopilot.
7. **Distinguish repair from prolongation.** See whether reform reaches the causes of damage, or merely postpones stoppage by accumulating local responses.
8. **Examine overlapping phases.** Do not paint all of society with one label; see whether consensus, endurance, and transition phases overlap by domain.

## Examples of Application
### Organizational Reform
When reorganizations, added meeting bodies, new KPIs, and institutional changes continue while the same problems recur, examine repair capacity rather than the volume of reform. If the cause of the problem cannot be shared, responsibility for change cannot be traced, and the history of the previous change cannot be revisited, updates may be prolongation treatment.

### Administration and Public Institutions
Even when counters and services continue, that alone does not mean they are healthy. If institutional purposes cannot be explained, responsible actors are absorbed into the institution, and change histories cannot be traced, the outward form may be operating while central circulation is weakening.

### Information Spaces
Do not treat the amount of discussion as evidence that consensus formation is alive. If the same words are used with different meanings, whose judgment it was is unclear, and past explanations do not connect to current explanations, discussion may increase without leading to self-repair.

### Social and Civilizational Observation
Do not place evaluative terms such as “dictatorship,” “corruption,” or “stagnation” first; examine what actually maintains order. Observe to what extent fear, profit, and surveillance have become primary drivers, and how myth and inertia prolong them.

## Success Criteria
Whether observation using this theory has succeeded is judged not by whether the subject was classified with strong words, but by whether the following distinctions can be made.

- The continuation of institutional outward form can be separated from the vital functions of understanding, responsibility, and history.
- Continuation can be separated from regeneration.
- Reform can be separated from repair.
- The mere existence of fear, profit, and surveillance can be separated from their becoming primary operating forces.
- Myth can be seen as a legitimizing function rather than merely as falsehood.
- Inertia can be seen as institutional autopilot rather than merely as negligence.
- Overlapping consensus, endurance, and transition phases can be handled.
- Observations can be returned to a record of structure rather than a condemnation of a particular group.

## Limitations
The original does not present endurance civilization as a single political-system name or national ranking. Fear, profit, and surveillance can also exist to some extent in a consensus civilization, so their existence alone must not be used to declare an endurance civilization. What must be observed is whether understanding, responsibility, and history support self-repair, or whether the three primary mechanisms have become the main drivers of order in their place.

Also, although the sequence consensus civilization → endurance civilization → transition civilization is often observed, it is not a fixed sequence in which society as a whole always proceeds at once. It is possible for one domain to be in a consensus phase, another in an endurance phase, and another in a transition phase.

The original does not present fixed numerical thresholds. The derivative side must not create an original criterion such as “endurance civilization begins when understanding falls by what percentage.”

## Preventing Misreading
- Do not make endurance civilization another name for “bad civilization.”
- Do not equate the continuation of institutions with health.
- Do not equate being broken with immediate collapse.
- Do not call continuation regeneration.
- Do not treat the existence of reform as evidence of self-repair capacity.
- Do not declare an endurance phase merely from the existence of fear, profit, and surveillance.
- Do not reduce myth to a mere lie or inertia to mere laziness.
- Do not treat the three phases as completely exclusive boxes.
- Do not repurpose the label to attack a particular country, ethnicity, political party, or ideology.
