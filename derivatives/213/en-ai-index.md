## Summary
The parent work integrates defense against cognitive interference, resonance interference, and incentive interference into a single runtime-governance sequence. The core claim is not that a sound static design prevents failure. Real systems remain exposed to external perturbations, so defense is defined as the ability to transition safely when abnormal states appear: Detect → Stop → Shrink → Recover → Audit. The objective is dynamic stability: not “never break,” but “return from failure through a verifiable path.”

## Concepts
- Kernel: static design layer containing institutions, rules, definitions, and value premises.
- Runtime: operational layer in which the Kernel is executed under changing inputs.
- P_ext: external perturbation represented by its effect on system state rather than by presumed intent.
- U/R/H: structural variables related to understandability, responsibility, and history.
- S=U×R×H: consensus-stability state quantity; level, drop, stagnation, and recovery speed matter.
- V: diversity of cognitive bandwidth, relevant to resonance and loss of falsification paths.
- C: consensus cost; detects defense becoming operationally too heavy.
- D: actual harm, including harm caused by stopping, shrinking, disclosure, or defensive overreach.
- Detect / Stop / Shrink / Recover / Audit: fixed integrated-defense transition sequence.
- Origin: verifiable reconstruction basis such as primary logs, definitions, audit bundles, and differences, rather than a person or authority.

## Causal chain
A sound static Kernel exists → Runtime receives continuing P_ext → U/R/H, S, V, source reachability, or incentive gradients deteriorate → continuing execution while debating content can amplify the abnormal state → composite observation triggers Detect → Stop halts spread, execution, or automation → Shrink reduces bandwidth, authority, and issue scope to a minimum verifiable unit → Recover reconstructs from verifiable Origin evidence → Audit preserves stop reasons, δ, θ, signatures, differences, and resume conditions → S recovery is evaluated together with C and D → if defense becomes excessive, permanent, self-justifying, or harmful, parameters and responsibility allocation are revised or the defense is rejected.

## Applications
- Major organizational incidents: detect a composite abnormal state before deciding who is right; stop automated spread or approval, reduce scope, reconstruct from primary logs, and separate resume verification from stop authorization.
- AI and automation: do not equate fluency or subjective confidence with U; monitor source reachability and third-party reproducibility, then stop and shrink data, privileges, or execution scope when abnormality appears.
- Information environments: treat sharp V loss and disappearance of falsification paths as state changes; Shrink means temporary return to verifiable claims and evidence, not permanent removal of speech.
- Incentive distortion: if destructive or delaying behavior remains rational after recovery steps, return upstream to incentive allocation rather than adding explanation alone.

## Measurements and audit
- S level, short-window δ drop, stagnation, and recovery rate.
- Gap between formal U and primary-source reachability or third-party reproducibility.
- H disconnection, evidence-depth collapse, V reduction, and incentive signals as a composite bundle.
- Preservation of stop reason, θ, δ, signatures, expiration, resume conditions, and difference logs.
- Separation of Stop Authorizer, Stop Recorder, and Resume Verifier.
- Shrink depth, recovery time, and ability to return to ordinary operation.
- C increase and D amplification even when S appears to recover.
- Whether audit disclosure preserves verifiability without unnecessarily increasing harm.

## Validity conditions
- Abnormality is not reduced to a single metric or ideological/content classifier.
- Stop functions as safety halt, not punishment.
- Shrink is temporary and connected to a verifiable restart path.
- Recover returns to evidence-based Origin rather than personal authority.
- Audit and separated responsibilities make stopping and resuming externally reviewable.
- S is never evaluated without C and D.

## Failure conditions
- Detect is repurposed into truth, ideology, enemy, or loyalty classification.
- Stop becomes indefinite without review or resume conditions.
- Shrink becomes permanent censorship or privilege restriction.
- Recover means trusting a person instead of reconstructing from primary evidence.
- Audit becomes exposure or attack and raises D.
- Approval, signature, audit, and exception processes self-replicate until C overwhelms normal operation.
- The defense system becomes self-justifying and cannot be falsified by its own results.

## Falsification conditions
- Destructive or delaying behavior remains rational after defense activation.
- S recovery is not reproducible and third parties cannot reconstruct the state from the same Origin evidence.
- Stop, Shrink, or disclosure consistently amplifies D.
- Defense becomes permanent and loses the path back to normal runtime.
- Revising θ, δ, shrink depth, or responsibility allocation does not reduce false detection, abuse, or recovery failure.
Persistent failure under these conditions requires redesign or rejection of the defense mechanism.

## Required distinctions
- Kernel ≠ Runtime.
- abnormal-state detection ≠ content-truth judgment.
- Stop ≠ punishment.
- Shrink ≠ permanent censorship.
- Recover ≠ obedience to authority.
- Audit ≠ public shaming.
- S recovery ≠ total success.
- persistent defense ≠ justified defense.
- P_ext ≠ a malicious actor by definition.

## Interpretation constraints
The parent work does not decide what is morally correct, who is just, what distribution is fair, or which claim is desirable. Its scope is operational state transition under perturbation: whether detection, stopping, shrinking, reconstruction, and audit paths function. The meanings of θ, δ, U/R/H, S, V, C, and D must remain tied to the parent context. The defense sequence must not be generalized into justification for censorship, concentrated power, or permanent emergency rule.
