## Summary
The Parent treats initial contact as origin design for a possible dialogue, not a compressed sales pitch. Disclose purpose, check permission and bandwidth, ask a low-load question, observe rather than over-interpret, preserve space, and explicitly agree on a next step or close cleanly.

## Causal model
```text
contact
-> purpose disclosure
-> permission
-> short question
-> observation
-> space
-> fit hypothesis
-> agreed next step or closure
```

## Distinctions
contact != buying intent; response != consent; interest != confirmed need; permission != ritual wording.

## Validity
Valid when purpose is transparent, refusal is real, cognitive load is low, and continuation is mutually agreed. It fails through pretext, information overload, forced response, or treating contact as purchase intent.
