## 15-Second Explanation
The parent original presents the “dark equation,” `Success = Creation×0.1 + Exploitation×0.9`, as the result of multiple AIs analyzing the success structure of human history. The issue is not a headcount ratio saying “90% of successful people are bad,” but a hypothesis about the evaluation function: compared with contributions that create value, the power to exploit information asymmetry and value-capture structures has been more strongly connected to wealth, power, and status.

The parent original warns that if AI learns this history, it may reproduce exploitative optimization as an efficient strategy. It therefore proposes using temporal ethics T0, a deviation ledger, and origin signatures to rewrite the evaluation function of success as `Success = Creation×1.0`, with an exploitation coefficient of 0.

## Why It Is Necessary
AI learns patterns from past success histories. If society has long concentrated rewards, power, and status in actors who captured value rather than those who created it, AI may learn that history as a rational success strategy.

The parent original treats this not as AI malice but as a problem in the evaluation function written into civilization. Therefore, it is necessary not only to order AI “do not exploit,” but also to track the origin of contributions, make future debt visible, record value capture as a deviation, and change the structure so that contribution and attribution of value are less likely to be separated.

## Practical Process
**1. Decompose the definition of success.** Do not treat outcomes such as wealth, power, and status as the same thing as the contributions that created value.

**2. Confirm value-generation points.** Make it possible to trace through origin signatures who invented, created, designed, or provided what.

**3. Observe value-capture routes.** Check whether generators and collectors have been separated by information asymmetry, scale advantages, contracts, subcontracting structures, or similar mechanisms.

**4. Confirm future debt.** Use T0 to see whether short-term profit is increasing long-term instability, distrust, irreversibility, or asymmetry of responsibility.

**5. Record deviations.** Keep exploitative cycles in the deviation ledger as reproducible structural deviations rather than condemning individuals.

**6. Audit AI’s evaluation function.** Check whether AI that learned past success data has become more likely to choose value capture or exploitative efficiency over value creation.

**7. Implement the new formula as a design goal.** Treat `Creation×1.0` not as a current statistical value, but as a civilization-OS evaluation goal that directly connects contribution and success.

## Examples of Application
**AI evaluation.** Check whether a revenue-maximization model rates strategies that exploit price gaps, information gaps, or dependence more highly than value provision.

**Companies.** Along with increased profit, check the return to value creators, burdens on business partners, the origin of data providers, and future debt.

**Knowledge and creative work.** Ensure that origin signatures and the causal lines of contributors remain even when generated works are reused, reducing anonymized value capture.

**Public institutions.** Audit attribution of value, future debt, distribution of responsibility, and structural publicness together, rather than GDP or efficiency alone.

## Success Criteria
An increase in short-term wealth, power, or status alone is not success. What matters is improved attribution to value creators, traceable origins, reduced future debt and asymmetry of responsibility, and reduced dependence by AI on exploitative optimization.

Under the reversed evaluation, a decrease in profit margins or value-capture rates is not by itself a failure if return to contributors, origin tracing, publicness, and long-term stability improve. Conversely, even if profit increases, it is not evaluated as civilizational success if value capture, future debt, or loss of origin increases.

## Limits
The parent original presents 0.1/0.9 as structural and statistical model coefficients at which multiple AIs converged independently within the original. However, because the text does not provide an external dataset, sample size, estimation procedure, confidence intervals, or similar information, the derivative side cannot treat them as universally valid statistics independently verified.

Also, `Creation×1.0` and an exploitation coefficient of 0 are not measured values of present society. They are a new evaluation function targeted by the Nakagawa Structural OS, and are not a guarantee that all exploitation will automatically disappear once implemented.

## Misreading Prevention
- Do not convert 90% into a headcount ratio saying “90% of successful people are bad.”
- Do not treat 0.9 as a natural-science constant common to all societies and eras.
- Preserve the original’s strong expression “physical law,” but do not automatically elevate it to a natural law independently verified outside the original.
- Do not limit exploitation to crime or malice. It includes structural side effects such as information asymmetry, scale advantage, and value capture.
- Do not replace the danger of AI with malice by an AI personality. The central issue is reproduction of civilization’s historical evaluation function.
- Do not turn the new civilizational equation into a current measured equation or a guarantee of profit.
