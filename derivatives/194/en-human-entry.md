## 15-Second Explanation

D-Series Vol. 3 is connection design for using legacy civilization’s funding, credit, law, and infrastructure without rejecting or assimilating into them. Core and Legacy are not mixed directly; a cleanroom is placed between them to wash inputs and package outputs. In contracts, decision rights, responsibility boundaries, and a Kill Switch are fixed so that one can leave if L7 erosion, chronic deficit, or direct mixing occurs.

## Why It Is Needed

To expand a new circulation to social scale, one cannot ignore the physical layers built by existing civilization over long periods: land, law, funding, credit, communications, sales channels, and so on. On the other hand, full fusion with a huge organization allows that organization’s evaluation axis, decision speed, and distributed responsibility to flow inward, diluting the design philosophy.

The Parent Original therefore abandons the binary choice between complete fusion and complete isolation and establishes a third path: connecting with a heterogeneous large structure “without mixing.” The purpose of connection is not to become friendly or unify values. It is to safely pass necessary functions and convert resources into a new circulation while preserving Core’s sovereignty over judgment.

## Practical Process

1. **Identify the leverage in the connection target.** Clarify what you are borrowing: funding, credit, legal legitimacy, sales channels, infrastructure, and so on.
2. **Separate Core / Legacy / Buffer.** Do not directly connect Core’s central people to Legacy; carve out joint projects as a Buffer = cleanroom.
3. **Wash inputs.** Separate intervention, precedent, evaluation axes, blame-shifting, and similar elements from money, brand, authority, and demands, and convert them into budgets, labels, specifications, and so on.
4. **Package outputs.** Do not change Core’s philosophy or design content; translate them into forms Legacy can handle, such as products, operational improvements, efficiency, or expertise.
5. **Place translators at the boundary.** An interface person who understands both OS logics but assimilates into neither side manages input and output.
6. **Write the contract as an API.** Put final decision rights, responsibility boundaries, unforeseen risks, approval rights, and termination conditions in writing.
7. **Reject ambiguous inputs.** Do not mix demands that cannot be put in writing, such as “the atmosphere,” “the relationship of trust,” or “just this once,” into specifications.
8. **Stage connection depth.** Start with a small function and increase depth while checking that the boundary is preserved.
9. **Audit finances and contamination.** Observe L7 erosion, coordination time, decision delays, direct mixing, and return versus cost.
10. **Withdraw when boundaries are violated.** Use the Kill Switch, organize deliverables and work, preserve value, and reallocate resources to the next connection.

## Examples of Application

**Partnership with a large corporation.** Do not enter the entire company; establish a limited project as the Buffer. Use the brand, sales channels, and budget, but keep final decision rights over design, philosophy, expression, and hiring on the Core side.

**Collaboration with a local government.** Use administrative access and social credit without fully transferring Core’s design decisions to precedent or committees. Translate external explanations into institutional vocabulary.

**Sponsor funding.** Even when receiving funding as a budget, do not automatically make the sponsor’s value function an internal standard. Write the scope of intervention and termination conditions first.

**Joint development.** Do not fully integrate from the beginning; start with a limited function. Before increasing connection depth, check whether boundaries, responsibility, and decision rights are actually being protected.

## Success Criteria

Success is not “being able to partner with a large organization.” It is a state in which Legacy resources accelerate Core’s circulation while separation of Core / Legacy / Buffer, final decision rights, responsibility boundaries, and the ability to withdraw are maintained.

Under the Parent Original’s disproof conditions, if L7 erosion advances within the observation window δ, chronic connection deficits exceed threshold θ, or direct mixing without a cleanroom becomes normal, this connection model is not a success but a target for revision or termination.

## Limitations

- This is not a theory that using external leverage automatically makes civilization renewal succeed.
- It does not claim that one should connect with every organization.
- A contract API or Kill Switch is not a license to ignore real laws or existing contracts.
- The Parent Original does not present a fixed θ value or fixed δ period common to every case.
- “Use,” “rewiring,” and “overwriting” contain structural metaphors; they do not recommend violence, deception, or illegal takeovers.

## Preventing Misreading

“Turning legacy civilization’s resources into fuel” does not mean deceiving the other side and taking its resources. It means converting funding, credit, institutions, infrastructure, and similar resources supplied by the other side into another circulation within a connection whose boundaries and contract are explicit.

A cleanroom is not an ideology of isolating the outside as an enemy. It is a buffer structure that keeps flows moving while preventing direct mixing. External demands and information pass through after translation and specification, and Core’s results are packaged into forms the outside can handle and returned.
