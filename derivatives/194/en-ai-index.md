## Summary
D-series Vol.3 describes strategic heterogeneous connection: using the capital, law, legitimacy, infrastructure, distribution and other leverage held by Legacy-OS without either fully rejecting it or assimilating into it. It separates Core, Legacy and Buffer, uses a clean room for input sanitization and output packaging, and treats contracts as APIs that fix decision rights, responsibility boundaries and a Kill Switch. The goal is not to destroy or convert the counterpart, but to convert external leverage into fuel for a new circulation while preserving L7 sovereignty and the ability to disconnect.

## Concepts
- Rewiring: retain existing physical layers while recoding meanings, roles and resource use.
- Merge / Disconnect / Connect: reject both full merger and full isolation; connect without mixing interiors.
- Core: the domain of design, theory, creation and final judgment.
- Legacy: external structures holding law, money, infrastructure, authority and legitimacy.
- Buffer / Clean Room: the intermediary zone preventing direct Core-Legacy mixing.
- Input Sanitization: separate usable resources from intervention, imposed evaluation criteria, precedent pressure and responsibility dumping.
- Output Packaging: preserve Core content while translating presentation into formats Legacy can process.
- Translator: a boundary manager who understands both protocols without belonging fully to either.
- Contract as API: specify inputs, outputs, authority, responsibility and termination conditions.
- Kill Switch: a pre-defined right and procedure to disconnect when boundaries are violated.
- Resource Conversion: redirect external leverage into the C-series circulation.
- T/S/R: Traceability / Sovereignty / Reversibility.

## Causal chain
```text
Legacy retains large-scale leverage
→ full isolation loses social-scale leverage
→ full merger lets Legacy evaluation and decision structures penetrate Core
→ choose Connect
→ separate Core / Legacy / Buffer
→ sanitize inputs and package outputs
→ translator manages the boundary
→ contract API fixes decision rights, responsibility and Kill Switch
→ external resources are converted into circulation fuel
→ audit L7 erosion, persistent negative return and direct mixing
→ disconnect when boundary conditions fail
→ preserve independent judgment and freedom to choose the next connection
```

## Applications
- Isolate a large-company partnership as a Buffer project rather than merging organizations.
- Use institutional access and legitimacy from public bodies while retaining Core design authority.
- Convert sponsorship money into budget while separating attached intervention demands.
- Start joint development with limited functions and deepen connection only after boundaries hold.
- On boundary violation, organize deliverables, disconnect and reallocate resources.

## Measurements and audit
- Frequency of direct Core-Legacy contact.
- Whether all material inputs and outputs pass through Buffer.
- Separation of external resources from attached noise and value pressure.
- Preservation of Core meaning during output packaging.
- Location of final decision rights.
- Written clarity of responsibility exclusions and risk allocation.
- Presence of non-API ambiguity such as “just this once” or unwritten expectations.
- Practical operability of the Kill Switch.
- Persistent return-versus-connection-cost balance.
- Signs of L7 erosion or direct mixing.

## Validity conditions
- Vol.1 interface design and Vol.2 asymmetric translation remain active prerequisites.
- Core, Legacy and Buffer remain structurally distinct.
- Changing Legacy's OS is not required for connection.
- Input Sanitization and Output Packaging operate in both directions.
- Decision rights, responsibility boundaries and termination conditions are explicit.
- Disconnectability exists from the beginning.

## Failure conditions
- Core is fully integrated into Legacy meetings, evaluation criteria and decision processes.
- External leverage is completely blocked, eliminating scaling access.
- The Buffer becomes nominal while direct mixing becomes normal.
- External resources import external value functions and responsibility transfer into Core.
- Packaging alters Core meaning itself.
- Trust, atmosphere or oral promises replace the contract API.
- The Kill Switch is absent or unusable.

## Falsification conditions
The parent article uses T/S/R — Traceability, Sovereignty and Reversibility — as re-agreement conditions. Hypothesis A becomes subject to rejection or revision if: (1) L7 erosion progresses within observation window δ; (2) a persistent condition in which connection cost exceeds return crosses threshold θ; or (3) direct mixing that bypasses the clean room becomes habitual. The parent does not define universal fixed values for θ or δ.

## Required distinctions
- utilization ≠ assimilation
- connection ≠ merger
- connection ≠ isolation
- packaging ≠ content alteration
- contract API ≠ trust itself
- structural complementarity ≠ coercive domination
- Kill Switch ≠ emotional retaliation

## Interpretation constraints
- Metaphors such as “consume,” “overwrite,” or “Trojan horse” must not be generalized into recommendations for violence, unlawful intrusion or deception.
- Legacy must not be reduced to a single malicious personality; the parent treats many of its traits as structural effects of large systems.
- Contract termination does not override applicable law or existing contractual duties.
- Protecting Core does not mean total exclusion of external information or criticism.
- The civilizational causal claims remain hypotheses subject to T/S/R and the stated falsification conditions, not universally established empirical laws.
