## 15-Second Explanation

D-Series Vol. 1 is neither a method for fighting legacy civilization nor a method for blending into it. It is interface design for treating legacy civilization as an “environment,” using necessary institutions, resources, and forms without handing over one’s own axis of judgment, L7, or flame to external evaluation. By combining non-hostility, non-assimilation, translation, semipermeable membranes, hygiene management, adapters, and connection-cost management, the goal becomes not “win/obey” but “pass through without contamination.”

## Why It Is Needed

Even when the OS for one’s thinking and judgment is updated, the foundations of daily life—body, work, taxes, law, family, and local society—remain within legacy institutions. As a result, one continues connecting while the premises inside and outside remain different.

If one chooses total confrontation, one becomes exhausted by colliding with the self-defense of a huge operating system. If one chooses total assimilation, friction may decrease, but majority common sense, evaluation, stability, and prolonged contact gradually pull internal judgment toward the outside. What is needed is to design the connection method before trying to change the other side.

## Practical Process

1. **Define legacy civilization as an environment, not an enemy.** Do not reduce it to individual malice; observe the characteristics of institutions and operating systems.
2. **Move the victory condition to Pass.** The goal is not to prove correctness, reform, or submit, but to receive necessary resources, preserve the core, and pass through.
3. **Maintain non-hostility.** Head-on collision has high connection costs, so do not create unnecessary battlefields.
4. **Maintain non-assimilation.** Adapt to forms where necessary, but do not entrust standards of judgment or conviction to external evaluation.
5. **Translate.** Convert internal structural language into vocabulary the other side can process, such as benefit, stability, precedent, and procedure.
6. **Design a semipermeable membrane.** Let money, infrastructure, safety, and necessary information through, while not allowing sovereignty over values, judgment axes, or the flame to flow in unconsciously.
7. **Observe signs of contamination.** Watch for more “well, whatever,” disappearing discomfort, no longer using internal language, and excessive dependence on evaluation.
8. **Manage hygiene.** Reconfirm L7 after external contact, put into words why judgment wavered, and reassess using internal standards.
9. **Use adapters.** Use forms, vocabulary, and behavior suited to the setting, separating the core from the external output layer.
10. **Recover from adapter fatigue.** Return to conversations and places where internal language can be used as-is, and recover from switching fatigue.
11. **Make connection costs visible.** Recognize the costs in time, cognition, emotion, and opportunity, then choose whether to pay, reduce, or withdraw.
12. **Balance adaptation and independence.** Meet procedures, forms, and roles while preserving freedom to withdraw and sovereignty over internal judgment.

## Examples of Application

**Workplace.** Follow reporting formats, meetings, honorific language, and deadlines. Do not make a manager’s evaluation the final basis for your own value judgment; check whether prolonged contact has deformed your judgment axis.

**Government and contracts.** You can understand and process required documents, deadlines, and forms without ideologically agreeing with an institution. Separate adapting to procedures from assimilating its values.

**Family and relatives.** Do not make being fully understood by everyone a condition of connection. Translate only what is necessary into external language and retain internal standards of judgment.

**Majority culture.** Do not dismiss “normal” and “everyone does it” as mere small talk. Recognize that, over time, they can pressure you to rewrite your standards of judgment, and design distance, contact time, and recovery.

## Success Criteria

- You can fulfill necessary roles without colliding with the outside.
- External evaluation has not become the final standard of internal judgment.
- Discomfort and internal language have not disappeared.
- Non-hostility has not turned into obedience, and non-assimilation has not turned into isolation.
- You can separate external-facing expression from the meaning retained internally.
- You can return to internal standards after contact.
- You have a place where adapter fatigue can be recovered from.
- You understand connection costs and can reduce or withdraw when necessary.
- You maintain freedom to withdraw and internal independence while preserving courtesy and adaptation.

## Limitations

The Parent Original does not ask you to leave society, treat every institution as an enemy, or always lie to the outside. Instead, it establishes a separation in which courtesy, social behavior, and fulfillment of responsibilities are maintained without surrendering internal judgment.

There is no universal fixed value for contact time or hygiene frequency. Examples such as daily, weekly, or monthly are illustrations for preventing maintenance from being left to mood, not uniform KPIs.

## Preventing Misreading

- Legacy civilization is treated as an environment, not a “group of bad people.”
- Non-hostility is not obedience.
- Non-assimilation is not antisocial behavior or isolation.
- Translation does not mean abandoning internal meaning.
- A semipermeable membrane is not complete isolation.
- Contamination is not moral dirt; it is the unconscious rewriting of a judgment axis.
- An adapter is not a false personality.
- Courtesy is not self-erasure.
- Being misunderstood is not the same as a failed connection.
- Withdrawal is not defeat.
