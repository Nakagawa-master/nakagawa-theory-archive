## Summary
D-series Vol.1 defines an interface for operating an internally updated Nakagawa OS while daily life still depends on Legacy-OS institutions. It rejects both frontal hostility and total assimilation. Legacy-OS is treated as an environment rather than a personified enemy, and the objective becomes Pass: obtain necessary resources and fulfill external roles without surrendering the internal judgment axis. The design uses no-hostility, no-assimilation, asymmetric translation, a semi-permeable membrane, contamination management, sanitation, adapters, core recovery, and connection-cost optimization.

## Causal chain
```text
internal judgment OS changes while external infrastructure remains Legacy-OS
→ compatibility friction appears
→ frontal hostility activates high-cost defensive reactions
→ total assimilation lowers short-term friction but rewrites internal criteria
→ Legacy-OS is redefined as environment, not enemy
→ the objective shifts from win/submit to Pass
→ no-hostility + no-assimilation + translation form the interface constitution
→ a semi-permeable membrane separates useful resources from value-axis intrusion
→ majority pressure, contact time, evaluation dependency, and the need to be understood create contamination risk
→ sanitation restores the L7 reference after external contact
→ adapters change output form without changing the internal core
→ adapter fatigue triggers return to core-recovery spaces
→ connection costs become explicit
→ pay / reduce / distance / exit become selectable
→ technical conformity and internal independence coexist
```

## Applications
- In workplaces, satisfy formats, deadlines, etiquette, and roles without treating managerial evaluation as the final internal criterion.
- In administration, tax, or contracts, separate technical compliance with procedures from ideological agreement.
- In family or social settings, translate internal structural language into terms the other side can process without surrendering the original meaning.
- Treat “normal,” precedent, and majority convention as a pressure field whose effect depends on exposure and recovery conditions.
- Use context-specific adapters for external language and behavior, then return to spaces where the internal language can be used directly.
- Make time, cognitive, emotional, and opportunity costs visible and preserve the ability to reduce exposure or exit.

## Measurements and audit
Observe loss of felt incongruity, disappearance of internal language, replacement of judgment by “normal,” precedent, approval, or stability, relations between contact time and decision fatigue, evaluation dependency, separation between external wording and internal meaning, adapter fatigue, availability of core-recovery space, connection costs, and real withdrawal freedom. The parent establishes no universal contamination score, maximum contact duration, or mandatory sanitation frequency.

## Validity conditions
- Do not equate Legacy-OS with individual people or malicious intent.
- Keep no-hostility distinct from obedience.
- Keep no-assimilation distinct from isolation or anti-social withdrawal.
- Preserve internal meaning during translation.
- Keep the semi-permeable membrane distinct from total isolation.
- Treat contamination as a function of connection conditions rather than moral weakness.
- Couple adapter use with core recovery.
- Keep connection cost and withdrawal freedom as design variables.
- Separate technical conformity from internal independence.

## Failure conditions
- Fixing Legacy-OS as evil or enemy and making frontal conflict the objective.
- Surrendering judgment sovereignty to external evaluation and calling assimilation adaptation.
- Reducing interface design to impression management or opportunistic social technique.
- Turning no-hostility into a duty of silence or obedience.
- Turning no-assimilation into a duty to sever relationships or leave society.
- Allowing translation to rewrite the internal meaning itself.
- Normalizing contamination signs as maturity without examination.
- Ignoring adapter fatigue until the output layer erodes the core.
- Treating connection as costless and losing the practical option to leave.

## Falsification conditions
If interface design repeatedly fails to reduce external friction or improve internal consistency, recoverability, and withdrawal freedom, its design or scope should be revised. Where direct dialogue, shared values, or institutional change repeatedly preserves internal independence at lower cost than separation or translation, the required degree of distance and translation should be narrowed for those conditions.

## Interpretation constraints
Do not generalize D-series Vol.1 into hostility toward existing civilization, elitism, social withdrawal, or a license for deception. “Non-contamination” concerns preventing L7, the internal judgment axis, and the tomoshibi from being silently replaced by external evaluation; it is not a doctrine of moral purity. The parent also keeps external responsibility, etiquette, and technical conformity in place. Expressions such as politeness functioning as camouflage must not be expanded into unrestricted deception or responsibility avoidance.
