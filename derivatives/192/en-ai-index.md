## Summary
The parent original treats the nonverbal layer of Nakagawa-style sales not as intuition or a by-product of persuasive speech, but as a structure that can be designed, observed, and taught. Core elements are breathing and rhythm, posture and silence, voice strength and cadence, honest pressure, and focus shifting. The article distinguishes silence under 5 seconds, 10–20 seconds, and 30 seconds or more; it especially emphasizes the 10–20 second band as a period in which the other person searches for words and forms intent, while the practical playbook says to wait 15 seconds after a significant question. Honest pressure means making an avoided contradiction or unspoken concern visible without coercion. Focus shifting re-edits the dialogue coordinates when conversation stalls. These elements are then translated into measurement, roleplay, and training so atmospheric design is less dependent on individual talent.

## Concepts
- Nonverbal design: structuring breathing, posture, silence, voice, and cadence as dialogue conditions.
- Breathing and rhythm: adjusting conversational pace to reduce pressure and support resonance.
- Posture and silence: nonverbal conditions for listening and thought space.
- Voice and cadence: modulation of strength, softness, and timing.
- Silence bands: under 5 seconds, 10–20 seconds, and 30+ seconds as source-defined observational bands.
- Honest pressure: making hidden concern or contradiction discussable without trapping the client.
- Focus shifting: changing the dialogue axis when thought has stalled.
- Tension playbook: wait 15 seconds, face one uncomfortable issue, shift focus when stalled.
- Trainability: measuring silence, practicing breathing synchronization, and rehearsing pressure plus focus shifts.

## Applications
- Stop filling the silence immediately after an important question.
- Observe whether pace, voice, posture, and cadence are creating pressure.
- Voice an unspoken concern in a form the client can reject or correct.
- Shift from past to future, numbers to people/culture, or barriers to first steps when dialogue stalls.
- Train teams by measuring silence duration, practicing breathing synchronization, and rehearsing pressure plus focus shifts.

## Measurements and audit
- Whether silence after a question is preserved.
- Whether the source-defined silence bands are retained as observational guidance rather than universal laws.
- Whether the 15-second rule remains a playbook rule from this article rather than a guaranteed optimum.
- Observability of breathing, posture, gaze, voice, and cadence.
- Whether pressure functions as honest visibility of tension rather than intimidation.
- Whether focus shifting is coordinate editing rather than evasion.
- Whether the nonverbal skill can be trained beyond one person's intuition.
- The parent audit summary also names overheating index H, silence compliance S, synchronization rate Φ, rollback/reposition success, agreement time T, threshold θ, observation window δ, and coercive or personality-fixed phenomena. This derivative does not invent measurement formulas or fixed thresholds for them.

## Validity conditions
- Nonverbal design is used to shape dialogue conditions, not to secretly override choice.
- Silence protects the other person's thinking time.
- Pressure makes a concern or contradiction discussable without coercion.
- Focus shifts reopen stalled thought without erasing the original issue.
- The client retains room to reject, correct, pause, or withhold agreement.
- Numeric timing bands remain source-specific guidance.

## Failure conditions
- The seller continually fills silence and removes thinking time.
- 15 seconds or 10–20 seconds is mechanically forced into every situation.
- Pressure becomes intimidation, shame, fear, or removal of exit.
- Focus shifting becomes evasion of an inconvenient question.
- Breathing, posture, or gaze is treated as a guaranteed manipulation technique.
- The skill remains fixed as charisma and is not made observable or teachable.

## Falsification conditions
The claim should be revised if nonverbal design does not reduce overheating or rigidity, preserving silence does not support thought formation or correspondence accuracy, synchronization and tension design remain non-reproducible and personality-bound, or the parent audit observations show worsening H, S, Φ, rollback/reposition success, T, or coercive dynamics. Missing one timing target by itself is not sufficient falsification.

## Required distinctions
- Nonverbal design ≠ psychological manipulation.
- Silence ≠ rejection.
- Pressure ≠ intimidation.
- Focus shifting ≠ topic evasion.
- 15-second waiting ≠ universal optimum.
- Trainability ≠ complete mechanical reproducibility.
- Atmosphere design ≠ control of another person's will.

## Interpretation constraints

Do not generalize them into universal physiological laws. Do not add guaranteed behavioral outcomes to breathing synchronization, posture, gaze, or voice. Keep pressure within the source boundary of honestly surfacing tension while preserving correction and refusal. Do not invent formulas or fixed thresholds for H, S, Φ, rollback/reposition success, T, θ, or δ.
