## 15-Second Explanation
The original does not read the Nikkei Average’s first-ever breach of 50,000 in October 2025 as proof that “Japan has become prosperous,” but as a sign that markets have begun valuing not only productive outcomes but also “where in the world one is connected.” As AI weakens the circulation between work and wages, money may remain while becoming unable to distribute sufficient legitimacy for social participation. Society shifts toward one in which access rights to AI, medicine, education, and limited economic zones, not only salaries, become rewards. The two axes that make this connection ethical are “correspondence” and “time ethics.”

## Why the 50,000-Yen Stock Price Is the Starting Point
The original reconsiders what the market is buying behind a 50,000-yen stock price. Against the background of a supply-chain position under alliance with the United States, indispensable fields such as semiconductors, materials, and measurement, and large-scale fiscal spending described as exceeding 10 trillion yen, it reads the value of companies and states as being judged not only by “what they made” but also by “which structure they connect to.”

In other words, the stock price prices in expectations of connection, not only substantive outcomes. The original treats this change as a sign that the economy is moving from a structure that runs on “the fruits of labour” to one that runs on “the structure of connection.”

## What Is a Connection-Reward Society?
In a “connection-reward society,” the centre of value shifts from labour volume alone to connection position. For a state, rewards depend on which alliance network it connects to; for a company, which node of a supply chain it occupies; for an individual, which information layer or knowledge network they inhabit.

This is not merely a society of personal connections. It is a change in which social participation itself begins to be determined by which structures one can connect to. The more connection position produces rewards, the more who distributes connection rights becomes a new question of power and legitimacy.

## How AI Changes Monetary Circulation
The traditional monetary economy has assumed the cycle “labour → wages → consumption → production.” The original says that AI is narrowing this cycle’s entry point by removing the training steps for newcomers and entry-level jobs, and beginning to replace analysis, management, and coordination work in the middle layer.

When people lose the entry point to work, more people lose the opportunity to obtain money itself. The “end of the monetary economy” in the original does not mean that banknotes or currency disappear. It means that money ceases to function sufficiently as an apparatus that proves legitimacy for social participation and distributes the qualifications to participate.

## Three Directions the World May Divide
**1. Upper-layer concentration model (deterioration type).** Only those with capital and knowledge receive AI’s fruits; other groups lose participation qualifications and are kept alive through subsidies and similar measures. The original sees a static new caste system beyond this.

**2. Political life support (adjustment type).** This direction artificially supports the former monetary cycle through a 32-hour workweek, robot taxes, profit sharing, and similar measures. In the original, this is distinguished as an adjustment to preserve the old cycle.

**3. Connection-allocation model (new-order type).** Rights to connect to important social structures themselves become rewards: not only salaries, but access to AI tools, priority participation in medical and educational spheres, entry qualifications for limited economic zones, and so on.

## Why Ethics Is Necessary
A society in which connection position produces value is not automatically a just society in which those with more connections are right. Without a structure that guarantees the legitimacy of connection, connection rights become a breeding ground for deception and privilege. The original presents two axes for addressing this problem.

### Correspondence
The value described through a connection and the role of the person who actually supported that value correctly correspond. The original treats a state in which the story of value matches actual contribution as an ethical structure.

### Time Ethics
Evaluate long-term contribution in line with the future beat rather than rewarding only immediate outcomes. The original expresses this as “converting future debt into credit capital,” showing a direction in which contributions that supported structure over the long term can be made visible later and connected to rewards.

## Applications
**1. AI adoption.** Look not only at the volume of work made efficient, but also at whether entry-level work for younger people and middle jobs have disappeared, fixing qualifications for connection to social participation.

**2. Education, medicine, and AI access.** Look not only at salary levels, but at whether rights to connect to important social infrastructure have become substantive rewards.

**3. Corporate evaluation.** Look not only at sales and production volume, but at how a position in indispensable supply chains and knowledge networks is reflected in value evaluation.

**4. Long-term contribution.** Make contributions that support future structure evaluable from the standpoint of time ethics, even when immediate results are small.

## Success Criteria
It is important that connection rights not become fixed among incumbent groups, that they correspond to actual contribution, and that long-term contributions be evaluated through time ethics so that they help make social participation possible again. The original’s audit checks the enclosure of connection rights at fixed points and makes the relationship between evaluation and reward explainable through T = correspondence, S = structural sustainability, R = recursion, and the public audit bundle.

## Limits
The original does not treat a 50,000-yen stock price as a future trading signal. Nor does it unconditionally affirm connection rights. The connection-reward society is described as a structure already beginning to emerge, but it may reverse into upper-layer concentration or fixed privilege if ethical design fails to keep pace.

The integrated audit summary places the symbols threshold θ and observation window δ for extreme concentration, but fixes no specific values. Therefore, do not add arbitrary percentages or periods while preserving evaluability.

## To Avoid Misreading
Do not turn a 50,000-yen stock price directly into proof of social prosperity.

Do not reduce a connection-reward society to a mere society of personal connections, popularity economics, or social-media ratings. The centre is the structural change in which the axes of social participation, value, and reward move to connection position.

Do not turn the “end of money” into a prediction that money itself will disappear. The original addresses the failure of money as an apparatus proving social legitimacy.

A 32-hour workweek, robot taxes, and profit sharing are examples of the political-life-support model, not the connection-allocation model itself.

Do not affirm connection rights without ethics; preserve correspondence and time ethics as the two axes of allocation legitimacy.
